# Securities and Exchange Commission

*government*

Securities regulator that dropped a string of crypto cases after Trump took office.

- Staff said in Feb 2025 meme coins are generally not securities.
- Received WLF's Oct 2024 Form D filing that cut the sale target to $30M.
- Sued Sun in 2023; settled and dismissed the case in 2026.
- Dismissed its Binance/Zhao suit with prejudice in May 2025.
- Securities regulator; per POGO, it has not opened a probe of the ALT5 deal.
