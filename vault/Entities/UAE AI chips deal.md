# UAE AI chips deal

*event*

- May 2025 US-UAE agreement on advanced AI chip exports, cited by critics as context.
- May 2025 AI Acceleration Partnership and subsequent export licences
- 2025 US commitment giving the UAE access to about 500,000 advanced AI chips a year.
- May 2025 AI Acceleration Partnership and later G42 chip licences
- May 2025 framework for advanced chip exports to the UAE
