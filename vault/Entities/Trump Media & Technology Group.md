*company*

Owner of Truth Social, largely owned by Trump's revocable trust; bitcoin treasury, ETFs, API, TAE merger.

- Truth Social parent; holds a bitcoin treasury.
- Owner of Truth Social; raised ~$2.44B in May 2025 for a bitcoin treasury
- Public company that owns Truth Social
- Owner of Truth Social and Truth+; launched Truth API in July 2026
- Trump-controlled owner of Truth Social; partnered with Crypto.com on ETFs, a CRO treasury and Truth Predict
