# Office of the Comptroller of the Currency

*government*

Bank regulator that provisionally approved WLF and Crypto.com charters.

- Federal bank regulator that charters and supervises national banks
- Conditionally approved Crypto.com's national trust bank charter on 2026-02-23
- Bank regulator that conditionally approved the WLF trust charter
- Bank regulator; granted WLF charter approval
- Granted WLF trust bank preliminary approval.
