# C07: MGX's $2 Billion Binance Investment Settled in USD1

**Verdict:** Mostly accurate · medium confidence  
**Type:** Check · Gulf Money

## The Claim

> Did Abu Dhabi's MGX use World Liberty's new USD1 stablecoin to settle its $2 billion investment in Binance, was the Tahnoon stake in World Liberty disclosed at the time, and what does the Trump family earn from USD1 reserves?

## Bottom Line

Documented: MGX, chaired by Tahnoon bin Zayed, used WLF's weeks-old USD1 to settle its $2B Binance investment.

## What Holds Up

- WLF announced USD1 had been selected as the official stablecoin to close MGX's $2B investment in Binance.
- USD1 was basically brand new at the time.
- The chairman of MGX is Tahnoon ('the spy').
- The WSJ links the MGX chairman to the 49% stake in WLF, which went undisclosed in the USD1 announcement.
- The deal catapulted USD1 from obscurity to a huge stablecoin.

## What Needs Correcting

- WLF earns interest on the reserves, potentially hundreds of millions for the Trump family over the presidency (partly accurate)
- **Claimed:** The WSJ associates the MGX chairman 'indirectly with his 49% stake'  
  **The record shows:** The WSJ reported that Aryam Investment 1, a Tahnoon-backed vehicle distinct from MGX, agreed in January 2025 to buy 49% of WLF for $500M.
- The quoted announcement was made by Zach Witkoff on 2025-05-01 at TOKEN2049 Dubai.
- 'Hundreds and hundreds of millions' is not a documented figure; contemporaneous estimates were up to about $80M/yr on the $2B, contingent on Binance holding the tokens, and shared with non-family WLF owners.

## What Is Unproven

- WLF said it is 'unfair to hold a private company to arbitrary standards' and that MGX chose USD1 on its merits.

## In More Detail

- WLF announced this on May 1, 2025 without disclosing that a Tahnoon-backed vehicle (Aryam Investment 1) had agreed in January 2025 to buy 49% of WLF, a stake the WSJ revealed in February 2026.
- USD1 jumped from about $128M to about $2.1B. The interest mechanism is real, but 'hundreds of millions' for the Trump family is a speculative cumulative upper bound (contemporaneous estimates: up to about $80M/yr on the $2B, shared beyond the family).
- WLF's 'unfair' quote could not be independently verified.

## Full Report

**Verdict: mostly-accurate (confidence: medium-high)**

The core facts hold up: MGX, chaired by Sheikh Tahnoon bin Zayed, used World Liberty Financial's (WLF) new USD1 stablecoin to settle its $2B Binance investment. WLF announced this in May 2025 without mentioning that a Tahnoon-backed vehicle (Aryam Investment 1) had agreed in January 2025 to buy 49% of WLF. The deal took USD1 from about $128M to about $2.1B in market cap within days. The "hundreds of millions" interest figure is a speculative upper bound, not a documented number. The Trump family gets only part of WLF's economics, and MGX and Binance both say WLF had no say in choosing USD1.

Note: web fetch to theblock.co and crypto.news was blocked in this session. Findings rest on search-result extracts from the listed sources, Abu Dhabi government releases (WAM, Abu Dhabi Media Office), and secondary coverage of the WSJ investigation. I could not read the WSJ article (paywalled) directly.

### The claim
- MGX and Binance had a deal, and WLF announced that USD1 would be used for it ("We are excited to announce today that USD1 has been selected as the official stablecoin to close MGX's $2 billion investment in Binance").
- USD1 was "basically brand new" at the time.
- The chairman of MGX (Tahnoon, "the spy") is linked by the WSJ to the 49% stake bought in WLF, and that link was not disclosed.
- WLF is said to have responded that it is "unfair to hold a private company to arbitrary standards when raising money" and that MGX chose USD1 on its merits.
- The deal "catapulted USD1 from obscurity to being an absolutely huge stablecoin."
- WLF invests the roughly $2B in reserves in money-market funds and similar holdings and keeps the interest, "potentially hundreds of millions" for the Trump family over the presidency.

### Sub-claims

1. **The announcement and the quote. Confirmed.** On May 1, 2025, at TOKEN2049 in Dubai, WLF co-founder Zach Witkoff said: "We are excited to announce today that USD1 has been selected as the official stablecoin to close MGX's $2 billion investment in Binance." Eric Trump appeared at the same event. MGX announced the Binance investment on March 12, 2025, without naming a stablecoin (Reuters/Fox Business, Bloomberg, Cointelegraph, The Block).
2. **USD1 was brand new. Confirmed.** WLF unveiled USD1 on March 25, 2025, two weeks after MGX announced the Binance deal. Forbes noted the token had almost no track record, even though MGX later cited its "compliance history." USD1 was under $130M in market cap on April 27, 2025.
3. **Tahnoon chairs MGX. Confirmed.** Official WAM and Abu Dhabi Media Office releases show Tahnoon bin Zayed chairing MGX's inaugural meeting and its board meetings in 2024–2026. He is the UAE's national security adviser, which is why he is nicknamed the "spy sheikh."
4. **The WSJ ties Tahnoon to a 49% stake in WLF, and it was not disclosed. Confirmed, with wording corrected.** On about February 1, 2026, the WSJ reported that Aryam Investment 1, a vehicle backed by Tahnoon, agreed in January 2025 (days before the inauguration) to buy 49% of WLF for $500M. Eric Trump reportedly signed the deal. About $187M of the $250M upfront payment went to Trump-family entities. People tied to G42 and MGX took WLF board seats. The phrase "associates him indirectly with his 49% stake" is imprecise: the WSJ links Tahnoon to the stake through Aryam, not through MGX itself. The stake predates the May 2025 USD1 announcement, which did not disclose it. MGX told Forbes it "has not invested in" WLF. That is technically consistent with the WSJ, because the buyer was Aryam, a different Tahnoon-linked entity.
5. **WLF's response: "unfair... arbitrary standards" and "chosen on merit." Partly verified.** I found no independent record of the "unfair / arbitrary standards" wording, so it rests on correspondence that has not been independently published and is unverified. That WLF says MGX chose USD1 on its merits is consistent with WLF's public statements. MGX said it picked USD1 for its backing assets and compliance. Binance (Richard Teng) said it was not involved in the choice.
6. **The deal catapulted USD1. Confirmed.** CoinGecko data cited by Cointelegraph and The Block shows a jump from about $128M to about $2.1B (+1,540%) around April 27–29, 2025, after a wallet received $2B of USD1 between April 16 and 29 (Arkham). USD1 became the 7th-largest stablecoin by May 2025, reached about $2.7B by October 2025 and more than $5B by early 2026. Binance held about 75–87% of the supply.
7. **The Trump family earns interest on the reserves, "hundreds of millions" potentially. Partly accurate and speculative.** The mechanism is real: the issuer keeps the yield on reserves, and holders get none. Fortune put the upper bound at about $80M a year on $2B, at roughly 4% and only if Binance keeps the tokens. Over a four-year term that could reach a few hundred million gross, so "potentially hundreds of millions" works only as a cumulative best case. It is not a documented figure. The family does not get 100%: DT Marks DEFI is entitled to 75% of net protocol revenue, WLF says the family's equity is now about 38%, and Aryam owns 49%. Omid Malekan of Columbia noted that WLF earns nothing if Binance redeems the tokens. MGX says it holds no USD1 and paid WLF no fees. A Bloomberg-based estimate puts WLF's 2026 USD1 income at about $147M across all reserves, not only the MGX $2B.

### Corrections
- Claimed: the WSJ ties the MGX chairman "indirectly with his 49% stake." The record shows: the WSJ (Feb 2026) reported that Aryam Investment 1, a Tahnoon-backed vehicle separate from MGX, agreed in January 2025 to buy 49% of WLF for $500M.
- The speaker of the announcement quote was Zach Witkoff, on May 1, 2025, at TOKEN2049 Dubai.
- "Hundreds and hundreds of millions" is not documented. Contemporaneous estimates were up to about $80M a year on the $2B, and that income is shared beyond the Trump family.

### Denials and other side
- WLF says MGX chose USD1 on its merits. In an unverified, single-source account, WLF also called holding a private company to such standards "unfair."
- MGX says it chose USD1 for its reserves and compliance, holds no USD1 now, paid no fees to WLF and has not invested in WLF.
- Binance says it had no role in the choice of stablecoin.
- WLF and the White House told the WSJ that Trump was not involved in the Aryam deal and that it bought no influence over policy. Trump said he did not know about it.
- Context for the critics' side: Senators Warren and Merkley wrote to MGX and Binance about the deal. A Circle shareholder suit alleges emoluments violations.
