# C91: Dominari: option grants, pre-announcement trading and the Trump sons' arrival

**Verdict:** Mostly accurate · medium confidence  
**Origin:** Connection hunt

## Bottom line

The figures in the brief hold up. On Feb. 10, 2025, the day before the Trump sons' advisory roles were announced, Dominari granted CEO Anthony Hayes and president Kyle Wool fully vested options on 5M shares each (10M in all).

## What holds up

- Hayes and Wool were granted 10M options on Feb. 10, 2025, the day before the Trump sons' advisory roles were announced.
- The options were struck after the run-up, and their value depended on the post-announcement jump.
- Hayes, Wool and other insiders bought stock at $3.47 in the same placement as the Trump sons, on the day of the option grant.
- Stockholders approved the plan increase that made the options exercisable, with a record date after the insiders' and sons' Feb. 2025 share issuances.
- Hayes and Wool were paid about $66M for 2025 (mostly accurate)
- Trump Organization executives beyond the sons hold Dominari stock, and two of them also sit on a Yorkville SPAC board.
- Wool's former firm Revere earned Dominari related-party underwriting revenue (mostly accurate)
- Unusual Machines had a similar pre-announcement run-up.
- SRM Entertainment (Tron Inc.) had a pre-announcement run-up before the Dominari-placed Justin Sun deal (mostly accurate)

## What needs correcting

- Thumzup and Gryphon (American Bitcoin's predecessor) had similar pre-announcement run-ups (false)
- **Claim:** The brief implies the options captured the pre-announcement run-up  
  **Record:** They were struck at the $6.16 Feb. 10 close, after the ~530-580% rise, and their gain came from the post-announcement jump. They were not exercisable until stockholders approved the plan increase on Apr. 1, 2025.
- 'Executives were paid ~$66M' counts grant-date option value (e.g. about $13.1M of Wool's ~$32M, single-source) alongside cash. The Feb. 2025 options were under water at end-2025.
- Thumzup and Gryphon show no reported pre-announcement run-up. Thumzup fell on its disclosure, and Gryphon's spike came on the announcement.
- No SEC, FINRA or exchange inquiry and no filed shareholder suit was found. The only shareholder item is a plaintiff-firm 'investigation' notice (Oct. 2025).

## What is unproven

- Who traded DOMH before the announcement is identified.
- SEC, FINRA or exchange inquiry, or shareholder suits, over the trading or the grants.

## In more detail

- The grant was struck at the $6.16 close, after a roughly 530-580% six-week run-up, and depended on a 10M-share plan increase that stockholders approved on April 1, 2025.
- The same day, Hayes, Wool and other insiders bought into the $3.47 private placement alongside the sons.
- Bloomberg reports about $66M of 2025 pay, a figure that includes grant-date option value.
- No source identifies who bought during the run-up, and no SEC, FINRA or Nasdaq inquiry, filed shareholder suit or lawmaker referral on the trading was found.
- Among the comparators, only Unusual Machines (documented) and SRM (single-source, 67% in two days) show a pre-announcement run-up.
- A new layer: Trump Organization executives Alan Garten, Lawrence Glick and Ron Lieberman are also Dominari shareholders, and Garten and Glick sit on a Yorkville SPAC board (C78).

## Sub-claims

### 1. Hayes and Wool were granted 10M options on Feb. 10, 2025, the day before the Trump sons' advisory roles were announced.

**Confirmed**

- Evidence grade: documented (Dominari 8-K, 10-Q and FY2025 annual report, via search excerpts; sec.gov was blocked for direct fetch).
- The compensation committee granted a nonqualified option on 5,000,000 shares each to Wool and Hayes.
- The 10-Q describes the options as 'fully vested' and granted on Feb. 10, 2025.
- The exercise price was fair market value on the grant date, and the options expire after ten years (2/10/2035).
- The annual report's option table lists 5,000,000 options at a $6.16 exercise price for each executive, and the proxy shows a $30.8M disclosed value per grant (5M x $6.16).
- The options could not be exercised until stockholders approved a 10M-share increase in the 2022 plan reserve.
- Aggregator Form 4 listings date the reported awards to Apr. 16, 2025, after that approval.
- One aggregator's '10,000,000 options to Wool at $3.85' conflicts with the filings and is discarded.
- The announcement came on Feb. 11, so 'the day before' is accurate.
- Advisory agreements with the sons were also dated Feb. 10 (C39).
- Audit: Hayes's Form 4 (signed 16 Apr 2025) confirms the $6.16 exercise price but gives the expiry as 9 Feb 2035 (not 10 Feb) and says the option becomes exercisable on the filing of a Form S-8.
- The '$30.8M disclosed value' equals 5M x $6.16; the audit could not confirm whether the proxy states it or it was derived.
- Amounts: $6.16; $30.8M; $3.85
- Dates: Feb. 10, 2025; Apr. 16, 2025; Feb. 11; Feb. 10; 16 Apr 2025
- Sources: [1](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/12239/000121390025012362/ea0230755-8k_dominari.htm) [2](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/12239/000121390025107872/ea0264187-10q_dominari.htm) [3](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0000012239/000121390026116919/ea0309773-03.pdf) [4](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/12239/000121390025107877/ea0261802-06.htm) [8](https://fintool.com/app/research/companies/DOMH/people/kyle-wool) <https://quantisnow.com/insight/sec-form-4-filed-by-ceo-hayes-anthony-5990818>

### 2. The options were struck after the run-up, and their value depended on the post-announcement jump.

**Confirmed**

- Evidence grade: documented for the prices; the paper-value arithmetic is inferred (our own calculation, which no source makes).
- Barron's put DOMH up 529% from $0.98 on Dec. 31, 2024 to $6.16 at the Feb. 10 close.
- The FT says about 580% in six weeks.
- The $6.16 strike is therefore the pre-announcement close, so the grant did not capture the January run-up.
- It did capture what followed: the stock rose as much as 84% on Feb. 11 (Bloomberg) and closed at about $13 on Feb. 13 (Axios).
- At $13 the 10M options had intrinsic value of about $68M (our arithmetic).
- DOMH ended 2025 just under $5 (C39), below the $6.16 strike, so at year end the options were out of the money (also our inference).
- Amounts: 529%; $0.98; $6.16; about 580%; 84%
- Dates: Dec. 31, 2024; Feb. 10; Feb. 11; Feb. 13
- Sources: [13](https://itiger.com/news/2516537088) [17](https://www.tipranks.com/news/the-fly/trading-in-obscure-stocks-soared-before-trump-appointments-disclosed-ft-says) [14](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2025-02-11/trump-s-sons-back-friend-s-penny-stock-spurring-84-price-jump) [15](https://www.axios.com/2025/02/14/trump-dominari-stock)

### 3. Hayes, Wool and other insiders bought stock at $3.47 in the same placement as the Trump sons, on the day of the option grant.

**Confirmed**

- Evidence grade: documented (Form 4/13D via Investing.com and aggregator listings; Forbes; Barron's).
- On Feb. 10, 2025 (closing Feb. 12), Wool bought 288,184 shares plus 288,184 Series A warrants ($3.72 strike, Feb. 2030) and Series B warrants for a combined $3.47 per share, about $1M of personal funds.
- His 13D shows 2,200,769 shares beneficially owned, including 1,241,989 held by his spouse, Soo Yu.
- Investing.com separately reports Hayes buying in the placement, and an aggregator's Form 4 summary puts his direct holding at 975,007 shares afterwards (single-source).
- Forbes reports that the CEO, COO, president and four board members together bought 664,000 shares at $3.47.
- Barron's says the private sale was 2,436,587 shares, and the sons each invested about $1M. Dominari described the $13.5M deal as priced 'at the market under Nasdaq rules'.
- That price was about 44% below (our arithmetic: $3.47 vs $6.16) the $6.16 close on the day the options were struck.
- Our explanation, which no source states, is that the Nasdaq minimum price can use a five-day average, which lags a fast-rising stock.
- The SEC record shows no open-market buying by Hayes or Wool during the January run-up; their only reported buys are the Feb. 10 placement.
- This is unverified because Form 4s were not read directly.
- Audit: Investing.com's Form 4 write-ups give Hayes 288,184 shares (the same as Wool), COO Christopher Devall 351,851 and director Kyle Haug 14,409.
- Those four alone total 942,628 shares (our arithmetic), more than the 664,000 Forbes reports for seven insiders; the discrepancy is unresolved and may reflect how warrants or units were counted.
- Forbes also reports six insiders received 402,000 bonus shares.
- Amounts: $3.72; $3.47; about $1M; $13.5M; about 44%
- Dates: Feb. 10, 2025; Feb. 12; Feb. 2030; Feb. 10
- Sources: [9](https://ng.investing.com/news/insider-trading-news/dominari-holdings-president-kyle-wool-buys-shares-worth-1m-93CH-1759054) [10](https://ng.investing.com/news/insider-trading-news/dominari-holdings-ceo-anthony-hayes-acquires-shares-in-private-placement-93CH-1759014) [11](https://quantisnow.com/insight/ceo-hayes-anthony-bought-288184-shares-increasing-direct-ownership-by-42-to-975007-units-5885153) [12](https://www.barchart.com/story/news/30872401/dominari-holdings-inc-announces-13-5-million-registered-direct-and-private-placement-offerings-priced-at-the-market-under-nasdaq-rules-and-declares-special-cash-dividend) [16](https://www.joemygod.com/2025/03/forbes-trump-sons-suspected-of-insider-trading/) [13](https://itiger.com/news/2516537088) <https://ng.investing.com/news/insider-trading-news/dominari-holdings-coo-christopher-devall-acquires-351851-shares-93CH-1759024> <https://ng.investing.com/news/insider-trading-news/dominari-holdings-director-kyle-haug-acquires-shares-in-private-placement-93CH-1759036>

### 4. Stockholders approved the plan increase that made the options exercisable, with a record date after the insiders' and sons' Feb. 2025 share issuances.

**Confirmed**

- Evidence grade: documented for the meeting facts (DEF 14A and 8-K via search excerpts); the voting-power observation is inferred.
- The special meeting on Apr. 1, 2025 had a Feb. 24, 2025 record date.
- It raised the 2022 plan reserve from 1,404,404 to 11,404,404 shares.
- A quorum of 6,027,100 shares (about 42% of eligible shares) attended, and the amendment passed 5,684,835 for, 321,884 against, 20,381 abstaining.
- Because the record date came after the Feb. 10-12 issuances, the sons (966,138 shares each per 13G) and Hayes and Wool (roughly 3.2M including Wool's spouse) could vote shares acquired that week.
- Together these come to about 5.1M, close to the 5.68M 'for' votes.
- That is our own observation: no source says how any of them voted, and the 13G share counts may include shares that were not entitled to vote.
- Proxy language confirmed that Hayes and Wool could not exercise unless the increase was approved.
- Amounts: about 42%
- Dates: Apr. 1, 2025; Feb. 24, 2025; Feb. 10-12
- Sources: [5](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/12239/000121390025021928/ea0231750-02.htm) [6](https://www.quantisnow.com/insight/dominari-holdings-inc-filed-sec-form-8-k-submission-of-matters-5975550) [7](https://equibles.com/stocks/domh/documents/4e001452-a705-4c94-88b3-1630fdd37187)

### 5. Hayes and Wool were paid about $66M for 2025.

**Mostly accurate**

- Evidence grade: documented for the totals (Bloomberg, Jan. 27, 2026, syndicated on Bloomberg Government); the breakdown is single-source.
- Bloomberg reports Hayes at about $34M and Wool at almost $32M for 2025.
- An aggregator gives Wool's breakdown as $500,000 salary, $11M cash bonus, $860,894 stock awards, $13.1M option awards and $6.2M non-equity incentive pay (about $31.8M).
- '$66M paid' therefore mixes cash with the accounting value of the Feb. 2025 options, which were under water at year end.
- In June 2025, Dominari amended both executives' employment agreements to drop their right to further stock grants in exchange for a cash bonus tied to net revenue (8-K).
- On Mar. 4, 2026, stockholders approved another 10M-share plan increase, of which the proxy contemplated 6M for special awards to Hayes and Wool.
- It also added an evergreen of up to 20% of outstanding shares each year from 2027 to 2032.
- Bloomberg ties the 2025 pay to deals with the Trump family.
- Audit: Bloomberg's 27 Jan 2026 headline ('Trump Tower Banker Duo Share $66 Million Pay Package for 2025') and the $34M/almost $32M split are confirmed.
- A Chinese-language relay surfaced in search says Dominari called the story inaccurate without specifying; we could not read it, so that rebuttal is unconfirmed.
- Amounts: about $34M; almost $32M; $500,000; $11M; $860,894
- Dates: Jan. 27, 2026; Feb. 2025; June 2025; Mar. 4, 2026; 27 Jan 2026
- Sources: [20](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-01-27/trump-tower-banker-duo-share-66-million-pay-package-for-2025) [21](https://news.bgov.com/banking-law/trump-tower-banker-duo-share-66-million-pay-package-for-2025) [8](https://fintool.com/app/research/companies/DOMH/people/kyle-wool) [22](https://www-web.itiger.com/news/2546207729) [23](https://www.boardroomalpha.com/sec/domh-8-k-2026-03-05-0001213900-26-024123) [24](https://www.itiger.com/hant/news/2617795435)

## Denials and context

- Dominari: it had 'no reason to believe that any of these people breached any duty, whether legal or fiduciary' and had no information about why investors bought before the announcement (FT/Forbes coverage, C39).
- Dominari spokesman Brett Maas (May 2025): neither advisers nor investors had sold shares (C39).
- Don Jr. bought Thumzup on his investment adviser's recommendation and has no other involvement with the company (Bloomberg, as reported by The Block).
- A pre-announcement run-up is consistent with leaks, but also with momentum or speculative trading.
  - No source shows that any named person traded on nonpublic information.
- The options were priced at fair market value on the grant date and approved by stockholders.
  - Grants at market price before a planned announcement are legal if disclosed, although governance experts criticize 'spring-loaded' timing (general point; no source applies that term to Dominari).
- The companies (Dominari, Unusual Machines) did not respond to FT requests for comment on the trading.
- sec.gov, panabee.com, quantisnow.com and equibles.com could not be resolved for direct fetch in this session.
  - Filing details come from search-result excerpts and secondary summaries.
- Dominari reportedly disputed Bloomberg's Jan 2026 pay story as containing inaccurate statements and misinterpretations, without saying what was wrong (one Chinese-language relay; unconfirmed).

## Full report

**Verdict:** mostly accurate (medium confidence)

**Summary.** The figures in the brief hold up against the filings.

- **The grant.** On Feb. 10, 2025, the day before Donald Trump Jr. and Eric Trump were announced as advisers, Dominari's compensation committee gave CEO Anthony Hayes and president Kyle Wool fully vested options on 5M shares each (10M in total).
- **The price and conditions.** The options were struck at fair market value, $6.16, which was the close after a roughly 530-580% six-week run-up. They could not be exercised until stockholders approved a 10M-share plan increase on April 1, 2025.
- **The placement.** The same day, Hayes, Wool and other insiders bought into the $3.47 private placement alongside the sons.
- **The pay.** Bloomberg puts the pair's 2025 pay at about $66M. That figure includes option value as well as cash.

Three parts of the brief are weaker:
- **Who traded is unknown.** No source identifies who bought during the run-up.
- **No inquiry or lawsuit.** No SEC, FINRA or Nasdaq inquiry and no filed shareholder suit were found.
- **The pattern does not generalise.** Of the comparators, only Unusual Machines (documented) and SRM/Tron (single source) show a run-up before the announcement. Thumzup and Gryphon do not.

The hunt also found a new layer: three further Trump Organization executives are Dominari shareholders, and two of them also sit on a Yorkville SPAC board.

**Access note.** sec.gov, panabee.com, quantisnow.com and equibles.com could not be resolved for direct fetch in this session. Filing details below come from search-result excerpts of the filings and from secondary coverage, cross-checked where possible.

### What the brief says

This is a third-layer hunt from C39. An analysis cited in C39's leads alleges three things:

### Connections and findings, with evidence grades

1. **The 10M option grant on Feb. 10, 2025: confirmed (documented).**
   - The 8-K reports a nonqualified option on 5,000,000 shares each to Wool and Hayes, at fair market value on the grant date, with a 10-year term.
   - The 10-Q says the options were "fully vested" and conditioned on stockholder approval of a reserve increase [1][2].
   - The FY2025 annual report lists each grant at a **$6.16** exercise price, expiring Feb. 10, 2035. The proxy shows $30.8M of disclosed value per grant [3][4]. *Audit:* Hayes's Form 4 gives the expiry as Feb. 9, 2035 and says the option becomes exercisable on filing of a Form S-8. The $30.8M equals 5M x $6.16; the audit could not confirm whether the proxy states it or it was derived.
   - Aggregator Form 4 listings show the awards reported on Apr. 16, 2025, after the approval.
   - One aggregator figure ("10M to Wool at $3.85") conflicts with the filings and is discarded.

2. **The strike was set after the run-up; the gain came from the announcement: confirmed (prices documented; the arithmetic is ours).**
   - Barron's: DOMH rose 529%, from $0.98 on Dec. 31 to $6.16 at the Feb. 10 close [13]. The FT figure is about 580% [17].
   - Bloomberg: up as much as 84% on Feb. 11 [14]. Axios: about $13 at the Feb. 13 close [15].
   - *Our own arithmetic, which no source states:* at $13 the 10M options carried about $68M of intrinsic value. The stock ended 2025 just under $5, so the options were then under water.

3. **Insiders bought at $3.47 alongside the sons on grant day: confirmed (documented).**
   - Wool's Form 4 and 13D show 288,184 shares plus Series A ($3.72) and Series B warrants, at a combined $3.47, for about $1M of personal funds. That brought his beneficial holding to 2,200,769 shares, including 1,241,989 held by his spouse Soo Yu [9].
   - Hayes also bought in the placement: Investing.com gives 288,184 shares, the same as Wool [10]. His direct holding of 975,007 shares afterwards comes from one aggregator [11].
   - Forbes: the CEO, COO, president and four directors bought 664,000 shares at $3.47, and six insiders received 402,000 bonus shares [16].
   - *Audit:* Investing.com also reports COO Christopher Devall buying 351,851 shares and director Kyle Haug 14,409. With Hayes and Wool that is 942,628 shares (our arithmetic), more than Forbes's 664,000. The mismatch is unresolved and may reflect how units or warrants were counted.
   - Dominari called the $13.5M raise "priced at the market under Nasdaq rules" [12]. That price was about 44% below (our arithmetic) the $6.16 close used for the options the same day.
   - *Inferred, no source states it:* the most likely reason is that Nasdaq's minimum-price test can use a five-day average, which lags a fast-rising stock.
   - No open-market buying by Hayes or Wool during January 2025 appears in coverage. This is unverified, because the Form 4s could not be read directly.

4. **The stockholder vote that unlocked the options: confirmed (documented); the voting-power point is inferred.**
   - The special meeting was held Apr. 1, 2025, with a **Feb. 24 record date**. It raised the plan reserve from 1,404,404 to 11,404,404 shares.
   - Quorum was 6,027,100 shares (about 42%). The amendment passed 5,684,835 for, 321,884 against and 20,381 abstaining [5][6][7].
   - *Inferred, no source makes this point:* the record date fell after the Feb. 10-12 issuances. The sons (966,138 shares each) and Hayes and Wool (about 3.2M with Wool's spouse) together held about 5.1M shares, close to the 5.68M votes in favour.
   - How anyone actually voted is not disclosed, and some of those shares may not have been entitled to vote.

5. **About $66M in 2025 pay: mostly accurate (totals documented; breakdown single-source).**

   - June 2025: both employment agreements were amended. The executives gave up further stock grants in exchange for a cash bonus tied to net revenue [22].
   - March 4, 2026: stockholders approved another 10M-share increase. The proxy contemplated 6M of it for special awards to Hayes and Wool, plus an evergreen of up to 20% of outstanding shares each year from 2027 to 2032 [23][24].

6. **Who traded before the announcement: unverified.**
   - Forbes: daily volume averaged about 1.2M shares from Dec. 30, against about 11,500 before [16]. TipRanks gives other figures [18].
   - The FT quoted Adam Pritchard (University of Michigan), who called the trading "clearly unusual" and said an adviser may not face the restrictions a director does [17][19].
   - No buyer has been named. The sons each stayed below 10% and so filed no Form 4s (C39).
