# C37: Jared Kushner's hacked phone

**Verdict:** Unverified · medium confidence  
**Origin:** Follow-up lead

> [!warning] Unverified single-source allegation: this could not be independently corroborated. It is reported as an allegation, not as an established fact.

## Bottom line

One specific allegation exists, but it rests on a single source.

## What holds up

- A credible outlet has reported that Kushner's phone was hacked.
- Official responses: denials from Kushner, his lawyer and Trump; no Dutch confirmation.
- Congress has linked the alleged hack to his current envoy role in the Russia and Iran talks.
- Chinese state hackers (Salt Typhoon) targeted Kushner's phone and communications in 2024.
- First-term communications-security concerns (WhatsApp with foreign leaders, Saudi spyware risk).

## What needs correcting

- Social-media claims that Dutch intelligence 'confirmed' the hack or that Kushner is a foreign intelligence operative (false)
- Headlines saying 'Dutch spies hacked Kushner's phone' compress the book's account: it says the GRU hacked the phone and the MIVD obtained screen images second-hand via a turned GRU officer.
- The alleged monitoring dates to 2017-18 (Trump's first term), not his 2025-26 envoy period; its relevance to current talks is critics' inference.
- Dollar figures in coverage are inconsistent ($700M+, $1.75B, $2B); the core alleged account is about $750M at First Abu Dhabi Bank.
- The 2024 Salt Typhoon reporting shows Kushner was targeted, not that his data was stolen.

## What is unproven

- Russia's GRU compromised Kushner's personal iPhone in 2017-18, and Dutch MIVD obtained access by turning a GRU hacker.
- The screenshots showed Kushner accessing a roughly $750M Saudi/UAE-funded account (part of $1.75B-$2B in secret funds).
- Kushner's phone was compromised by Pegasus, Paragon or Iranian hackers.

## In more detail

- Matthew Cole's book 'Princes and Thieves', excerpted by Vanity Fair on 22 Oct 2026, says Russia's GRU hacked Kushner's personal iPhone in 2017-18 and that Dutch military intelligence (MIVD) gained screen-image access via a turned GRU hacker, seeing him log into a roughly $750M UAE/Saudi-funded account at First Abu Dhabi Bank.
- No government has confirmed it: the MIVD declined comment, and Kushner, his lawyer and Trump deny it.
- The firmly documented incident is narrower: in Oct 2024 Chinese Salt Typhoon hackers targeted Kushner's phone and call data via US telecoms, with no public evidence that his data was taken.

## Sub-claims

### 1. A credible outlet has reported that Kushner's phone was hacked.

**Confirmed**

- Vanity Fair published an excerpt of national-security reporter Matthew Cole's book 'Princes and Thieves' (Simon & Schuster/Atria) on 22 Oct 2026.
- It was widely picked up by Dutch (NL Times, DutchNews, NOS) and US outlets.
- This confirms only that the allegation was reported, not that the hack happened.
- Dates: 22 Oct 2026
- Sources: [1](https://gizmodo.com/report-dutch-spies-hacked-jared-kushners-iphone-watched-him-use-sleazy-middle-eastern-slush-fund-2000829742) [3](https://nltimes.nl/2026/10/23/dutch-intelligence-service-spied-phone-trumps-son-law-jared-kushner-book) [4](https://www.dutchnews.nl/2026/10/dutch-spies-hacked-jared-kushners-phone-alleges-book/)

### 2. Russia's GRU compromised Kushner's personal iPhone in 2017-18, and Dutch MIVD obtained access by turning a GRU hacker.

**Unverified**

- This is a single-source book claim resting on an unnamed source who called Cole in spring 2020; Cole does not say he saw the Dutch material.
- In the book's account the MIVD turned a GRU hacker in late 2017 (work for them or face arrest in Britain), gained access to GRU-held screenshots, and from early 2018 could see the screen while the phone was in use.
- There was no microphone, camera or manipulation capability.
- It is unclear whether Kushner knew.
- No US or Dutch official has confirmed any part of this.
- Dates: late 2017; early 2018
- Sources: [2](https://cybernews.com/security/jared-kushner-hacked-iphone-gru-russia/) [15](https://www.status.news/p/jared-kushner-vanity-fair-book-matthew-cole) [16](https://www.democracynow.org/2026/11/2/matthew_cole_jared_kushner_book)

### 3. The screenshots showed Kushner accessing a roughly $750M Saudi/UAE-funded account (part of $1.75B-$2B in secret funds).

**Unverified**

- The book alleges about $750M of Saudi and Emirati origin was routed via an Azerbaijani bank into a First Abu Dhabi Bank account held by the UAE government-funded Watani Al Emarat Foundation, with Kushner a signatory, not the owner.
- It also alleges about $1B sent via Azerbaijan to an Israeli government account and a third, defence-related account.
- Headline totals vary ($700M+, $1.75B, $2B).
- No bank records were published.
- Axios's Barak Ravid said parts 'don't add up', and the Jerusalem Post pulled its write-up, citing 'significant factual questions'.
- Amounts: about $750M; about $1B; $700M+; $1.75B; $2B
- Sources: [12](https://lavocedinewyork.com/en/news/2026/10/23/kushner-denies-vanity-fair-claims-of-secret-funds-from-uae-azerbaijan/) [13](https://jam-news.net/kushners-links-to-azerbaijani-bank/) [10](https://mediaite.com/media/news/political-world-piles-on-vanity-fairs-bombshell-jared-kushner-report-bananas)

### 4. Official responses: denials from Kushner, his lawyer and Trump; no Dutch confirmation.

**Confirmed**

- The MIVD and the Dutch Defence Ministry told NOS and Reformatorisch Dagblad they do not comment on methods, knowledge or sources.
- Kushner called the claims 'completely insane' and said they 'never happened'.
- Chad Mizelle sent Condé Nast a letter on 23 Oct 2026 calling the excerpt defamatory per se and demanding takedown; it says Cole and Simon & Schuster were warned on 30 Jul and 10 Aug. Trump called it 'a totally false story' on 23 Oct. No suit, retraction or correction had been found by mid-Nov 2026.
- Dates: 23 Oct 2026; 30 Jul; 10 Aug; 23 Oct; mid-Nov 2026
- Sources: [3](https://nltimes.nl/2026/10/23/dutch-intelligence-service-spied-phone-trumps-son-law-jared-kushner-book) [6](https://www.newsweek.com/jared-kushners-lawyer-puts-vanity-fair-on-notice-over-false-claims-12614525) [7](https://thehill.com/media/6170355-kushner-attorneys-warn-vanity-fair/) [9](https://mediaite.com/media/news/its-a-totally-false-story-trump-slams-vanity-fair-story-on-jared-kushner)

### 5. Congress has linked the alleged hack to his current envoy role in the Russia and Iran talks.

**Confirmed**

- Rep. Jamie Raskin (House Judiciary ranking member) demanded in Oct 2026 that Kushner recuse 'from negotiating or representing the United States in any capacity' in the Russia and Iran talks until he produces records of foreign-government payments, citing the slush-fund and GRU phone-hack reports.
- This extends Raskin's 17 Apr 2026 probe.
- That the GRU holds actual leverage over Kushner is inference, not reported fact.
- Dates: Oct 2026; 17 Apr 2026
- Sources: [17](https://democrats-judiciary.house.gov/media-center/press-releases/ranking-member-raskin-demands-jared-kushner-recuse-from-us-envoy-role-in-russia-and-iran-conflicts-amid-explosive-reports-of-secret-middle-eastern-slush-funds-and-phone-hacking-by-russian-intelligence-service) [18](https://democrats-judiciary.house.gov/media-center/press-releases/ranking-member-raskin-opens-sweeping-investigation-into-us-special-envoy-for-peace-jared-kushner-s-foreign-entanglements-and-staggering-conflicts-of-interest)

### 6. Chinese state hackers (Salt Typhoon) targeted Kushner's phone and communications in 2024.

**Confirmed**

- In late Oct 2024 the NYT and others reported that China-linked hackers inside US telecom carriers targeted Eric Trump and Kushner, among others.
- The FBI said call logs and unencrypted texts may have been exposed.
- What was taken from Kushner was never disclosed.
- A source close to him said there was 'no indication that anyone got access to the data'.
- This is confirmed targeting; compromise is unproven.
- Dates: late Oct 2024
- Sources: [19](https://gvwire.com/2024/10/29/trump-family-members-and-biden-aides-among-china-hack-targets/) [20](https://www.israelhayom.com/2024/10/30/fbi-china-hacked-trump-family-phones/)

### 7. First-term communications-security concerns (WhatsApp with foreign leaders, Saudi spyware risk).

**Confirmed**

- In Mar 2019 Rep. Cummings said Kushner's lawyer Abbe Lowell had told the committee Kushner used WhatsApp for official business; Lowell disputed parts of that.
- A Saudi royal-court source told CNN that Kushner messaged MBS on WhatsApp (single source).
- WhatsApp patched an NSO spyware flaw in May 2019.
- In Jan 2020 UN rapporteur Agnes Callamard urged Kushner to check or replace his phone after the Bezos hack linked to an MBS account.
- None of this showed an actual compromise of his phone.
- Dates: Mar 2019; May 2019; Jan 2020
- Sources: [21](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/politics/cummings-jared-kushner-and-ivanka-trumps-private-emails-texts-raise-security-concerns) [22](https://amp.cnn.com/cnn/2019/03/23/politics/kushner-whatsapp-concerns) [23](https://www.washingtonexaminer.com/?p=1409170) [24](https://www.washingtonexaminer.com/?p=2001998)

### 8. Kushner's phone was compromised by Pegasus, Paragon or Iranian hackers.

**Unverified**

- No credible report was found.
- Iran-linked groups leaked material from other Trump-circle accounts (e.g. Kash Patel's Gmail, Mar 2026).
- The Feb 2026 Gabbard whistleblower complaint concerns an NSA intercept of foreign nationals discussing Kushner, not a hack of his device.
- Dates: Mar 2026; Feb 2026
- Sources: [28](https://www.nbcnewyork.com/news/national-international/iranian-hackers-emails-allegedly-stolen-kash-patel/6482486/) [26](https://san.com/cc/kushner-cited-in-classified-whistleblower-complaint-against-dni-gabbard-report/)

### 9. Social-media claims that Dutch intelligence 'confirmed' the hack or that Kushner is a foreign intelligence operative.

**False**

- Dutch authorities declined comment and confirmed nothing.
- Nothing in the book or in any verified record calls Kushner an operative; that version is viral distortion.
- Sources: [29](https://hasbara.co.il/facts-myths/myth-jared-kushner-intelligence-allegations) [3](https://nltimes.nl/2026/10/23/dutch-intelligence-service-spied-phone-trumps-son-law-jared-kushner-book)

## Denials and context

- Kushner: the claims are 'completely insane', 'outrageous and categorically false' and 'never happened'; he points to the absence of bank statements, account numbers or dates.
- Chad Mizelle: 'no credible evidence'; 'There are no accounts and never were'; demand letter to Condé Nast on 23 Oct 2026 threatening defamation action.
- Trump (23 Oct 2026): 'a totally false story', 'fake news'.
- MIVD and the Dutch Defence Ministry: no comment on operational methods, sources or knowledge; neither confirmation nor denial.
- Skeptics: Axios's Barak Ravid says key details 'don't add up' (e.g. a Bank of Israel account with Netanyahu as signatory); the Jerusalem Post removed its story over 'significant factual questions'.
- Kushner's public denials centre on the accounts; no coverage found has him specifically addressing whether his phone was compromised.
- Cole stands by the reporting (Democracy Now, 2 Nov 2026).
  - Vanity Fair had not responded publicly to the takedown demand.

## Full report

**Verdict: unverified** (confidence: medium)

A specific, widely reported allegation exists: Russia's GRU hacked Kushner's personal iPhone in 2017-18, and Dutch military intelligence (MIVD) later piggy-backed on that access. It comes from a single source, Matthew Cole's book *Princes and Thieves*, excerpted in Vanity Fair on 22 Oct 2026. Cole relies on unnamed sources and says he has not seen the Dutch material. No government has confirmed it. The MIVD and the Dutch Defence Ministry declined to comment. Kushner, his lawyer and Trump deny it.

One related incident is solidly reported. In Oct 2024 Chinese state hackers (Salt Typhoon) **targeted** Kushner's phone and call data through US telecom networks. That reporting does not show any of his data was actually taken. Kushner's camp says there is "no indication that anyone got access."

### What the lead asks

The lead is a user-requested follow-up to C15 (Kushner and Affinity Partners), and the video does not mention it. It asks whether Kushner's phone or communications were hacked: by whom, when, what was accessed, and how this connects to his envoy role, his negotiations and Affinity's Gulf investors.

**Method note:** direct fetches of cybernews.com, dailycaller.com and democrats-judiciary.house.gov failed because those hosts are blocked in this environment. The findings below come from search-result text across about 30 outlets: Newsweek, The Hill, TheWrap, Mediaite, NL Times, DutchNews, Gizmodo, IBTimes, The Week, Democracy Now, Sinclair/KOMO, La Voce di New York, JAMnews and others. No primary Vanity Fair text was read.

### Sub-claims

1. **A credible outlet has reported that Kushner's phone was hacked. Confirmed, as a fact of reporting.** Vanity Fair ran an excerpt of Cole's book on 22 Oct 2026; Cole is a national-security reporter. Simon & Schuster (Atria / One Signal) is publishing the book. Dutch outlets (NL Times, DutchNews, NOS, Reformatorisch Dagblad) and US outlets (Newsweek, The Hill, Gizmodo, Mediaite) picked it up widely.

2. **Russia's GRU compromised his personal iPhone in 2017-2018. Unverified, single source.** According to the book:
   - The GRU first hacked the phone, which some outlets describe as an iPhone X.
   - In late 2017 the MIVD turned a GRU hacker who was travelling in Britain, offering him a choice between working for them and arrest by the British.
   - That gave the MIVD access to a GRU server holding screenshots of the phone.
   - From early 2018 the Dutch could see his screen while he used it. Cole says this was "not malware where they could manipulate the phone": there was no microphone, camera or audio access.

   Cole's account rests on "a source I knew to be reliable" who called him in late spring 2020. Reporting notes that Cole does not say he has seen the Dutch intelligence material. It is unclear whether Kushner ever knew. No US or Dutch official has confirmed any part of this.

3. **The screenshots showed him logging into a UAE/Saudi-funded "foreign-policy fund." Unverified, disputed.**
   - **Account A:** about $750M of Saudi and Emirati origin, routed through an Azerbaijani bank into a First Abu Dhabi Bank (Dubai) account held by the UAE government-funded Watani Al Emarat Foundation. Kushner is described as a signatory, not the owner. This is the most consistently reported figure.
   - **Account B ("Beta"):** about $1B allegedly sent via Azerbaijan to an Israeli government account.
   - **Account C:** a defence-related account.

   Headline totals vary between about $1.75B and $2B. Axios's Barak Ravid said parts "don't add up." He argued that an account of that kind at the Bank of Israel is not technically possible and that Netanyahu could not be a signatory. The Jerusalem Post pulled its write-up, citing "significant factual questions." No bank records, account numbers or transaction dates were published.

4. **Official responses. Confirmed.**
   - **MIVD and the Dutch Defence Ministry:** no comment on operational methods, the extent of their knowledge, or their sources. They neither confirmed nor denied.
   - **Kushner:** called it "completely insane," "outrageous and categorically false," and said it "never happened."
   - **Chad Mizelle (his lawyer):** "no credible evidence." On 23 Oct he sent Condé Nast a letter calling the excerpt defamatory per se and demanding it be taken down and records preserved. The letter says Cole and Simon & Schuster were warned on 30 Jul and 10 Aug.
   - **Trump:** called it "a totally false story" and "fake news" in a White House gaggle on 23 Oct.
   - No lawsuit, retraction or Vanity Fair correction was found as of mid-Nov 2026.
   - Note: the denials focus mainly on the accounts. None of the coverage I found has Kushner specifically addressing whether the phone was compromised.

5. **Congress has tied the allegation to his current envoy role. Confirmed.** House Judiciary ranking member Jamie Raskin issued a release titled "Raskin Demands Jared Kushner Recuse from U.S. Envoy Role in Russia and Iran Conflicts Amid Explosive Reports of Secret Middle Eastern Slush Funds and Phone Hacking by Russian Intelligence Service." It demands recusal "from negotiating or representing the United States in any capacity" until he produces records of foreign-government contacts and payments. This builds on Raskin's investigation opened on 17 Apr 2026. Democrats are in the minority and cannot issue subpoenas. The explicit concern is that the GRU, the service of Russia's negotiating counterpart, may hold kompromat while Kushner negotiates on Ukraine. That concern is inference; no evidence of actual leverage has been reported.

6. **Chinese hackers targeted his phone and call data in 2024. Confirmed as targeting; access unproven.** In late Oct 2024 the NYT, the Washington Post and others reported that Salt Typhoon-linked hackers inside US telecom carriers targeted Eric Trump and Kushner, along with Trump, Vance and Biden-Harris aides. The FBI said call logs and unencrypted SMS may have been exposed. Investigators did not say what, if anything, was taken from Kushner. A source close to him said: "Everybody's been trying to hack Jared ... there's no indication that anyone got access to the data."

7. **First-term communications-security concerns. Confirmed (context, not a hack).**
   - **WhatsApp:** in March 2019 House Oversight chair Elijah Cummings wrote that Kushner's lawyer Abbe Lowell had said Kushner "has used—and continues to use—WhatsApp" for official business. Lowell disputed parts of that account and said whether classified material was shared was "above my paygrade." A Saudi royal-court source told CNN that Kushner messaged MBS on WhatsApp; this is single-source.
   - **NSO flaw:** in May 2019 WhatsApp patched an NSO Group flaw that could infect phones with a missed call, and outlets noted Kushner used the app.
   - **Security clearance:** Trump ordered a top-secret clearance for Kushner over the objections of career officials.
   - **UN warning:** after the Bezos phone hack was attributed to an MBS WhatsApp account, UN rapporteur Agnes Callamard said in Jan 2020 that Kushner should check or replace his phone.
   - None of this produced evidence that his phone was actually compromised.

8. **Pegasus, Paragon or Iranian compromise of Kushner's phone. No credible report found.** Iran-linked groups ("Robert" and Handala) leaked material from Trump-circle accounts, including Kash Patel's Gmail in March 2026. No report names Kushner as a victim. The Feb 2026 Gabbard whistleblower complaint is about an NSA intercept of **foreign nationals discussing** Kushner (described by Ynet as Iran-linked). It is not a hack of his device.

9. **Social media and rumour.** Viral claims go further than the book: that Kushner is a foreign "intelligence operative," or that Dutch intelligence "confirmed" the hack. These are unsupported. A fact-check by an Israeli advocacy site debunks the "operative" version, though that site also misdates the book's monitoring.

### Corrections and caveats

- The book places the monitoring in **2017-18, during Trump's first term**, not during the current envoy period. Its present-day relevance (possible GRU kompromat during the Ukraine talks) is an inference by critics.
- "Dutch spies hacked Kushner's phone" is how many headlines put it. In the book's account the GRU did the hacking and the MIVD gained screen-image access second-hand.
- Dollar totals in headlines ($700M+, $1.75B, $2B) are not consistent. The $750M FAB account is the core claim.
- The 2024 Salt Typhoon reports show targeting, not confirmed theft of Kushner's data.

### Connections to the rest of the story

- **Affinity and Gulf investors (C15):** the alleged $750M fund is described as Saudi and Emirati money accessible to Kushner while he was a White House adviser. That money would predate PIF's $2B commitment to Affinity in 2021. If true, it would make the Gulf relationship far older and more personal than the Affinity record shows. Nothing ties the alleged accounts to Affinity itself.
- **Envoy role:** Raskin used the hacking and fund allegations to demand recusal from the Russia and Iran talks. This adds to CREW's October 2026 lawsuit and the Wyden-Garcia inquiries.
- **Azerbaijan, Israel and the UAE:** the alleged routing via Azerbaijan and the Abraham Accords-era timing are new threads that are not in the video.
