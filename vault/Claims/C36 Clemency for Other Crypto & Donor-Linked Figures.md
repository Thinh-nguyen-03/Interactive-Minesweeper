# C36: Clemency for Other Crypto & Donor-Linked Figures

**Verdict:** Mostly accurate · high confidence  
**Type:** Check · Pardons, Lobbyists & Access

## The Claim

> Beyond Changpeng Zhao, did Trump grant clemency to Ross Ulbricht, the BitMEX founders, Nikola founder Trevor Milton and others with crypto or donor ties, and did the Justice Department disband its crypto enforcement team?

## Bottom Line

Trump pardoned Ross Ulbricht (Jan 21, 2025), BitMEX's Hayes, Delo, Reed and Dwyer plus the operator HDR Global Trading (Mar 27, 2025), and Nikola founder Trevor Milton (Mar 28, 2025), who with his wife gave more than $1.8M to pro-Trump and allied committees in autumn 2024 (AP; breakdowns vary).

## What Holds Up

- Trump pardoned Ross Ulbricht.
- Trump pardoned the BitMEX co-founders and BitMEX itself.
- Trump pardoned Nikola founder Trevor Milton, a donor.
- Lobbyists were paid for the CZ pardon.
- The DOJ disbanded its crypto enforcement unit (NCET).

## What Needs Correcting

- Roger Ver received clemency via a settlement or deferred prosecution (partly accurate)
- Reportedly, others got clemency too (partly accurate)
- Roger Ver got a DOJ deferred prosecution agreement and paid about $50M. He received no presidential clemency.
- Trevor Milton's case was EV securities fraud, not a crypto case.
- The BitMEX pardon covered four individuals (Hayes, Delo, Reed, Dwyer) and the entity HDR Global Trading.
- The $450K CZ lobbying fee went to Ches McDowell's Checkmate. Goody Guillén's firm got about $290K.
- Milton's 'more than $1.8M' is AP's total; one breakdown has ~$920K to the Trump 47 Committee (Oct 2024) and ~$750K to the RFK Jr.-linked MAHA Alliance (Sept 2024), so not all of it went to Trump committees in October.

## What Is Unproven

- Implied: donations or lobbying bought these grants.

## In More Detail

- The DOJ disbanded the NCET on Apr 7, 2025, and Binance paid lobbyists including Ches McDowell ($450K) and Teresa Goody Guillén (about $290K) for the CZ pardon.
- Roger Ver got a DOJ deferred prosecution deal (about $50M paid), not clemency.
- No evidence shows that any grant was bought.

## Full Report

**Verdict: mostly accurate (confidence: high on the grants and dates, low on any link between money and clemency)**

### The claim
This check builds on the Changpeng Zhao pardon (C09) and the SEC dropping its crypto cases (C23). The claim says Trump also granted clemency to other crypto or donor-linked figures:
- Ross Ulbricht
- the BitMEX co-founders, and BitMEX itself
- Trevor Milton of Nikola, a donor
- Roger Ver, through a settlement or deferred prosecution
- "reportedly others"

It adds that the DOJ disbanded its National Cryptocurrency Enforcement Team (NCET). It asks for the dates, any donations or lobbying payments, and the official justifications.

Note: Many mainstream outlets (NYT, WSJ, Politico, Reuters) could not be read directly. The findings below rely on search results that agree across several outlets. Where only secondary coverage was available, that is noted.

### Evidence by sub-claim

1. **Ross Ulbricht (Silk Road) was pardoned. Confirmed.**
   - Trump granted a "full and unconditional" pardon on Jan 21, 2025, his second day in office, and announced it on Truth Social.
   - Ulbricht had served more than 11 years of a life sentence without parole.
   - Justification: Trump had promised the pardon at the Libertarian National Convention in May 2024. His post thanked the libertarian movement for its support and called the prosecutors part of the "weaponization of government" against him.
   - No donation or lobbying payment linked to this pardon was found. The link is a campaign pledge to a voting bloc.

2. **The BitMEX co-founders and BitMEX itself were pardoned. Confirmed.**
   - Pardons were signed on Mar 27, 2025 and reported on Mar 28 for:
     - Arthur Hayes
     - Benjamin Delo
     - Samuel Reed
     - Gregory Dwyer, an early employee. First reports named only three men.
     - HDR Global Trading, the corporate operator of BitMEX. BitMEX confirmed this in a statement to Reuters.
   - All had pleaded guilty in 2022 to Bank Secrecy Act violations, meaning they failed to keep an anti-money-laundering program.
   - The individuals had received probation or home detention, not prison.
   - HDR had been sentenced in January 2025 to a $100M fine.
   - No official justification was published. Delo said the DOJ had "wrongfully targeted" BitMEX.
   - No donation or lobbying payment linked to these pardons was found in this search.
   - Whether the pardon cancelled or refunded the $100M corporate fine is **unverified**. One crypto news flash says it was "waived".

3. **Trevor Milton (Nikola), a donor, was pardoned. Confirmed.**
   - The White House confirmed the pardon on Mar 28, 2025.
   - Milton had been convicted in 2022 of securities and wire fraud and sentenced in December 2023 to four years. He was free on appeal.
   - Donations: AP, citing FEC records, says Milton and his wife gave more than $1.8M less than a month before the election. Another breakdown of the records has about $920K to the Trump 47 Committee in October 2024 and $750K to the RFK Jr.-linked MAHA Alliance in September, so not all of it went to Trump committees in October (audit qualification). Outlets differ on whether the money went to the campaign or to a pro-Trump committee.
   - Trump said the pardon was "highly recommended by many people". He also said the case against Milton was that "he was one of the first people that supported" Trump. In effect, he called the prosecution political retaliation.
   - Effects of the pardon:
     - It wiped out the restitution prosecutors were seeking, about $660-680M, before any amount was ordered.
     - The SEC later dropped its separate civil case without giving a reason, about six months after the pardon (Bloomberg Law).
     - Milton's SEC lawyer was Brad Bondi, brother of the Attorney General at the time.
   - Milton is not a crypto figure. He belongs on this list as "donor-linked", not "crypto".

4. **Roger Ver got clemency through a settlement or deferred prosecution. Partly accurate. This was a DOJ plea deal, not a presidential act of clemency.**
   - Ver was indicted in 2024 on tax evasion and mail fraud charges over his 2014 renunciation of US citizenship.
   - He entered a deferred prosecution agreement, announced Oct 14, 2025. He paid about $48-50M in back taxes, penalties and interest, and admitted the facts of failing to report his bitcoin holdings. The indictment is to be dismissed once he meets the terms.
   - Lobbying: Ver paid Roger Stone $600,000 in early 2025. Lobbying disclosures describe the work as lobbying on "ending the exit tax" and crypto tax reform. Stone said he never lobbied the president or the executive branch on Ver's case, and Ver dropped him in late 2025 or early 2026 (Florida Bulldog).
   - Per the NYT (via secondary coverage), Ver also hired lawyers tied to Trump's legal circle.
   - Ver has not been pardoned. "Clemency" overstates what happened.

5. **Lobbyists were paid for the CZ pardon. Confirmed, based on disclosures reported by Politico and the WSJ through secondary coverage.**
   - Ches McDowell's Checkmate Government Relations was paid $450,000 for about a month's work on "executive relief", lobbying the White House and Treasury in late September 2025.
   - Teresa Goody Guillén (BakerHostetler) was paid about $290,000 by Binance and Zhao in 2025. She also represented World Liberty Financial.
   - McDowell is a friend of Donald Trump Jr. He told the WSJ that he raised Zhao's case with the president, and that Trump Jr. had left the room and did not help.
   - Goody Guillén said Zhao was "pardoned for justice", not because of any business deal.
   - The pardon itself was announced on Oct 23, 2025. See C09 for the White House justification, which cited no fraud victims and Biden overreach.

6. **The DOJ disbanded its crypto enforcement unit (NCET). Confirmed.**
   - Deputy Attorney General Todd Blanche's memo of Apr 7, 2025 ("Ending Regulation by Prosecution") disbanded the NCET, created in 2022, "effective immediately".
   - The memo stated that "The Department of Justice is not a digital assets regulator."
   - It told prosecutors to stop bringing regulatory-style cases against exchanges, mixers and wallets, and to focus on fraud against investors, terrorism and cartels.
   - It cited Trump's January 2025 executive order on digital assets.
   - The Market Integrity and Major Frauds Unit was also told to stop crypto enforcement work.

7. **"Reportedly others". Partly confirmed: other donor-linked pardons exist, while other crypto requests are pending or were rejected.**
   - **Paul Walczak** was pardoned on Apr 23, 2025. He was a nursing-home executive convicted of tax crimes. His mother, the Republican donor Elizabeth Fago, had attended a $1M-per-person MAGA Inc. dinner at Mar-a-Lago weeks earlier (NYT). Whether she actually paid is unclear. The pardon erased about $4.4M in restitution.
   - **Keonne Rodriguez** (Samourai Wallet) was sentenced to 5 years in November 2025. In December 2025, Trump said "I'll look at it." No pardon was found as of this search.
   - **Sam Bankman-Fried** filed a formal pardon application in June 2026. Trump told the NYT in January 2026 that he did not plan to pardon him, and the White House said the odds were slim.
   - No other crypto pardon in 2026 was found.

### Corrections
- Roger Ver received a DOJ deferred prosecution agreement, not presidential clemency, and he paid about $50M. Calling it clemency is inaccurate.
- Trevor Milton is an EV fraud case, not a crypto case. His donor link, more than $1.8M in October 2024, is documented.
- The BitMEX pardon covered four people (Hayes, Delo, Reed and Dwyer) plus the corporate entity HDR Global Trading.
- The $450,000 lobbying fee went to Ches McDowell's Checkmate, not to Teresa Goody Guillén, whose firm received about $290,000.

### Denials and context
- White House: these pardons correct "weaponization" and overreach by the Biden DOJ. Trump said Milton was targeted for supporting him, and that he had promised the Ulbricht pardon.
- No official written justification was found for the BitMEX pardons.
- Stone denies lobbying the executive branch on Ver's case. McDowell denies that Trump Jr. helped. Goody Guillén denies any business link to the CZ pardon.
- The BitMEX defendants and the HDR entity had already been sentenced, and the individuals faced no prison time. The pardons mostly cleared their records.
- The DOJ memo is policy, not a pardon. Fraud cases continued, and Tornado Cash developer Roman Storm was still tried in 2025.
- No evidence was found that any of these grants was bought. Money is documented only in the Milton donations, the Walczak dinner, the CZ lobbying and the Ver-Stone payment. Causation remains insinuation.
