# C76: Russia-linked money around the family and envoys

**Verdict:** Partly accurate · medium confidence  
**Origin:** Connection hunt

## Bottom line

The Russia-adjacent links are real but mostly indirect. Several are documented.

## What holds up

- Steve Witkoff's real-estate business is in active partnership with Len Blavatnik's Access Industries while Witkoff negotiates with Russia.
- A Putin-linked Russian oligarch, Umar Kremlev, paid for parts of Donald Trump Jr.'s May 2026 Bahamas wedding celebrations.
- Kirill Dmitriev (RDIF, sanctioned 2022) runs a direct channel to Witkoff and Kushner and pitched US-Russia business deals as part of the peace process ('make money not war').
- The Dmitriev-Kushner channel predates the current talks (2016-17 transition).
- Gentry Beach, a friend of Donald Trump Jr., struck an LNG deal with sanctioned Russian gas producer Novatek for an Alaska project (mostly accurate)
- Kushner's Cadre took money from Yuri Milner, whose fund had channelled Russian state money.

## What needs correcting

- The sale of sanctioned Lukoil's ~$20B foreign assets entered the Witkoff/Kushner talks, with a bidding consortium that includes Gulf partners of Witkoff and Kushner (partly accurate)
- The Trump Organization has had buyers from Russia (partly accurate)
- Kimberly Guilfoyle (ambassador to Greece) pay-to-play allegation belongs in the Russia-linked cluster (misleading)
- **Claim:** Brief lists Guilfoyle under Russia-linked ties  
  **Record:** The WSJ allegation concerns a US donor (Eric Deters) and no Russia connection was found. She resigned on 13 Oct 2026 at the White House's request.
- **Brief said:** The oligarch funded Don Jr.'s 'wedding parties'  
  **Record shows:** The family's account is two after-parties, while ProPublica reports an island rental and fireworks across the weekend. Kremlev says he was repaid (6 Oct 2026).
- Byline Times calls Blavatnik a former adviser to Dmitriev. The pre-2022 RDIF advisers named in coverage we could see were Leon Black and Stephen Schwarzman; Blavatnik's role is unconfirmed.
- Reports of Russian buyers of Trump-branded property are 2017-18 analyses of older sales, mostly in licensed buildings; none found for 2025-26.

## What is unproven

- Blavatnik was a former adviser to Kirill Dmitriev / RDIF, putting Witkoff's financing 'in the orbit' of Dmitriev.
- World Liberty Financial sold tokens to Russia-linked buyers.

## In more detail

- A Putin-linked oligarch, Umar Kremlev, paid for parts of Don Jr.'s wedding celebrations through a Dubai IBA entity; the family confirmed this and later repaid him.
- Witkoff's family firm is in active partnership with Len Blavatnik's Access Industries.
- Dmitriev runs a direct business-flavoured channel with Witkoff and Kushner, which dates back to a 2016-17 plan routed through Kushner.
- A Trump Jr. friend struck an LNG deal with sanctioned Novatek.
- Other links are single-source or unverified: that Blavatnik advised Dmitriev, that the Lukoil sale entered the talks via Putin, and that WLF sold to Russia-linked wallets.
- The Guilfoyle pay-to-play allegation is documented, but it has no Russia link.
- No source shows a personal financial stake for Witkoff or Kushner in a Russia deal.

## Sub-claims

### 1. Steve Witkoff's real-estate business is in active partnership with Len Blavatnik's Access Industries while Witkoff negotiates with Russia.

**Confirmed**

- Documented (trade press, multiple deals): Witkoff Group and Access Industries bought HFZ's XI condo project out of a ~$900M Dec 2021 foreclosure and renamed it One High Line (The Real Deal, 2021-12-23).
- They refinanced it with a $1.15B JPMorgan/Tyko loan (Aug 2024) and landed a further $525M loan (Jan 2026).
- They also co-own Banyan Cay (West Palm Beach) and announced a North Beach, Miami condo project with Sandor Scher (Feb 2025).
- The 'undisclosed conflict' framing is single-source commentary: Counteroffensive (Apr 2025), Popular Information, and Byline Times (1 Dec 2025).
- They note the partnership continued after Ukraine sanctioned Blavatnik in Dec 2023, on grounds Ukraine never published.
- Witkoff sold a $120M stake in his firm in Sep 2025.
- His son Alex still runs operations, so the family's exposure to the partnership continues; that last point is our INFERENCE.
- Access Industries says Blavatnik and Witkoff 'have never spoken about the Russia-Ukraine negotiations'.
- Blavatnik says he exited Russia in 2022.
- The tie shows a commercial relationship, not influence on policy.
- Amounts: ~$900M; $1.15B; $525M; $120M
- Dates: Dec 2021; 2021-12-23; Aug 2024; Jan 2026; Feb 2025
- Sources: [4](https://therealdeal.com/new-york/2021/12/23/witkoff-and-access-industries-buy-hfzs-languishing-xi-condo-project) [5](https://therealdeal.com/new-york/2024/08/16/witkoff-blavatnik-refinance-one-high-line-condo-project) [6](https://therealdeal.com/new-york/2026/01/30/one-high-line-developers-land-525m-loan) [3](https://www.counteroffensive.news/cp/161746319) [2](https://popular.info/p/top-trump-negotiator-retains-partnership) [1](https://bylinetimes.com/2025/12/01/trump-envoy-has-financial-ties-with-former-adviser-to-putins-money-man-now-leading-kremlin-peace-talks/) [7](https://therealdeal.com/national/2025/09/16/steve-witkoff-sells-stake-in-real-estate-firm-for-120m)

### 2. Blavatnik was a former adviser to Kirill Dmitriev / RDIF, putting Witkoff's financing 'in the orbit' of Dmitriev.

### 3. A Putin-linked Russian oligarch, Umar Kremlev, paid for parts of Donald Trump Jr.'s May 2026 Bahamas wedding celebrations.

**Confirmed**

- Documented (ProPublica, 14 Sep 2026, and confirmed by the family): Kremlev, president of the International Boxing Association (IBA), spent 'hundreds of thousands of dollars'.
- This included renting one of the two private islands used and a fireworks show.
- The money came through IB Challenger, a Dubai entity affiliated with the IBA.
- Trump Jr.'s spokesperson did not dispute it and called Kremlev 'a personal friend of Don' but 'not someone he has a business relationship with'.
- Bettina Anderson says he paid for two after-parties, not the wedding itself.
- The family called it a generous gift and pledged to repay; on 6 Oct 2026 Kremlev said he had been repaid (AP).
- The IOC has previously criticised the IBA for depending on Gazprom sponsorship.
- Kyiv Post could not identify IB Challenger's own funding sources.
- Response: Rep. Robert Garcia (Oversight ranking Democrat) sent records requests on 15 Sep 2026.
- These are voluntary in the minority.
- Sen. John Curtis (R-UT) asked the Senate Judiciary Committee to investigate.
- AG Todd Blanche asked 'What is there to investigate'.
- Trump said he had 'no idea who Umar is'.
- We found no report that Kremlev sought or received any US government benefit.
- Dates: 14 Sep 2026; 6 Oct 2026; 15 Sep 2026
- Sources: [8](https://www.propublica.org/article/donald-trump-jr-wedding-bankrolled-russian-oligarch-umar-kremlev-putin) [9](https://www.propublica.org/article/donald-trump-jr-wedding-umar-kremlev-payments-investigation) [10](https://www.axios.com/2026/09/14/umar-kremlev-donald-trump-jr-wedding-bahamas) [11](https://www.axios.com/2026/09/15/donald-trump-jr-wedding-investigation-kremlev) [12](https://meduza.io/en/news/2026/09/14/russian-oligarch-umar-kremlev-spent-hundreds-of-thousands-of-dollars-to-help-fund-donald-trump-jr-s-bahamas-wedding) [13](https://meduza.io/en/news/2026/09/16/the-u-s-attorney-general-was-asked-whether-he-d-investigate-russian-boxing-chief-umar-kremlev-s-reported-financing-of-trump-jr-s-wedding-festivities-his-response-what-is-there-to-investigate) [14](https://www.adn.com/nation-world/2026/09/22/gop-senator-calls-for-probe-of-trump-jr-after-wedding-party-bankrolled-by-russian-oligarch/) [15](https://www.wcax.com/2026/10/06/russian-oligarch-says-donald-trump-jr-has-paid-him-back-wedding-celebration-expenses/) [16](https://www.kyivpost.com/post/88580) [17](https://www.flashscore.pt/noticias/iba-too-slow-with-reforms-and-too-dependent-on-russia-s-gazprom-for-funds-according-to-ioc/lEZDblNO/)

### 4. Kirill Dmitriev (RDIF, sanctioned 2022) runs a direct channel to Witkoff and Kushner and pitched US-Russia business deals as part of the peace process ('make money not war').

**Confirmed** · Evidence: Documented

- Dmitriev visited Washington on 2-3 Apr 2025 at Witkoff's invitation.
- CNN and Reuters, citing anonymous sources, reported a temporary sanctions waiver for the visa.
- He then held three days of talks with Witkoff and Kushner in Miami (24-26 Oct 2025, Axios), which produced the 28-point plan of Nov 2025.
- Axios published its text.
- Under the plan, $100B of frozen Russian assets would go into US-led Ukraine reconstruction, with the US taking 50% of the profit.
- The remaining frozen funds would go into a joint US-Russian investment vehicle.
- Further meetings came in Jan/Feb 2026 (Miami) and in Mar 2026, the latter on energy cooperation (Moscow Times/Interfax).
- The WSJ's 29 Nov 2025 'Make Money Not War' (Hinshaw, Faucon, Ballhaus, Grove, Parkinson) reports Dmitriev pitching joint deals in rare earths, Arctic energy and shipping.
- The WSJ quotes Witkoff hoping Russia, Ukraine and the US become 'business partners'.
- The White House says it took input from both sides.
- Note: the business deals are state-to-state or corporate proposals.
- No source shows a personal stake for Witkoff or Kushner in them.
- Amounts: $100B; 50%
- Dates: 3 Apr 2025; 26 Oct 2025; Nov 2025; Feb 2026; Mar 2026
- Sources: [18](https://euromaidanpress.com/2025/04/03/putin-envoy-dmitriev-trump-envoy-witkoff-met-in-washington/) [19](https://themoscowtimes.com/2025/04/04/russian-envoy-seeks-cooperation-with-us-at-landmark-washington-talks-a88601) [20](https://euromaidanpress.com/2025/11/19/trump-team-meets-top-putin-envoy-for-three-days-in-miami-drafts-secret-ukraine-peace-deal-axios-says) [21](https://www.axios.com/2025/11/20/trump-ukraine-peace-plan-28-points-russia) [22](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/trump-administration-proposed-28-point-russia-ukraine-peace-plan/) [23](https://www.tovima.com/wsj/make-money-not-war-trumps-real-plan-for-peace-in-ukraine/) [24](https://www.pravda.com.ua/eng/news/2025/11/29/8009576) [25](https://www.themoscowtimes.com/2026/03/12/putin-envoy-kirill-dmitriev-meets-with-witkoff-and-kushner-to-discuss-russian-energy-a92208/pdf)

### 5. The Dmitriev-Kushner channel predates the current talks (2016-17 transition).

**Confirmed**

- Documented (Mueller report, Vol. I): in late Nov 2016 the UAE national security adviser (Tahnoon bin Zayed) introduced Dmitriev to Rick Gerson, a hedge-fund friend of Kushner.
- Gerson and Dmitriev drafted a two-page US-Russia 'reconciliation plan', which Dmitriev implied Putin had cleared.
- Dmitriev sent it on 16 Jan 2017, and Gerson passed it to Kushner, who shared it with Bannon and Tillerson.
- Kushner told the special counsel no one followed up.
- Separately, Dmitriev met Erik Prince in the Seychelles in Jan 2017, a meeting George Nader brokered.
- This shows that the same UAE-Dmitriev-Kushner triangle seen in C06/C15 existed nine years earlier.
- It does not show any money flow.
- Dates: late Nov 2016; 16 Jan 2017; Jan 2017
- Sources: [26](https://www.themoscowtimes.com/2019/04/22/the-russia-trump-links-in-muellers-report-explained) [27](https://www.bloombergquint.com/politics/kushner-friend-worked-with-putin-deputy-on-reconciliation-plan)

### 6. The sale of sanctioned Lukoil's ~$20B foreign assets entered the Witkoff/Kushner talks, with a bidding consortium that includes Gulf partners of Witkoff and Kushner.

**Partly accurate**

- Single-source for the core point: the NYT (early Oct 2026, three unnamed people, read via relays because the site was blocked) reports that Putin raised the Lukoil asset sale with Witkoff and Kushner in the Kremlin on 5 Sep 2026.
- The bidding consortium is led by Todd Boehly, who gave $1M-$2M to Trump causes (outlets disagree on the amount).
- Relaying reports name its members as IHC (the Tahnoon-chaired Abu Dhabi group), Al Hail Holding, the Qatar-based Al-Khayyat brothers and the US DFC, which would take a mid-teens stake.
- Reports describe the Al-Khayyats as former partners of Kushner and Ivanka Trump in the southern Albania resort (C45).
- That is single-source in what we saw.
- Carlyle had agreed a deal in Jan 2026 and then withdrew.
- Any sale needs OFAC and Kremlin approval.
- Witkoff's spokesperson: he 'has no conflict of interest and no financial stake in this matter'.
- The NYT found no indication that Witkoff or Kushner would profit personally.
- Amounts: $1M-$2M
- Dates: early Oct 2026; 5 Sep 2026; Jan 2026
- Sources: [28](https://www.pravda.com.ua/eng/news/2026/10/03/8056300/) [29](https://meduza.io/en/news/2026/10/05/lukoil-s-foreign-asset-sale-becomes-part-of-u-s-russia-talks-on-ending-ukraine-war-nyt-reports) [30](https://kyivindependent.com/u-s-peace-negotiations-with-russia-could-now-involve-multi-billion-dollar-lukoil-deal-nyt-reports/) [31](https://www.wionews.com/world/us-russia-talks-lukoil-assets-todd-boehly-trump-investors-1791021292256) [32](https://meduza.io/en/news/2026/09/23/former-chelsea-co-owner-todd-boehly-seeks-to-buy-lukoil-s-foreign-assets)

### 7. Gentry Beach, a friend of Donald Trump Jr., struck an LNG deal with sanctioned Russian gas producer Novatek for an Alaska project.

**Mostly accurate**

- Documented (NYT, Feb 2026; relayed by Moscow Times, ADN): Beach, a Dallas investor, signed an LNG technology agreement with Novatek after the Aug 2025 Alaska summit, for the North Slope 'Polar Train' project.
- Beach said it would use a mobile LNG plant built at Novatek's Murmansk site.
- In Oct 2026 the FT reported (relayed by Ukrainska Pravda) that Novatek was allegedly financing and controlling the ~$25B project and that most of its management left.
- That part is single-source.
- Novatek told the NYT it planned a minority stake.
- Beach describes a social friendship with the Trump family and denies doing business with them.
- No source shows US approval was granted because of the friendship.
- Amounts: ~$25B
- Dates: Feb 2026; Aug 2025; Oct 2026
- Sources: [33](https://www.themoscowtimes.com/2026/02/20/us-investor-linked-to-trump-signed-gas-deal-with-russias-novatek-nyt-a92006) [34](https://www.adn.com/business-economy/energy/2026/03/02/texas-investor-with-trump-family-ties-pursues-project-to-tap-north-slope-gas/) [35](https://www.thebarentsobserver.com/news/us-investor-with-ties-to-trump-family-looks-to-murmansk/445637) [36](https://www.pravda.com.ua/eng/news/2026/10/24/8059533/)

### 8. World Liberty Financial sold tokens to Russia-linked buyers.

**Unverified** · Evidence: Single-source on-chain analysis by Accountable.US (Sep 2025)

- It says WLF sold more than 10,000 WLFI to a wallet ('0x9009...') that had transacted in A7A5, the US-sanctioned ruble-backed stablecoin.

- These letters are documented, but they do not establish the underlying fact.

- Some researchers call the hits false positives.
- We found no government finding.
- Dates: Sep 2025; 18 Nov 2025; 13 May 2026

### 9. The Trump Organization has had buyers from Russia.

**Partly accurate** · Evidence: Documented but historical

- Reuters (2017) counted at least 63 buyers with Russian passports or addresses paying $98.4M in seven Trump-branded south Florida towers.
- A 2018 analysis found 86 all-cash purchases, nearly $109M, by buyers tied to Russia or other former Soviet states at 10 Trump-branded properties.
- Many of these were licensed buildings developed by others such as Gil Dezer.
- The Trump Organization says it does not track buyers' nationality.
- We found no reporting of Russian buyers of Trump properties in 2025-26.
- Amounts: $98.4M; nearly $109M
- Dates: 2025-26
- Sources: [40](https://therealdeal.com/miami/2017/03/19/russians-reportedly-paid-98-4m-for-units-at-trump-branded-condos-in-south-florida) [41](https://therealdeal.com/miami/2018/06/19/russian-buyers-shelled-out-109m-in-cash-for-trump-properties-in-sunny-isles-new-york)

### 10. Kimberly Guilfoyle (ambassador to Greece) pay-to-play allegation belongs in the Russia-linked cluster.

**Misleading**

- The pay-to-play allegation is documented, but it has no Russia link.
- The WSJ (Oct 2026) published messages showing that in July 2025, before her confirmation, Guilfoyle pressed donor Eric Deters to wire $100,000 to her Amex account.
- She also relayed his messages to the IRS and offered to lobby DOJ for him.
- Deters calls it 'pay to play'.
- Her lawyer Jesse Binnall disputes the messages' authenticity.
- Oversight Democrats requested records on 8 Oct 2026.
- The White House asked her to resign, and she resigned on 13 Oct 2026, vowing to 'vigorously defend' herself.
- AP also mentions scrutiny of her ties to Greek energy executives.
- Deters has filed a defamation suit, which is single-source.
- Nothing we found ties Guilfoyle, Deters or the Greek matters to Russia.
- Amounts: $100,000
- Dates: Oct 2026; July 2025; 8 Oct 2026; 13 Oct 2026
- Sources: [43](https://www.tovima.com/wsj/honey-please-kimberly-guilfoyle-pushed-donor-to-wire-100000-to-her-amex-account/) [42](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/politics/exclusive-house-democrats-ask-kimberly-guilfoyle-for-records-related-to-alleged-pay-to-pay-scheme) [44](https://www.opb.org/article/2026/10/14/trump-s-ambassador-to-greece-kimberly-guilfoyle-resigns/) [45](https://www.foreignpolicyjournal.com/2026/10/14/u-s-ambassador-kimberly-guilfoyle-resigns-from-greece-post-amid-misconduct-allegations/) [46](https://www.allsides.com/news/2026-10-17-0700/politics-eric-deters-files-defamation-lawsuit-against-kimberly-guilfoyle)

### 11. Kushner's Cadre took money from Yuri Milner, whose fund had channelled Russian state money.

**Confirmed**

- Documented (Paradise Papers, Nov 2017: ICIJ, Guardian, Bloomberg).
- In 2011 VTB funded a $191M Twitter stake through Milner's DST vehicles, and a Gazprom Investholding-backed vehicle held about $1B of Facebook shares.
- The Milner family trust invested $850,000 in Cadre in 2015.
- Milner says VTB played no role in the Cadre investment, and the DST/VTB stakes were sold by 2014-15.
- This is historical context that shows no current tie.
- Amounts: $191M; about $1B; $850,000
- Dates: Nov 2017; 2014-15
- Sources: [47](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2017-11-04/early-backer-of-facebook-linked-to-russia-bank-kushner-platform) [48](https://m.washingtontimes.com/news/2017/nov/8/jared-kushner-linked-to-man-named-in-paradise-pape/)
