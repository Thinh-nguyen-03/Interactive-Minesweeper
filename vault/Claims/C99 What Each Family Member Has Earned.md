# C99: What Each Family Member Has Earned

**Verdict:** Partly accurate · medium confidence  
**Type:** Check · Family Ventures & Wealth

## The Claim

> What has each member of the Trump family earned or gained since January 2025?

## Bottom Line

Only one family member's 2025 income is itemised in a public filing: Melania Trump.

## What Holds Up

- Melania Trump received about $17.2M of income in 2025 from her film, NFTs and memoir.
- Unnamed Trump family members own 30% of the entities that receive World Liberty and USD1 money.
- Unnamed Trump family members hold 20% of many of the foreign licensing companies that collected fees in 2025.
- Eric Trump's American Bitcoin stake made him a paper billionaire, and has since lost most of its value.
- The Trump sons received Dominari shares and could sell them (mostly accurate)
- The sons hold founder shares in the New America Acquisition I SPAC.
- Donald Trump Jr. is paid in equity for board and advisory roles (GrabAGun, Unusual Machines, PSQ, Kalshi, Polymarket) (mostly accurate)
- Kalshi ended Donald Trump Jr.'s advisory contract in November 2026.
- Jared Kushner's Affinity Partners has collected large management fees from Gulf sovereign funds.
- Kushner became a billionaire on Forbes's estimate (mostly accurate)
- Press net-worth estimates for the sons.

## What Needs Correcting

- Amazon paid about $40M for the Melania film, most of it to the First Lady (partly accurate)
- Barron Trump has earned tens of millions from World Liberty and is worth about $150M (partly accurate)
- Kushner gained from the EA buyout and the Paramount bid (partly accurate)
- **Claimed:** Outlets split on whether the $10.71M film fee is Donald Trump's or Melania Trump's income (C44)  
  **The record shows:** The primary 2025 OGE report lists it in Part 5, 'Spouse's Employment Assets & Income', under MKT World LLC, alongside $6,011,259 of NFT proceeds and $521,161 from the memoir. It is her income reported on his form.
- **Claimed:** DT Marks DEFI is a 'Trump-owned' entity (C42, C04)  
  **The record shows:** The 2024 and 2025 OGE reports list it as 69% DTTM Operations (the President's trust structure), 1% DT Marks Defi Member Corp and 30% 'Trump Family Members'. DT Marks SC has the same split. So part of the World Liberty money accrues to unnamed relatives. CAP's memo describes the split as 70/30.
- **Claimed:** Forbes estimated Eric Trump at about $400M (Dec 2025) (C29)  
  **The record shows:** That figure has been superseded. Forbes put him at about $280M on 28 Apr 2026.
- **Claimed:** Eric's American Bitcoin stake was worth about $160M (Dec 2025) (C29)  
  **The record shows:** That was right for early December (about $166M at the 4 Dec 2025 close, our arithmetic). It fell to about $116M by the 31 Dec 2025 close and, on 68,432,664 pre-split shares after the July 2026 1-for-15 reverse split, to about $23M at the $5.07 close on 13 Nov 2026.
- A circulating claim that Don Jr. 'sold 1,504,322 Dominari shares' misreads the May 2025 S-3 resale table. No sale is documented, which matches C39.
- **Claimed:** The brothers 'own' about 20% of American Bitcoin  
  **The record shows:** That was the combined Trump-linked holders' stake at launch. Only Eric's stake appears in the proxy, and Don Jr.'s is undisclosed.
- 16 Nov 2026 (audit): Claimed (earlier version of this check): Kalshi's ending of Donald Trump Jr.'s advisory contract was unverified. The record shows: Kalshi confirmed a mutual end to the role on 12 Nov 2026, citing his firm's Polymarket investment.
- 16 Nov 2026 (audit): The foreign licensing income on the President's form is not his alone: unnamed family members hold 20% limited-partner interests in twelve licensing LLCs that reported $54.49M of 2025 fees.

## What Is Unproven

- Melania Trump received money from the $MELANIA meme coin.
- Donald Trump Jr. holds an American Bitcoin stake of known size.
- Kushner raised about $5B of new Gulf money in 2025-26.
- Eric Trump earned money from ALT5 Sigma, Metaplanet, the Trump Organization and his book.
- Ivanka Trump, Lara Trump, Tiffany Trump, the Boulos family, Kimberly Guilfoyle and Charles Kushner have documented earnings in the term.

## In More Detail

- The spouse section of Donald Trump's 2025 OGE report (released 30 June 2026) lists $10.71M from the 'Melania' film, $6,011,259 from NFTs and $521,161 from her memoir, all paid through MKT World LLC. For everyone else the record is mostly equity at paper value, set out in SEC filings: Eric Trump's American Bitcoin stake (68.4M pre-split shares, about $23M at the 13 Nov 2026 close, against roughly $600M+ near the September 2025 peak), the sons' Dominari, SPAC founder, GrabAGun, Unusual Machines and Kalshi shares, and press net-worth estimates (Forbes: Eric about $280M in April 2026, Don Jr. about $300M in December 2025 after about $500M in September 2025, Barron about $150M in October 2025, Kushner above $1B in September 2025).
- The same OGE report shows that 30% of DT Marks DEFI and DT Marks SC, the entities receiving the World Liberty and USD1 money, belongs to unnamed 'Trump Family Members', but no filing says how much any son or Barron received.
- Jared Kushner's Affinity Partners was scheduled, per a Senate Finance Committee letter, to collect $90M more in management fees from foreign investors between September 2024 and August 2026; actual receipts and his personal take are not public.
- The same report shows unnamed family members holding 20% limited-partner interests in many of the foreign licensing companies, which reported $54.5M of 2025 fees; no split by person is given.
- Salaries, board fees and advisory pay for the sons are almost entirely undisclosed.
- Kalshi ended Donald Trump Jr.'s advisory role by mutual agreement on 12 Nov 2026, citing his firm's investment in Polymarket.

## Sub-Claims

### 1. Melania Trump received about $17.2M of income in 2025 from her film, NFTs and memoir.

**Confirmed** · Evidence: Documented (primary document read)

- Part 5 ('Spouse's Employment Assets & Income') of Donald Trump's 2025 OGE Form 278e lists MKT World LLC (Palm Beach), described as set up 'to enter into various agreements, including but not limited to appearance and speaking engagements and the sale of NFTs and other collectables, to receive net proceeds from the book "Melania", and the film "Melania"'.
- It reports net proceeds of $10,710,000 from a licence agreement with Designers Manager for the film, $6,011,259 from NFT and collectible sales, and $521,161 from the Skyhorse memoir, $17,242,420 in total.
- The same section shows MBWT LLC (Ljubljana real estate) with capital gains of $100,001-$1,000,000.
- No speaking income is itemised for 2025.
- For comparison, the 2024 report lists $216,710.74 in NFT licence fees under MKT World.
- These are net proceeds as reported on the form; they are not profit after tax.
- Amounts: $10,710,000; $6,011,259; $521,161; $17,242,420; $100,001-$1,000,000

### 4. Unnamed Trump family members own 30% of the entities that receive World Liberty and USD1 money.

**Confirmed** · Evidence: Documented (primary document read)

- In both the 2024 and 2025 OGE reports, DT Marks Defi LLC (line 124 of the 2025 report) and DT Marks SC LLC (line 114) are owned 'DT Marks Defi/SC Member Corp: 1%, DTTM Operations LLC 69%, & Trump Family Members 30%'.
- DTTM Operations sits under the President's revocable trust.

- The form does not name the family members or split the 30%.
- Whether these income amounts are entity-level totals or the President's share is not stated on the form.
- Inferred (our arithmetic, asserted by no source): if they are entity-level, 30% is about $237M; if they are the President's 70% share (CAP's reading of the equity line), the family members' 30% would be about $338M. Neither figure is a documented payment to any named person.
- Amounts: 1%; 69%; 30%; $65,625,000; $526,810,321

## Full Report

**Verdict: partly accurate** (confidence: medium)

The question asks what each relative has earned or gained since the term began on 20 January 2025. The documented answer is narrower than the headlines suggest:

- **Cash or reported income is itemised for one person only: Melania Trump.** The spouse section of Donald Trump's 2025 OGE report lists about $17.2M of net proceeds (film $10.71M, NFTs $6.01M, memoir $0.52M).
- **For the sons, the record is mostly equity at paper value.** It comes from SEC filings (American Bitcoin, Dominari, the SPAC, GrabAGun, Unusual Machines) and from press estimates. Their salaries, board fees, Trump Organization distributions and 1789 Capital economics are not public, because they file no disclosures.
- **A family slice of the World Liberty money exists but is not attributed to anyone.** The President's own report shows that 30% of DT Marks DEFI and DT Marks SC, the entities that receive the World Liberty and USD1 money, belongs to unnamed "Trump Family Members". No filing says who they are or what each received.
- **Kushner's fees are documented as a schedule, not as receipts.** Affinity Partners was due $90M more in foreign management fees from September 2024 to August 2026, per the Senate Finance Committee. What was actually paid, and what reached Kushner personally, is not public.
- **Unnamed family members also hold 20% of many foreign licensing companies.** Twelve licensing LLCs owned 79% by the President's structure and 20% by "Trump Family Members" reported $54.49M of 2025 fees. No split by person is given.
- **The other relatives' in-term money is barely documented.** For Ivanka, Lara and Tiffany Trump, the Boulos family, Charles Kushner and Kimberly Guilfoyle, we found little or no documented income during the term.

**Rules used here.** Paper value is never added to cash. Net-worth estimates are not income. Money received during the presidency is not presented as money earned because of it.
