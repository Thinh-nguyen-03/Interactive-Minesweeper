# C89: Unannounced Trump Organization Deals in Hungary & Serbia

**Verdict:** Partly accurate · medium confidence  
**Type:** Connection check · Foreign Deals

## The Claim

> Do corporate filings point to Trump Organization deals in Hungary and Serbia that have not been announced, and is any partner or fee known?

## Bottom Line

Forbes's July 2025 reading of corporate filings did point to unannounced Serbia and Hungary deals, and CREW's August 2026 FOI requests name DT Marks Budapest LLC and DT Marks Budapest Member Corp. But no partner, fee or project has been disclosed for either country, the Serbian entity's name was not found, and the 2025 disclosure (as summarised by PolitiFact and CNBC) shows no Hungary or Serbia income.

## What Holds Up

- Forbes: corporate filings point to unannounced Trump deals in Serbia and Hungary
- DT Marks Budapest LLC and DT Marks Budapest Member Corp. exist
- Grand Budapest dropped before any Trump deal surfaced
- Don Jr. visited Vučić and Budapest in spring 2025 to expand family business ties
- US decisions favourable to Orbán's Hungary in the same period (mostly accurate)
- NIS resolution routes through Hungary's MOL with possible ADNOC minority

## What Needs Correcting

- Eagle Hills (Alabbar) links the Belgrade and Budapest projects; Kushner floated for Grand Budapest (partly accurate)
- Serbia: Trump licence for the Belgrade project, entity and fee (partly accurate)
- Serbia: US policy (NIS sanctions, tariffs) relative to the Belgrade project (misleading)
- The claim calls Hungary and Serbia 'deals'; the filings show entities, not signed deals with known partners or fees.
- The 'financial shield'/currency swap was claimed by Orbán and denied by Trump in Dec 2025; it is not an established US decision.
- The Russian-energy exemption was for one year per the White House, not open-ended as Orbán claimed.
- The NIS relief came after the Belgrade project collapsed and is tied to a sale to MOL; the waiver was refused in Oct 2025 while the project was alive.

## What Is Unproven

- Partner and fee for the Hungary entity
- Complete list of DT Marks/DTTM entities formed since Nov 2024 with country, partner and fee

## In More Detail

- The richest new link is Eagle Hills (Mohamed Alabbar), the Gulf partner on both Kushner's Trump-branded Belgrade project and Orbán's dropped EUR 12.3B Grand Budapest plan.
- Hungary won a run of US sanctions decisions in 2025, but no source ties them to the family's business.
- Serbia's US relief came only after the Belgrade project collapsed.

## Denials & Context

- White House: the president's dealings are unrelated to the Trump Organization; no conflicts of interest.
- Trump Organization: it only licenses its brand to private developers; its 2025 ethics policy bars only deals directly with foreign governments.
- Eric Trump: the Belgrade hotel contract would be with the developer, not the Serbian state.
- Don Jr.'s spokesperson rejected conflict-of-interest suggestions over his Serbia visit.
- Affinity and Eagle Hills did not answer VSquare; a source said Affinity was not involved in Grand Budapest.
- Orbán was the administration's closest EU ally, which gives an ideological explanation for the Hungary decisions.
  - The Rogán designation was a last-days Biden action, and Hungary and Slovakia also hold EU pipeline-oil carve-outs.
- forbes.com, citizensforethics.org, congress.gov, govinfo.gov and the Delaware register could not be read directly; findings rest on search snippets from several outlets.

### Corrections

- The claim calls Hungary and Serbia "deals". The filings show **entities**, not signed deals with known partners or fees. Budapest's anchor project was dropped in early 2025. Belgrade collapsed in Dec 2025.
- The "currency-swap/financial shield" was claimed by Orbán and **denied by Trump** (Dec 2025). It is not an established US decision.
- The Russian-energy exemption is a one-year exemption per the White House, not the open-ended exemption Orbán claimed.
- The NIS relief came **after** the Belgrade project died, and is tied to a sale to MOL. In Oct 2025, while the project was alive, the waiver was refused.

### Denials and context

- The White House says the president's dealings are unrelated to the Trump Organization, and that neither he nor his family has conflicts of interest. The Trump Organization says it only licenses its brand to private developers. Its 2025 ethics policy bars only deals directly with foreign governments.
- Eric Trump: the Belgrade hotel contract would be with the developer, not the Serbian state.
- Don Jr.'s spokesperson rejected conflict-of-interest claims over his Serbia visit.
- Affinity Partners and Eagle Hills did not answer VSquare about Budapest. A source said Affinity was not involved.
- There are other explanations for the Hungary decisions. Orbán was the administration's closest ideological ally in the EU. Energy exemptions for landlocked pipeline-dependent states also have EU parallels, since Hungary and Slovakia hold EU carve-outs. And the Rogán delisting followed a Biden-era designation made days before he left office.
