# C02: The $MELANIA coin and Melania Trump's NFT income

**Verdict:** Mostly accurate · medium confidence  
**Type:** Check · Crypto ventures

## The claim

> Did Melania Trump launch her own meme coin right after $TRUMP, did she report about $6 million from the coin and NFTs, and has the coin lost about 99% of its value?

## Bottom line

Melania Trump did launch $MELANIA on Jan 19, 2025, about two days after $TRUMP, and the token is down roughly 99% from its $13+ peak, which is confirmed.

## What holds up

- Melania launched her own meme coin right after $TRUMP, before the inauguration.
- $MELANIA is down ~99%, worse than $TRUMP (~95%).

## What needs correcting

- Melania reported ~$6 million from her coin and NFTs (partly accurate)
- **Claimed:** Melania 'reported $6 million on her coin and NFTs'  
  **The record shows:** The disclosure lists $6,011,259 from NFTs/digital collectibles (licensing via Designers Manager) and no meme-coin proceeds.
- The figure appears as spouse income on Donald Trump's 2025 OGE 278e (released 2026-06-30), not a report filed by Melania herself.

## What is unproven

- Molly White tracked the shell companies behind Melania's coin/NFT income.

## In more detail

- The '$6 million' figure is real ($6,011,259 on the President's 2025 disclosure).
- That income was listed as NFT/digital-collectible sales under a licensing deal, not as meme-coin proceeds.
- It was also reported as spouse income on Donald Trump's filing, not in a report of her own.

## Full report

**Verdict: mostly-accurate (confidence: medium-high)**

### The claim
- "Suddenly Melania launches a meme coin" (right after $TRUMP, in the days before the inauguration).
- "When Melania reported $6 million on her coin and NFTs, Molly [White] tracked those shell companies."
- "Melania has been down the worst at around ninety nine percent of its value lost."

### Sub-claims and evidence

#### 1. Melania launched her own meme coin right after $TRUMP — CONFIRMED
$MELANIA, a Solana token, launched on Sunday, January 19, 2025, the evening before the inauguration. Melania Trump announced it on X ("You can buy $MELANIA now"). That was about two days after $TRUMP, which went live late on January 17, 2025, with some outlets dating it January 18. Contemporaneous reporting said $TRUMP fell sharply, by roughly 38-48% within hours, once $MELANIA debuted (The Block, Nairametrics). The coin is issued through MKT World LLC, a Florida company tied to the First Lady, according to the token's terms and to DL News.

#### 2. "Melania reported $6 million on her coin and NFTs" — PARTLY ACCURATE / imprecise
- The figure is real. The President's 2025 annual financial disclosure (OGE 278e) was released on June 30, 2026, and lists Melania's income as a spouse. It shows **$6,011,259** from the sale of NFTs and other digital collectibles, through a license agreement with "Designers Manager". Fortune reported this was about 28 times her 2024 NFT income (Fortune, Voz, Newsweek, American Progress memo).
- Correction: the disclosure line is for **NFTs and collectibles**. It does not list $MELANIA meme-coin proceeds. The Center for American Progress assigns this income to "$MELANIA coin and related digital ventures", but that is the group's own attribution, not a line item in the filing. "Her coin and NFTs" therefore overstates what was disclosed.
- She did not file her own report. The figure appears as spouse income on Donald Trump's disclosure.
- Her total disclosed 2025 income was about $17.2M. Most of it, about $10.7M net, came from the Amazon "Melania" documentary. Her memoir added about $0.5M.
- "Shell companies": the entities involved are MKT World LLC, the Florida company behind the coin and her NFT and speaking income, and the licensing counterparty. I found reporting on these entities (DL News) and Molly White's general tracking of Trump crypto ventures (Citation Needed, Democracy Now). I did **not** find a specific Molly White piece on Melania's entities, so that attribution is unverified. Calling them "shell companies" is a characterization, not a documented finding.

#### 3. $MELANIA down ~99% — CONFIRMED
The token peaked at about $13.05-$13.73 on January 19-20, 2025. It hit an all-time low of about $0.066 on June 6, 2026 (CoinGecko, crypto.news). In late October 2026 it traded around $0.08-0.087 (CoinCheckup), which is about 99.4% below the peak (CryptoSlate snapshot: 99.41%). Earlier milestones on the way down:
- March 2025: about 95% down (Benzinga).
- Later in 2025: about 98% down (CoinMarketCap).

The statement that $MELANIA has done worse than $TRUMP (about 92-95% down) is consistent with trackers and Whale Alert ("92–99%").

### Other side / context
- No statement from Melania Trump's office or the White House on the disclosure or the coin's collapse was found. A White House spokesperson did not respond to Fortune on the related lawsuit.
- A 2025 investor class action alleged that Meteora co-founder Benjamin Chow and Hayden Davis (Kelsier) ran a pump-and-dump scheme on MELANIA and other tokens. Insiders allegedly extracted about $10M from MELANIA. Melania Trump was **not** a defendant. The amended complaint described the public figures as "props".
- A New York federal judge later dismissed the LIBRA/M3M3 claims and refused to add MELANIA to the case, per Solana Compass. I have not confirmed this ruling on the docket. Chow and Davis have denied wrongdoing in related matters.
- Meme coins are lawful products, and the SEC staff said in February 2025 that meme coins are generally not securities. The losses are documented. Wrongdoing by Melania Trump is not alleged in any court.
