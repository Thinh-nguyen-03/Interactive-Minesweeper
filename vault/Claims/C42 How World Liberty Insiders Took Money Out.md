# C42: How World Liberty Insiders Took Money Out

**Verdict:** Mostly accurate · medium confidence  
**Type:** Check · Crypto Ventures

## The Claim

> How much have the Trump and Witkoff families received from World Liberty Financial through the 75% revenue entitlement, token sales and borrowing against tokens?

## Bottom Line

The documented record supports most of the claim. DT Marks DEFI (70% Trump's companies, 30% unnamed family members) is entitled to 75% of WLF's net revenue, including token-sale proceeds.

## What Holds Up

- DT Marks DEFI LLC is entitled to 75% of WLF's net token-sale and protocol revenue.
- Trump disclosed income from WLF.
- Steve Witkoff received about $107M of 2025 income from the holding company that owns his WLF interest.
- WLF insiders borrowed stablecoins against about 5B WLFI tokens as the price hit lows (mostly accurate)
- WLF froze the tokens of Justin Sun and other holders.
- WLF promoted the $SPSC memecoin.

## What Needs Correcting

- WLFI price history and retail losses (partly accurate)
- The claim says 'WLF insiders' borrowed. The reporting shows WLF's own treasury or multisig wallets borrowed on Dolomite, and no personal borrowing by Trump or Witkoff family members is documented. Loan size: about $75M per CoinDesk; the $150M and $261M estimates are unverified.
- '75% of net revenue' is the Gold Paper revenue entitlement. DT Marks DEFI's equity stake in WLF Holdco was 75% at the end of 2024 and 38.25% at the end of 2025, according to Trump's OGE filings.
- Witkoff's $106,938,819 came from Witkoff Holdings LLC, a broad holding company that owns his WLF interest among other businesses; the filing does not show how much came from WLF itself.
- Entity id note: C04 uses dt-marks-defi-llc and C13 uses dt-marks-defi for the same company. This file uses dt-marks-defi-llc.
- The 2025 WLF income figure was given as a contested $594M-$800M. The filing resolves it (2026-11-16): $592.4M on DT Marks DEFI's lines and $789.3M including DT Marks SC's $196.9M from Stablecoin Holdco; the form's exact line amounts are given, not ranges.
- 2026-11-16 (disclosure audit, C96/C97): Claimed: Trump 'disclosed about $57M from WLF' for 2024; the stake was 'about 60%, then 40%, then 38.25%'; the 22.5B-token stake was 'valued at about $1.3B per the 2025 filing'. The record shows: the $57,355,532 is World Liberty Financial, Inc.'s own revenue line, not a payment to Trump; the forms give only 75% (end-2024) and 38.25% (end-2025); the form lists 15.75B WLFI under DTTM Operations valued 'Over $50,000,000', and $1.3B is CAP's market valuation of 22.5B tokens in July 2026.
- 2026-11-17 (holdings audit, C104/C105): Claimed: about $107M of 2025 income 'from a WLF-affiliated holding company', up from '$34M in 2024'. The record shows: the payer is Witkoff Holdings LLC (it owns WC Digital Fi, WC Digital SC and real-estate, golf and hotel businesses); the form splits the $106,938,819 into $68,875,000 cash and $38,063,819 cryptocurrency and attributes none of it to WLF; the $34,363,535 covers January 2024 to August 2025.

## What Is Unproven

- Witkoff's divestment from WLF is disputed.
- Who owns StringZ Holding.

## In More Detail

- Trump's filings list $57.4M of 2024 token sales on World Liberty Financial, Inc.'s own line (company revenue, not a payment to him) and, for 2025, $592.4M on DT Marks DEFI's lines ($526.8M of token proceeds and $65.6M from a WLF Holdco equity sale), or $789.3M including $196.9M to DT Marks SC from Stablecoin Holdco.
- Steve Witkoff reported $106.9M of 2025 distributions from Witkoff Holdings LLC, a broad holding company that owns his WLF interest among other businesses (the form does not say how much came from WLF), while the White House says he has divested, though no buyer or certificate has been shown.
- WLF borrowed about $75M of stablecoins on Dolomite against about 5B WLFI as the token hit record lows.
- Sun's wallet and 271 others were frozen, WLF promoted the $SPSC memecoin, and WLFI fell about 80-85% from its $0.33 peak.
- Three points need correction or remain open: the borrower was WLF's own treasury, not individual insiders; no source puts a dollar figure on retail losses; and StringZ Holding's reported Tahnoon backing rests on a single WSJ report citing anonymous sources, which the OCC record does not confirm.

## Sub-Claims

### 1. DT Marks DEFI LLC is entitled to 75% of WLF's net token-sale and protocol revenue.

**Confirmed**

- The October 2024 Gold Paper gives DT Marks DEFI 22.5B WLFI (Trump's OGE filings list 15.75B, 70% of that, under DTTM Operations LLC) and 75% of 'net protocol revenues'.
- These cover revenue from any source, including token-sale proceeds and fees, after a $30M reserve and agreed expenses.
- The other 25% goes to Axiom Management Group, owned by Herro and Folkman.
- On the $550M raised, Decrypt estimated about $390M for Trump-side entities, though one analyst disputes that reading.
- This entitlement is separate from DT Marks DEFI's equity in WLF Holdco, which Trump's filings give as 75% at the end of 2024 and 38.25% at the end of 2025; WLF's own statements gave about 60% and 40% in between.
- Amounts: 70%; 75%; $30M; 25%; $550M
- Dates: October 2024
- Sources: [1](https://www.nbcnews.com/tech/crypto/trump-family-gets-75-crypto-coin-revenue-no-liability-new-document-rev-rcna176088) [2](https://benzinga.com/markets/cryptocurrency/24/10/41395007/trump-affiliated-entity-will-get-75-of-world-liberty-financials-revenue-and-22-of-wlfi-tok) [3](https://decrypt.co/310250/trump-390-million-payday-world-liberty-token-sale) [5](https://www.americanprogress.org/wp-content/uploads/sites/2/2026/05/2026-05-05-Memo-on-Understanding-Trumps-Crypto-Wealth-Google-Docs.pdf)

### 2. Trump disclosed income from WLF.

**Confirmed**

- Trump's 2024 OGE filing (released June 2025) lists $57,355,532 of token sales on the line of World Liberty Financial, Inc. itself (line 391; Bloomberg Tax reported $57.7M).
- That is the company's revenue (100% owned by WLF Holdco, then 75% by DT Marks DEFI), not a distribution to Trump; DT Marks DEFI's own 2024 line shows no income.
- The 2025 filing (released June 30, 2026) resolves the outlets' disagreement: DT Marks DEFI's lines show $526,810,321 of token-sale proceeds distributed by WLF (lines 124.3-124.12: $236,250,000 in cash, the figure The Block itemized, and the rest in nine crypto wallets) and $65,625,000 from selling WLF Holdco equity, $592.4M in all (Bloomberg and NOTUS's 'about $594M' adds staking rewards); adding $196,875,000 to DT Marks SC from Stablecoin Holdco gives $789.3M (Reuters and the NYT's 'almost $800M').
- DT Marks DEFI and DT Marks SC are 70% owned through Trump's companies (DTTM Operations 69% plus a 1% member corporation) and 30% by unnamed 'Trump Family Members'; the form does not say whether the amounts on these lines are the entities' totals or Trump's share.
- The form lists 15.75B WLFI under DTTM Operations, valued only 'Over $50,000,000'; the 'about $1.3B' for 22.5B tokens is CAP's July 2026 valuation at about $0.059, not a disclosed figure.
- These are revenue figures, not profit.
- Amounts: $57,355,532; $57.7M; 100%; 75%; $526,810,321
- Dates: June 2025; June 30, 2026; July 2026
- Sources: [6](https://www.ctvnews.ca/world/article/trump-pocketed-over-57-million-from-crypto-coin-sales/) [7](https://news.bloombergtax.com/crypto/trump-earned-57-7-million-from-crypto-venture-disclosure-shows) [4](https://unchainedcrypto.com/trumps-2025-financial-disclosure-shows-over-1-billion-in-crypto-income-as-bitcoin-slid-50/) [8](https://www.citationneeded.news/trumps-crypto-disclosure/) [39](https://news.bgov.com/crypto/witkoff-reports-107-million-from-entity-that-held-crypto-stake) [40](https://www.notus.org/trump-white-house/trump-annual-financial-disclosure-crypto-earnings) [41](https://www.theblock.co/news/regulation/2026-06-30-president-trump-financial-disclosures-hundreds-millions-crypto-related-income-406782)

### 3. Steve Witkoff received about $107M of 2025 income from the holding company that owns his WLF interest.

**Confirmed**

- His annual OGE report (signed 29 Jul 2026, covering calendar 2025; published on whitehouse.gov and reported by ABC in September 2026) shows $106,938,819 of distributions from Witkoff Holdings LLC. That is a broad holding company: besides WC Digital Fi LLC (WLF) and WC Digital SC LLC (SC Financial) it owns Banyan Cay, Ocean Terrace and golf and hotel operating companies.
- An endnote splits the sum into $68,875,000 in cash and $38,063,819 in cryptocurrency.
- The filing does not show how much came from WLF itself and, as published, carries no reviewer certification.
- The comparison figure of $34,363,535 is from his new-entrant report and covers January 2024 to August 2025, not 2024 alone.
- His 2025 new-entrant report listed WC Digital Fi LLC, which WLF's documents describe as an affiliate of Witkoff and his family.
- Amounts: $106,938,819; $68,875,000; $38,063,819; $34,363,535
- Dates: 29 Jul 2026; September 2026; January 2024; August 2025
- Sources: [9](https://www.goodmorningamerica.com/news/story/steve-witkoff-earned-107m-trump-affiliated-crypto-venture-136307300) [10](https://yellow.com/news/witkoff-107m-world-liberty-financial) [11](https://ground.news/daily-briefing/ba32a445-8595-4aa0-867f-7d43da64fc50) [42](https://www.whitehouse.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Witkoff-Steven-2026-Annual.pdf)

### 5. WLF insiders borrowed stablecoins against about 5B WLFI tokens as the price hit lows.

**Mostly accurate**

- WLF-controlled treasury or multisig wallets, not individual insiders, pledged about 5B WLFI on Dolomite and borrowed about $75M (about 65.4M USD1 plus 10.3M USDC).
- More than $40M was sent to Coinbase Prime, and the USD1 pool was drained to about 93-100% utilization.
- Dolomite's co-founder Corey Caplan is a WLF adviser.
- WLFI fell 12-19% to then-record lows of about $0.077-0.080, erasing about $427M of market value.
- WLF called the concern 'FUD' and said it repaid $25M by April 11, leaving about $50M. The larger $150M and $261M estimates are unverified.
- In August 2026 CryptoSlate reported a 112.6M USD1 position near liquidation.
- Amounts: about $75M; More than $40M; about 93-100%; 12-19%; about $0.077-0.080
- Dates: April 11; August 2026
- Sources: [14](https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2026/04/09/trump-s-world-liberty-financial-borrows-usd75-million-against-its-own-token-trapping-depositors-on-dolomite) [15](https://unchainedcrypto.com/world-liberty-financial-borrows-75-million-against-its-own-token-on-a-protocol-its-advisor-co-founded-unchained/) [16](https://www.theblock.co/news/defi/2026-04-10-world-liberty-rebuts-dolomite-criticism-396967) [17](https://decrypt.co/363928/trump-linked-wlfi-erases-427-million-token-unlock-proposal-defi-loan) [18](https://fortune.com/2026/04/10/trump-world-liberty-financial-crypto-tokens-insider-loans/) [19](https://beincrypto.com/world-liberty-financial-repays-25-million-loan) [20](https://cryptoslate.com/trump-linked-world-liberty-financial-wins-occ-bank-approval-as-112-million-defi-position-sits-near-liquidation/)

### 6. WLF froze the tokens of Justin Sun and other holders.

**Confirmed**

- WLF blacklisted Sun's address around Sept 4-5, 2025, after about $9M of WLFI moved, freezing about 540-600M unlocked tokens plus a reported 2.4B locked tokens.
- WLF said 272 wallets were frozen in total, mostly over phishing.
- In November 2025 it burned and reallocated about 166.7M WLFI to holders who passed new KYC checks.
- Sun was still frozen in December 2025, with his stake down more than $60M. A vesting vote in May 2026 locked 17B early-supporter tokens for two years.
- Sun said he could not vote because his tokens were frozen.
- Amounts: about $9M; more than $60M
- Dates: Sept 4-5, 2025; November 2025; December 2025; May 2026

## Denials & Context

- WLF says the freezes protect users from phishing, calls concerns about the Dolomite loan 'FUD' because the position is overcollateralized, and calls Sun's allegations baseless.
- The White House (Anna Kelly, David Warrington) says Witkoff has fully divested and recuses from matters affecting his interests.
- The OCC says career staff reviewed the WLF trust charter, and says the major shareholders, including StringZ, signed passivity commitments.
- 'Extraction' is a characterization.
  - The money flows are documented, but no regulator or court has found wrongdoing, and the SEC has opened no probe (see C13).
- Source limits: Fortune, CoinDesk and the ABC affiliate pages could not be read directly, and the WSJ is paywalled.
  - Findings rely on search-result text cross-checked across outlets.

## Full Report

**Verdict: mostly accurate** (confidence: medium)

The claim bundles eight threads that build on C04, C05, C13, C14 and C29. Most hold up on the documented record: the 75% revenue entitlement, Trump's disclosed WLF income, Witkoff's roughly $107M of 2025 income, the Dolomite borrowing against about 5B WLFI, the token freezes, the $SPSC promotion and the WLFI price collapse. Three parts need correction or stay open:

- **The borrowing.** It was done by World Liberty's own treasury or multisig wallets, not by individual insiders borrowing personally. "Insider loans" is a headline phrase, and the loan size is disputed.
- **Retail losses.** No source quantifies WLFI retail losses in dollars.
- **StringZ Holding.** Its ownership rests on one Wall Street Journal report citing anonymous sources. The OCC record names StringZ but not who owns it.

**Source limits.** Fortune, CoinDesk and the ABC affiliate pages could not be read directly. The WSJ is paywalled. Findings rest on search-result text from several outlets, cross-checked against each other where possible.

### What was checked
This check asks how money flowed out of World Liberty Financial (WLF) to the Trump and Witkoff families and to insiders, and what happened to the token and its outside holders. 

### Sub-claims

#### 1. DT Marks DEFI LLC is entitled to 75% of WLF net token-sale and protocol revenue: confirmed, with later ownership changes
- WLF's October 2024 "Gold Paper" gives DT Marks DEFI LLC 22.5B WLFI tokens and 75% of "net protocol revenues". These are defined as revenue "from any source", including token-sale proceeds and platform fees, after a $30M initial reserve and agreed expenses. The remaining 25% goes to Axiom Management Group, owned by co-founders Chase Herro and Zak Folkman. [1][2]
- After the $550M token-sale total in March 2025, Decrypt estimated about $390M for Trump-side entities. Reuters-based reporting put the figure at about $400M, and Bitcoin.com at $413M. One crypto analyst disputed that reading of the Gold Paper. [3]
- The ownership of the operating company is a separate question from the revenue entitlement. Trump's OGE filings give DT Marks DEFI's stake in WLF Holdco as 75% at the end of 2024 and 38.25% at the end of 2025; WLF's own statements gave about 60% and 40% in between; the drop to 38.25% followed the Aryam 49% deal covered in C14. A May 2026 Center for American Progress memo notes that WLF's own statements on these figures are inconsistent. [4][5]

#### 2. Trump's disclosed WLF income: confirmed
- **2024 OGE filing (released June 2025):** $57,355,532 of token sales on the line of World Liberty Financial, Inc. itself (line 391). That is company revenue, not a distribution to Trump; DT Marks DEFI's own 2024 line shows no income. Bloomberg Tax reported $57.7M. [6][7]
- **2025 filing (released June 30, 2026):** the filing resolves the outlets' disagreement. DT Marks DEFI's lines show $526,810,321 of token proceeds distributed by WLF (The Block's $236.25M is only the cash line) and $65,625,000 from the WLF Holdco equity sale, $592.4M in all ("about $594M" with staking). Adding $196,875,000 to DT Marks SC from Stablecoin Holdco gives $789.3M (Reuters and the NYT's "almost $800M"). Both entities are about 70% Trump's and 30% unnamed family members'. The form does not say whether the amounts are entity totals or Trump's share. *(Corrected 16 Nov 2026; see the audit addendum.)* The form lists 15.75B WLFI under DTTM Operations, valued only "Over $50,000,000". The "about $1.3B" for 22.5B tokens is CAP's July 2026 market valuation, not a disclosed figure. These are revenue figures, not profit. C29 covers the full totals. [4][8]

#### 3. Steve Witkoff's about $107M of 2025 income and the disputed divestment: confirmed for the income, unresolved for the divestment
- ABC News reported in September 2026 that Witkoff's annual OGE report shows nearly $107M of income [9][10][11]. The report was signed on 29 July 2026 and covers calendar 2025.
- **What the form shows (corrected 17 Nov 2026).**
  - The payer is Witkoff Holdings LLC, which paid $106,938,819 of distributions: $68,875,000 in cash and $38,063,819 in cryptocurrency, per the form's endnote.
  - Witkoff Holdings is a broad holding company. Besides WC Digital Fi LLC (WLF) and WC Digital SC LLC (SC Financial), it owns real-estate, golf and hotel businesses.
  - The comparison figure of $34,363,535 comes from his new-entrant report and covers January 2024 to August 2025, not 2024 alone.
- The filing does not show how much of that money came from WLF itself. As published, it carries no reviewer certification. [10][11]
- His August 2025 new-entrant report listed WC Digital Fi LLC, which WLF's documents describe as "an affiliate of Steve Witkoff and certain of his family members". It also listed WC Digital SC LLC and SC Financial Technologies LLC. [11]
- **Divestment timeline:**
  - May 2025: WLF said he was "in the process" of divesting.
  - September 2025: Bloomberg reported he still held WLF assets. [12]
  - October 2025: eight Senate Democrats, led by Sen. Schiff, wrote that his disclosure still showed WLF crypto. [13]
  - February 2026: White House counsel said he "has also divested".
  - September 2026: White House spokeswoman Anna Kelly said he had "fully divested".
- No divestiture certificate and no buyer has been identified publicly. One outlet mentions a $120M sale of a stake in his real-estate firm, also with no buyer named; that comes from a single source. [9][10]

#### 4. WLF "insiders" borrowed stablecoins against about 5B WLFI as the price hit lows: mostly accurate, but the borrower was WLF itself
- On April 9, 2026, CoinDesk reported that WLF-controlled wallets pledged about 5B WLFI on Dolomite and borrowed about $75M in stablecoins. That was roughly 65.4M USD1 plus 10.3M USDC. More than $40M was sent to Coinbase Prime. The borrowing drained Dolomite's USD1 pool to about 93-100% utilization, which left other depositors unable to withdraw. [14][15][16]
- Dolomite co-founder Corey Caplan is a WLF adviser and has been described as its CTO. [14][16]
- WLFI fell to then-record lows of about $0.077-0.080 (down 12-19%), erasing about $427M in market value. [17][18]
- WLF called the concerns "FUD". It said the position was "nowhere near liquidation" and that it would "simply supply more collateral" if needed. It said it repaid $15M on April 9 and $10M on April 11, which left about $50M outstanding. [16][17][19]
- Other estimates are larger: about $261M (Sandmark) and $150M USDC against $406M of collateral (Arkham). These may count different wallets or dates and are **unverified**. [15][17]
- CryptoSlate later reported a WLF multisig position of at least 112.6M USD1 with a health factor of about 1.07, close to liquidation. That rests on a single source. [20]
- **Correction:** Fortune's headline says "insider loans", but the reporting describes WLF treasury or multisig wallets. I found no evidence that individual Trump or Witkoff family members borrowed personally.

#### 5. Freezing of Justin Sun's and other holders' tokens: confirmed
- Around September 4-5, 2025, WLF blacklisted Sun's address after about $9M of WLFI moved, as flagged by Arkham and Nansen. The freeze covered about 540-600M unlocked WLFI plus a reported 2.4B locked tokens. [21][22][23]
- Sun said "I am innocent" and called the freeze unreasonable. [24]
- WLF said it froze 272 wallets in total "to protect users". By its account, about 215 were linked to phishing and about 50 had reported being compromised. [25][26]
- In November 2025 WLF burned and reallocated about 166.7M WLFI (about $22M) from compromised wallets, but only to users who passed renewed KYC. [26]
- As of December 2025 (Bubblemaps), Sun was still blacklisted and his frozen stake had lost more than $60M in value. [27]
- WLF's April 2026 vesting proposal covered about 62.3B tokens. It burns up to 4.5B (10%) of the 45.2B founder and partner tokens, and puts the 17B early-supporter tokens on a two-year cliff followed by two-year vesting. Holders who decline stay locked indefinitely.
- The proposal passed in May 2026 with more than 99% approval on about 6.6B votes. Critics called the vote rigged. Sun said he could not vote because his tokens were frozen. The lawsuits are covered in C05. [28][29][30]

#### 6. The $SPSC memecoin promotion: confirmed
- On November 24, 2025, WLF said it would invest in $SPSC, a Solana memecoin named after Chase Herro's "sh*t in a can … piss … human skin" rant and paired mainly against USD1. The token briefly rose 130-143%, to a market cap of about $6.8M. Arkham data showed WLF had not yet bought any. [31][32]
- I found no reliable figure for a later SPSC crash. Any claim that WLF "pumped and dumped" it is **unverified**.

#### 7. WLFI price history and retail losses: price confirmed, losses partly substantiated
- Presale prices were $0.015, then $0.05. Trading opened on September 1, 2025, and the all-time high was about $0.33 that day. [33]
- The price fell about 54% over the next five months. It hit record lows of about $0.077 in April 2026 and about $0.05 in early May 2026. [17][33][34]
- Prices from May to October 2026 sit around $0.05-0.06, roughly 80-85% below the peak. I found no reliable dated price for November 2026.
- Anyone who bought on the open market after launch is deep underwater. Most early-supporter tokens are still locked.
- No source gives a dollar figure for WLFI retail losses, so "retail losses" is **not quantified**. Investors in ALT5 (C13) lost about 90%.

#### 8. Who owns StringZ Holding: partly substantiated
- The OCC's August 14, 2026 conditional approval for World Liberty Trust Company lists StringZ Holding RSC (DE) LLC as an investor in WLTC Holdings, with passivity commitments. It does not name the beneficial owner or the size of the stake (see C12). [35][36]
- The WSJ (August 27, 2026, citing people familiar with the matter) reported that a group linked to Sheikh Tahnoon bin Zayed is behind StringZ, which holds 49% of WLTC Holdings. It also reported that a Trump-family entity holds about 38%. [36][37][38]
- I found no primary document confirming Tahnoon's ownership or StringZ's links to Aryam Investment 1. The ownership stays **unverified** in the primary record.

### Corrections
- "Insiders borrowing" should read "WLF's own treasury or multisig wallets borrowed". No personal borrowing by insiders is documented.
- "75% of net revenue" describes the revenue entitlement in the Gold Paper. DT Marks DEFI's equity stake in WLF Holdco was 75% at the end of 2024 and 38.25% at the end of 2025, according to Trump's OGE filings.
- Witkoff's $106.9M came from Witkoff Holdings LLC, a broad holding company that owns his WLF interest among other businesses, not necessarily from WLF itself. The filing splits it only into cash ($68.9M) and crypto ($38.1M).

### Other side
- WLF says the freezes protect users from phishing, that the Dolomite loan is overcollateralized, and that Sun's allegations are baseless.
- The White House says Witkoff has fully divested and recuses from matters affecting his interests.
- The OCC says career staff reviewed the charter.
- Critics include Senate Democrats (Schiff, Warren, Slotkin), Bubblemaps and Molly White. Their concern is that money flowed to the families while token holders were locked up or diluted. The documents show the money flows, but "extraction" is a characterization; no regulator has found wrongdoing.
