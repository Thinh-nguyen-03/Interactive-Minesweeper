# C75: Congo minerals diplomacy: Boulos, Gentry Beach and the firms that benefited

**Verdict:** Partly accurate · medium confidence  
**Origin:** Connection hunt

## Bottom line

The brief's main pieces are documented.

## What holds up

- Massad Boulos (Tiffany Trump's father-in-law, Senior Advisor for Africa) led the US minerals-for-security diplomacy that produced the June 27, 2025 DRC-Rwanda peace agreement and the December 4, 2025 Washington Accords.
- Gentry Beach, a longtime friend of Donald Trump Jr. and former Trump 2016 finance co-chair, led a consortium with Mercuria seeking the rights to the Rubaya coltan mine.
- Beach traded on his Don Jr. relationship abroad (DRC, Pakistan), and Trump Jr. distanced himself with a cease-and-desist letter (mostly accurate)
- Virtus Minerals, the US firm that won Chemaf under the US-DRC pact with strong administration backing, overstated its mining experience (Reuters).
- Nigeria's UBA prepared financing for a DRC minerals bid.
- KoBold Metals (Gates/Bezos-backed) got the Manono lithium deposit with administration help, crediting Rubio and Boulos.
- Orion's US-government-backed consortium (DFC + Abu Dhabi's ADQ) moved on a 40% stake in Glencore's DRC mines, a deal snagged on sanctioned billionaire Dan Gertler's royalties.
- Erik Prince's Vectus Global has a security/tax deal with the DRC.
- Kinshasa paid Trump-linked lobbyists while courting a minerals deal (mostly accurate)

## What needs correcting

- The brief pairs Beach with Chemaf. In the record, the Chemaf bidders were Virtus/Orion (the winner), Buenassa (UBA-financed), Gecamines and earlier Norinco. Beach was associated with Rubaya.
- **Claim:** The brief lists UBA's financing among firms that benefited  
  **Record:** It was a drafted $50M facility for Buenassa, a losing rival to the US-backed Virtus, with no reported close.
- Beach's company is reported as America First Global in the Congo coverage and as White-Bridge Global in the Pakistan coverage.
- The 'Washington Accords' were signed on 4 Dec 2025. The June 2025 document was the initial DRC-Rwanda peace agreement.

## What is unproven

- Beach sought Chemaf.
- Boulos or the Trump family holds a financial interest in any DRC asset awarded under the framework.

## In more detail

- Massad Boulos fronted the US minerals-for-security diplomacy that produced the June 27, 2025 DRC-Rwanda peace agreement and the December 4, 2025 Washington Accords.
- Don Jr.'s friend Gentry Beach (America First Global) led a consortium with Mercuria that sought the M23-held Rubaya coltan mine, and Beach toured Central Africa seeking concessions until Don Jr.'s lawyer sent him a cease-and-desist (WSJ).
- The assets that actually changed hands under the US framework went to firms with no documented Trump-family tie: Virtus Minerals (Chemaf; Reuters found it overstated its mining experience), KoBold Metals (Manono) and the DFC/ADQ-backed Orion consortium (Glencore stake MOU).
- Beach is not documented as a bidder for Chemaf; that bid was Virtus/Orion, and UBA's $50M facility was for a rival bidder, Buenassa.
- Erik Prince's Vectus works for Kinshasa on taxes and security, but State says it has no DRC contract with him.
- No source documents a Boulos or Trump-family financial stake in any DRC asset.

## Connections searched for

## Sub-claims

### 1. Massad Boulos (Tiffany Trump's father-in-law, Senior Advisor for Africa) led the US minerals-for-security diplomacy that produced the June 27, 2025 DRC-Rwanda peace agreement and the December 4, 2025 Washington Accords.

**Confirmed** · Evidence: Documented (multiple outlets; established in C65/C66)

- Rwanda and the DRC signed a US-brokered peace agreement in Washington in late June 2025 (BusinessDay/Reuters, 29 Jun 2025).
- On 4 Dec 2025 Trump hosted the signing of the Washington Accords, a set of five documents: a peace agreement, a regional integration framework, a US-DRC Strategic Partnership Agreement, a US-Rwanda economic framework and a US-DRC security MoU (Fox News, Signal SCV/AP, AllAfrica).
- Under the strategic partnership, the DRC names assets for a 'Strategic Asset Reserve'.
- Qualifying projects must guarantee control by a US person or entity and send offtake to the US market (Egmont Institute analysis).
- Africa Confidential called Boulos and Beach 'prime movers' in the business-infused talks (single-source characterisation).
- Dates: late June 2025; 29 Jun 2025; 4 Dec 2025
- Sources: [43](https://www.businessday.co.za/bd/world/2025-06-29-rwanda-congo-sign-peace-deal-in-us-to-end-fighting-attract-investment/) [44](https://www.foxnews.com/politics/trump-hosts-washington-accords-rwanda-congo-peace-deal-signing-vows-us-rare-earths-purchases.amp) [45](https://signalscv.com/2025/12/trump-presides-over-peace-signing-between-democratic-republic-of-congo-and-rwanda/) [46](https://egmontinstitute.be/the-washington-agreements-peace-for-business-is-not-enough/) [8](https://www.africa-confidential.com/article/id/15443/washington-tries-a-new-push-in-kinshasa)

### 2. Gentry Beach, a longtime friend of Donald Trump Jr. and former Trump 2016 finance co-chair, led a consortium with Mercuria seeking the rights to the Rubaya coltan mine.

**Confirmed**

- Documented (Reuters, relayed by Mining.com, Miningmx and Northern Miner, June-July 2025; also FT and Africa Confidential).
- Beach chairs America First Global.
- He confirmed his interest to Reuters but gave no details.
- Reuters reported his group proposed taking a majority stake, with Kinshasa keeping about 30%.
- Swiss trader Mercuria would market the output, and output would flow legally via Rwanda to a proposed Kigali smelter, with investment possibly over $500M. Rubaya is held by AFC/M23.
- The UN estimates the rebels take at least $800,000 a month in coltan taxes there.
- In February 2026 Kinshasa put Rubaya on the shortlist of assets offered to the US (Reuters, 19 Feb 2026), and a State Department official said US firms were interested 'contingent on' the peace process.
- We found NO report of a signed Rubaya deal through Nov 2026.
- Africa.com relays sources saying the Trump family has no direct involvement.
- Beach previously ran mining ventures in Congo from 2004 (Reuters).
- Amounts: about 30%; over $500M; at least $800,000
- Dates: July 2025; February 2026; 19 Feb 2026; Nov 2026
- Sources: [1](https://www.mining.com/trump%E2%80%91ally-eyes-congo-mine-vital-to-global-tech/) [2](https://www.miningmx.com/news/markets/61591-us-investor-targets-congo-mine-for-peace-deal/) [3](https://www.mining.com/web/mercuria-sizes-up-congo-coltan-mine-as-trump-pursues-peace-deal) [4](https://www.engineeringnews.co.za/article/congo-offers-tantalum-deposit-under-m23-control-to-us-in-minerals-pact-document-shows-2026-02-19) [5](https://www.mining.com/web/us-companies-eyeing-congo-mining-assets-state-dept-official-says/) [6](https://africa.com/top-10-news/trump-ally-eyes-congos-conflict-mine-in-us-backed-peace-deal) [7](https://www.africa-confidential.com/profile/id/5365/gentry-thomas-beach)

### 3. Beach traded on his Don Jr. relationship abroad (DRC, Pakistan), and Trump Jr. distanced himself with a cease-and-desist letter.

**Mostly accurate**

- Single-source for the letter (WSJ, about Oct 2025, relayed by the Daily Beast, Raw Story and Ground News; we could not fetch the original).
- The WSJ reported that Beach toured Central Africa seeking mining concessions under a self-styled 'economic diplomacy' campaign he credited to President Trump, and that he showed senior Congolese officials what they described as text messages with Trump Jr. Per the same report, Trump Jr.'s lawyer sent a cease-and-desist letter in August 2025 telling Beach to stop presenting himself as Don Jr.'s business partner, and Pakistani officials asked Don Jr. whether Beach spoke for the administration; Don Jr. said he was not doing business with Beach.
- Beach denies ever claiming a business partnership and denies receiving the letter, though the WSJ says it reviewed texts in which he confirmed receipt.
- Documented separately: Beach led an investor delegation (White-Bridge Global) to Islamabad on 30 Jan 2025, pitching billions for minerals, real estate and energy (Arab News, Dawn).
- Also documented (AP, Dec 2018): Trump Jr. and Beach had business ties, and the Trump Organization called the relationship 'strictly personal'.
- The NZ Herald reports Beach met Tshisekedi in Kinshasa and separately met US Ambassador Mike Hammer.
- Dates: Oct 2025; August 2025; 30 Jan 2025; Dec 2018
- Sources: [9](https://www.thedailybeast.com/donald-trump-jr-clashes-with-rogue-buddy-gentry-beach-for-playing-diplomat/) [10](https://www.rawstory.com/trump-jr-destructive-information-friend/) [11](https://ground.news/article/he-drops-trump-jrs-name-in-pursuit-of-billion-dollar-deals-its-getting-awkward) [12](https://www.arabnews.pk/node/2588150/pakistan) [13](https://www.dawn.com/news/1888588/new-us-admin-has-nothing-but-respect-for-pakistan-says-trump-aide) [14](https://komonews.com/news/nation-world/trump-jr-partners-with-donor-who-pitched-govt) [15](https://nzherald.co.nz/world/on-the-banks-of-the-congo-river-a-5-star-hotel-for-cobalt-fortune-hunters/KBTJJBU3OPDCNSGNRF3D74YKTE)

### 5. Virtus Minerals, the US firm that won Chemaf under the US-DRC pact with strong administration backing, overstated its mining experience (Reuters).

**Confirmed**

- Documented (Reuters exclusive, 21 Apr 2026, relayed by Kitco, Mining.com, Yahoo and Table.Media).
- Virtus is a Delaware company founded by US special-forces and intelligence veterans.
- Its website claimed a Congo track record from operating a copper-cobalt processing plant.
- Reuters found the plant was idle and that Virtus never acquired it.
- State said it 'fully supports' Virtus.
- The chain of events: Orion and Virtus bid in July 2025, and a February 2026 MOU had Orion financing about $600M of debt, with $200M more from Virtus and Lloyds (Mongabay).
- On 23-24 Feb 2026 Tshisekedi replaced Gecamines' chair and CEO, and three Reuters sources said their opposition to the Virtus bid was part of the reason; Reuters said it was unclear whether the reshuffle was linked to the US talks.
- On 1 Apr 2026 Under Secretary Jacob Helberg announced Kinshasa's approval: about $30M for the equity plus assumed debt (owed partly to Trafigura) and a $750M capital commitment.
- Africa Intelligence (single-source) describes an 'aggressive pressure campaign' by Trump officials for Virtus.
- We found NO Trump-family, Boulos or Beach tie to Virtus or its owners.
- The benefit is to a US firm the administration picked, not a documented family associate.
- Amounts: about $600M; $200M; about $30M; $750M
- Dates: 21 Apr 2026; July 2025; February 2026; 24 Feb 2026; 1 Apr 2026
- Sources: [16](https://www.kitco.com/news/off-the-wire/2026-04-22/us-firm-key-congo-minerals-deal-overstated-its-mining-experience) [17](https://finance.yahoo.com/sectors/energy/articles/exclusive-us-firm-key-congo-075755712.html) [18](https://table.media/en/africa/news-en/dr-congo-virtus-minerals-accused-of-misrepresenting-its-mining-experience) [20](https://news.mongabay.com/2026/02/us-firm-virtus-minerals-closes-in-on-deal-for-crucial-drc-copper-and-cobalt-mines/) [21](https://www.kitco.com/news/off-the-wire/2026-02-24/sacked-congo-state-miner-chiefs-opposed-virtus-chemaf-takeover) [22](https://www.engineeringnews.co.za/article/us-firm-virtus-minerals-buys-congolese-cobalt-producer-chemaf-2026-04-01) [23](https://africasacountry.com/2026/05/the-new-scramble-for-congo)

- There is no report it closed, and Virtus won.
- No source ties UBA, Tony Elumelu or Buenassa to Boulos or his Nigerian business (SCOA/Fadoul).

- Amounts: $50M

### 7. KoBold Metals (Gates/Bezos-backed) got the Manono lithium deposit with administration help, crediting Rubio and Boulos.

**Confirmed** · Evidence: Documented

- Dates: 7 May 2025; 12 Feb 2026; 21 Jan 2026; April 2026

### 8. Orion's US-government-backed consortium (DFC + Abu Dhabi's ADQ) moved on a 40% stake in Glencore's DRC mines, a deal snagged on sanctioned billionaire Dan Gertler's royalties.

**Confirmed** · Evidence: Documented

- Orion CMC was launched in Oct 2025 with $600M each from DFC and ADQ ($1.8B total).

- Amounts: $600M; $1.8B; 40%; about $9B; 49%
- Dates: Oct 2025; 3 Feb 2026; Nov 2026; Jan 2026
- Sources: [28](https://www.aap.com.au/aapreleases/cision20260203ae77602) [29](https://www.businessday.co.za/companies/2026-02-03-glencore-to-sell-40-of-drc-assets-to-white-house-backed-firm/) [30](https://www.arabianbusiness.com/industries/banking-finance/abu-dhabis-adq-to-partner-us-government-in-1-8bn-critical-minerals-project) [31](https://news.metal.com/en/newscontent/103827661-Dan-Gertlers-royalty-claims-are-holding-up-a-9-billion-Western-bid-for-Glencores-copper-and-cobalt-mines-in-Congo) [32](https://www.mining.com/web/us-says-billionaire-gertlers-royalties-must-go-to-congo-for-sanctions-deal/) [34](https://www.adq.ae/newsroom/his-highness-sheikh-tahnoon-bin-zayed-commends-adqs-contributions-to-building-global-business-platforms)

### 9. Erik Prince's Vectus Global has a security/tax deal with the DRC.

**Confirmed** · Evidence: Documented (Reuters Apr 2025; UN Group of Experts via AFP and Courthouse News; GI-TOC)

- The UN experts say a five-year contract was signed in Dec 2024 through companies partly based in the UAE, and that it shifted toward security: drone support around Uvira, and planning to retake Uvira in Feb 2026.

- State says it has no DRC contracts with Prince.
- Prince and Vectus did not respond to AFP or the NYT.
- There is no documented tie to Boulos's diplomacy beyond working for the same client government in the same conflict (inferred overlap).
- Amounts: $100-200M
- Dates: Apr 2025; Dec 2024; Feb 2026; September 2026; Oct 2026

### 11. Boulos or the Trump family holds a financial interest in any DRC asset awarded under the framework.

**Unverified**

- NOT FOUND (consistent with C65/C66).
- Of the assets that moved (Chemaf to Virtus, Manono to KoBold, the Glencore stake MOU to Orion CMC), none has a documented Boulos-family or Trump-family stake.

## Denials and context

- Beach denies claiming a business partnership with the Trump family and denies receiving the cease-and-desist letter.
  - Trump Jr. told inquirers he does not do business with Beach (WSJ).
- Sources told Africa.com the Trump family has no direct involvement in the Rubaya project.
- State 'fully supports' Virtus.
  - Gecamines and Congo's mines ministry did not respond to Reuters on Virtus's credentials.
- State says it has no DRC contracts with Erik Prince.
  - Prince and Vectus did not respond to AFP or the NYT.
- KoBold declined to comment on the White House talks with AVZ.
  - The White House says it named no specific buyer.
- M23/AFC calls the US-DRC partnership unconstitutional.
  - Analysts (Egmont, RANE) say the reserve mostly concerns Katanga, not the war-torn east, and expect the momentum to falter.
- US backing for named US firms is open industrial policy.
  - Steering assets to them is not evidence of a personal benefit to officials.
