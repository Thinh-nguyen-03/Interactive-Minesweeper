# C09: Binance SEC case dropped and CZ pardon

**Verdict:** Mostly accurate (confidence: high)

## What the video said

> The SEC lawsuit against Binance was dropped; Trump later pardoned Binance founder Changpeng Zhao (CZ); Trump said 'I don't know who he is'; CZ said he 'didn't do anything' to get it but that without a pardon Binance is severely limited in the US.

## Summary

The SEC dismissed its case against Binance and Zhao with prejudice on May 29, 2025. Trump pardoned Zhao on Oct 22-23, 2025, and on 60 Minutes (Nov 2, 2025) said 'I don't know who he is'. On the All-In podcast (about Feb 2026), Zhao said he 'didn't do much' to get the pardon and that without it Binance could hardly enter the US properly. The video wrongly has Binance doing the $2B deal: MGX invested $2B in Binance, settled in USD1. Its quid-pro-quo insinuation rests on a documented sequence of events, with no proof of causation.

## Corrections

- Transcript says Binance is 'doing' the $2B stablecoin deal; the record shows MGX invested $2B into Binance, with the deal settled in USD1.
- Transcript says Trump 'firmly denies knowing' Zhao; Trump said he didn't think he had met him and added 'maybe I did'.
- The SEC dismissal (May 29, 2025) was with prejudice and also covered Zhao personally. The pardon followed on Oct 22-23, 2025.
- The CZ quote comes from the All-In podcast (about Feb 2026). Its 'UBO' and 'severely limited' wording is not independently verified.

## Denials and context

- White House (Leavitt): the Biden DOJ overreached, there were no fraud victims, and the White House Counsel vetted the pardon.
- Trump: he was told it was a 'Biden witch hunt', he doesn't know Zhao, and his sons are in crypto.
- Zhao: he did not do much to get the pardon and has no business relationship with World Liberty Financial. His lawyers threatened to sue Sen. Warren over her claim that he 'financed President Trump's stablecoin and lobbied for a pardon'.
- Binance CEO Richard Teng: Binance had no role in MGX choosing USD1, and USD1 was listed elsewhere first.
- The SEC civil case was separate from the 2023 criminal guilty pleas ($4.3B penalty for Binance; 4 months in prison for Zhao). Dropping the SEC case did not erase the criminal conviction.

## Full report

**Verdict: mostly accurate (confidence: high on the core facts, medium on CZ's exact wording)**

### What the video says (transcript lines 407-453)
- Binance "just had an SEC lawsuit against them. It was dropped."
- "Later, Binance's founder himself was pardoned by Trump."
- "Binance are the people who are doing this $2 billion stablecoin deal."
- Trump denies knowing Changpeng Zhao: "Why did you pardon him? OK, are you ready? I don't know who he is. I don't know him. I don't believe I've ever..."
- CZ says he "didn't do much / didn't do anything" to get the pardon, but that without a pardon "it's quite hard for Binance to enter the U.S. in a proper way", that he is "the UBO of Binance and Binance U.S.", that "without a pardon, Binance is severely limited to do stuff in the U.S.", and that if the U.S. wants to be the crypto capital "you cannot not have the largest player".
- Insinuation: the USD1 deal, the benefit to the Trump family and the pardon are "just a couple of coincidences".

### Evidence by sub-claim

#### 1. The SEC lawsuit against Binance was dropped: confirmed
The SEC sued Binance, its U.S. affiliate and Zhao in June 2023. It alleged inflated trading volumes, diverted customer funds and misleading statements about surveillance controls. On May 29, 2025, the SEC, Binance and Zhao filed a joint stipulation in D.C. federal court dismissing the case **with prejudice**, so it cannot be brought again. Reuters dated its story May 30. The SEC said it acted "in the exercise of its discretion and as a policy matter." The dismissal fits a broader 2025 pullback from crypto enforcement, which also saw the Coinbase case dropped in February. [1][2][3]

Context: the SEC civil case was separate from the November 2023 DOJ criminal case. In that case Binance pleaded guilty and paid about $4.3B, and Zhao pleaded guilty to a Bank Secrecy Act/AML violation and served 4 months in prison in 2024. Dropping the SEC case did not erase the criminal conviction. [1]

#### 2. Trump later pardoned Zhao: confirmed
Trump signed the pardon on Wednesday, October 22, 2025, and the White House announced it on October 23. That was about five months after the SEC dismissal, so "later" is correct. Press Secretary Karoline Leavitt said the Biden administration had pursued Zhao despite no allegations of fraud or identifiable victims. She called the prosecution part of a "war on crypto" that was now over, and said the White House Counsel's Office had reviewed the pardon. [4][5]

#### 3. "Binance are the people doing this $2 billion stablecoin deal": partly accurate
On March 12, 2025, MGX (Abu Dhabi, chaired by Sheikh Tahnoon bin Zayed) announced a $2B minority investment **into** Binance. On May 1, 2025, Zach Witkoff said at Token2049 in Dubai, alongside Eric Trump, that the investment would be settled in World Liberty Financial's USD1. So Binance was the **recipient** of the $2B, and MGX was the investor that chose USD1. Binance CEO Richard Teng says Binance had no role in MGX's choice of USD1. [6][7][8]

#### 4. Trump said he doesn't know who Zhao is: confirmed
The quote comes from CBS *60 Minutes* with Norah O'Donnell, which aired November 2, 2025. Trump said: "Okay, are you ready? I don't know who he is. I know he got a four-month sentence..." He also said he didn't think he had ever met Zhao, adding "Maybe I did". He said he had been told Zhao was a victim of a "Biden witch hunt" and that he knew "nothing about the guy." When pressed on the appearance of corruption, Trump said he'd "rather not" be asked. Gizmodo reported that this exchange was cut from the broadcast. [9][10][11][12]

Correction: the video says Trump "firmly denies knowing" Zhao. Trump actually hedged ("maybe I did"). Calling the person "he just pardoned from a crime" is fair: Zhao had pleaded guilty.

#### 5. CZ said he "didn't do much / anything" and that without a pardon Binance is limited in the U.S.: mostly confirmed
DL News reported these remarks from CZ's interview with Chamath Palihapitiya on the All-In podcast, which listings date to about February 10, 2026. Zhao said he "didn't do much" to get the pardon, and: "Without the pardon it's quite hard for Binance to enter the US in a proper way." [13][14] I could not open a primary transcript of the "UBO of Binance and Binance U.S." and "severely limited" lines; outlet hosts were blocked from this session. They are consistent with the reported remarks but **unverified verbatim**.

Separately, Zhao confirmed in May 2025 that he had applied for a pardon. That application was submitted about two weeks before the SEC dismissal. He denied reports that he offered Binance.US equity in exchange. [15]

#### 6. The implied link (USD1 investment, benefit to the Trump family, pardon): documented sequence, causation unproven
These facts are documented:
- Binance received the $2B in USD1, which helped make USD1 one of the largest stablecoins.
- The WSJ reported that Binance engineers helped build USD1's technology, and that Zhao's representatives sought talks with Trump allies to resolve Binance's legal problems.
- Zhao lobbied for and received a pardon.

No public evidence shows a quid pro quo. Zhao says "there is no business relationship between me, Binance, and World Liberty Finance." Teng denies Binance took part in MGX's choice of USD1. Zhao's lawyers threatened to sue Sen. Elizabeth Warren over her statement that he "financed President Trump's stablecoin and lobbied for a pardon." Warren, Sen. Chris Murphy and others call the pardon corrupt. [16][17][18][19]

### Corrections
- Binance was the **recipient** of the $2B investment, not the party "doing" the deal. MGX invested and chose USD1 as the settlement currency.
- Trump did not flatly deny ever meeting Zhao. He said he didn't think he had, then added "maybe I did".
- The SEC dismissal (May 29, 2025) was with prejudice and also covered Zhao personally. The pardon came on October 22-23, 2025, so "later" is correct.
- The CZ quote is from the All-In podcast, which listings date to about February 2026. His exact "UBO" and "severely limited" wording is not independently verified here.

### Denials and other side
- White House: the prosecution was Biden-era overreach with no fraud victims, and the Counsel's Office vetted the pardon.
- Trump: he was told it was a "witch hunt", he doesn't know Zhao, and his sons are in crypto.
- Zhao: he was surprised by the pardon and did not do much to get it, he has no business ties to World Liberty Financial, and rivals lobbied against his pardon.
- Binance (Teng): it had no role in MGX choosing USD1, and USD1 was listed elsewhere first.
- Critics (Warren, Murphy): the pardon followed Binance's help to a Trump-family venture.
