# C66: The extended family: Boulos, Kushner seniors, Lara, Tiffany, Barron, Ivanka

**Verdict:** Partly accurate · medium confidence  
**Origin:** Connection hunt

## Bottom line

The in-law roles are documented.

## What holds up

- Massad Boulos (Tiffany Trump's father-in-law) was given a senior US government role and fronts the DRC/Rwanda minerals-for-security diplomacy.
- Tiffany Trump and Michael Boulos vacationed on the yacht of a Turkish oil trader (BGN International) that stands to benefit from the Libyan oil output Massad Boulos was promoting.
- Michael Boulos took $100,000 from a Saudi businessman who sought help recovering assets seized in the Saudi corruption crackdown and an invitation to the Trump wedding; House Oversight Democrats opened an inquiry (mostly accurate)
- Michael Boulos earned a fee on a superyacht sale to Jared Kushner that the NYT says was overpriced by about $2.5M.
- Michael Boulos is a trustee of the Trump presidential library foundation.
- Charles Kushner, pardoned by Trump in 2020, was made ambassador to France (and Monaco).
- Josh Kushner's Thrive Capital is one of OpenAI's largest backers and is tied into OpenAI through a circular deal, while OpenAI's Stargate project was launched at the White House.
- Barron Trump holds a WLF stake and has launched other ventures, one tied to a Trump donor (mostly accurate)
- Kai Trump (Don Jr.'s daughter) has endorsement deals.

## What needs correcting

- Massad Boulos's own business is a Nigerian truck/equipment dealer (SCOA Nigeria) controlled by his wife's family, far smaller than the 'billionaire' label, and his financial disclosure is not public (partly accurate)
- Nigeria awarded a $700M contract to the Chagoury family, Boulos's reported long-time patrons, weeks before Boulos met President Tinubu (partly accurate)
- Kushner Companies relied on Gulf-linked financing for 666 Fifth Avenue while Jared Kushner served in the first Trump White House (partly accurate)
- A senior GSA procurement official who is himself a Thrive investor oversaw federal contracting with Thrive-backed companies (Ramp, Databricks) (partly accurate)
- Lara Trump hosts a Fox News show and runs ventures that depend on government decisions (partly accurate)
- Ivanka Trump has ventures that depend on government decisions (partly accurate)
- **Claim:** The brief implies a Boulos minerals/Congo business deal  
  **Record:** The record shows Massad Boulos's official role in DRC minerals diplomacy, not a family stake in Congolese minerals.
- The brief asks about Thrive stakes in Kalshi/Polymarket. None was found; the family prediction-market link is Don Jr./1789 Capital (C25).
- **Brief said:** Kushner Companies has foreign financing  
  **Record shows:** The documented Gulf-linked episode (666 Fifth, 2017-18) is historical, and Brookfield denied any Qatari role. Current Gulf money flows to Affinity Partners, not Kushner Companies.
- Several aggregators conflate Massad and Michael Boulos: it was Massad, not Michael, who spent decades in Lagos running SCOA's truck business. Some summaries also wrongly name Michael as the government adviser.
- Barron's WLF figures (about $40M proceeds, about 7.5% stake, about $150M net worth) are Forbes estimates, not disclosed numbers.

## What is unproven

- Thrive Capital holds stakes in Kalshi or Polymarket.

## In more detail

- Massad Boulos runs US Africa diplomacy, including DRC minerals and Libyan oil, and Charles Kushner, whom Trump pardoned, is ambassador to France.
- A single NYT investigation (Aug-Sept 2025) documents Boulos-family money and access channels: a $100K payment from a Saudi businessman, now under a House Oversight inquiry; a yacht fee on a sale to Jared Kushner; and a vacation on a Turkish oil trader's yacht that would benefit from Libyan output.
- The strongest Kushner-sibling link to government decisions is a GSA procurement chief reported to be a Thrive investor while dealing with Thrive-backed Ramp and Databricks, and the investment itself is single-source.
- Barron's Sollos venture uses a Trump donor's house as its address.
- Lara, Ivanka and Kai have no federal-dependent ventures we could find, and Thrive-Kalshi/Polymarket and Boulos-business-DRC-minerals links are unsupported.

### 5. Michael Boulos took $100,000 from a Saudi businessman who sought help recovering assets seized in the Saudi corruption crackdown and an invitation to the Trump wedding; House Oversight Democrats opened an inquiry.

### Connections found

#### 1. Massad Boulos: in-law turned senior adviser running minerals diplomacy (documented)
- Massad Boulos is the father of Michael Boulos, Tiffany Trump's husband. He was named Senior Advisor for Africa in April 2025, alongside his Arab-affairs role. His first stop was Kinshasa (3 Apr 2025). There he said a US-DRC minerals deal would be signed alongside a Congo-Rwanda peace agreement, and that US and Western companies would invest billions afterward (AP, NPR). Five House Democrats demanded the deal's terms (Africa Confidential).
- What this shows: a family in-law runs diplomacy that trades on minerals access.
- What it does not show: we found no Boulos-family commercial interest in DRC minerals.

#### 2. Boulos's own business: SCOA Nigeria / Fadoul group (documented; disclosure gap single-source)
- The NYT (Dec 2024, via Naharnet, Mediaite and Business Insider) reported that Boulos spent about 20 years selling trucks for SCOA Nigeria PLC, which his father-in-law's Fadoul group controls. SCOA's profit was under $66K and its market value about $865K, far from the "billionaire" tag. Boulos says he never called himself one, and SCOA's audit-committee chair defended him.
- Tablet (via KIVU Today) could not find his public OGE 278e. State says he is "compliant with all employment requirements". A confidential OGE 450 would not be public, so a missing 278e does not by itself show he did not file.

#### 3. Nigeria: Chagoury contract and the Tinubu meeting (contract documented; Boulos link single-source)
- Chagoury-owned ITB Nigeria was picked for a roughly $700M Lagos ports renovation in March 2025 (ThisDay citing Africa Intelligence; Sahara Reporters). Boulos met Tinubu in Paris in mid-April 2025 (AllAfrica).
- Only Tablet describes the Chagourys as Boulos's long-time patrons and sets the two events side by side. No source alleges a quid pro quo.

#### 4. Libya oil and the Bayegan yacht (single-source: one NYT investigation, widely relayed)
- In summer 2025, Tiffany and Michael Boulos sailed on *Phoenix 2*, owned by Ercument and Ruya Bayegan of BGN International. Tiffany posted from the yacht a day after Massad Boulos announced plans to raise Libyan oil output, and BGN trades Libyan oil.
- On 23 July, Boulos dined privately with Libya's national security adviser. He at first denied the dinner, then acknowledged it.
- The couple's spokesperson called it a private vacation. Nothing illegal is alleged.

#### 5. Michael Boulos and the $100K from a Saudi businessman (inquiry documented; facts single-source)
- The NYT reported that Abdulelah Allam wired $100,000 to Michael Boulos on 22 June 2022, the day of a Virginia meeting arranged by Boulos's cousin James Frangi. An invitation to the Nov 2022 Mar-a-Lago wedding was discussed.
- Rep. Robert Garcia wrote to Boulos, Allam and Frangi on 29 Aug 2025. His office describes the payment as being for leveraging Trump ties to help Allam recover assets Saudi Arabia seized.
- The accounts conflict. Boulos's side says Allam settled a debt Frangi's company owed Boulos, while Allam's side calls it a loan. Allam got neither an invitation nor his assets.

#### 6. Michael Boulos and the Kushner yacht (single-source: NYT, 21 Aug 2025)
- Through Frangi's brokerage, Jared Kushner bought the 50m *Solstice*, under construction in Perama, Greece. The NYT reports about a $2.5M overcharge and about $300K to Boulos.
- Boulos's side says it was a prenegotiated finder's fee and that he has ended his business with Frangi.
- This is an intra-family link that does not depend on any government decision. It matters because the same Frangi channel recurs in item 5.

#### 7. Michael Boulos as library foundation trustee (documented; see C48)
He is a trustee of the Donald J. Trump Presidential Library Foundation, with Eric Trump and James Kiley. The foundation got IRS exemption in 10 days and a state-deeded Miami parcel.

Wikipedia also lists a Monaco accreditation in Oct 2025, which we did not verify elsewhere. In Aug 2025 he rebuked Macron over antisemitism and was summoned by the French foreign ministry.
