# C84: Heritage Advisors and Sheikh Sultan bin Jassim

**Verdict:** Mostly accurate · medium confidence  
**Type:** Connection check · Pardons, lobbyists and access

## The claim

> Did one Qatari fund pay consulting income to Lee Zeldin, take a large stake in Newsmax and act as the investor in the Menendez-Daibes case?

## Bottom line

The fund's three documented roles hold up. Heritage Advisors / Sheikh Sultan bin Jassim paid EPA chief Lee Zeldin's consultancy in 2023, holds ~19.7M Newsmax shares through a disclosed SEC ownership chain (about 15% and ~$1.6B at the March 2025 listing price, both our arithmetic, against ~$50M invested), and paid $45M for a quarter of Fred Daibes's Edgewater holdings in the Menendez case.

## What holds up

- Heritage Advisors is Sheikh Sultan bin Jassim Al Thani's vehicle, and its Newsmax stake sits in a disclosed US ownership chain (Naples Investment Holdco LLC -> Heritage Investments LP -> Heritage GP -> a trust of which he is sole beneficiary).
- The same fund was the 'Qatari Investment Company' in the Menendez case, closing a $45M deal for a quarter stake in Daibes's Edgewater properties, and the joint entity controls the base of Wael Hana's IS EG Halal (mostly accurate)
- A former Biden aide arranged the 2021 meeting between Menendez, Sheikh Sultan and Qatari official Ali Al Thawadi.
- Lee Zeldin was paid by Heritage Advisors in 2023, before joining the administration, and divested his consulting firm under his ethics agreement.
- Heritage Advisors' other investments: Fort Partners (Miami), The News Movement, Indy Eleven (mostly accurate)
- Daibes paid Javelin Advisors (Keith Schiller) $1M for a pardon bid, and Menendez himself sought a Trump pardon; neither got clemency (mostly accurate)

## What needs correcting

- Newsmax's dealings with the administration: favourable treatment? (misleading)
- C73 gives the Newsmax stake only as the ~$50M invested in 2019-20. SEC filings show 19,737,553 Class B shares, about 15% (our arithmetic), worth roughly $1.65B at the first-day close.
- The claim's phrase 'Newsmax's dealings with the administration' implies favourable treatment. The documented record shows Newsmax opposing the FCC's Nexstar-Tegna approval and leaving the Pentagon under Hegseth's rules; its defamation settlements were private disputes.
- Heritage's deal with Daibes was finalized in December 2022 for $45M for a quarter stake (FDD, Daily Beast). The indictment's 2023 joint-venture language refers to the same relationship.
- C73 could not read the Free Beacon jet article. Its claim is that Sheikh Sultan is an HBJ confidant and business partner, from an anonymous source; it does not say Heritage owns the jet.
- Audit: the Daibes '$1M' was graded documented here but single-source in C83; it rests on NBC's three unnamed sources (the LDA filings document the engagement). Sub-claim 9 is now mostly-accurate and labels say so.

## What is unproven

- Sheikh Sultan is a confidant and business partner of Hamad bin Jassim (HBJ), the former Qatari PM reported to be the prior owner of the 747-8 (P4-HBJ) that Trump accepted as Air Force One.
- Any EPA matter involving Qatari interests during Zeldin's tenure.
- Any source connecting the Zeldin, Newsmax and Menendez/Javelin threads to each other.

## In more detail

- The new layers are a Daily Beast report that the Heritage-Daibes entity controls co-defendant Wael Hana's IS EG Halal site, and a single anonymous-sourced Free Beacon claim tying Sheikh Sultan to Hamad bin Jassim, reported prior owner of the Qatari Air Force One jet.
- No source shows coordination among the threads or any US government benefit: we found no EPA matter involving Qatar, Newsmax is suing Trump's FCC rather than benefiting from it, and Daibes's Javelin pardon bid (reportedly $1M, per NBC's unnamed sources) failed.

### 1. Heritage Advisors is Sheikh Sultan bin Jassim Al Thani's vehicle, and its Newsmax stake sits in a disclosed US ownership chain (Naples Investment Holdco LLC -> Heritage Investments LP -> Heritage GP -> a trust of which he is sole beneficiary).

**Confirmed**

- Documented (SEC filing as relayed by filing aggregators; sec.gov itself could not be read directly).
- A Form 3 filed around 1 May 2025, after Newsmax's 31 March 2025 NYSE listing, shows Naples Investment Holdco LLC holding 19,737,553 Class B shares.
- Heritage Investments LP is Holdco's sole member, and Sheikh Sultan is sole beneficiary of the Heritage Trust at the top of the chain.
- ICIJ/WaPo (March 2024) reported the stake had earlier been moved into a Cayman Islands structure.
- Measured against Ruddy's 39.24M shares (about 30.4%), Holdco's block is roughly 15% of outstanding shares.
- That is our arithmetic, and it matches a Forbes-based 15% figure relayed by a low-authority ownership site.
- At the first-day close of $83.51 the block was worth about $1.65B, against the roughly $50M paid in 2019-20; this is also our arithmetic.
- The stock fell sharply afterwards, and we did not establish the current value or any sale after the 29 September 2025 lockup expiry.
- Amounts: about 30.4%; roughly 15%; 15%; $83.51; about $1.65B
- Dates: 1 May 2025; 31 March 2025; March 2024; 2019-20; 29 September 2025

### 2. The same fund was the 'Qatari Investment Company' in the Menendez case, closing a $45M deal for a quarter stake in Daibes's Edgewater properties, and the joint entity controls the base of Wael Hana's IS EG Halal.

**Mostly accurate** · Evidence: Documented for the deal; the IS EG overlap is single-source

- The superseding indictment (January 2024) alleges Menendez introduced Daibes to a royal principal of a Qatari investment company, which signed a letter of intent and in 2023 formed a joint venture with a Daibes company to invest tens of millions in a New Jersey project.
- Trial documents and press identify the fund as Heritage Advisors (NYT, Real Deal, FDD).
- FDD, citing property records, says Heritage finalized a $45M deal with Daibes in December 2022.
- The Daily Beast reports that this bought a one-quarter stake in Daibes's Edgewater properties through a joint entity, Hudson Edgewater Associates.
- According to the Daily Beast, that entity controls the site that serves as IS EG Halal's base of operations; IS EG Halal is co-defendant Wael Hana's company in the Egypt strand of the same case.
- Heritage's lawyers say Sheikh Sultan had no relationship with IS EG and did not know about the overlap until the outlet asked.
- Neither Sheikh Sultan nor Qatar was charged.
- Amounts: $45M
- Dates: January 2024; December 2022
- Sources: [8](https://therealdeal.com/new-york/tristate/2024/01/03/updated-menendez-indictment-links-nj-developer-to-qatar) [9](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/politics/prosecutors-say-sen-menendez-cashed-in-by-linking-qatari-royal-family-member-with-new-jersey-businessman) [10](https://newjerseyglobe.com/congress/new-menendez-indictment-alleges-corrupt-involvement-with-qatari-government/) [7](https://www.fdd.org/analysis/op-eds/2025/03/10/qatari-cash-may-be-coming-soon-to-a-town-near-you/) [6](https://www.thedailybeast.com/big-qatari-payday-for-senator-bob-menendezs-pal-fred-daibes-linked-to-probe)

### 5. Lee Zeldin was paid by Heritage Advisors in 2023, before joining the administration, and divested his consulting firm under his ethics agreement.

- Heritage Advisors (London) is listed as a client of Zeldin Strategies, with compensation above $5,000 for April-December 2023.
- Zeldin said the work required no FARA registration and that he never lobbied or did media work for the firm.
- E&E News reports he drew $775,000 in salary from Zeldin Strategies overall.
- His 2025 ethics agreement commits him to divest the firm and to recuse from EPA matters involving his wife Diana Zeldin's clients, since she works there; this is per search excerpts, as OGE PDFs could not be read directly.
- Whether Heritage stayed a client after his confirmation is not established.
- His 2026 annual 278e (signed 13 May 2026) exists, but we could not read its entries.
- Per Quincy, the Heritage engagement began shortly after a 2023 Newsweek op-ed in which Zeldin criticised Qatari influence.
- Amounts: $5,000; $775,000
- Dates: December 2023; 13 May 2026

### 7. Newsmax's dealings with the administration: favourable treatment?

**Misleading**

- Documented, and it cuts against a capture narrative. (a) FCC: in March 2026 Newsmax joined DirecTV, cable groups and public-interest groups in suing to block the FCC Media Bureau's 19 March 2026 approval of the Nexstar-Tegna merger.
- The challengers cited Trump's February Truth Social endorsement and Chair Carr's support.
- In July 2026 the D.C. Circuit dismissed the challenge for lack of jurisdiction over a bureau-level order, not on the merits. (b) Pentagon: Newsmax got Pentagon workspace in Hegseth's February 2025 rotation, but in October 2025 it refused his new press rules and turned in its badges, and in December 2025 it was among outlets denied briefing access. (c) The defamation settlements were private: Smartmatic $40M (Axios, March 2025) and Dominion $67M (AP, August 2025).
- Neither involved the government. (d) Commercial: Trump Media's Truth+ carries Newsmax (C21).
- The documented relationship is commercial carriage plus Ruddy's personal closeness to Trump.
- We found no FCC or other federal benefit to Newsmax.
- Amounts: $40M; $67M
- Dates: March 2026; 19 March 2026; July 2026; February 2025; October 2025
- Sources: [30](https://www.tvtechnology.com/regulatory-legal/newsmax-pay-tv-groups-sue-fcc-to-block-nexstar-tegna-merger) [31](https://barrettmedia.com/2026/03/22/newsmax-joins-directv-challenge-of-nexstar-media-group-merger-with-tegna/) [32](https://thedesk.net/2026/07/appeals-court-tosses-challenge-to-fccs-approval-of-nexstar-tegna-deal/) [33](https://www.foxcarolina.com/2025/10/16/journalists-turn-access-badges-exit-pentagon-rather-than-agree-new-reporting-rules/) [34](https://www.click2houston.com/news/politics/2025/12/01/outlets-that-reach-millions-denied-access-to-rare-pentagon-news-briefings-this-week/) [35](https://www.axios.com/2025/03/14/newsmax-smartmatic-defamation-lawsuit-40-million-settle) [36](https://m.washingtontimes.com/news/2025/aug/18/newsmax-agrees-pay-67m-settlement-defamation-case-2020-election/) [37](https://www.thestar.com.my/tech/tech-news/2025/07/07/trump-media-launches-tv-streaming-platform-with-newsmax-channel-globally)

## Denials and context

- Heritage says it operates independently of any government, invests only for value, and has no editorial or management control over media holdings.
  - Its lawyers say Sheikh Sultan bought the Newsmax stake for profit and not on behalf of Qatar.
- Heritage's lawyers say Sheikh Sultan had no relationship with IS EG Halal and did not know of the property overlap.
- Neither Sheikh Sultan nor Qatar was charged in the Menendez case.
  - Doyle says he was unpaid and cooperated with law enforcement.
- Newsmax denied softening its coverage of Qatar.
- Zeldin's spokesperson declined to comment.
  - A Senate Republican aide said he disclosed all that was required.
  - Zeldin says the work required no FARA registration and involved no lobbying.
- Daibes's lawyer says he ended the Javelin engagement in June 2025.
  - The White House said the President alone decides pardons.
- Source note: icij.org, sec.gov and oge.gov could not be read directly; findings rest on search-result text across several outlets.

### Connections found (each graded)

#### 1. Sheikh Sultan, a Cayman/US holding chain, and about 15% of Newsmax
**Grade: documented (SEC Form 3, relayed by filing aggregators).**

- **The holding.** A Form 3 filed around 1 May 2025 shows **Naples Investment Holdco LLC** holding **19,737,553 Newsmax Class B shares**. Newsmax had listed on the NYSE on 31 March 2025.
- **The ownership chain.** Holdco's sole member is **Heritage Investments LP**, which sits under Heritage GP and a trust. Sheikh Sultan is the trust's sole beneficiary. ICIJ and the Washington Post had reported in 2024 that the stake was moved into a Cayman Islands structure.
- **Its size (our arithmetic).** Ruddy holds 39.24M shares, about 30.4%, so the Heritage block is roughly **15%**. That matches a Forbes-based figure relayed by a low-authority ownership site.
- **Its value (our arithmetic).** At the first-day close of $83.51 it was worth about **$1.65B**, against about $50M paid in 2019-20.
- **What is not established.** The lockup expired on 29 September 2025. We did not establish later sales or the current value, and the stock fell sharply after its debut.

#### 2. The Menendez deal: $45M for a quarter of Daibes's Edgewater holdings, including IS EG Halal's base
**Grade: deal documented; IS EG overlap single-source (Daily Beast).**

- **The indictment.** The January 2024 superseding indictment says Menendez introduced Daibes to a royal principal of a "Qatari Investment Company". It says the fund signed a letter of intent, then formed a 2023 joint venture to invest tens of millions in a New Jersey project.
