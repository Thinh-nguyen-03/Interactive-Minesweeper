# C78: The Blank-Cheque Companies Run by Trump Media's Interim Chief

**Verdict:** Mostly accurate · medium confidence  
**Type:** Connection check · Trump Media & Markets

## The Claim

> How many blank-cheque companies tied to the Trump family does Kevin McGurn run while serving as Trump Media's interim CEO, and what roles do Yorkville and Clear Street play?

## Bottom Line

The SPAC web is real and larger than the claim: Yorkville controls four blank-cheque vehicles (MCGA, Texas Ventures III, Blue Water III, Yorkville International), McGurn is CEO of at least three of them while serving as TMTG interim CEO (and ran Texas Ventures III until Apr 22, 2026), and Trump Organization EVPs Glick and Garten sit on Texas Ventures III's board, which is merging with PlusAI at $800M. Two parts of the claim are off: McGurn resigned from the Trump sons' New America SPAC on Aug 5, 2026 (Dominari's Kyle Wool now runs it), and the Dominari/D. Boral underwriting cluster is separate from the Yorkville cluster, joined only through McGurn.

## What Holds Up

- Kevin McGurn has run or sat on the board of at least six blank-cheque vehicles since 2025, four of them sponsored by Yorkville, while also advising and then running Trump Media.
- Texas Ventures Acquisition III was taken over by a Yorkville sponsor, and Trump Organization executives sit on its board.
- Texas Ventures III's target: PlusAI autonomous-trucking software, at an $800M pre-money valuation, with Yorkville financing.
- Blue Water Acquisition Corp III, a $10B Citgo bidder, was taken over by Yorkville with TMTG's CEO and general counsel on its board (mostly accurate)
- Yorkville International Capital, a McGurn-led SPAC aimed at Venezuela, drew a Senate Finance inquiry.
- Yorkville Acquisition Corp (MCGA), the vehicle for the Trump Media CRO treasury, has $181M in trust and no deal after the termination (mostly accurate)
- McGurn left the Trump sons' New America Acquisition I and Dominari's Kyle Wool took over. Ex-White House ethics lawyer Stefan Passantino joined the board.
- Founder-share economics: the Trump sons hold 5M NWAX founder shares worth about $50M on paper, and Yorkville bought SPAC founder blocks for well under $1 a share (mostly accurate)
- D. Boral Capital and Dominari are the recurring underwriters for the Trump-sons/McGurn vehicles, and D. Boral runs its own SPAC with McGurn on the board.
- Clear Street, co-lead agent on Trump Media's bitcoin raise and a leading crypto-treasury underwriter, launched a $1B IPO and then postponed it.
- Cohen & Company Capital Markets and Troy Rillo/Yorkville Securities link the TMTG bitcoin raise to the Yorkville SPACs.
- SEC review status of the Truth Social crypto ETFs: no approval found; the filings were repeatedly delayed and then refiled (mostly accurate)

## What Needs Correcting

- Trump Organization and Trump Media executives sit on SPAC boards controlled by Trump Media's own largest financier (partly accurate)
- The claim says McGurn runs New America Acquisition I while TMTG interim CEO: he resigned as its CEO and director effective Aug 5, 2026, and Kyle Wool, who became chairman Feb 6, 2026, is now CEO.
- The claim says McGurn runs Texas Ventures III: he did, from the Sept 2025 Yorkville takeover until he resigned as CEO on Apr 22, 2026 (8-K), the day after becoming TMTG interim CEO. Troy Rillo has been CEO and CFO since. (The original pass had marked this unverified.)
- The claim lists D. Boral, Dominari and Yorkville as recurring sponsors/underwriters together: Dominari/D. Boral underwrote NWAX and D. Boral's own SPAC, while the Yorkville SPACs used Cohen & Company (YICC) and no Dominari/D. Boral role was found in them.
- C62 lists Texas Ventures III's Trump Org directors as single-source: the FY2025 10-K names Lawrence Glick and Alan Garten, so this is documented. Nunes and Glabe were also TVA III directors until they resigned in Feb 2026 (8-K), so that is documented too.
- Clear Street's IPO was not completed: it was cut from about $1.05B to about $351-364M and postponed on Feb 12, 2026.

## In More Detail

- Clear Street's tie is limited to the TMTG raise, and its IPO was postponed in Feb 2026.
- No Truth Social crypto ETF approval was found.
- The governance overlaps are documented; no source shows a transaction benefiting the Trump family beyond the sons' NWAX founder shares.

## Full Report

**Verdict: mostly-accurate (medium confidence).** The web is real, and larger than the claim describes. Yorkville affiliates control four blank-cheque companies:

- Yorkville Acquisition Corp (MCGA)
- Texas Ventures Acquisition III (TVA)
- Blue Water Acquisition Corp III (BLUW)
- Yorkville International Capital (YICC)

Kevin McGurn is CEO of at least three of them while serving as Trump Media's interim CEO. Two Trump Organization executive vice presidents, Lawrence Glick and Alan Garten, sit on TVA III's board, and TVA III is merging with PlusAI at an $800M pre-money valuation.

Two points in the claim need correcting:

- **McGurn no longer runs the Trump sons' SPAC.** He resigned from New America Acquisition I on Aug 5, 2026, and Dominari's Kyle Wool now runs it.
- **The two clusters are separate.** The Dominari/D. Boral underwriting cluster and the Yorkville sponsor cluster are joined only through McGurn.

Clear Street's Trump tie is the TMTG bitcoin raise alone, and it postponed its own IPO in February 2026. I found no SEC approval for any Truth Social crypto ETF.

Each governance overlap is documented. No source shows a transaction that benefits the Trump family beyond the sons' NWAX founder shares, and nothing here shows a regulatory favour.

### Connections (sub-claims)

1. **McGurn holds the most posts in the web.** *Documented.*
   - CEO of MCGA, BLUW and YICC.
   - CEO, chairman and CFO of NWAX at various points until Aug 5, 2026, and sole member of its sponsor.
   - Director of D. Boral Acquisition I.
   - TMTG adviser since December 2024 and interim CEO since Apr 21, 2026.

   Renaissance Capital's YICC note describes him as TMTG's interim CEO. He was also CEO of TVA III from the Sept 2025 takeover until he resigned on Apr 22, 2026, the day after becoming TMTG interim CEO (8-K; *documented*, audit correction). Rillo then became CEO while staying CFO, which is why the PlusAI release names him. Multiple SPAC directorships are common, and conflicts are disclosed in the prospectuses.

2. **Texas Ventures III: a Yorkville takeover with Trump Organization directors.** *Documented (8-K, 10-K).*
   - TV Partners III sold control to Yorkville Acquisition Sponsor II for $7.4M. That covered 7.5M founder shares and 4.7M warrants, about $0.99 per founder share, against $10 per public unit.
   - The old board resigned.
   - The 10-K lists Glick (Trump Org EVP of Development) and Garten (Trump Org EVP and Chief Legal Officer) as directors.
   - Then-TMTG CEO Nunes and TMTG general counsel Glabe were directors until they resigned in Feb 2026: an 8-K (date of report Feb 23, 2026, seen via search summaries) records their resignations and Garten and Glick joining the audit and compensation committees (*documented*, audit correction; previously single-source).
   - I found no director pay or founder-share grants for the Trump Org executives.

3. **PlusAI merger.** *Documented.* The deal was signed Sep 2-3, 2026:
   - about $800M pre-money valuation;
   - up to about $300M of capital, of which more than $60M is committed (including Yorkville Advisors Global funds) and about $236M sits in trust;
   - an S-4 filed Oct 9, 2026.

   PlusAI's $1.2B deal with Churchill Capital IX ended by mutual consent on Apr 20-21, 2026. An aggregator reports that 17.7M of 22.5M shares were redeemed at an extension vote (*unverified*). I found no federal action involving PlusAI, so any regulatory angle on autonomous trucking is speculation and is not asserted here.

4. **Blue Water III: from Citgo bidder to TMTG-staffed shell.** The sponsor change is *documented* (8-K). McGurn's directorship is *documented* (filing profile). Nunes, Glabe and Angelo as directors, and the $7.2M price, are *single-source* (Siegelman/NewsTRACS).
   - Under founder Joseph Hernandez, BLUW bid about $10B for PDV Holding in September 2025 and dropped the bid by mid-November.
   - Elliott's Amber Energy won the auction at about $5.9B.
   - Fortune (Sep 2026) reports that OFAC has since blocked further leadership changes at Citgo's parent companies.
   - I found no sign that the Yorkville-run BLUW pursued Citgo.

5. **Yorkville International: a Venezuela SPAC and a Senate inquiry.** *Documented.*
   - $200M IPO in June 2026, with an emphasis on Latin America and Venezuela.
   - Officers: McGurn (CEO), Rillo (CFO) and Angelo (chairman). Cohen & Company was sole book-runner.
   - On Jun 25, 2026 Sen. Wyden wrote to Angelo. He cited the parent firm's billions in Trump-business ties and the eased Venezuela sanctions, and asked whether Yorkville had sought an OFAC licence.

   I found no Yorkville reply and no licence on record.

6. **MCGA after the CRO unwind.** *Documented.* The trust was $181.17M at Sep 30, 2026. The Trump Media Group CRO Strategy deal was mutually terminated on Aug 7, 2026 (see C41 and C63). I found no new target or liquidation.
