# C57: The UAE network: Tahnoon, G42, MGX and their US deals

**Verdict:** Mostly accurate (confidence: medium)

## Lead investigated

> Leads from C06/C08/C34: map Sheikh Tahnoon's entities and their US-linked deals — Aryam's 49% of World Liberty (board seats for G42's Peng Xiao and lawyer Martin Edelman), MGX (Binance $2B in USD1; Stargate; a stake in the TikTok US joint venture approved by Trump; OpenAI/xAI/Anthropic investments), G42 (chip licences, Microsoft, Stargate UAE campus), a G42 crypto executive reportedly advising WLF, StringZ Holding in WLF's trust bank, ADQ/Lunate in Kushner's Affinity. Verify each link, date and amount; note which US approvals each needed and all denials.

## Summary

Almost every link in the brief is documented. Tahnoon-linked vehicles bought 49% of World Liberty (Aryam, Jan 2025) and reportedly 49% of the holding company behind its OCC trust bank (StringZ, per the WSJ in Aug 2026). MGX settled its $2B Binance deal in USD1, funds Stargate, holds 15% of the Trump-approved TikTok US joint venture, and has invested in OpenAI, xAI and Anthropic. G42 has Microsoft's $1.5B, US chip licences and the Stargate UAE campus, and a G42 crypto executive advised WLF. Three points are weaker. ADQ is not documented as an Affinity investor; Lunate is, and it is majority-owned by a Tahnoon-overseen vehicle. StringZ's ownership rests on anonymous WSJ sourcing that the OCC filing does not confirm. Several 2026 G42 developments come from single sources. No quid pro quo has been documented, and WLF, the White House and Trump deny any link to policy.

## Corrections

- Brief says 'ADQ/Lunate' invested in Affinity; reporting names Lunate (with QIA), not ADQ. ADQ's link is historical, through Tahnoon's chairmanship and seeding of Lunate.
- StringZ's Tahnoon ownership and 49% stake come from anonymous WSJ sourcing (2026-08-27); the OCC decision names StringZ but not its owner or stake size.
- The TikTok joint venture stake is 15% for MGX; early reports describing a 45% combined Oracle/Silver Lake/MGX share were superseded by the Dec 2025 terms.
- MGX's exact role in the OpenAI round (co-lead vs lead participant) is described inconsistently; per-investor amounts in the OpenAI, xAI and Anthropic rounds are undisclosed.

## Denials and context

- Trump said he did not know about the Aryam deal ('my sons are handling that'); the White House says his assets are in a trust run by his children.
- A WLF spokesperson said any claim the Aryam deal had anything to do with the administration's chip actions is '100% false'; spokesman David Wachsman said Trump and Witkoff had no involvement.
- A person familiar with the Aryam investment told the WSJ it did not involve G42 money and was never discussed with Trump (anonymous).
- MGX says it chose USD1 itself and has not invested in WLF (per C07 sourcing).
- The OCC said career staff reviewed the WLTC charter and rejected conflict-of-interest comments; passivity commitments bar StringZ and the Trump entity from controlling the bank.
- Much of the MGX AI portfolio (Stargate, OpenAI, Anthropic, xAI, Aligned) is ordinary large-scale sovereign investing alongside US firms; there is no evidence it required or received favours. The policy-relevant approvals are the chip licences (Commerce/BIS), the TikTok determination (the president) and the bank charter (OCC).
- Disclosure: this write-up was produced with an Anthropic model; Anthropic is one of the MGX portfolio companies listed here. Facts about that investment are taken from Anthropic's own announcements and press coverage.
