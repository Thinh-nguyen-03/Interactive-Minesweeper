# C57: The UAE network: Tahnoon, G42, MGX and their US deals

**Verdict:** Mostly accurate · medium confidence  
**Type:** Check · Gulf money

## The claim

> Which entities controlled by Sheikh Tahnoon bin Zayed hold stakes in Trump-family ventures, and what US approvals and deals did those same entities obtain?

## Bottom line

Almost every link in the claim is documented. Tahnoon-linked vehicles bought 49% of World Liberty (Aryam, Jan 2025) and reportedly 49% of the holding company behind its OCC trust bank (StringZ, per the WSJ in Aug 2026).

## What holds up

- Aryam Investment 1, backed by Tahnoon, bought 49% of World Liberty Financial, and G42's Peng Xiao and lawyer Martin Edelman took WLF board seats.
- MGX, chaired by Tahnoon, settled its $2B Binance investment in WLF's USD1.
- MGX is a Stargate investor.
- MGX took a stake in the TikTok US joint venture that Trump approved.
- MGX has invested in OpenAI, xAI and Anthropic.
- G42 has a Microsoft partnership, US chip licences and the Stargate UAE campus (mostly accurate)
- A G42 crypto executive reportedly advised WLF (mostly accurate)
- StringZ Holding, linked to Tahnoon, is in WLF's trust bank (mostly accurate)

## What needs correcting

- ADQ/Lunate invested in Kushner's Affinity Partners (partly accurate)
- The claim says 'ADQ/Lunate' invested in Affinity; reporting names Lunate (with QIA), not ADQ. ADQ's link is historical, through Tahnoon's chairmanship and seeding of Lunate.
- StringZ's Tahnoon ownership and 49% stake come from anonymous WSJ sourcing (2026-08-27); the OCC decision names StringZ but not its owner or stake size.
- The TikTok joint venture stake is 15% for MGX; early reports describing a 45% combined Oracle/Silver Lake/MGX share were superseded by the Dec 2025 terms.
- MGX's exact role in the OpenAI round (co-lead vs lead participant) is described inconsistently; per-investor amounts in the OpenAI, xAI and Anthropic rounds are undisclosed.

## In more detail

- MGX settled its $2B Binance deal in USD1, funds Stargate, holds 15% of the Trump-approved TikTok US joint venture, and has invested in OpenAI, xAI and Anthropic.
- G42 has Microsoft's $1.5B, US chip licences and the Stargate UAE campus, and a G42 crypto executive advised WLF.
- Three points are weaker.
- ADQ is not documented as an Affinity investor; Lunate is, and it is majority-owned by a Tahnoon-overseen vehicle.
- StringZ's ownership rests on anonymous WSJ sourcing that the OCC filing does not confirm.
- Several 2026 G42 developments come from single sources.
- No quid pro quo has been documented, and WLF, the White House and Trump deny any link to policy.

## Sub-claims

### 1. Aryam Investment 1, backed by Tahnoon, bought 49% of World Liberty Financial, and G42's Peng Xiao and lawyer Martin Edelman took WLF board seats.

**Confirmed**

- Per the WSJ (2026-02-01), as relayed by several outlets, Aryam agreed on about 2025-01-16 to pay $500M for 49%.
- $250M was paid upfront, about $187M of it to Trump-family entities.
- Two Aryam executives who also hold top G42 posts, Peng Xiao (G42 CEO) and Martin Edelman (a US lawyer listed as a G42 official; one dossier calls him G42's general counsel), joined WLF's five-member board.
- No US approval was sought or disclosed.
- Sens. Warren and Kim asked Treasury Secretary Bessent in Feb 2026 to have CFIUS review the stake, and a House inquiry opened on 2026-02-05.
- No public CFIUS outcome was found.
- An unnamed person familiar with the deal said it involved no G42 money.
- WLF spokesman David Wachsman confirmed the investment to the WSJ; Trump said he had no knowledge of it.
- Amounts: $500M; 49%; $250M; about $187M
- Dates: 2026-02-01; 2025-01-16; Feb 2026; 2026-02-05
- Sources: [1](https://www.theblock.co/news/deals/2026-02-01-uae-sheikh-secretly-acquired-49-of-trumps-world-liberty-financial-days-before-inauguration-wsj-387931) [2](https://cointelegraph.com/news/uae-backed-firm-buys-49-percent-trump-linked-world-liberty-wsj) [4](https://www.theblock.co/post/389958/sens-warren-kim-demand-cfius-review-of-uaes-reported-500-million-stake-in-world-liberty-financial-reuters) [5](https://cryptobriefing.com/senate-democrats-hearings-trump-crypto-deal/) [43](https://cointelegraph.com/news/trump-denies-involvement-500m-uae-wlfi-stake)

### 2. MGX, chaired by Tahnoon, settled its $2B Binance investment in WLF's USD1.

**Confirmed**

- Established in C07. MGX announced the $2B minority stake on 2025-03-12, and WLF said on 2025-05-01 that USD1 would settle it.
- Tahnoon chairs MGX, which G42 and Mubadala founded in 2024.
- No US approval applied, since this was a foreign investor buying into a non-US exchange.
- MGX says it chose USD1 itself and that it has not invested in WLF.
- Amounts: $2B

### 3. MGX is a Stargate investor.

**Confirmed**

- SoftBank, OpenAI, Oracle and MGX were named initial equity funders of the $500B Stargate project at the White House on 2025-01-21 (see C08).
- MGX also co-founded the AI Infrastructure Partnership with BlackRock, GIP, Microsoft and Nvidia.
- That partnership, with MGX and GIP, agreed in Oct 2025 to buy Aligned Data Centers for about $40B, and the deal closed around July 2026.
- Coverage said a CFIUS review was expected, but no outcome was found.
- Amounts: $500B; about $40B
- Dates: 2025-01-21; Oct 2025; July 2026

### 4. MGX took a stake in the TikTok US joint venture that Trump approved.

**Confirmed**

- On 2025-09-25 Trump signed an executive order declaring the framework a qualified divestiture under the 2024 divest-or-ban law.
- Vice President Vance put the US entity's value at about $14B. Agreements were signed around 2025-12-18/19.
- Oracle, Silver Lake and MGX each hold 15% of TikTok USDS Joint Venture LLC. Affiliates of existing ByteDance investors hold 30.1%, and ByteDance keeps 19.9%.
- The deal closed on 2026-01-22 (Axios, Hollywood Reporter), the day before the enforcement deadline.
- The joint venture's board named Adam Presser CEO; its seven-member board includes MGX's chief strategy and safety officer David Scott alongside Silver Lake's Egon Durban and Oracle's Kenneth Glueck.
- The recommendation algorithm is to be retrained on US data and held in Oracle's cloud.
- The approval was the president's own determination under the statute.
- MGX's individual price was not disclosed.
- Amounts: about $14B; 15%; 30.1%; 19.9%
- Dates: 2025-09-25; 2025-12-18; 2026-01-22
- Sources: [14](https://www.macrumors.com/2025/09/25/tiktok-trump-executive-order/) [15](https://www.techspot.com/news/109635-trump-approves-14-billion-tiktok-deal-backed-oracle.html) [16](https://fortune.com/2025/12/19/tiktok-agrees-u-s-joint-venture-deal-with-oracle-silver-lake-and-mgx/) [17](https://enterpriseam.com/uae/2025/12/22/inside-mgxs-investment-in-tiktok/) [40](https://axios.com/2026/01/23/tiktok-deal-trump-app-ban) [41](https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/business/digital/tiktok-us-deal-closes-silver-lake-oracle-1236481939/)

### 6. G42 has a Microsoft partnership, US chip licences and the Stargate UAE campus.

**Mostly accurate**

- Microsoft invested $1.5B for a minority stake on 2024-04-16.
- The deal is governed by an Intergovernmental Assurance Agreement with US and UAE government input, Brad Smith joined G42's board, and G42 reportedly agreed to drop Huawei gear and exit China.
- Stargate UAE, a 1GW G42-built cluster with OpenAI, Oracle, Nvidia, Cisco and SoftBank inside a 5GW US-UAE campus, was announced in May 2025.
- Its first 200MW was due in 2026, and no confirmation that it is online was found.
- Commerce authorized Microsoft's chip transfers to the UAE in Sept 2025, then up to 35,000 GB300-equivalent chips for G42 (and as many for Saudi Arabia's Humain) on about 2025-11-20, with security conditions.
- Later 2026 items: the 2026-07-10 BIS move of the UAE into Country Group A:5 (naming G42 and Core42 for licence-free chip receipt) is documented in C80, including a Senate Banking minority statement.
- The reported G42 reincorporation as a US company remains unconfirmed: beyond a WSJ-relayed report (July 2026), Bloomberg (about 2026-09-04) reported only exploratory talks to sell a US-majority stake or create a US vehicle, driven by the licence-free window that runs to about April 2027; G42 has not confirmed a transaction and no completed restructuring was found through mid-Nov 2026.
- A Bloomberg-cited report that initial permits excluded G42 conflicts with the November approval.
- Amounts: $1.5B
- Dates: 2024-04-16; May 2025; Sept 2025; 2025-11-20; 2026-07-10
- Sources: [24](https://www.cnbc.com/2024/04/16/microsoft-to-invest-1point5-billion-in-emirati-ai-firm-g42-takes-minority-stake.html) [25](https://www.thenationalnews.com/future/technology/2025/11/20/uae-ai-nvidia-chips-us/) [26](https://www.middleeastainews.com/p/us-approves-up-to-70000-advanced) [27](https://agbi.com/analysis/trade/2026/07/us-export-rules-keep-uae-in-ai-technology-slow-lane/) [29](https://gulfnews.com/business/markets/stargate-uae-first-phase-of-200mw-to-complete-by-2026-1.500310364) [28](https://www.tomshardware.com/tech-industry/artificial-intelligence/u-s-finally-grants-nvidia-license-to-ship-ai-gpus-to-uae-500-000-blackwell-gpus-coming-to-the-gulf-region) <https://www.banking.senate.gov/newsroom/minority/senator-warren-statement-on-trump-administrations-removing-export-controls-on-ai-chips-to-uae> <https://thenextweb.com/news/g42-us-ownership-chip-access-export-control-endgame> [42](https://mobileworldlive.com/ai-cloud/g42-weighs-us-ownership-to-secure-chip-access)

### 8. StringZ Holding, linked to Tahnoon, is in WLF's trust bank.

**Mostly accurate**

- OCC Corporate Decision #1385 (preliminary conditional approval, 2026-08-14) lists StringZ Holding RSC (DE) LLC among investors giving passivity commitments, but it does not name StringZ's owner or the size of its stake.
- The WSJ reported on 2026-08-27, citing people familiar, that Tahnoon's group is behind StringZ and that it owns 49% of WLTC Holdings, the bank's parent, with a Trump-family entity holding about 38%.

### 9. ADQ/Lunate invested in Kushner's Affinity Partners.

**Partly accurate**

- Lunate and QIA added $1.5B to Affinity in Dec 2024, bringing AUM to $4.6B, and Kushner names Lunate among Affinity's three largest LPs with PIF and QIA.
- No source shows ADQ itself as an investor.
- The tie to Tahnoon runs through ownership: Lunate is majority-owned by Chimera Investment, which Tahnoon oversees, and it was seeded with ADQ assets while Tahnoon chaired ADQ.
- ADQ was folded into L'IMAD, chaired by Crown Prince Khaled bin Mohamed, in Jan 2026.
- As a private fund raise, this needed no US approval.
- Amounts: $1.5B; $4.6B
- Dates: Dec 2024; Jan 2026
- Sources: [34](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2024-12-20/jared-kushner-s-affinity-gets-1-5-billion-more-from-qatar-abu-dhabi-s-lunate) [35](https://uk.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/kushners-affinity-partners-raises-15-billion-extends-fund-investment-period-to-2029-93CH-3848327) [36](https://circuit.news/2024/06/19/lunates-105-billion-in-assets-make-it-an-overnight-mena-powerhouse/) [37](https://www.semafor.com/article/01/30/2026/abu-dhabi-rolls-263b-sovereign-wealth-fund-adq-into-new-investment-vehicle-limad)

## Denials and context

- Trump said he did not know about the Aryam deal ('my sons are handling that'); the White House says his assets are in a trust run by his children.
- A WLF spokesperson said any claim the Aryam deal had anything to do with the administration's chip actions is '100% false'; spokesman David Wachsman said Trump and Witkoff had no involvement.
- A person familiar with the Aryam investment told the WSJ it did not involve G42 money and was never discussed with Trump (anonymous).
- MGX says it chose USD1 itself and has not invested in WLF (per C07 sourcing).
- The OCC said career staff reviewed the WLTC charter and rejected conflict-of-interest comments; passivity commitments bar StringZ and the Trump entity from controlling the bank.
- Much of the MGX AI portfolio (Stargate, OpenAI, Anthropic, xAI, Aligned) is ordinary large-scale sovereign investing alongside US firms; there is no evidence it required or received favours.
  - The policy-relevant approvals are the chip licences (Commerce/BIS), the TikTok determination (the president) and the bank charter (OCC).
- Disclosure: this write-up was produced with an Anthropic model; Anthropic is one of the MGX portfolio companies listed here.
  - Facts about that investment are taken from Anthropic's own announcements and press coverage.

### Summary

Almost every link in the claim is documented.

- **World Liberty:** Tahnoon-linked vehicles hold 49% of World Liberty Financial (Aryam, signed January 2025). The WSJ reported in August 2026 that they also hold 49% of the holding company behind WLF's OCC trust bank (StringZ).
- **MGX:**
  - It settled its $2B Binance investment in USD1.
  - It is a Stargate equity funder.
  - It holds 15% of the TikTok US joint venture that Trump approved by executive order.
  - It invested in xAI, Anthropic and OpenAI in 2026.
- **G42:** It has Microsoft's $1.5B, Commerce chip authorizations and the Stargate UAE campus. Its head of crypto reportedly advised WLF.

Three points are weaker:

- **"ADQ/Lunate":** Lunate (with QIA) is the documented Affinity investor. ADQ is not.
- **StringZ:** That Tahnoon is behind StringZ rests on anonymous WSJ sourcing. The OCC decision names StringZ but not its owner or stake.
- **G42 in 2026:** Several of G42's 2026 developments are single-sourced.

No quid pro quo has been documented. WLF, the White House and Trump deny any link between the UAE money and US policy.

Research note: the original pages at most news sites were not read directly, and the research ended before the TikTok closing details and the G42 reincorporation report could be re-checked. Findings rely on search-result coverage across several outlets, plus the earlier checks' sourcing.

- That Tahnoon owns StringZ and that its stake is 49% are anonymous WSJ sourcing, not OCC-disclosed facts.
- MGX holds 15% of the TikTok US joint venture. Early reports of a 45% combined Oracle/Silver Lake/MGX share were superseded.
- MGX's exact role in the OpenAI round, and its dollar amounts in the OpenAI, xAI and Anthropic rounds, are undisclosed or inconsistently described.
