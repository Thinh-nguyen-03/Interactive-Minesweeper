# C71: Who actually bought World Liberty tokens: Zhou, Web3Port, Aqua 1 and foreign wallets

**Verdict:** Mostly accurate · medium confidence  
**Origin:** Connection hunt

## Bottom line

Multi-outlet reporting documents the Web3Port ($10M) and Aqua 1 ($100M) WLFI purchases, Web3Port's role in the $38M MOVE dump, WLF's own MOVE buys, and Reuters' finding that 36 of the top 50 WLFI wallets are likely foreign (~$804M).

## What holds up

- Web3Port bought WLFI right after the inauguration
- Web3Port was the market maker behind the $38M MOVE dump and was offboarded by Binance
- Aqua 1 presented as a UAE fund but is unregistered there and was funded from an OKX account
- Aqua 1 is a renamed Web3Port vehicle controlled by Guren 'Bobby' Zhou, an uncharged UK money-laundering suspect (mostly accurate)
- Up to 75% of each post-threshold purchase flowed to the Trump-family DT Marks DEFI LLC (mostly accurate)
- Reuters found 36 of the 50 largest WLFI wallets likely foreign-controlled (~$804M) (mostly accurate)
- DWF Labs, another Dubai-based WLFI buyer, has a reported manipulation history
- No US agency (CFIUS, Treasury/FinCEN, DOJ, SEC) is known to have reviewed these purchases

## What needs correcting

- WLF itself bought MOVE, the day after Web3Port's WLFI purchase, matching a reported token-swap template (partly accurate)
- WLFI was sold to wallets with illicit-actor exposure (partly accurate)
- One relayed summary says Web3Port was 'expelled in 2023'; the documented Binance offboarding was 2025-03-18.
- Aqua 1's announced $100M compares with about $80M seen moving on-chain to WLF (Reuters); the difference is unexplained.
- Caixin's 'blacklisted' description of Zhou has no basis we could verify; Caixin searches returned only the unrelated Qian Zhimin case.

## In more detail

- The NYT ties Aqua 1 to a renamed Web3Port entity and to uncharged UK laundering suspect Bobby Zhou, and reports up to $75M flowed to the Trump-and-sons company.
- The source of the funds is unknown beyond about $90M arriving from an OKX account, and no US agency is known to have reviewed any purchase despite four Senate Democratic letters.
- The link between Web3Port's buy and WLF's MOVE buy is only our own inference.

## Sub-claims

### 1. Web3Port bought WLFI right after the inauguration

**Confirmed** · Evidence: Documented

- On 2025-01-27, Web3Port Foundation announced it had bought 200M WLFI for $10M, calling it a 'long-term partnership' with more purchases planned.
- CB Insights lists co-investors Comma3, KuCoin and Sui.
- Arkham, cited by the NYT, traced about $20M from a Web3Port-controlled wallet that month (see C67).
- The $10M figure is Web3Port's own claim.
- Amounts: $10M; about $20M
- Dates: 2025-01-27
- Sources: [13](https://www.cbinsights.com/company/web3port/financials) [11](https://www.theblock.co/news/business/2025-07-15-aqua1-web3port-world-liberty-362612) [1](https://theblock.co/news/regulation/2026-08-09-world-liberty-received-100-million-from-businessman-investigated-for-money-laundering-nyt-411221)

### 2. Web3Port was the market maker behind the $38M MOVE dump and was offboarded by Binance

**Confirmed** · Evidence: Documented

- About a day after MOVE listed (2024-12-09/10), wallets tied to Rentech and Web3Port sold about 66M MOVE (about $38M), and the price fell about 47%.
- On 2025-03-18, Binance offboarded the unnamed market maker and froze the proceeds; Movement turned them into a $38M buyback.
- ZachXBT (via Blockworks) and CoinDesk named Web3Port, which did not comment.
- CoinDesk reported that the Rentech contract lent about half of MOVE's circulating supply to one counterparty.
- Rentech denies misrepresentation.
- Co-founder Rushi Manche was suspended, then ousted.
- One relayed summary dates Web3Port's 'expulsion' to 2023; the documented exchange action is from March 2025.
- Amounts: about $38M; about 47%; $38M
- Dates: 2024-12-09; 2025-03-18; March 2025
- Sources: [14](https://blockworks.co/news/binance-movement-market-maker-investigation) [15](https://www.theblock.co/amp/post/347931/binance-move-market-maker-movement-38-million-usdt-buyback-program) [16](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/binance-offboards-market-maker-said-100720331.html) [17](https://decrypt.co/317524/movement-labs-suspends-co-founder-amid-market-maker-controversy?amp=1) [18](https://rekt.news/all-the-wrong-moves) [19](https://crypto.news/wlfi-linked-move-and-web3port-implicated-in-38m-token-dump-scandal/)

### 3. WLF itself bought MOVE, the day after Web3Port's WLFI purchase, matching a reported token-swap template

**Partly accurate**

- Purchases documented; the link is inferred.
- On 2025-01-28, Arkham recorded WLF buying about 2.4M MOVE (about $1.85-2M) via CoW Swap, about two hours before Movement's mainnet announcement.
- WLF bought more in February 2025, and later transfers to Ceffu show a loss of about 60-70%.
- Blockworks (Feb 2025) reported that WLF pitched protocols a deal: buy $10M of WLFI plus a 10% fee, and WLF buys the protocol's token.
- Tron and Movement denied any swap, and Manche denied coordination.
- Our own observation: MOVE's market maker bought exactly $10M of WLFI one day before WLF bought MOVE.
- No source asserts that the two purchases were linked.
- Amounts: about $1.85-2M; about 60-70%; $10M; 10%
- Dates: 2025-01-28; February 2025; Feb 2025
- Sources: [20](https://www.theblock.co/post/337497) [21](https://info.arkm.com/research/wlf-purchases-1-85m-move) [22](https://www.theblock.co/post/340838/trump-backed-world-liberty-financial-buys-more-wbtc-move-tokens) [23](https://www.theblock.co/news/markets/2025-01-28-i-dont-think-anything-was-coordinated-movement-labs-rushi-manche-insider-trading-allegations-trump-world-liberty-337575) [24](https://dev.coindesk.com/markets/2025/02/04/tron-move-deny-token-swap-deal-for-world-liberty-fi-inclusion)

### 4. Aqua 1 presented as a UAE fund but is unregistered there and was funded from an OKX account

**Confirmed** · Evidence: Documented, mainly Reuters (2025-07-11)

- ADGM said Aqua 1 'is not registered, licensed, or affiliated' with it.
- The website dates from late May 2025, and the founder 'Dave Lee' has little public footprint.
- The wallet aqua1.eth received about $90M from an OKX account between March and June 2025; its only large outflows were about $80M in two transfers to WLF.
- The gap between that and the $100M headline is unexplained.
- Cryptopolitan found no UAE SCA licence.
- The September 2026 Senate letter repeats the OKX point and notes OKX's guilty plea to US anti-money-laundering violations.
- Amounts: about $90M; about $80M; $100M
- Dates: 2025-07-11; late May 2025; June 2025; September 2026
- Sources: [8](https://whbl.com/2025/07/11/trumps-100-million-crypto-mystery-man/) [41](https://media.dealstreetasia.com/stories/trump-crypto-mystery-man-449016) [10](https://www.cryptopolitan.com/who-is-trumps-100-million-crypto-mystery-man/) [37](https://www.banking.senate.gov/imo/media/doc/20260915lettertobessentandblancheonaqua1.pdf)

### 5. Aqua 1 is a renamed Web3Port vehicle controlled by Guren 'Bobby' Zhou, an uncharged UK money-laundering suspect

**Mostly accurate**

- Evidence grade: the purchases and records are documented; the identification is single-source (the NYT, 2026-08-09, relayed by six or more outlets).
- A BVI Web3Port entity became Aqua 1 GP Limited about two weeks before the 2025-06-26 announcement.
- Zhou was arrested in the UK in 2021 and is uncharged; officials said the case was still open in late July 2026.
- A Feb 2024 Court of Appeal judgment confirms he was released under investigation, and a Nov 2025 filing alleges a laundering scheme with five others dating from 2019.
- Two employees were reportedly charged in Sept 2025 (single-source).
- He was a director of about 13 UK flooring companies (2008-2019).
- His Caduceus project (about $7.6M raised) falsely claimed backing from China Merchants Securities UK and the 'Bin Zayed Group'; both denied it.
- The source of the funds is unknown.
- Caixin's 'blacklisted' label is unverified.
- Aqua 1 and Lee denied Web3Port ties in July 2025, and Zhou did not respond.
- Amounts: about $7.6M
- Dates: 2026-08-09; 2025-06-26; late July 2026; Feb 2024; Nov 2025
- Sources: [1](https://theblock.co/news/regulation/2026-08-09-world-liberty-received-100-million-from-businessman-investigated-for-money-laundering-nyt-411221) [2](https://cointelegraph.com/news/world-liberty-financial-uk-money-laundering-donald-trump) [3](https://unchainedcrypto.com/world-libertys-100-million-aqua-1-buyer-tied-to-active-uk-money-laundering-probe/) [4](https://crypto.news/world-libertys-100m-wlfi-buyer-linked-to-uk-money-laundering-probe/) [5](https://www.ibtimes.co.uk/trump-crypto-venture-100m-investment-1813193) [6](https://cryip.co/uk-court-records-dissolved-companies-100m-wlfi-buyer/) [7](https://news.bitcoin.com/wlfi-100-million-bobby-zhou-money-laundering/) [11](https://www.theblock.co/news/business/2025-07-15-aqua1-web3port-world-liberty-362612) [40](https://www.caixinglobal.com/2026-08-26/the-blacklisted-chinese-businessman-behind-a-100-million-trump-crypto-bet-102478200.html) [42](https://www.thedailybeast.com/trump-crypto-business-got-millions-from-chinese-tycoon-under-investigation)

### 6. Up to 75% of each post-threshold purchase flowed to the Trump-family DT Marks DEFI LLC

**Mostly accurate**

- The rule is documented (Gold Paper, see C42): 75% of net revenue after a $30M reserve and expenses.
- The NYT reports that up to $75M of Aqua 1's $100M went to the company controlled by Trump and his sons, with Witkoff family affiliates also benefiting (single-source as relayed; the split is unclear).
- For Web3Port ($10M → up to about $7.5M), DWF ($25M → up to about $18.75M) and Sun's January 2025 $45M (→ up to about $33.75M), the figures are our inferred gross ceilings before expenses.
- No source publishes per-buyer payouts.
- Amounts: 75%; $30M; up to $75M; $100M; $10M
- Dates: January 2025
- Sources: [1](https://theblock.co/news/regulation/2026-08-09-world-liberty-received-100-million-from-businessman-investigated-for-money-laundering-nyt-411221) [2](https://cointelegraph.com/news/world-liberty-financial-uk-money-laundering-donald-trump) [33](https://www.banking.senate.gov/newsroom/minority/as-congress-considers-potential-market-structure-legislation-warren-and-reed-press-doj-and-treasury-on-national-security-risks-posed-by-trump-affiliated-crypto-ventures-sale-of-tokens-to-illicit-actors)

### 7. Reuters found 36 of the 50 largest WLFI wallets likely foreign-controlled (~$804M)

**Mostly accurate**

- Single-source analysis (Reuters special report, 2025-10-28), known here via Cointelegraph and Cryptopolitan.
- 36 of the top 50 wallets were likely overseas, holding about $804M. Only 4 were US-linked, including ALT5 Sigma (about $781M) and a WLF security adviser (about $35M); 10 were unclassified.
- More than a dozen experts reviewed the analysis and called it reasonable.
- WLF's lawyer called it inaccurate and misleading.
- Attribution is probabilistic, and foreign predominance is expected under the Reg S sale design.
- Amounts: about $804M; about $781M; about $35M
- Dates: 2025-10-28
- Sources: [25](https://www.dailymaverick.co.za/article/2025-10-28-special-report-inside-the-trump-familys-global-crypto-cash-machine/) [26](https://cointelegraph.com/news/how-the-trump-family-turned-crypto-into-an-800m-gold-rush) [27](https://www.cryptopolitan.com/global-wallets-help-build-trump-familys-1b/)

### 8. DWF Labs, another Dubai-based WLFI buyer, has a reported manipulation history

**Confirmed** · Evidence: Documented reporting, with the allegation disputed

- DWF bought $25M of WLFI on 2025-04-16 (C67).
- The WSJ (May 2024) reported that Binance surveillance staff found about $300M of DWF wash trading in 2023; Binance deemed the evidence insufficient, and the investigator was let go.
- DWF and Grachev deny the allegations.
- Any link between DWF and Web3Port is inferred co-occurrence only.
- Amounts: $25M; about $300M
- Dates: 2025-04-16; May 2024
- Sources: [28](https://www.theblock.co/news/business/2024-05-09-dwf-labs-denies-report-that-it-did-300-million-of-wash-trading-on-binance-last-year-293429) [29](https://hotfix.coindesk.com/business/2024/05/09/binance-fired-investigator-who-uncovered-market-manipulation-at-client-dwf-labs-wsj)

### 9. No US agency (CFIUS, Treasury/FinCEN, DOJ, SEC) is known to have reviewed these purchases

**Confirmed**

- Evidence grade: a documented absence of any public review, and documented requests for one.
- WLF sold under Reg D (accredited US buyers) and Reg S (non-US buyers), with US sales capped and about 90% aimed abroad.
- WLF says all of more than 85,000 participants passed KYC, and its spokesman says the company 'meets or exceeds industry standards'.
- Senate Democrats wrote four times: Warren/Reed to DOJ and Treasury on 2025-11-18 (illicit wallets); Warren/Kim to Bessent on 2026-02-13 (CFIUS review of Aryam); Warren/Reed to Bessent and Blanche on 2026-05-14 (AB partner and Prince Group-sanctioned people); and about 2026-09-15 on Aqua 1, Zhou and OKX.
- No public reply or opened inquiry was found.
- That CFIUS may lack reach over governance-token purchases is our own inferred analysis.
- Amounts: about 90%
- Dates: 2025-11-18; 2026-02-13; 2026-05-14; 2026-09-15
- Sources: [30](https://www.theblock.co/news/defi/2024-09-30-trump-backed-world-liberty-financial-opens-up-kyc-verification-on-website-318680) [31](https://cryptoslate.com/donald-trumps-world-liberty-will-limit-token-sales-in-the-us-to-30-million/) [32](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20250317855322/en/Trump-Inspired-World-Liberty-Financial-Closes-$550-Million-Across-Token-Sales) [33](https://www.banking.senate.gov/newsroom/minority/as-congress-considers-potential-market-structure-legislation-warren-and-reed-press-doj-and-treasury-on-national-security-risks-posed-by-trump-affiliated-crypto-ventures-sale-of-tokens-to-illicit-actors) [34](https://www.theblock.co/post/389958/sens-warren-kim-demand-cfius-review-of-uaes-reported-500-million-stake-in-world-liberty-financial-reuters) [35](https://www.banking.senate.gov/newsroom/minority/ahead-of-committee-crypto-market-structure-markup-warren-and-reed-call-for-investigation-into-world-liberty-financials-latest-apparent-failure-to-detect-ties-to-sanctioned-criminals) [37](https://www.banking.senate.gov/imo/media/doc/20260915lettertobessentandblancheonaqua1.pdf)

### 10. WLFI was sold to wallets with illicit-actor exposure

**Partly accurate**

- Accountable.US (Sept 2025) reported WLFI sales to wallets that had touched a Lazarus-labelled address, Nobitex (Iran), a ruble platform, and Tornado Cash (at least 62 users).
- Researcher Nick Bax showed the 'Lazarus' link came from a joke Dream Cash airdrop, and a falsely flagged user had about $95K of WLFI frozen.
- The Nobitex and Tornado Cash items were not rebutted in what we found.
- Evidence grade: single-source (watchdog), partly rebutted.
- Amounts: about $95K
- Dates: Sept 2025
- Sources: [38](https://cointelegraph.com/news/senators-trump-linked-wlfi-national-security-threat) [39](https://unhashed.beehiiv.com/p/did-trump-s-token-reach-enemies) [33](https://www.banking.senate.gov/newsroom/minority/as-congress-considers-potential-market-structure-legislation-warren-and-reed-press-doj-and-treasury-on-national-security-risks-posed-by-trump-affiliated-crypto-ventures-sale-of-tokens-to-illicit-actors)

## Denials and context

- WLF says it follows all laws and runs an industry-standard KYC program, and called Reuters' analysis inaccurate and misleading.
- Aqua 1 and Dave Lee denied Web3Port ties (July 2025); Zhou did not respond to the NYT and is uncharged.
- Movement Labs and Tron deny any token-swap deal; Manche denies insider coordination; Rentech denies misrepresentation.
- DWF Labs and Grachev deny the wash-trading allegations; Binance said they were not fully substantiated.
- Reg S made non-US buyers lawful and expected; wallet attribution is probabilistic.
- Accountable.US's Lazarus link was rebutted as a joke-airdrop artifact.

### Connections (sub-claims)

#### 1. Web3Port bought WLFI the week after the inauguration (documented)
On 2025-01-27, Web3Port Foundation announced it had bought 200M WLFI for $10M. It called the deal a "long-term partnership" and said more purchases were planned. CB Insights logs it as a round alongside Comma3 Ventures, KuCoin and Sui. Arkham, as cited by the NYT, traced about $20M from a Web3Port-controlled wallet that month (relayed in C67). The $10M figure is Web3Port's own claim.

#### 2. Web3Port was the MOVE market maker behind the $38M dump, and Binance offboarded it (documented)
- **The dump:** MOVE listed on 2024-12-09/10. Within about a day, wallets tied to Web3Port and an intermediary called Rentech sold roughly $38M of MOVE (about 66M tokens). The price fell by about 47%.
- **Binance's action:** On 2025-03-18, Binance offboarded the unnamed market maker and froze the proceeds. Movement turned the recovered funds into a $38M USDT buyback.
- **Naming Web3Port:** ZachXBT (via Blockworks) and then CoinDesk named Web3Port as the market maker. Web3Port did not respond to requests for comment.
- **The Rentech contract:** CoinDesk reported that the deal effectively lent about half of MOVE's circulating supply to one counterparty. Movement said it was "led to believe" Rentech was a Web3Port subsidiary. Rentech denies misleading anyone.
- **Fallout:** Co-founder Rushi Manche was suspended, then ousted. Coinbase suspended MOVE trading on 2025-05-15.
- **Correction:** One relayed summary says Web3Port was "expelled in 2023". The documented exchange action is from March 2025. Treat "2023" as an error unless a source shows otherwise.

#### 3. WLF bought MOVE the day after Web3Port's WLFI buy (purchases documented; link inferred)
- **WLF's MOVE buys:** On 2025-01-28, Arkham recorded WLF buying about 2.4M MOVE (about $1.85-2M) through CoW Swap, about two hours before Movement announced its developer mainnet. WLF bought more MOVE in February 2025. Arkham later tracked MOVE moving to the custodian Ceffu at a 60-70% loss.
- **Movement's denials:** Manche denied any coordination or insider trading.
- **The swap-deal report:** In February 2025, Blockworks reported that WLF representatives pitched protocols a deal: the protocol buys $10M of WLFI plus a 10% fee, and WLF buys the same amount of the protocol's token. Tron and Movement denied any such swap.
- **Our cross-reference (inferred):** MOVE's market maker bought exactly $10M of WLFI on the 27th, and WLF bought MOVE on the 28th. This matches the reported swap template. **No source asserts that Web3Port's WLFI purchase and WLF's MOVE purchase were linked.** Movement denies a swap, and no one has shown Web3Port acting for Movement in the WLFI purchase.

#### 4. Aqua 1: a "UAE fund" with no UAE registration, funded through OKX (documented, mostly Reuters)
Reuters ("Trump's $100 million crypto mystery man", 2025-07-11) reported the following:
- Abu Dhabi Global Market said Aqua 1 "is not registered, licensed, or affiliated" with it.
- The website was created in late May 2025.
- The founder, "Dave Lee", had little public footprint.
- The wallet tagged aqua1.eth received about $90M from an OKX account between March and June 2025. Its only large outflows were two transfers totalling about $80M to WLF.

Cryptopolitan found no UAE Securities and Commodities Authority licence for Aqua 1. The $100M headline figure versus about $80M seen on-chain has not been reconciled publicly. The September 2026 Senate letter repeats the OKX funding point and notes that OKX pleaded guilty to US anti-money-laundering violations.

#### 5. Aqua 1 is Web3Port under a new name, run by Zhou (documented reporting; identification rests on the NYT)
The NYT story ran on 2026-08-09, headlined "Trump Crypto Took $100 Million From a Businessman With Red Flags". It was relayed by The Block, Cointelegraph, Unchained, crypto.news, IBTimes UK and Bitcoin.com. Its findings:
- **The rename:** A BVI Web3Port entity renamed itself Aqua 1 GP Limited about two weeks before the 2025-06-26 announcement.
- **Zhou's arrest:** Zhou was arrested in Britain in 2021 on suspicion of money laundering. He has not been charged, and UK officials said the case was still open in late July 2026.
- **Court records:** A February 2024 Court of Appeal judgment confirms that he was released under investigation. A filing in November 2025 accuses him, with five others, of a laundering scheme dating from 2019.
- **Employees charged:** Two longtime employees were reportedly charged in September 2025 (single-source).
- **Earlier career:** Zhou was a director of about 13 UK flooring companies between 2008 and 2019, most now dissolved. His Caduceus project advertised backing from China Merchants Securities UK and the "Bin Zayed Group". Both told the Times those claims were false and unauthorized.
- **Source of funds:** The NYT could not determine where the $100M came from.

Denials: Aqua 1 and Lee denied any Web3Port tie in July 2025. Zhou did not respond to the NYT's many messages. Caixin (2026-08-26) calls Zhou "blacklisted", but its text and the basis for that word remain unverified. Searches of Caixin turned up only an unrelated London bitcoin-laundering case, the Qian Zhimin case.

#### 6. How much of each purchase reached Trump-family entities (rule documented; per-purchase amounts mostly our arithmetic)
**The rule:** The Gold Paper (C42) gives DT Marks DEFI LLC 75% of net revenue, including token sales, after a $30M reserve and expenses. Sun's November 2024 purchase pushed sales past that threshold (C04).

**Aqua 1:** The NYT reports up to **$75M** of Aqua 1's $100M went to the Trump-and-sons company, with the Witkoff family also benefiting. This is single-source as relayed. One outlet folds the Witkoff affiliates into the $75M, so the split is unclear.

**Other purchases (our arithmetic, inferred):** Applying the same 75% to the other post-threshold purchases gives these gross ceilings before expenses:

| Buyer | Purchase | Up to (to DT Marks DEFI) |
|---|---|---|
| Web3Port | $10M | about $7.5M |
| DWF Labs | $25M | about $18.75M |
| Justin Sun | $45M (Jan 2025 tranche) | about $33.75M |

No source has published per-buyer payouts for these. Trump's 2025 disclosure and C42 give only the totals.

#### 7. Reuters: 36 of the 50 largest WLFI wallets are likely foreign (single-source analysis, relayed)
Reuters' special report "Inside the Trump family's global crypto cash machine" (2025-10-28) included a wallet review, as relayed by Cointelegraph and Cryptopolitan:
- **Foreign wallets:** 36 of the 50 largest WLFI wallets were likely controlled by overseas buyers, holding about **$804M**.
- **US wallets:** Only 4 were US-linked. These include ALT5 Sigma (about $781M) and a WLF security adviser (about $35M).
- **Unknown:** 10 could not be classified.
- **Wider findings:** Reuters put Trump-family crypto income at more than $800M in the first half of 2025. More than a dozen experts reviewed the analysis and called it reasonable.

WLF's lawyer called the valuation and income analysis "inaccurate and misleading". Attribution of wallets is probabilistic. Foreign predominance is also built into the sale design (see 9).

#### 8. DWF Labs: another Dubai-based buyer with a manipulation record (documented)
- **The purchase:** DWF bought $25M of WLFI on 2025-04-16 and committed USD1 liquidity (C67).
- **The WSJ report:** In May 2024, the WSJ reported that Binance surveillance staff concluded DWF had done about $300M of wash trading in 2023. Binance found the evidence insufficient, and the lead investigator was let go.
- **Denials:** DWF and Andrei Grachev call the allegations unfounded. Binance says the claims were not "fully substantiated".
- **Inferred overlap:** Web3Port and DWF were both market makers that had been in trouble at Binance, and both bought WLFI in early 2025. No source links the two firms.

#### 9. Has any US agency reviewed these purchases? None publicly (documented absence; requests documented)
**WLF's sale design:** The sale ran under Reg D for accredited US buyers and Reg S for non-US buyers. US sales were capped, and roughly 90% of tokens were aimed abroad. WLF says all buyers passed KYC, and more than 85,000 participants were screened. WLF's spokesman David Wachsman told the NYT that the company "maintains a compliance program that meets or exceeds industry standards" and follows all laws.
