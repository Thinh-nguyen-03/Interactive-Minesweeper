# C69: Master timeline: money events versus government actions

**Verdict:** Partly accurate · medium confidence  
**Origin:** Connection hunt

## Bottom line

We built a 277-event master timeline from the 81 claim files plus 19 verified additions (data/timeline.json).

## What holds up

- Method: a script over all 81 claim files extracted 489 dated events (397 from relationship edges, 92 from dated sentences in sub-claim findings) and found 51 money/official pairs sharing a counterparty within 45 days; after hand review and a report cross-check, nearly all were either already connected by the original reports or artefacts.
- Microsoft: Commerce approved its UAE Nvidia-chip export licences in September 2025, roughly 2-6 weeks before Microsoft appeared among ballroom donors at the 15 Oct 2025 East Room dinner (list published 23 Oct); a $10M amount was later reported.
- Lockheed Martin: the Pentagon definitized a $12.5B F-35 Lot 18-19 contract on 29 Sep 2025, 16 days before the ballroom donor dinner; the Saudi F-35 sale was announced 18 Nov 2025, 34 days after it. Lockheed's gift was later reported as $10M.
- Alphabet/Google: GSA announced the 'Gemini for Government' OneGov deal on 21-22 Aug 2025, 39 days before YouTube agreed (29 Sep 2025) to pay $24.5M to settle Trump's suit, $22M of it to the ballroom.
- Pakistan: US EXIM's $1.25B financing for the Reko Diq mine (approval reported 28 Nov 2025, announced 10 Dec 2025) came 35-47 days before Pakistan's government signed a USD1 payments MoU (14 Jan 2026) with SC Financial Technologies, the Delaware co-owner of the USD1 brand. Zach Witkoff signed for SC Financial.
- PIF: the EA take-private (PIF about 93.4%, Affinity about 1.1%) cleared its last regulatory sign-offs, reportedly including CFIUS, by 30 Jul 2026 and closed on 4 Aug 2026. Two to seven days later, PIF-funded LIV Golf played LIV Golf New York at Trump National Bedminster (6-9 Aug 2026). The US-Saudi 123 agreement was signed on 22 Jul 2026, 15 days earlier.
- Qatar: Affinity's reported $5B+ solicitation of Gulf funds including QIA (NYT, Mar 2026) was followed on 1 May 2026 by a $4.01B Patriot sale to Qatar under an emergency waiver of congressional review.
- Ripple and Palantir, both named ballroom donors on 23 Oct 2025, received federal approvals or contracts just outside the 45-day window: Ripple's conditional OCC trust charter on 12 Dec 2025 (50 days) and Palantir's ShipOS deal of up to $448M on 9 Dec 2025 (47 days).
- Nvidia's $25M anonymous ballroom commitment is dated October 2025 in the WaPo spreadsheet. Commerce approved Nvidia-chip sales to G42 and Humain on 19 Nov 2025 (about 19-49 days later), and Trump allowed H200 sales to China on 8 Dec 2025.
- Binance: 49 days after CZ's pardon (23 Oct 2025), Binance moved its BUSD-backed B-Token reserves into USD1 (11 Dec 2025).
- Re-verified tight pairs that the original reports had already connected: Justin Sun's $45M WLFI purchase (20 Jan 2025) followed 37 days later by the SEC's joint stay request (26 Feb 2025); and the CFTC's joint motion to vacate Gemini's consent order (27-28 May 2026) followed 22-23 days later by the Winklevoss twins' BTC gifts to MAGA Inc (19 Jun 2026).
- Cases where timing cuts against a favour reading: Amazon was on the ballroom donor list three to four weeks after agreeing to pay a $2.5B FTC penalty (25 Sep 2025); Charles Ergen's $25M ballroom commitment (Dec 2025 and Feb 2026) was followed by an FCC approval (12 May 2026) that imposed an 'unprecedented' $2.4B escrow condition on EchoStar.

## What needs correcting

- The heuristic 'same counterparty within 45 days' mostly re-finds pairs the reports already made. The new findings rest on manual cross-referencing, and the gift dates for most ballroom donors are not public: only the 15 Oct dinner and the 23 Oct 2025 list are, so day gaps for those pairs are measured from the list, not from the payment.
- Microsoft's licence approval is dated only to 'September 2025', so its gap is a range (about 14-44 days to the dinner).

## In more detail

- Most close pairs of a money event and an official act involving the same counterparty within 45 days had already been connected by the individual reports (Sun/SEC, MGX/Binance, Paramount/FCC, Winklevoss/CFTC).
- The new pairs come mainly from dating the October 2025 ballroom donor list against federal actions: Microsoft's UAE chip licences, Lockheed's F-35 contract, Google's OneGov deal, plus Pakistan's EXIM loan followed by the USD1 MoU and the EA/CFIUS clearance followed by LIV at Bedminster.
- All are timing proximity only, graded inferred, and several have documented innocent explanations or timing that cuts against a favour reading.
- No source we found asserts that any of the new pairs is linked.

## Connections searched for

> Connection hunt by data analysis: from every `data/claims/*.json` (relationships with dates, sub-claim findings) and research report, build one dated master timeline of (a) money or benefits flowing to Trump-family-linked entities and (b) official acts (dismissals, pardons, approvals, licences, contracts, appointments, executive orders, sales). Write it to the report as a table, then identify pairs involving the same counterparty within 45 days of each other that the individual reports did not connect, verify the dates of the 15 closest pairs with fresh searches, and present them graded as `inferred` timing proximity unless a source asserts a link. Also list proximities that look suspicious but have an innocent documented explanation.

## Sub-claims

### 1. Method: a script over all 81 claim files extracted 489 dated events (397 from relationship edges, 92 from dated sentences in sub-claim findings) and found 51 money/official pairs sharing a counterparty within 45 days; after hand review and a report cross-check, nearly all were either already connected by the original reports or artefacts.

**Confirmed** · Evidence: Inferred (our own analysis)

- Of 51 candidate pairs, 47 crossed claim files, but a date-co-occurrence check showed 47 were already discussed together in at least one report (e.g. Justin Sun/SEC stay in C05, MGX-USD1/SEC Binance dismissal in C09, Paramount/FCC in C33, Winklevoss/CFTC in C43).
- The 4 the heuristic flagged as new were artefacts (a settlement paired with itself, a court scheduling order, a duplicate pardon date).

### 2. Microsoft: Commerce approved its UAE Nvidia-chip export licences in September 2025, roughly 2-6 weeks before Microsoft appeared among ballroom donors at the 15 Oct 2025 East Room dinner (list published 23 Oct); a $10M amount was later reported.

**Confirmed** · Evidence: Inferred timing proximity; no source we found links the licence to the donation

- Dates: Brad Smith disclosed on 2025-11-03 that the licences (about 60,400 A100-equivalents including GB300s) were approved in September 2025; the exact day is not public, so the gap to the 15 Oct dinner is between about 14 and 44 days.
- Microsoft was on the White House's 23 Oct 2025 list (CNBC, NBC); WaPo-sourced reporting puts its gift at $10M, but the date of the gift itself is not public.
- Context that cuts the other way: on 2025-09-02 Microsoft gave GSA a OneGov discount worth an estimated $3.1B in year one (a concession to the government, not a benefit to Microsoft), and on 2025-09-26 Trump publicly demanded Microsoft fire Lisa Monaco (C68).
- Microsoft framed the licences as the result of stricter safeguards; G42, which Microsoft partly owns, still had to wait until 2025-11-19.
- Amounts: $10M; an estimated $3.1B
- Dates: 2025-11-03; September 2025; 15 Oct; 23 Oct 2025; 2025-09-02
- Sources: [1](https://www.cnbc.com/2025/11/03/nvidia-shares-rise-after-us-approves-chip-sales-to-the-uae.html) [2](https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/microsoft-becomes-first-company-granted-export-license-by-us-govt-to-ship-nvidia-gpus-to-uae/) [3](https://www.cnbc.com/2025/10/23/trump-white-house-east-wing-ballroom-donors-apple-amazon-meta-microsoft-google.html) [5](https://thehill.com/homenews/administration/6152301-white-house-ballroom-private-donations/amp/) [10](https://www.gsa.gov/about-us/newsroom/news-releases/multibillion-dollar-gsa-onegov-agreement-with-microsoft-brings-steep-discounts-09022025)

### 3. Lockheed Martin: the Pentagon definitized a $12.5B F-35 Lot 18-19 contract on 29 Sep 2025, 16 days before the ballroom donor dinner; the Saudi F-35 sale was announced 18 Nov 2025, 34 days after it. Lockheed's gift was later reported as $10M.

**Confirmed** · Evidence: Inferred; listed under documented innocent explanations

- The 29 Sep modification finalized a handshake deal announced on 2024-11-25 under the Biden administration and had to be signed before the 30 Sep end of the fiscal year.
- Lockheed has been the F-35 prime contractor for decades, so some contract action falls inside any 45-day window.
- The Saudi F-35 request predates both (C56), and as of Nov 2026 the sale was a 17 Sep 2026 congressional notification, not a contract.
- Lockheed's presence on the 23 Oct 2025 list is documented; the $10M figure is from WaPo via The Hill.
- Amounts: $10M
- Dates: 29 Sep; 2024-11-25; 30 Sep; Nov 2026; 17 Sep 2026
- Sources: [6](https://www.defensenews.com/air/2025/09/30/pentagon-awards-contract-for-newest-f-35s/) [7](https://breakingdefense.com/2025/09/lockheed-pentagon-finalize-deal-for-296-f-35s/) [8](https://defensenews.com/air/2024/11/25/pentagon-lockheed-reach-handshake-deal-for-next-f-35s) [3](https://www.cnbc.com/2025/10/23/trump-white-house-east-wing-ballroom-donors-apple-amazon-meta-microsoft-google.html) [5](https://thehill.com/homenews/administration/6152301-white-house-ballroom-private-donations/amp/)

### 4. Alphabet/Google: GSA announced the 'Gemini for Government' OneGov deal on 21-22 Aug 2025, 39 days before YouTube agreed (29 Sep 2025) to pay $24.5M to settle Trump's suit, $22M of it to the ballroom.

**Confirmed** · Evidence: Inferred; documented innocent explanation

- The OneGov deal is a price concession to the government ($0.47 per agency), on terms GSA was also striking with OpenAI, Anthropic, Microsoft and AWS around the same time.
- It does not benefit Google the way a licence or a dismissal would.
- The YouTube settlement ended a suit Trump filed in 2021.
- No source links the two.
- Amounts: $0.47
- Sources: [9](https://www.nextgov.com/acquisition/2025/08/gsa-google-reach-agreement-gemini-availability-and-pricing/407586/) [11](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/youtube-settles-trump-lawsuit-white-house-ballroom) [10](https://www.gsa.gov/about-us/newsroom/news-releases/multibillion-dollar-gsa-onegov-agreement-with-microsoft-brings-steep-discounts-09022025)

## Denials and context

- Nvidia says its ballroom support is independent of business interests.
  - Binance says USD1 integration is unrelated to the pardon.
  - WLF says it is not compensated under the Pakistan MoU.
  - The Trust for the National Mall says anonymous giving is ordinary nonprofit practice.
- Many of the official acts are routine or generic: OneGov discounts went to many vendors, the Ripple charter came in a batch of five, the Lockheed F-35 deal was agreed in Nov 2024, the Qatar emergency arms sales also covered Kuwait, Israel and the UAE, and LIV's Bedminster date was set in January 2026.
- Timing proximity is not evidence of causation, and this report does not claim it.

## Full report

**Verdict: partly-accurate (confidence: medium).** The brief asked for new money-to-official-act proximities. We found a handful that the individual reports had not paired. All of them are timing proximity only (`inferred`), and no source asserts a link for any of them. Most of the tightest pairs in the dataset had already been connected by earlier reports. Several new pairs have a documented innocent explanation, and some cut against a favour reading. **Nothing here shows causation, and this report does not claim it.**

### Method

1. A script (kept outside the repo) loaded all 81 `data/claims/*.json` files. It extracted every dated relationship, plus every dated sentence in sub-claim findings (ISO dates, "Mar 27, 2025", "27 March 2025"). It then sorted each event into one of two kinds:
   - **money**: a payment, investment, purchase, donation, licence fee or settlement flowing to a Trump-family-linked entity or associate (WLF, TMTG, Trump Organization, DT Marks entities, the library foundation, the ballroom, MAGA Inc., inaugural committees and so on);
   - **official**: an act by a US government body or Trump-administration official (dismissal, pardon, approval, licence, charter, contract, loan, appointment, executive order, sale, waiver).
2. Counterparties were normalised by entity id. They were expanded one hop through ownership edges (parent/owner and subsidiary) and through a manual sovereign map: MGX, G42 and Aryam to the UAE; QIA and Qatari Diar to Qatar; PIF, LIV and Humain to Saudi Arabia.
3. Result: **489 events** (397 from edges, 92 from text) and **51 candidate pairs** within 45 days, 47 of them across claim files. A check for whether both dates appear in one report showed 47 of the 51 were already discussed together. The 4 left over were artefacts: a settlement paired with itself, a court scheduling order, and duplicate pardon dates. Other artefacts discarded by hand: ADGM's statement on Aqua 1 (not a US act, not a favour), Cantor's TMTG advisory role paired with an EXIM letter to Critical Metals (wrong money direction), and Trump's OGE disclosure paired with DOJ's Paramount clearance (a disclosure is not a payment).
4. The automated pass mostly re-found known pairs, so we cross-referenced by hand. The main gap was that no report had dated the **October 2025 ballroom donor list** against federal actions. We then used about 20 searches to verify dates for 13 pairs.

The master timeline below has **277 events**: 258 from claim edges, collapsed across duplicates, plus 19 verified additions. It is saved as `data/timeline.json`. Grades are a heuristic: `single-source` where the edge label cites one outlet, a leak or anonymous sourcing, `inferred` where the label says so, and `documented` otherwise. Month-precision dates are shown as YYYY-MM.
