# C19: Strategic Bitcoin Reserve, TMTG treasury, Cumberland/Don Wilson

**Verdict:** Mostly accurate (confidence: medium)

## What the video said

> Trump signed an executive order creating a Strategic Bitcoin Reserve; around then Trump Media & Technology Group (TMTG) announced a $2.5B raise for its own bitcoin treasury; one investor was Don Wilson (DRW), whose crypto firm Cumberland had an SEC enforcement case dismissed with prejudice weeks earlier; Cumberland denies any connection.

## Follow-up checks

## Summary

Trump signed EO 14233 creating a Strategic Bitcoin Reserve on 2025-03-06, and the SEC dismissed its Cumberland DRW case with prejudice on 2025-03-27. TMTG announced a ~$2.5B bitcoin-treasury raise on 2025-05-27, which closed at ~$2.44B gross. DRW Investments (Don Wilson's firm) bought ~4M TMTG shares (~$100M) as part of it. The timing is loose ('around that time' is ~12 weeks), the investor was DRW rather than Wilson personally, and the dismissal was agreed in principle on 2025-02-20, before the TMTG deal was public. No quid pro quo is documented beyond the sequence of events.

## Full report

**Verdict: mostly accurate (confidence: medium-high).** The core facts hold up: the executive order, the TMTG raise of about $2.5B, DRW's roughly $100M purchase, and the SEC dismissing the Cumberland case with prejudice about nine weeks earlier. Four points need fixing. "Around that time" stretches about 12 weeks. The investor was DRW's investment arm, not Wilson personally. The deal closed at $2.44B gross, not $2.5B. The settlement was agreed in principle on 2025-02-20, months before the TMTG deal existed. Nothing documented connects the two events beyond their timing.

### What the video says (transcript lines 611-617)
- The government announced a Strategic Bitcoin Reserve, and Trump signed an executive order creating it.
- "Around that time", TMTG said it was setting up its own bitcoin reserve with $2.5 billion raised from investors.
- Don Wilson is one of those TMTG investors. "Weeks prior", the SEC enforcement case against his crypto company, Cumberland, had been dismissed with prejudice.
- When Coffeezilla asked for comment, Cumberland denied any quid pro quo or connection between the Trump Media investment and the SEC's dismissal.

### Evidence by sub-claim

1. **Executive order creating a Strategic Bitcoin Reserve: CONFIRMED.** Trump signed Executive Order 14233, "Establishment of the Strategic Bitcoin Reserve and United States Digital Asset Stockpile", on 2025-03-06 [1][2][3].
   - The reserve is funded with bitcoin the government already holds from forfeitures. That bitcoin may not be sold.
   - Any further purchases must be "budget-neutral".
   - The order does not commit taxpayer money to buying bitcoin.

2. **TMTG announced a $2.5B raise for its own bitcoin treasury "around that time": MOSTLY ACCURATE (timing is loose).**
   - TMTG announced the plan on 2025-05-27, about 12 weeks after the order [4][5][6]. It was set for roughly 50 institutional investors: about $1.5B in common stock plus $1B in 0% convertible notes.
   - The deal closed on 2025-05-30 at about $2.44B gross [7][8]. That was 55.8M shares at $25.72 (about $1.44B) plus the $1B in notes. Net proceeds were about $2.32B.
   - Crypto.com and Anchorage Digital were named as custodians.
   - The SEC declared the related resale registration statement effective on 2025-06-13 [9].

3. **Don Wilson invested in TMTG: CONFIRMED IN SUBSTANCE.**
   - Reports from mid-June 2025, citing the Financial Times and SEC filings, say DRW Investments bought nearly 4 million TMTG shares (about 3.8M+) worth about $100M as part of the bitcoin-treasury financing [9][10][11]. DRW is the Chicago trading firm Wilson founded and controls.
   - The same reports put Jane Street's purchase at about $375M, making it the largest investor.
   - Precision point: the investor was DRW's investment arm, not Wilson personally. Cumberland is DRW's crypto trading unit.
   - I could not open the original FT article. These figures come from outlets quoting it.

4. **The SEC case against Cumberland was dismissed with prejudice "weeks prior": CONFIRMED.**
   - The SEC sued Cumberland DRW in October 2024 (N.D. Ill., No. 1:24-cv-09842). It alleged Cumberland acted as an unregistered dealer in more than $2B of crypto asset securities.
   - On 2025-03-04, Cumberland said it had a joint filing with SEC staff to dismiss, agreed in principle on 2025-02-20 [12][13].
   - SEC Litigation Release LR-26276 (2025-03-27) says the SEC filed a joint stipulation to dismiss with prejudice [14][15]. There was no admission and no penalty. Some reports date the court's dismissal to 2025-03-31 [16].
   - The SEC said it dropped the case to "facilitate the Commission's ongoing efforts to reform and renew its regulatory approach to the crypto industry". It dropped the Kraken and Consensys cases the same week.
   - The TMTG purchase came about 8-9 weeks after the dismissal, so "weeks prior" is fair.

5. **Cumberland denied any quid pro quo or connection: UNVERIFIED BUT CONSISTENT.**
   - The denial was given privately to Coffeezilla, so I could not check it directly.
   - It matches DRW's public statements in June 2025 [10][11]. DRW said it treats bitcoin on its balance sheet as a strategic decision and that the transaction is "purely viewed from this perspective". A spokesperson added that Wilson "has long called for a clear regulatory framework".

### Corrections
- **Timing.** The video says TMTG's raise came "around" the time of the order. The order was signed 2025-03-06; TMTG announced on 2025-05-27, about 12 weeks later.
- **Amount.** The video says "$2.5 billion raised". That was the announced target. The deal closed at about $2.44B gross and about $2.32B net.
- **Who invested.** The video says Don Wilson invested. The documented buyer is DRW Investments, his firm. Cumberland is DRW's crypto unit.
- **When the dismissal was agreed.** The dismissal was agreed in principle with SEC staff on 2025-02-20 and announced on 2025-03-04. TMTG's treasury plan was announced on 2025-05-27, so the settlement came before the plan was public.

### Denials and context
- **Cumberland/DRW** deny any link between the two events. They say the TMTG stake was a balance-sheet and bitcoin decision.
- **The SEC** dropped the case as part of a broad reversal of Gensler-era crypto cases that also covered Coinbase, Kraken and Consensys. Cumberland was one of many.
- **About 50 institutions** took part in the raise. Jane Street put in about $375M, nearly four times DRW's stake.
- **The family link.** TMTG is controlled by Donald J. Trump's revocable trust, so investing in it benefits a company the President's trust owns. Watchdog groups such as CREW have raised "policy-for-investment" concerns [18] (single, secondary Chinese-language report).
- **No quid pro quo is documented.** The only link is the sequence of events, so the video's framing is insinuation rather than demonstrated cause.
