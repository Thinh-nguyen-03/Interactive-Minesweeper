# C112: Gaza Reconstruction, the Board of Peace & Who Is Involved

**Verdict:** Partly accurate · medium confidence  
**Type:** Check · Foreign Deals

## The Claim

> Who has a documented role or financial interest in Gaza's reconstruction plans?

## Bottom Line

The plans are documented in order: Trump's 4 Feb 2025 'Riviera of the Middle East' remarks, the 29 Sep 2025 20-point plan, the ceasefire of 10 Oct 2025, UN Security Council resolution 2803 (17 Nov 2025, 13-0, China and Russia abstaining) and the Board of Peace, whose charter Trump ratified at Davos on 22 Jan 2026 with himself as chairman.

## What Holds Up

- On 4 Feb 2025 Trump said the US would 'take over' Gaza, 'own it' and make it the 'Riviera of the Middle East'.
- The 29 Sep 2025 20-point plan created a Trump-chaired 'Board of Peace', and the UN Security Council endorsed it in Resolution 2803.
- The Board of Peace is chaired by Trump and its executive boards include Kushner, Witkoff, Blair, Rowan and others, with a mandate that goes beyond Gaza.
- Kushner and Witkoff drafted reconstruction proposals ('Project Sunrise', $112B; the 'New Gaza' master plan shown at Davos) that favour real-estate development.
- Signed contracts or commitments for Gaza reconstruction exist (mostly accurate)

## What Needs Correcting

- About $17 billion has been pledged to the Board of Peace and it is held in a World Bank account (partly accurate)
- A leaked 'GREAT Trust' prospectus proposed a US-led trusteeship and 'Trump Riviera' redevelopment of Gaza, and it became policy (partly accurate)
- The Gaza Humanitarian Foundation (GHF) was US- and Israel-backed, used private contractors, and its funding is documented (partly accurate)
- Common shorthand says the Board of Peace has a $17 billion fund. The record shows: $17B is an announced pledge total (US $10B, nine states about $7B), not money received; the FT reported in May 2026 that the World Bank-administered account was empty.
- Common shorthand says the Riviera remark was an official plan. The record shows: it was a spoken proposal at a press conference on 4 Feb 2025; the 20-point plan of 29 Sep 2025 says nobody will be forced to leave and the UN-endorsed Board of Peace was a different instrument.
- Common shorthand says the GREAT Trust was adopted. The record shows: no official source adopts it; BCG and the Tony Blair Institute disavow the relocation parts, and the later White House plans (Project Sunrise, New Gaza) are separate documents.
- C15 describes Kushner as holding no official title. The 16 Jan 2026 White House statement lists him on both Board of Peace executive boards, and a Getty caption relayed by the Jerusalem Post (Sept 2026) calls him 'US Special Envoy for Peace'; C15's volunteer-status reading (White House position, CREW suit Oct 2026) is not contradicted but should note the board roles.
- C105 lists Witkoff's formal posts; add that he sits on both Board of Peace executive boards per the 16 Jan 2026 statement.
- **Claimed:** The FT's finding rests on 'five sources'  
  **The record shows:** The FT reporter says five (NPR); other outlets relaying the FT say four; either way it is one investigation relayed by others.
- **Claimed:** GHF received $30M  
  **The record shows:** $30M was approved in June 2025; a report says about $3M had been sent by the end of July 2025; later disbursement is not documented in what we found.

## What Is Unproven

- Named people on the Board of Peace have private business interests that overlap with Gaza: Kushner (Affinity Partners), Witkoff, Rowan (Apollo), Gabay, Blair.
- Any firm tied to the Trump family or administration holds a documented contract, commitment or stated financial interest in Gaza reconstruction.

## In More Detail

- Named insiders have documented roles: Kushner and Witkoff sit on both executive boards, with Tony Blair, Marc Rowan (Apollo), Ajay Banga, Marco Rubio, Robert Gabriel and, on the Gaza board, the developer Yakir Gabay.
- No firm owned by or tied to the Trump family has been documented with a contract, commitment or stated financial interest in Gaza reconstruction, and none of the leaked or presented plans is a signed commitment.
- Pledges of about $17B (US $10B, nine states $7B) were announced on 19 Feb 2026, but the World Bank account had received nothing by late May 2026 according to the FT; some donations went to a JPMorgan account the board controls.
- The only construction award found is a provisional August 2026 contract to Louisiana firm Arkel International for a small military outpost, frozen because Israel has not admitted contractors.
- Conflict-of-interest concerns about Kushner's Affinity Partners, Rowan and others rest on indirect holdings and Gulf investor overlap, are reported by the Telegraph and others, and are denied by the board; no authority has made a finding.

## Sub-Claims

### 1. On 4 Feb 2025 Trump said the US would 'take over' Gaza, 'own it' and make it the 'Riviera of the Middle East'.

**Confirmed**

- Grade: documented (official record of the remarks, American Presidency Project, text read).
- At the joint press conference with Netanyahu, Trump said 'The U.S. will take over the Gaza Strip, and we will do a job with it too.
- We'll own it and be responsible for dismantling all of the dangerous unexploded bombs'; he foresaw 'a long-term ownership position' and, asked where residents would go, said 'the Riviera of the Middle East.
- This could be so magnificent.'
- This was a spoken proposal: no document, funding or contract followed it.
- Reporting at the time says Press Secretary Leavitt then said Trump was not committing US money to reconstruction and that no US troops were planned (search excerpt, Fortune; not read).
- Sources: [1](https://www.presidency.ucsb.edu/documents/the-presidents-news-conference-with-prime-minister-benjamin-netanyahu-israel-0) [28](https://www.axios.com/2025/02/05/trump-gaza-takeover-palestinians-israel) [29](https://fortune.com/2025/02/05/trump-riviera-middle-east-gaza-palestine-israel-rubio-leavitt/)

### 2. The 29 Sep 2025 20-point plan created a Trump-chaired 'Board of Peace', and the UN Security Council endorsed it in Resolution 2803.

**Confirmed** · Evidence: Documented

- The White House released the 20-point 'Comprehensive Plan to End the Gaza Conflict' on 29 Sep 2025; its governance point (as quoted by outlets reproducing the text; the whitehouse.gov page was not retrievable) places Gaza under a technocratic Palestinian committee supervised by a Board of Peace chaired by Trump, with Tony Blair named, which 'will set the framework and handle the funding for the redevelopment of Gaza'.
- A ceasefire took effect on 10 Oct 2025.
- The Security Council adopted resolution 2803 on 17 Nov 2025 by 13 votes to none with China and Russia abstaining; UN press coverage says the Board and the authorised international stabilisation force run to 31 Dec 2027 (the resolution text itself could not be opened here, so wording is from UN press and the White House statement of 16 Jan 2026, which says the resolution 'endorsed' the plan and 'welcomed the establishment of the Board of Peace').
- The Board's own charter, unpublished at the time of the vote, does not mention Gaza (Carnegie).
- Dates: 29 Sep 2025; 10 Oct 2025; 17 Nov 2025; 31 Dec 2027; 16 Jan 2026
- Sources: [2](https://www.whitehouse.gov/briefings-statements/2026/01/statement-on-president-trumps-comprehensive-plan-to-end-the-gaza-conflict/) [5](https://www.jns.org/israel-news/full-text-trumps-comprehensive-plan-to-end-the-gaza-conflict) [6](https://press.un.org/en/2025/sc16225.doc.htm) [7](https://carnegieendowment.org/research/2026/03/the-board-of-peace-and-funding-for-gaza-reconstruction-on-whose-account)

### 3. The Board of Peace is chaired by Trump and its executive boards include Kushner, Witkoff, Blair, Rowan and others, with a mandate that goes beyond Gaza.

**Confirmed** · Evidence: Documented

- White House statement of 16 Jan 2026 (read in full): founding Executive Board Marco Rubio, Steve Witkoff, Jared Kushner, Sir Tony Blair, Marc Rowan, Ajay Banga, Robert Gabriel; Aryeh Lightstone and Josh Gruenbaum named senior advisers 'leading day-to-day strategy and operations'; Nickolay Mladenov High Representative for Gaza; Maj.
- Gen. Jasper Jeffers commander of the stabilisation force; a Gaza Executive Board of Witkoff, Kushner, Hakan Fidan, Ali Al-Thawadi, Hassan Rashad, Blair, Rowan, Reem Al-Hashimy, Mladenov, Yakir Gabay and Sigrid Kaag; the Palestinian technocratic committee (NCAG) led by Ali Shaath.
- Trump ratified the charter in Davos on 22 Jan 2026 (White House release).
- A reproduction of the full charter text (the copy obtained by Middle East Eye, reproduced by TRANSCEND; not an official White House or UN page, article numbering unverified) was read in full in the audit and supports the Carnegie reading in substance: Art. 3.2 names Donald J. Trump as inaugural Chairman, separately from his role as US representative, with 'exclusive authority to create, modify, or dissolve subsidiary entities'; Art. 3.3 allows replacement 'only following voluntary resignation or as a result of incapacity, as determined by a unanimous vote of the Executive Board', with a successor the Chairman designates; Art. 4.1 has the Chairman select the Executive Board, remove its members and veto its decisions; Art. 3.1 makes Board decisions, meeting agendas and alternates 'subject to the approval of the Chairman', with budgets voted by member states under that approval.
- The phrase 'in his personal capacity' is Carnegie's reading (the text names him, not the office of president), as is the characterisation of an indefinite term.
- Art. 5.2 says the Executive Board 'shall authorize the institution of controls and oversight mechanisms with respect to budgets, financial accounts, and disbursements'; no donor-reporting rule for any account is in the charter text. permanent membership for states contributing $1B in the first year is reported (AP via PBS, citing an unnamed US official).
- Criticism of the charter came from the UK, France, Germany, Italy and Sen. Markey (Carnegie).
- Amounts: $1B
- Dates: 16 Jan 2026; 22 Jan 2026
- Sources: [2](https://www.whitehouse.gov/briefings-statements/2026/01/statement-on-president-trumps-comprehensive-plan-to-end-the-gaza-conflict/) [3](https://www.whitehouse.gov/briefings-statements/2026/01/president-trump-ratifies-board-of-peace-in-historic-ceremony-opening-path-to-hope-and-dignity-for-gazans/) [7](https://carnegieendowment.org/research/2026/03/the-board-of-peace-and-funding-for-gaza-reconstruction-on-whose-account) [8](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/world/1-billion-contribution-secures-permanent-seat-on-trumps-board-of-peace)

### 4. About $17 billion has been pledged to the Board of Peace and it is held in a World Bank account.

**Partly accurate**

- Grade: announcement documented; delivery and custody reported by the FT and disputed in part.
- At the first meeting on 19 Feb 2026 Trump announced $10B from the US (unspecified use) and about $7B from nine states (Kazakhstan, Azerbaijan, UAE, Morocco, Bahrain, Qatar, Saudi Arabia, Uzbekistan, Kuwait); a US official said the nine-state pledges were voluntary.
- The Intercept says Board documents show only eight formal pledges (single source, search excerpt).
- The FT reported (27 May 2026; the reporter told NPR she had 'five different sources with direct knowledge', other outlets say four; relayed by NPR on 1 June, and by Gulf News, The New Arab, i24 and Kyiv Post, all of which derive from the one FT investigation, so this is one original report with many relays, not independent confirmation) that the World Bank-administered account had received nothing, including from the US, while donations from the UAE and Morocco, and possibly private donors, went to a JPMorgan account to which the board has direct access with no reporting requirement (per the FT reporter on NPR: 'no requirement ... that anyone reports who's donating, how much, how it's being spent'; relayed figures are about $20M from the UAE and about $3M from Morocco, reportedly for office costs and salaries; the board says it will report to its executive board 'at a time deemed appropriate'); none of the money was spent inside Gaza.
- The Board of Peace replied that the World Bank fund is 'one of many funding mechanisms' not yet used because Gaza has not reached the reconstruction phase, and that donors chose the JPMorgan account.
- A separate report says the State Department plans to redirect about $1.2B of aid toward the board's goals (search excerpt, unverified).
- A Board of Peace six-month roadmap of $2.45B was proposed in Sept 2026 (search excerpt).
- No public accounting of receipts or disbursements was found.
- Amounts: $10B; about $7B; about $20M; about $3M; about $1.2B
- Dates: 19 Feb 2026; 27 May 2026; 1 June; Sept 2026
- Sources: [10](https://www.npr.org/2026/06/01/nx-s1-5837603/funds-for-trumps-board-of-peace-arent-in-world-bank-account-so-where-are-they) [11](https://www.arabnews.com/node/2645143/middle-east) [9](https://www.middleeasteye.net/news/board-peace-members-commit-7bn-gaza-relief-us-adding-further-10bn) [12](https://theintercept.com/2026/03/02/trump-board-peace-human-right-abuses/) [13](https://www.local10.com/news/world/2026/09/23/trumps-board-of-peace-proposes-245-billion-blueprint-to-begin-gaza-reconstruction/)

### 5. A leaked 'GREAT Trust' prospectus proposed a US-led trusteeship and 'Trump Riviera' redevelopment of Gaza, and it became policy.

**Partly accurate**

- Grade: document documented (a 38-page deck, read from a public mirror); authorship and official status single-source or disputed.
- The deck, 'The GREAT Trust' (Gaza Reconstitution, Economic Acceleration and Transformation), proposes a US-led custodianship, $70B-$100B public investment drawing $35B-$65B private, a land-trust and tokenisation model, a '$5K/person' relocation package and 'The Elon Musk Smart Manufacturing Zone', 'The American Data Safe Haven' and 'Gaza Trump Riviera & Islands' as named projects; those labels are concept names, not contracts or Trump Organization commitments.
- Outlets citing the Washington Post (31 Aug 2025) say it was prepared by Israeli businessmen including Michael Eisenberg and Liran Tancman, who were involved in setting up the Gaza Humanitarian Foundation, with financial modelling by Boston Consulting Group staff.
- BCG said it fired two partners (Matt Schlueter, Ryan Ordway) on 4 June 2025 over unauthorised work on the GHF project, said it had not been and would not be paid, and later said the modelling was unauthorised.
- The Tony Blair Institute said staff joined planning calls but that it never authored or endorsed relocation.
- The White House described a 27 Aug 2025 Trump meeting with Blair and Kushner as 'simply a policy meeting'.
- Several firms named in a Gaza plan reportedly denied involvement (search excerpt; it is not clear that this refers to this deck).
- The 20-point plan, per outlets reproducing it, says no one will be forced to leave; the later Kushner-Witkoff and Board decks are separate documents, and no relocation payment appears in the coverage of them that was read.
- Amounts: $70B-$100B; $35B-$65B; $5K
- Dates: 31 Aug 2025; 4 June 2025; 27 Aug 2025
- Sources: [14](https://www.protagon.gr/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/TheGREATTrust.pdf) [15](https://www.middleeasteye.net/news/four-takeaways-plan-trying-bring-trump-gaza-riviera-life) [16](https://www.newarab.com/news/two-bcg-execs-lose-roles-over-gaza-displacement-plan) [17](https://www.consultancy-me.com/news/10991/boston-consulting-group-under-fire-for-role-in-the-gaza-humanitarian-foundation) [18](https://today.lorientlejour.com/article/1468320/tony-blair-institute-involved-in-controversial-gaza-reconstruction-project.html)

### 6. Kushner and Witkoff drafted reconstruction proposals ('Project Sunrise', $112B; the 'New Gaza' master plan shown at Davos) that favour real-estate development.

**Confirmed** · Evidence: Documented as proposals, not commitments

- The Wall Street Journal (reported 23 Dec 2025) described a 32-slide 'sensitive but unclassified' deck by Kushner, Witkoff and adviser Josh Gruenbaum, costing about $112B over a decade with nearly $60B from US-anchored grants and loan guarantees, to be pitched to Egypt, Turkey and Gulf states; the deck did not say which companies or countries would invest.
- On 22 Jan 2026 in Davos Kushner presented a 'New Gaza' master plan (coastal towers, data centres, New Rafah with over 100,000 housing units, an airport; Board estimate of over $25B for utilities) and said Israeli-Cypriot developer Yakir Gabay volunteered to design the blueprint without profit motive.
- In April 2026 the plan was reported scaled back to a Rafah pilot.
- Funding for any of it depends on donors and Israeli permission.
- Amounts: about $112B; nearly $60B; over $25B
- Dates: 23 Dec 2025; 22 Jan 2026; April 2026
- Sources: [19](https://abcnews4.com/news/nation-world/trump-administration-floats-112-billion-plan-to-rebuild-gaza-as-futuristic-coastal-hub-ai-united-states-jared-kushner-steve-witkoff-middle-east-marco-rubio) [20](https://www.jta.org/2026/01/22/israel/jared-kushner-presents-plan-for-new-gaza-at-davos-as-trump-launches-board-of-peace-with-israel-absent) [21](https://themedialine.org/headlines/jared-kushner-at-davos-lays-out-plan-for-new-gaza-as-economic-hub-by-2035) [22](https://prospect.org/2026/02/09/board-of-peace-profits-trump-kushner-gaza/)

### 7. The Gaza Humanitarian Foundation (GHF) was US- and Israel-backed, used private contractors, and its funding is documented.

**Partly accurate** · Evidence: Partly documented

- GHF began distributing in late May 2025 and suspended operations at the 10 Oct ceasefire and announced on 24 Nov 2025 that it was winding down, saying it had delivered more than 187 million meals (AP, CNN, Reuters via Dawn, The New Arab: confirmed by several outlets), handing its model to the US-led Civil-Military Coordination Center (AP).
- The State Department announced $30M for GHF on 26-27 June 2025 (Al Jazeera, Irish Times, JNS, Medialine: several outlets); one report says only about $3M had been sent by the end of July, so 'approved' is not 'paid'.
- Reuters (Jul 2025) reported the State Department approved $30M in June 2025 and that Witkoff aides Aryeh Lightstone and Charles Leith told Congress on 8-9 July that Israel agreed to match it; Reuters could not verify the Israeli payment, Israel did not confirm, and GHF does not disclose donors (Johnnie Moore: 'we need more of it').
- GHF said a Western European country gave over $100M (unnamed; search excerpt).
- Contractors reported by Reuters: Safe Reach Solutions (logistics, led by a former CIA officer; McNally Capital, a Chicago private equity firm, has an economic interest) and UG Solutions (armed US veterans).
- Witkoff and Ambassador Huckabee toured a GHF site on 1 Aug 2025.
- Deaths near sites are disputed by GHF and the Israeli military.
- No Trump-family firm is reported as a GHF funder or contractor.
- Several of the same planners later appear in the Board of Peace structure (Lightstone; Eisenberg and Tancman per the FT via secondary summaries).
- Amounts: $30M; about $3M; over $100M
- Dates: late May 2025; 10 Oct; 24 Nov 2025; 27 June 2025; Jul 2025
- Sources: [23](https://www.al-monitor.com/originals/2025/07/exclusive-trump-officials-told-congress-israel-agreed-match-30-million-ghf) [24](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/world/u-s-backed-gaza-humanitarian-foundation-shutters-operations) [25](https://www.arabnews.pk/node/2603567) [26](https://www.yahoo.com/news/articles/huckabee-witkoff-visit-us-backed-144448958.html) [27](https://www.axios.com/2025/06/06/gaza-humanitarian-foundation-state-department-funding)

### 8. Named people on the Board of Peace have private business interests that overlap with Gaza: Kushner (Affinity Partners), Witkoff, Rowan (Apollo), Gabay, Blair.

**Unverified** · Evidence: Roles documented; financial interest in Gaza reconstruction not documented

- Documented background: Affinity manages about $6.2B, almost all from non-US clients led by Saudi PIF with QIA and Lunate (C15); Witkoff's 278e shows WLF and other holdings inside Witkoff Holdings (C105).
- The Telegraph (reported 10 Oct 2026, relayed by the Jerusalem Post, original not read) says Affinity is the largest shareholder of Phoenix Financial, which held Israeli defence firm Smart Shooter, and raised questions about Rowan and about Bashar Masri's companies (Masri's lawyers call allegations speculation); the board called the allegations baseless ('no Board of Peace involvement, no Board of Peace decision, and zero demonstrated personal benefit') and an unnamed source said business interests were never discussed; Affinity says it does not control Phoenix's investment decisions; Rowan's role is described as unpaid service.
- Carnegie notes the charter has no conflict-of-interest rules.
- The Prospect and Carnegie say executive-board members are largely business or personal associates of Trump.
- No filing, contract, ethics finding or investigation tying any of these people or firms to a Gaza reconstruction contract was found.
- Kushner's lawyer has said his unpaid volunteer role was cleared by ethics lawyers; CREW sued in Oct 2026 (C15).
- Amounts: about $6.2B
- Dates: 10 Oct 2026; Oct 2026
- Sources: [34](https://www.jpost.com/middle-east/article-911389) [35](https://www.jpost.com/international/article-910380) [22](https://prospect.org/2026/02/09/board-of-peace-profits-trump-kushner-gaza/) [7](https://carnegieendowment.org/research/2026/03/the-board-of-peace-and-funding-for-gaza-reconstruction-on-whose-account)

### 10. Signed contracts or commitments for Gaza reconstruction exist.

**Mostly accurate** · Evidence: Documented but small

- The one construction award found is Arkel International (Louisiana), provisionally awarded in early Aug 2026 (Guardian, relayed by the Irish Times, Ynet and others) to build a 100m x 100m outpost at a former GHF site for about 150 of a planned 500 Moroccan and Kosovar troops; a board official said several awards were being prepared and none finalised; no value was reported.
- By 15 Oct 2026 Israel had not admitted contractors, machinery or materials, so the project was frozen, and the board expected movement only after the 27 Oct Israeli election and the US midterms.
- The UAE reportedly pledged $1.5B for 'New Rafah' (Prospect; single source).
- No signed donor agreement, reconstruction contract or procurement procedure was found.
- This is not a Trump-family firm; any link between Arkel and the Board's leaders was not reported.
- Amounts: $1.5B
- Dates: early Aug 2026; 15 Oct 2026; 27 Oct
- Sources: [33](https://www.theweek.in/news/middle-east/2026/10/15/gaza-reconstruction-project-stalled-israel-blocks-contractors.html) [32](https://www.irishtimes.com/world/middle-east/2026/08/06/trumps-gaza-board-of-peace-issues-first-building-contract-for-military-base/) [34](https://www.jpost.com/middle-east/article-911389) [22](https://prospect.org/2026/02/09/board-of-peace-profits-trump-kushner-gaza/)

## Denials & Context

- Board of Peace: conflict-of-interest reporting is baseless ('no Board of Peace involvement, no Board of Peace decision, and zero demonstrated personal benefit'); an unnamed source said business interests were never discussed.
  - The board says the World Bank fund was unused because Gaza has not reached the reconstruction phase and 'we're not hoarding money in a bank account'.
- Affinity Partners: no control over Phoenix's investment decisions; Rowan's board role is not-for-profit service.
- Tony Blair Institute: staff joined calls but it never authored, developed or endorsed relocation.
- BCG: dismissed two partners, says the Gaza work was unauthorised and unpaid.
- Israel: ministries did not confirm funding GHF; Reuters could not verify the matching $30M. GHF: donors are private; it denies violence inside its sites.
- White House (Feb 2025): US money would not go to Gaza reconstruction; (Aug 2025) the Blair/Kushner meeting was a routine policy meeting.
- Context: the ceasefire is partial; Israel reportedly controls about 60% of Gaza, no civilian administration operates inside it, and deliveries average about 227 trucks a day against a plan minimum of 600 (The Week, Oct 2026).
  - Israel's election on 27 Oct 2026 and US midterms preceded a coalition process still open on 17 Nov 2026.

### 1. The Riviera remarks (4 Feb 2025)

**Grade: documented (official text of the remarks).** At the White House press conference with Netanyahu, Trump said the US would "take over the Gaza Strip", "own it" and "level the site", create "economic development", and described "a long-term ownership position". Asked where residents would go meanwhile, he said "the Riviera of the Middle East. This could be so magnificent." This was a spoken proposal. No document, budget or contract followed. Press reports say Press Secretary Leavitt then said Trump was not committing US money for reconstruction and no US troops (search excerpt only; Fortune page not retrievable).

### 2. The 20-point plan, ceasefire and UN resolution 2803

**Grade: documented.** The plan was released on 29 Sep 2025. As reproduced by outlets (the whitehouse.gov page could not be retrieved), its governance point puts Gaza under a technocratic Palestinian committee supervised by a Board of Peace chaired by Trump with Tony Blair named, which "will set the framework and handle the funding for the redevelopment of Gaza"; an economic development plan by a panel of experts and a special economic zone are separate points; the plan says no one will be forced to leave. The ceasefire began 10 Oct 2025. On 17 Nov 2025 the Security Council adopted resolution 2803 by 13-0 with China and Russia abstaining. UN press coverage says the Board and the stabilisation force are authorised to 31 Dec 2027. I could not open the resolution text (docs.un.org returned only a viewer shell), so the operative wording ("transitional administration", funding role) comes from press summaries and from the White House statement of 16 Jan 2026, which says the resolution "endorsed" the plan and "welcomed the establishment of the Board of Peace". Carnegie notes that the charter, unpublished at the time of the vote, does not mention Gaza and sets a broader mandate.

### 3. The Board of Peace: chair, members, mandate

**Grade: documented (White House statements read in full).**

- **Chair:** Trump, ratifying the charter at Davos on 22 Jan 2026. Audit: a reproduction of the full charter text (the Middle East Eye copy as reproduced by TRANSCEND; not an official page) was read and supports Carnegie's reading in substance: Art. 3.2 names Trump as inaugural Chairman and gives him exclusive authority over subsidiary entities; Art. 3.3 lets him be replaced only by resignation or incapacity (unanimous Executive Board) and has him designate a successor; Art. 4.1 has him select and remove Executive Board members and veto its decisions; Art. 3.1 makes Board decisions and agendas subject to his approval. "In his personal capacity" and "indefinite term" are Carnegie's characterisations (the text names him, not the presidency). Art. 5.2 requires the Executive Board to set up controls and oversight for accounts; the charter has no donor-reporting rule; the UK, France, Germany, Italy and Sen. Markey raised concerns. Permanent membership for states contributing $1B in the first year was reported by AP citing an unnamed US official.
- **Founding Executive Board (16 Jan 2026):** Marco Rubio, Steve Witkoff, Jared Kushner, Tony Blair, Marc Rowan, Ajay Banga, Robert Gabriel. Senior advisers: Aryeh Lightstone and Josh Gruenbaum. High Representative for Gaza: Nickolay Mladenov. Stabilisation force commander: Maj. Gen. Jasper Jeffers.
- **Gaza Executive Board:** Witkoff, Kushner, Hakan Fidan, Ali Al-Thawadi, Hassan Rashad, Blair, Rowan, Reem Al-Hashimy, Mladenov, Yakir Gabay, Sigrid Kaag. The Palestinian committee (NCAG) is led by Dr Ali Shaath, who the Israeli authorities have reportedly barred from entering Gaza (The Week, Oct 2026).
- **Blair:** named in the plan; the FT reported he was reconsidered after criticism in Sept 2025 (search excerpt), but the January 2026 statements list him on both boards.
