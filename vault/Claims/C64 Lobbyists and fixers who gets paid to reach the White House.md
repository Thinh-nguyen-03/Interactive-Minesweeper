## Full report

**Verdict: mostly-accurate** (confidence: medium)

This is a connection hunt; the video does not cover it. The record supports most of the access layer described in the brief:
- **Ballard Partners** links to officials and clients in this project: Bondi's Qatar FARA work, Wiles's years at the firm, crypto clients (Kraken, Blockchain.com, Ripple), the Saudi embassy and ballroom donors.
- **Jeff Miller's Miller Strategies** works for a cluster of crypto and prediction-market firms: Crypto.com, Gemini, Tether and Kalshi. Donald Trump Jr. is an adviser to Kalshi.
- **Checkmate (Ches McDowell)** has clients that overlap with this project's stories: Binance's pardon push, Pakistan and a partnership with Ballard.
- **Pakistan** paid three Trump-tied firms from April to May 2025: Seiden Law, Javelin Advisors (Keith Schiller and George Sorial) and Checkmate. In the same weeks, World Liberty Financial (WLF) signed its Pakistan deal.
- **Kash Patel** consulted for the Qatari embassy before signing security deals with Qatar as FBI director.

Several named players have no documented link to a client in this project: Mercury in 2025, Corey Lewandowski, Arthur Schwartz, and Continental Strategy (apart from Albania). No source we found shows that any lobbying payment produced an outcome. Every causal link below is either timing or allegation.

**Method note:** Sludge (readsludge.com), the FARA eFile PDFs and the Senate LDA database could not be fetched from this environment. Findings rest on search-result excerpts from several outlets: Sludge, the Washington Examiner, Denver Gazette/Colorado Politics, Dawn, Nukta, Sunday Guardian, O'Dwyer's, Politico (via Raw Story, CryptoPotato and Florida Politics), CBS, Public Citizen, Stateline, Mother Jones, Jewish Insider, Bloomberg and others. The shared web-search budget ran out before I could check the exact date of Bondi's jet memo or the Al Jazeera Pakistan piece's full text. Both are flagged below.

### Connections (sub-claims)

#### 1. Pam Bondi: Ballard's Qatar account, then the jet memo. Evidence: documented. Verdict: confirmed
- **The Qatar contract:** Ballard Partners registered under FARA for Qatar's government in 2019, with Bondi named on the contract. The firm was paid $115,000 a month.
- **Bondi's tenure on it:** She left Ballard in Nov 2019 and returned in Mar 2020, resuming the Qatar work. The Quincy Institute says she was registered for Qatar's embassy until 2021.
- **The memo:** In May 2025 Bondi reportedly signed the Justice Department memo finding acceptance of the Qatari 747 "legally permissible". It relied on the plane passing to the Trump library foundation. American Oversight sued in Jul 2025 for the memo.
- **Correction to a common claim:** A Yahoo fact check rates "Bondi personally made $115K a month" as false. The $115K was the firm's fee.
- **What this shows:** The attorney general once lobbied for the government whose gift she then cleared. It does not show that the memo was wrong or influenced. No recusal by Bondi on the memo was found.
- **Unverified:** The exact date of the memo.

#### 2. Kash Patel: paid consultant to Qatar's embassy, then signs security deals with Qatar. Evidence: documented. Verdict: confirmed
- **The disclosure:** Patel's financial disclosure, made public on Feb 1, 2025 after his hearing, listed the Qatari embassy as a client of his firm Trishul LLC. Trishul earned about $2.1M, but Qatar's share is reported only as a range of more than $5,000.
- **No registration:** He did not register under FARA.
- **Ethics waiver:** A later disclosure shows an ethics waiver, dated Mar 4, for a "particular matter related to Qatar".
- **The security deals:** In Dec 2025, as FBI director, he signed bilateral security agreements with Qatar (Jewish Insider). He said he would recuse himself from related work for one year and keep Trishul dormant.
- **What is not known:** What the Qatar work involved remains undisclosed.

#### 3. Susie Wiles: Ballard, then Mercury. Evidence: employment documented; any Qatar work single-source. Verdict: partly-accurate
- **Ballard:** Wiles lobbied at Ballard. Public Citizen gives her years as 2011 to 2019. OpenSecrets lists a later end date.
- **Mercury:** She was co-chair of Mercury Public Affairs' Florida and DC offices from Feb 2022 to 2024.
- **Mercury's Qatar work:** Mercury held Qatar embassy contracts in 2015 ($930K for six months) and 2019 ($360K for a year). Both predate her arrival.
- **The Qatar claim:** Only an Italian outlet (Il Foglio) says she "led" Mercury while it represented Qatar. I found no 2025 Mercury contract with Qatar, the UAE, Binance or Justin Sun.
- **Other foreign clients:** Her documented foreign clients include Nigeria's PDP and Globovision.
- **Firm growth:** Both firms signed many foreign clients after the 2024 election: Ballard at least 15, Mercury at least 7 (Washington Examiner).

#### 4. Ballard's crypto clients and the XRP post. Evidence: hires documented; the White House freeze single-source (Politico, anonymous). Verdict: mostly-accurate
- **Hires (C52):** Kraken and Blockchain.com hired Ballard in Dec 2024 for $60K combined. Kraken's SEC suit was dismissed on Mar 27, 2025 (C23).
- **The XRP post:** Politico reported that a Ballard employee pressed Trump at a Mar-a-Lago event in early Mar 2025 to promote a crypto reserve. Trump's Mar 2025 Truth Social post then named XRP.
  - David Sacks reportedly called Wiles to complain.
  - Staff were told not to take meetings with Ballard, and Trump reportedly said "He is not welcome in anything anymore."
  - Ballard then dropped Politico's parent company as a client.
- **Ripple's position:** Ripple is a Ballard client and a ballroom donor (item 6). The SEC and Ripple dropped their cross-appeals on Aug 7, 2025, leaving the $125M penalty in place (C23).
- **What this shows:** A lobbying contact preceded a presidential post that moved prices. It also shows the access backfired.
- **Ballard's own scale:** He reportedly raised more than $50M for Trump's 2024 campaign. His firm made a record of about $88.1M in 2025 lobbying revenue.

#### 5. Ballard and Saudi Arabia. Evidence: documented (FARA, as reported by Sludge and the Washington Examiner/Denver Gazette). Verdict: confirmed
- **The contract:** Ballard signed with the Saudi embassy on Mar 31, 2025, at $60,000 a month ($720K for a year) for communications, events and outreach.
- **Other foreign clients:** Sludge (Feb 2026) also lists the DRC, Japan, South Korea (Korea Zinc), Uzbekistan and an Albanian client.
- **Saudi Arabia elsewhere in this project:** the Saudi PIF in Affinity (C15), and the F-35 sale and major non-NATO ally status.
- **Not established:** No source ties Ballard's Saudi work to any of those decisions. That link is **inferred** (co-occurrence only).

#### 6. The ballroom donors are lobbying clients. Evidence: documented (Public Citizen, Nov 2025; CBS). Verdict: confirmed
- **The count:** 17 of 26 known corporate ballroom donors were clients of Ballard, Miller Strategies or Reince Priebus's Michael Best Strategies.
  - Ballard: 11, including Ripple, Palantir and Amazon.
  - Miller: 6, including **Tether**, Apple and Palantir.
- **Fundraising roles:** Ballard and Jeff Miller attended a White House ballroom fundraising dinner.
- **Senators' questions:** Sens. Blumenthal, Warren and Schiff sent questions to Ballard, Miller, Priebus and fundraiser Meredith O'Rourke. All four declined to comment to CBS.
- **Tether's other ties:** Tether links to Cantor and Lutnick (C35, C50).

#### 7. Miller Strategies' crypto and prediction-market cluster. Evidence: each hire documented; the pattern is our own. Verdict: mostly-accurate
- **Who Jeff Miller is:** Finance chair of Trump's inaugural festivities.
- **His clients:**
  - Crypto.com, whose SEC probe was closed on Mar 27, 2025 (C43, C52);
  - Gemini, whose CFTC vacatur bid was denied in Oct 2026 (C52);
  - Tether, a ballroom donor;
  - **Kalshi**, which paid Miller Strategies $240K in the first half of 2026, its largest outside firm (Stateline, Oct 2026).
- **The Trump Jr. link:** Donald Trump Jr. is a strategic adviser to Kalshi and an investor in and adviser to Polymarket (C25).
- **What no source says:** No source says Miller and Trump Jr. coordinated on Kalshi. That link is **inferred**.
- **Polymarket's lobbyist:** Polymarket's first federal lobbyist, registered Jul 2025, was David Urban, a former Trump campaign adviser (Stateline).

#### 8. Checkmate / Ches McDowell. Evidence: documented. Verdict: confirmed
- **Binance:** Paid $450K for Sept 2025 "executive relief" lobbying before the CZ pardon (C36). McDowell is a friend of Trump Jr. and says Trump Jr. did not help.
- **Revenue:** Checkmate reported more than $22M in 2025 federal revenue (Bloomberg Government), a jump of more than 31,000%.
- **Other clients:**
  - the Sports Betting Alliance;
  - the Panama Maritime Authority, at $195K a month;
  - **Pakistan**, through the Dubai-based Greystone Investment & Management, with a $100K Phase 1 from May 15, 2025 (Nukta; Sunday Guardian).
- **Ballard partnership:** Ballard and Checkmate announced a strategic partnership (Florida Politics). I could not establish its date.

#### 9. Pakistan: three Trump-tied firms paid while WLF signs its Pakistan deal. Evidence: each element documented; the overlap is inferred. Verdict: mostly-accurate
The firms:
- **Seiden Law:** Signed with Pakistan on Apr 8, 2025, at $200K a month for six months (FARA 7570, via Nukta).
- **Javelin Advisors:** Keith Schiller (Trump's former bodyguard) and George Sorial (former Trump Organization counsel) registered on Apr 24-25, 2025 (FARA 7579) as Seiden's subcontractor, at about $50K a month. One outlet says $125K.
- **Checkmate:** From May 15, 2025 (see item 8).

The WLF track:
- **The letter of intent:** On Apr 26, 2025, the day after Javelin's FARA registration, WLF's Zach Witkoff, Zak Folkman and Chase Herro signed it with the Pakistan Crypto Council in Islamabad.
- **Bin Saqib:** The Council's CEO, Bilal Bin Saqib, became a WLF adviser in Apr 2025 and later left the role on entering government. WLF says he was unpaid.
- **Earlier context:** CZ had been named a Council adviser earlier that month.

What followed:
- **The lunch:** Trump hosted army chief Asim Munir on Jun 18, 2025, with Steve Witkoff and Rubio present.
- **The trade deal:** A Jul 2025 trade deal set a 19% tariff.
- **The USD1 MOU:** In Jan 2026, WLF affiliate SC Financial signed an MOU to bring USD1 into Pakistan's payments framework.
- **How it is credited:** Bloomberg (Mar 29, 2026) credits Bin Saqib's crypto outreach with winning over "Trump world". Al Jazeera (Jul 2026) frames it as "$500m for Trump, access for Pakistan".

What remains unsourced:
- No source we found says Seiden, Javelin or Checkmate arranged the WLF deal or the lunch.
- Dawn's insiders described the lunch as secured through "unconventional means" without naming anyone.
- The overlap of lobbyists and the WLF deal is our **inference** from timing.

#### 10. Jason Miller (SHW Partners) and India. Evidence: documented (FARA, as reported by ThePrint, Business Today and Dawn). Verdict: confirmed
- **The contract:** India's embassy hired SHW on Apr 24, 2025, at $1.8M a year ($150K a month). It renewed on Apr 24, 2026, running to Apr 23, 2027.
- **The work:** Filings show SHW requested calls with trade and White House officials. Miller met Ambassador Sergio Gor three times from Nov to Dec 2025.
- **Why it matters here:** India and Pakistan hired Trump-tied firms on the same day, with Pakistan's firms tied to WLF's deal partner.
- **Kash Patel:** I found no record of foreign clients for Patel beyond Qatar (item 2) in this search.

#### 11. Brad Bondi: the Attorney General's brother as defense counsel. Evidence: documented (C36). Verdict: confirmed
- **The role:** Brad Bondi was Trevor Milton's SEC lawyer.
- **The outcome:** Milton, a $1.8M donor, was pardoned on Mar 28, 2025. The SEC dropped his case in Sep 2025 (C36, C49).
- **What this does not show:** It shows a family tie inside the pardon and enforcement chain. It does not show that Pam Bondi intervened.

#### 12. Roger Stone and Keith Schiller as pardon intermediaries. Evidence: documented (C36, C49). Verdict: confirmed
- **Stone:** Roger Ver paid him $600K, disclosed as exit-tax lobbying. Ver fired him in Dec 2025, and Stone was later hired for Nigerian Christians advocacy (Florida Bulldog).
- **Schiller:** His Javelin Advisors took $1M from Fred Daibes, who got no pardon.
- **The common thread:** Javelin links the pardon market (C49) to Pakistan (item 9).

#### 13. Continental Strategy (Carlos Trujillo) and Albania. Evidence: documented for the contract; any Kushner link is not connected. Verdict: partly-accurate
- **The contract:** In late Apr 2025, Albania's opposition Democratic Party (Sali Berisha) signed a reported $250K-a-month, two-year contract with Continental Strategy. A third party pays it. Albania opened a probe of a $6M DP lobbying contract.
- **Other clients:** Continental also registered Vima World SL. Its revenue rose about 2,800% in Q1 2025.
- **No Kushner link:** Kushner's Albania projects are with Rama's government (C45), the other side of Albanian politics. No link to Continental was found.
- **Ballard and Rama:** Ballard represented Rama's Socialist Party in 2017 at $20K a month. Sludge lists an Albanian client for Ballard in 2025-26 but does not say which. That item is unverified.

#### 14. Lewandowski, Arthur Schwartz and Mercury: no client in this project found. Verdict: unverified
- **Lewandowski:** He is a DHS special government employee under House Oversight inquiry (Rep. Garcia) and an American Oversight FOIA suit over his status and any private clients. No client in this project was found.
- **Schwartz:** Arthur Schwartz appears only as 1789 Capital's spokesman (C51). No lobbying client was found.
- **Mercury:** No 2025 client in this project was found.

### Not connected in the coverage we found
These links come from our own cross-referencing. No source asserts them.
- **Javelin's FARA registration for Pakistan (Apr 25, 2025) and WLF's Pakistan letter of intent (Apr 26, 2025).** *Inferred* from timing. No source says the lobbyists brokered it.
- **Checkmate's work for both Binance (C36) and Pakistan, whose Crypto Council named CZ an adviser in Apr 2025.** *Inferred*. The shared CZ thread is our observation.
- **Ballard's Saudi embassy contract (Mar 2025) and the Saudi F-35 sale and major non-NATO ally decisions, or the PIF's money in Affinity.** *Inferred* (co-occurrence).
- **Miller Strategies working for Kalshi while Trump Jr. advises Kalshi.** *Inferred*. Both facts are documented, but no coordination is reported.
- **Tether as both a Miller Strategies client and a ballroom donor, and its Cantor/Lutnick ties (C35, C50).** The client and donor facts are documented. Any link to Lutnick's decisions is *inferred*.
- **Ballard as the firm both Bondi and Wiles came from, and the firm that represents Kraken, Ripple and the Saudi embassy.** The employment is documented. Any influence through them is *inferred*. Politico's reporting cuts against it: Wiles's White House froze Ballard out in spring 2025.
- **Bondi's Qatar work and the Qatari Diar/Dar Global Trump golf deal (C16, C31).** *Inferred*. Nothing links them.

### Corrections
- **Bondi's fee:** Bondi did not personally earn $115K a month; that was Ballard's fee.
- **Wiles and Qatar:** The claim that Wiles "led" Mercury's Qatar work is single-source, and Mercury's documented Qatar contracts predate her joining.
- **Albania:** The 2025 Albanian lobbying with Trump-world firms (Continental, LaCivita and Manafort) is for Berisha's opposition, not for Rama's government, which is Kushner's partner.
- **Ballard's access:** Ballard's access is contested. Politico reports that he was frozen out after the Mar 2025 XRP post.

### Denials and context
- **Ballard:** The firm declined to comment to Sludge and did not respond to the Washington Examiner. Ballard, Miller and Priebus declined to comment to CBS on the ballroom.
- **Patel:** He says Trishul is dormant and that he recused himself from Qatar matters for one year.
- **WLF:** Bin Saqib was unpaid and stepped away from the adviser role on entering government.
- **White House:** It says there are no conflicts of interest.
- **McDowell:** He says Trump Jr. did not help Binance.
- **Stone:** He denies lobbying the executive branch for Ver.
- **Legality:** Paid lobbying and FARA work are legal and disclosed. What these records show is access and timing, not a bought outcome.
