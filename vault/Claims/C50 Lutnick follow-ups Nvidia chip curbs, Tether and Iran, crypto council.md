## Summary

Each of the five leads matches real, dated reporting or official documents. Axios (11 Nov 2026) reported, from unnamed sources, that Lutnick held up Trump-approved curbs on firms suspected of diverting Nvidia chips to China. Blumenthal's PSI minority staff tied Tether's USDT to Iranian shadow banking (28 Sep 2026) and then pressed Cantor (8 Oct 2026) on any Tether loan behind the Lutnick ownership transfer. By executive order, the Commerce Secretary sits on the President's Working Group on Digital Asset Markets. Cantor's $2B bitcoin-lending business exists, but Tether's part in it was only ever reported as talks. On tariff refunds, Cantor salespeople pitched refund-rights trades. Cantor says it never executed one, and no profit is documented. Every policy-for-family-gain link is still an allegation, and Commerce and Cantor deny them.

## Corrections

- The brief implies Blumenthal's loan probe targets the Commerce Secretary. The 8 Oct 2026 letter was addressed to Cantor chairman Brandon Lutnick. Warren and Wyden wrote to Howard Lutnick and Tether directly on 29 Apr 2026.
- 'Crypto council' is the President's Working Group on Digital Asset Markets (EO 14178). Commerce's seat is set by the order, not 'possible'.
- Tether's role in Cantor's $2B bitcoin-lending business is documented only as talks (Bloomberg, Nov 2024), not as a confirmed funder.
- No tariff-refund trade by Cantor is documented. Wired reported a sales pitch and an email claiming a ~$10M trade, and Cantor says none was executed.

## Denials and context

- Commerce (spokesperson Kristen Eichamer) called Axios's account of a Bessent-Lutnick Situation Room confrontation false, and did not immediately address the claim that Lutnick stalled the curbs. The Axios reporting rests on unnamed sources.
- Megaspeed says Nvidia, Malaysia's trade ministry and BIS inspections found no illegal chip transfers. The Megaspeed probe was reportedly paused at DOJ's request.
- Tether: USDT 'is not a haven for sanctioned actors'. It says it helped freeze ~$550M of Iran-linked USDT in 2026 and claims a 94% volume drop, with no stated methodology.
- Cantor: it never executed or held tariff-refund positions. Commerce: Lutnick 'has no insight or strategic control over Cantor Fitzgerald'.
- Cantor previously declined to give the size or use of the Tether loan. It says the trusts' purchase was funded from multiple sources at market terms (see C35).
- The congressional probes are led by Democrats in the minority without subpoena power (Blumenthal at PSI, Warren and Wyden, Raskin). None alleges a specific crime by Lutnick.
- Research limits: blumenthal.senate.gov did not resolve in this environment, and other direct fetches were not attempted for blocked news sites. Findings rest on search summaries that agree across several outlets. Cantor's replies to the Raskin and Blumenthal letters, and any result of those inquiries, were not found.

## Full report

**Verdict: mostly-accurate** (confidence: medium)

Parents: C35 (Lutnick, Cantor and Tether) and C08 (UAE chips and Stargate). This is a follow-up lead the video did not cover. C35 already established the Cantor custody of Tether's reserves, the $600M convertible bond, the May 2025 transfer to the children's trusts and the Bloomberg report of the Tether loan, so this report does not repeat them.

### Summary

All five leads point to real, dated reporting or official documents. Two are overstated:

- Tether's role in Cantor's $2B bitcoin-lending programme is documented only as talks.
- No completed Cantor trade in tariff-refund claims is documented, and Cantor denies executing any.

The chip-curb story rests on Axios's unnamed sources, and Commerce partly denies it. Every link between policy and Lutnick-family gain is still an allegation from minority-party lawmakers.

### Sub-claims

#### 1. Axios: Lutnick stalled curbs on Nvidia-chip diversion to China: confirmed (as reporting)
On 11 Nov 2026 Axios published two scoops based on unnamed high-level sources. They say:
- Trump approved action against firms suspected of diverting Nvidia chips to China at least three times in 2026.
- Lutnick held it up, arguing it "will start a trade war with China".
- Treasury Secretary Bessent confronted Lutnick in the Situation Room over Megaspeed, a Singapore company. [1][2]

Reuters' summary adds that Trump consulted Elon Musk before approving the action. [3]

Other coverage adds three points:
- BIS has posted no Entity List additions since Oct 2025.
- The Megaspeed probe was paused at DOJ's request.
- The NYT reported that a Commerce under secretary stopped the probe after Nvidia intervened. [5]

The other side:
- Commerce spokesperson Kristen Eichamer called the Bessent exchange "false" and did not address the rest.
- Megaspeed says inspections by Nvidia, Malaysia and BIS found no illegal transfers. [5]

No independent confirmation of the internal deliberations was found.

#### 2. Blumenthal probe of Tether and Iranian shadow banking: confirmed
On 28 Sep 2026 the PSI minority staff (ranking member Blumenthal) released "Tethered to Terrorism". It found that 84% of 846 assessed sanctioned or seizure-targeted Iran-linked wallets transacted mainly in USDT. Referral letters went to Treasury Secretary Bessent and to the Attorney General. [6][7][8]

This is a minority-staff report, not a committee finding or an enforcement action. Tether says it helped freeze about $550M of Iran-linked USDT in 2026. Ardoino later claimed a 94% drop in Iran-linked volumes without giving a methodology. [12][13]

#### 3. Blumenthal probe of the Tether loan to the Lutnick children's trust: mostly accurate
The 8 Oct 2026 letter was addressed to Cantor chairman Brandon Lutnick, not to the Commerce Secretary. It makes 13 requests with a 23 Oct deadline. They cover:
- Cantor's Tether services and custody arrangements.
- The terms of Howard Lutnick's exit, and any Tether loan or support that helped move the stake to his children.

The letter also cites more than $250M received by Howard Lutnick, including a $192M Cantor distribution, and an estimated ~$10B value for Cantor's Tether position. [9][10][11]

Summaries say "children" rather than naming the trust. The "Dynasty Trust A" detail comes from Bloomberg (18 Mar 2026) and the Warren/Wyden letters of 29 Apr 2026 (see C35). [14] No reply from Cantor was found.

#### 4. Lutnick's seat on the White House crypto council: confirmed
EO 14178 (23 Jan 2025) created the President's Working Group on Digital Asset Markets, chaired by David Sacks. It names the Commerce Secretary as a member, so the seat is not merely "possible". Lutnick is listed in coverage of the group's 30 Jul 2025 report. [15][16][17][18]

Sludge, Salon and Accountable.US flagged the Tether conflict in Feb 2025. Lutnick declined at confirmation to commit to recusing, and no recusal from the group was found. His actual role in the group's work is not documented.

#### 5. Cantor's ~$2B bitcoin-lending programme "with Tether": partly accurate
- Jul 2024: Cantor announced the $2B programme, with further $2B tranches planned. [21]
- Nov 2024: Bloomberg reported that Cantor was in talks for Tether's backing, as one of several funders. [19][20]
- 2025 launch: coverage names FalconX (~$100M) and Maple Finance as first borrowers, and Anchorage Digital and Copper as custodians. [22][23]

None of the launch reports found confirms that Tether funded the programme.

#### 6. Lutnick's sons' Cantor deals that depend on tariff policy (refund-claims trading): partly accurate
In July 2025 Wired reported that Cantor salespeople offered to buy importers' IEEPA tariff-refund rights at 20-30% of duties paid. One email claimed a ~$10M trade had "already" been done.

After the Supreme Court's February 2026 ruling against the IEEPA tariffs, Rep. Raskin wrote to Howard and Brandon Lutnick on 27 Feb 2026 asking for records. [24][25]

Cantor's answer: it "has never executed any transactions or taken any position on tariffs refund claims". Salespeople explored the idea and the firm dropped it. Semafor reported that Cantor did not profit from the ruling. [26][27]

Commerce's answer: Lutnick "has no insight or strategic control over Cantor Fitzgerald".

The pitch is documented. A completed trade or profit is not.

### Corrections
- Blumenthal's loan letter went to Brandon Lutnick at Cantor. Warren and Wyden wrote to Howard Lutnick and Tether directly.
- The "crypto council" is the President's Working Group, and the Commerce seat is set by the executive order.
- Tether's part in Cantor's bitcoin lending is documented only as talks.
- No tariff-refund trade by Cantor is documented.

### Denials and context

- Megaspeed says no illegal chip transfers were found.
- Tether cites ~$550M in Iran-linked freezes and a 94% volume decline.
