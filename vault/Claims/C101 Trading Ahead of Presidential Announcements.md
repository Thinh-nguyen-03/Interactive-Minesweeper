# C101: Trading Ahead of Presidential Announcements

**Verdict:** Mostly accurate · medium confidence  
**Type:** Check · Trump Media & Markets

## The Claim

> Were well-timed trades placed before market-moving presidential announcements, and has anyone been identified?

## Bottom Line

Market data, on-chain records and exchange data show well-timed, large trades shortly before at least six market-moving presidential announcements or actions since January 2025: leveraged crypto longs before the 2 March 2025 crypto-reserve post, same-day index calls in the minutes before the 9 April 2025 tariff pause, a roughly $1.1B crypto short built up over about 28 hours before the 10 October 2025 China-tariff post, with the last additions a minute before it, Polymarket bets before the Maduro raid and the February 2026 Iran strikes, and oil-futures selling minutes before Trump's Iran posts of 23 March and 7 April 2026.

## What Holds Up

- A trader opened large leveraged crypto longs shortly before Trump's 2 March 2025 Truth Social post announcing a US crypto strategic reserve.
- Bullish options were bought in the minutes before Trump's 9 April 2025 tariff-pause post, hours after he posted 'THIS IS A GREAT TIME TO BUY!!! DJT'.
- A Polymarket bettor profited from the 3 January 2026 capture of Maduro and has been identified.
- Fresh prediction-market accounts profited from bets on the 28 February 2026 US-Israeli strikes on Iran.
- Large oil-futures trades were placed minutes before Trump's Iran statements of 9 March, 23 March and 7 April 2026.

## What Needs Correcting

- A crypto whale opened a roughly $1.1B short shortly before Trump's 10 October 2025 post announcing 100% tariffs on China, and the trader has been identified (partly accurate)
- Claimed (C58): the 23 March 2026 oil trades are an unexplained bet by unknown traders. The record shows: the WSJ (May 2026) reported that CFTC documents name Qube Research & Technologies, Totsa and Forza Fund among the traders, with modest gains (about $5M, $200K and $10M) out of the roughly $800M traded, and that some trades followed a Semafor article published about 33 minutes before the post. None of the firms has been accused, and Qube says its trading is model-driven.
- Claimed (C58): Van Dyke faces 'money-laundering-type' counts. The indictment has five counts: unlawful use of confidential government information for personal gain, theft of nonpublic government information and commodities fraud (all under the Commodity Exchange Act), wire fraud, and an unlawful monetary transaction (a money-laundering statute). He pleaded not guilty on 28 April 2026; trial is set for 7 December 2026, and a defence motion to dismiss was opposed by prosecutors in August 2026, with no ruling found.
- Claimed (some summaries): the 10 October 2025 short was opened '30 hours' before the post. Claimed (House Oversight letter of 29 September 2026 and memo of 13 November 2026): it was established 'within minutes' of the announcement. The record shows: both describe parts of the same build-up. The article the committee cites says the account was funded at 16:39 UTC on 9 October and began shorting soon after (about 28-30 hours before the 20:50 UTC post), and that shorts were added until about 20:49 UTC. An earlier version of this check said the '30 hours' figure conflicted with the letter; it comes from the letter's own cited source.
- Claimed: a $2.5M options position became $70M+ on 9 April 2025. This appears only on blogs. Reuters documented an SPY $509 call block going from about $624K to about $10M, and Unusual Whales' own write-up concerns a $504 call that went from $5.72 to $41.02.
- 2026-11-16 (audit): the earlier statement that the only reported pre-2 April 2025 activity was one State Department official's sale was incomplete; officials' disclosed sales from 19 March to 2 April 2025 are listed in C102.

## What Is Unproven

- Unusual trades were placed before the 2 April 2025 'Liberation Day' tariff announcement.
- Well-timed bets were placed before the June 2025 US strikes on Iran.
- Anyone in the administration, or close to Trump, has been shown to have traded ahead of these announcements.

## In More Detail

- Whether any of this was trading on inside information is unverified in every case but one.
- The only trader charged is Army Master Sgt. Gannon Van Dyke, accused over the Maduro bets; he has pleaded not guilty and is not tied to the White House.
- A Hyperliquid wallet was attributed by on-chain analysts to Garrett Jin, who denies any Trump link.
- Three firms the WSJ found among the 23 March oil traders (Qube, Totsa, Forza) have not been accused of anything, and some of those trades followed a Semafor report published before the post.
- No authority has made a finding against any official, and the White House calls the insinuations baseless.
- No case of unusual market-wide trading was found before the 2 April 2025 'Liberation Day' announcement, though officials' own sales in the days before it are disclosed (C102).
- Separately, Trump's own accounts made 327 disclosed stock purchases on 8 April 2025, the day before the pause (C102); those are not the options trades examined here, and the filing does not show who directed them.

## Sub-Claims

### 1. A trader opened large leveraged crypto longs shortly before Trump's 2 March 2025 Truth Social post announcing a US crypto strategic reserve.

**Confirmed**

- (1) On-chain data, as reported by HyperDash and Lookonchain via Cointelegraph, Decrypt and The Block on 3 March 2025, shows that a wallet deposited about $5.9M USDC on Hyperliquid on 1 March.
- It opened about $200M of 50x-leveraged BTC and ETH longs, the first ETH long at 14:49 UTC on Sunday 2 March.
- Trump's first reserve post came about 35 minutes later and named XRP, SOL and ADA.
- A follow-up post naming BTC and ETH came nearly two hours after that.
- The wallet closed for a profit of about $6.8M. (2) Some crypto outlets asked whether this was 'insider trading?'. (3) No lawmaker or regulator has alleged wrongdoing over this trade as far as found. (4) The trader is unidentified, and no regulator has made a finding.
- Hyperliquid is an offshore platform outside CFTC supervision.
- A crypto reserve had been a campaign promise, the White House crypto summit was already scheduled, and Lookonchain described the wallet as a frequent high-leverage trader, so the timing alone does not show advance knowledge.
- Insider trading here is unverified.
- Amounts: about $5.9M; about $200M; about $6.8M
- Dates: 3 March 2025; 1 March; 2 March
- Sources: [7](https://cointelegraph.com/news/trader-banks-nearly-7-million-from-trumps-crypto-reserve-announcement) [8](https://decrypt.co/308424/trader-nets-6-8m-after-well-timed-bitcoin-ethereum-bets-ahead-of-trumps-reserve-announcement) [9](https://www.theblock.co/post/344105/trader-makes-7-million-in-one-day-going-50x-long-bitcoin-ether-ahead-of-trumps-crypto-reserve-announcement)

### 2. Unusual trades were placed before the 2 April 2025 'Liberation Day' tariff announcement.

**Unverified**

- No market-data analysis was found that shows anomalous options, futures or crypto positioning just before the 2 April 2025 Rose Garden announcement.
- The event was pre-scheduled and held after the US stock-market close, and the coverage of 'well-timed' trades in that period concerns the 9 April pause instead.
- Disclosure filings do show sales by officials in the days before 2 April: Attorney General Bondi's Trump Media sale dated 2 April, Dan Scavino's on 1 April (single-source), Sergio Gor's on 27 March (his own 278-T), a trade-office official's on 25 and 27 March, and State Department officials' on 19 and 31 March (ProPublica).
- These are disclosure-filing matters covered in C102.
- ProPublica states there is no evidence that any of them was informed by nonpublic information, and no authority has alleged that it was.
- Dates: 2 April 2025; 9 April; 2 April; 1 April; 27 March
- Sources: [6](https://www.propublica.org/article/us-officials-stock-sales-trump-tariffs)

### 3. Bullish options were bought in the minutes before Trump's 9 April 2025 tariff-pause post, hours after he posted 'THIS IS A GREAT TIME TO BUY!!! DJT'.

**Confirmed**

- Timeline (ET), per reporting: 9:33 a.m. 'BE COOL!
- Everything is going to work out well' (cited by Reuters); 9:37 a.m. 'THIS IS A GREAT TIME TO BUY!!!
- DJT'; from about 1:01 p.m. a burst of buying in same-day SPY calls and in QQQ and TQQQ calls; 1:18 p.m. the 90-day pause post; the S&P 500 then rose about 9.5% on the day. (1) The data, from Reuters, the WSJ, Unusual Whales and Benzinga: Reuters reported that SPY $509 same-day calls went from about $624K to about $10M in value, and Unusual Whales flagged those calls as up about 2,100% in an hour.
- Unusual Whales' own write-up describes a $504 same-day call that went from $5.72 to $41.02, with nearly 20,000 contracts traded just before the post.
- Record overall volume of about 85M contracts that day makes outliers hard to isolate, Reuters noted. (2) Analysts noted the timing.
- Reuters could not tell whether one trader or many placed the bets, or whether anyone cashed out.
- Figures of '$2.5M to $70M+' come from blogs only and are unverified. (3) Democratic senators (Warren, Schumer, Schiff and others) and Rep. Waters asked the SEC to investigate (see C58), and Rep. Ocasio-Cortez called for congressional trade disclosure. (4) No trader has been identified.
- The SEC declined to comment, and no SEC action has been reported.
- The White House said the 9:37 post was meant to reassure markets.
- Insider trading is unverified.
- Amounts: about 9.5%; $509; about $624K; about $10M; about 2,100%
- Sources: [1](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/well-timed-options-trades-ahead-211154780.html) [2](https://unusualwhales.substack.com/p/unusual-options-trading-before-trumps) [3](https://itiger.com/news/2526480315) [4](https://www.benzinga.com/government/regulations/25/04/44728276/spy-qqq-call-volumes-spiked-minutes-before-tariff-pause-announcement-alexandria-ocasio-cortez-demands-disclosure-from-congress-members) [5](https://gigazine.net/gsc_news/en/20250416-70-million-60-seconds)

### 4. A crypto whale opened a roughly $1.1B short shortly before Trump's 10 October 2025 post announcing 100% tariffs on China, and the trader has been identified.

**Partly accurate**

- (1) The data: on-chain records show that a newly funded Hyperliquid account built about $1.1B of BTC and ETH perpetual shorts and closed them for a profit reported at about $150M-$200M. That day's crash liquidated about $19B of leveraged positions.
- Trump's 100%-tariff post is timed at about 20:50 UTC (4:50 p.m. ET).
- The reports of timing describe one build-up, not two conflicting events.
- An analysis by Charles-Henry Monchau of Bank Syz (21 October 2025, read in full; republished by Investing.com on 22 October and cited as the factual source in both the House Oversight letter and the committee's 13 November 2026 memo) says the account received about $80M at 16:39 UTC on 9 October and soon opened a bitcoin short of about $420M (citing Arkham), which grew over the following hours; ether shorts were added early on 10 October; a large short was in place by 15:30 UTC (citing Lookonchain; its figure of 6,200 BTC conflicts with other reports of about 3,600 BTC); and more was added in the last hour, the final 200 BTC about one minute before the 20:50 UTC post.
- The Block and Cointelegraph also put the last addition at 20:49 UTC.
- The same article's summary line says the position was 'opened 30 hours before Trump's post'.
- So the first shorts came roughly 28-30 hours before the post and the last additions within a minute of it: the letter's 'within minutes' describes the end of the build-up and '30 hours' its start.
- The 9 October start rests on that article's reading of on-chain data and was not independently re-derived here.
- Part of the position therefore predates even the earlier, public tariff threat of 10 October.
- An earlier Trump post the same day threatening a 'massive increase of tariffs' on China had already pushed bitcoin and stocks lower.
- That threat was public, so part of the move was foreseeable from public information. (2) Arkham Intelligence labelled the wallet a 'Trump insider whale', and on-chain sleuth 'Eye' linked it to Garrett Jin, a former BitForex CEO.
- Other analysts disputed the link. (3) No regulator has made an allegation.
- On 29 September 2026 House Oversight Chairman James Comer (R) wrote to Hyperliquid's Jeff Yan citing reports of a large short opened 'within minutes of a presidential announcement concerning a U.S. tariff policy in October 2025 that was not publicly known at the time'.
- He asked for KYC, surveillance and referral records by 13 October 2026.
- The letter calls the trade 'precisely timed to a nonpublic government decision' and says it 'mirrors a pattern of insider trading' the committee is investigating, but it names no trader, relies on a press article for the facts, and makes no finding. (4) Identification rests only on contested on-chain attribution.
- Jin said on 13 October 2025 that he has 'no connection with the Trump family' or Donald Trump Jr., that this 'isn't insider trading', and that the funds belong to clients.
- Hyperliquid said it is reviewing Comer's request.
- No official finding has been made.
- Insider trading is unverified.
- Amounts: about $1.1B; about $150M-$200M; about $19B; 100%; about $80M
- Dates: 21 October 2025; 22 October; 13 November 2026; 9 October; 10 October
- Sources: [10](https://www.theblock.co/post/374383/alleged-hyperliquid-whale-denies-trump-ties-thanks-cz-for-doxing-and-pitches-market-stabilization-fund) [11](https://cointelegraph.com/news/garrett-jin-whale-denies-allegations-shorting-bitcoin) [12](https://www.scmp.com/tech/blockchain/article/3328948/bitcoin-crash-sparks-search-mystery-crypto-trader-who-profited-us200-million) [13](https://www.nysun.com/article/anonymous-crypto-trader-made-at-least-160-million-shorting-bitcoin-ethereum-in-moments-before-trumps-china-tariff-announcement) [14](https://coingape.com/bitcoin-falls-after-trump-threatens-massive-increase-in-tariffs-on-china) [15](https://oversight.house.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Hyperliquid-Letter-9.29.26.pdf) [16](https://www.theblock.co/post/417192/comer-presses-crypto-com-hyperliquid-predictit-identity-checks-suspicious-trades) [41](https://blog.syzgroup.com/slow-food-for-thought/the-1011-crypto-crash-and-the-mystery-of-the-1-billion-whale)

### 5. Well-timed bets were placed before the June 2025 US strikes on Iran.

**Unverified**

- The Algorithmic Data Collective, a data-journalism group, documented small Polymarket bets placed before US jets struck Fordow on 21 June 2025, at about 6 p.m. Washington time.
- One account bet nearly $9,000 at long odds, and another ('0xoracle') bet about $4,000 and won more than $20,000.
- No large oil-futures or options anomaly before the strike or the 23 June ceasefire post was found in the coverage searched.
- The bettors are unidentified, and no authority has opened an inquiry that was found.
- These sums are small, and the strikes had been widely anticipated.
- Amounts: nearly $9,000; about $4,000; more than $20,000
- Dates: 21 June 2025; 23 June
- Sources: [17](https://acdatacollective.org/work/profiting-from-war-mysterious-bets-on-polymarket/)

### 6. A Polymarket bettor profited from the 3 January 2026 capture of Maduro and has been identified.

**Confirmed**

- This is the one identified case.
- According to DOJ (SDNY, unsealed 23 April 2026), Army Master Sgt. Gannon Ken Van Dyke, a Fort Bragg soldier who helped plan the raid, opened a Polymarket account around 26 December 2025.
- He placed about 13 bets from 27 December to 2 January, staking about $33,000, and made about $409,881.
- On 6 January he asked Polymarket to delete his account.
- The indictment has five counts: unlawful use of confidential government information for personal gain, theft of nonpublic government information, commodities fraud, wire fraud and an unlawful monetary transaction.
- The CFTC's parallel civil complaint (read in full) dates the trading to 30 December 2025 to 2 January 2026 (more than 436,000 'Yes' shares) and puts the profit at more than $404,000.
- He pleaded not guilty on 28 April 2026.
- Trial was set for 7 December 2026.
- His motion to dismiss argues that the Polymarket contracts were event contracts rather than swaps; prosecutors opposed it in August 2026 (ABC News), and no ruling was found.
- Polymarket says it flagged the trades.
- The bets concerned a military operation, not a presidential post, and nothing ties the case to the White House or the Trump family.
- He is presumed innocent.
- Amounts: about $33,000; about $409,881; more than $404,000
- Dates: 23 April 2026; 26 December 2025; 27 December; 2 January; 6 January
- Sources: [18](https://abc7ny.com/post/us-soldier-charged-using-intel-win-400k-maduro-raid-appear-court-nyc/18985601/) [19](https://decrypt.co/370571/december-trial-date-us-soldier-insider-trading-polymarket) [20](https://www.goodmorningamerica.com/news/story/doj-arrests-soldier-made-400000-betting-maduros-removal-132325426) [21](https://www.npr.org/transcripts/nx-s1-5667232) [42](https://www.cftc.gov/PressRoom/PressReleases/9217-26) [43](https://www.goodmorningamerica.com/news/story/soldier-charged-polymarket-trades-seeks-dismissal-135797525)

### 8. Large oil-futures trades were placed minutes before Trump's Iran statements of 9 March, 23 March and 7 April 2026.

**Confirmed**

- (1) The data. 9 March: the BBC found unusually large bets on falling oil 47 minutes before Trump told CBS the war was 'very complete, pretty much'.
- 23 March: the FT, BBC, Reuters and CBS reported about $500M-$580M of Brent and WTI futures traded in roughly one minute around 6:49-6:50 a.m. ET, about 15 minutes before Trump posted about 'VERY GOOD AND PRODUCTIVE CONVERSATIONS' with Iran and a delay to strikes on Iranian energy sites.
- The WSJ, using LSEG data, put the window's total at more than $800M, and US oil then fell by as much as 13%.
- 7 April: about $950M of bets on falling oil came hours before the two-week ceasefire post, and at least 50 new Polymarket accounts bet on a ceasefire in the hours before it.
- A study by Timothy Graham and colleagues at the Queensland University of Technology (QUT) covered 173 oil-related posts in a 73-day window and found 15 events with abnormal pre-post trading, with only oil-related posts clearly abnormal.
- The authors put the chance of the pattern at about one in a billion and said their data 'doesn't prove that insider trading is taking place'. (2) The inference is contested.
- Some experts call it 'deeply suspicious'.
- Others point to hedging, momentum, or news already public.
- The WSJ reported that some 23 March trades followed a Semafor article published about 33 minutes before the post. (3) Sens. Warren, Whitehouse and Murphy and Rep. Torres pressed the CFTC and SEC. (4) The CFTC, with DOJ, is examining the trades (Reuters, 15 April 2026) and sought trader identities from CME and ICE.
- The WSJ (May 2026) named Qube Research & Technologies (about $5M gain), Totsa (TotalEnergies' trading arm, about $200K) and Forza Fund (about $10M) among the 23 March traders.
- None has been accused of wrongdoing, and Qube said its trading is 'model-driven'.
- Bloomberg reported on 13 October 2026, citing anonymous sources, that an adviser to Chair Selig had a staff enforcement referral retracted.
- An agency official said the review was being broadened internationally (see C58 and C103).
- No charges or findings have been reported.
- Amounts: about $500M-$580M; more than $800M; 13%; about $950M; about $5M
- Dates: 9 March; 23 March; 7 April; 15 April 2026; May 2026
- Sources: [24](https://torre.news/580-million-in-oil-bets-placed-moments-before-trumps-iran-post-ft) [32](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/04/15/regulators-reportedly-zeroing-in-on-suspicious-trades-ahead-of-trump-post.html) [36](https://www.aa.com.tr/en/americas/suspicious-800m-oil-trade-linked-to-trump-remarks-trigger-probe/3942896) [37](https://www.hedgeweek.com/cftc-probes-800m-oil-trading-spike/) [38](https://news.az/news/suspicious-800m-oil-trades-after-trump-remarks-spark-regulatory-probe) [29](https://risingnepaldaily.com/news/78995) [30](https://stories.theconversation.com/the-market-moves-before-trump-posts/) [31](https://www.qut.edu.au/news/realfocus/the-market-moves-before-trump-posts) [33](https://www.banking.senate.gov/newsroom/minority/warren-whitehouse-probe-suspicious-oil-trades-surrounding-trump-iran-announcements) [34](https://ritchietorres.house.gov/posts/rep-torres-calls-on-cftc-to-investigate-suspicious-polymarket-trades-ahead-of-trumps-iran-ceasefire-announcement) [35](https://wtop.com/national/2026/04/well-timed-bets-on-polymarket-tied-to-the-iran-war-draw-calls-for-investigations-from-lawmakers/) [40](https://politicalwire.com/2026/10/13/cftc-staff-told-to-retract-analysis-of-suspicious-oil-trades/)

### 9. Anyone in the administration, or close to Trump, has been shown to have traded ahead of these announcements.

**Unverified**

- No filing, charge, court record or regulatory finding identifies an administration official, a Trump family member or a close associate as having placed any of the trades in these episodes.
- Separately, Trump's own 2025 annual disclosure (read in full) lists 327 stock purchases in one of his investment accounts on 8 April 2025, the day before the pause (C102).
- Those are disclosed portfolio trades, not the options trades in this episode; the filing does not show who directed them, and the White House says independent managers run the accounts.
- Officials' sales in the days before 2 April are also disclosed (C102).
- The traders who have been named publicly are a soldier who has been charged (Van Dyke), a crypto investor named only by contested on-chain attribution who denies any link (Jin), and three trading firms the WSJ named without any accusation (Qube, Totsa, Forza).
- Reuters (29 March 2026) and the WSJ (March 2026) both summarised the pattern and both stated that there is no evidence of leaks or of insiders trading in any of the incidents.
- White House spokesman Kush Desai called the insinuations 'baseless and irresponsible reporting' and said the White House does not tolerate officials profiting illegally from inside information.
- On 24 March 2026 the White House emailed staff warning against trading futures or prediction markets on nonpublic information (see C58).

## Denials & Context

- The White House (Kush Desai, March 2026) called suggestions that officials traded on inside information 'baseless and irresponsible reporting'.
  - In April 2025 it said the 'great time to buy' post was meant to reassure markets.
- Garrett Jin denies any connection with the Trump family or Donald Trump Jr. and says the October 2025 short was not insider trading.
  - He says the funds belong to clients and objected to Changpeng Zhao sharing his personal information.
- Qube Research & Technologies told the WSJ its trading is model-driven and not based on comments or geopolitical updates.
  - Forza Fund could not be reached, and an associated Chinese firm said it was unaware of any CFTC investigation.
- Hyperliquid said it is aware of Chairman Comer's request and is reviewing it.
- CFTC Chair Selig said in April 2026 that enforcement against threats to markets is 'a top priority'.
  - The CFTC does not confirm or deny investigations.
  - An agency official told Bloomberg that the referral was withdrawn so the review could be broadened internationally.
- Polymarket says it detected and reported the Maduro trades.
  - Polymarket and Kalshi have tightened their insider-trading rules.
  - Donald Trump Jr. advises Kalshi and, through 1789 Capital, invested in Polymarket (see C25).
  - That ties the family to the platforms, not to any bet.
- Experts note that trades ahead of news can reflect hedging, momentum, reaction to earlier public posts or press reports (such as Semafor on 23 March 2026, or the morning tariff threat on 10 October 2025), or informed speculation.
  - Insider-trading law for commodities and event contracts is complex, and proving the source of information is hard.
- Well-timed trading is not proof that anyone traded on inside information.
  - Except for the Van Dyke charges, which are unproven, no trader in these episodes has been charged and no official finding has been made.
- Trump's own 8 April 2025 purchases (C102) are recorded in his annual disclosure; the White House says his assets are held in fully discretionary accounts managed by independent third-party institutions, and the filing does not show who directed the trades.

## Full Report

**Verdict:** mostly-accurate (confidence: medium)

### The claim

The question: were unusually large or well-timed trades placed shortly before market-moving presidential announcements since January 2025, and has anyone been identified? Each episode below is separated into four parts:

1. What the market data shows.
2. What analysts or outlets infer from it.
3. Who alleges wrongdoing.
4. Whether any trader has been identified or any authority has made a finding.

This check builds on C58 (market-moving posts and insider-trading allegations) and C20 (Trump's stock posts and his own portfolio). Trades by officials, family members and members of Congress are covered in C102. Probes, letters and the applicable law are covered in C103.

### Summary

Data documents well-timed, large positions before at least six announcements or actions:

- The 2 March 2025 crypto-reserve post.
- The 9 April 2025 tariff pause.
- The 10 October 2025 100% China-tariff post.
- The 3 January 2026 Maduro raid.
- The 28 February 2026 Iran strikes.
- Trump's Iran statements of 9 March, 23 March and 7 April 2026.

Well-timed trading is not proof of insider trading.

- **The one charged case** is a soldier who helped plan the Maduro raid (Van Dyke). He is accused of betting on Polymarket, has pleaded not guilty, and is not tied to the White House.
- **The October 2025 crypto short** has been attributed to Garrett Jin only by contested on-chain analysis, and he denies any link to the Trump family.
- **The 23 March 2026 oil trades:** three trading firms the WSJ named are not accused of wrongdoing. Some of their trades followed a Semafor report published before Trump's post.
- **No official found:** no filing, charge or regulatory finding identifies any official, family member or associate as a trader in these episodes. The White House calls the insinuations baseless.
- **2 April 2025:** no anomalous market-wide trading was found before the "Liberation Day" announcement, though officials' own sales in the days before it are disclosed (C102).
- **Trump's own accounts:** his annual disclosure lists 327 stock purchases in one of his accounts on 8 April 2025, the day before the pause (C102). These are disclosed portfolio trades, not the anonymous options trades below, and the filing does not show who directed them.

### Episodes

#### 1. 2 March 2025: crypto strategic reserve post. Trade confirmed; insider trading unverified

- **Data.** On-chain data (HyperDash and Lookonchain, reported by Cointelegraph, Decrypt and The Block on 3 March 2025) shows the following:
  - On 1 March a wallet deposited about $5.9M USDC on Hyperliquid.
  - It opened about $200M of 50x BTC and ETH longs. The first ETH long was at 14:49 UTC on Sunday 2 March [7][8][9].
  - Trump's first reserve post came about 35 minutes later and named XRP, SOL and ADA. BTC and ETH were added in a second post nearly two hours later.
  - The wallet made about $6.8M.
- **Inference.** Some crypto outlets asked "insider trading?".
- **Context.** A reserve had been a campaign promise and the White House crypto summit was already scheduled. Lookonchain described the wallet as a frequent high-leverage trader.
- **Allegation.** No lawmaker or regulator has alleged wrongdoing over this trade, as far as found.
- **Identified?** No. Hyperliquid is offshore and outside CFTC supervision.

#### 2. 2 April 2025: "Liberation Day" tariffs. Unverified (nothing found)

- No analysis was found showing anomalous pre-announcement positioning.
- The announcement was pre-scheduled and came after the US stock-market close.
- Disclosure filings do show officials' sales in the days before 2 April [6]: Bondi's Trump Media sale dated 2 April, Scavino's on 1 April (single-source), Gor's on 27 March (his own 278-T), a trade-office official's on 25 and 27 March, and State Department officials' on 19 and 31 March. These are covered in C102. ProPublica states there is no evidence any of them used nonpublic information, and no authority has alleged it.

#### 3. 9 April 2025: "THIS IS A GREAT TIME TO BUY!!! DJT" and the tariff pause. Trades confirmed; insider trading unverified

- **Timeline (ET):**

  | Time | Event |
  |---|---|
  | 9:33 a.m. | "BE COOL! Everything is going to work out well" (cited by Reuters) |
  | 9:37 a.m. | "THIS IS A GREAT TIME TO BUY!!! DJT" |
  | about 1:01 p.m. | Burst of same-day SPY call buying, plus QQQ and TQQQ calls |
  | 1:18 p.m. | 90-day pause post; the S&P 500 then rose about 9.5% on the day |

- **Data.**
  - Reuters reported that a block of SPY $509 same-day calls went from about $624K to about $10M in value [1].
  - Unusual Whales flagged those calls as up about 2,100% in an hour. Its write-up also cites a $504 call that went from $5.72 to $41.02, with nearly 20,000 contracts traded just before the post [2].
  - The WSJ reported the trades from just after 1 p.m. [3].
  - About 85M options contracts traded that day, a record, which Reuters said makes suspicious trades hard to isolate.
  - The "$2.5M to $70M+" figure is blog-only and unverified [5].
- **Inference.** Reuters could not tell whether one trader or many placed the bets, or whether any profits were realised.
- **Allegation.**
  - Senate Democrats and Rep. Waters asked the SEC to investigate (see C58).
  - Rep. Ocasio-Cortez called for trade disclosure by members of Congress [4].
- **Identified?** No.
  - The SEC declined to comment, and no SEC action has been reported.
  - The White House said the 9:37 post was meant to reassure markets.

#### 4. 10 October 2025: 100% China tariff post and the Hyperliquid short. Trade confirmed; identification contested; insider trading unverified

- **Data.**
  - A newly funded Hyperliquid account built about $1.1B of BTC and ETH perpetual shorts and closed them for a profit reported at about $150M-$200M [10][12][13].
  - Trump's 100% post is timed at about 20:50 UTC (4:50 p.m. ET).
  - **Timeline of the build-up.** An analysis by Charles-Henry Monchau of Bank Syz (21 October 2025, read in full) [41], republished by Investing.com and cited as the factual source in both the House Oversight letter and the committee's November 2026 memo, describes one build-up:
    - 16:39 UTC, 9 October: the account receives about $80M and soon opens a bitcoin short of about $420M (citing Arkham), which grows over the following hours.
    - Early 10 October: ether shorts added; a large short is in place by 15:30 UTC (citing Lookonchain; its 6,200 BTC figure conflicts with other reports of about 3,600 BTC).
    - Last hour before the post: more added, the final 200 BTC about one minute before 20:50 UTC. The Block and Cointelegraph also put the last addition at 20:49 UTC.
  - The same article's summary says the position was "opened 30 hours before Trump's post". So "30 hours" describes the start of the build-up and the letter's "within minutes" its end. They do not conflict. The 9 October start rests on that article's reading of on-chain data and was not independently re-derived here.
- **Important context.**
  - Earlier that day a Trump post threatening "a massive increase of tariffs" on China had already pushed bitcoin and stocks down [14]. Part of the move was therefore foreseeable from public information. Part of the position, however, predates even that post.
  - About $19B of leveraged positions were liquidated that day.
- **Inference.**
  - Arkham Intelligence labelled the wallet a "Trump insider whale".
  - On-chain sleuth "Eye" linked it to Garrett Jin, a former BitForex CEO. Other analysts disputed the link.
- **Allegation.**
  - On 29 September 2026 House Oversight Chairman James Comer (R) wrote to Hyperliquid co-founder Jeff Yan [15][16]. The letter cites reports of a large short opened "within minutes of a presidential announcement concerning a U.S. tariff policy in October 2025 that was not publicly known at the time".
  - It asks for records by 13 October 2026, back to 1 January 2024, covering KYC, surveillance criteria, referrals to DOJ and the CFTC, geo-block evasion, and staff with security clearances.
  - The letter (read in full in the audit) calls the trade "precisely timed to a nonpublic government decision" and says it "mirrors a pattern of insider trading" the committee is investigating. It names no trader, relies on a press article for the facts, and makes no finding.
- **Identified?** Only by contested on-chain attribution.
  - Jin said on 13 October 2025 that he has "no connection with the Trump family" or Donald Trump Jr., that it "isn't insider trading", and that the funds belong to clients [10][11]. He also objected to Changpeng Zhao sharing his personal information.
  - Hyperliquid said it is reviewing Comer's request.
  - No official finding has been made.

#### 5. June 2025: US strikes on Iran. Unverified

- The Algorithmic Data Collective documented small Polymarket bets placed before the strike on Fordow on 21 June 2025, at about 6 p.m. Washington time [17]:
  - One account bet nearly $9,000.
  - Another ("0xoracle") bet about $4,000 and won more than $20,000.
- No oil-futures or options anomaly was found in the coverage searched.
- The bettors are unidentified, and no inquiry was found. The sums are small and the strikes had been widely anticipated.

#### 6. 3 January 2026: capture of Maduro. Identified and charged (not convicted)

DOJ (SDNY, unsealed 23 April 2026) alleges the following [18][19][20][21]:

- Army Master Sgt. Gannon Ken Van Dyke, who helped plan the raid, opened a Polymarket account around 26 December 2025.
- He placed about 13 bets from 27 December to 2 January, staking about $33,000, and made about $409,881.
- On 6 January he asked Polymarket to delete his account, falsely saying he had lost access to his email.

Charges and status:

- The indictment has five counts: unlawful use of confidential government information for personal gain, theft of nonpublic government information, commodities fraud, wire fraud and an unlawful monetary transaction.
- The CFTC's parallel civil complaint (read in full) [42] dates the trading to 30 December 2025 to 2 January 2026 (more than 436,000 "Yes" shares) and puts the profit at more than $404,000.
- He pleaded not guilty on 28 April 2026. Trial is set for 7 December 2026. His motion to dismiss argues the Polymarket contracts were event contracts, not swaps; prosecutors opposed it in August 2026 [43], and no ruling was found.
- Polymarket says it flagged the trades.

The bets concerned a military operation, not a presidential post. Nothing ties the case to the White House or the Trump family.

#### 8. 9 March, 23 March and 7 April 2026: Iran statements and oil futures. Data confirmed; firms named but not accused; no finding

**Timeline.**

- **9 March.** The BBC found unusually large bets on falling oil 47 minutes before Trump told CBS the war was "very complete, pretty much" [29].
- **23 March.**
  - About $500M-$580M of Brent and WTI futures traded in roughly one minute around 6:49-6:50 a.m. ET [24][25].
  - About 15 minutes later Trump posted about "VERY GOOD AND PRODUCTIVE CONVERSATIONS" with Iran and a delay to strikes on Iranian energy sites.
  - The WSJ (LSEG data) put the total for the window at more than $800M [36][37][38]. US oil then fell by as much as 13%.
- **7 April.**
  - About $950M of bets on falling oil came hours before the two-week ceasefire post.
  - At least 50 new Polymarket accounts bet on a ceasefire in the hours or minutes before it [35].

**Systematic analysis.** A study by Timothy Graham, Ella Choarzy and Stephen Harrington at the Queensland University of Technology (QUT) is summarised in The Conversation [30][31]:

- It covered 173 oil-related posts in a 73-day window and found 15 events with unusual pre-post trading.
- Only oil-related posts produced clearly abnormal moves, put at about one in a billion by chance.
- The authors state that the data "doesn't prove that insider trading is taking place".

**Inference.**

- Experts quoted by Reuters (29 March 2026) ranged from "deeply suspicious" to pointing out that trades may be hedges [27].
- The WSJ wrote that there is no evidence of leaks or of insiders trading in any of the incidents [28].
- The WSJ also reported that some 23 March trades followed a Semafor article published about 33 minutes before the post [37].

**Allegation.**

- Sen. Murphy publicly asked who placed the trades.
- Sens. Warren and Whitehouse asked the CFTC to investigate (10 April 2026) [33].
- Rep. Torres asked for a review of the Polymarket ceasefire bets and the oil trades [34].

**Identified or found?**

- The CFTC, with DOJ, is examining trades on CME and ICE and sought trader identities (Reuters, mid-April 2026) [32].
- Selig said enforcement is "a top priority" [39].
- The WSJ (May 2026) named Qube Research & Technologies (about $5M gain), Totsa, TotalEnergies' trading arm (about $200K) and Forza Fund (about $10M) [37][38]:
  - None is accused.
  - Qube says its trading is "model-driven".
  - Forza could not be reached.
- Bloomberg reported on 13 October 2026, citing anonymous sources, that an adviser to Selig, Mel Gunewardena, had a staff enforcement referral retracted. An agency official said the review was being broadened internationally [40] (see C58 and C103).
- No charges or findings have been reported.
