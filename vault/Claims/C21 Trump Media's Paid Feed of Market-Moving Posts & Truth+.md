# C21: Trump Media's Paid Feed of Market-Moving Posts & Truth+

**Verdict:** Mostly accurate · high confidence  
**Type:** Check · Trump Media & Markets

## The Claim

> Did Trump Media announce a paid real-time feed of Truth Social's most market-moving posts, reportedly priced at up to $100,000 a month, and what is its Truth+ streaming service?

## Bottom Line

TMTG did announce 'Truth API' on July 16, 2026. It is a paid, real-time licensed feed of 'the platform's most market-moving Truths', and interim CEO Kevin McGurn said 'Markets already move on Truth Social posts.'

## What Holds Up

- TMTG announced a paid API offering a real-time feed of the platform's most market-moving truths.
- Interim CEO Kevin McGurn said 'markets already move on Truth Social posts.'
- The company is reportedly charging $100,000 a month and has multiple buyers.

## What Needs Correcting

- You can pay to get the president's market-moving truths early, or at least in real time (partly accurate)
- TMTG also announced Truth+, a subscription streaming service offering Patriot packages with non-woke movies (partly accurate)
- The $100,000/month figure is the top of a $60,000-$100,000 range. The FT first reported it from anonymous sources, and TMTG confirmed the range on its August 2026 earnings call.
- 'Multiple buyers' understates it: TMTG claims more than 10 customer agreements, and Reuters named Bloomberg, Susquehanna and Jump Trading.
- Truth+ and its Patriot Package were not announced alongside the API. Truth+ dates to 2024, the Patriot Package to July 2025 and the non-woke film catalogue to August 2025.
- 'Early' access is disputed. TMTG says posts are public to all at the same moment and the API only speeds up machine delivery.

## In More Detail

- The FT, via Reuters, reported pricing of up to $100,000 a month, and TMTG later confirmed a $60K-$100K range and more than 10 customer agreements.
- Truth+ and its 'non-woke' Patriot Package are real, but they launched in 2024-2025, not alongside the API.
- TMTG also disputes that the feed gives 'early' access rather than faster delivery.

## Denials & Context

- TMTG calls Truth API an 'industry-standard' data product with significant demand and says it offers only 'fractionally faster' access to public posts.
  - It accused Senate Democrats of misrepresenting the service.
  - The White House referred questions to TMTG.
- Sens. Elizabeth Warren and Adam Schiff asked SEC Chair Paul Atkins on July 28-29, 2026 to investigate.
  - The SEC acknowledged receipt and declined to comment further.
  - No opened probe was found.
- Trump's revocable trust is TMTG's largest shareholder (about 41% per Reuters and Fortune citing filings), so feed revenue benefits him indirectly.
- The 'markets already move' premise holds only for some posts.
  - A JPMorgan analysis, reported by Axios in July 2026, found most Trump posts no longer move bond markets.
- TMTG is heavily loss-making (about $3.7M revenue and a $712M net loss for 2025), and McGurn said only 'modest' API revenue had been recognized.
- On about 2026-08-12, The Intercept and the Freedom of the Press Foundation sued Trump in his official capacity, together with White House aides, in federal court in Manhattan.
  - They allege Truth API violates the First and Fifth Amendments.
  - TMTG is not a defendant, and TMTG says presidential communications already flow through many platforms that offer subscription APIs.
  - The case was still pending as of late Oct 2026.
- Reuters (2026-10-28) said it could not independently verify TMTG's claim of more than 10 customer agreements; the edge sold is reportedly about 50 ms over the app.

## Full Report

**Verdict: mostly-accurate** (confidence: high on the API, medium on the Truth+ framing)

### The claim
- TMTG announced a paid API giving a "real time feed of the platform's most market moving truths."
- Interim CEO Kevin McGurn said: "markets already move on Truth Social posts."
- The company "is reportedly charging $100,000 a month and has multiple buyers."
- "So literally, you can pay money to get access to the president's market moving truths early, or at least in real time."

### Research note
Direct fetches of news sites (TheWrap, The Next Web and others) were blocked in this environment, so the findings below rest on search-result snippets from several independent outlets (Reuters syndications, CNN/Scripps syndications, NPR, Al Jazeera, Fortune, Cointelegraph, Quartz, the Senate Banking Committee minority, Benzinga and others). Every URL cited was returned by search. No outlet was relied on alone.

### Sub-claims

#### 1. TMTG announced a paid API with a real-time feed of the "most market-moving Truths": CONFIRMED
- **Announcement:** On July 16, 2026, Trump Media & Technology Group announced **Truth API** in a GlobeNewswire release. It is a licensed data service that gives financial-services partners "the fastest access to Truth Social's most influential posts."
- **The release quote:** McGurn is quoted saying Truth API "delivers a direct, licensed, real-time feed of the platform's most market-moving Truths while advancing our strategy to monetize proprietary assets through a high-margin, recurring revenue stream." The claim's wording matches.
- **Coverage and launch:** The feed covers the 10 most-followed or most influential accounts, which reportedly include Trump's. It includes an archive back to 2022 and is aimed at high-frequency and algorithmic trading firms. It went live on August 1, 2026.

#### 2. McGurn: "markets already move on Truth Social posts": CONFIRMED
- **The quote:** Several outlets (Cointelegraph, Arab News/AP, The Hill and others) quote McGurn saying "Markets already move on Truth Social posts" when announcing the product.
- **His role:** McGurn became interim CEO on about April 21-22, 2026, when Devin Nunes stepped down to chair the President's Intelligence Advisory Board.

#### 3. "Reportedly charging $100,000 a month and has multiple buyers": CONFIRMED, with nuance
- **Original report:** On July 17, 2026, the Financial Times reported that TMTG had pitched traders up to **$100,000 a month**, with a discounted roughly **$60,000 a month** rate on a three-year commitment. Reuters syndicated the story and said it could not verify it independently. At announcement, CNN said no pricing was available. The claim's "reportedly" is therefore apt.
- **Company confirmation:** In August 2026, on its first-ever analyst earnings call, Trump Media said it had signed **more than 10 customer agreements**, mostly with HFT firms. It said customers pay **between $60,000 and $100,000 a month**. McGurn said only "a modest amount of revenue" had been recognized so far.
- **Named customers:** Reuters, citing sources, later named **Bloomberg LP, Susquehanna International Group and Jump Trading** as subscribers. All three declined to comment.
- **Correction:** $100,000 is the top of the price range, not a flat fee.

#### 4. "Pay money to get access to the president's market-moving truths early, or at least in real time": PARTLY ACCURATE (characterization)
- **The company's position:** TMTG says every user sees posts at the same time. In its account, the API only provides "fractionally faster" machine-readable delivery of public information (McGurn), and it calls the API an "industry-standard" product.
- **How others describe it:** A spokesperson said delivery is "significantly faster" than a push notification. Some outlets (PhoneArena, Scripps headlines) described it as "early access."
- **The hedge:** The claim's own fallback phrase, "or at least in real time," is the defensible reading. Whether the latency gap gives a trading edge is the point in dispute, not an established fact of pre-publication access.
- **Ownership:** Trump is TMTG's largest shareholder through his revocable trust. Fortune and Reuters, citing filings, put the stake at roughly 41%; earlier figures were about 52-53%. Revenue from the feed therefore accrues in part to him.

#### 5. Truth+ is a subscription streaming service with "Patriot packages" and non-woke content: CONFIRMED, but not a new announcement
- **Not new:** Truth+ (originally "TMTG+") debuted in 2024, came to Roku in March 2025 (marketed as "the singular option for non-woke TV") and launched globally on July 7, 2025.
- **Patriot Package:** This paid tier ($9.99/month, free during beta) went into beta around early July 2025. It carries channels such as Newsmax, OAN, Real America's Voice, Salem and RSBN.
- **Films:** On August 7, 2025, Trump Media added Great American Media's on-demand catalogue to the Patriot Package. CEO Devin Nunes pitched it as "non-woke entertainment that affirms American values."
- **Timing issue:** The phrase "Meanwhile, they also announced" implies the launch came at the same time as the July 2026 API. Truth+ and the Patriot Package predate the API by one to two years.
- **NYT criticism:** The New York Times (via The Nightly) noted that Truth+ produces no original content and that much of its programming is available elsewhere, often free.

### Corrections
- The $100,000/month figure is the top of a $60,000-$100,000 range. It was first reported by the FT from anonymous sources, and the company later confirmed the range on its August 2026 earnings call.
- "Multiple buyers" understates it. TMTG says it has more than 10 customer agreements, and Reuters named Bloomberg, Susquehanna and Jump Trading.
- Truth+ and its Patriot Package were not newly announced alongside the API. Truth+ dates to 2024, the Patriot Package to July 2025 and the "non-woke" movie catalogue to August 2025.
- "Early" access is disputed. TMTG says posts are public to everyone at the same moment, and the API only delivers them faster to machines.

### Denials and context
- **TMTG:** Truth API is an industry-standard data product with broad demand. Posts appear to all users at the same time, and the API only offers fractionally faster delivery. TMTG accused Senate Democrats of misrepresenting the service. The White House referred questions to TMTG.
- **Critics:** Sens. Elizabeth Warren and Adam Schiff wrote to SEC Chair Paul Atkins (letter dated July 28-29, 2026) asking for an investigation into whether the feed is legal and fair. The SEC acknowledged receipt and gave no further comment. Tyler Gellasch of the Healthy Markets Association warned of "a two-tiered market." Sen. Ron Wyden also criticized the product, and a Washington Examiner opinion piece urged Trump to shut it down.
- **Market impact cuts both ways:**
  - Coverage in Axios of a JPMorgan analysis (July 2026) found that most Trump posts no longer move bond markets, although de-escalation posts still did.
  - A Conversation analysis found prices sometimes moved before the posts.
  - The premise "markets already move" is therefore true for some posts and not for most.
- **Lawsuit:** On about 2026-08-12, The Intercept and the Freedom of the Press Foundation sued Trump in his official capacity, together with White House aides, in federal court in Manhattan. They allege Truth API violates the First and Fifth Amendments. TMTG is not a defendant, and TMTG says presidential communications already flow through many platforms with subscription APIs. The case was pending as of late Oct 2026 [25][26].
- **Customer count:** The Reuters report naming Bloomberg, Susquehanna and Jump is dated 2026-10-28. Reuters said it could not independently confirm TMTG's "more than 10" customer figure. Company officials put the speed edge at about 50 ms [27].
- **Business context:** TMTG is loss-making. It reported about $3.7M in revenue and a $712M net loss for 2025. The API is pitched as a recurring revenue source.
