# C92: The White House Ballroom Donor List

**Verdict:** Partly accurate · medium confidence  
**Type:** Connection check · Tech & Media Companies

## The Claim

> Who donated to the White House ballroom, how much did each reportedly give, and what business did they have pending before the federal government?

## Bottom Line

The October 2025 roster of about 37 donors is documented. The amounts come from a single leaked spreadsheet relayed by the Washington Post (67 donations, about $333M, at least $77M anonymous), which Reuters did not verify.

## What Holds Up

- The two lists: the White House's October 2025 roster (about 37 names, no amounts) and the Washington Post's leaked spreadsheet (October 2026: 67 donations above $10,000, about $333M).
- Top tier ($25M each): Apple, Nvidia, the Adelson Family Foundation and Charles Ergen (mostly accurate)
- Rocket Companies (a subsidiary, $10M): the pledge came about three weeks after the FHFA approved Rocket's $9.4B Mr. Cooper takeover (2025-08-26). Rocket was not on the public list (mostly accurate)
- Isabela Herrera ($2.5M, Sep 2025): her father, banker Julio Herrera Velutini, was pardoned on 2026-01-15, about four months later (mostly accurate)
- The Winklevoss twins (listed separately in Oct 2025): the White House withdrew Brian Quintenz's CFTC nomination on 2025-09-30, about three weeks before the list, after the twins lobbied against him (mostly accurate)
- Counter-cases: donors that received adverse federal action within six months (Booz Allen, Comcast, Union Pacific, Meta, Google, Amazon, AT&T).
- Donors who were already appointees: Ambassadors Joseph Popolo Jr. ($1M, Netherlands) and Benjamin Leon Jr. (listed, Spain), and SBA Administrator Loeffler.
- Already in C68/C69, not repeated here: Nvidia (Oct 2025 gift; UAE/Saudi licences 2025-11-19; H200 China 2025-12-08), Microsoft ($10M; UAE licences Sep 2025), Palantir ($10M; Navy ShipOS 2025-12-09), Lockheed ($10M; F-35 Lot 18-19 2025-09-29), Ripple (charter 2025-12-12) (mostly accurate)

## What Needs Correcting

- Apple ($25M): the Aug 2025 chip-tariff exemption and the DOJ antitrust case (partly accurate)
- Charles Ergen/EchoStar ($25M): the FCC closed its EchoStar probe on 2025-09-08, three months before his first anonymous $12.5M. It approved the $40B AT&T and SpaceX spectrum sales on 2026-05-12, about three months after his second $12.5M (partly accurate)
- The Loeffler-Sprecher fund ($5M): a sitting cabinet official's household gave. Her husband's ICE invested up to $2B in Polymarket on 2025-10-07, a company in which Donald Trump Jr. and 1789 Capital have stakes (partly accurate)
- Charles and Marissa Cascarilla (Paxos CEO), listed in Oct 2025: the OCC approved Paxos's national trust charter conversion on 2025-12-12, 50 days after the list (partly accurate)
- Tether America (Oct 2025 list): it hired former White House crypto council director Bo Hines in Sept 2025 and launched USAT on 2026-01-27 through federally chartered Anchorage, with Lutnick's former firm Cantor as reserve custodian (partly accurate)
- T-Mobile ($5M): the FCC approved its $4.4B UScellular deal and its Metronet JV in July 2025, after T-Mobile told Chair Carr it was ending DEI programmes (partly accurate)
- Micron (Oct 2025 list): Commerce renegotiated its $6.1B CHIPS award and added $275M on 2025-06-12, alongside a $200B investment pledge (partly accurate)
- Altria and Reynolds American (Oct 2025 list): the White House withdrew the menthol ban on 2025-01-21, and the FDA authorized Altria's on! PLUS pouches in late Dec 2025 (partly accurate)
- Advocacy-group aggregates: Public Citizen counts most corporate donors as federal contractors or as facing enforcement (partly accurate)
- The claim's 'Ergen $25M' is right but was given in two $12.5M tranches (Dec 2025, anonymous; Feb 2026), per the Post.
- Google's $22M is the YouTube settlement routed to the ballroom (C33), not a voluntary gift.
- AT&T's $5M is marked 'pledged' in the spreadsheet, and AT&T denies giving or planning to give.
- The leaked figures were current as of late September 2026 and exclude in-kind gifts (e.g. Carrier's offered HVAC system). Reuters did not verify the document.
- The 2026-10-15 date on leaked amounts is the publication date, not the gift date. Only Nvidia (Oct 2025), Ergen (Dec 2025 and Feb 2026), Herrera (Sep 2025) and Rocket (about mid-Sep 2025) have gift timing in the coverage we found.
- Crypto donors' amounts (Coinbase, Ripple, Tether, Winklevoss) were not found in the leak coverage we could reach. A '$5M each' figure in crypto media appears to describe political spending and is unverified as ballroom giving.

## What Is Unproven

- Brandon Lutnick ($2.5M) and the Lutnick Family listing.

## In More Detail

- Many donors had a significant federal matter within six months of the list date.
- The tightest documented pairings are Rocket (FHFA approval three weeks before a $10M pledge), Herrera ($2.5M four months before her father's pardon), Ergen (FCC probe closed before, spectrum approval after), the Winklevoss twins (CFTC nominee withdrawn three weeks before the list) and Paxos (OCC charter 50 days after).
- Every link from gift to decision is inferred.
- Gift dates are mostly unknown, and the counter-cases (Booz Allen, Comcast, Union Pacific, Meta, Google, Amazon) and batch or generic decisions cut against a systematic pay-for-favour reading.

**Verdict: partly accurate (confidence: medium).**
- **The donor roster is documented.** The White House released it on 23 Oct 2025.
- **The amounts rest on one leaked spreadsheet.** The Washington Post reported it on 15 Oct 2026, and Reuters did not verify it.
- **Most of the federal matters are documented.** Many donors had one within six months of the list date.
- **Every link from a gift to a decision is our own inference.** No source asserts a quid pro quo, and the Post says its reporting does not show that any donation led to a government action.
