# C58: Market-Moving Presidential Posts & Insider-Trading Allegations

**Verdict:** Mostly accurate · medium confidence  
**Type:** Check · Trump Media & Markets

## The Claim

> Is there evidence that people traded ahead of Trump's market-moving statements, such as the 9 April 2025 post hours before the tariff pause, and has any regulator investigated?

## Bottom Line

Most of the pattern in the claim is documented. On 9 April 2025 Trump posted 'THIS IS A GREAT TIME TO BUY!!!' less than four hours before the 90-day tariff pause, Reuters documented a burst of bullish options buying minutes before the pause post, Senate and House Democrats asked the SEC to investigate, and Trump bragged on camera that friends had made billions.

## What Holds Up

- Trump posted 'THIS IS A GREAT TIME TO BUY' on 9 April 2025, hours before announcing the tariff pause.
- Unusual options activity came just before the pause announcement.
- Democrats asked the SEC to investigate.
- Trump boasted about his friends' gains after the pause.
- Prediction-market bets were placed ahead of military announcements.
- The White House responded to the betting concerns.
- The Intercept and the Freedom of the Press Foundation sued over Truth Social-exclusive announcements and the paid Truth API.
- Joe Rogan made an 'insider trading' remark about the Truth API (mostly accurate)

## What Needs Correcting

- Well-timed trades came before other Trump announcements on Iran, Intel and the Fed (partly accurate)
- Prediction-market bets were placed ahead of pardon announcements (misleading)
- The best-documented pardon-market case was a trader betting on Biden's January 2025 pardons (NPR/Bubblemaps, April 2026), not on Trump pardons.
- Gillis, not Rogan, used the phrase 'literally insider trading' about the Truth API. Rogan questioned its legality.
- The '$2.5 billion' (Schwab) and '$900 million' (Penske) figures are Trump's on-camera claims about paper gains, not documented trading profits.
- Estimates of the oil-futures bets before Trump's Iran posts conflict: about $2.6B (ABC) versus about $7B (Reuters).
- Secondary-site claims that a $2.5M options position became $70M+ on 9 April 2025 are not confirmed by Reuters, which put one SPY call block at about $624K rising to about $10M.
- Size estimates for the 23 March 2026 oil bet vary further: about $500-760M in the trades covered by the retracted CFTC referral (Bloomberg via secondary outlets), separate from the roughly $950M bet before the 7 April post.

## In More Detail

- Later episodes are documented too: a CFTC/DOJ probe of oil-futures trades placed before Trump's Iran posts (March-April 2026), suspicious Polymarket bets before the Maduro raid and the Iran strikes, a White House email warning staff not to trade on nonpublic information, the Intercept/FPF suit over the paid Truth API, and a Rogan podcast exchange calling the API 'insider trading'.
- No public evidence or official finding ties Trump, his family or named officials to any of these trades.
- The one criminal case is a soldier charged over the Maduro bets.
- The Intel and Fed 'well-timed trades' threads are thin, and the best-known pardon bets were on Biden's pardons, not Trump's.
- Audit update: Bloomberg reported in October 2026 that an adviser to CFTC Chair Selig had staff retract an enforcement referral over the 23 March 2026 oil bets (the CFTC says enforcement work continues), and House Democrats said in February 2026 the SEC never substantively answered the April 2025 requests.

## Sub-Claims

### 1. Trump posted 'THIS IS A GREAT TIME TO BUY' on 9 April 2025, hours before announcing the tariff pause.

**Confirmed**

- On the morning of 9 April 2025 Trump posted 'THIS IS A GREAT TIME TO BUY!!! DJT' on Truth Social.
- Less than four hours later he posted a 90-day pause on the higher 'reciprocal' tariffs for most countries, excluding China.
- The S&P 500 rose about 9.5% that day.
- The White House said the post was meant to reassure markets, not to manipulate them.
- Amounts: about 9.5%
- Dates: 9 April 2025
- Sources: [1](https://www.npr.org/2025/04/10/nx-s1-5360062/why-some-are-accusing-trump-of-manipulating-stock-markets) [2](https://www.adn.com/nation-world/2025/04/09/trump-told-people-to-buy-hours-later-his-tariff-pause-sent-markets-soaring/)

### 2. Unusual options activity came just before the pause announcement.

**Confirmed**

- Reuters (10 April 2025) reported that unidentified traders placed bullish bets worth millions of dollars on SPY and QQQ calls in the minutes before the pause post.
- SPY $509 calls expiring that day went from about $624,000 to about $10 million in value.
- Reuters could not tell whether one trader or many placed the bets, or whether anyone cashed out.
- Claims that a $2.5M position grew past $70M come from secondary sites and are unverified.
- Nobody has been identified, and no regulator has reported a finding.
- Amounts: $509; about $624,000; about $10 million; $2.5M; $70M
- Dates: 10 April 2025
- Sources: [3](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/well-timed-options-trades-ahead-211154780.html) [4](https://www.benzinga.com/government/regulations/25/04/44728276/spy-qqq-call-volumes-spiked-minutes-before-tariff-pause-announcement-alexandria-ocasio-cortez-demands-disclosure-from-congress-members)

### 3. Democrats asked the SEC to investigate.

**Confirmed**

- On 11 April 2025 Sens. Warren and Schumer, joined by Schiff, Wyden, Gallego, Kelly and others, wrote to SEC Chair Paul Atkins.
- They asked whether Trump, Cabinet members, donors or other insiders had engaged in insider trading or market manipulation, and whether the White House had contacted the SEC.
- Maxine Waters sent a separate House letter on 10 April 2025.
- Rep. Ocasio-Cortez urged members of Congress to disclose their trades.
- An SEC spokesperson declined to comment.
- In a 2026-02-24 letter, House Financial Services Democrats said that nearly a year after their April 2025 request to SEC Chair Atkins, the SEC Inspector General and GAO they had 'received no substantive response', and that GAO said it lacks authority to compel an investigation.
- Senators Warner and Schiff sent a further letter in April 2026 on trading ahead of policy announcements.
- No public SEC response, investigation or enforcement action was found through Nov 2026.
- Dates: 11 April 2025; 10 April 2025; 2026-02-24; April 2025; April 2026
- Sources: [5](https://www.banking.senate.gov/imo/media/doc/20250411 Letter to SEC re DJT Tariff Insider Trading.pdf) [6](https://www.wyden.senate.gov/news/press-releases/wyden-warren-schumer-senate-colleagues-call-on-sec-to-launch-investigation-into-possible-trump-tariff-market-manipulation-insider-trading) [7](https://democrats-financialservices.house.gov/UploadedFiles/04.10.2025-_Ltr_SEC.GAOIT.pdf) [36](https://democrats-financialservices.house.gov/uploadedfiles/02.24.2026_ltr_req_iti.pdf)

### 4. Trump boasted about his friends' gains after the pause.

**Confirmed**

- In an Oval Office video from 9 April 2025, Trump pointed to Charles Schwab and Roger Penske and said 'He made two-and-a-half billion today, and he made $900 million. That's not bad.'
- The figures are Trump's own claims; Forbes estimated Penske's gain at about $900M. Nothing suggests either man traded on advance knowledge, and the remark referred to paper gains in existing holdings.
- Amounts: $900 million; about $900M
- Dates: 9 April 2025
- Sources: [8](https://newrepublic.com/post/193860/donald-trump-brags-tariff-pause-made-billionaires-richer) [9](https://www.commondreams.org/news/trump-brags-billionaire-friends)

### 5. Well-timed trades came before other Trump announcements on Iran, Intel and the Fed.

**Partly accurate**

- Iran: documented. Reuters reported in mid-April 2026 that the CFTC, working with DOJ, was probing oil-futures trades on CME and ICE made shortly before Trump's Iran posts of 23 March 2026 (a pause in strikes and talks with Iran) and 7 April 2026.
- The CFTC sought 'Tag 50' trader identities.
- Estimates of the bets range from about $2.6B (ABC) to about $7B (Reuters).
- CFTC Chair Selig said the agency would pursue insider trading but did not comment on specifics.
- Update: Bloomberg reported on 2026-10-13, citing people familiar, that Mel Gunewardena, a senior adviser to Selig, told a market-oversight supervisor to retract a staff referral to enforcement over oil bets placed minutes before the 23 March post (reported at roughly $500-760M), an intervention described as unusual; the supervisor had left the agency by July.
- A source quoted afterwards said the enforcement division is still examining the trades and that all referred entities are under investigation; the CFTC does not confirm or deny investigations.
- Sen. Whitehouse had asked Selig in April to examine the 23 March trades and a roughly $950M short bet before the 7 April ceasefire post.
- No charges had been reported as of mid-Nov 2026. Intel: thin.
- A trading-platform blog noted unusual Intel options activity before the 22 August 2025 government-stake news, and Rep. Tim Moore bought Intel shares on 29 July 2025.
- Neither shows insider trading, and no probe was found.
- Fed: Warsh's odds on Polymarket and Kalshi jumped before his nomination, but that tracked press reports, and no investigation was found.
- Amounts: about $2.6B; about $7B; roughly $500-760M; roughly $950M
- Dates: mid-April 2026; 23 March 2026; 7 April 2026; 2026-10-13; 23 March
- Sources: [10](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/commodities/articles/us-probes-suspicious-oil-trades-185104849.html) [11](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/insider-trading-oil-futures-trump-iran-post) [12](https://abcnews4.com/news/nation-world/feds-probe-oil-futures-trades-ahead-of-trump-iran-strait-of-hormuz-announcements-prices-trading-activity-report) [32](https://blog.deriv.com/blog/intel-stock-price-surge-2025-trump-semiconductor-policy) [31](https://www.theblock.co/news/regulation/2026-01-30-kevin-warsh-polymarket-odds-fed-chair-387788) [33](https://www.ibtimes.co.uk/cftc-insider-trading-referral-iran-oil-bet-1825415) [34](https://financefeeds.com/cftc-staff-told-to-withdraw-insider-trading-referral-on-oil-bets-before-trumps-iran-post/)

### 6. Prediction-market bets were placed ahead of military announcements.

**Confirmed**

- Maduro: a new Polymarket account turned about $34K of bets into about $410K just hours before the 3 January 2026 capture of Maduro.
- On 23 April 2026, SDNY unsealed an indictment of Army Master Sgt. Gannon Ken Van Dyke on Commodity Exchange Act, wire-fraud and money-laundering-type counts.
- Prosecutors say he helped plan the raid.
- The CFTC filed a parallel civil case, its first insider-trading case on event contracts.
- Polymarket says it flagged the trades.
- Iran: Bubblemaps found six fresh wallets that made about $1M betting on a US strike by 28 February 2026, and the NYT found 150+ accounts betting the day before.
- No one has been identified or charged for the Iran bets.
- None of these cases is tied to Trump or his family.
- Amounts: about $34K; about $410K; about $1M
- Dates: 3 January 2026; 23 April 2026; 28 February 2026
- Sources: [13](https://www.npr.org/transcripts/nx-s1-5667232) [14](https://www.debevoise.com/insights/publications/2026/04/polymarket-insider-trading-charges-illustrate-doj) [15](https://www.dlapiper.com/insights/publications/2026/05/doj-and-cftc-criminal-and-civil-insider-trading-charges) [16](https://www.theblock.co/post/391650/fresh-accounts-netted-1-million-on-polymarket-hours-before-us-airstrikes-on-iran-bubblemaps) [17](https://www.aljazeera.com/economy/2026/3/4/traders-mint-money-on-betting-platforms-on-us-israel-strike-on-iran)

- I found no documented suspicious wagers ahead of Trump pardons such as CZ's.
- No prosecutors have announced an investigation into the pardon bets.
- Amounts: about $316K
- Dates: 16 April 2026

### 8. The White House responded to the betting concerns.

**Confirmed**

- On 24 March 2026, the day after Trump's Iran strike-pause post, the White House Management Office emailed staff.
- It said using nonpublic information to trade prediction-market or futures contracts breaks ethics rules and can carry criminal penalties.
- Spokesman David Ingle confirmed the email and called any suggestion that officials were trading 'baseless and irresponsible'.
- In April 2025 the White House also denied market manipulation, and Sen. Cornyn called the insider-trading idea 'ridiculous'.
- Dates: 24 March 2026; April 2025
- Sources: [20](https://qz.com/white-house-staff-warning-prediction-markets-insider-trading-041026) [21](https://www.deseret.com/politics/2026/04/10/white-house-warn-staff-against-prediction-market-bets-insider-trading-iran-war) [22](https://www.thedailybeast.com/white-house-warns-staff-after-mysteriously-well-timed-bets-on-donald-trumps-decisions)

### 9. The Intercept and the Freedom of the Press Foundation sued over Truth Social-exclusive announcements and the paid Truth API.

**Confirmed**

- The suit, The Intercept Media, Inc. v. Trump (1:26-cv-06867), was filed in SDNY on 12 August 2026 and assigned to Judge J. Paul Oetken.
- It names Trump, Natalie Harp and Dan Scavino in their official capacities, plus the EOP and the White House Office.
- It alleges that putting official announcements on a platform that sells faster paid access ($60K-$100K a month) violates the First and Fifth Amendments.
- The plaintiffs, represented by a Yale Law clinic, sought a preliminary injunction to stop official White House posting there while Truth API operates.
- DOJ argues courts cannot direct how the president performs official duties and that TMTG, a private company, runs Truth API.
- Oetken set an answer deadline of 30 October 2026 plus a two-week extension.
- A press report indicates he reserved decision after a PI hearing in early October.
- I found no ruling as of mid-November 2026.
- Amounts: $60K-$100K
- Dates: 12 August 2026; 30 October 2026; mid-November 2026
- Sources: [23](https://theintercept.com/2026/08/12/trump-media-truthsocial-premium-first-amendment/) [24](https://freedom.press/issues/lawsuit-early-access-fee-for-trump-truth-social-posts-unconstitutional/) [25](https://www.courthousenews.com/profoundly-corrupt-trump-sued-over-scheme-to-charge-100k-for-early-truth-social-access/) [27](https://www.newsweek.com/donald-trump-natalie-harp-ordered-to-answer-truth-lawsuit-before-midterms-12567210) [28](https://www.newsweek.com/judge-weighs-injunction-over-trump-truth-api-service-12515987) [26](https://law.yale.edu/yls-today/news/clinic-files-preliminary-injunction-against-paid-truth-api-service)

### 10. Joe Rogan made an 'insider trading' remark about the Truth API.

**Mostly accurate**

- On a Joe Rogan Experience episode from mid-August 2026, guest Shane Gillis called the paid Truth API 'literally insider trading'.
- Rogan asked how it could be legal and called the $100K price 'literally crazy'.
- Most coverage pins the 'insider trading' phrase on Gillis, with Rogan agreeing, so crediting Rogan alone is loose.
- TMTG says critics 'invented a new theory of insider trading based on publicly available information'.
- No regulator or court has ruled that the API is illegal.
- Amounts: $100K
- Dates: mid-August 2026
- Sources: [29](https://www.mediaite.com/media/podcasts/oh-my-god-joe-rogan-stunned-over-truth-social-selling-access-to-trumps-posts/) <https://pro.edgex.exchange/en-US/news/article/joe-rogan-shane-gillis-trump-truth-social-plan> [30](https://fortune.com/2026/08/12/trump-media-truth-api-insider-trading/)

## Denials & Context

- The White House denied market manipulation in April 2025, saying the 'great time to buy' post was meant to calm markets.
  - Sen. Cornyn called the insider-trading allegations ridiculous.
- David Ingle said implications that administration officials bet on prediction markets were 'baseless and irresponsible reporting' and confirmed the staff-warning email.
- TMTG says the Truth API only delivers public data faster.
  - DOJ argues the Intercept/FPF suit improperly seeks to dictate presidential communications.
- No public evidence identifies who placed the 9 April 2025 options trades, the Iran oil-futures trades or the Iran-strike Polymarket bets.
  - The only person charged (Van Dyke) is a soldier, not a political appointee or Trump associate.
- Experts note that market moves around presidential statements can reflect fast reaction, leaks to the press or informed speculation, not only insider trading.
  - Prediction-market odds on the Fed chair tracked press reporting.
- Trump Jr. advises both Kalshi and Polymarket (see C25), and 1789 Capital invested in Polymarket.
  - That ties the family to the platforms on which the suspicious bets were placed, but not to the bets themselves.
- Coverage after mid-2026 of whether the CFTC oil probe produced charges, and of any ruling in the Intercept suit, could not be checked: the research ended first and the original pages at CourtListener, Inner City Press and Mediaite were not read directly.
- The CFTC does not confirm or deny investigations; after the Bloomberg referral report a source said all entities referred to enforcement remain under investigation.
  - The referral account rests on anonymous sourcing.

## Full Report

**Verdict:** mostly-accurate (confidence: medium)

### Summary

Most of the pattern described in the claim is on the record:

- **9 April 2025.** Trump posted "THIS IS A GREAT TIME TO BUY!!!" less than four hours before the 90-day tariff pause. Reuters documented a burst of bullish options buying minutes before the pause post. Senate and House Democrats asked the SEC to investigate. Trump said on camera that Charles Schwab and Roger Penske had made billions that day.
- **Iran oil trades.** In March-April 2026 the CFTC, with DOJ, probed oil-futures trades placed shortly before Trump's Iran posts.
- **Prediction markets.** Suspicious Polymarket bets came before the Maduro raid and before the Iran strikes. A soldier was charged over the Maduro bets.
- **White House email.** Staff were warned not to trade on nonpublic information.
- **Lawsuit.** The Intercept and the Freedom of the Press Foundation (FPF) sued over the paid Truth API. The case is pending.
- **Rogan podcast.** On the Joe Rogan Experience, guest Shane Gillis called the Truth API "literally insider trading."

What is **not** established: no public evidence, investigation finding or charge ties Trump, his family or any named official to any of these trades. The Intel and Fed "well-timed trades" threads are thin. The best-documented "pardon bet" was on Biden's pardons, not Trump's.

This builds on C20 (stock posts), C21 and C41 (Truth API, the Warren-Schiff letter to the SEC, the Intercept lead), C25 (Don Jr.'s Kalshi and Polymarket roles) and C30 (Rogan).

### Sub-claims

#### 1. "Great time to buy" post before the tariff pause: confirmed
- On the morning of 9 April 2025, Trump posted "THIS IS A GREAT TIME TO BUY!!! DJT" on Truth Social.
- Less than four hours later he announced a 90-day pause on most "reciprocal" tariffs, excluding China. The S&P 500 rose about 9.5% that day [1][2].
- The White House said he was reassuring markets.

#### 2. Options activity: confirmed (identity of traders unknown)
- Reuters reported that unidentified traders placed bullish SPY and QQQ call bets worth millions of dollars in the minutes before the pause post [3].
- One block of SPY $509 same-day calls went from about $624K to about $10M in value.
- Reuters could not tell whether one trader or many placed the bets, or whether any gains were realised.
- Claims of a "$2.5M to $70M+" trade come from secondary sites only and are unverified.
- AOC demanded that members of Congress disclose their trades [4].

#### 3. Democrats asked the SEC to investigate: confirmed; no SEC action found
- On 11 April 2025, Warren and Schumer, joined by Schiff, Wyden, Gallego, Kelly and others, wrote to SEC Chair Atkins [5][6].
- They asked whether Trump, Cabinet members, donors or insiders had traded on the pause, and whether the White House had contacted the SEC.
- Maxine Waters sent a House letter on 10 April 2025 [7].
- An SEC spokesperson declined to comment. I found no SEC response, probe or enforcement action. Audit: House FSC Democrats wrote on 2026-02-24 that they had "received no substantive response" in nearly a year, and that GAO says it cannot compel an investigation.

#### 4. Trump boasting about friends' gains: confirmed
- In an Oval Office clip from 9 April 2025, Trump said of Schwab and Penske: "He made two-and-a-half billion today, and he made $900 million. That's not bad." [8][9]
- These are paper gains that Trump claimed. There is no suggestion either man traded on advance knowledge.

#### 5. Well-timed trades before other announcements: partly-accurate
- **Iran (documented).** Reuters reported in mid-April 2026 that the CFTC, working with DOJ, was probing oil-futures trades on CME and ICE [10][11][12].
  - The trades came before Trump's 23 March 2026 post (pausing strikes and announcing talks with Iran) and his 7 April 2026 post.
  - The CFTC sought "Tag 50" trader identities from the exchanges.
  - Estimates of the size conflict: about $2.6B (ABC) versus about $7B (Reuters).
  - CFTC Chair Selig vowed to pursue insider trading but did not comment on the specific case.
  - Audit update: Bloomberg reported on 2026-10-13 (anonymous sources) that Selig adviser Mel Gunewardena had a supervisor retract a staff referral to enforcement over the 23 March bets; a source later said enforcement is still examining the trades. No charges as of mid-Nov 2026.
- **Intel (thin).** A trading-platform blog pointed to unusual Intel options activity before the 22 August 2025 government-stake news [32]. Rep. Tim Moore bought Intel shares on 29 July 2025. Neither shows insider trading, and I found no probe.
- **Fed (thin).** Kevin Warsh's odds on Polymarket and Kalshi rose before his nomination [31]. The moves tracked press reports, and I found no investigation.

#### 6. Prediction-market bets before military news: confirmed
- **Maduro raid.**
  - A new Polymarket account turned about $34K into about $410K hours before Maduro's capture on 3 January 2026 [13].
  - SDNY unsealed an indictment of Army Master Sgt. Gannon Ken Van Dyke on 23 April 2026. The charges are three Commodity Exchange Act counts, wire fraud and an unlawful monetary transaction. Prosecutors say he helped plan the raid [14][15].
  - The CFTC brought a parallel civil case, its first insider-trading case on event contracts.
  - Polymarket says it flagged the trades to DOJ.
- **Iran strikes.**
  - Bubblemaps found six freshly funded wallets that made about $1M on a "US strikes Iran by 28 Feb 2026" contract [16].
  - The NYT found 150+ accounts betting the day before the strikes [17][18].
  - No one has been identified or charged.
- None of these bets is linked to Trump or his family. Don Jr. advises both Kalshi and Polymarket (C25).

#### 7. Bets before pardon announcements: misleading
- The documented case is Biden's pardons. Bubblemaps told NPR (16 April 2026) that a Polymarket trader made about $316K on Biden's pre-emptive pardons and the Hunter Biden pardon [19].
- I found no documented suspicious wagers before Trump pardons such as CZ's.
- Prosecutors have announced no probe of the pardon bets.

#### 8. Official responses: confirmed
- On 24 March 2026, the day after Trump's Iran pause post, the White House Management Office emailed staff [20][21][22].
  - It said that trading prediction or futures contracts on nonpublic information breaks ethics rules and can bring criminal penalties.
  - Spokesman David Ingle confirmed the email. He called any suggestion that officials were trading this way "baseless and irresponsible reporting."
- In April 2025 the White House denied manipulation, and Sen. Cornyn called the allegations ridiculous.

#### 9. The Intercept and FPF suit: confirmed (pending)
- **The case.** *The Intercept Media, Inc. v. Trump*, 1:26-cv-06867, was filed in SDNY on 12 August 2026 and assigned to Judge J. Paul Oetken [23][24][25].
- **Defendants.** Trump, Natalie Harp and Dan Scavino (in their official capacities), plus the EOP and the White House Office.
- **The claim.** Routing official announcements through a platform that sells faster access ($60K-$100K a month) violates the First and Fifth Amendments.
- **The relief sought.** Represented by a Yale Law clinic, the plaintiffs asked for a preliminary injunction against official posting there while Truth API runs [26].
- **DOJ's position.** Courts cannot direct how the president carries out official duties, and TMTG, a private company, runs Truth API.
- **Status.** Oetken set a 30 October 2026 answer deadline, plus two weeks [27]. A press report indicates he reserved decision after a PI hearing in early October [28]. I found no ruling.

#### 10. Rogan's "insider trading" remark: mostly-accurate
- On a mid-August 2026 episode, guest Shane Gillis called the Truth API "literally insider trading" [29][30].
- Rogan asked how it could be legal and called the $100K price "literally crazy."
- So the phrase is mainly Gillis's.
- TMTG says critics "invented a new theory of 'insider trading' based on publicly available information."

### Corrections
- The documented pardon bet concerned Biden's pardons, not Trump's.
- Gillis, not Rogan, said "literally insider trading."
- The $2.5B and $900M figures are Trump's own claims about paper gains.
- Estimates of the oil-futures bets conflict ($2.6B vs $7B).
- The "$70M options trade" is not confirmed by Reuters.

### Denials and context
- The White House, Ingle and Cornyn deny any manipulation or insider trading by officials.
- TMTG and DOJ defend the Truth API as faster delivery of public data.
- No one tied to the administration has been publicly identified as a trader. The only person charged is a soldier.
- Fast markets, press leaks and informed speculation can explain some price moves.
- **Limits of this check.** The research ended before I could check post-mid-2026 updates on the CFTC oil probe or the SEC's handling of the April 2025 letters. The original pages at CourtListener, Inner City Press and Mediaite were not read directly, so the Intercept docket status rests on search snippets.
