# C102: Trades by Officials, Family & Associates

**Verdict:** Mostly accurate · medium confidence  
**Type:** Check · Trump Media & Markets

## The Claim

> What trades by officials, family members and associates around government actions are documented, and which allegations go beyond the record?

## Bottom Line

Disclosure filings document many well-timed trades around tariff and policy news: Attorney General Bondi's $1M-$5M+ Trump Media sale on 2 April 2025, Scavino's $1M-$5M Trump Media sale on 1 April, Duffy's February 2025 sales before the reciprocal-tariff plan, 327 purchases in one of Trump's own accounts on 8 April 2025, the day before the tariff pause (verified in his OGE annual report; disclosed on 30 June 2026, about 13 months after the 45-day deadline, with late fees paid), at least $82M of bonds bought by Trump in autumn 2025, and heavy trading by members of Congress such as Greene and Moskowitz around the 9 April pause.

## What Holds Up

- Attorney General Pam Bondi sold Trump Media shares and warrants on 2 April 2025, the day of the 'Liberation Day' tariff announcement.
- Deputy Chief of Staff Dan Scavino and personnel director Sergio Gor sold Trump Media stock days before 2 April 2025 (mostly accurate)
- Transportation Secretary Sean Duffy sold stocks shortly before a tariff announcement (mostly accurate)
- More than a dozen other executive-branch officials and aides made well-timed sales before tariff announcements (mostly accurate)
- Trump's own accounts bought stocks the day before the 9 April 2025 tariff pause, and the trades were disclosed late.
- Trump bought large amounts of corporate and municipal bonds while in office, including debt of companies affected by his policies.
- Members of Congress bought or sold stocks around the 9 April 2025 tariff pause.
- The prediction-market platforms have ties to the Trump family.

## What Needs Correcting

- Small listed companies saw unusual trading before Trump family involvement was announced (Dominari, Unusual Machines, PSQ, GrabAGun, American Bitcoin/Gryphon, ALT5 Sigma) (partly accurate)
- Traders have been identified for prediction-market bets tied to government decisions (partly accurate)
- **Claimed:** The Transportation Secretary sold stock shortly before the April 2025 tariff announcements  
  **The record shows:** Duffy's reported sales were on 11 and 13 February 2025, around the 13 February reciprocal-tariff plan, not in April.
- **Claimed:** No public evidence ties Trump to trades around the tariff pause (C58 summary, read broadly)  
  **The record shows:** That statement holds for the unidentified options traders, but one of Trump's own accounts made 327 purchases ($3.6M-$12.8M by range) on 8 April 2025, disclosed on 30 June 2026 (verified in the OGE report); the filing does not show who directed them. Applied to C58 on 2026-11-16.
- C65 gives Bondi's sale as $1M-$5M of shares plus $250K-$500K of warrants (Bloomberg Law: $1M-$5.5M combined). The two ranges add to $1.25M-$5.5M; no change of substance.
- Dates of Rep. Greene's purchases differ by outlet: most reports give 8-9 April 2025, others add 2 April or 3-4 April. The $21,021-$315,000 total is consistent.
- C13 does not mention the reported unusual trading in ALT5 Sigma before the 11 Aug 2025 announcement; The Block reported it without accusing the company.
- Unusual Machines' run-up (more than 220% in four weeks) came before Don Jr. joined on 27 Nov 2024, before the second term began.
- 2026-11-16 (audit): earlier text said Trump's 8 April 2025 purchases surfaced 'more than 14 months later' in 'July 2026'. The record shows: OGE received the annual report on 29 June 2026 and it was made public on 30 June, nearly 15 months after the trades and about 13 months after the 45-day 278-T deadline. The count (327, all purchases) and the $12.8M maximum were confirmed against Part 7; the minimum is about $3.6M.
- 2026-11-16 (audit): earlier text described an 'unnamed White House lawyer' and 'no response from Rana'. ProPublica names the lawyer (a career acting general counsel of the White House Office of Administration, who says his wife made the sale) and reports that Rana declined to comment; Rana was already ambassador to Slovakia when the report appeared.
- 2026-11-16 (audit): Gor's 27 March 2025 Trump Media sale ($15,001-$50,000) is now documented from his own 278-T; Scavino's 1 April sale remains single-source, and the reported Scavino capital gains are unverified.
- 2026-11-16 (audit): Van Dyke faces five counts, not only commodities-fraud and wire-fraud counts.
- 2026-11-17: Claimed: Bondi's ethics agreement required divestiture within 90 days of taking office, and she was confirmed in early Feb 2025. The record shows: the agreement says 'not later than 90 days after my confirmation'; she was confirmed on 4 Feb 2025, so the deadline was about 5 May 2025. Her 278-T was signed 5 May 2025 and OGE-certified 12 May 2025 (read in full in C108).

## What Is Unproven

- Any of these documented trades has been shown to rest on misuse of non-public information.

## In More Detail

- Pre-announcement run-ups are documented for Dominari and Unusual Machines and reported for ALT5 Sigma, but no buyer has been identified.
- In prediction markets the only charged US case tied to a government action is a soldier's Maduro bets.
- Don Jr. advises Kalshi and Polymarket and 1789 Capital invested in Polymarket, but no family bets are documented.
- None of the trades has led to a finding that anyone used non-public information.
- Officials say outside managers made the trades or that the sales were required by ethics agreements; the White House says Trump's assets are in fully discretionary accounts run by independent third-party institutions.

### 1. Attorney General Pam Bondi sold Trump Media shares and warrants on 2 April 2025, the day of the 'Liberation Day' tariff announcement.

**Confirmed**

- Grade: documented (her OGE periodic transaction report, signed 5 May 2025 and OGE-certified 12 May 2025, made public in mid-May 2025; the form was read in full in C108, as was ProPublica's report). (1) Filing: a sale of Trump Media & Technology Group shares valued at $1M-$5M plus warrants valued at $250K-$500K, both dated 2 April 2025 (combined $1.25M-$5.5M by our arithmetic; Bloomberg Law gives $1M-$5.5M).
- ProPublica: DJT closed at $18.76 on 2 April and opened at $17.92 the next morning.
- The form does not give the time of day; Trump announced the tariffs after the market close that day, and DJT shares fell in the following sessions (CBS reports about 13% within days). (2) Inference by outlets: the timing was unusually fortunate. (3) Allegation: Rep. Jamie Raskin asked DOJ Inspector General Michael Horowitz on 20 May 2025 to investigate whether Bondi or other DOJ officials traded on non-public information. (4) No finding: no inspector-general result or other official finding was found as of Nov 2026.
- Context: her ethics agreement required her to divest the TMTG holding 'not later than 90 days after my confirmation' (she was confirmed on 4 Feb 2025, so the deadline was about 5 May 2025), so a sale in that window was required; only its date is in question.
- Response: the DOJ did not answer early press questions; at a June 2025 House hearing Bondi said she had complied with ethics rules and had lost money on the stock (as summarised by Ground News; the hearing record was not read).
- Builds on C65, which already records this sale.
- Amounts: $1M-$5M; $250K-$500K; $1.25M-$5.5M; $1M-$5.5M; $18.76

### 3. Transportation Secretary Sean Duffy sold stocks shortly before a tariff announcement.

**Mostly accurate**

- Grade: documented (OGE records reported by ProPublica, republished by other outlets; the forms were not read). (1) Filing: Duffy sold shares in about three dozen companies (34 stocks) on 11 and 13 February 2025, together $90K-$650K in reported ranges, before and on the day Trump announced his plan for 'reciprocal' tariffs (13 February 2025).
- The claim as often repeated places these sales in April 2025; they were in February. (2) ProPublica said it was unclear whether Duffy had any advance information about the timing or scale of the tariff plan. (3) No official has alleged a violation on the record that we found. (4) No inquiry or finding.
- Response: a Transportation Department spokesperson said an outside manager made the trades in a retirement account, that Duffy 'had no input on the timing of the sales', and that his ethics agreement required him to sell seven of those holdings within his first three months.
- Audit: ProPublica's article (19 May 2025) was read in full and confirms 34 stocks worth $90,000-$650,000 sold on 11 and 13 February, seven of them required by the ethics agreement (the other 27 worth $27,000-$405,000), and the department's statement that the trades were made by an outside manager in a retirement account and that Duffy 'had no input on the timing of the sales'.
- Amounts: $90K-$650K; $90,000-$650,000; $27,000-$405,000
- Dates: 13 February 2025; April 2025; 19 May 2025; 13 February

### 5. Trump's own accounts bought stocks the day before the 9 April 2025 tariff pause, and the trades were disclosed late.

**Confirmed**

- Grade: documented (Trump's OGE Form 278e annual report for 2025, received by OGE on 29 June 2026 and made public on 30 June; the cover page and Part 7 were read in full from the PDF). (1) Filing: our count of Part 7 finds 327 transactions dated 8 April 2025, all purchases, all in the account labelled 'Investment Account #7'.
- By range: 139 at $1,001-$15,000, 176 at $15,001-$50,000, 7 at $50,001-$100,000 and 5 at $100,001-$250,000, which sums to $3.63M-$12.84M. Names include Apple, Microsoft, Nvidia, Amazon and Alphabet.
- No transactions are listed on 7 or 9 April.
- The same account made 462 transactions (224 purchases, 238 sales) on 4 April and 273 purchases ($3.0M-$10.5M) on 10 April, and the report lists about 21,000 transactions across eight accounts in 2025, so 8 April was one of many heavy-trading days; ABC News noted that the account made similar trades through the year and traded more in volatile periods, consistent with rebalancing.
- Late disclosure: the reviewing official's comments (dated 30 June 2026) read 'The filer was granted a 45-day extension to file this annual report' and 'The filer paid late filing fees related to transactions not previously reported on 278-Ts.'
- The note does not say which transactions or how much was paid; Sludge reports that no 278-T covered the April trades or virtually any 2025 stock trade, and that the statutory late fee is $200.
- A 278-T is due within 30 days of notification and no later than 45 days after a trade, so these trades surfaced about 13 months after the deadline and nearly 15 months after they were made.
- On 9 April the S&P 500 rose about 9.5%. (2) Outlets infer the timing is notable; the filing does not show who placed or directed the trades, the time of day, or any gain. (3) Critics and Democratic members (including Sen. Schiff and Rep. Levin) have cited the trades and asked for full disclosure; no authority has alleged a violation that we found. (4) No finding.
- Responses: the White House has said all of the President's assets are held in 'fully discretionary accounts managed by independent third-party financial institutions' and that there are no conflicts of interest (wording attributed to spokesperson Anna Kelly; repeated in July 2026 coverage, though the original occasion was not confirmed); ABC News reported that the White House said the accounts are run by professional advisers who do not regularly communicate with Trump, and Trump told reporters 'I'm profiting?
- Because the stock market's going up, everybody's profiting'; a spokesperson also pointed to trading by former Speaker Nancy Pelosi.
- The Trump Organization said in May 2026 (of the 2026 trades) that neither Trump, his family nor the company 'plays any role in selecting, directing, or approving specific investments' and that they 'receive no advance notice of trading activity'.
- The accounts are not a blind trust.
- This is a material addition to C58, which addressed only unidentified options traders around the pause.
- Amounts: $1,001-$15,000; $15,001-$50,000; $50,001-$100,000; $100,001-$250,000; $3.63M-$12.84M
- Dates: 29 June 2026; 30 June; 8 April 2025; 9 April; 4 April

### 8. Small listed companies saw unusual trading before Trump family involvement was announced (Dominari, Unusual Machines, PSQ, GrabAGun, American Bitcoin/Gryphon, ALT5 Sigma).

**Partly accurate**

- Grade: documented for price and volume moves in two cases; no trader identified in any case.
- Dominari: a roughly 530-580% six-week run-up before the 11 Feb 2025 announcement, with no buyer identified (C39, C91; not repeated).
- Unusual Machines: shares rose more than 220% in the four weeks before Don Jr. joined its advisory board on 27 Nov 2024 (before the term began), with volume well above earlier 2024 (FT); the company said knowledge of his planned involvement was limited to three senior officers, the board and counsel.
- PSQ Holdings: shares nearly tripled on the day (3 Dec 2024) reports said Don Jr. would join its board; no pre-announcement run-up was reported.
- GrabAGun: fell about 24% on its July 2025 debut; no pre-announcement anomaly reported (C39).
- Gryphon/American Bitcoin: spike came on the 12 May 2025 announcement, no reported run-up (C91).
- ALT5 Sigma: The Block reported 'some evidence of unusual trading activity' before the 11 Aug 2025 WLFI-treasury announcement while stating ALT5 is not accused of wrongdoing; price figures for the days before conflict between outlets, and the stock fell on the day (C13 did not record this).
- Regulators: the WSJ reported in Sept 2025 that the SEC and FINRA had contacted some of the 200+ crypto-treasury companies about volume and price spikes before announcements; outlets did not establish that ALT5 was among them, and one secondary report naming Trump Media among the companies under scrutiny is unconfirmed.
- No enforcement action, named trader or official finding was found for any of these companies through Nov 2026.

## Denials & Context

- A disclosed trade is not evidence of misuse of non-public information.
  - Periodic transaction reports give only value ranges and dates, not time of day, profit, or who made the decision.
- Bondi's ethics agreement required her to sell the Trump Media stake; DOJ did not answer early questions on the timing, and she later told a House hearing she complied with ethics rules and lost money (secondary summary).
- The White House says Trump's assets are in fully discretionary accounts run by independent third-party institutions, that the advisers do not regularly communicate with him, and that there are no conflicts; it also pointed to trading by Nancy Pelosi.
  - The Trump Organization says neither Trump nor his family selects, directs or approves investments or receives advance notice of trades.
  - The accounts are not a blind trust, and the filing does not show who directed any trade.
- The Transportation Department says an outside manager made Duffy's trades without his input and that some sales were required by his ethics agreement.
- Greene says her portfolio manager 'bought the dip'; Moskowitz and Khanna say outside advisers or an independent trustee manage their trades; Bresnahan says he does not trade his own stocks.
- Unusual Machines said only three senior officers, its board and counsel knew of Don Jr.'s planned role.

## Full Report

**Verdict:** mostly-accurate (confidence: medium)

**Question:** What trades by officials, family members and associates around government actions are documented, and which allegations go beyond the record?

### Summary

Disclosure filings and on-record reporting document many well-timed trades around tariff and policy news:

- **Attorney General Pam Bondi** sold $1M-$5M of Trump Media shares plus $250K-$500K of warrants on 2 April 2025, the day of the "Liberation Day" tariffs. Her ethics agreement required the sale; the date is what drew attention.
- **Dan Scavino** (deputy chief of staff) sold $1M-$5M of Trump Media stock on 1 April 2025 (USA Today; single-source). **Sergio Gor** sold $15,001-$50,000 of Trump Media on 27 March 2025 (his own 278-T, read in full).
- **Transportation Secretary Sean Duffy** sold 34 stocks ($90K-$650K) on 11 and 13 February 2025, around the reciprocal-tariff plan, **not in April**.
- **ProPublica** found more than a dozen officials and aides with well-timed sales, including State Department official Gautam Rana ($830K-$1.7M on 19 March 2025).
- **Trump's own accounts:** his OGE annual report (read in full) lists 327 purchases in one account on 8 April 2025, the day before the tariff pause, worth $3.6M-$12.8M by range. The report was made public on 30 June 2026, about 13 months after the 45-day deadline, and the reviewer notes that late fees were paid. The same account also traded heavily on other days (462 trades on 4 April, 273 purchases on 10 April). His accounts also bought at least $82M of corporate and municipal bonds between late August and early October 2025.
- **Members of Congress** traded heavily around the 9 April pause: Greene ($21K-$315K of purchases), Moskowitz ($20K-$300K), Bresnahan (171 trades on 8 April), Khanna (438 transactions), Hern (up to $5M in one trade). The Campaign Legal Center counted 2,200+ trades by 53 members.
- **Small caps:** pre-announcement run-ups are documented for Dominari and Unusual Machines and reported for ALT5 Sigma. No buyer has been identified. SEC and FINRA reportedly contacted some crypto-treasury companies, with no action known.
- **Prediction markets:** the only US charge tied to a government action is a soldier's Maduro bets. Don Jr. advises Kalshi and Polymarket, and 1789 Capital invested in Polymarket, but no family bet is documented.

What is **not** established: none of these trades has produced a charge, inspector-general finding, SEC/CFTC action or ethics-committee finding against the official, member or family member concerned. Several sales were mandated divestitures, and most filers say outside managers made the trades.

This extends C58 (market-moving posts, unidentified options and oil traders), C20 and C68 (Trump's 2026 stock trades), C65 (Bondi), C25 and C38 (prediction markets and Don Jr.), and C39 and C91 (Dominari). The investigations themselves are covered in C103.

**Evidence rule applied:** a documented, disclosed trade is not by itself evidence that anyone used non-public information. Each point separates (1) the filing, (2) what outlets infer, (3) who alleges wrongdoing, and (4) whether any trader has been identified or any authority has made a finding.

### Sub-claims

#### 1. Bondi's Trump Media sale on 2 April 2025: confirmed (trade); misuse unverified
- (1) Filing: her OGE periodic transaction report, public in mid-May 2025, shows a sale of $1M-$5M of TMTG shares and $250K-$500K of warrants on 2 April 2025 [1][2][54] (together $1.25M-$5.5M). Trump announced the tariffs after the close. The form does not show the time of day. DJT closed at $18.76 that day and opened at $17.92 the next morning (ProPublica, read in full), and fell about 13% within days (CBS).
- (2) Outlets called the timing fortunate. (3) Rep. Raskin asked DOJ Inspector General Horowitz to investigate on 20 May 2025 [3][4]. (4) No finding was found as of Nov 2026.
- Context: her ethics agreement required divesting the stake "not later than 90 days after my confirmation". She was confirmed on 4 February 2025, so the deadline was about 5 May 2025. The 278-T itself (read in full in C108) was signed 5 May 2025 and OGE-certified 12 May 2025.
- Response: DOJ did not answer early questions. At a June 2025 House hearing Bondi said she complied with ethics rules and lost money on the stock (secondary summary [5]).

#### 2. Scavino and Gor sold Trump Media stock before 2 April: mostly-accurate (Gor documented; Scavino single-source)
- **Gor (documented):** his own 278-T, signed 20 May 2025 and read in full, lists a sale of Trump Media & Technology Group worth $15,001-$50,000 on 27 March 2025 [55].

#### 4. Other officials' well-timed sales: mostly-accurate

#### 5. Trump's accounts bought the day before the pause: confirmed (trades); who directed them is not shown
- (1) **The filing (read in full).** Trump's OGE Form 278e for 2025 was received by OGE on 29 June 2026 and made public on 30 June [57]. Our line-by-line count of Part 7 finds:
  - 327 transactions dated 8 April 2025, all purchases, all in the account labelled "Investment Account #7".
  - By range: 139 at $1,001-$15,000, 176 at $15,001-$50,000, 7 at $50,001-$100,000 and 5 at $100,001-$250,000. That sums to **$3.63M-$12.84M**. Names include Apple, Microsoft, Nvidia, Amazon and Alphabet.
  - No transactions on 7 or 9 April. The same account made 462 trades (224 purchases, 238 sales) on 4 April and 273 purchases ($3.0M-$10.5M) on 10 April. The report lists about 21,000 transactions across eight accounts in 2025, so 8 April was one of many heavy-trading days. ABC News noted the account traded similarly through the year and more in volatile periods, consistent with rebalancing [58].
- **Late disclosure.** The reviewing official's comments (30 June 2026) read: "The filer was granted a 45-day extension to file this annual report" and "The filer paid late filing fees related to transactions not previously reported on 278-Ts." The note does not say which trades or how much was paid. Sludge reports that no 278-T covered the April trades or virtually any 2025 stock trade, and that the statutory late fee is $200 [13]. A 278-T is due no later than 45 days after a trade, so these surfaced about 13 months after the deadline and nearly 15 months after the trades.
- (2) The filing does not show who placed or directed the trades, the time of day, or any gain.
- (3) Critics and Democrats (including Sen. Schiff and Rep. Levin) cited the trades and asked for full disclosure [15]. (4) No authority has alleged a violation or made a finding.
- **Responses.**

The wording is attributed to spokesperson Anna Kelly and was repeated in July 2026 coverage [59]; the original occasion was not confirmed.
  - ABC News: the White House said the accounts are run by professional advisers who "do not regularly communicate with Trump". Trump told reporters: "You know why I'm profiting? Because the stock market's going up, everybody's profiting." [58]
  - A spokesperson also pointed to trading by Nancy Pelosi [15].
  - The Trump Organization (May 2026, on the 2026 trades): neither Trump, his family nor the company "plays any role in selecting, directing, or approving specific investments", and they "receive no advance notice of trading activity" [60].
  - The accounts are not a blind trust.
