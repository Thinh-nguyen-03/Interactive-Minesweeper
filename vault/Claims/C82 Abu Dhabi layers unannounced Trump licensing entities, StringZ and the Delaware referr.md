# C82: Abu Dhabi layers: unannounced Trump licensing entities, StringZ and the Delaware referral

**Verdict:** Mostly accurate · medium confidence  
**Origin:** Connection hunt

## Bottom line

The entity and date links are documented. DT Marks Abu Dhabi LLC and DT Marks Abu Dhabi Member Corp were formed in Delaware on 30 Jan 2025, 14 days after Eric Trump signed the Aryam 49% deal.

## What holds up

- DT Marks Abu Dhabi LLC and DT Marks Abu Dhabi Member Corp were formed in Delaware on 30 Jan 2025, two weeks after the Aryam deal and ten days after the inauguration.
- Two Damac subsidiaries paid $5M each in licence fees to the new Abu Dhabi entities in 2025, for projects that have not been announced.
- Eric Trump announced Abu Dhabi plans in Abu Dhabi in Dec 2024, five weeks before signing the Aryam deal, with Dar Global named as the intended partner.
- Rep. Khanna referred the Emirati WLF investor's Delaware domicile to the US Attorney for Delaware.
- The Trump side's denials.

## What needs correcting

- The brief pairs 'DT Marks Abu Dhabi' and 'Al Raha Beach' as if formed together. Per Forbes and CREW they were formed about six months apart: DT Marks Abu Dhabi LLC and Member Corp on 30 Jan 2025, and DT Marks Al Raha Beach LLC in July 2025 (CREW).
- The Abu Dhabi licence fees came from Damac subsidiaries, not from Dar Global, the partner named in Dec 2024, and not from any Tahnoon vehicle.
- No source establishes that StringZ appears on the ADGM register or shares officers or addresses with Aryam Investment 1. That part of the brief is unverified, not disproven.

## What is unproven

- DT Marks Al Raha Beach LLC was formed in Delaware on the same day Trump signed the data-centre permitting executive order (July 2025).
- StringZ Holding RSC was registered in Abu Dhabi in April 2025 and in Delaware a month later, and it appears on the ADGM register sharing officers or addresses with Aryam Investment 1.
- The Al Raha Beach district's master developer sits in a Tahnoon-chaired corporate group.
- Sens. Warren and Kim asked CFIUS chair Bessent to review the Aryam stake; outcome.

## In more detail

- DT Marks Al Raha Beach LLC was formed on the day of the July 2025 data-centre permitting order (CREW only).
- Two Damac subsidiaries paid the new entities $5M each in 2025 for Abu Dhabi projects that are still unannounced, while the Abu Dhabi partner Eric Trump and Dar Global had talked up in Dec 2024 was Dar Global, not Damac.
- StringZ's April 2025 Abu Dhabi and May 2025 Delaware registrations rest on the WSJ alone.
- I found no ADGM register entry and no shared officers or addresses with Aryam Investment 1, so that overlap is unverified.
- Khanna's referral to the Delaware US Attorney and the Warren/Kim CFIUS request are documented, but no answer, opened matter or CFIUS outcome had been reported by Nov 2026.

## Sub-claims

### 1. DT Marks Abu Dhabi LLC and DT Marks Abu Dhabi Member Corp were formed in Delaware on 30 Jan 2025, two weeks after the Aryam deal and ten days after the inauguration.

**Confirmed**

- Evidence grade: documented for the formation (Forbes, 24 Jul 2025, from Delaware filings; repeated by CREW in 2026).
- Forbes: on 30 Jan 2025 a firm tied to the president created both companies.
- The names follow the template of more than 30 other Trump licensing entities, and corporate filings pointed to at least three unannounced deals (Abu Dhabi, Serbia, Hungary).
- Eric Trump signed the Aryam Investment 1 agreement for 49% of WLF on about 16 Jan 2025 (WSJ).
- The formation therefore came 14 days later.
- That sequence is our own observation: no source links the licensing entity to the Aryam deal, and the counterparty that later paid it was Damac, not a Tahnoon vehicle.
- Audit: Forbes's own Instagram post (relayed) and Forbes Türkiye repeat the 30 Jan 2025 formation of both entities; Forbes Türkiye adds later DT Marks Doha LLC/Member Corp filings.
- Amounts: 49%
- Dates: 24 Jul 2025; 30 Jan 2025; 16 Jan 2025

- No source ties Eric's Abu Dhabi meetings to Tahnoon's vehicles.

- Amounts: 49%
- Dates: mid-December 2024; 16 Jan 2025; 30 Jan

## Denials and context

- WLF says neither Trump nor Steve Witkoff had any involvement in the Aryam transaction; Trump says he knows nothing about it.
- CNBC found no evidence that Damac payments influenced the data-centre permitting order.
  - That order is general policy.
- Under the Trump Organization's January 2025 ethics policy, deals with private foreign companies are permitted.
  - Damac is private, while Aryam and StringZ are reportedly backed by a senior UAE official.
- The registration dates and formation-date coincidences rest on CREW, Forbes and the WSJ.
  - I could not query the Delaware or ADGM registers from here, and citizensforethics.org, forbes.com and thenationalnews.com were blocked for direct fetch, so their findings come from search snippets and syndicated copies.
