# C77: Greenland, the seabed and rare earths: Cantor-linked firms before Lutnick's department

**Verdict:** Mostly accurate · medium confidence  
**Origin:** Connection hunt

## Bottom line

Most of the brief's links are documented. Cantor was a top shareholder of Critical Metals when U.S. officials steered Tanbreez to it, then EXIM offered a $120M LOI and officials discussed an ~8% stake that never closed.

## What holds up

- Cantor Fitzgerald holds a position in Critical Metals Corp (Tanbreez, Greenland), and Washington helped steer Tanbreez to that company and then offered it financing and discussed an equity stake (mostly accurate)
- Critical Metals' share price moved with Trump's Greenland push.
- Trump-circle figures hold a stake in the firm helping to develop Tanbreez (GreenMet).
- The Metals Company, advised by Cantor, is seeking the first U.S. deep-sea mining permits from NOAA, which sits under Lutnick; Lutnick's recusal is unclear (mostly accurate)
- Cantor also raised money for a second NOAA seabed applicant, American Ocean Minerals (merging with Odyssey Marine).
- Perpetua Resources' EXIM loan benefits a major Trump donor: John Paulson is its largest shareholder.

## What needs correcting

- Schiller and Sorial sell critical-minerals access through two vehicles at once: GreenMet (Greenland) and Javelin Advisors (Pakistan minerals lobbying) (partly accurate)
- Trump allies got shares in the Cantor-placed $1.5B USA Rare Earth PIPE announced the same day as the $1.6B Commerce letter of intent (partly accurate)
- **Claim:** The brief calls the Critical Metals tie a 'Cantor stake'  
  **Record:** It was a roughly 1% shareholding at end-2024 (reported third-largest investor), with conflicting later data and no confirmed 2026 holding.
- There is no U.S. government stake in Critical Metals. Only talks (Oct 2025) and a non-binding $120M EXIM letter of interest exist, and an official later said no investment was being actively considered.
- NOAA has not issued TMC any commercial recovery permit. Its consolidated application is in EIS review, with a decision expected around Q1 2027.
- 'Which Trump allies got shares': the only named investors (Schwarzman, Griffin, Cohen) come from a Senate letter, not from USAR disclosures.
- Perpetua's 'Cantor-linked' status rests on a 2021 offering and analyst coverage. Its more consequential political link is its largest shareholder, John Paulson.
- Gains: USAR traded around $9.30 in Oct 2026 against the $21.50 PIPE price, so PIPE buyers were not in profit at that date.

## What is unproven

- Ronald Lauder, the Trump friend credited with the Greenland-purchase idea, has a stake in Tanbreez or Critical Metals.
- GreenMet also links to Deputy Defense Secretary Steve Feinberg's Cerberus.

## In more detail

- Cantor advises TMC and co-arranged a >$150M raise for a second NOAA seabed applicant (AOMC) while Lutnick's NOAA decides their permits.
- Cantor led USAR's $1.5B PIPE, and John Paulson, a top Trump fundraiser, is the largest holder of EXIM-backed Perpetua.
- Trump associates Schiller and Sorial own part of GreenMet, Tanbreez's development partner.
- Not substantiated: any Lutnick recusal (unknown), the Warren letter's claim that named Trump allies bought PIPE shares, and family gains (PIPE buyers were ~57% under water by Oct 2026).

### 1. Cantor Fitzgerald holds a position in Critical Metals Corp (Tanbreez, Greenland), and Washington helped steer Tanbreez to that company and then offered it financing and discussed an equity stake.

**Mostly accurate**

- Evidence: documented for each step; Cantor's current stake is not. (1) Jan 2025: Reuters reported that U.S. officials lobbied Tanbreez's then-owner, Greg Barnes, not to sell to a Chinese-linked buyer.
- Critical Metals got the project for $5M cash and $211M in its own stock, and Reuters described Cantor, then still led by Howard Lutnick (nominated but not yet confirmed), as Critical Metals' third-largest investor. (2) A 13F aggregator puts Cantor at about 1.25M shares (~1%) at 31 Dec 2024.
- Another shows a cut to about 161K shares in Q1 2025; the two conflict, and Cantor does not appear among top holders in 2026 aggregator lists.
- No primary 13F was readable here. (3) Jun 2025: EXIM sent a non-binding letter of interest for a loan of up to $120M over 15 years.
- Separately, the company applied for a $50M Defense Production Act grant. (4) Oct 2025: Reuters reported talks to convert that grant into an equity stake of roughly 8%.
- Bloomberg then quoted an official saying no investment was being actively considered.
- No completed U.S. stake or final EXIM loan was found through Nov 2026. (5) Cantor is also the company's research broker: it initiated coverage on 2026-07-14 with a Speculative Buy and an $18 target (C60).
- What this does not show: any Cantor gain from the Jan 2026 rally, because its holding at that time is unknown.
- Amounts: $5M; $211M; ~1%; up to $120M; $50M
- Dates: Jan 2025; 31 Dec 2024; Jun 2025; Oct 2025; Nov 2026
- Sources: [3](https://www.mining.com/web/us-lobbied-greenland-rare-earths-developer-tanbreez-not-to-sell-to-china/) [9](https://equibles.com/stocks/crml/holdings) [4](https://www.miningweekly.com/article/us-export-import-bank-considers-120m-loan-for-greenland-rare-earths-project-2025-06-17) [5](https://www.nasdaq.com/articles/critical-metals-corp-secures-120-million-letter-interest-exim-bank-tanbreez-project) [1](https://www.miningweekly.com/article/trump-administration-eyes-stake-in-company-developing-greenland-rare-earths-mine-2025-10-06) [2](https://www.mining.com/us-government-pours-cold-water-on-critical-metals-deal) [8](https://thedeepdive.ca/a-western-company-just-locked-up-greenlands-rarest-deposit-and-its-backers-have-washington-ties/)

### 3. Trump-circle figures hold a stake in the firm helping to develop Tanbreez (GreenMet).

**Confirmed**

- Evidence: documented (OCCRP/NPR 2026-01-22, Bloomberg 2025 feature, CNBC via CB Insights, Mining.com).
- GreenMet, the trade name of Greentech Minerals Holdings (founded 2021), agreed with Critical Metals to help develop Tanbreez.
- It brokers deals between Washington and critical-minerals companies.
- CEO Drew Horn was Mike Pence's policy director and a senior adviser to the DNI in Trump's first term.
- OCCRP found that George Sorial (former Trump Organization EVP and chief compliance counsel) and Keith Schiller (Trump's longtime bodyguard and former Oval Office operations director) are listed as beneficial shareholders, with Schiller also named as a director.
- Both say they are passive minority investors with no management role, and Sorial says he has no involvement with Greenland.
- Libra Group, an early backer, sold its non-controlling stake on 2025-11-04.
- In Oct 2026 GreenMet signed a $1M contract to help a DRC miner (Kamituga Mining) build ties with the U.S. government.
- What this does not show: that GreenMet's Trump-circle shareholders shaped EXIM's or the Pentagon's handling of Tanbreez.
- GreenMet's exact economic interest in Tanbreez is reported inconsistently.
- Amounts: $1M
- Dates: 2026-01-22; 2025-11-04; Oct 2026

### 6. The Metals Company, advised by Cantor, is seeking the first U.S. deep-sea mining permits from NOAA, which sits under Lutnick; Lutnick's recusal is unclear.

**Mostly accurate**

- Evidence: the link and timeline are documented (AP, NOAA, company releases); the recusal is unverified.
- AP (2026-05-21) reports that TMC 'has received financial advice from Cantor Fitzgerald', and other outlets call Cantor one of TMC's leading bankers.
- AP also notes that Lutnick 'is now in charge of NOAA and could be influential in the final decision on permits'.
- The April 2025 seabed-mining executive order gave Lutnick 60 days to expedite licences and permits.
- Timeline: TMC filed within about a week of the order; NOAA's streamlined rule allowing consolidated applications came in January 2026; TMC filed the first consolidated exploration-plus-commercial-recovery application on 2026-02-06; NOAA found it in full compliance around 2026-04-28 to 05-01; Federal Register publication, with a notice of intent for an EIS on 2026-08-17; public comment closed 2026-10-19; TMC expects a decision by end of Q1 2027.
- No commercial permit had been issued as of Nov 2026.
- Earthjustice and others are suing over NOAA's certification of a TMC USA exploration application.
- Recusal: the NYT reported in 2025 that Lutnick's ethics agreement would require him, at least initially, to recuse from matters involving 106 corporate entities.
- Whether TMC is on that list, and whether Lutnick has stepped back from TMC decisions, was not found.
- Commerce says he complies with all divestiture and recusal requirements.
- Korea Zinc took 5% of TMC for $85M in Jun 2025.
- Amounts: 5%; $85M
- Dates: 2026-05-21; April 2025; January 2026; 2026-02-06; 2026-04-28
- Sources: [22](https://wtop.com/news/2026/05/trumps-push-for-deep-sea-mining-spawns-new-companies-and-fast-tracked-rules/) [23](https://www.bnnbloomberg.ca/business/2026/05/21/trumps-push-for-deep-sea-mining-spawns-new-companies-and-fast-tracked-rules/) [24](https://www.noaa.gov/news-release/noaa-advances-review-of-first-ever-deep-seabed-mining-commercial-recovery-application) [25](https://www.barchart.com/story/news/37171286/tmc-usa-files-first-consolidated-deep-seabed-mining-application-increasing-expected-commercial-recovery-permit-area-to-65-000-km2) [26](https://www.barchart.com/story/news/1644737/noaa-determines-tmc-usas-consolidated-deep-seabed-mining-application-is-in-full-compliance) [27](https://earthjustice.org/press/2026/lawsuit-challenges-trump-administration-greenlighting-of-international-deep-sea-mining-exploration-permit) [33](https://www.yahoo.com/news/howard-lutnick-trumps-commerce-pick-194648767.html) [28](https://www.mining.com/?p=1181501)

- Warren, Wyden and Van Hollen's 2026-02-26 letter says 'billionaire Trump allies appear to have had access to shares', naming Steve Schwarzman, Ken Griffin and Steven A. Cohen as Trump campaign and inauguration donors.
- USAR has not published a buyer list, and no filing found here confirms the three as PIPE buyers.
- A 13F aggregator lists Citadel Advisors with about 5.6M USAR shares in Q3 2026, but 13F holdings do not show PIPE participation.

- Gains: USAR traded at about $9.30 on 2026-10-23, roughly 57% below the PIPE price, so PIPE buyers had paper losses at that date, not gains.
- Cantor's fee is the only certain income.
- USAR says Cantor was not involved in the government deal.
- Amounts: $21.50; up to $277M; $1.3B; $17.17; about $50M

## Denials and context

- Commerce: Lutnick divested Cantor and complies with all divestiture and recusal requirements in his ethics agreement.
  - It says neither he nor his department has discussed rare-earth companies with Cantor.
- USA Rare Earth says Cantor had no role in the government transaction.
  - Cantor denies wrongdoing and calls itself a capital-raising partner.
- Sorial and Schiller call themselves passive minority investors in GreenMet.
  - GreenMet did not answer NPR.
  - Lauder and the White House declined to comment on his Greenland holdings.
- Cantor is one of the most active small-cap mining and SPAC banks, so some overlap with federally backed miners is structural.
  - The national-security rationale for non-Chinese rare-earth supply is bipartisan.
- Source limits: direct fetches of Senate sites, NPR member stations and 13F aggregators failed in this session (host not allowed).
  - Details rely on search-result summaries, and 13F figures come from aggregators that conflict.

## Full report

**Verdict: mostly-accurate** (confidence: medium). This is a connection hunt that goes a second layer deeper than C60, C50 and C35.

### Summary

Most of the links in the brief are on the record:

- **Critical Metals (Greenland).** Cantor was among Critical Metals' largest shareholders when U.S. officials steered the Tanbreez deposit away from a Chinese-linked buyer and toward the company. EXIM then offered a $120M letter of interest, and officials discussed taking a roughly 8% stake. The stake never closed.
- **Deep-sea mining.** Cantor advises The Metals Company (TMC). It also co-arranged a raise of more than $150M for a second NOAA seabed applicant, American Ocean Minerals (AOMC). NOAA, inside Lutnick's Commerce Department, decides both companies' permits.
- **USA Rare Earth.** Cantor led USAR's $1.5B private share sale (PIPE).
- **Perpetua.** EXIM approved a $2.9B loan for Perpetua, whose largest shareholder is the Trump fundraiser John Paulson.
- **GreenMet.** Trump's former bodyguard Keith Schiller and former Trump Organization counsel George Sorial own part of GreenMet, Tanbreez's development partner.

Three things in the brief are not established:

- **Recusal.** Whether Lutnick has recused himself from the seabed permits could not be determined.
- **Trump allies in the PIPE.** The claim rests only on a Senate letter.
- **Gains.** No family or ally gain is documented. PIPE buyers were about 57% under water in October 2026.

### What the brief asked and what the record shows

#### 1. Critical Metals / Tanbreez (Greenland)
- **January 2025 (Reuters):** U.S. officials lobbied Tanbreez's owner, Greg Barnes, not to sell to a Chinese-linked bidder. Critical Metals bought control for $5M in cash plus $211M in its own stock. Reuters named Cantor, then still led by Lutnick, as Critical Metals' third-largest investor. *Documented.*
- **Size of Cantor's stake:** about 1.25M shares (around 1%) at the end of 2024, according to a 13F aggregator. Another aggregator shows a cut to about 161K shares in Q1 2025, and the two conflict. No 2026 holding was found. Cantor's research arm initiated coverage of the stock on 2026-07-14 (C60). *Single-source / aggregator.*
- **June 2025:** EXIM sent a non-binding letter of interest for a loan of up to $120M. Critical Metals also applied for a $50M Defense Production Act grant. *Documented.*
- **October 2025:** Reuters reported talks to convert the grant into a stake of roughly 8%. Bloomberg then quoted an official saying no investment was being actively considered. No U.S. stake and no final EXIM loan had been reported by November 2026. *Documented.*
- **January 2026:** during Trump's Greenland push, CRML shares rose nearly 150%. In April 2026, Greenland approved Critical Metals taking 92.5% of Tanbreez. Who held the stock during the spike is unknown. *Documented (price); unverified (who gained).*

#### 2. GreenMet: Trump's inner circle in Tanbreez
- GreenMet is a Washington critical-minerals dealmaker run by Drew Horn, who was Mike Pence's policy director. It agreed to help develop Tanbreez.
- OCCRP and NPR (2026-01-22) found that George Sorial (former Trump Organization EVP and compliance chief) and Keith Schiller (former bodyguard and Oval Office operations director) are listed as beneficial shareholders. Schiller is also a director.
- Both men say they are passive investors. *Documented.*
- Libra Group, an early backer, sold its stake in November 2025. In October 2026, GreenMet took a $1M contract to help a DRC miner build ties with the U.S. government.

#### 3. Lauder and Greenland
Bolton told Forbes that Ronald Lauder gave Trump the idea of buying Greenland. Politiken and the Irish Times report that Lauder co-owns Greenland Water Bank and is linked to a hydropower plan run through Greenland Development Partners. No link between Lauder and Tanbreez, Critical Metals or Cantor was found. *Documented holdings; Tanbreez link unverified.*

#### 4. The Metals Company and NOAA
- **The Cantor link:** AP reports that TMC "has received financial advice from Cantor Fitzgerald". AP also notes that Lutnick "is now in charge of NOAA and could be influential in the final decision on permits." *Documented.*
- **Timeline:**
  - April 2025: Trump's executive order gave Commerce 60 days to speed up seabed permits.
  - About a week later: TMC filed its applications.
  - 2026-02-06: TMC filed the first combined exploration and commercial-recovery application.
  - Around 2026-04-28 to 05-01: NOAA found the application in full compliance.
  - 2026-08-17: NOAA published a notice of intent to prepare an environmental impact statement.
  - 2026-10-19: public comment closed.
  - TMC expects a decision by the end of Q1 2027. No commercial permit has been issued.
- Earthjustice is suing over NOAA's certification of a TMC USA exploration application.
- **Recusal:** the NYT reported in 2025 that Lutnick would initially recuse himself from matters involving 106 entities. Whether TMC is on that list was not found. *Unverified.*

#### 5. A second Cantor-banked seabed applicant: American Ocean Minerals
On 2026-04-08, AOMC agreed an all-stock merger with Odyssey Marine Exploration worth about $1B. Reuters reports that Citigroup and Cantor (chaired by Brandon Lutnick) arranged the private placement of more than $150M and act as AOMC's capital-markets advisers. NOAA advanced AOMC's Area-1 application on 2026-07-07. The investors' names and Cantor's fees are not public. *Documented.*

#### 6. USA Rare Earth PIPE and "Trump allies"
- **The deal:** the PIPE closed on 2026-01-29: about 69.8M shares at $21.50, with Cantor as lead placement agent and Moelis as co-agent. Commerce signed the final agreements on 2026-06-03: $277M in funding plus a $1.3B loan, for about 16.1M shares and about 17.6M warrants (reported exercise price $17.17).
- **The allies claim:** a 2026-02-26 letter from Warren, Wyden and Van Hollen says Trump allies "appear to have had access" to the PIPE shares, and names Schwarzman, Griffin and S. A. Cohen. No USAR disclosure confirms this. Citadel shows up as a USAR shareholder in 13F filings (about 5.6M shares in Q3 2026), but 13F filings do not show who bought in the PIPE. *Single-source allegation.*
- **Follow-up letters:** on 2026-07-07, Warren, Wyden, Van Hollen and Lofgren wrote to Brandon Lutnick and USAR CEO Barbara Humpton. They asked how Cantor was chosen, what it was paid and whether its people met Commerce officials.
- **Fees:** coverage says USAR booked about $50M in PIPE costs. Cantor's share is undisclosed.
- **Gains:** the stock was around $9.30 on 2026-10-23, so PIPE buyers had paper losses.

#### 7. Perpetua: the EXIM loan and Paulson
- **Paulson's position:** John Paulson owns about 25-26% of Perpetua and is its largest shareholder. He bought $100M of a $425M raise in June 2025 at $13.20 a share.
- **Paulson and Trump:** he hosted an April 2024 Trump fundraiser that raised $50.5M. He withdrew from consideration for Treasury Secretary.
- **Federal support:** Stibnite was named a FAST-41 priority project in April 2025. The Pentagon has given more than $80M. EXIM notified Congress of a $2.7B loan, and in May 2026 its board approved $2.9B, still subject to final documents.
- **Cantor's role is thin:** Cantor Canada co-led a 2021 offering and Cantor covers the stock as an analyst. Cantor was not on the 2025 raise.
- **A parallel case:** Paulson's Trilogy Metals stake gained an estimated $60-70M on paper on 2025-10-06, the day the U.S. took a stake in the company. He later sold it. *Documented.*

### Not connected in the coverage we found (our own cross-referencing)
- **Schiller and Sorial as a minerals-access pair** (*inferred*). They co-own GreenMet (Tanbreez) and co-founded Javelin Advisors, whose FARA-registered Pakistan work had a critical-minerals mandate (C64, C72). Each fact is documented. No source links the two ventures.
- **GreenMet, Cerberus and Feinberg** (*inferred*). In 2022, GreenMet helped arrange a $50M Cerberus-led investment in Torngat Metals. Feinberg, a Cerberus co-founder, is now Deputy Defense Secretary, and the Pentagon runs the kind of DPA grants Critical Metals sought. The deal predates this administration, and no source ties it to Tanbreez.
- **Cantor across the NOAA queue** (*inferred as a pattern*). Cantor is linked to at least two of the roughly nine companies seeking seabed access (TMC and AOMC). Each link is documented. "Cantor banks the permit queue" is our characterisation.
- **Cantor and the CRML spike** (*inferred*). Cantor's position in Critical Metals in January 2026 is unknown, so no gain can be attributed to it.
- **Cantor and Cerberus** (*documented in C74; inferred here*). C74 records Cantor as Cerberus's backer and prime broker. That closes a loop between Lutnick's and Feinberg's former firms, but nothing ties it to these deals.

### Denials and context
- **Commerce:** Lutnick divested from Cantor and complies with all recusal requirements.
- **USAR and Cantor:** USAR says Cantor had no role in the government deal. Cantor denies any wrongdoing.
- **GreenMet's shareholders:** Sorial and Schiller say they are passive investors.
- **Lauder and the White House:** both declined to comment.
- **Context:** Cantor is a dominant small-cap mining bank, so some overlap with these companies is structural.
