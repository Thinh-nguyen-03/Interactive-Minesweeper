# C01: $TRUMP meme coin

**Verdict:** Mostly accurate (confidence: medium)

## What the video said

> Trump launched an official $TRUMP meme coin two days before his January 2025 inauguration; he 'raked in' about $600 million from it via shell companies; the coin has since lost ~95% of its value and almost all buyers lost money; Trump defended crypto involvement by saying the US must lead or China will.

## Summary

Trump launched the official $TRUMP meme coin on the evening of January 17, 2025, about 2.5 days before his inauguration. His own 2025 OGE financial disclosure reports about $635-636M in meme-coin royalty and licensing income through CIC Digital LLC, so the '$600M' figure is a slight understatement of self-reported gross income. TRUMP (about 95% down in early 2026, about 98% by November 2026) and MELANIA (about 99% down) have collapsed, and on-chain data shows most buyers lost money, though no exact share exists. Trump did make the 'lead or China takes over' remark (CNBC, July 2026). The suggestion that he launched the coin 'so China wouldn't do it first' is the video's sarcastic paraphrase.

## Full report

**Verdict: mostly accurate (confidence: medium-high)**

The core facts check out. Trump launched the official $TRUMP meme coin on the evening of Friday, January 17, 2025, about two and a half days before his January 20 inauguration. His 2025 financial disclosure reports about $635-636 million in meme-coin royalty and licensing income through CIC Digital LLC. The coin now trades about 95-98% below its peak, and Melania's coin is down about 99%. Trump did tie crypto to competition with China in a July 2026 CNBC interview.

Three things need flagging:
- The "$600M" figure rounds a self-reported amount of gross licensing income down.
- The "shell companies" label is the video's own description.
- "Almost exclusively lost money" is directionally right, but no exact count of losing buyers exists.

### What the video says (transcript lines 101-135, 193-231)
- "Two days before Trump took office… Trump just launched a meme coin."
- "When the president raked in $600 million with Trump coin, she [Molly White] tracked the shell companies." Melania "reported $6 million on her coin and NFTs."
- A clip shows Trump answering a question about his family's crypto business: "I was there before I was in office… We have to be at the top. Otherwise, China is going to take it over."
- Melania's coin is down about 99% and Trump's about 95%. Buyers "have almost exclusively lost money" unless they were very early.

### Sub-claims

1. **Launched two days before the inauguration: confirmed, with an approximate figure.** Trump announced the coin on Truth Social and X on the evening of Friday, January 17, 2025 (US time). In UTC that was January 18, so some outlets date the launch to the 18th. The inauguration was Monday, January 20. "Two days" is a fair round number, and some outlets wrote "three days." Two Trump-linked LLCs, CIC Digital LLC and Fight Fight Fight LLC, together held 80% of the supply. That 80% was locked and set to unlock over three years. About 200M of the planned 1B tokens were released at launch.

2. **"Raked in $600 million": mostly accurate.** Trump's annual OGE financial disclosure for calendar 2025 was released June 30, 2026. It lists "Celebration Coins" royalties of $635,068,835 under CIC Digital LLC, and about $636.1M on the CIC Digital licensing and crypto line. The total is gross licensing and royalty income that Trump reported himself, so "$600M" slightly understates it. Earlier outside estimates ranged widely:
   - Forbes put it at about $315M.
   - Chainalysis found about $324M in trading fees went to creator-linked wallets by May 2025.
   - Coinbase's Conor Grogan estimated more than $800M.

3. **"Shell companies": characterization.** The proceeds flow through LLCs rather than to Trump directly. CIC Digital LLC is a Trump Organization affiliate. Fight Fight Fight LLC is run by longtime Trump business partner Bill Zanker. Both are opaque, but "shell companies" is the video's pejorative label, not a legal finding.

4. **Molly White tracked these entities: plausible but only partly verified.** Molly White writes the *Citation Needed* newsletter. She published an analysis of Trump's $1.4B crypto disclosure and has commented widely on the meme coin, for example on Democracy Now in May 2025. I did not find her specific entity-tracking work.

5. **Melania "$6 million": confirmed with a caveat.** Her 2025 OGE filing shows more than $6M from NFTs and "other collectibles" under a license agreement with Designers Manager. The filing does not name $MELANIA. Linking the income to the coin is an inference made by analysts, for example at the Center for American Progress.

6. **Trump's answer about crypto and China: confirmed.** He gave it in a White House interview with CNBC's Joe Kernen around July 2, 2026, after the disclosure came out. Asked whether he knew about his family's crypto ventures, he said he "could know" but didn't, called it "nothing illegal," and said the US must lead or "China's going to take it over." The video then says he launched the coin "so China wouldn't do it first." That is Coffeezilla's sarcastic paraphrase. Trump did not give China as a reason for the meme coin specifically.

7. **TRUMP down about 95%, MELANIA down about 99%: confirmed, and now understated for TRUMP.** TRUMP peaked at about $73.43 on January 19, 2025. A February 2026 snapshot showed it about 95% down, matching the video. By November 9-13, 2026 it was around $1.05-1.10, about 98% down, after falling further when the midterms were held. MELANIA peaked at about $13.73 and is down about 99%.

8. **Buyers have almost all lost money: directionally supported, not precisely quantified.** In May 2025 Chainalysis data reported by CNBC showed:
   - about 764,000 of roughly 2M buyer wallets were in the red;
   - 58 wallets had made more than $10M each, about $1.1B combined.

   Prices have fallen much further since then. Wallet counts do not equal people. BeInCrypto and Whale Alert estimate retail losses across TRUMP and MELANIA at about $4.3B, with more than 2M wallets underwater. I found no audited figure for the share of buyers who lost money.

### Corrections
- The launch was the evening of January 17, 2025 (January 18 UTC), about 2.5 days before the inauguration. "Two days" is approximate.
- The money figure comes from Trump's own disclosure: about $635-636M in royalty and licensing income through CIC Digital for 2025. It is gross licensing income, not an independently traced profit.
- TRUMP is now about 98% below its peak (November 2026), not 95%. The 95% figure matched early 2026.
- Trump's China remark was about crypto in general, not a reason for launching the meme coin.

### Denials and other side
- Trump said on Truth Social that the coin was "not intended to be… an investment opportunity."
- The White House says the president's assets are held in a trust managed by his children and that ethics rules are being followed.
- Trump told CNBC there was "nothing illegal" and that his son Eric and outside managers run the businesses.
- Trump never directly answered the conflict-of-interest question. Critics, including the WSJ editorial board, called the arrangement "an unseemly display of using the Presidency for family profit."
