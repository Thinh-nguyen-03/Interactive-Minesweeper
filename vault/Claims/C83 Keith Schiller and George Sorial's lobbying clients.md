# C83: Keith Schiller and George Sorial's lobbying clients

**Verdict:** Mostly accurate · medium confidence  
**Type:** Connection check · Pardons, lobbyists and access

## The claim

> Which clients have paid Javelin Advisors, the firm of Trump's former bodyguard Keith Schiller and former Trump Organization executive George Sorial, and for what?

## Bottom line

Javelin Advisors' FARA filings show it is owned in equal thirds by Schiller, Sorial and Robert Seiden.

## What holds up

- Javelin Advisors is a three-way partnership: Keith Schiller, George Sorial and Robert Seiden co-own it equally, so Seiden Law's Pakistan subcontract went to a firm Seiden himself co-owns.
- Javelin was set up in December 2024, after the election, by Schiller and Sorial, with an LDA registration effective 10 Dec 2024.
- Fred Daibes paid Javelin about $1M in 2025 to seek clemency; he got none and dropped the firm by June 2025 (mostly accurate)
- Greg Lindberg, the insurance magnate who pleaded guilty in a $2B fraud and bribery scheme, hired Javelin in 2025 to lobby for an 'executive pardon'.
- Taiwan's de facto embassy (TECRO) is a Javelin foreign principal under a new FARA registration in March 2026.
- Taiwanese food group I-Mei Foods signed Javelin for US government relations tied to potential contracts; Schiller attended the signing in Taiwan (mostly accurate)
- Schiller and Sorial are shareholders in GreenMet, whose CEO Drew Horn brokers US-government ties for mining clients; Sorial's 'no management role' statement conflicts with a bio listing him on GreenMet's board (mostly accurate)
- Since Nov 2024 GreenMet has sold US-government access to foreign mining clients: a $1M Kamituga Mining (DRC) contract in 2026, Locksley Resources (Australia) in 2025, and reported Nigeria/Cameroon deal-hunting (mostly accurate)

## What needs correcting

- Javelin's Pakistan work had a rare-earths mandate and reached Trump allies in Congress (Ronny Jackson and others) (partly accurate)
- US decisions touched Schiller/Sorial-linked clients: EXIM's $120M Tanbreez letter (GreenMet partner), Pakistan's 2025 gains, and the US-DRC minerals framework (partly accurate)
- The claim describes Javelin as Schiller and Sorial's firm; FARA filings show a third equal owner, Robert Seiden, whose law firm was Pakistan's prime contractor.
- The claim cites Daibes' '$1M' as established; the amount rests on NBC's three unnamed sources, while the LDA forms establish the engagement and its 'executive relief' purpose.
- The claim implies GreenMet holds Tanbreez equity; it is a development partner, and Critical Metals (92.5%) and European Lithium hold the project (C77).

## What is unproven

- Logical Strategies LLC, a New York entity with undisclosed owners, is a Javelin lobbying client; reportedly about $1.2M in Q3 2025 on EPA's asbestos rule.
- Schiller and Sorial have documented White House access or official meetings since Nov 2024.

## In more detail

- So Pakistan's $200K/month minerals contract with Seiden Law was partly passed to a firm Seiden co-owns.
- Beyond Pakistan and Daibes, Javelin's documented clients include Greg Lindberg (a pardon bid that is still unresolved), Taiwan's TECRO (FARA, March 2026), I-Mei Foods and an opaque LLC, Logical Strategies.
- Schiller and Sorial's other shared vehicle, GreenMet, sells Washington access to foreign miners (Kamituga, DRC: $1M) while partnering on Tanbreez.
- What is not documented is any White House meeting or access since November 2024, or any US decision credited to them: Daibes got no clemency, and the policy overlaps are our own inference.

## Sub-claims

### 1. Javelin Advisors is a three-way partnership: Keith Schiller, George Sorial and Robert Seiden co-own it equally, so Seiden Law's Pakistan subcontract went to a firm Seiden himself co-owns.

**Confirmed** · Evidence: Documented (Javelin's own FARA filings)

- Javelin's FARA registration No. 7705 (received 2026-03-23) says the firm is 'co-owned and controlled equally by George A Sorial, Keith Schiller, and Robert W. Seiden' and lists all three as managing directors; Sorial is the named responsible individual and the address is 1701 Pennsylvania Ave NW, Suite 200, Washington DC.
- Business Today (2026-01-07) also reports Seiden holds a stake.
- The earlier Pakistan registration (No. 7579, 2025-04-25) carried the same ownership language per C64's audit.
- Combined with C64/C72: Pakistan paid Seiden Law $200K/month (FARA 7570, signed 2025-04-08), and Seiden subcontracted about $50K/month to Javelin.
- So part of the Pakistan fee flowed to a firm owned by the prime contractor's own principal.
- No source says this was improper; FARA allows it, and it was disclosed.
- The filings themselves could not be read directly; their contents are taken from search-engine extracts of the PDFs.
- Amounts: $200K/month; about $50K/month
- Dates: 2026-03-23; 2026-01-07; 2025-04-25; 2025-04-08
- Sources: [7](https://efile.fara.gov/docs/7705-Registration-Statement-20260323-1.pdf) [8](https://efile.fara.gov/docs/7705-Exhibit-AB-20260323-1.pdf) [20](https://www.businesstoday.in/world/us/story/ronny-jackson-former-white-house-doctor-identified-as-key-link-in-us-pakistans-500-million-mining-deal-509756-2026-01-07) [5](https://efile.fara.gov/docs/7579-Registration-Statement-20250425-1.pdf)

### 4. Greg Lindberg, the insurance magnate who pleaded guilty in a $2B fraud and bribery scheme, hired Javelin in 2025 to lobby for an 'executive pardon'.

**Confirmed**

- Evidence grade: documented (LDA disclosures as reported by NOTUS, Insurance Business and others).
- NOTUS says Lindberg hired Javelin 'this summer' (2025); another report puts it in late October.
- Insurance Business says Lindberg appeared on Javelin's filings for three quarters, with 'executive pardon' named only in the first.
- On 2025-12-23 North Carolina Insurance Commissioner Mike Causey, the official Lindberg was convicted of trying to bribe, publicly urged Trump not to pardon him.
- Sen. Thom Tillis was quoted saying he told Pardon Attorney Ed Martin it was a bad idea; January 2026 North Carolina coverage (seen via search extracts) says Sen. Ted Budd also weighed in against a pardon.
- No grant or denial was found through our search; the latest coverage we found (about March 2026) describes the request as pending.
- Lindberg's earlier co-defendant, ex-Rep. Robin Hayes, was pardoned at the end of Trump's first term.
- Lindberg's lawyer said pardons should be given when the facts warrant it.
- This is a new pardon client not covered in C49 or C64.
- Dates: 2025-12-23; January 2026; March 2026
- Sources: [12](https://www.notus.org/money/donald-trump-pardons-clemency-lobbyists-roger-stone-boosie-badazz) [13](https://www.insurancebusinessmag.com/us/news/breaking-news/greg-lindberg-hires-trumplinked-lobbyists-in-bid-for-presidential-pardon-554551.aspx) [14](https://www.ncdoi.gov/news/press-releases/2025/12/23/commissioner-causey-urges-president-trump-not-pardon-greg-lindberg) [15](https://ncnewsline.com/2025/12/24/nc-insurance-commissioner-urges-trump-not-to-pardon-convicted-felon-greg-lindberg/) [16](https://www.insurancejournal.com/news/southeast/2026/01/09/853762.htm)

  - An undated bio still lists Sorial on GreenMet's board.
- Daibes' lawyer: no work with Schiller or Javelin since June 2025.
  - Schiller and Javelin did not answer NBC.
  - The White House does not comment on pardons.
- Lindberg's lawyer: pardons should be given when the facts warrant it.
  - Opposed by NC Commissioner Causey and Sens. Tillis and Budd.

  - No source alleges that Schiller or Sorial broke the law or produced any government outcome.
