# C98: Trump Media, Licensing & Merchandise Income During the Presidency

**Verdict:** Mostly accurate · high confidence  
**Type:** Check · Family Ventures & Wealth

## The Claim

> What has Trump earned from Trump Media, licensing, properties and merchandise since January 2025?

## Bottom Line

Outside crypto, Trump's own 2025 disclosure (filed 30 June 2026) shows large business revenue but almost no new cash from Trump Media.

## What Holds Up

- Trump's Trump Media stake is held in his revocable trust, has not been sold and pays nothing.
- The paper value of the Trump Media stake has fallen sharply during the term.
- Golf clubs and resorts, including Mar-a-Lago, earned record revenue in 2025.
- Foreign licensing and development deals (Dar Global and others) paid Trump tens of millions in 2025.
- Branded merchandise and royalties (watches, sneakers, Bible, guitars, fragrances, books) earn Trump royalties.
- Trump took on new borrowing in 2025.

## What Needs Correcting

- The ethics arrangement keeps Trump away from the business and bars deals with foreign governments (partly accurate)
- Claimed (C31, The National/IndexBox): about $21.9M from Dar Al Arkan in 2025, 'nearly identical' to 2024. The record shows: the three Dar Al Arkan licensee lines sum to $20.55M, and adding the $5.25M Doha fee from Dar Global Holdings gives $25.80M, the figure CNBC reports. The Gulf total of about $38.4M is correct.
- Claimed (C31/C88, citing Eastern Herald): Damac's roughly $12.5M is an estimate because the form uses ranges. The record shows: all four Damac-linked fee lines are exact dollar amounts and sum to $12,589,884. Only asset values on those lines are ranges.
- Claimed (C20 and some Forbes pieces): the trust holds about 52-53% of TMTG. The record shows: about 52% was correct from the Dec 2024 transfer until the end of May 2025 (220,657,014 shares outstanding at 31 Dec 2024). After the 29 May 2025 private placement of 55,857,181 shares the same 114.75M shares were 41.5% at Dec 2025 (13D/A) and about 41% at Nov 2026 (prospectus).
- Press figures for foreign licensing differ: CNN 'nearly $60M', CNBC $59.5M, CREW $59M of licensing and development fees, and our line-by-line sum of all foreign licensing, management and development lines $63.1M. The difference is definitional, not an error.
- Front Office Sports attributes Bedminster's $37.6M to Lamington Family Holdings LLC. The record shows: the revenue is reported by Lamington Farm Club, LLC; Lamington Family Holdings holds a 10-acre lot.
- C17's 2024 royalty figures (watches $2.8M, sneakers and fragrances $2.5M, Bible $1,306,035, NFTs about $1.16M) check out against the 2025 filing. In 2025, every line except watches fell sharply.
- 16 Nov 2026 (audit): the first version of this check dated the end of the trust's 52% stake to March 2025; it lasted until the 29 May 2025 private placement.

## What Is Unproven

- Mar-a-Lago's initiation fee was raised to $1 million.
- LIV Golf events and other bookings at Trump courses pay the Trump Organization.
- Trump Mobile and the T1 phone generate income for Trump.
- Trump's 2026 business income is known.

## In More Detail

- Reported 2025 revenue includes about $413.6M from US golf clubs and resorts, among them Mar-a-Lago at $77.5M and Doral at $121.9M. Scotland added about GBP 30.4M and Doonbeg about EUR 18.9M. Foreign licensing, management and development fees come to about $63.1M by our line-by-line sum, with $38.4M of that from the Gulf; press tallies of $59-60M are narrower.
- Unnamed family members hold 20% of the licensing companies that received $54.5M of those fees.
- Merchandise royalties were about $5.0M and book royalties about $3.0M. All of these are revenue, not profit.
- Trump Media is paper value only.
- SEC filings show the trust's 114,750,000 shares were never sold, paid no dividend and were not reported as pledged, while their market value fell from about $4.6B before the inauguration to about $0.53-0.64B in Oct-Nov 2026.
- Several items have no documented figure: 2026 income (not yet disclosed), LIV Golf fees, Mar-a-Lago initiation income, and any Trump Mobile payment to Trump.

## Sub-Claims

### 1. Trump's Trump Media stake is held in his revocable trust, has not been sold and pays nothing.

**Confirmed**

- The December 2025 Schedule 13D/A shows 114,750,000 DJT shares held by the Donald J. Trump Revocable Trust, of which Trump is settlor and sole beneficiary.
- Donald Trump Jr. is sole trustee with sole voting and investment power.
- That was 41.5% of 276,497,911 shares outstanding; the November 2026 TAE merger prospectus gives about 41%.
- The same count is in his 2025 and 2026 OGE filings and in the April 2025 resale registration, which registered all 114.75M shares for possible resale.
- The 13D/A reports no transactions in the prior 60 days and no pledge.
- The only Forms 4 filed under Trump's own SEC number date from December 2024.
- The OGE 2026 filing lists the stake as 'Over $50,000,000' with income 'None (or less than $201)'.
- TMTG's 10-K says there are no current plans to pay cash dividends, and its Q3 2026 10-Q says the planned digital-token distribution to shareholders was discontinued.
- Trump's name-and-likeness licence to TMTG is royalty-free: $100 paid once, and no other amounts paid to him.
- Under a December 2025 support agreement, the trust must vote for the TAE merger (vote set for 14 Dec 2026) and faces transfer restrictions.
- Amounts: 41.5%; about 41%; Over $50,000,000; less than $201; $100
- Dates: December 2025; November 2026; April 2025; December 2024; 14 Dec 2026

### 2. The paper value of the Trump Media stake has fallen sharply during the term.

**Confirmed**

- At 114,750,000 shares, our arithmetic on closing prices gives: $40.03 on 17 Jan 2025, about $4.59B; $35.59 on 21 Jan 2025, the first trading day of the term, about $4.08B; $13.24 on 31 Dec 2025, about $1.52B; $5.59 on 30 Oct 2026 (the price stated in the SEC prospectus), about $641M; and $4.64 on 13 Nov 2026, about $532M. That is a paper decline of about $4.06B since just before the inauguration.
- DJT never closed above its 21 Jan 2025 level in the period.
- Forbes and Benzinga relayed similar stake values ($683M at $5.95 on 3 Nov 2026).
- TMTG's own results swung from a FY2025 net loss of $712.3M on $3.68M of revenue to Q3 2026 net income of $409.7M, mostly from investment gains.
- None of this reaches Trump as cash.
- Amounts: $40.03; about $4.59B; $35.59; about $4.08B; $13.24
- Dates: 17 Jan 2025; 21 Jan 2025; 31 Dec 2025; 30 Oct 2026; 13 Nov 2026

- CNN's 'more than $290M' covers five properties and matches the filing ($293.4M).

- These are gross revenue figures; the form does not report profit, and Doral carries a mortgage of over $50M.
- Amounts: $77,482,488; $50,122,259; $121,861,496; $110,418,239; $37,603,295

### 6. Foreign licensing and development deals (Dar Global and others) paid Trump tens of millions in 2025.

**Confirmed**

- Exact amounts from the 2026 OGE filing: - Gulf: $38.39M in total. - Dar Al Arkan entities: Dubai tower $10,361,459, Saudi Arabia $9,243,574 and Oman $949,642. - Dar Global Holdings: Doha $5,250,000. - So Dar-linked fees come to $25.80M, the same figure CNBC reports. - Damac: Abu Dhabi $5,000,000, Al Raha Beach $5,000,000, Dubai golf licence $1,335,140 and Dubai golf management $1,254,744, for $12.59M. - Elsewhere: $24.66M. - India: $10.21M across Gurgaon, Hyderabad (a Reliance 4IR entity), Noida, Pune, Mumbai Worli and Kolkata. - Bucharest: $5M. - Vietnam (Hung Yen): $5M. - Korea: a $2M non-refundable development fee under a letter of intent with Base Co. - Philippines: $1.59M. - Istanbul: $0.58M. - Lido, Indonesia, management: $0.28M. Our line-by-line foreign total is $63.06M. CNBC reports $59.5M, CNN 'nearly $60M' and CREW $59M of 'licensing and development fees'; the differences come from which management and development lines each counts.
- The Diriyah hotel and T1 Mobile licensing entities reported no 2025 income.
- Fees are paid whether or not a project is built.
- Not all of this accrues to the President: twelve of the fee-earning LLCs ($54.49M of the $63.06M) are owned 79% by his structure and 20% by unnamed 'Trump Family Members' (see C99).
- Amounts: $38.39M; $10,361,459; $9,243,574; $949,642; $5,250,000

## Denials & Context

- Trump Organization statement on the 2026 filing: it 'demonstrates that The Trump Organization continues to maintain a strong financial position, supported by world class, valuable assets, substantial liquidity and a conservative balance sheet', and its length 'underscores our commitment to transparency' (NBC News via Yahoo).
- White House statement: 'Neither the President nor his family has ever engaged — or will ever engage — in conflicts of interest' (NBC News via Yahoo).
- Trump told reporters: 'You know why I'm profiting?
  - Because the stock market's going up.
  - Everybody's profiting', and 'I never speak to any of the people that run the money' (CNN).
- The Trump Organization says outside financial institutions manage the investments using automated trading.
  - The January 2025 ethics agreement puts the businesses in a trust run by his children, bars new deals with foreign governments but allows private foreign ones, and Eric Trump said the company would 'vastly exceed' its legal and ethical obligations (AP).
- The White House says the G20 at Doral will be 'at-cost' with 'no profit' from the State Department or any foreign government.
- Context: these are gross revenues from businesses that existed before the term (except the newer licences).
  - They are not profits, and nothing here shows the income was earned because of the presidency.
  - The 2025 licence fees come from private developers, which the 2025 ethics policy permits.
- Context: the fall in Trump Media's value is a paper loss, as was the earlier paper gain.
  - No sale has been reported.
- Context: 'Trump Family Members' hold 20% limited-partner interests in most new foreign licensing LLCs, so the fee lines are not wholly the President's income; the form shows no distributions.

## Full Report

**Verdict: mostly accurate** (confidence: high for 2025 figures; 2026 is not yet disclosed)

The question is what Donald Trump and the Trump Organization have earned outside crypto since January 2025, with cash kept separate from paper value. Most of it can be answered from primary documents.

- **Cash and reported income (calendar 2025).** Trump's own OGE Form 278e, filed 30 June 2026, reports:
  - about $413.6M of revenue from US golf clubs and resorts, including Mar-a-Lago at $77.5M and Doral at $121.9M;
  - GBP 30.4M from his two Scottish courses and EUR 18.9M from Doonbeg, about $59-63M in total;
  - about $63.1M of foreign licensing, management and development fees by our line-by-line sum ($38.4M from the Gulf);
  - about $5.0M of merchandise royalties and about $3.0M of book royalties.

  All of these are revenue, not profit.
- **Paper value.** Trump Media pays him nothing. The trust's 114,750,000 shares have not been sold, earn no dividend and were not reported as pledged. Their market value fell from about $4.1-4.6B at the start of the term to about $0.53-0.64B in October-November 2026.
- **No figure available.** We found no number for income in 2026, LIV Golf fees, the share of Mar-a-Lago revenue that comes from its reported $1M initiation fee, or any Trump Mobile payment to Trump.

**Method and access.** The 927-page 2026 OGE filing, the 2025 filing, and the SEC filings (Schedule 13D/A, 10-K, 10-Q, the TAE merger prospectus and the 2025 resale prospectus) were downloaded from OGE and EDGAR and searched in full; the business schedules were read line by line. Sums labelled "ours" are our own arithmetic from those lines. DJT prices come from market data and match the two prices stated in the SEC prospectus. CNN, NBC (via Yahoo), CREW, IndexBox and Front Office Sports were read in full. CNBC and Forbes blocked direct access, so their figures rest on search excerpts.

### The claim
What have Donald Trump and the Trump Organization earned since January 2025 from Trump Media (stake value, any sales or dividends), foreign and domestic licensing and development deals, golf clubs and resorts including Mar-a-Lago, and branded merchandise? Cash and paper value are kept separate.

### Sub-claims

#### 1. The Trump Media stake is held in trust, unsold and paid nothing: confirmed
- **Who holds it.** Schedule 13D/A (22 Dec 2025): the Donald J. Trump Revocable Trust holds 114,750,000 DJT shares. Trump is settlor and sole beneficiary. Donald Trump Jr. is sole trustee with sole voting and investment power. The stake was 41.5% of 276,497,911 shares. The November 2026 TAE merger prospectus gives "approximately 41%". [4][5]
- **How the stake was diluted.** The April 2025 resale prospectus registered all 114.75M shares for possible resale, when they were about 52% of 220.6M shares. The May 2025 share issuance (55.9M PIPE shares) cut the percentage without any sale. [8][6]
- **No sales.** The only Forms 4 under Trump's own SEC number are from December 2024. The 13D/A reports no transactions in the prior 60 days and no pledge. [9][4]
- **No income.** The 2026 OGE filing lists the stake as "Over $50,000,000" with income "None (or less than $201)". [1]
- **No dividend or token.** The FY2025 10-K says there are no current plans to pay cash dividends. The Q3 2026 10-Q says the planned digital-token distribution to shareholders (record date 2 Feb 2026) was discontinued. [7][6]
- **No licence fee.** TMTG's name-and-likeness licence is royalty-free: $100 paid once, "and as of the date of this Annual Report… has not paid any other amounts" to Trump. [7]
- **Merger lock-up.** Under a 18 Dec 2025 support agreement, the trust must vote for the TAE merger (special meeting 14 Dec 2026) and is subject to transfer restrictions. [4][5]
