# C24: Trump Media's ETFs, the TAE fusion merger and its losses

**Verdict:** Mostly accurate · medium confidence  
**Type:** Check · Trump Media and markets

## The claim

> Does Trump Media sell Truth Social-branded ETFs, did it announce an all-stock merger worth about $6 billion with fusion company TAE Technologies, and how do its revenue and losses compare?

## Bottom line

Trump Media's Truth.Fi brand launched five Truth Social ETFs on 2025-12-30, including the three named in the claim.

## What holds up

- TMTG sells Truth Social American Security & Defense, American Next Frontiers and American Icons ETFs that are basically a non-woke S&P 500 (mostly accurate)
- TMTG announced a ~$6B all-stock merger with fusion company TAE Technologies.
- Fusion is not commercially viable yet and depends heavily on regulation and government approval.
- TMTG stock is way down.
- Revenues are a few million dollars against $712 million in losses (mostly accurate)

## What needs correcting

- **Claimed:** The ETFs are 'basically the S&P 500, but not woke'  
  **The record shows:** They are narrow thematic, screened baskets (defense, frontier tech, iconic brands) from a five-fund lineup.
- The $712M loss is the FY2025 figure and was mostly unrealized crypto markdowns. Q3 2026 showed a ~$410M paper profit, and the nine-month 2026 loss is ~$234M.
- The TAE merger is announced but not completed: the shareholder vote is 2026-12-14. Devin Nunes, the planned co-CEO, stepped down as TMTG CEO in April 2026.

## What is unproven

- The losses were funded by the president's most diehard supporters.

## In more detail

- TMTG announced an all-stock merger with fusion developer TAE Technologies valued at more than $6B on 2025-12-18.
- FY2025 revenue was about $3.7M against a net loss of $712.3M, and DJT stock is near multi-year lows.
- Several details are off: the ETFs are thematic baskets, not an 'S&P 500 but not woke'; the $712M loss was mostly unrealized crypto markdowns and dates from 2025 (Q3 2026 showed a $410M paper profit); and the TAE deal is still pending, with a vote set for 2026-12-14.

## Full report

**Verdict: mostly-accurate** (confidence: medium-high)

### The claim
- TMTG sells "non-woke" index funds: the Truth Social American Security & Defense ETF, the Truth Social American Next Frontiers ETF and the Truth Social American Icons ETF. They are described as "basically the S&P 500, but not woke."
- TMTG announced a merger with TAE Technologies, a fusion-energy company, in a "$6 billion stock deal". Fusion depends heavily on regulation going the right way and is not commercially viable yet.
- TMTG's stock is "way down." The company has revenue of "a few million dollars" against "$712 million in losses."

### Evidence by sub-claim

#### 1. Truth Social-branded ETFs: confirmed, with one wrong characterization
- Truth.Fi, Trump Media's financial brand, launched five ETFs on NYSE Arca on 2025-12-30. Three of them are the funds named in the claim: Truth Social American Security & Defense (TSSD), Truth Social American Next Frontiers (TSNF; some outlets print TSFN) and Truth Social American Icons (TSIC). [1][2][3][4]
- How the funds are run: Yorkville America Equities is the adviser, Tuttle Capital Management is the subadviser, and MarketVector administers the indexes. The funds use "anti-DEI" / America-first screening, which one report says comes from the 1792 Exchange corporate-policy criteria. [2][3][4]
- Correction: these are not "the S&P 500 but not woke." Each fund is a narrow thematic basket (defense, frontier technology, iconic American brands). Commentators noted that TSSD puts about 45% of its holdings in IT, and that the funds overlap with one another. Early assets were small, about $46M combined. [1][4]
- Fair context: TMTG licenses the brand, and the funds are run by Yorkville. TMTG does not itself manage them.

#### 2. TAE Technologies merger: confirmed
- The deal was announced 2025-12-18. It is an all-stock merger valued at more than $6B. Each company's shareholders would own about 50% of the combined company. Devin Nunes and TAE CEO Michl Binderbauer were named as planned co-CEOs. TMTG agreed to give TAE up to $200M in cash at signing, plus $100M more on the initial SEC filing. [5][6][7][8]
- TAE is backed by Alphabet. The combined company said it planned to site and begin building a 50 MWe utility-scale fusion plant in 2026, subject to approvals. Later filings say the deal does not obligate the company to build one. [8][9][10]
- The point that fusion is reliant on regulation is widely made. Former Bush ethics lawyer Richard Painter, quoted via the Washington Post, called it "a huge conflict of interest" because fusion commercialization would depend on regulatory decisions and government support. Axios noted the deal pushes the president's business interests into the heavily regulated nuclear sector. Reports also said Binderbauer had met DOE officials to urge federal funding days before the announcement. [6][9]
- "Not commercially viable yet": no fusion company produces commercial power. This is consistent with all the coverage.
- Status as of 2026-11-14: the deal has not closed. Nunes stepped down as CEO on 2026-04-21 and was replaced by interim CEO Kevin McGurn, so the co-CEO plan has changed. The SEC declared the S-4 effective on 2026-11-05. The shareholder vote is set for 2026-12-14, and the outside date is 2026-12-18. The originally planned Truth Social spin-off was dropped. The target close slipped from "mid-2026" to year-end 2026. [11][12][13][14]

#### 3. Stock way down: confirmed
- Axios reported DJT had lost about 70% of its value in 2025 before the TAE news, which made shares jump. In late October 2026 DJT traded around $5.8 to $6.4, near its 52-week low (52-week high about $17). [6][15]

#### 4. Revenue of a few million against $712M in losses: confirmed for FY2025
- The FY2025 10-K was filed 2026-02-27. It reports revenue of about $3.7M and a net loss of $712.3M. Some outlets print $711.2M or $712.1M. [16][17][18]
- Context often omitted: most of the loss was non-cash, unrealized markdowns on digital assets ($403.2M on digital assets and $178.8M on digital-asset securities). Operating cash flow was +$14.8M. [16][18]
- The claim does not give a period for the figure. It is the 2025 full-year number. In Q3 2026, reported 2026-11-09, TMTG posted net income of about $409.7M on $3.0M revenue, driven by crypto gains. The nine-month 2026 net loss was about $234.2M. If stated in late 2026, "$712M in losses" refers to 2025 and is not the current run-rate. [19][20]
- "Funded by the president's most diehard supporters": this is a characterization. Retail investors are prominent in DJT, but much of the treasury money came from a roughly $2.4B institutional raise (see C19). Unverified as stated.

### Corrections
- The company's name is TAE Technologies (sometimes misrendered as "Tay Technologies Inc").
- The ETFs are thematic, screened baskets, not an S&P 500 clone.
- The $712M loss is FY2025 and is mostly unrealized crypto markdowns. Q3 2026 showed a $410M paper profit.
- The TAE merger has been announced but not completed. The vote is 2026-12-14. Nunes is no longer CEO.

### Denials and other side
- White House press secretary Karoline Leavitt said neither the President nor his family have ever engaged, or will ever engage, in conflicts of interest. [9]
- Trump's TMTG stake is held in a revocable trust managed by Donald Trump Jr., who sits on the TMTG board. [6]
