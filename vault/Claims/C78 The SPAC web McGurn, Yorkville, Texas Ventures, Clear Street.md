# C78: The SPAC web: McGurn, Yorkville, Texas Ventures, Clear Street

**Verdict:** Mostly accurate · medium confidence  
**Origin:** Connection hunt

## Bottom line

The SPAC web is real and larger than the brief: Yorkville controls four blank-cheque vehicles (MCGA, Texas Ventures III, Blue Water III, Yorkville International), McGurn is CEO of at least three of them while serving as TMTG interim CEO, and Trump Organization EVPs Glick and Garten sit on Texas Ventures III's board, which is merging with PlusAI at $800M. Two parts of the brief are off: McGurn resigned from the Trump sons' New America SPAC on Aug 5, 2026 (Dominari's Kyle Wool now runs it), and the Dominari/D. Boral underwriting cluster is separate from the Yorkville cluster, joined only through McGurn.

## What holds up

- Kevin McGurn has run or sat on the board of at least six blank-cheque vehicles since 2025, four of them sponsored by Yorkville, while also advising and then running Trump Media.
- Texas Ventures Acquisition III was taken over by a Yorkville sponsor, and Trump Organization executives sit on its board.
- Texas Ventures III's target: PlusAI autonomous-trucking software, at an $800M pre-money valuation, with Yorkville financing.
- Blue Water Acquisition Corp III, a $10B Citgo bidder, was taken over by Yorkville with TMTG's CEO and general counsel on its board (mostly accurate)
- Yorkville International Capital, a McGurn-led SPAC aimed at Venezuela, drew a Senate Finance inquiry.
- Yorkville Acquisition Corp (MCGA), the vehicle for the Trump Media CRO treasury, has $181M in trust and no deal after the termination (mostly accurate)
- McGurn left the Trump sons' New America Acquisition I and Dominari's Kyle Wool took over. Ex-White House ethics lawyer Stefan Passantino joined the board.
- Founder-share economics: the Trump sons hold 5M NWAX founder shares worth about $50M on paper, and Yorkville bought SPAC founder blocks for well under $1 a share (mostly accurate)
- D. Boral Capital and Dominari are the recurring underwriters for the Trump-sons/McGurn vehicles, and D. Boral runs its own SPAC with McGurn on the board.
- Clear Street, co-lead agent on Trump Media's bitcoin raise and a leading crypto-treasury underwriter, launched a $1B IPO and then postponed it.
- Cohen & Company Capital Markets and Troy Rillo/Yorkville Securities link the TMTG bitcoin raise to the Yorkville SPACs.
- SEC review status of the Truth Social crypto ETFs: no approval found; the filings were repeatedly delayed and then refiled (mostly accurate)

## What needs correcting

- Trump Organization and Trump Media executives sit on SPAC boards controlled by Trump Media's own largest financier (partly accurate)
- Brief says McGurn runs New America Acquisition I while TMTG interim CEO: he resigned as its CEO and director effective Aug 5, 2026, and Kyle Wool, who became chairman Feb 6, 2026, is now CEO.
- **Brief said:** McGurn runs Texas Ventures III: Siegelman/NewsTRACS say so  
  **Record shows:** The Sept 2026 PlusAI release names Troy Rillo as CEO, so this is unverified.
- **Claim:** Brief lists D  
  **Record:** Boral, Dominari and Yorkville as recurring sponsors/underwriters together: Dominari/D. Boral underwrote NWAX and D. Boral's own SPAC, while the Yorkville SPACs used Cohen & Company (YICC) and no Dominari/D. Boral role was found in them.
- C62 lists Texas Ventures III's Trump Org directors as single-source: the FY2025 10-K names Lawrence Glick and Alan Garten, so this is documented. Nunes's directorship remains single-source.
- Clear Street's IPO was not completed: it was cut from about $1.05B to about $351-364M and postponed on Feb 12, 2026.

## In more detail

- Clear Street's tie is limited to the TMTG raise, and its IPO was postponed in Feb 2026.
- No Truth Social crypto ETF approval was found.
- The governance overlaps are documented; no source shows a transaction benefiting the Trump family beyond the sons' NWAX founder shares.
