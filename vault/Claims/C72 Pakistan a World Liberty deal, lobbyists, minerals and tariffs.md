# C72: Pakistan: a World Liberty deal, lobbyists, minerals and tariffs

**Verdict:** Mostly accurate · medium confidence  
**Type:** Connection check · Foreign deals

## The claim

> Did Pakistan hire Trump-connected lobbyists and sign a letter of intent with World Liberty Financial in the weeks before it obtained a tariff deal and a minerals agreement?

## Bottom line

Every dated step in the claim is documented: the Seiden/Javelin/Checkmate hires, the 26 Apr 2025 WLF letter of intent (with Munir in attendance), the ceasefire, Munir's lunch, the Nobel nomination, the 19% tariff and oil deal, the USSM $500M minerals MoU, Bin Saqib's regulator admitting Binance and HTX, the 14 Jan 2026 USD1 MoU and the Sep 2026 Powerus order.

## What holds up

- Timeline anchor: Seiden Law was hired before the Pahalgam attack; Javelin registered and WLF signed its letter of intent within days after it, with Munir in the room.
- Seiden Law's Pakistan mandate was explicitly about minerals, and it was passed to Trump's former bodyguard's firm.
- Javelin's filings put Trump's former White House physician, Rep. Ronny Jackson, in the minerals channel during the India-Pakistan conflict (mostly accurate)
- During Operation Sindoor, Pakistan's embassy lobbyists pitched a minerals-and-trade partnership to US officials.
- Ceasefire -> Nobel nomination -> Munir lunch: Pakistan credited Trump, India did not.
- Trade deal, 'massive oil reserves', 19% tariff and a first US crude cargo (Jul 2025).
- Minerals chain: USSM-FWO $500M MoU, the Oval Office mineral box, the first shipment, the Pasni pitch and EXIM's Reko Diq loan.
- Bin Saqib's regulator opened Pakistan to Binance and Justin Sun's HTX (Dec 2025).
- The 14 Jan 2026 USD1 MoU was signed by Zach Witkoff with the finance minister, witnessed by Sharif and Munir.
- Powerus, the Trump sons' drone company, got a Pakistan MoD order the day after meeting Munir (Sep 2026).

## What needs correcting

- Checkmate was paid via a Dubai entity (Greystone) for Pakistan-related government-relations work (partly accurate)
- Pakistan's 2026 role as US-Iran broker is tied by reporters to the WLF courtship (partly accurate)
- Al Jazeera's '$500m for Trump' figure is a Pakistan payment (false)
- The claim implies all three lobbying hires followed Pahalgam; Seiden Law signed on 2025-04-08, before the 2025-04-22 attack. Only Javelin's registration (24-25 Apr) and the WLF letter of intent (26 Apr) came days after it.
- Javelin was Seiden's subcontractor at about $50K/month, not a direct Pakistan contract; Checkmate's counterparty is Dubai-based Greystone, not the Pakistani government.
- The Powerus 'order' is a limited procurement order of undisclosed value plus a non-binding MoU; at signing Powerus had not yet completed its merger with the Trump-son-backed Aureus Greenway (closed ~2026-10-01).
- Al Jazeera's '$500m for Trump' refers to WLF token-sale revenue, not to any Pakistani payment or to the $500M USSM MoU.
- Some coverage calls Ronny Jackson a senator; he is a US Representative (R-TX) and Trump's former White House physician.
- Checkmate/Greystone: the two-year, ~$1.2M defence-and-minerals contract is Ervin Graves Strategy Group's (effective 2026-05-01), not Checkmate's; Checkmate's filed engagement is a $100K Phase I with a $200K Phase II option for general government-relations work.

## In more detail

- The second layer adds Rep. Ronny Jackson (via Javelin filings, single-source), a minerals-focused Seiden mandate, and Squire Patton Boggs' 14 May 2025 minerals pitch.
- What is not substantiated is causation: no source shows the lobbyists arranged the WLF deal, the lunch, the tariff or the Powerus order.
- Seiden was hired before, not after, the Pahalgam attack.

## Sub-claims

### 1. Timeline anchor: Seiden Law was hired before the Pahalgam attack; Javelin registered and WLF signed its letter of intent within days after it, with Munir in the room.

**Confirmed** · Evidence: Documented

- Seiden Law (FARA 7570) signed with Pakistan on 2025-04-08, two weeks BEFORE the 2025-04-22 Pahalgam attack.
- Javelin Advisors (Keith Schiller, George Sorial) signed a consulting agreement dated 2025-04-24 and registered under FARA (No. 7579) on 2025-04-25, as Seiden's subcontractor.
- On 2025-04-26 Zach Witkoff, Zak Folkman and Chase Herro signed the WLF letter of intent with the Pakistan Crypto Council in Islamabad.
- Arab News and Dawn report that the delegation also met PM Shehbaz Sharif, army chief Gen. Asim Munir and Deputy PM Ishaq Dar.
- Business Today, citing Dawn, says Munir personally attended the closed-door session (secondary).
- The sequence is documented.
- That the hires and the letter of intent were coordinated is not reported anywhere (see 'Not connected').
- Dates: 2025-04-08; 2025-04-22; 2025-04-24; 2025-04-25; 2025-04-26
- Sources: [2](https://nukta.com/pakistans-multi-million-dollar-lobbying-push-to-reset-its-image-in-the-us) [3](https://efile.fara.gov/docs/7579-Registration-Statement-20250425-1.pdf) [5](https://www.dawn.com/news/1906732) [6](https://www.arabnews.pk/node/2598563) [7](https://www.businesstoday.in/india/story/behind-donald-trumps-ceasefire-claim-a-family-crypto-deal-with-general-asim-munir-in-the-room-476657-2025-05-17) [13](https://fortune.com/2025/05/10/india-pakistan-hostilities-us-ceasefire-kashmir-war-trump/)

### 2. Seiden Law's Pakistan mandate was explicitly about minerals, and it was passed to Trump's former bodyguard's firm.

**Confirmed** · Evidence: Documented (Global Witness analysis of FARA filings; Nukta; Dawn)

- Global Witness reports that the Seiden contract, $200K/month, was to 'promote the exploration and sale of' rare earths and other critical minerals for Pakistan, and that Seiden outsourced it to Javelin.
- It names Robert Seiden and George Sorial as Trump-circle figures representing Pakistan.
- Javelin's share was $50K/month (Politico, Dawn).
- An unofficial FARA-compilation site says Javelin's engagement ended on 2025-10-08 after four payments totalling $200K. Treat that termination detail as single-source.
- No 2026 renewal of either contract was found.
- Audit note: the monthly figure varies by source.
- Nukta and Global Witness give $200K/month for six months; CAPSS (an Indian think tank) lists $125K/month.
- The FARA PDFs could not be read directly to settle it.
- Amounts: $200K/month; $50K/month; $200K; $125K/month
- Dates: 2025-10-08
- Sources: [1](https://www.globalwitness.org/en/campaigns/transition-minerals/revealed-trump-linked-firms-cash-in-on-mineral-lobbying-deals-as-us-cuts-aid) [2](https://nukta.com/pakistans-multi-million-dollar-lobbying-push-to-reset-its-image-in-the-us) [4](https://www.dawn.com/news/1907874) [49](https://ithildin.pages.dev/dossiers/javelin-advisors-llc) [53](https://capssindia.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/CAPSS_InFocus_MS_20_01_26.pdf)

### 3. Javelin's filings put Trump's former White House physician, Rep. Ronny Jackson, in the minerals channel during the India-Pakistan conflict.

**Mostly accurate** · Evidence: Single-source

- The Economic Times reported this from FARA filings by Javelin Advisors and Conscience Point Consulting (another Seiden subcontractor), and Business Today and others relayed it.
- The underlying filings could not be read directly.
- Reported details: an in-person meeting between Jackson and Pakistani officials on 2025-05-01 on the rare-earths deal; a 2025-05-28 call followed by an email with a draft MoU, which Javelin's filing describes as 'Delivery of Proposed MoU'; and Javelin contacting Jackson's office during the 7-10 May conflict (ThePrint).
- Jackson (R-TX) was Trump's White House physician.
- No comment from Jackson was found.
- An unofficial site claims a $4,000 contribution to Jackson on 2025-10-28 tied to Javelin.
- That is unverified against FEC records and is not relied on.
- Audit (Nov 2026): no response from Jackson or Javelin and no non-Indian outlet independently confirming the filing entries was found, so the grade stays single-source.
- Amounts: $4,000
- Dates: 2025-05-01; 2025-05-28; 10 May; 2025-10-28; Nov 2026
- Sources: [8](https://www.businesstoday.in/world/us/story/ronny-jackson-former-white-house-doctor-identified-as-key-link-in-us-pakistans-500-million-mining-deal-509756-2026-01-07) [9](https://www.bizzbuzz.news/international/trump-ally-ronny-jackson-linked-to-pakistans-500-million-mining-deal-during-op-sindoor-1383465) [10](https://theprint.in/diplomacy/pakistans-lobbying-blitz-in-us-amid-op-sindoor-50-emails-worries-about-indias-resuming-strikes/2821399)

### 4. During Operation Sindoor, Pakistan's embassy lobbyists pitched a minerals-and-trade partnership to US officials.

**Confirmed** · Evidence: Documented (FARA filings as reported by ThePrint, IANS and others, Jan 2026)

- Squire Patton Boggs, the embassy's long-standing registrant, sent 50+ emails to Congress and officials between 7 and 10 May 2025, stressing fears that India would resume strikes.
- On 2025-05-14, four days after the ceasefire, it emailed a US diplomat about a renewed partnership 'centred on trade, especially mines and minerals.'
- Separately, the embassy hired Ervin Graves Strategy Group in Oct 2025 ($25K/month, three months), partly on keeping Pakistan off the FATF grey list (Business Today), then signed a two-year, ~$1.2M contract with it effective 2026-05-01 ($50K/month) that seeks resumed US-Pakistan defence dialogue and has a section on minerals cooperation (AFP via Arab News/Malay Mail, Dawn).
- Most of this coverage comes from Indian outlets with an adversarial framing, but it rests on public filings.
- Amounts: $25K/month; ~$1.2M; $50K/month
- Dates: Jan 2026; 10 May 2025; 2025-05-14; Oct 2025; 2026-05-01

### 6. Checkmate was paid via a Dubai entity (Greystone) for Pakistan-related government-relations work.

**Partly accurate**

- Evidence: the contract is documented; its defence-and-minerals scope is not, and the paying entity's ownership is not established.
- Checkmate Government Relations (FARA No. 7534; Ches McDowell, a Trump Jr. associate per C64) contracts not with Pakistan's government but with Dubai-based Greystone Investment & Management LLC, from 2025-05-15, with $100K paid on 2025-05-14 for a 30-day Phase I and a $200K Phase II option (O'Dwyer's, FARA exhibits).
- Its filings describe general government-relations advice and outreach to US officials.
- Audit correction: the 'two-year, $1.2M agreement covering defence cooperation and minerals' ('support efforts to identify opportunities for cooperation') that Daily Times/AFP describe is Pakistan's embassy contract with Ervin Graves Strategy Group, effective 2026-05-01 at $50K/month for 24 months, not Checkmate's.
- No coverage found names Greystone's owners or its tie to the Pakistani state.
- That is a gap, not a finding.
- Amounts: $100K; $200K; $1.2M; $50K/month
- Dates: 2025-05-15; 2025-05-14; 2026-05-01
- Sources: [18](https://www.odwyerpr.com/story/public/23166/2025-06-18/checkmate-checks-for-pakistan.html) [2](https://nukta.com/pakistans-multi-million-dollar-lobbying-push-to-reset-its-image-in-the-us) [50](https://www.arabnews.pk/node/2651238/pakistan) [19](https://dailytimes.com.pk/1524579/defence-and-minerals-inside-pakistans-lobbying-push-in-washington/)

## Denials and context

- India denies any US mediation in the May 2025 ceasefire; Pakistan credits Trump.
- A senior Trump administration official said the Pasni port proposal was not discussed; a Pakistani military official said Munir has no official advisers.
- Pakistani officials stress the Jan 2026 USD1 MoU is non-binding and does not authorise adoption; Bin Saqib says the Binance/HTX NOCs are not blanket approvals.
- Powerus spokesperson: the president has no involvement in Powerus; the Trump sons describe themselves as passive investors (C51).
- WLF says Bin Saqib's adviser role was unpaid and ended when he joined government (C64).
- Mother Jones notes it is 'not clear' the WLF relationship explains Pakistan's Iran-broker role.
  - Pakistan's pivot also has strategic drivers (Iran access, China hedging, minerals) that cut against a purely transactional reading.
- FARA registration is a disclosure regime; nothing here shows illegal conduct.
  - No DOJ action on these filings was found.

- **Correction:** the two-year, ~$1.2M defence-and-minerals contract that Daily Times/AFP describe is the embassy's contract with **Ervin Graves Strategy Group** (effective 2026-05-01, $50K/month). It is not Checkmate's.

### Corrections
- **Seiden's timing:** Seiden predates Pahalgam. Only Javelin's registration and the WLF letter of intent fall days after the attack.
- **Javelin and Checkmate:** Javelin was a subcontractor. Checkmate's counterparty is Greystone, not the Pakistani government.
- **Powerus:** the order was a limited procurement order of undisclosed value. The merger had not yet closed when it was placed.
- **Ronny Jackson:** he is a Representative, not a Senator.
- **"$500m":** there are three separate $500M figures, and they must not be conflated.

### Denials and context
- **India:** denies that the US mediated the ceasefire.
- **Pasni:** US and Pakistani officials deny the port proposal.
- **Pakistani officials:** stress that the MoUs and NOCs are non-binding or preliminary.
- **Powerus and the Trump sons:** the sons say they are passive investors, and Powerus says the president is not involved.
- **WLF:** says Bin Saqib was unpaid.
- **Strategic drivers:** Pakistan also has non-transactional reasons for its pivot (Iran access, hedging against China, minerals).
- **Legal status:** FARA disclosures are not findings of wrongdoing, and we found no DOJ action.
