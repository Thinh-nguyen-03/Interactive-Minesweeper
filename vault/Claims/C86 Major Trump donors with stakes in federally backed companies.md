# C86: Major Trump donors with stakes in federally backed companies

**Verdict:** Partly accurate · medium confidence  
**Type:** Connection check · Defence, minerals and contracts

## The claim

> Did major Trump donors such as John Paulson hold stakes in companies that received federal loans, equity stakes or price floors?

## Bottom line

Paulson is the clearest case. He is a documented Trump fundraiser and the largest holder of Perpetua, which won a $2.9B EXIM loan approval (not yet closed).

## What holds up

- John Paulson (Trump fundraiser) is Perpetua Resources' largest shareholder, and Perpetua won a $2.9B EXIM loan approval in 2026.
- Paulson profited from the October 2025 U.S. stake in Trilogy Metals and later sold out.
- Todd Boehly, a Trump donor, leads a bid for Lukoil's foreign assets in which a federal agency (DFC) would take equity (mostly accurate)
- Jeff Yass, a top MAGA Inc. donor, holds ByteDance and is in the federally approved TikTok USDS joint venture (mostly accurate)
- Congress has asked whether Trump donors hold equity in companies receiving federal equity stakes.

## What needs correcting

- Schwarzman, Griffin and Steven A. Cohen, as Trump allies, bought discounted shares in the Cantor-placed USA Rare Earth PIPE that sat alongside the Commerce stake (partly accurate)
- Kelcy Warren (Energy Transfer) got federal backing after giving to Trump (partly accurate)
- The claim calls Griffin a Trump ally. He gave $1M to the inauguration but did not endorse Trump in 2024, and in 2026 he publicly accused the administration of enriching officials' families.
- We found no Trump or MAGA Inc. donation from Steven A. Cohen. His documented Trump-family link is Point72's 4% Alt5 Sigma stake.
- Perpetua's federal backing did not begin under Trump. The record of decision, the first EXIM letter of interest ($1.8B, April 2024) and most of the DoD awards came under Biden.
- Kelcy Warren and Harold Hamm received regulatory decisions (an LNG deadline extension, BLM permits), not federal equity stakes, loans or price floors. Lake Charles LNG was suspended in December 2025.
- Trilogy's biggest paper winner was Electrum (~18.5%), not Paulson (~8.7%).

## What is unproven

- Paulson's other big 2025 bet, Donlin Gold (Alaska), received federal backing.
- Harold Hamm (Continental Resources) got federal stakes, loans or price floors.

## In more detail

- He also gained an estimated $60-70M on paper from the Trilogy stake before selling out in Q4 2025.
- Context: much of Perpetua's federal support began under Biden.
- Yass ($16M to MAGA Inc.) and Boehly ($1M+) are documented donors whose holdings sit in a federally approved TikTok venture and a DFC-backed Lukoil bid.
- That bid is pending and unsigned. The rest is weaker.
- The Schwarzman/Griffin/Cohen PIPE purchase rests on one Senate letter, Griffin is a public critic, and no Cohen donation to Trump was found.
- Warren and Hamm got regulatory help, not stakes, loans or price floors.
- No realised donor windfall is documented apart from Paulson's undisclosed Trilogy sale.

### 1. John Paulson (Trump fundraiser) is Perpetua Resources' largest shareholder, and Perpetua won a $2.9B EXIM loan approval in 2026.

**Confirmed** · Evidence: Documented

- This extends C77. Paulson hosted Trump's April 2024 Palm Beach fundraiser ($50.5M raised) and was considered for Treasury Secretary.
- Paulson & Co. bought $100M of Perpetua's ~$425M June 2025 raise at $13.20 a share.
- Bloomberg Government says he has put about $185M into the company in total.
- 13F trackers put Paulson & Co. at 32.3M shares, about 25-26% of Perpetua, in Q1 2026, the largest holder by a wide margin (audit re-check; aggregator figures).
- Under Trump: Stibnite became a FAST-41 priority project in April 2025, received its final Army Corps permit in May 2025, and won EXIM board approval for $2.9B in May 2026 (EXIM had notified Congress of $2.7B in March 2026).
- The loan is still subject to definitive documents and conditions precedent (expected in H2 2026 per the May 2026 announcement), and no final close was found through Nov 2026.
- Context that cuts the other way: the Forest Service record of decision (2025-01-03), the first ~$1.8B EXIM letter of interest (April 2024) and most of the ~$80M in DoD awards all came under Biden.
- Paper gain: our own arithmetic, not a sourced figure.
- The roughly 7.58M June 2025 placement shares were worth about $203M at the 2026-05-21 close of US$26.72, a paper gain of about $100M on that tranche alone.
- No Paulson statement on a conflict was found.
- Amounts: $50.5M; $100M; ~$425M; $13.20; about $185M
- Dates: April 2024; June 2025; April 2025; May 2025; May 2026

### 2. Paulson profited from the October 2025 U.S. stake in Trilogy Metals and later sold out.

**Confirmed** · Evidence: Documented

- Paulson & Co. held about 8.7% of Trilogy (fourth-largest holder).
- On 2025-10-06 the Department of War signed a binding letter of intent to pay $35.6M in total for about 10% of Trilogy, in two parallel halves: $17.8M directly to Trilogy for 8,215,570 units at $2.17 (each with three-quarters of a warrant), and $17.8M to South32 for 8,215,570 existing Trilogy shares plus a call option on 6,161,678 more; all $35.6M is to be reinvested in Ambler Metals.
- The warrants and option could take the stake toward 17.5%.
- The administration also approved the Ambler Road.
- The stock rose more than 200%.
- Popular Information and Dermot Cole estimated Paulson's paper gain at $60-70M. His 13F shows he sold all 14.3M shares between 2025-10-01 and 2025-12-31.
- The exact dates and prices are not disclosed, so the realised gain is unknown.
- Paulson has not explained the sale.
- Context: the largest holder, Thomas Kaplan's Electrum (~18.5%, worth >$136M in March 2026), gained more on paper.
- We found no reporting that describes Kaplan as a Trump donor, and his giving was not checked.
- Amounts: about 8.7%; $35.6M; about 10%; $17.8M; $2.17
- Dates: 2025-10-06; 2025-10-01; 2025-12-31; March 2026
- Sources: [1](https://popular.info/p/trump-approves-mining-road-delivers) [2](https://www.dermotcole.com/reportingfromalaska/2025/10/7/billionaire-trump-donor-gains-70-million-in-ambler-windfall) [3](https://www.mining.com/web/billionaire-paulson-sells-stake-in-alaska-focused-trilogy-metals/) [4](https://alaskabeacon.com/2026/03/09/after-spending-big-on-donlin-billionaire-sells-stake-in-company-with-ambler-prospects/) [5](https://www.kyuk.org/economy/2026-03-11/after-spending-big-on-donlin-billionaire-sells-stake-in-company-with-ambler-prospects) [6](https://www.northernminer.com/news/u-s-takes-10-trilogy-stake-in-alaska-push/1003894826/) <https://www.mining-technology.com/news/us-government-buy-stake-trilogy-metals/> <https://www.fool.com/investing/2026/02/19/why-trilogy-metals-stock-is-crashing-this-week/>

## Denials and context

- Cantor says it had no role in USA Rare Earth's government negotiations or financing.
- Paulson has not explained his Trilogy sale.
  - An economist quoted by the Alaska Beacon said the sale does not necessarily signal anything.
- In 2024 Yass's spokesperson said he had never donated to Trump.
  - In 2025 he gave $16M to MAGA Inc. Susquehanna says it had no economic interest in Trump Media and held DWAC only as a market maker.
- Witkoff's spokesperson denies any conflict in the Lukoil talks (C76).
  - No Boehly or DFC statement was found.
- Energy Transfer called the Biden-era DOE denial 'political'.
  - Continental did not respond to requests for comment.
- A documented donation plus a federal action is not evidence of a quid pro quo.
  - No source shows that any donation caused any federal decision.
