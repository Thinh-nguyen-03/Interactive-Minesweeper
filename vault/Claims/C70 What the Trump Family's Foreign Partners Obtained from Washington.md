# C70: What the Trump Family's Foreign Partners Obtained from Washington

**Verdict:** Mostly accurate · medium confidence  
**Type:** Connection check · Foreign Deals

## The Claim

> What did the Trump family's foreign business partners, or their countries, obtain from the US government in the same period?

## Bottom Line

For most named partners, a family deal and a favourable US decision in the same period are both documented:

- Damac (about $12.5M in 2025 fees, plus a $20B data-centre pledge announced with Trump and a permitting order)
- Dar Global and Qatari Diar (Gulf licences, alongside F-35s, chips, the 123 agreement, arms sales and the jet)
- Kinh Bac (a $5M fee, then a tariff cut from 46% to 20%)
- The UAE network (WLF and USD1, then chip licences)
- Binance (USD1 venue, then SEC dismissal and pardon)
- Kazakhstan (sons' tungsten stake, then $1.6B in EXIM/DFC letters)
- Pakistan, the clearest two-way case, where WLF affiliates signed state MoUs and the Pakistani Ministry of Defence ordered drones from Trump-sons-backed Powerus

## What Holds Up

- Damac / Hussain Sajwani (UAE): licence fees to the Trump Organization alongside a $20B US data-centre pledge and a data-centre permitting order (mostly accurate)
- Dar Global / Dar Al Arkan (Saudi Arabia): Trump licensing deals running alongside major US-Saudi decisions.
- Qatari Diar / QIA (Qatar): Trump golf deal with a sovereign-owned developer, then the jet and arms approvals.
- Kinh Bac City / Dang Thanh Tam (Vietnam): $1.5B Trump golf project fast-tracked during tariff talks (mostly accurate)
- Aryam / MGX / G42 (UAE, Tahnoon network): money into WLF and USD1, then the US chip approvals.
- Binance / Changpeng Zhao: USD1 settlement through Binance, then the SEC dismissal, the pardon and the monitor talks.
- Pakistan: WLF deals with the Pakistani state and an order for a Trump-sons-backed drone maker, alongside tariff and diplomatic gains.
- Kazakhstan: Trump sons' stake in the Cove Kaz tungsten venture, then US financing and diplomacy (mostly accurate)

## What Needs Correcting

- Hary Tanoesoedibjo / MNC (Indonesia): Trump resort partner, with the Indonesian tariff deal and Prabowo's hot-mic request to meet Eric (partly accurate)
- Indian licensees (Tribeca/Kalpesh Mehta; Reliance 4IR Realty): Trump licence fees alongside US decisions on India and on Reliance (partly accurate)
- Serbia: Kushner/Trump-branded Belgrade hotel and US sanctions treatment of Serbia's Russian-owned oil company NIS (partly accurate)
- Azerbaijan: a Trump-family deal paired with US decisions (the TRIPP corridor, the strategic partnership) (partly accurate)
- Japan (Metaplanet / SoftBank): Eric Trump's Metaplanet role alongside the US-Japan $550B deal and SoftBank's projects (partly accurate)
- The claim's 'Bilt' has no documented Trump-family connection.
- Damac's 2025 fee total is reported as $12.5M (The National, CREW) or $11.3M (CNBC); the two outlets disagree.
- El Salvador and Azerbaijan have documented US policy actions, but no second-term Trump-family business deal was found.
- The Indonesian government halted, rather than fast-tracked, the MNC Lido project (Feb 2025), and named a suspect in Sept 2025.
- The NIS sanctions waiver for Serbia was refused in Oct 2025 while the Kushner project was still live.

## What Is Unproven

- Albania: Kushner's Sazan/Zvernec projects and any US decision in Albania's favour.
- El Salvador: a Trump-family business deal paired with US favours (CECOT deportations, bitcoin).
- 'Bilt' as a foreign partner of the Trump family.

## In More Detail

- Indonesia, India's tariffs and Serbia's NIS waivers cut against a favour narrative.
- No family deal was found for El Salvador, Azerbaijan, Albania-side US concessions, Japan/SoftBank or 'Bilt'.
- No case shows causation, and all parties deny a link.

### 1. Damac / Hussain Sajwani (UAE): licence fees to the Trump Organization alongside a $20B US data-centre pledge and a data-centre permitting order.

**Mostly accurate** · Evidence: Documented

- Deal with the family: Damac built the Trump International Golf Club Dubai (first term) and still pays management/licence fees.
- Trump's 2026 disclosure (covering 2025) shows about $12.5M from Damac-linked entities per The National and CREW, or $11.3M per CNBC's analysis, the outlets disagree.
- The total includes two new $5M licence fees paid by Damac subsidiaries to new Trump entities, including DT Marks Al Raha Beach LLC, for Abu Dhabi projects that have not been announced (CREW; CNBC via Times Brasil).
- On the US side: on 7 Jan 2025 at Mar-a-Lago, President-elect Trump announced Sajwani's pledge of $20B or more for US data centres through Damac's Edgnex arm, in eight states, about 2GW over four years.
- Trump suggested expedited reviews.
- In July 2025 Trump signed the 'Accelerating Federal Permitting of Data Center Infrastructure' order, and CREW reports DT Marks Al Raha Beach LLC was formed in Delaware the same day.
- That date match is CREW's finding (single-source); it shows proximity, not causation.
- In Jan 2026 at Davos Sajwani said Damac had secured US land and power worth about $12B (about 1GW).
- I found no confirmed US site in operation.
- The permitting order is a general policy, not a Damac-specific approval.
- CNBC found no evidence any Damac payment influenced it, and the White House says there are no conflicts.
- Amounts: about $12.5M; $11.3M; $5M; $20B; about $12B
- Dates: 7 Jan 2025; July 2025; Jan 2026

### Key connections, with evidence grades

1. **Pakistan: state money into family-linked firms (documented).** Pakistan's government signed a USD1 payments MoU with SC Financial Technologies. SC Financial is the Delaware co-owner of the USD1 brand, and Steve Witkoff's August 2025 disclosure lists it as an asset. Zach Witkoff signed the MoU, and his delegation also met PM Sharif and Field Marshal Munir. Eight months later Pakistan's Ministry of Defence placed an initial drone order with Powerus, which the Trump sons back through American Ventures. Munir received the Powerus delegation. Both events followed the 19% tariff deal and Munir's White House lunch. WLF says it is not paid under the MoU, Don Jr.'s spokesperson denies any role in the Powerus deal, and the White House says there are no conflicts. Powerus had licensed its interceptor in India three months earlier.
2. **Damac: licence fees, a pledge and a permitting order (documented; date match single-source).** Damac-linked entities paid $11.3-12.5M in 2025, including $10M for Abu Dhabi projects that have not been announced. That came a year after Sajwani stood beside Trump to pledge $20B in US data centres. CREW reports that one of the new Trump licensing LLCs was formed the same day as the July 2025 permitting order. CNBC found no evidence that the payments influenced the order. The order is general policy, not a Damac-specific approval.
3. **Reliance: a $10M fee for a project that may not exist, then a sanctions licence (payment documented; pairing inferred).** A Reliance unit paid the Trump Organization a $10M development fee for a Mumbai licence that the WSJ could not tie to any project. In February 2026 the US issued Reliance a licence to buy Venezuelan oil directly. CREW draws the connection. No source shows causation, and Reliance declined to comment.
4. **Kinh Bac: the partner gains from tariff policy itself (documented; Forbes framing single-source).** Beyond the $5M licence fee and the fast-tracked approval (C59), Forbes reports that Dang Thanh Tam's industrial-park business grew on tariff-driven supply-chain shifts. The partner's interest in US-Vietnam tariff outcomes is therefore commercial and direct, not only through the golf licence.
5. **Sokolov / Tripp+ (documented).** The State Department named Konstantin Sokolov, a White House ballroom donor with Armenian telecom and mining holdings and a past running a Kremlin-adjacent investment firm (Reuters), to chair the $201M fund attached to the Trump-brokered South Caucasus corridor. This is the only Azerbaijan-region connection found, and it is a donor-to-appointment link, not a family business deal.

### Not connected in the coverage we found (our own cross-referencing)

- **Serbia NIS waivers and the Kushner Belgrade hotel.** Inferred; no source links them. The timing argues *against* a favour: the waiver was refused in October 2025 while the project was still alive.
- **Reliance's Venezuela licence and its $10M Trump fee.** Inferred by us; CREW frames it as "wins" without asserting causation.
- **Metaplanet (Eric Trump) and SoftBank or the US-Japan $550B deal.** Inferred and weak; no source links them.
- **Damac's July 2025 Trump LLC formation and the permitting order.** The date match is CREW's (single-source). That the order benefited Damac specifically is not shown.
- **Indonesia's tariff cut and MNC.** Inferred from the hot mic. The identity of "Hary" is unconfirmed, and Jakarta acted *against* the Trump project.
- **Pakistan's tariff, the Munir lunch, the WLF deals and Powerus.** Each element is documented. That they are linked is inference.
