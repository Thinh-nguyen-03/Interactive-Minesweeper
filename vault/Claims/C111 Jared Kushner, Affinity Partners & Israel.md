# C111: Jared Kushner, Affinity Partners & Israel

**Verdict:** Mostly accurate · medium confidence  
**Type:** Check · Foreign Deals

## The Claim

> What has Jared Kushner's fund invested in Israel, and how does that overlap with his diplomatic role?

## Bottom Line

The investments are documented in company-side reporting and CNN: Affinity Partners bought 15% of Shlomo Group's vehicle and credit arm (signed Sept 2023, closed 1 Feb 2024 at about $110M) and 4.95% of Phoenix in July 2024 (about NIS 466M), doubling to 9.9% on 15 Jan 2025 (NIS 932M in all), then sold 2.5% on 28 Jul 2026 for about NIS 1.05B ($343M), keeping a 7.4% stake worth about NIS 3.3B. Phoenix holds, per CNN, more than $456M in nine Israeli military suppliers (about $265M in Elbit); Affinity holds none directly.

## What Holds Up

- Affinity Partners bought 4.95% of Phoenix Holdings (now Phoenix Financial) in July 2024 for about NIS 466M (about $128.5M) and exercised an option for a second 4.95% in January 2025, five days before the inauguration.
- In late July 2026 Affinity sold about a quarter of its Phoenix stake for about NIS 1.05B (about $343M) and remains the largest shareholder.
- Affinity bought 15% of Shlomo Group's vehicle leasing, rental and credit business: signed September 2023 at about NIS 570M ($150M), closed 1 February 2024 at about $110M.
- Phoenix has invested more than $456M in nine companies that supply Israel's military, including about $265M in Elbit Systems.
- Kushner has been a central negotiator of the Gaza ceasefire, the hostage deal and regional diplomacy since 2025, with the title 'envoy of peace' and no pay.
- The CREW lawsuit challenges Kushner's volunteer envoy status and lack of disclosure.
- Dated overlap: Affinity's Israeli investments and Kushner's diplomatic role overlap in time (overlap only, no cause shown).
- Complaints and congressional letters: who wrote what, and the replies.

## What Needs Correcting

- These are Affinity's only documented investments in Israeli companies (partly accurate)
- Affinity's capital for these Israeli investments came from Gulf sovereign investors, and Gulf investors approved the Israeli investments (partly accurate)
- Kushner and his family business have other ties to Israel (loans, investments by Israeli institutions) (partly accurate)
- **Claimed:** The July 2026 sale was a return of 'more than five times its initial stock purchase' (coverage of CNN's story and the Garcia letter)  
  **The record shows:** The NIS 1.05B proceeds are about 4.5 times the cost of the quarter sold (about NIS 233M; sale at NIS 168.5 a share against NIS 37.50 paid, our arithmetic) and about 1.1 times the whole NIS 932M outlay. Calcalist's own multiple, for the whole position (remaining shares plus dividends), is 'roughly 4.5 times'. We could not find the basis of 'more than five times'; use about 4.5 times.
- **Claimed:** Affinity agreed to buy the Shlomo stake 'for $150 million' (CNN)  
  **The record shows:** $150M (NIS 570M) was the September 2023 signing price at a NIS 3.8B valuation; the deal closed on 1 Feb 2024 at about $110M at a NIS 2.7B valuation (Globes, Calcalist).
- Claimed (earlier check C15): CREW 'argues he is at least a special government employee'. The record shows (search excerpts of Bloomberg Law and NBC; complaint not read): the suit, filed 14 Oct 2026 (D.D.C., 26-cv-3580), argues that as chief negotiator he is an official who must file disclosures, and invokes the Appointments Clause; the 'special government employee' wording was not found in the coverage. C15's 'sued in Oct 2026' is right.
- **Claimed:** Sale 'in January' or dated 27 July (JNS item and some aggregators)  
  **The record shows:** The sale was on Tuesday 28 Jul 2026 (JNS of 29 Jul); January 2025 was the second purchase.
- Garcia's letter says Affinity is the largest shareholder in 'an Israeli company with more than $456 million invested in nine companies that supply Israel's military'. The record shows: CNN's count is of Phoenix's holdings in a partial set of filings, and Affinity's stake in Phoenix is 7.4% (9.9% before the sale), so Affinity's look-through share of that $456M is a fraction of it (our arithmetic: about 7-10%, about $34M-$45M if the holdings were proportionate; asserted by no source).
- Fee figure for context (C99): the Garcia-Wyden letter of 19 Mar 2026 estimates Affinity collected more than $60M of foreign fees in 2025, including $39M from Saudi Arabia; this is the Democrats' estimate under the fee terms, not a reported receipt.
- Phoenix stake after the second purchase: this check says 9.9% (2 x 4.95%); Calcalist (29 Jul 2026) says 9.83%. The difference is rounding or share-count timing; the 7.4% remaining stake is stated by both Calcalist and Globes. The 28 Jul sale was to three global institutions, the largest Fidelity Investments (about 1.6%, Calcalist), via Jefferies at NIS 168.5 a share (4% discount).

## What Is Unproven

- Allegation: Kushner's diplomacy advanced the Phoenix investment or the military-linked stakes, or he profited from the Gaza war.

## In More Detail

- His role in the Oct 2025 ceasefire, the Board of Peace and Iran talks is documented, as is his unpaid, undisclosed status and the CREW suit of 14 Oct 2026.
- The two overlap in time only: CNN found no indication that his diplomacy affected the investments, he and the White House deny any connection, and no authority has made a finding.
- Gulf funding of Affinity is documented, but no source traces the Israeli deals to Gulf money, and Gulf approval is reported once (Globes, for Shlomo).

### 2. In late July 2026 Affinity sold about a quarter of its Phoenix stake for about NIS 1.05B (about $343M) and remains the largest shareholder.

**Confirmed** · Evidence: Documented (Ynet/Calcalist, JNS citing Globes, CNN, Garcia letter)

- JNS (29 Jul 2026) says the sale took place 'on Tuesday', that is 28 Jul 2026: 2.5% of Phoenix (Calcalist: 2.45%) sold to US institutions through Jefferies at a 4% discount to the morning's price, cutting Affinity from 9.9% to 7.4%, a remaining stake worth about NIS 3.3B (about $1.06B-$1.08B).
- Ynet/Calcalist adds that the proceeds exceed Affinity's whole NIS 932M outlay, that about NIS 230M of dividends were also received, and that Phoenix's market value rose roughly fivefold since July 2024 (to about NIS 44B).

- The basis is not stated in the sources we read.

- Realised proceeds go to the fund; what share reaches Kushner personally as sole owner of the management company, after fund-level costs and investors' share, is not public.
- Those 'more than $1 billion' and NIS 3.6B gain figures include unrealised value; do not add them to cash.

### 3. Affinity bought 15% of Shlomo Group's vehicle leasing, rental and credit business: signed September 2023 at about NIS 570M ($150M), closed 1 February 2024 at about $110M.

- CNN's '$150 million' is the figure announced at signing, not the closing price.
- No sale of the Shlomo stake and no 2025-26 update was found.
- Amounts: 15%; 18%; $2 billion; about $740M; about $110M
