# C17: Trump merchandise and the T1 phone

**Verdict:** Mostly accurate (confidence: high)

## What the video said

> The Trump family sells NFTs, watches, golden shoes/sneakers, a Trump Bible, fragrances, and the Trump Mobile T1 phone, which was advertised as 'Made in America' until the website wording was changed; a buyer (Coffeezilla) says he was doxxed after ordering.

## Summary

Every product named exists and pays Trump royalties: NFTs (Dec 2022), watches, gold sneakers, the God Bless the USA Bible, Victory 47 fragrances and the Trump Mobile T1. The T1 site did drop its 'Made in the USA' banner in late June 2025. Coffeezilla's own address and email were exposed in a May 2026 Trump Mobile pre-order data leak. 'The family sells' glosses over the fact that these are mostly licensing deals run by third parties, and 'doxxed' describes a site vulnerability, not a targeted act.

## Corrections

- The transcript implies the family sells these goods directly. Most are licensing deals (CIC Ventures, NFT INT LLC, Trump Mobile's operators) that pay royalties to Trump, and Trump Mobile's site disclaims Trump Organization manufacture or sale.
- 'Doxxed' is a loose word: Coffeezilla's data was exposed in a site-wide pre-order vulnerability (May 2026), not published by anyone targeting him.
- The company never fully dropped the U.S. claim. It later said the first T1 units are 'assembled in the U.S.', which has not been independently verified.

## Denials and context

- Trump Mobile spokesperson Chris Walker (June 2025): 'T1 phones are proudly being made in America. Speculation to the contrary is simply inaccurate.'
- Trump Mobile confirmed the data exposure, attributed it to a third-party platform, and said it was evaluating whether to notify customers.
- Coffeezilla's estimate of about 30,000 real pre-orders (some outlets say 10,000), against roughly 590,000 claimed earlier, is his own inference from the leaked IDs.
- Coffeezilla himself says these consumer products matter less than the regulated industries the family is entering.

## Full report

**Verdict: mostly-accurate** (confidence: high)

### What the video says (transcript lines 565-567)
Coffeezilla lists the "NFT business, the watches, the shoes, the Trump Bible, the Trump fragrances" and "the T1 mobile phone, which I ordered and then got doxxed for, which was said to be made in America until they changed the website." He calls golden shoes "obviously stupid" and says he will focus instead on regulated industries.

### Findings by sub-claim

1. **NFTs: confirmed.** "Trump Digital Trading Cards" were announced on Truth Social on 15 Dec 2022 at $99 each. About 44,000-45,000 sold out within roughly a day and brought in about $4.45M. Trading volume fell about 99% by Jan 2023. The licensee was NFT INT LLC, not Trump or the Trump Organization. Trump's 2025 OGE disclosure (covering 2024) reported about $1.16M in NFT licensing income. [1][2][3]
2. **Watches: confirmed.** Trump-branded watches launched in Sept 2024. Some models cost up to $100,000. The 2025 disclosure reported $2.8M in Trump Watches royalties. [4][3]
3. **Shoes, including golden sneakers: confirmed.** The gold "Never Surrender" high-tops ($399) were unveiled at Sneaker Con in Philadelphia in Feb 2024, alongside $199 models. Sneakers and fragrances together brought in about $2.5M of disclosed 2024 income. The licensee was CIC Ventures LLC. [5][6][3]
4. **Trump Bible: confirmed.** The "God Bless the USA Bible" ($59.99, promoted Mar 2024) earned $1,306,035 in royalties through Greenwood ("Greenwood Bible") according to the 2025 disclosure. [6][3]
5. **Fragrances: confirmed.** "Victory 47" cologne and perfume ($99) went on sale in late 2024. [7]
6. **T1 phone advertised as "Made in America" until the website changed: confirmed.**
   - Trump Mobile launched on 16 June 2025 at Trump Tower. Don Jr. and Eric Trump announced it with the $499 T1 and a $47.45/month "47 Plan".
   - The site's banner read "Our MADE IN THE USA 'T1 Phone'". By about 25-26 June 2025 it said "designed with American values in mind" or "American-proud design". The listed specs also changed.
   - Spokesperson Chris Walker said the phones "are proudly being made in America."
   - The phone was delayed repeatedly and began shipping in May 2026. CEO Pat O'Brien then said the first units are "assembled in the U.S."
   - In Aug 2025 and Jan 2026, Democrats led by Sen. Elizabeth Warren asked the FTC to examine the marketing. The Jan 2026 letter was co-led by Rep. Robert Garcia. [8][9][10][11][12][13]
7. **Coffeezilla ordered a T1 and was "doxxed": the event is confirmed, but "doxxed" is his loose word for it.**
   - In May 2026 an anonymous researcher told Coffeezilla and penguinz0 about a flaw in Trump Mobile's pre-order system. It exposed customers' names, emails, mailing addresses, phone numbers and order IDs, but not card numbers.
   - Coffeezilla said his own address and email were among the exposed records. Trump Mobile confirmed the exposure to TechCrunch, blamed a third-party platform, patched the flaw, and said it was weighing whether to notify customers.
   - No report says anyone targeted or published his data specifically. "Doxxed" here means a data exposure, not a deliberate act against him.
   - From the IDs in the leak, Coffeezilla estimated only about 30,000 orders, against roughly 590,000 claimed earlier. Some outlets report his estimate as about 10,000. [14][15][16][17]

### Corrections and context
- "The Trump family sells" overstates it. Most of these products are **licensing deals**: third parties such as CIC Ventures, NFT INT LLC and Trump Mobile's operators make and sell them, and pay royalties for the name. Trump Mobile's own site says its products are not made or sold by the Trump Organization. Even so, the royalties flow to Trump, and his sons fronted Trump Mobile.
- "Doxxed" means his data was exposed through a site vulnerability, not that he was deliberately targeted.
- "Made in America until they changed the website" is accurate for June 2025. The company still claims U.S. assembly, and the share of the phone actually made in the U.S. has not been independently verified.
- The leaked order counts (about 30k or 10k) are Coffeezilla's own estimates, not company figures.
