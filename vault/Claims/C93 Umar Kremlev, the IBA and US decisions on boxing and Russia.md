# C93: Umar Kremlev, the IBA and US decisions on boxing and Russia

**Verdict:** Partly accurate · medium confidence  
**Origin:** Connection hunt

## Bottom line

The gift is documented: Kremlev paid, through the IBA-affiliated Dubai entity IB Challenger, for two post-wedding nights (about $100k island, $70k fireworks per ProPublica), and the family says it repaid an undisclosed amount.

## What holds up

- Kremlev paid for parts of Don Jr.'s May 2026 Bahamas wedding weekend through IB Challenger, a Dubai entity affiliated with the IBA (the brief's premise, from C76).
- The IBA has asked the Trump administration to intervene on Olympic boxing at LA28.
- Status of Olympic boxing governance, and the US government's role in it.
- The IBA's Gazprom sponsorship history.
- Kremlev's other contacts with the Trump family: the IBA Istanbul forum, Sep 2025.
- Kremlev's other contacts: the Mongolia hunting trip, late Aug 2026.
- All responses on the record.
- Kremlev's background: convictions, name change, Kremlin honours.

## What needs correcting

- Kremlev's sanctions and travel exposure (partly accurate)
- **Claim:** The brief implies US 'decisions on boxing' are in play  
  **Record:** The IOC/CAS decided Olympic boxing governance (2023-2025), and Trump's LA28 executive order concerns security, transport and entry/exit. No US decision on boxing governance was found.
- 'Trump's executive order on Olympic sports': the documented LA28 order creates a DHS-housed task force. We found no order on boxing governance or a federal boxing commission. The closest is the Ali Revival Act, a congressional bill, which strengthens the Association of Boxing Commissions' role.
- Some relays date the Istanbul forum to Sep 2026 and the Mongolia hunt to Aug 2025. The better-supported dates are 29 Sep 2025 (Istanbul) and late Aug 2026 (Mongolia).
- One relay calls the IBA 'sanctioned'. We found no US sanction on the IBA or on Kremlev; Ukraine has sanctioned Kremlev.

## What is unproven

- Kremlev and Trump Jr. discussed business deals in Kazakhstan in 2026.
- Kremlev's motive was to win US help to regain control of Olympic boxing.
- Link to Dana White/TKO's boxing push and the Muhammad Ali Act revision.

## In more detail

- The IBA's interest before the US government is also documented (its Jan 2025 letter asking Trump to 'look into' its exclusion from LA28 boxing), as are further contacts (Istanbul forum Sep 2025, Mongolia hunt Aug 2026 per FT) and a single-source, denied claim of business talks in Kazakhstan.
- But the record shows no US action, request or benefit tied to the gift: Olympic boxing governance was settled by the IOC/CAS before the wedding, no US sanctions or visa decision on Kremlev was found, and no link to TKO/Dana White or the Ali Act exists in coverage.

### 3. Status of Olympic boxing governance, and the US government's role in it.

**Confirmed** · Evidence: Documented

- The IOC suspended the IBA in 2019 and withdrew its recognition in June 2023, citing governance, financial-transparency and refereeing concerns.
- CAS dismissed the IBA's appeal (award dated 2 Apr 2024, CAS 2023/A/9757).
- The IOC gave World Boxing provisional recognition on 26 Feb 2025.
- Its Executive Board proposed boxing for LA28 on 17 Mar 2025, and the IOC Session approved it later that month.
- The IOC has told National Olympic Committees that boxers whose national federation stays with the IBA cannot compete at LA28.
- The US government's formal role is the White House Task Force on the 2028 Games, created by Trump's executive order in Aug 2025 and housed at DHS, with Vice President Vance as vice chair.
- Its stated remit is security, transport and entry/exit processes; the IOC welcomed it.
- We found nothing showing the task force, or any US body, has a say in which federation governs Olympic boxing.
- In short, the IOC decided the governance question before the wedding, and the US has no documented lever over it.
- Dates: June 2023; 2 Apr 2024; 26 Feb 2025; 17 Mar 2025; Aug 2025
- Sources: [14](https://tas-cas.org/en/general-information/news-detail/article/cas-dismisses-the-appeal-filed-by-the-international-boxing-association-iba-against-the-decision-of) [15](https://www.tas-cas.org/fileadmin/user_upload/Award_Final_9757__for_publ._.pdf) [16](https://www.the-star.co.ke/sports/2024-04-05-ioc-decision-to-strip-iba-recognition-justified--cas) [18](https://www.flashscore.com/news/ioc-provisionally-recognises-world-boxing-as-federation-for-2028-la-olympics/QcpDEYSI) [19](https://olympics.com/ioc/news/boxing-to-be-part-of-la28-sports-programme) [20](https://www.ringmagazine.com/news/boxing-wins-unanimous-decision-to-return-to-olympics-ahead-of-la-2028-ByzSyrbtWWDRSmP5XnT00) [21](https://www.thesportsexaminer.com/boxing-ioc-tells-national-olympic-committees-to-ignore-national-federations-affiliated-with-the-iba/) [22](https://www.thesportsexaminer.com/los-angeles-2028-trump-signs-executive-order-to-create-white-house-task-force-on-2028-olympic-paralympic-games-preparations/) [23](https://theannapurnaexpress.com/story/56995) [59](https://www.cityam.com/olympics-recognises-world-boxing-after-ditching-russia-led-iba/)

### 4. The IBA's Gazprom sponsorship history.

**Confirmed** · Evidence: Documented (IOC statements; CAS award as summarised by outlets)

- The IOC criticised the IBA in 2022 as too dependent on Gazprom.
- The CAS panel's concerns, as reported, were that Gazprom was a single revenue source, a short-horizon deal and a Russian state-owned company.
- The Gazprom deal reportedly ended in Dec 2022, though the logo stayed on the IBA website for a time and no terms of a replacement sponsorship (Sting) were put in evidence.
- IB Challenger's own funding sources are unknown (Kyiv Post).
- No source shows Gazprom money in the wedding payments, and that link is not asserted here.
- Dates: Dec 2022

### 5. Kremlev's sanctions and travel exposure.

**Partly accurate**

- One relay describes the IBA as 'sanctioned'; we found no basis for that as a US measure and do not adopt it.
- US gov interest is therefore potential (any future US sanctions or visa decision would sit with Treasury and State), not one shown to be in play.

## Denials and context

- Trump Jr.'s spokesperson: Kremlev is 'a personal friend of Don', 'not someone he has a business relationship with'.
  - The two 'have never entered into any business deals with each other and have no plans to do so'.
  - They were introduced by a mutual hunting friend, and Trump Jr. was unpaid for the Istanbul panel.
- Trump Jr. and Bettina Anderson: a 'generous gift' covering two post-wedding nights, not the wedding.
  - Political motive rejected; repayment pledged and, per both sides, made (amount undisclosed).

  - It says the IBA itself did not pay and that he was not in Putin's official China delegation.
- Trump: 'no idea who Umar is', then said his son would repay and that it is not illegal to pay for a friend's party.
  - Melania Trump's spokesman says she has no relationship with Kremlev.
  - Eric Trump's spokesperson says Eric has no knowledge of him.
- AG Blanche saw nothing to investigate.
  - Garcia's requests are voluntary minority letters.
  - Curtis framed his request as covering family members of both parties and did not allege lawbreaking.
- Context cutting against the 'influence' reading: the IOC had already excluded the IBA and recognised World Boxing before the wedding.
  - The US has no documented lever over Olympic federation recognition, and the Telegraph itself judged the IBA far from regaining control.
- Context cutting the other way: the IBA did petition Trump directly in Jan 2025.
  - Kremlev's contacts with Trump Jr. continued after the wedding (the Mongolia hunt), and the two reportedly avoided being photographed together there (FT, one participant).

## Full report

**Verdict: partly-accurate (confidence: medium).** The gift is documented. So is the IBA's interest in the US government: in January 2025 it publicly asked Trump to "look into" boxing's exclusion from LA28. Kremlev's later contacts with Don Jr. are also on the record, mostly well sourced and partly single-source.

What the record does **not** show is any US action, request or benefit tied to the gift. The IOC and CAS settled Olympic boxing governance before the wedding. We found no US sanctions or visa decision on Kremlev, and the coverage contains no link between Kremlev and TKO, Dana White or the Ali Act revision. A documented gift between people the family calls friends is not evidence of a quid pro quo.

Origin: a third-layer hunt from C76, which established the payments, the IB Challenger route, the repayment claim and the first responses. This report extends that work and does not repeat it.

#### 3. Olympic boxing governance and the US role (documented)

- The IOC says boxers from IBA-aligned national federations cannot compete at LA28.
- Trump's Aug 2025 executive order created a White House Task Force on the 2028 Games. It sits at DHS, with Vance as vice chair, and its remit is security, transport and entry/exit. The IOC welcomed it.
