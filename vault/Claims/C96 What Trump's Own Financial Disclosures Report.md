# C96: What Trump's Own Financial Disclosures Report

**Verdict:** Confirmed · high confidence  
**Type:** Check · Family Ventures & Wealth

## The Claim

> What income did Donald Trump report in his own financial disclosure filings during his second term?

## Bottom Line

Trump's annual OGE Form 278e for calendar 2025 (927 PDF pages, received 29 June 2026, released 30 June 2026) and his report for calendar 2024 (signed 13 June 2025) were both read in full from OGE's own site.

## What Holds Up

- The second-term filings exist, are public, and cover defined calendar years.
- Crypto was the largest declared income source for 2025: meme-coin royalties and World Liberty proceeds.
- The 2024 filing showed far smaller crypto income: $57.35M of World Liberty token sales and about $1.16M of NFT licensing.
- Golf clubs and resorts, including Mar-a-Lago, rose in 2025.
- Foreign licensing and development fees rose sharply, led by Gulf developers.
- Trump Media is a large holding but produced no reported income.
- Royalties from books, Bibles, watches, sneakers, guitars and fragrances are declared line by line.
- Speaking, pensions, settlements and gifts appear as other income.
- Major liabilities declared.
- Compared with the August 2024 candidate filing (mostly accurate)
- What the form does not capture.

## What Needs Correcting

- **Claimed:** Trump 'disclosed about $57M from WLF' for 2024 (C42, C11)  
  **The record shows:** The $57,355,532 is booked on the line for World Liberty Financial, Inc. as that company's token-sale revenue (100% owned by WLF Holdco, then 75% owned by DT Marks DEFI). It is not a distribution to Trump; DT Marks DEFI's own line shows no 2024 income.
- **Claimed:** The Block itemizes '$236.25M of token proceeds' (C29, C42)  
  **The record shows:** $236,250,000 is only the cash line (124.3). The filing lists nine further crypto-wallet lines of token-sale proceeds distributed by WLF, for $526,810,321 in total.
- **Claimed:** DT Marks DEFI's stake in WLF Holdco was 'reported at about 60%, then 40%, then 38.25%' (C42)  
  **The record shows:** The filings give 75% at the end of 2024 (line 390, 2025 report) and 38.25% at the end of 2025 (line 405, 2026 report). Intermediate figures come from press and company statements, not from the forms.
- **Claimed:** 'The 22.5B-token stake was valued at about $1.3B.' (C42)  
  **The record shows:** The forms list 15.75 billion WLFI governance tokens under DTTM Operations, valued only as 'Over $50,000,000'. 15.75B is 70% of the 22.5B allocation, consistent with the 30% family share of DT Marks DEFI (our arithmetic). The $1.3B is a press valuation, not a disclosed figure.
- **Claimed:** WLF component is 'about $594M' per Bloomberg (C29)  
  **The record shows:** $526.81M of token proceeds plus $65.63M from the equity sale is $592.44M; adding $1.82M of staking rewards gives about $594.3M. The Stablecoin Holdco figure is exactly $196,875,000, on the DT Marks SC line, not a WLF line.
- **Claimed:** The figures are described as income 'Trump' made (C11, C29, C42)  
  **The record shows:** The crypto receipts are listed on the lines of DT Marks DEFI and DT Marks SC, which are 70% owned through Trump's companies and 30% by unnamed 'Trump Family Members'. The form does not say whether the amounts are the entities' totals or Trump's share, so they are entity-line amounts, not a stated personal figure (2026-11-16 audit; this replaces an earlier wording that called them entity totals).
- **Claimed:** Trump 'owes E. Jean Carroll $50 million' (some outlets, AP)  
  **The record shows:** The form lists one Carroll judgment at 'Over $50,000,000' and another at $1,000,001 to $5,000,000; the exact amounts are not on the form.
- **Claimed:** An 847-page report (InvestmentNews)  
  **The record shows:** The PDF has 927 pages; the form proper is paginated '1 of 847', followed by a 23-page Schedule 1 and a trademark exhibit.
- 2026-11-16 audit: the $42,250,000 wallet line (124.4) is labelled 'Cryptocurrency Wallet Virtual USD Key' on the form, not USD1; the earlier text named it USD1. The combined crypto total with staking and interest is $1.427B, not $1.428B.

## In More Detail

- For 2025 the form lists about $1.42B of crypto-related receipts: $635.1M in meme-coin royalties to CIC Digital, $526.8M of World Liberty token-sale proceeds and $65.6M from selling WLF Holdco equity (both via DT Marks DEFI), and $196.9M via DT Marks SC from Stablecoin Holdco, the USD1 business.
- It also lists about $416M of US golf and club revenue (Mar-a-Lago $77.5M, Doral $121.9M), about $63M of foreign licensing, development and management fees, $86.5M of media settlements, and about $8M of book and merchandise royalties.
- For 2024 the comparable figures were $57.4M of WLF token sales (booked at World Liberty Financial, Inc.), about $350M of US golf revenue and about $46M of foreign fees.
- These are gross entity-level revenues, many shown only as ranges.
- Unnamed 'Trump Family Members' own 30% of the two crypto entities and 20% of most new licensing entities.
- The form does not say whether the amounts on those lines are the entities' totals or only Trump's share, so his own economic share cannot be read from it; none of these figures is personal net income.

## Sub-Claims

### 1. The second-term filings exist, are public, and cover defined calendar years.

**Confirmed**

- OGE's President and Vice President index lists two second-term annual reports.
- The 2025 annual report covers calendar 2024.
- Its cover is dated 2025, it was signed 13 June 2025, OGE certified it the same day, and it had a 30-day extension.
- It lists 'Entities Dissolved 1/1/2024 through 12/31/2024', which fixes the period (Reuters noted the report did not state its period outright).
- The 2026 annual report covers calendar 2025, including the 19 days before the 20 January 2025 inauguration.
- OGE received it on 29 June 2026, the ethics official signed it on 30 June 2026, and it had a 45-day extension; a reviewer's note says the filer paid late-filing fees for transactions not previously reported on Form 278-T. The PDF runs to 927 pages.
- The form itself is numbered 'of 847' and is followed by a 23-page Schedule 1 and a trademark exhibit, which explains why some outlets report 847 pages.
- OGE also lists 22 periodic transaction reports (278-T) filed from August 2025 to October 2026.
- The August 2024 candidate report was not found on OGE's index; it is described here from CREW's and Axios's readings.
- Dates: 13 June 2025; 20 January 2025; 29 June 2026; 30 June 2026; August 2025

### 2. Crypto was the largest declared income source for 2025: meme-coin royalties and World Liberty proceeds.

**Confirmed**

- From the 2026 filing, Schedule 1: - **CIC Digital LLC (100% owned by Trump's revocable trust):** $635,068,835 in royalties under a 'License agreement with Celebration Coins' (line 21.6), plus $493,846 from a sublicense with JBCZ Group (21.7), $510,808 of Ethereum staking rewards (21.4a) and $45,932 of USDC interest.
- Its bitcoin wallet is valued at 'Over $50,000,000', the form's top bracket. - **DT Marks DEFI LLC (lines 124.2 to 124.12):** $65,625,000 net proceeds from selling equity in WLF Holdco LLC, and token-sale proceeds 'distributed by World Liberty Financial' totalling $526,810,321.
- That total is our sum of $236,250,000 in cash plus nine crypto-wallet lines: ETH $150.6M, USDC $56.0M, a wallet the form labels 'Virtual USD Key' $42.25M, BTC $33.5M and smaller LINK, AAVE, ENA, MOVE and ONDO lines.
- The entity also earned $1,821,628 of ETH staking rewards. - **DT Marks SC LLC (line 114):** $196,875,000 of 'net proceeds received from capital contribution from new member(s) of Stablecoin Holdco LLC and sale of Class C Units'.
- Stablecoin Holdco itself reports $8,326,828 of net operating income (line 204.1).
- Ownership as the form gives it: line 124 (Schedule 1, page 8 of 23) 'DT Marks Defi Member Corp: 1%, DTTM Operations LLC 69%, & Trump Family Members 30%'; line 114 (page 7 of 23) 'DT Marks SC Member Corp: 1%, DTTM Operations LLC 69%, & Trump Family Members 30%'.
- Both member corporations and DTTM Operations trace to Trump's own holding companies, so his indirect stake is 70%.
- Line 405 gives WLF Holdco as 'DT Marks Defi LLC: 38.25% & Third Parties: 61.75%' and says 'All Distributions from token and equity sales are reported on lines 124.2 through 124.12'; line 204 gives Stablecoin Holdco as 'DT Marks SC LLC - Limited Partner - 38.25%; & Third Parties - 61.75%', with distributions from the equity sale 'reported on line 114'.
- A year earlier (2025 report, line 390) WLF Holdco was 'DT Marks Defi LLC: 75% & Third Parties: 25%'.
- The form lists these amounts on the lines of DT Marks DEFI LLC (line 124) and DT Marks SC LLC (line 114), each shown as owned 'DT Marks ...
- Member Corp: 1%, DTTM Operations LLC 69%, & Trump Family Members 30%'.
- It does not say whether the amounts are the LLCs' total receipts or only the part attributable to Trump's 70%, and it has no pro-rata note.
- If they are entity totals, Trump's indirect share of the $789.3M is about $552.5M; if they are his share, the two LLCs received about $1.13B and unnamed family members about $338M more that is not on his form (our arithmetic either way).
- Outlets read it both ways: the Senate Banking minority letter treats the DT Marks DEFI lines as the entity's receipts, while CAP treats the $262.5M stake-sale figure as Trump's 70% share.
- Token holdings: line 133.2 (page 9 of 23), under DTTM Operations LLC (owned 'DJT Holdings LLC: 99% & DTTM Operations Managing Member Corp: 1%', i.e. wholly Trump's), lists '15,750,000,000 Governance tokens - World Liberty Financial', valued 'Over $50,000,000'.
- The 22.5 billion figure in press reports comes from World Liberty's own disclaimer ('DT Marks DEFI LLC along with certain family members'), not from the form; 15.75B is 70% of 22.5B (our arithmetic).
- The four receipt lines (meme-coin royalties, token proceeds, WLF Holdco equity sale, Stablecoin Holdco proceeds) sum to $1,424,379,156; with the JBCZ sublicence $1.425B, and with staking rewards and interest $1.427B. That matches Reuters' 'more than $1.4B', with 'almost $800M' from World Liberty (526.8 + 65.6 + 196.9 = $789.3M; PolitiFact's $791M adds staking rewards), and Bloomberg's corrected $1.4B.
- Amounts: 100%; $635,068,835; $493,846; $510,808; $45,932

#### 10. What the form does not capture

1. **Ranges.** Many amounts are brackets with no upper bound.
2. **Revenue, not profit.** Business lines show gross revenue.
3. **Family shares.** Income is listed on the lines of entities in which unnamed family members own 20-30%, and the form does not say whether those amounts are entity totals or Trump's share. Their separate ventures do not appear, and family members are not named.
4. **Unrealised value.** Gains or losses on shares, tokens and wallets are not income.
5. **Salary.** The government salary is excluded.
6. **Timing.** Calendar 2025 includes the three days of TRUMP meme-coin sales before the inauguration.

Revenue reported during the term is not the same as revenue earned because of the office.
