# C80: Sovereign-fund megadeals needing US sign-off: PGA–PIF, EA, Aligned, G42

**Verdict:** Mostly accurate · medium confidence  
**Origin:** Connection hunt

## Bottom line

Most links in the brief are documented, but the approvals behind them are often not.

## What holds up

- Trump personally brokered PGA Tour-PIF talks at the White House.
- While Trump was mediating, PIF-funded LIV played at Trump courses, and the PGA Tour also returned to Trump Doral.
- CFIUS handled the $55B EA buyout by PIF, Silver Lake and Kushner's Affinity (mostly accurate)
- MGX took part in the $40B Aligned Data Centers takeover with BlackRock/GIP, Nvidia and Microsoft, and it needed US approval (mostly accurate)
- In 2026 the UAE got an export-control upgrade that named G42 for licence-free chips, after Tahnoon lobbied the White House.
- Lunate took over G42's China fund (mostly accurate)

## What needs correcting

- Cross-deal connection: the same counterparties recur across these approvals and the Trump family's own deals (partly accurate)
- **Brief said:** Trump was 'brokering' the PGA-PIF deal: the Tour asked him to join, the February 2025 sessions produced no agreement, and the talks later stalled  
  **Record shows:** As of 2026 there is no PGA Tour-PIF deal.
- Brief implies G42's US reincorporation has happened: only a relayed WSJ report and Bloomberg's 'exploratory talks' exist, and G42 has not confirmed either.
- The 'takeover' of G42's China fund by Lunate was reported as a transfer of management (Bloomberg, 2024), not a confirmed change of ownership.
- CFIUS clearance of EA rests on press accounts of an EA filing; no Treasury decision or mitigation agreement is public.
- Some pages relaying golf coverage carry 2026 crawl-date banners on 2025 stories; an apparent 'October 2026' Trump-PGA-PIF meeting is a February 2025 article.

## What is unproven

- LIV has a new lead investor after PIF's exit, and that investor's identity is known.
- G42 is reincorporating as a US company.

## In more detail

- Trump hosted PGA Tour leaders and PIF governor Yasir Al-Rumayyan at the White House in February 2025, yet no PGA-PIF deal followed.
- PIF then said it would stop funding LIV after 2026, and LIV's new lead investor was still unnamed as of August 2026.
- The PGA Tour itself moved a $20M signature event to Trump Doral in 2026.
- EA's $55B buyout by PIF, Silver Lake and Kushner's Affinity closed on 4 Aug 2026, and press reports say all regulatory approvals, CFIUS included, came by 30 Jul 2026.
- No CFIUS decision or mitigation terms were made public.
- MGX's $40B Aligned purchase closed in July 2026, and no CFIUS outcome was reported.
- BIS moved the UAE to Country Group A:5 on 10 Jul 2026 and named G42 and Core42 for licence-free chip receipt.
- The report that G42 will reincorporate in the US rests on secondary relays of the WSJ, and on a Bloomberg report of 'exploratory talks' that G42 would not confirm.
- Lunate's takeover of G42's China fund is single-source (Bloomberg, 2024), and nothing later was found.
- Nothing shows that any approval was traded for family business.

### 1. Trump personally brokered PGA Tour-PIF talks at the White House.

**Confirmed** · Evidence: Documented (AP, ESPN, Golf Channel, Golf Digest)

- Monahan and Adam Scott met Trump at the White House on 2025-02-04, at the Tour's request.
- On 2025-02-20 Monahan, Tiger Woods and Scott held a session of about four hours with Trump and PIF governor Yasir Al-Rumayyan.
- The joint statement called it 'constructive' but announced no deal.
- The proposal under discussion was about $1.5B of PIF money into PGA Tour Enterprises, the same size as the investment by the US owners' group SSG.
- Golf Digest (March 2025) reported that the talks then stalled.
- Al-Rumayyan reportedly found the Tour's proposals inadequate.
- AP said that not even Trump could smooth the way.
- Brian Rolapp became PGA Tour CEO in mid-2025 and has since said there is 'no merger, no conversations' with LIV.
- The Justice Department's antitrust review of the 2023 framework agreement was the original approval hurdle.
- We found no 2025-26 DOJ decision.
- The Tour invited Trump in.
- He did not insert himself.
- Amounts: about $1.5B
- Dates: 2025-02-04; 2025-02-20; March 2025; mid-2025; 2025-26

### 4. CFIUS handled the $55B EA buyout by PIF, Silver Lake and Kushner's Affinity.

**Mostly accurate** · Evidence: Documented

- The deal closed on 2026-08-04 at $210 a share and EA was delisted.
- Press reports, Gameluster among them, say the remaining regulatory approvals came by 2026-07-30, and some trade outlets say this was confirmed in an EA SEC filing that included CFIUS.
- The EU cleared the deal under merger rules on around 2026-07-23 and under its foreign-subsidies rules on around 2026-07-31.
- As late as July, the merger agreement's outside date (2026-09-28, extendable to 12-28) was described as hinging on CFIUS.
- Single-source/unverified: we found no Treasury statement and no public mitigation terms.
- An automated commentary site says no data firewall was disclosed.
- Treasury Secretary Bessent chairs CFIUS.
- The deal's critics are on record: Sens. Blumenthal and Warren (letter of 2025-10-14) wrote that the investors appear to be 'betting $1 billion that Mr. Kushner can deliver' approvals, and Lawfare (Vinita Singh, 2025-10-03) predicted 'the CFIUS review that will never be'.

- Audit: GamingBolt, Mobilegamer.biz and Tech-Insider again report that EA's 30 Jul 2026 SEC filing said all required approvals were obtained, with CFIUS the last hurdle that pushed closing past the original 30 Jun target; ABC News (as relayed) gives 5 Aug rather than 4 Aug as the closing date.
- Still no Treasury statement or mitigation terms.
- Amounts: $210; $1 billion
- Dates: 2026-08-04; 2026-07-30; 2026-07-23; 2026-07-31; 2026-09-28

### 5. MGX took part in the $40B Aligned Data Centers takeover with BlackRock/GIP, Nvidia and Microsoft, and it needed US approval.

- The deal closed in July 2026 (The Next Web, 2026-07-21; DCD), with about $5B of extra growth capital.

- So the approval process is unverified, and the closing implies only that the parties' conditions were met.
- Tahnoon chairs MGX (C57).

### 6. In 2026 the UAE got an export-control upgrade that named G42 for licence-free chips, after Tahnoon lobbied the White House.

**Confirmed** · Evidence: Documented (BIS final rule, Skadden, Mondaq/Akin, Al-Monitor, Khaleej Times, DCD)

- BIS removed the UAE from Country Groups D:3/D:4 and added it to A:5, announced around 2026-07-10 and published in the Federal Register around 2026-07-14.
- Group 42 Holding (G42) and Core42 are listed in Supplement 8 to Part 740 as approved recipients of advanced-computing items without a licence, though not under License Exception STA.
- Their approval lapses after about 270 days, reported as around 2027-04-06, unless BIS extends it.
- Commerce said it would 'favorably review' MGX licence applications.
- Commerce cited UAE technology-protection measures and the UAE's support for the US in the 2026 Iran war.
- Single-source (WSJ, relayed): Tahnoon led the lobbying and went directly to the White House after the war began.
- Sen. Warren (statement of 2026-07-10) called the rule a reward despite diversion risk and tied it to the Tahnoon-linked 49% stake in World Liberty.
- The White House and WLF deny any link.
- Audit: law-firm alerts (Latham, Skadden, Morgan Lewis, Akin, Steptoe) give the rule's effective date as 10 Jul 2026, and say the G42/Core42 approval expires on 6 Apr 2027 unless they become 'U.S. companies' or obtain approval via BIS's advisory-opinion process, which is the stated driver of the G42 restructuring talks in the next item.
- The Tahnoon lobbying account is still known only through relays of the WSJ.
- Amounts: 49%
- Dates: 2026-07-10; 2026-07-14; 2027-04-06; 10 Jul 2026; 6 Apr 2027
- Sources: [18](https://www.skadden.com/insights/publications/2026/07/bis-liberalizes-export) [19](https://www.mondaq.com/bis-upgrades-the-uae-from-country-group-d-to-country-group-a-under-the-ear-allowing-increased-trade-in-advanced-computing-and-defense-items/1818120) [20](https://www.al-monitor.com/originals/2026/07/us-makes-it-easier-export-nvidia-ai-chips-and-military-equipment-uae) [21](https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/us-govt-eases-ai-chip-export-controls-on-uae-following-countrys-support-in-iran-war/) [22](https://www.banking.senate.gov/newsroom/minority/senator-warren-statement-on-trump-administrations-removing-export-controls-on-ai-chips-to-uae)

### 8. Lunate took over G42's China fund.

**Mostly accurate**

- Single-source (Bloomberg, 2024-07-08, anonymous sources; Lunate declined to comment; widely relayed).
- Management of 42XFund passed from a G42 subsidiary to Lunate.
- 42XFund is a Shanghai-based growth fund launched in 2022 with about $10B and holding stakes in ByteDance and JD.com.
- Reports describe a change of manager, not a confirmed transfer of ownership.
- Bloomberg said this kept the assets inside Tahnoon's orbit.
- It came after G42 told the FT in February 2024 that it had divested its China holdings as part of the Microsoft deal.
- Bloomberg described Lunate as an IHC unit, while C57 records it as majority-owned by Chimera Investment; both are Tahnoon-linked.
- We found no 2025-26 follow-up on the fund.
- This needed no US approval, but it bears on whether G42 really split from China, a premise of every later US chip licence.
- Amounts: about $10B
- Dates: 2024-07-08; February 2024; 2025-26
- Sources: [28](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2024-07-08/abu-dhabi-investment-firm-lunate-takes-on-ai-firm-g42-s-china-fund) <https://www.bnnbloomberg.ca/business/technology/2024/07/08/abu-dhabi-investment-firm-lunate-takes-on-ai-firm-g42s-china-fund/>

### 9. Cross-deal connection: the same counterparties recur across these approvals and the Trump family's own deals.

**Partly accurate**

- Each node is documented; the pattern is inferred (our cross-reference, asserted by no source). (1) Lunate, which took over G42's China fund, is also one of the three largest LPs in Kushner's Affinity (C15/C57). (2) Silver Lake sits in the EA buyout with Affinity, in the TikTok JV with MGX (C68) and on G42's cap table, so it has been co-investor with both the Kushner/PIF side and the Tahnoon side of three US-approval-dependent deals. (3) Microsoft and Nvidia co-own AIP, which bought Aligned with MGX, while Microsoft owns part of G42 and Nvidia sells chips under the UAE licences; both also funded Trump's ballroom (C68). (4) Tahnoon's MGX received 'favorable review' language in the same rule that freed G42, while his Aryam holds 49% of World Liberty (C57).
- The overlap shows that the same small set of capital pools needs, and receives, US decisions while also dealing with the family.
- It does not show coordination or exchange.
- Amounts: 49%
- Sources: [28](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2024-07-08/abu-dhabi-investment-firm-lunate-takes-on-ai-firm-g42-s-china-fund) [25](https://thenextweb.com/news/g42-us-ownership-chip-access-export-control-endgame) [22](https://www.banking.senate.gov/newsroom/minority/senator-warren-statement-on-trump-administrations-removing-export-controls-on-ai-chips-to-uae)

## Denials and context

- The PGA Tour says it asked Trump to help.
  - Monahan said Trump 'bolstered' the talks.
  - Rolapp later disavowed a LIV merger.
- PIF's exit from LIV and LIV's restructuring are commercial decisions.
  - No source attributes them to US policy.
- EA's buyers say the deal is a long-term investment, and Andrew Wilson remains CEO.
  - Affinity's stake is about 1.1% (press estimate).
  - Kushner holds no formal government role (C15).
- Commerce justified the UAE upgrade by the UAE's security commitments and its support in the Iran war.
  - The White House says the chip decisions are unrelated to Trump-family crypto business, and WLF denies any link.
- G42 said it does not comment on confidential discussions.
  - Lunate declined to comment on the China fund.
- No CFIUS decisions are published by law.
  - The absence of a public decision is normal and is not itself evidence of a skipped review.

### Summary

The deals themselves are well documented. The approvals behind them often are not.

- **Golf:** Trump hosted the PGA Tour and PIF governor Yasir Al-Rumayyan at the White House in February 2025. No deal followed. PIF is quitting LIV after 2026, and LIV's new lead investor had not been named as of August 2026.
- **Trump Doral:** In the same period, the PGA Tour moved a $20M signature event to Trump Doral. So both sides of the dispute Trump was mediating held events at his resort.
- **EA:** The $55B EA buyout closed on 4 Aug 2026. Press reports say CFIUS cleared it, but no decision or mitigation terms are public.
- **Aligned:** MGX's $40B Aligned purchase closed in July 2026. No CFIUS outcome was reported.
- **UAE chips:** On 10 Jul 2026 the UAE was upgraded to Country Group A:5. G42 and Core42 got licence-free chip access for about 270 days, and MGX got a promise of "favorable review".
- **G42 in the US:** The report that G42 will become a US company is relayed, contested and unconfirmed.
- **Lunate and G42's China fund:** Lunate's takeover of the fund is a single-source 2024 Bloomberg report. Lunate is also an LP in Kushner's Affinity, which links G42's China fund to the Kushner fund through the same holder.
