# C22: Crypto.com's deal with Trump Media

**Verdict:** Mostly accurate · medium confidence  
**Type:** Check · Trump Media and markets

## The claim

> Did Crypto.com agree to buy $50 million of Trump Media stock months after the SEC closed its investigation of the exchange, and what else has Crypto.com obtained from federal regulators?

## Bottom line

Each core fact is documented. Crypto.com agreed on 2025-08-26 to take $50M of TMTG stock as part of a CRO-treasury deal, and that swap closed on 2025-09-05 (TMTG paid for $105M of CRO half in its own shares); the larger SPAC treasury plan was terminated on 2026-08-07.

## What holds up

- Crypto.com agreed to purchase $50M in TMTG stock in a strategic partnership.
- The partnership came months after the SEC closed its investigation into Crypto.com with no action (mostly accurate)
- This followed Trump nominating a new pro-crypto SEC commissioner (mostly accurate)
- Crypto.com spent $36M on contributions to Trump and Trump-affiliated PACs (mostly accurate)
- The CFTC filed an amicus brief in favor of Crypto.com in a prediction-markets lawsuit Crypto.com was facing (mostly accurate)
- Crypto.com's national trust bank charter application was provisionally approved.

## What needs correcting

- **Claimed:** Crypto.com was 'facing' a prediction-markets lawsuit  
  **The record shows:** Crypto.com (through CDNA) was the plaintiff suing Nevada regulators, and it lost at the Ninth Circuit on 2026-08-28 despite the CFTC brief.
- **Claimed:** $36M went to 'Trump affiliated PACs'  
  **The record shows:** The figure is Molly White's tally: about $35M to MAGA Inc. plus $1M to the inauguration.
- The 'new pro-crypto SEC commissioner' was Paul Atkins, named Chair in December 2024, before the probe closed.
- The first Crypto.com/TMTG partnership (ETFs) was announced 2025-03-24, three days before the SEC closure, not months after.
- **Claimed:** The charter was 'provisionally approved'  
  **The record shows:** The OCC's approval is conditional, and final approval is pending.
- The $50M of TMTG stock did change hands: on 2025-09-05 TMTG paid for 684.4M CRO (about $105M) half in cash and half in its own stock. Crypto.com received the shares as payment for tokens, not through a cash purchase. The larger CRO Strategy SPAC treasury plan was terminated on 2026-08-07.

## In more detail

- The SEC closed its Crypto.com probe with no action on 2025-03-27.
- Crypto.com gave about $36M ($35M to MAGA Inc. plus $1M to the inauguration).
- The CFTC filed an amicus brief for Crypto.com on 2026-02-17, and the OCC conditionally approved its trust charter on 2026-02-23.
- The claim errs in describing Crypto.com as the party 'facing' the lawsuit: it was the plaintiff, and it lost at the Ninth Circuit.
- Beyond timing, no quid pro quo is documented, and Crypto.com denies any link.

## Sub-claims

### 1. Crypto.com agreed to purchase $50M in TMTG stock in a strategic partnership.

**Confirmed**

- Announced 2025-08-26 with the formation of Trump Media Group CRO Strategy, a treasury company to be listed through a merger with Yorkville Acquisition Corp (ticker MCGA).
- Audit correction: the bilateral part closed on 2025-09-05 (The Block, Blockworks).
- TMTG acquired 684.4M CRO (about $105M at about $0.153), paid 50/50 in cash and TMTG stock.
- Crypto.com therefore received about $50M of DJT shares (about 2.8M shares per one report), both sides locked up.
- It was a stock-for-token swap, not a cash purchase of stock.
- The separate CRO Strategy SPAC treasury plan was never completed.
- TMTG, Crypto.com and Yorkville mutually terminated it on 2026-08-07, citing market conditions, and also dropped the Yorkville ETF servicing deal and the direct Truth Social prediction-market integration, replacing the latter with a marketing deal.
- Amounts: about $105M; about $0.153; about $50M
- Dates: 2025-08-26; 2025-09-05; 2026-08-07
- Sources: [1](https://decrypt.co/336876/trump-media-crypto-com-reveal-6-4-billion-cronos-treasury-plan) [2](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/trump-media-crypto-com-build-130116236.html) [3](https://blockworks.co/news/trump-media-crypto-com-sign-155m-deal) [24](https://www.theblock.co/news/business/2025-09-05-trump-media-closes-deal-to-buy-684-million-cro-tokens-369646) [25](https://blockworks.co/news/trump-media-finalizes-cronos-purchase) [26](https://theblock.co/news/deals/2026-08-07-trump-media-crypto-com-end-partnerships-as-companies-shifts-priorities-411198) [27](https://axios.com/2026/08/07/trump-media-crypto-treasury-deals)

### 5. The CFTC filed an amicus brief in favor of Crypto.com in a prediction-markets lawsuit Crypto.com was facing.

**Mostly accurate**

- The CFTC under Mike Selig filed the brief on 2026-02-17 in the Ninth Circuit (No. 25-7187), arguing that event contracts are swaps under exclusive CFTC jurisdiction.
- But Crypto.com Derivatives North America was the plaintiff: it sued the Nevada Gaming Control Board in June 2025.
- It lost its preliminary injunction bid in October 2025 and lost at the Ninth Circuit on 2026-08-28.
- A cert petition (No. 26-344) was filed in September 2026.
- Dates: 2026-02-17; June 2025; October 2025; 2026-08-28; September 2026

### 6. Crypto.com's national trust bank charter application was provisionally approved.

**Confirmed**

- Crypto.com applied in October 2025.
- On 2026-02-23 the OCC granted conditional approval for Foris DAX National Trust Bank, covering custody, staking and settlement, with no deposits or loans.
- Final approval depends on pre-opening conditions, and I found no report of final approval.
- Dates: October 2025; 2026-02-23
- Sources: [20](https://www.americanbanker.com/payments/news/crypto-com-gets-conditional-approval-for-occ-trust-charter) [21](https://theblock.co/post/390841/crypto-com-secures-conditional-occ-approval-in-bid-to-become-a-federally-regulated-bank) [22](https://www.occ.treas.gov/topics/charters-and-licensing/interpretations-and-actions/2026/cd1367.pdf)

## Denials and context

- A Crypto.com spokeswoman told AP there is 'absolutely no connection' between the SEC decision and its political activity.
  - TMTG called AP's story politically driven.
- The White House (Karoline Leavitt) denies that the President or his family engage in conflicts of interest.
- The SEC dropped or closed almost all Gensler-era crypto exchange cases in early 2025 (Coinbase, Kraken, Robinhood, Uniswap and others), and the Cumberland DRW case was dismissed the same day.
  - Crypto.com's closure fits that broad pattern.
- The CFTC's jurisdictional position helps all prediction-market operators (for example Kalshi), not only Crypto.com.
  - Other crypto firms received similar OCC conditional charters in the same period.
- Cutting the other way: Crypto.com is MAGA Inc.'s largest donor, and its CDNA exchange powers TMTG's own Truth Predict product, so the CFTC brief also indirectly supports a Trump-owned business.

## Full report

**Verdict: mostly accurate (confidence: medium-high).** Every main fact in the claim is documented:
- Crypto.com agreed to buy $50M of TMTG stock.
- The SEC closed its probe of Crypto.com with no action.
- Crypto.com gave about $36M to Trump-linked committees.
- The CFTC filed an amicus brief backing Crypto.com.
- The OCC conditionally approved Crypto.com's national trust bank charter.

The errors are in the framing:
- Crypto.com was the **plaintiff** in the prediction-markets case. It sued Nevada; it was not a defendant.
- The $36M figure comes from Molly White's tracker. Most of it (about $35M) went to MAGA Inc., and $1M went to the inauguration.
- The charter approval is conditional. It is not a final approval.
- The $50M of TMTG stock did change hands, but as payment for tokens: on 2025-09-05 TMTG paid for $105M of CRO half in cash and half in its own shares. The larger SPAC treasury plan was terminated on 2026-08-07.
- No quid pro quo is documented. The link is timing, which Crypto.com denies.

### The claim
TMTG announced a strategic partnership in which Crypto.com agreed to buy $50M of TMTG stock. This came months after the SEC closed its investigation of Crypto.com, and after Trump nominated a new pro-crypto SEC commissioner. "They spent $36 million on Trump contributions to Trump affiliated PACs." The SEC probe ended with no action. The CFTC later filed an amicus brief for Crypto.com "in a prediction markets-related lawsuit that they were facing." Crypto.com's national trust bank charter application was "provisionally approved."

### Sub-claims

#### 1. Crypto.com agreed to buy $50M of TMTG stock in a strategic partnership: CONFIRMED (closed as a stock-for-token swap; audit-corrected)
- On 2025-08-26, TMTG, Crypto.com and Yorkville Acquisition Corp announced "Trump Media Group CRO Strategy," a treasury company holding Crypto.com's CRO token.
  - It was to go public by merging with a SPAC, under the ticker MCGA.
  - Its planned funding was about $1B in CRO, $200M cash, $220M in warrants and a $5B equity line of credit.
- As part of the deal, Crypto.com agreed to buy $50M of TMTG common stock, and TMTG agreed to buy about $105M of CRO. Both sides' shares carry a lock-up [1][2][3].
- **Audit correction:** the bilateral deal **closed on 2025-09-05**. TMTG acquired 684.4M CRO (about $105M at about $0.153 per token), paid 50/50 in cash and TMTG stock, so Crypto.com received about $50M of DJT shares (about 2.8M shares per one report). Both sides are locked up [23][24]. This was a swap of stock for tokens, not a cash purchase of stock.
- The SPAC treasury company (Trump Media Group CRO Strategy) was never completed. TMTG, Crypto.com and Yorkville mutually terminated it on 2026-08-07, citing market conditions. They also dropped the Yorkville ETF servicing deal and the planned direct prediction-market integration in Truth Social, replacing the latter with a marketing deal for Crypto.com's prediction products [25][26].
- An earlier, non-binding Crypto.com/TMTG ETF partnership was announced on 2025-03-24 and made binding on 2025-04-22 [8][9].

#### 2. The deal came "months after" the SEC closed its investigation with no action: CONFIRMED, with a nuance
- Crypto.com CEO Kris Marszalek and Chief Legal Officer Nick Lundgren announced on 2025-03-27 that the SEC had closed its investigation with no action [4][5].
  - The SEC had sent a Wells notice in August 2024, and Crypto.com sued the SEC in October 2024.
- The $50M share deal came about five months later, so "months after" is accurate.
- However, the first TMTG/Crypto.com partnership (the ETFs) was announced on 2025-03-24, three days **before** the probe was closed [5][8]. That makes the overlap closer than the claim implies, but it also means the partnership was not purely a reward after the closure.

#### 3. "This follows Trump nominating a new pro-crypto SEC commissioner": ACCURATE in substance, imprecise
- Trump named Paul Atkins, a crypto-friendly pick, as SEC **Chair** in December 2024, and made Mark Uyeda acting chair in January 2025 [10].
- I found no 2026 SEC commissioner nomination that fits the claim.

#### 4. $36M in contributions to Trump-affiliated PACs: MOSTLY CONFIRMED
- The figure matches Molly White's "quid pro quo" tracker: $36M "to Trump's campaign, inauguration, and pro-Trump PACs (since 2024)". The tracker page could not be fetched; I relied on search snippets [6].
- AP (Dec 2025) reported a $1M inauguration gift plus $10M to MAGA Inc. in February 2025 [7].
- FEC filings show two more $10M gifts from parent company Foris DAX between September and October 2025, and $5M in January 2026 [11][12].
- That adds up to about $35M to MAGA Inc. plus $1M to the inauguration, which equals $36M.
- Nearly all of the money went to one super PAC (MAGA Inc.), plus the inauguration fund. The giver is Crypto.com.

#### 5. The SEC investigation ended with no action: CONFIRMED
- The probe was closed with no enforcement action and no settlement [4][5].
- Some headlines call this "settling with the SEC." That is wrong: there was no settlement.

#### 6. The CFTC filed an amicus brief for Crypto.com in a prediction-markets lawsuit: CONFIRMED, with one wording error
- On 2026-02-17, the CFTC under Chair Mike Selig filed an amicus brief in the Ninth Circuit (No. 25-7187) supporting Crypto.com against the Nevada Gaming Control Board [13][14][15].
  - The brief argued that the CFTC has exclusive jurisdiction because event contracts are swaps.
  - It was the agency's first amicus filing in the state-versus-federal prediction-markets fight.
- Crypto.com was not "facing" a lawsuit. Its unit, Crypto.com Derivatives North America (formerly NADEX), **sued** Nevada pre-emptively in June 2025 after a cease-and-desist letter [16].
- Judge Andrew Gordon denied a preliminary injunction in October 2025, and Crypto.com stopped offering Nevada event contracts on 2025-11-03 [16].
- On 2026-08-28, the Ninth Circuit ruled against Crypto.com and Kalshi despite the CFTC brief [17]. A Supreme Court cert petition (No. 26-344) was filed in September 2026 [18].
- So far, the government's help did not win Crypto.com the case.

#### 7. The national trust bank charter was "provisionally approved": CONFIRMED (conditional)
- Crypto.com applied in October 2025. On 2026-02-23 the OCC granted conditional approval for Foris DAX National Trust Bank [19][20][21].
- The bank may do custody, staking and settlement, but may not take deposits or make loans.
- Final approval depends on meeting pre-opening conditions. I found no report of final approval as of Nov 2026.
- Other crypto firms received similar conditional charters in the same period, so this was not unique to Crypto.com.

### Corrections
- The claim implies Crypto.com was defending a lawsuit. In fact Crypto.com (through CDNA) was the plaintiff suing Nevada regulators, and it lost at the Ninth Circuit in August 2026.
- The "$36M" figure is Molly White's tally. It is about $35M to MAGA Inc. plus $1M to the inauguration, not money spread across multiple PACs.
- The $50M of TMTG stock went to Crypto.com as half the payment for $105M of CRO in a swap that closed on 2025-09-05, not as a cash purchase. The larger CRO Strategy SPAC plan was terminated on 2026-08-07.
- The "pro-crypto SEC commissioner" was Paul Atkins, named Chair in December 2024, before the SEC probe was closed.
- The first Crypto.com/TMTG partnership (the ETFs, 2025-03-24) came three days before the SEC closure, not months after.

### Denials and context
- A Crypto.com spokeswoman told AP there is "absolutely no connection" between the SEC decision and its political activity [7].
- TMTG called the AP story politically driven [7].
- The White House (Karoline Leavitt) denies any conflicts of interest [6].
- The SEC dropped or closed nearly all Gensler-era crypto exchange cases in early 2025 (Coinbase, Kraken, Robinhood, Uniswap and others). Crypto.com's closure fits that wider pattern.
- The CFTC's amicus position is consistent with its long-standing jurisdictional view, and it benefits every prediction-market operator (Kalshi and others), not only Crypto.com.
- On the other side: Crypto.com is MAGA Inc.'s largest donor (about 10%+ of its funds). Its CDNA exchange also powers TMTG's own "Truth Predict" product, so the CFTC brief indirectly helps a Trump-owned business [22].
