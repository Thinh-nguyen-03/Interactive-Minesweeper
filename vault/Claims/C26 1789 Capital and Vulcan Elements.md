# C26: 1789 Capital and Vulcan Elements

**Verdict:** Mostly accurate · medium confidence  
**Origin:** Claim from the video · transcript lines 737-799

## Bottom line

Don Jr. is a 1789 Capital partner.

## What holds up

- Don Jr. is a partner at 1789 Capital.
- 1789 Capital has over $1 billion in assets.
- 1789 reportedly has returns of over 200% (mostly accurate)
- 1789 invested in Vulcan Elements shortly before Vulcan announced a ~$1B, 10,000-metric-ton magnet facility enabled by a US government partnership (mostly accurate)
- ProPublica reported 'the call came from the White House: we have to get this deal done' and that Vulcan's was the only deal initiated by a top aide to the president.
- 1789 says it is a passive minority growth-equity investor and does not call the government.

## What needs correcting

- 1789 profits by investing in companies that get government contracts, which Don Jr. is skilled at anticipating (misleading)
- **Video said:** 1789 bought 'right before' the government got involved  
  **Record shows:** It invested in August 2025, about three months before the 2025-11-03 announcement.
- **Claim:** Transcript frames it as a Pentagon 'contract'  
  **Record:** It was a $620M Office of Strategic Capital direct loan (initially conditional) plus $50M in Commerce CHIPS incentives and government warrants or equity.
- **Claim:** Transcript quotes 'We have to get this deal done'  
  **Record:** ProPublica's quote is 'The call came from the White House: We have to get this done.'
- **Video said:** 'over one billion dollars in assets'  
  **Record shows:** That was the September 2025 figure, and 2026 reports put 1789 at about $2-3.5B.
- **Video said:** Returns of 'over 200 percent'  
  **Record shows:** The NYT reported roughly or nearly 200% (unrealized) as of 2026-06-30.
- ProPublica identifies the White House aide who initiated the deal as Peter Navarro, not Don Jr. or 1789.

## In more detail

- The firm passed $1B in assets in September 2025 and later grew to roughly $2-3.5B. The NYT reported returns of roughly 200% as of mid-2026.
- 1789 invested in Vulcan Elements in August 2025, about three months before a $1.4B government-backed package that included a $620M Pentagon Office of Strategic Capital loan.
- ProPublica did report that the deal was the only one initiated by a top White House aide (Peter Navarro) and quoted 'The call came from the White House: We have to get this done.'
- The video compresses the timing, calls the loan a 'contract', and implies Don Jr. anticipated or arranged the deal, which no reporting documents; he and 1789 deny any role.

## What the video said

> Don Jr. is a partner at 1789 Capital, which has over $1B in assets and reported 200%+ returns; it invested in rare-earth magnet maker Vulcan Elements shortly before the company announced a ~$1B+ facility with US government backing; ProPublica reported the deal was the only one initiated by a top White House aide ('the call came from the White House'); 1789 says it is a passive minority investor and does not call the government.

## Denials and context

- Pentagon: 'outside affiliations, investors, or political connections play absolutely no role in the Department's funding decisions.'
- 1789 Capital says it played no role in the loan and learned of it only when it was public; a Trump Jr. spokesperson says he never spoke to Navarro about Vulcan, and NYT coverage says Trump Jr. claims never to have met or spoken with anyone at Vulcan.
- Vulcan CEO John Maslin told reporters and House staff (2026-04-16) there was no political favoritism; House Democrats say ProPublica's account contradicts this.
- Navarro reportedly called the reporting 'fake news' and defended the startups on supply-chain-resilience grounds.
- Rare-earth magnets are a genuine national-security bottleneck given China's dominance and export controls, and the package drew about $550M in private capital, so there is a policy rationale independent of 1789.
- No evidence has been published that Don Jr. or 1789 lobbied for the loan; the documented intervention is Navarro's.
- ReElement withdrew from its $80M conditional OSC loan process in July 2026.

## Full report

**Verdict: mostly accurate** (confidence: medium-high)

The core facts hold up: Don Jr.'s partnership, the asset and return figures, the August 2025 investment followed by the November 2025 government package, and ProPublica's "call came from the White House" reporting. The video gets a few details loose. It says "right before," when the gap was about three months. It misquotes ProPublica slightly and calls the loan a "contract." Its insinuation that Don Jr. "anticipated" or arranged the deal is not backed by any documented evidence that he was involved.

- 1789 says these are "common sense" investments, that "we stay totally out of that", and that "we don't have to call the government".
- ProPublica: "the call came from the White House. We have to get this deal done", and "Vulcan's was the only deal initiated by a top aide to the president".
- 1789 says it takes "minority positions in growth equity", has no strategic control, and is "simply economic investors".

### Evidence by sub-claim

1. **Don Jr. is a partner at 1789 Capital: confirmed.** Bloomberg reported on November 12, 2024 that he would join 1789 Capital as a partner instead of taking a White House role. The firm was co-founded by Omeed Malik (2022), with Chris Buskirk as co-founder and partner [1]. Axios reports that he does not sit on the investment committee of the growth-equity fund [6].

2. **"Over $1 billion in assets": confirmed, but outdated and understated.** Reuters reported on September 8, 2025 that 1789 had crossed $1 billion in assets [2]. Later reporting puts the figure much higher. Fox Business said the growth fund went from about $200M to about $2B in a year. Secondary summaries of the FT put total assets near $3.5B by 2026, and 1789 closed a $1.2B real-estate fund in August 2026 [5][6][7].

3. **"Over 200 percent" returns: confirmed (approximately).** The New York Times (Maureen Farrell, July 22, 2026) reported that the main fund showed returns of roughly or nearly 200% as of June 30, 2026, helped largely by its Polymarket stake [3][4]. The PitchBook comparison is about 21% for 2023-vintage VC funds. The figure is "nearly/about 200%", not clearly "over". These are paper returns early in the fund's life.

4. **1789 invested in Vulcan Elements, then the government deal followed: confirmed, but "right before" is loose.** 1789 participated in Vulcan's $65M Series A in August 2025, reportedly at about a $200M valuation [8][10]. On November 3, 2025, Vulcan and ReElement announced a $1.4B partnership with the Department of War/Pentagon and Commerce. It included a $620M Office of Strategic Capital (OSC) direct loan to Vulcan, $50M in Commerce CHIPS incentives, an $80M OSC loan to ReElement, about $550M in private capital, and government warrants or equity. Vulcan would build a 10,000-metric-ton magnet plant [9][10][11]. On November 18, 2025, NC Gov. Josh Stein announced the plant's site in Benson (Johnston County), described as a roughly $918M-$1B facility [12]. The quoted "billion dollar ten thousand metric ton... facility enabled by our partnership with the United States government" matches Vulcan's announcement. The investment came about **three months** before the government package, so it was not literally "right before". The OSC loan was the largest that office had made [10].

5. **"Investing in companies that get government contracts", and Don Jr.'s "remarkable skill in anticipating": insinuation.** Several 1789 portfolio companies have since won federal funding. ProPublica also noted that Unusual Machines, a company Don Jr. advises and holds a stake in, was under review for its own Pentagon loan [13]. However, no reporting found documents that Don Jr. had advance knowledge. Vulcan's award was a **loan** with warrants, not a "contract". The video also says "telling the Pentagon, we need a contract", which is the wrong instrument.

6. **ProPublica: "the call came from the White House" and "only deal initiated by a top aide": confirmed, with a small misquote.** ProPublica (Robert Faturechi, May 28, 2026) reported that Peter Navarro personally pushed OSC to fund Vulcan. Navarro is a White House trade adviser and described as a friend of Don Jr. A Pentagon official is quoted: "The call came from the White House: We have to get this done." The video says "this deal done". ProPublica also reported that, of the dozens of companies OSC was considering, Vulcan's was the only deal initiated by a top presidential aide. Staff were pushed to close it in weeks rather than months [13][14][15]. ProPublica says the aide was Navarro. It does **not** report that Don Jr. or 1789 made the call.

7. **1789 says it is a passive minority investor and does not call the government: confirmed as the firm's position.** I found the substance in reporting but could not verify the exact video wording or who spoke it (likely Malik or Buskirk in an interview). 1789 told ProPublica it played no role in Vulcan getting the loan and did not know of the deal before it was public. A Trump Jr. spokesperson said he does not discuss portfolio companies with federal officials and did not speak to Navarro about Vulcan. NYT secondary coverage says Trump Jr. claimed he had never met or spoken with anyone at Vulcan [4][13][14]. Profiles describe 1789 as typically taking minority growth-equity stakes [6].

### Corrections
- The video says 1789 bought "right before" the government got involved. In fact it invested in August 2025, about three months before the November 3, 2025 announcement of the $1.4B package. That package included a $620M Pentagon OSC loan to Vulcan, conditional at first.
- The video calls it a Pentagon "contract". It was a direct loan, plus Commerce CHIPS incentives and government warrants or equity.
- The video quotes ProPublica as "We have to get this deal done". The reported quote is "We have to get this done."
- The video says "over one billion dollars in assets". That was true in September 2025, but by 2026 reporting put 1789 at about $2B-$3.5B+.
- The video says returns were "over 200%". The NYT reported roughly or nearly 200% (as of June 30, 2026), and these are unrealized.
- The person ProPublica says initiated the deal is White House adviser Peter Navarro, not Don Jr. or 1789.

### Denials and context
- The Pentagon said "outside affiliations, investors, or political connections play absolutely no role in the Department's funding decisions" [13].
- 1789 says it played no role. A Trump Jr. spokesperson denies contact with Navarro about Vulcan. Vulcan CEO John Maslin had told reporters and House staff (April 16, 2026) that there was no political favoritism. House Democrats later said ProPublica's account contradicted him [16].
- Navarro reportedly called the reporting "fake news" and defended Vulcan and ReElement on supply-chain-resilience grounds. The White House cited national interest [16].
- Supporting context: rare-earth magnets are a genuine national-security bottleneck, given China's dominance and its 2025 export controls. The deal had bipartisan-style industrial-policy logic, and it included about $550M of private capital.
- Oversight: Sens. Warren, Blumenthal and Kim wrote letters (January and June 2026), and Sen. Schiff wrote to DoD (July 31, 2026). Reps. Huffman and Garcia demanded documents from Vulcan (August 11, 2026). Rep. Raskin opened an inquiry into 1789 (August 26, 2026), to which 1789 "fired back". House Democrats forced a failed vote to subpoena Trump Jr. [16][17][18][19][20][21]. ReElement withdrew from its $80M conditional loan in July 2026 [16].
