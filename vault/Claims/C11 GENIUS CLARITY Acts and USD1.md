# C11: GENIUS / CLARITY Acts and USD1

**Verdict:** Mostly accurate (confidence: medium)

> Trump pushed the GENIUS Act (stablecoin law) and CLARITY Act, including reported night-time calls to lawmakers to secure votes, while his family profits from USD1; a Trump son boasted on X that USD1 is the second-largest GENIUS-compliant stablecoin.

## Summary

Trump publicly pushed the GENIUS Act and lobbied House holdouts personally, including late-night calls that he described himself, while his family's World Liberty Financial issues the USD1 stablecoin. The CLARITY Act passed only the House and failed a Senate cloture vote in September 2026. No X post with the exact boast 'second largest GENIUS compliant stablecoin' could be found. The closest documented posts are Eric Trump's January 2026 post that USD1 passed PYUSD and his September 2025 description of USD1 as 'GENIUS Act compliant'. The 'leg up' framing is interpretation, not a documented causal fact.

## Corrections

- The video pairs the GENIUS and CLARITY Acts as legislation Trump is getting passed. Only GENIUS became law (signed 2025-07-18). CLARITY passed the House 294-134 but failed Senate cloture 49-50 on 2026-09-15.
- Transcript says a Trump son boasted USD1 is 'the second largest GENIUS compliant stablecoin'. No such post was found. Eric Trump's documented January 2026 X post said USD1 had passed PayPal's PYUSD, and by total market cap USD1 was roughly 4th-6th, not 2nd.
- The 'nighttime calls' are sourced mainly to Trump's own remarks at the signing (calls at 2-4 a.m.). Press reports document an evening Oval Office meeting with holdouts on 2025-07-15.
- The write-up previously gave '$57.3M from WLF' as the 2025 disclosure figure; that was the June 2025 filing covering 2024. The filing for calendar 2025 (released 2026-06-30) reports about $526M from WLF token sales.

## Denials and context

- White House: Trump's assets are in a trust managed by his children; 'there are no conflicts of interest.'
- The GENIUS Act was bipartisan: 18 Senate Democrats and 102 House Democrats voted yes, so passage was not Trump's doing alone.
- Democratic amendments to bar the president and his family from profiting from stablecoins were not included. Reporting says the ethics provision covers members of Congress but not the president's family.
- 'GENIUS compliant' is a WLF/Trump-family marketing claim. Implementing rules are not yet effective, and BPI disputes that USD1's reserves would qualify.

## Full report

**Verdict: mostly-accurate (confidence: medium)**

Transcript lines 469-481.

### What the video says
- Trump is "deeply involved" in passing the GENIUS Act and the CLARITY Act, and the legislation directly affects Trump-family businesses.
- "His son boasts on Twitter that USD1 is now the second largest GENIUS compliant stable coin."
- Trump helped shape the GENIUS Act and made public statements supporting it.
- There were "reports of nighttime calls from Donald Trump to essentially solicit votes."
- Meanwhile his family profits from its own stablecoin, USD1. USD1 got "a leg up" because the rules were written around the same time USD1 was created.

### Evidence by sub-claim

1. **Trump pushed the GENIUS Act: confirmed.** After the Senate passed the bill 68-30 on June 17, 2025, Trump posted on Truth Social that the House should move "LIGHTNING FAST" and pass a "clean" GENIUS Act: "Get it to my desk, ASAP — NO DELAYS, NO ADD ONS" [3][4]. He also stepped in personally when 12-13 House Republicans (mostly Freedom Caucus members) voted down the procedural rule 196-223 on July 15, 2025. That evening he announced from the Oval Office that 11 of the 12 holdouts had agreed to back it [5][6][7]. The House passed the bill 308-122 on July 17, and Trump signed it on July 18, 2025 [1][2]. "Shaping the legislation" is supported in a looser sense: the White House pushed for a clean bill, and Democratic amendments that would have barred the president and his family from profiting were not adopted [9][10]. I found no evidence that Trump personally drafted the text.

2. **Trump pushed the CLARITY Act: confirmed, with context.** CLARITY was part of the same "Crypto Week" package. The holdout deal covered the rule for all the crypto bills, and the House passed CLARITY 294-134 on July 17, 2025 [2][11]. The video implies CLARITY was being passed alongside GENIUS. In fact it never became law. It cleared Senate Banking 15-9 in May 2026, then failed a Senate cloture vote 49-50 on September 15, 2026, and it is stalled as of November 2026 [11][12][13].

3. **"Nighttime calls" to get votes: confirmed, mainly from Trump's own words.** News reports document an evening Oval Office meeting (Trump posted shortly before 9 p.m. ET on July 15, 2025) and "multiple conversations with Trump" before the holdouts switched [5][6][7]. At the signing ceremony, Trump himself said he had been making phone calls "at 2:00, 3:00, 4:00 in the morning" and recalled being "about four short at 3:30 in the morning" [8]. So the late-night calls are documented, but the detail about the hour comes from Trump's own account.

4. **The family profits from USD1: confirmed in substance.** World Liberty Financial (WLF) issues USD1. The Trump family held a reported ~60% stake in WLF, and a Trump-affiliated entity was reported to receive 75% of token-sale revenue [9]. Trump's June 2025 financial disclosure (covering 2024) reported about $57.3M from WLF token sales; his disclosure for calendar 2025, released June 30, 2026, reported about $526M more from WLF token sales [21]. USD1 reserve income flows to WLF. The figures for that income and for Binance are covered in C10.

5. **A Trump son boasted on X that USD1 is "the second largest GENIUS compliant stablecoin": unverified as worded.** I could not find a post with that exact wording. The closest documented statements are these:
   - On about January 23, 2026, Eric Trump posted on X that USD1 (~$4.4-4.5B) had overtaken PayPal's PYUSD, calling it "a major milestone" [14][15].
   - In September 2025, Eric Trump called USD1 "the fastest growing stablecoin of all time" and "GENIUS Act compliant" [16].
   
   If you count only US-regulated stablecoins, passing PYUSD would leave USD1 behind only USDC. That makes the paraphrase plausible, but it is a framing. By overall market cap, USD1 ranked around 4th-6th [16][17].
   
   "GENIUS compliant" is a company marketing claim, not a regulatory finding. The Act's rules are not yet in effect, and the Bank Policy Institute (June 2026) argued that USD1's money-market-fund reserves would not meet GENIUS reserve requirements [18].

6. **The rules were written around the time USD1 was created: confirmed on timing.** Sen. Hagerty introduced the GENIUS Act on February 4, 2025. WLF announced USD1, with BitGo as reserve custodian, on March 25, 2025. That was during the bill's drafting and before the Senate committee markup [19][20]. The claim that USD1 got a "leg up" is the video's interpretation. It is plausible given the timing and the Trump-family link, but it is not a documented causal fact.

### Corrections
- The CLARITY Act passed only the House (294-134, July 17, 2025). It failed Senate cloture 49-50 on September 15, 2026 and is not law. Only the GENIUS Act was enacted (signed July 18, 2025).
- I found no evidence of an X post calling USD1 "the second largest GENIUS compliant stablecoin." The documented posts are Eric Trump's January 2026 post that USD1 passed PYUSD and his September 2025 statement that USD1 is "GENIUS Act compliant." By total market cap, USD1 was not second.
- The "nighttime calls" come from Trump's own remarks at the signing. The press documented an evening Oval Office meeting and phone negotiations.

### Denials and other side
- The White House said Trump's assets are in a trust managed by his children and that "there are no conflicts of interest" [9].
- WLF and the Trump family present USD1 as transparent, regulated and GENIUS-aligned.
- The GENIUS Act passed with bipartisan support (18 Senate Democrats, 102 House Democrats), so it was not passed by Trump alone.
- Democrats (Warren, Merkley, Schumer and others) pushed amendments to bar the president and his family from profiting. Those amendments were not adopted. Reporting says the final ethics provision covers members of Congress but not the president's family [9][10].
