# C60: Government equity stakes in critical-minerals companies

**Verdict:** Mostly accurate · medium confidence  
**Type:** Check · Defence, minerals and contracts

## The claim

> When the administration took stakes in or lent to critical-minerals companies, were firms linked to the Trump or Lutnick families positioned to profit?

## Bottom line

The core of the claim is documented. A June 28, 2026 NYT investigation (Lipton and Sonne) counted at least 14 critical-minerals companies tied to the Trump or Lutnick families that had received or were seeking more than $8.9B in federal support (mostly commitments and letters of interest), and Senate Democrats built July 2026 letters on it.

## What holds up

- Reporting (NYT) found Trump-family or Lutnick-family-linked firms held positions in, or advised, a dozen-plus critical-minerals companies that received or sought federal backing.
- 1789 Capital (Don Jr.) invested in Vulcan Elements before its federal loan and equity package.
- Dominari / American Ventures (Trump sons) took a position via Skyline Builders in the Cove Kaz / Kaz Resources tungsten project backed by U.S. financing, and Cantor also profited (mostly accurate)
- Cantor Fitzgerald was banker on USA Rare Earth's private raise alongside the Commerce Department's equity-and-loan deal.
- The Metals Company, a Cantor client, sought deep-sea mining permits from NOAA, which sits under Lutnick's Commerce Department.
- Cantor holds a position in Critical Metals Corp (Tanbreez, Greenland), a company the administration discussed taking a stake in (mostly accurate)

## What needs correcting

- Perpetua Resources, ASP Isotopes and Westwater, all on the NYT list, have Cantor ties and federal support (partly accurate)
- The claim groups MP Materials, Lithium Americas and Intel with the family-linked deals; the NYT list of 14 as reported does not include them and no family-firm position was found. The one later link is Trump's personal portfolio buying Intel shares in Q1 2026, after the government stake (AP, 2026-05-19).
- 'Around a dozen-plus' is the NYT's 'at least 14' companies; the $8.9B is support provided or under consideration (LOIs, conditional loans, warrants), not money disbursed.
- USA Rare Earth's federal package is a non-binding LOI ($277M funding plus $1.3B loan for ~10% equity), with no price floor; Cantor's role was the parallel $1.5B private PIPE, not the government deal.
- Kaz Resources' $1.6B is non-binding EXIM and DFC letters of interest; the Trump sons' stake is indirect, via a Skyline SPV, and not named in SEC filings.
- The Metals Company's connection is a NOAA permit process, not federal funding.

## What is unproven

- The administration's equity stakes in MP Materials, Lithium Americas, Trilogy Metals and Intel are among the deals where Trump- or Lutnick-linked firms were positioned to profit.
- Family-linked firms made documented gains from the government actions.
- Family members or Lutnick influenced the federal decisions.

## In more detail

- The strongest instances are 1789/Vulcan, Dominari/American Ventures via Skyline-Kaz Resources (with Cantor raising $210M for a project partner), and Cantor as lead placement agent on USA Rare Earth's $1.5B PIPE alongside a $1.6B Commerce LOI.
- The Cantor-TMC and Cantor-Critical Metals links are also real.
- But MP Materials, Lithium Americas and Intel are not on the reported list and no family tie to them was found.
- Family gains are largely unquantified, and no evidence shows influence; Commerce, the White House, Cantor and the Trump family deny wrongdoing.
- Audit update: Trump's own accounts bought Intel shares in early 2026 after the government stake (AP, May 2026), a personal-portfolio link rather than a family firm in the deal; Cantor's role in American Ocean Minerals' >$150M placement is documented, while ties for Energy Fuels, Titan and Guardian Metal remain unverified.

## Sub-claims

### 1. Reporting (NYT) found Trump-family or Lutnick-family-linked firms held positions in, or advised, a dozen-plus critical-minerals companies that received or sought federal backing.

**Confirmed**

- Confirmed as reporting. A New York Times investigation by Eric Lipton and Paul Sonne (published 2026-06-28, syndicated by the Boston Globe) counted at least 14 companies with financial ties to the Trump or Lutnick families.
- The government had provided, or was considering providing, more than $8.9B in support to them, and some had permit applications before Commerce.
- Secondary coverage gives the 14 as Kaz Resources, USA Rare Earth, Perpetua Resources, ASP Isotopes, Critical Metals, Vulcan Elements, The Metals Company, Trilogy Metals, American Ocean Minerals, Guardian Metal Resources, Titan Mining, Westwater Resources, ReElement/American Resources Corp. and Energy Fuels.
- The Times reported that Cantor Fitzgerald, now run by Lutnick's sons, frequently served as adviser, underwriter or placement agent.
- On 2026-07-30/31, Sens. Blumenthal, Schiff and Warren cited the $8.9B and 14-deal figures in letters to DoD, DOE, Commerce, Interior and EXIM, plus a separate letter to OSC on Vulcan.
- The $8.9B is mostly commitments, letters of interest and conditional loans, not disbursed money.
- Amounts: more than $8.9B; $8.9B
- Dates: 2026-06-28; 2026-07-30
- Sources: [5](https://www.bostonglobe.com/2026/06/28/world/trump-cut-billion-dollar-mining-deal-his-sons-stand-profit/) [9](https://x.com/DanLinnaeus/status/2072232463716208784) [1](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/31/senate-democrats-trump-lutnick-critical-mineral-deals.html) [2](https://www.blumenthal.senate.gov/newsroom/press/release/blumenthal-schiff-and-warren-raise-concerns-about-trump-administration-directing-taxpayer-dollars-toward-critical-mining-companies-linked-to-trump_lutnick-families) [3](https://www.blumenthal.senate.gov/imo/media/doc/07-30-26_letter_from_sens_blumenthal_schiff_warren_to_dod_doe_doc_doi_and_export-import_bankpdf.pdf) [8](https://www.sej.org/headlines/trump-s-sons-stand-profit-critical-minerals-arms-race)

### 2. 1789 Capital (Don Jr.) invested in Vulcan Elements before its federal loan and equity package.

**Confirmed**

- This is covered in C26 and not repeated in full here.
- 1789 joined Vulcan's $65M Series A in August 2025.
- On 2025-11-03 a $1.4B package followed, including a $620M OSC conditional loan, $50M in Commerce CHIPS money and government warrants/equity.
- ProPublica reported that White House aide Peter Navarro initiated the deal.
- 1789 and Don Jr. deny any role, and no reporting shows foreknowledge.
- Amounts: $65M; $1.4B; $620M; $50M
- Dates: August 2025; 2025-11-03

### 3. Dominari / American Ventures (Trump sons) took a position via Skyline Builders in the Cove Kaz / Kaz Resources tungsten project backed by U.S. financing, and Cantor also profited.

**Mostly accurate**

- This is covered in C47. The FT (May 2026) and NYT (June 2026) reported that the sons bought into Skyline in August 2025 through a Dominari-linked SPV.
- That was before Tokayev reportedly told Trump on 2025-09-22 that Cove Kaz would get the project, and before the deal was signed on 2025-11-06.

- SEC filings do not name the brothers, the FT found no evidence of foreknowledge or influence, and a Trump family lawyer called the NYT report 'libelous'.
- Amounts: 20%; up to $900M; about $700M; about $400M; $210M
- Dates: May 2026; June 2026; August 2025; 2025-09-22; 2025-11-06

### 4. Cantor Fitzgerald was banker on USA Rare Earth's private raise alongside the Commerce Department's equity-and-loan deal.

**Confirmed**

- On 2026-01-26 USA Rare Earth announced a non-binding Commerce CHIPS letter of intent for about $1.6B: $277M in federal funding and a $1.3B senior secured loan.
- The government would receive 16.1M shares and about 17.6M warrants, roughly 10% (8-16% depending on warrant exercise).
- The LOI had no price floor.
- The same day USAR announced a $1.5B common-stock PIPE at $21.50 a share, anchored by Inflection Point.
- Cantor Fitzgerald, chaired by Brandon Lutnick, was lead placement agent and Moelis was co-agent.
- USAR said Cantor was not involved in the government transaction.
- On 2026-02-26 Warren, Wyden and Van Hollen wrote to Lutnick, also alleging that billionaire Trump allies got access to PIPE shares.
- That is an allegation, and the specific investors are not confirmed here.
- Commerce reportedly did not answer by the 2026-03-11 deadline.
- Cantor's fee is not public in the sources found.
- Amounts: about $1.6B; $277M; $1.3B; roughly 10%; 8-16%
- Dates: 2026-01-26; 2026-02-26; 2026-03-11
- Sources: [16](https://www.eenews.net/articles/us-takes-equity-stake-invests-1-6b-in-rare-earths-miner/) [17](https://www.mining.com/?p=1197844) [18](https://pulse2.com/usa-rare-earth-1-6-billion-deal/) [19](https://www.fortune.com/2026/01/26/trump-admin-buys-stake-usa-rare-earth-wave-deals-critical-minerals) [12](https://www.warren.senate.gov/newsroom/press-releases/warren-wyden-van-hollen-sound-alarm-on-lutnick-familys-conflicts-of-interest-involving-new-commerce-rare-earth-deal/) [13](https://www.warren.senate.gov/imo/media/doc/warren_letter_to_secretary_lutnick_re_usar_critical_minerals_deal.pdf) [14](https://www.wyden.senate.gov/news/press-releases/wyden-presses-trumps-commerce-secretary-on-conflict-of-interest-after-familys-firm-brokers-investment-in-critical-minerals) [15](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/02/26/warren-lutnick-usa-rare-earth-usar-critical-minerals.html)

### 5. The Metals Company, a Cantor client, sought deep-sea mining permits from NOAA, which sits under Lutnick's Commerce Department.

**Confirmed**

- Cantor Fitzgerald is reported as one of TMC's leading bankers and financial advisers.
- TMC said in March 2025 it would seek U.S. licences, filed its applications under the Deep Seabed Hard Mineral Resources Act within about a week of Trump's April 2025 seabed-mining executive order, and refiled under NOAA's streamlined rules in January 2026.
- NOAA certified an exploration application, the first since Trump took office, and Earthjustice and conservation groups sued in 2026.
- No commercial recovery permit had been reported issued.
- Whether Lutnick recused himself from TMC matters was not established.
- This is a permit, not money, so it presumably counts toward the 14 but not the $8.9B.
- Amounts: $8.9B
- Dates: March 2025; April 2025; January 2026
- Sources: [23](https://news.mongabay.com/2025/03/deep-sea-miner-tmc-seeks-u-s-approval-potentially-bypassing-global-regulator/) [25](https://www.engineeringnews.co.za/article/deep-sea-mining-company-seeks-international-permit-under-new-trump-rules-2026-01-23) [24](https://earthjustice.org/press/2026/lawsuit-challenges-trump-administration-greenlighting-of-international-deep-sea-mining-exploration-permit)

### 8. The administration's equity stakes in MP Materials, Lithium Americas, Trilogy Metals and Intel are among the deals where Trump- or Lutnick-linked firms were positioned to profit.

**Unverified**

- The government actions are real.
- In July 2025 the DoD bought $400M of MP Materials convertible preferred stock (conversion at $30.03, about 15%, making it the largest shareholder), plus warrants, a 10-year $110/kg NdPr price floor and offtake, and a planned $150M loan.
- In October 2025 DOE took warrants for about 5% of Lithium Americas plus 5% of the Thacker Pass JV with GM when restructuring its $2.26B loan (a $435M drawdown and $182M of deferred repayments).
- On 2025-08-22 the government agreed to take 433.3M Intel shares (9.9%) for $8.9B at $20.47 a share, funded from unpaid CHIPS ($5.7B) and Secure Enclave ($3.2B) grants, plus a conditional 5% warrant; the stake is passive.
- On 2025-10-06 the government took about 10% of Trilogy Metals for $35.6M, with warrants that could lift it to about 17.5%, alongside Ambler Road permits; Trilogy shares roughly tripled.
- But MP Materials, Lithium Americas and Intel do not appear on the NYT list of 14 as reported, and this research found no reported Trump- or Lutnick-family position in them.
- Trilogy is on the NYT list, but the nature of its family tie could not be confirmed from accessible sources.
- Treat the claim's grouping of these stakes with the conflicted deals as unsubstantiated except for Trilogy.
- The MP, Lithium Americas and Intel terms are stated from widely reported 2025 deal announcements and were not re-sourced in this check.
- Audit addition: one Trump-family position in Intel has since been reported.
- AP (2026-05-19) reported that Trump's own first-quarter 2026 periodic transaction report showed Intel purchases (one secondary source dates one to 2 March 2026), months after the government took its stake; a Trump spokesperson said third-party managers have 'sole and exclusive' authority over the portfolio.
- This is a post-stake portfolio holding by Trump himself, not a family firm positioned in the deal, and no MP Materials or Lithium Americas holding by the family was found.
- Amounts: $400M; $30.03; about 15%; $110; $150M
- Dates: July 2025; October 2025; 2025-08-22; 2025-10-06; 2026-05-19

## Denials and context

- White House spokesman Kush Desai: 'the only special interest guiding the Trump administration's decision-making is the best interest of the American people.'
- Commerce: neither Lutnick nor anyone at the department 'has interacted with or had any discussions whatsoever with Cantor Fitzgerald' regarding the rare-earth companies.
  - Lutnick says he divested Cantor; critics note his stake went to trusts and adult sons who run the firm.
- Cantor says it merely serves as a partner for companies raising capital in critical minerals; USA Rare Earth said Cantor was not involved in the government transaction.
- 1789 Capital and Don Jr. deny any role in the Vulcan loan (C26); a Trump family lawyer called the NYT Kaz report 'libelous' and demanded a retraction (C47).
- Context cutting the other way: the administration has done many mineral deals (one tally is about 160 deals and $40B), so overlap with a dominant critical-minerals banker such as Cantor is partly structural.
  - The national-security rationale for reducing China dependence is bipartisan.
- Source limits: the original pages at NYT/Boston Globe, CNBC, Senate sites and most outlets were not read directly, and the research ended before MP Materials, Lithium Americas, Intel, Energy Fuels, Titan, Guardian and American Ocean Minerals could be checked individually.
  - Those items rely on search-result summaries.

## Full report

**Verdict: mostly-accurate** (confidence: medium)

### Summary

The core of the claim is documented. On 2026-06-28 the New York Times published an investigation by Eric Lipton and Paul Sonne (syndicated by the Boston Globe). It counted **at least 14 critical-minerals companies** with financial ties to the Trump or Lutnick families that had received, or were being considered for, **more than $8.9B** in federal support. Senators Blumenthal, Schiff and Warren built their 2026-07-30/31 letters on those findings.

The best-documented instances:
- **1789 Capital and Vulcan Elements**
- **Dominari / American Ventures and the Kazakh tungsten project**, through Skyline Builders and Kaz Resources. Cantor also reportedly raised $210M for a partner in the project.
- **Cantor as lead placement agent on USA Rare Earth's $1.5B private raise**, announced the same day as a $1.6B Commerce letter of intent.

Cantor's links to The Metals Company and Critical Metals are also real.

The claim overreaches in two places:
- **MP Materials, Lithium Americas and Intel are not on the reported list of 14.** No family-firm position in them was found. (Audit: Trump's own accounts bought Intel shares in Q1 2026, after the government stake, per AP.)
- **Family gains are largely unquantified, and no evidence shows influence over any decision.** The White House, Commerce, Cantor and the Trump family deny wrongdoing.

### The claim

The claim says the administration took equity stakes in, or gave loans or price floors to, MP Materials, Lithium Americas, Trilogy Metals, Vulcan Elements, Intel, USA Rare Earth and others. It says reporting (NYT, FT, Bloomberg) links Trump-family or Lutnick-family firms, as investors or advisers, to a dozen or more of those companies. The firms named are 1789 Capital, Cantor Fitzgerald, and Dominari/American Ventures (through Skyline and Cove Kaz).

### Corrections

- MP Materials, Lithium Americas and Intel are not among the 14 reported companies, and no family tie to them was found through family firms; Trump's personal accounts did buy Intel shares in Q1 2026, after the government stake (AP, 2026-05-19).
- "Dozen-plus" means "at least 14". The $8.9B is support provided or under consideration, not disbursed.
- USA Rare Earth's federal package is a non-binding letter of intent with no price floor. Cantor's role was the parallel private placement.
- Kaz Resources' $1.6B consists of non-binding EXIM and DFC letters of interest. The sons' stake in it is indirect.
- The Metals Company's link is a NOAA permit, not federal money.
