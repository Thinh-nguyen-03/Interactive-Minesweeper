# C73: Qatar's Ties to People in & Around the Administration

**Verdict:** Mostly accurate · medium confidence  
**Type:** Connection check · Pardons, Lobbyists & Access

## The Claim

> Which people in or around the administration have documented Qatari financial or client ties, and what decisions on Qatar did they take part in?

## Bottom Line

Most of the Qatari ties in the claim are documented. They include Bondi's Ballard FARA work, Patel's embassy consulting plus a March 2025 waiver and Dec 2025 FBI-Qatar agreements, Kerik's April 2025 contract, QIA money in Kushner's Affinity and the WBD bid, the QIA Park Lane purchase, Barrack's Colony-QIA history and his attendance at UCC's Syria signing, the Qatari Diar licence, and a Qatari royal's $50M Newsmax stake.

## What Holds Up

- Pam Bondi was a registered Qatar agent at Ballard and then signed the DOJ memo clearing the jet, which is still unreleased.
- Kash Patel consulted for Qatar's embassy, got a March 2025 waiver for Qatar matters, and signed FBI security agreements in Doha in Dec 2025.
- EPA Administrator Lee Zeldin consulted for Heritage Advisors, the fund of Qatari royal Sheikh Sultan bin Jassim Al Thani, which also invested $50M in Newsmax and was the Qatari investor in the Menendez-Daibes bribery case (mostly accurate)
- Qatar's embassy hired pardoned Trump ally Bernard Kerik's firm in April 2025.
- Jared Kushner's Affinity takes QIA money, sought more from Qatar while acting as envoy, and co-backed the Paramount bid for WBD alongside QIA.
- Envoy Tom Barrack's firm partnered with Qatari state funds for years, and as envoy he presided over deals for Qatar's UCC Holding (mostly accurate)
- The Trump Organization licensed its name to a golf and villa project with QIA's Qatari Diar.
- Qatari money reached Newsmax and other conservative media around Trump (mostly accurate)
- US decisions benefiting Qatar in 2025-26: the security-guarantee order, the Idaho facility, Al Udeid commitments and arms sales.

## What Needs Correcting

- Chief of staff Susie Wiles's former firm Mercury represented Qatar (partly accurate)
- An Apollo-run trust partly owned by Qatar put $100M into the Witkoff Group in 2025 (misleading)
- Claimed: an Apollo trust partly owned by Qatar put $100M into the Witkoff Group. A relayed correction says the lender was Apollo Global Management, not the Qatar-invested subsidiary (a $100M golf-resort refinancing).
- Claimed: Kerik hired Apr 2025. Correct, but he died on 29 May 2025, so the engagement lasted under two months.
- Al Udeid: the May 2025 fact sheet gives a $38B statement of intent (potential investments), not a firm commitment and not $10B.
- C64 says Patel would recuse from Qatar work for a year. His Nov 2024 pledge had expired, and a March 2025 waiver allowed participation.
- The security EO is dated 29 Sep 2025 and was published 1 Oct 2025; the claim's 'Oct 2025' refers to publication.

## What Is Unproven

- Ballard Partners has a current Qatar embassy contract.
- The jet's retrofit cost and provenance tie back into the network.

## In More Detail

- Also documented: Zeldin's consulting for the same royal's Heritage Advisors, the fund in the Menendez-Daibes case.
- The US decisions (security EO, Idaho facility, $38B Al Udeid statement, arms sales up to the $4.5B KC-46A) are documented.
- Weaker than claimed: the Witkoff $100M Qatar-trust link was corrected (the lender was Apollo itself), Wiles's Mercury-Qatar tie predates her, and a current Ballard-Qatar contract is unverified.
- No source shows any tie buying a decision; the timing overlaps are our inference.

### 1. Pam Bondi was a registered Qatar agent at Ballard and then signed the DOJ memo clearing the jet, which is still unreleased.

**Confirmed**

### 2. Kash Patel consulted for Qatar's embassy, got a March 2025 waiver for Qatar matters, and signed FBI security agreements in Doha in Dec 2025.

**Confirmed** · Evidence: Documented

- Patel's Trishul LLC listed the Qatari embassy as a client, and he did not register under FARA (C64).
- A waiver issued on 4 Mar 2025 by the FBI's ethics official covers 'a particular matter' related to Qatar; the public filing does not say what that matter is (Responsible Statecraft; Jewish Insider).
- His Nov 2024 one-year recusal pledge had expired the month before he signed bilateral security agreements in Doha (Dec 2025), which the FBI did not announce.
- Public Citizen asked OGE to rescind the waiver, and a separate FARA complaint is unresolved.
- The nature of his Qatar work is still undisclosed.
- Dates: 4 Mar 2025; Nov 2024; Dec 2025
- Sources: [16](https://jewishinsider.com/2025/12/fbi-kash-patel-qatar-security-deals-lobbying-disclosure/) [17](https://responsiblestatecraft.org/kash-patel-qatar/)

### 3. EPA Administrator Lee Zeldin consulted for Heritage Advisors, the fund of Qatari royal Sheikh Sultan bin Jassim Al Thani, which also invested $50M in Newsmax and was the Qatari investor in the Menendez-Daibes bribery case.

**Mostly accurate**

- No source links these threads, and no EPA decision touching Qatar was found.
- Amounts: $5,000; about $50M; $1M
- Dates: December 2023; 2025-02-11; Jan 2024; 2019-20

## Denials & Context

- Qatar and QIA say investment decisions are separate from diplomacy.
  - QIA denies any 666 Fifth role, and Brookfield denies Qatari involvement.
- Apollo says Qatar-linked investors do not direct its trust; the White House called the NYT Witkoff story a 'bogus smear'.
- DOJ says career ethics officials cleared Bondi's role in the jet memo, which remains unreleased.
- Newsmax denies softening Qatar coverage; Carlson denies taking Qatari money.
- Hegseth says the Idaho facility is not a Qatari base, and the plan dates to 2022.
  - The MQ-9B request predates Trump, and the 2026 sales followed the Iran war.
- Qatar's lobbying is legal and disclosed under FARA; a documented tie is not proof of influence.
- The Witkoff family threatened the NYT with a defamation suit over the Sept 2025 story; Qatar's International Media Office says Qatar declined to invest in Alex Witkoff's fund pitch.

### What was checked

Map every documented Qatari financial or client tie to people now in or around the administration. Set those ties against the US decisions that benefited Qatar (the jet, the security-guarantee executive order, arms sales, Al Udeid, the Idaho facility), with dates and evidence grades.

#### 2. Kash Patel: Qatar embassy consulting, a waiver, then FBI-Qatar security deals
**Grade: documented.**

**Correction to C64:** C64 says he "would recuse from related work for one year". The pledge was made in November 2024, and the waiver already allowed him to take part.

#### 3. Lee Zeldin: consulting for a Qatari royal's fund that ties into Newsmax and the Menendez case
**Grade: each link documented; the chain is our inference.**

**(a) Zeldin's consulting.** Zeldin's 2025 nominee disclosure (OGE 278e) lists **Heritage Advisors** (London) as a consulting client of Zeldin Strategies. NYT, ICIJ and The Real Deal identify it as the "Qatari Investment Co." in the superseding Menendez indictment (January 2024). Prosecutors alleged that Menendez steered that fund to invest with developer **Fred Daibes**, partly by taking pro-Qatar actions. Neither the Sheikh nor Qatar was accused.

**(c) Heritage Advisors and Newsmax.** The same Sheikh Sultan invested about **$50M in Newsmax in 2019-2020** (Washington Post/ICIJ, March 2024).

**(d) Daibes and the pardon market.** Daibes later paid Keith Schiller's Javelin Advisors **$1M in Q1 2025** in a failed bid for a pardon (C64). Javelin also worked for Pakistan.

**What this shows and does not show.** One Qatari royal's fund links an EPA administrator, a leading pro-Trump cable network, and a convicted senator's co-defendant, who then bought access to the Trump pardon market. No source connects these threads to each other. We found no EPA decision touching Qatar.

#### 7. Steve Witkoff: Park Lane and the corrected $100M
