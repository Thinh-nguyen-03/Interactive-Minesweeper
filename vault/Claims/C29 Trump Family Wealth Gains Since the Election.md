# C29: Trump Family Wealth Gains Since the Election

**Verdict:** Mostly accurate · medium confidence  
**Type:** Check · Family Ventures & Wealth

## The Claim

> Do Trump's financial disclosures show crypto as his largest income source, at about $1.4 billion of $2.2 billion, and how much richer have Eric, Donald Jr. and Barron Trump become while most retail buyers lost money?

## Bottom Line

Trump's 2025 OGE disclosure (released June 30, 2026) shows crypto as his largest income source.

## What Holds Up

- Per his financial disclosure, Trump made about $1.4B from crypto out of about $2.2B, more than from any other business.
- Per Forbes, Eric Trump has gotten 10x richer since the election.
- Per Forbes, Don Jr. has gotten six times richer.
- Per Forbes, Barron Trump is worth about $150M.

## What Needs Correcting

- The ventures that made the family billions have lost money for most of their actual American investors (partly accurate)
- **Claimed:** About $1.4B from crypto  
  **The record shows:** That is the Reuters/NYT/Bloomberg count, and it holds. The AP's figure of about $1.2B appears to leave out a roughly $197M Stablecoin Holdco (USD1) line; Bloomberg initially reported $1.2B and corrected to $1.4B when it added that line. All of these figures are revenue, not profit.
- **Claimed:** $2.2B total  
  **The record shows:** That is the NYT's minimum figure; Forbes estimated about $2.4B.
- The Forbes figures for the sons are from December 2025 and the Barron figure is from Sept/Oct 2025. They predate American Bitcoin's further collapse, so they likely overstate current values.
- **Claimed:** Most 'American' investors lost money  
  **The record shows:** The loss data covers wallets worldwide and does not show nationality.
- The World Liberty component was given as a contested $594M-$800M range. The filing resolves it (2026-11-16): $592.4M on DT Marks DEFI's lines ($526,810,321 token proceeds plus $65,625,000 WLF Holdco equity sale) and $789.3M including DT Marks SC's $196,875,000 from Stablecoin Holdco. The reconciliation earlier marked as our inference is confirmed by the primary document.
- 2026-11-16 (disclosure audit, C96/C97): Claimed: the World Liberty and meme-coin amounts are income 'Trump' made. The record shows: the World Liberty and stablecoin amounts are on the lines of DT Marks DEFI and DT Marks SC, which are 70% Trump's and 30% unnamed family members'; the form does not say whether they are entity totals or his share. The $635.1M meme-coin royalties go to CIC Digital, wholly his trust's, and are licence income, not coin sales.

## What Is Unproven

- This may have been one of Trump's most profitable years ever.

## In More Detail

- Reuters, the NYT and Bloomberg count more than $1.4B from crypto within at least $2.2B total; the AP counted about $1.2B, apparently before a Stablecoin Holdco (USD1) line of about $197M was added.
- The filing itself resolves the apparent dispute over the World Liberty share: $592.4M on DT Marks DEFI's lines ($526.8M of token proceeds plus $65.6M from a WLF Holdco equity sale), or $789.3M including $196.9M to DT Marks SC from Stablecoin Holdco, the USD1 business; outlets' 'about $594M' and 'almost $800M' are these two classifications.
- The lines belong to entities 30% owned by unnamed family members, and the form does not say whether the amounts are entity totals or Trump's share.
- TRUMP coin licensing added about $635M. The Forbes figures are real but dated: Eric about $400M and 10x (Dec 2025), Don Jr. about $300M and 6x (Dec 2025), Barron about $150M (Oct 2025).
- Most TRUMP coin, WLFI and ABTC buyers are underwater.
- The filing shows revenue, not profit, so 'most profitable year' cannot be verified, and the loss data does not show investors were specifically American.

## Sub-Claims

### 1. Per his financial disclosure, Trump made about $1.4B from crypto out of about $2.2B, more than from any other business.

**Confirmed**

- The 927-page 2025 OGE filing was released 2026-06-30.
- The more-than-$1.4B crypto total holds: Reuters and the NYT report it, and Bloomberg corrected its own headline from $1.2B to $1.4B after adding a Stablecoin Holdco LLC line (about $196.9M from new capital contributions and the sale of units) that it had first left out.
- The World Liberty figures differ only in classification, as the filing itself shows (C96, C97): token-sale proceeds distributed by WLF to DT Marks DEFI total $526,810,321 (lines 124.3-124.12; The Block's $236.25M is only the cash line 124.3), the WLF Holdco equity sale $65,625,000 (line 124.2) and the Stablecoin Holdco proceeds to DT Marks SC $196,875,000 (line 114).
- $592.4M counts DT Marks DEFI only (Bloomberg, CNBC and NOTUS's 'about $594M' adds $1.8M of staking rewards); $789.3M adds DT Marks SC (Reuters and the NYT's 'almost $800M', of which $262.5M from selling interests).
- Both entities are 70% owned through Trump's companies (DTTM Operations 69% plus a 1% member corporation) and 30% by unnamed 'Trump Family Members', and the form does not say whether the amounts on these lines are the entities' totals or Trump's share.
- About $635M came from TRUMP meme-coin royalties via CIC Digital.
- $592.4M + $196.9M + $635.1M is $1.424B. NYT: at least $2.2B total income, up from a minimum of $622M for 2024.
- Forbes: about $2.4B. AP: nearly $1.2B from crypto.
- Real estate, golf and resorts brought in about $500-575M, so crypto was the largest category.
- The form reports many entries as ranges, and all these figures are revenue/income, not profit.
- Amounts: $1.4B; $1.2B to $1.4B; about $196.9M; $526,810,321; $236.25M
- Dates: 2026-06-30

**Partly accurate**

- TRUMP coin: Chainalysis counted 813K losing wallets (about $2B lost) in the first 19 days and about 764K losing wallets later.
- Nansen, cited by the NYT, counted about 989K wallets in loss (about $3.8B) by June 2026, with the token down about 97% from its peak.
- WLFI hit record lows of about $0.055-0.077 in April-May 2026, more than 80% below its peak.
- ABTC fell 75-95% from its Sept 2025 debut.
- So most retail buyers did lose money.
- Wallet data does not show nationality, however, so the 'American' framing is unsupported, and some revenue streams (licensing, USD1) did not involve retail losses.

**Verdict: mostly-accurate (confidence: medium)**

### The claim

- The president made more from crypto than from any of his other businesses: about $1.4 billion out of about $2.2 billion.
- This "may have been" one of his most profitable years ever.
- According to Forbes, Eric Trump has become 10 times richer since the election and Don Jr. six times richer, and Barron Trump has an estimated net worth of $150 million.
- Many of the investments that made the Trump family so much money have lost money for most of their actual American investors.

### Method note

Direct fetches of every primary outlet I tried failed in this environment: Bloomberg Law, Forbes (.com and .com.au), The Block, The Daily Beast and GV Wire. Everything below comes from search-result snippets that agree across several outlets: Reuters syndications, AP syndications, NYT-sourced reports, Forbes headlines and URLs, CoinDesk, Fortune and Decrypt. I did not read the OGE filing (Form 278e, 927 pages) itself.

### Sub-claims

#### 1. Trump's disclosure shows about $1.4B from crypto out of about $2.2B, and crypto was his biggest business. **Confirmed, with a caveat**
- Trump's annual OGE disclosure covering calendar 2025 was released on **June 30, 2026**. It is about 927 pages.
- **Reuters**, the NYT and Bloomberg all report **more than $1.4B** in crypto income. Bloomberg first reported $1.2B, then corrected its headline to $1.4B after adding a **Stablecoin Holdco LLC** (USD1) line of about **$196.9M** that its first count left out.
- The **World Liberty share** differs between outlets only in classification, as the filing itself shows (C96, C97):
  - **DT Marks DEFI:** $526,810,321 of token-sale proceeds distributed by WLF (lines 124.3-124.12; The Block's $236.25M is only the cash line 124.3) plus $65,625,000 from selling WLF Holdco equity (line 124.2) = **$592.4M**. That is Bloomberg, CNBC and NOTUS's "about $594M" (with $1.8M of staking rewards).
  - **Adding DT Marks SC:** $196,875,000 from new members of Stablecoin Holdco, the USD1 business (line 114), gives **$789.3M**. That is Reuters and the NYT's "almost $800M", including $262.5M from selling interests.
  - Both entities are about 70% Trump's and 30% unnamed family members'. The form does not say whether the amounts are entity totals or Trump's share.
- The rest of the breakdown:
  - about **$635M from TRUMP meme-coin licensing/royalties**, through CIC Digital and Celebration Coin.
- The **AP** got "**nearly $1.2B**" from crypto businesses. That figure appears to leave out the Stablecoin Holdco line. The total still holds: $592.4M + $196.9M + $635.1M is $1.424B.
- The **NYT** put total 2025 income at **at least $2.2B**, more than triple the minimum of $622M reported for 2024. **Forbes** estimated about $2.4B.
- Crypto was the largest category. Real estate, golf and resorts brought in about $500-575M.
- Caveat: these numbers are revenue/income as the form reports it. The form does not show profit.

#### 2. "This may have been one of his most profitable years ever." **Plausible but unverifiable as worded**
- Forbes said the year was about three times the roughly $760M Trump took in for 2024, and the Daily Beast called it his "most lucrative year." Several outlets noted the haul tops his entire first term's income.
- However, the AP and NYT both point out that the disclosure gives **revenue, not profit**. "Most profitable" therefore cannot be shown from the filing. The claim's hedge ("may have been") is fair.

#### 3. Per Forbes, Eric Trump is about 10x richer since the election. **Confirmed (as of Dec 2025)**
- Forbes (Kyle Mullins, **Dec 4, 2025**) ran the headline "Eric Trump has gotten 10 times richer since dad's election." It put him at about **$400M**.
- His largest holding was his American Bitcoin stake, about $160M in early Dec 2025. The rest is mainly World Liberty tokens and USD1-related value.
- Eric disputed parts of Forbes' valuation but offered no alternative figure.
- The estimate is now dated. ABTC later fell 87-95% from its debut peak, so his current figure is probably lower.

#### 4. Per Forbes, Don Jr. is about 6x richer. **Confirmed (as of Dec 2025)**
- Forbes (Dec 8, 2025) ran the headline "How Donald Trump Jr.'s fortune jumped six-fold in a year." It put him at about **$300M**, six times his November 2024 level.
- Forbes noted he had been near $500M in September 2025, before American Bitcoin's stock fell.

#### 5. Per Forbes, Barron Trump is worth about $150M. **Confirmed (Forbes estimate, Sept/Oct 2025)**
- Forbes' family tally (Sept 22, 2025) and the article "How 19-Year-Old Barron Trump Is Worth $150 Million" (Oct 6, 2025) both give this figure.
- It assumes Barron holds about 10% of the roughly $1.5B that WLF added to the family fortune. Forbes heavily discounted his locked tokens.
- It is an estimate, not a disclosed figure.

#### 6. Most of the actual investors in these ventures lost money. **Mostly accurate for the main ventures; "American" is not established**
- **TRUMP coin:**
  - Chainalysis/NYT counted 813,294 wallets that lost about $2B in the first 19 days. A later Chainalysis/CNBC count found about 764K losing wallets, while 58 wallets made more than $10M each.
  - A Nansen analysis cited by the NYT found 988,905 wallets in loss, about $3.8B combined, by June 2026. The token was down about 97% from its peak.
- **WLFI:** the token hit record lows of about $0.055-0.077 in April-May 2026, more than 80% below its September 2025 peak of about $0.46.
- **American Bitcoin (ABTC):** down 75% by January 2026 and 87-95% from its Sept 2025 debut later in the year. A Forbes-cited estimate puts retail losses at about $500M.
- Wallet data does not reveal nationality, so the claim that these were "American" investors is not supported. Some ventures, such as USD1 and the family's licensing income, did not involve retail buyers losing money.

### Corrections
- The $1.4B crypto figure is the Reuters/NYT/Bloomberg count, and it holds. The AP's about $1.2B appears to omit the roughly $197M Stablecoin Holdco line. All of these figures are revenue, not profit.
- The World Liberty component is $592.4M on DT Marks DEFI's lines or $789.3M including DT Marks SC. The earlier "contested $594M-$800M" was resolved from the filing on 16 Nov 2026. See sub-claim 1.
- (16 Nov 2026) These amounts sit on the lines of entities that are 30% owned by unnamed family members. The form does not say whether they are entity totals or Trump's share. The $635.1M is licence royalties to CIC Digital, not coin sales.
- The $2.2B is a minimum figure from the NYT tally. Forbes estimated about $2.4B.
- The Forbes figures for the sons date from Dec 2025 (Eric and Don Jr.) and for Barron from Sept/Oct 2025. They predate ABTC's deeper slide, so current values are probably lower.
- The investor-loss data covers wallets worldwide, not specifically "American" investors.

### Denials / other side
- White House spokesperson Anna Kelly said neither the president nor his family "has ever engaged, or will ever engage, in conflicts of interest."
- The White House also said Trump's assets are "in a trust managed by his children," or held in "fully discretionary accounts managed by independent third-party financial institutions."
- The Trump Organization called the filing "one of the most comprehensive financial disclosure reports ever submitted." World Liberty Financial declined to comment.
- Critics note that Trump remains the beneficiary of the trust. Former acting OGE head Don Fox said every post-Watergate president managed his finances "as though he were subject to conflicts of interest." Trump has not done so.
- Eric Trump disputed Forbes' valuation.
