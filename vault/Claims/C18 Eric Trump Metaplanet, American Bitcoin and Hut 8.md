# C18: Eric Trump: Metaplanet, American Bitcoin and Hut 8

**Verdict:** Mostly accurate (confidence: medium)

## What the video said

> Eric Trump became a strategic board adviser to Japanese bitcoin-treasury company Metaplanet; Eric and Don Jr. co-own American Bitcoin, 80% owned by Hut 8, which contributed its mining equipment for that 80% with no known cash changing hands; CEO Matt Prusak told Wired the Trumps brought capital-markets access and 'narrative'; the Trump family is linked to about five crypto treasury companies; American Bitcoin stock has performed poorly.

## Follow-up checks

## Summary

Every core fact checks out: Eric Trump's Metaplanet advisory role (March 21, 2025), the Trump brothers' stake in American Bitcoin, the 80% equity Hut 8 got for contributing its miners in a cashless deal, Prusak's 'capital markets and narrative' quote to Wired, and the stock's collapse. The framing goes too far in suggesting Hut 8 gave away its whole mining business. Hut 8 kept its energy and data-center assets, controls and consolidates American Bitcoin, and earns colocation fees from it. The 'five treasury companies' count is a rough but defensible estimate.

- The transcript's 'Asher' is Asher Genoot, CEO of Hut 8.
- The video says Hut 8 gave away '100% of your Bitcoin mining firm'. Hut 8 actually contributed its miner fleet, kept its energy and data-center business, earns colocation and managed-services fees, and controls and consolidates American Bitcoin.
- After the Gryphon merger, 80% is Hut 8's approximate voting power rather than exactly its economic stake.
- Metaplanet's body is a 'Strategic Board of Advisors', not its board of directors.

## Denials and context

- Hut 8 and American Bitcoin present the structure as a way to raise and deploy capital for mining without straining Hut 8's balance sheet.
- Eric Trump blamed the December 2025 drop on pre-merger investors taking profits, said he would not sell, and called the company's fundamentals 'virtually unmatched'.
- No specific regulatory favour to American Bitcoin is documented. The conflict-of-interest point is inference from the president's crypto policy role, not proof of a quid pro quo.

## Full report

**Verdict: mostly accurate** (confidence: medium-high)

### What the video says (transcript lines 569-607)
- Eric Trump became a "strategic board advisor" to the Japanese bitcoin company Metaplanet as Trump took office and called himself the "crypto president".
- Eric and Don Jr. are part of / part owners of American Bitcoin, which is 80% owned by Hut 8, "an energy infrastructure company".
- The Trump family is linked to "like five" bitcoin/crypto treasury companies (Molly White).
- American Bitcoin is a treasury company and supposedly a miner, made public through a merger.
- Hut 8 gave its mining equipment to American Bitcoin for an 80% share, with no cash known to have changed hands. The video asks "why would you give away 100% of your Bitcoin mining firm".
- CEO Matt Prusak told Wired that the Trump brothers brought two things: access to capital markets and "narrative".
- Eric Trump: "We do have the goal to be the biggest... Asher and I talk about that all the time." ("Asher" is Hut 8 CEO Asher Genoot.)
- American Bitcoin's stock has performed poorly.

### Evidence by sub-claim

1. **Eric Trump and Metaplanet: confirmed.** On March 21, 2025, Tokyo-listed Metaplanet named Eric Trump the first member of its newly formed Strategic Board of Advisors. That was about two months after the January 20 inauguration. Reuters, CoinDesk, Decrypt, The Block and Cointelegraph all reported it [1][2][3][4]. The body is an advisory board, not Metaplanet's board of directors. The video's wording ("strategic board advisor") is close enough.

2. **Eric and Don Jr. co-own American Bitcoin, and Hut 8 owns 80%: confirmed, with nuance.** In February 2025 Dominari Holdings announced American Data Centers Inc. (ADC), formed with Eric Trump and Donald Trump Jr. Both brothers sit on Dominari's board or advise it [5][6]. On March 31, 2025, Hut 8 contributed its ASIC miners to ADC in exchange for newly issued shares equal to 80% of ADC's equity. ADC was renamed American Bitcoin, and the Trump-linked ADC holders kept 20% [7][8][9]. After the Gryphon Digital Mining merger closed and the company listed on Nasdaq as ABTC on September 3, 2025, Hut 8 held a majority of the stock and about 80% of the voting power [10][11]. Its post-merger economic stake is not exactly 80%, so "80% owned" is accurate as of launch and as a voting figure afterwards. Eric Trump is co-founder and chief strategy officer. Bloomberg estimated his stake at about 7.5% at listing [12].

3. **Hut 8 as an "energy infrastructure company": confirmed.** Hut 8 calls itself an "energy infrastructure platform", with Power, Digital Infrastructure and Compute segments [13].

4. **Mining equipment for 80%, no known cash: largely confirmed, but the framing overstates it.** Hut 8's 8-K and press release describe a contribution of "substantially all" of its ASIC miners, reportedly about 61,000 machines, in exchange for newly issued equity. Reports call the deal cashless or all-stock [7][6]. No cash payment from the Trump side has been reported. However, Hut 8 did not give away "100% of its mining firm." It kept its power, data-center and energy assets, and it became American Bitcoin's exclusive colocation and managed-services provider, so it earns fees [7][13]. Hut 8 also consolidates American Bitcoin's results, because it controls the company. The Gryphon merger was all-stock. American Bitcoin raised capital separately through private placements in 2025.

5. **Prusak's quote to Wired: confirmed (via secondary sources).** Jessica Klein's Wired piece, "The Inside Story of Eric Trump's American Bitcoin" (2025), quotes Prusak as saying the Trump brothers brought "two things to the table": access to capital markets and "narrative" [14]. I could not fetch the wired.com original from this environment. The quote is consistent across reposts and search summaries. Prusak was CEO at launch. He later became president and interim CFO, and left on August 4, 2026 to join Giga Energy [15][16].

6. **"About five" Trump-linked treasury companies: plausible, but only approximate.** Documented links include:
   - American Bitcoin (Eric and Don Jr., co-owners)
   - Trump Media & Technology Group (about $2B bitcoin treasury; the president is the largest shareholder)
   - ALT5 Sigma (WLFI-token treasury tied to World Liberty Financial; Zach Witkoff named chairman)
   - Thumzup Media (Don Jr. took a stake in July 2025; multi-coin treasury and Dogecoin mining via Dogehash)
   - Metaplanet (Eric is an adviser, not an owner) [17][18][19][20]

   That makes five, though the links range from ownership to an advisory role. I found no published Molly White article listing exactly five, so the count is the interviewee's rough estimate.

7. **Stock performance poor: confirmed.** ABTC jumped at its September 3, 2025 debut and then fell. It dropped about 40% on December 2, 2025 when the private-placement lockup expired. Eric Trump said he would not sell. Bloomberg put the decline at about 70% from peak by December 2025 [21][22][23]. By mid-2026 reports put it more than 90-95% below its peak.
