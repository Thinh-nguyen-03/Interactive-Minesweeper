# C114: Trump Family & Associates: Business in Israel

**Verdict:** Partly accurate · medium confidence  
**Type:** Check · Foreign Deals

## The Claim

> What business ties do the Trump family and associates have in Israel, and where is there none?

## Bottom Line

Documented: the Trump name was licensed for an abandoned Ramat Gan tower (2006-08); Eric Trump led preliminary hotel talks with Israeli developer Nitsba in 2023 and spring 2025 (nothing signed, he said 'no plans at this time'); and in Feb 2026 Eric Trump became a strategic investor in the merger of Israeli drone maker XTEND with JFB Construction (closed 3 Sep 2026), alongside Unusual Machines, which Donald Trump Jr. advises.

## What Holds Up

- Trump Organization / Trump family licensing or development projects in Israel have existed, but none has been built or has paid Trump.
- The Trump Organization held talks in 2023-25 on branded hotels in Jerusalem and Tel Aviv, led by Eric Trump; none was signed.
- Eric Trump invested in Israeli drone maker XTEND in its $1.5B merger with JFB Construction Holdings (announced 17 Feb 2026), alongside Unusual Machines, which Donald Trump Jr. advises.

## What Needs Correcting

- XTEND holds US government contracts and Israeli government ties, creating a federal-contract link to a Trump son (partly accurate)
- Globes (2017) describes the 2006 Ramat Gan buyers as 'Crescent Heights and the Trump group'; Globes (2008) and Ynet describe Trump as the name licensor (25% of unit sales) who sued Crescent Heights after it flipped the lot. We treat Trump as licensor, not buyer or owner of the site; the sources conflict.
- C105's statement that no Saudi or Israeli dealing was found for Witkoff stands; this check also found none.
- C98's foreign licensing total ($63.1M) contains no Israeli line; the Israeli trademark registrations in the same filing carry no income.
- **Claimed:** Eric Trump is a direct investor in XTEND  
  **The record shows:** The company's February 2026 release names him as a strategic investor; XTEND's CEO says (3 Sep 2026) he is not a direct investor and his exposure is through American Ventures. Capital at closing is reported as $152M (Globes), $110M or $100M (other reports).

## What Is Unproven

- Trump's 2025 financial disclosure shows an Israeli entity or Israel-related income (nothing found; Israeli trademark registrations only).
- Israeli investors or buyers in World Liberty Financial, Trump Media (including its TAE merger) or American Bitcoin.
- Israeli firms with deals involving close associates (Lutnick's Cantor, 1789 Capital, Dominari, Musk).
- Steve Witkoff and his family have business dealings in Israel.
- Donald Trump Jr. visited Israel or made statements about doing business there.
- Any US policy decision toward Israel was taken because of these Trump business interests.

## In More Detail

- XTEND's Nov 2025 Pentagon contract predates his investment, and no influence on any award is documented.
- Trump's 2025 disclosure shows no Israeli entity or income, only Israeli trademark registrations.
- Nothing was found for Israeli buyers in World Liberty, Trump Media or American Bitcoin, for Witkoff or his family, for Don Jr. visits or statements, or for Cantor, 1789 Capital or Dominari deals with Israeli firms.
- Audit note: XTEND's CEO says Eric Trump holds his interest through American Ventures, not directly, and has no board seat; reports differ on how much capital was raised at closing.

## Sub-Claims

### 1. Trump Organization / Trump family licensing or development projects in Israel have existed, but none has been built or has paid Trump.

**Confirmed**

- Grade: documented, with the key facts old. (1) Ramat Gan, 2006-08: Globes (2008, read in full) reports Trump licensed the 'Trump Plaza' name to Crescent Heights (Sonny Kahan) for a 70-storey tower on the former Elite chocolate-factory lot, with Trump to receive 25% of each unit sold plus royalties.
- Crescent Heights paid about $44M and resold the lot to Azorim (Shaya Boymelgreen) for about $80M. Trump sued in Manhattan in 2008; Ynet reports a $45M claim that was rejected by the court.
- The tower was never built. (2) Netanya hotel and Ashkelon golf park (Ynet, 2016, excerpt): both stayed 'on paper'; Trump's lawyers wrote to Ashkelon's mayor expressing interest. (3) Ynet also reports a Trump Organization sales office in Israel opened by Ivanka Trump to market US apartments.
- No Israeli project has been completed or is documented as having paid Trump.
- Amounts: 25%; about $44M; about $80M; $45M
- Dates: 2006-08
- Sources: [6](https://en.globes.co.il/en/article-1000342879) [7](https://en.globes.co.il/en/article-1001189541) [5](https://www.ynetnews.com/business/article/4879842)

### 2. The Trump Organization held talks in 2023-25 on branded hotels in Jerusalem and Tel Aviv, led by Eric Trump; none was signed.

**Confirmed**

- Grade: documented by the New York Times (Oct 2024 and June 2025), read only through reproductions and syndication. (1) Oct 2024: talks in 2023 with Israeli firm Nitsba and with Lockwood-linked partners on the Haleom Hotel on the former Foreign Ministry site in Jerusalem, and on the 47-storey Sarona tower in Tel Aviv near IDF headquarters; Eric Trump toured Israel in February 2023 (met the Dan Tel Aviv manager) and told the Times 'The deal absolutely would have gotten done if not for Oct. 7'.
- Lockwood told the Times the Trump campaign called off talks weeks before the war. (2) June 2025 (Times, via Yeshiva World News, read in full): preliminary spring-2025 talks with Nitsba Holdings on branding and managing the Sarona Hotel (800 rooms); Eric Trump said 'Israel has always been a market we would love to explore, but we have no plans at this time, and any discussions have been strictly preliminary.'
- Nitsba chairman Haim Tsuff said the brand could help win approval for 12 extra residential storeys.
- Nitsba did not respond to the Times.
- The Times also reported Eric toured the Princess Hotel in Eilat and passed. (3) No signing, announcement or later news was found through 17 Nov 2026.
- Dates: Oct 2024; June 2025; February 2023; Oct. 7; 17 Nov 2026
- Sources: [2](https://lockwoodhotels.com/press/trump-family-business-eyes-hotel-deals-in-israel) [3](https://www.thedailybeast.com/trump-organization-looking-into-new-hotel-deals-in-israel) [4](https://www.theyeshivaworld.com/news/israel-news/2419075/trump-tower-tel-aviv-trump-organization-eyes-tel-avivs-tallest-hotel-for-luxury-branding-deal.html)

### 4. Eric Trump invested in Israeli drone maker XTEND in its $1.5B merger with JFB Construction Holdings (announced 17 Feb 2026), alongside Unusual Machines, which Donald Trump Jr. advises.

**Confirmed** · Evidence: Documented

- The JFB/XTEND press release (SEC Exhibit 99.1) names Eric Trump as a strategic investor alongside Unusual Machines, American Ventures, Protego Ventures (described as Israel-based), Aliya Capital and Agostinelli Group; reported commitments of $152M with $42M funded at signing; implied valuation $1.5B; Stefan Passantino (first-term deputy White House counsel) joined JFB's board (Manufacturing Dive, read in full; Snopes, read in full).
- XTEND is Tel Aviv-based; it opened a US headquarters in Tampa in July 2025.
- Bloomberg (via DroneXL, read in full) estimates the stake held through American Ventures, a vehicle listing Donald Jr. and Eric as backers, at about $340M of an overall roughly $750M paper portfolio across XTEND, Powerus and Unusual Machines; this is a single-outlet estimate and Eric Trump's own dollar amount was not found.
- The merger closed on 3 Sep 2026 (Pulse 2.0, read in full) with $110M total capital, listing on the NYSE as XTND on 4 Sep, Dominari Securities as JFB's placement agent.
- Eric Trump has said he believes drones are a better investment than newspapers.
- XTEND did not respond to Al Jazeera's request for comment; no response from the Trump family on conflicts was found beyond that.
- Audit additions (17 Nov 2026): the 17 Feb 2026 announcement and the $152M commitments ($42M at signing) are confirmed by a second outlet (Globes, Al Jazeera, JNS excerpts; the SEC exhibit itself was not readable).
- Details disputed between outlets: IntelliNews says the private investment was cut from $152M to $100M while Globes still cites $152M, and the $110M total capital is from one trade report; treat the amounts at closing as unreconciled.
- Investor names vary in spelling between outlets (Aliya or Alaya Capital).
- Eric Trump's role: the company's February release names him as a strategic investor, but XTEND's CEO told DroneXL (3 Sep 2026) that Eric Trump 'is not a direct investor in XTEND and holds no board seat', that his exposure runs through American Ventures, and that there is 'no connection' between XTEND and Eric Trump beyond a meeting during due diligence; the stake estimate of about $340M (Bloomberg) is a single-source estimate of paper value, not a disclosed amount.
- The merger closing (3 Sep 2026) and NYSE trading (4 Sep, ticker XTND) are confirmed by Globes, Calcalist and DroneXL.
- Amounts: $152M; $42M; $1.5B; about $340M; roughly $750M
- Dates: July 2025; 3 Sep 2026; 4 Sep; 17 Nov 2026; 17 Feb 2026
- Sources: [14](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2024306/000149315226006957/ex99-1.htm) [8](https://www.manufacturingdive.com/news/eric-trump-15-billion-jfb-xtend-ai-merger-construction-drone-defense/812526/) [9](https://www.snopes.com/fact-check/eric-trump-drone-company-iran-war/) [10](https://dronexl.co/2026/03/13/trump-family-750-million-drone-portfolio) [12](https://pulse2.com/xtend-and-jfb-complete-business-combination-with-110-million-in-total-capital/)

### 5. XTEND holds US government contracts and Israeli government ties, creating a federal-contract link to a Trump son.

**Partly accurate**

- Grade: contract documented by the company and trade press; any link to the sons' role is inferred.
- Manufacturing Dive (full) reports an undisclosed multi-million, firm-fixed-price Department of Defense (War) award in Nov 2025 for close-quarters one-way-attack drone kits, a place among 25 vendors in the Drone Dominance Program, a 2020 US Army Special Forces Skylord project, and a partnership page with Israel's Ministry of Defense that has since been deleted.
- DroneXL/Bloomberg adds that XTEND was not among the 11 firms in Gauntlet I, and that in March 2026 it began mobilising fabrication equipment at the request of Israel's Ministry of Defense.
- The Nov 2025 award predates Eric Trump's Feb 2026 investment.
- Nothing documents that either son influenced an award.
- The same Bloomberg report records Unusual Machines and Donald Trump Jr.'s representatives saying he played no role in securing its separate Army award.
- Snopes notes it could not establish what the brothers knew about the Feb 2026 US-Israeli strikes on Iran.
- Dates: Nov 2025; March 2026; Feb 2026
- Sources: [8](https://www.manufacturingdive.com/news/eric-trump-15-billion-jfb-xtend-ai-merger-construction-drone-defense/812526/) [10](https://dronexl.co/2026/03/13/trump-family-750-million-drone-portfolio) [9](https://www.snopes.com/fact-check/eric-trump-drone-company-iran-war/)

- Eric Trump (to the Times, June 2025): 'Israel has always been a market we would love to explore, but we have no plans at this time, and any discussions have been strictly preliminary.'
  - In Oct 2024 he said the deal 'absolutely would have gotten done if not for Oct. 7'.
- Lockwood Development Partners told the Times that the Trump campaign called off the Jerusalem negotiations weeks before the war.

**Question:** What business ties do the Trump family and associates have in Israel or with Israeli companies, and where is there none?

### Summary

Three things are documented; most of the rest is "nothing found".

- **Past licensing project (never built):** in 2006-08 the Trump name was licensed to Crescent Heights for a 70-storey "Trump Plaza" tower in Ramat Gan, with Trump to receive 25% of unit sales. The developer flipped the site and Trump sued. Nothing was built.
- **Hotel talks, nothing signed:** Eric Trump held talks on a Jerusalem hotel and the Sarona tower in Tel Aviv in 2023, and preliminary talks with Nitsba on Sarona in spring 2025. He told the Times in June 2025: "we have no plans at this time". No later news was found as of 17 Nov 2026.
- **An Israeli company with a Trump son as investor:** on 17 Feb 2026 Eric Trump was named a strategic investor in the merger of Tel Aviv-founded drone maker XTEND with JFB Construction Holdings. Unusual Machines, which Donald Trump Jr. advises, and American Ventures (backers include both sons) were co-investors. The merger closed on 3 Sep 2026 and the company trades on the NYSE as XTND. XTEND won a Pentagon contract in Nov 2025, before Eric Trump invested. Nothing documents any influence on an award.

Not found: any Israeli entity or income in Trump's 2025 disclosure (only six Israeli trademark registrations); Israeli investors in World Liberty Financial, Trump Media (including the TAE merger) or American Bitcoin; Israeli dealings by Steve Witkoff or his family; any visit or business statement on Israel by Donald Trump Jr.; Israeli deals tied to Cantor, 1789 Capital or Dominari.

These are private-sector activities of the president's family. No source found shows a deal with the Israeli government, and no authority has made any finding.
