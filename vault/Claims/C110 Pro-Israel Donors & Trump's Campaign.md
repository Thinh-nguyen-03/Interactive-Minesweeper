# C110: Pro-Israel Donors & Trump's Campaign

**Verdict:** Mostly accurate · medium confidence  
**Type:** Check · Foreign Deals

## The Claim

> What did the largest pro-Israel donors give to Trump's campaign and allies, and what has he said about them?

## Bottom Line

The money is documented in FEC records. Miriam Adelson, an American donor, gave about $106M to the pro-Trump super PAC Preserve America in 2024 (17 itemised gifts of $1M or more to Republican committees that cycle total $142M).

## What Holds Up

- Miriam Adelson (an American donor, born in Israel) gave about $100M or more to Preserve America PAC and other pro-Trump and Republican committees in 2024.
- Adelson has continued to give in 2025-26, including to Trump's own super PAC, and her family foundation is among the largest ballroom donors.
- Trump has said on the record that Adelson gave his campaign $250M, and Reuters reports he credited Adelson and her husband with shaping his Israel decisions.
- AIPAC's super PAC, United Democracy Project, spent heavily in 2024 and 2026, on whom.
- Trump has commented on the pro-Israel lobby's influence over Congress.

## What Needs Correcting

- Other large American donors also gave to Trump-aligned committees or the ballroom: Singer and Lauder are identified with pro-Israel causes (Singer funds UDP; Lauder leads the World Jewish Congress); Perlmutter is included only as a large Trump donor and is not shown to be a pro-Israel donor (partly accurate)
- Documented requests from donors to Trump or his team: Adelson's public third-term appeal and her reported behind-the-scenes role on the hostage deal (partly accurate)
- The Massie primary shows pro-Israel groups and Trump working together against a Republican critic of Israel (partly accurate)
- An aggregator page (PowerMap) says Adelson gave $120M in 2024, one $95M gift, and $25M to the 2025 inauguration. FEC rows show $106M to Preserve America in six gifts in 2024 (the $95M figure is the Q3 total of four gifts); the inauguration figure reported by news outlets is $1M. We disregarded that page.
- A search snippet attributed 'Paul Singer $1 million' and 'Adelson $1 million' to the 2025 inaugural committee; the CBS article behind part of that snippet is about the 2017 inauguration (Sheldon Adelson $5M, Singer $1M). The 2025 Adelson $1M comes from press summaries of the committee filing and was not verified against the FEC line.
- Trump's statement that Adelson gave his campaign 'indirectly and directly $250 million' is his own figure and not supported by one filing total; FEC shows about $106M to Preserve America and $142M across Republican committees in 2024, plus about $67M so far in 2025-26.
- Audit (17 Nov 2026): the first draft said C86 and C64 list the Adelson family foundation as a ballroom donor without amounts; neither file mentions Adelson. The Washington Post list puts the foundation at $25M committed (single-source), now recorded in both older checks.

## What Is Unproven

- Adelson made her 2024 giving conditional on Trump backing West Bank annexation.
- Any agreement tying donor money to US policy toward Israel is documented.

## In More Detail

- In the 2025-26 cycle she has given $25M to MAGA Inc. and $40M to the Senate and House leadership funds.
- The Adelson Family Foundation is reported to have committed $25M to the White House ballroom.
- Paul Singer, a major funder of the AIPAC super PAC, gave $5M to MAGA Inc. and $2.5M to Preserve America in 2024.
- Trump has said on the record that Adelson gave his campaign 'indirectly and directly $250 million' (his figure, not a filing total) and, in his own account, that she offered another $250M for a third term; Reuters reports he credited her and her husband at the Knesset with earlier Israel decisions (2017-2019).
- AIPAC's super PAC (United Democracy Project, UDP; an American committee) spends mainly in congressional primaries, mostly against or for Democrats, not on presidential races; in 2026 its largest single Republican target was Rep. Thomas Massie, whom Trump also opposed.
- No document shows an agreement tying any donation to a policy.
- The only report of such a condition (annexation, 2024) was denied by Adelson's spokesman, and Trump publicly said in October 2025 that annexation 'won't happen'.

## Sub-Claims

### 1. Miriam Adelson (an American donor, born in Israel) gave about $100M or more to Preserve America PAC and other pro-Trump and Republican committees in 2024.

**Confirmed**

- Evidence grade: documented (FEC itemised receipts, 2023-24 cycle, queried directly; all dates are in 2024).
- Preserve America PAC (pro-Trump super PAC): $5M (3 May), $25M (26 Jul), $25M (13 Aug), $25M (9 Sep), $20M (17 Sep), $6M (28 Oct) = $106M. The FEC list also shows, from the same donor: Senate Leadership Fund $15M (10M on 3 May, 5M on 20 Sep); Congressional Leadership Fund $10M (5M, 4M, 1M); RJC Victory Fund $5M (9 Aug); 170 Freedom Milwaukee 2024 Host Committee (the Republican National Convention host committee) $3M; Truth and Courage PAC $2M; Hardworking Americans Inc. $1M. The 17 gifts of $1M or more total $142M. Contributions to Trump's campaign committee itself are capped at a few thousand dollars and were not retrieved.
- Press reports of 'about $95M in the third quarter, $100M year-to-date' match the FEC rows (the $6M followed on 28 Oct).
- One aggregator page that gives $120M and a single $95M gift does not match FEC data and was disregarded.
- Preserve America is a super PAC and legally cannot coordinate with the campaign.
- Amounts: $5M; $25M; $20M; $6M; $106M
- Dates: 2023-24; 3 May; 26 Jul; 13 Aug; 9 Sep
- Sources: <https://api.open.fec.gov/v1/schedules/schedule_a/ (contributor_name=Adelson, Miriam; two_year_transaction_period=2024)> [5](https://www.factcheck.org/2024/08/preserve-america-pac-2) <https://forward.com/fast-forward/665095/miriam-adelson-donates-100m-to-trump-campaign-making-good-on-reported-promise/> [13](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/5/18/massie-race-breaks-spending-record-as-pro-israel-groups-target-trump-critic)

### 2. Adelson has continued to give in 2025-26, including to Trump's own super PAC, and her family foundation is among the largest ballroom donors.

- Preserve America PAC itself gave $750,000 to MAGA KY on 27 Jun 2025 (FEC); its 2025 donors were not retrieved.

### 3. - Ronald Lauder (World Jewish Congress president): no 2024 gift of $250K or more to a Trump committee in the FEC pull; he reportedly gave MAGA Inc. $5M in March 2025 (Daily Beast, Revolving Door Project) and was reported in August 2026 to have stopped giving to Republicans.

- Not checked: Haim Saban, Marc Rowan, Blair Frank (UDP's largest donors) and their Trump-side giving, because FEC rate limits cut the search short.
- Amounts: $5M; $2.5M; $419,600; about $25M; $13M

### 4. Trump has said on the record that Adelson gave his campaign $250M, and Reuters reports he credited Adelson and her husband with shaping his Israel decisions.

**Confirmed**

- Evidence grade: documented (on-record remarks reported by Reuters, the Daily Beast and others; no official transcript fetched). (1) Knesset, Jerusalem, 13 Oct 2025 (Reuters): Trump told the Knesset 'She loves Israel', and Reuters reports he credited the Adelsons with his 2017 Jerusalem recognition, the 2018 embassy move and the 2019 Golan sovereignty recognition (Reuters' paraphrase; no official transcript was fetched), said 'Stand up, Miriam', recalled that the couple would regularly call and visit the Oval Office, and, gesturing at her, said 'She's got $60 billion in the bank', adding 'I think she is saying, "No, more,"'.
- Other outlets quote him asking her 'what do you love more, the United States or Israel?', saying she refused to answer and 'That might mean Israel' (wording varies between outlets). (2) White House Hanukkah reception, 16 Dec 2025 (Daily Beast, 17 Dec): Trump praised her for giving his campaign 'indirectly and directly $250 million' ('she was number one' in other reports).
- The $250M figure is Trump's own and is not reconciled with filings: FEC rows show about $106M to Preserve America and $142M across Republican committees in 2024. (3) (not re-verified in the audit) August 2024 at Bedminster: Trump called the Medal of Freedom 'much better' than the Medal of Honor in remarks that referred to Adelson's 2018 medal.
- These remarks describe access and, per Reuters, credit for earlier decisions; they do not describe an agreement tying money to a decision.
- Amounts: $60 billion; $250 million; $250M; about $106M; $142M
- Dates: 13 Oct 2025; 16 Dec 2025; 17 Dec; August 2024
- Sources: [7](https://theprint.in/world/trump-salutes-mega-donor-miriam-adelson-for-help-shaping-us-decisions-on-israel/2763153) [8](https://www.thedailybeast.com/megadonor-miriam-adelson-offers-donald-trump-250m-to-run-for-third-term) [24](https://www.al-monitor.com/originals/2025/10/trump-salutes-mega-donor-miriam-adelson-help-shaping-us-decisions-israel) <https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2025/10/13/who-is-miriam-adelson-the-pro-israel-donor-trump-lauded-at-the-knesset> <https://www.casino.org/news/trump-suggests-miriam-adelson-loves-israel-more-than-us/>

### 5. Documented requests from donors to Trump or his team: Adelson's public third-term appeal and her reported behind-the-scenes role on the hostage deal.

**Partly accurate**

- Evidence grade: documented for what was said in public; single-source for anything private.
- At the 16 Dec 2025 Hanukkah reception Adelson told Trump: 'I met Alan Dershowitz and he said the legal thing about four more years, and I said, "Alan, I agree with you. We can do it!"
- Think about it' (Daily Beast); outlets (Mediaite, JWeekly) quote Trump saying she offered him 'another $250 million' for a run; that sum rests on Trump's own account and Adelson's reported words were 'Think about it'.
- The Constitution's 22nd Amendment limits a president to two elected terms and Trump has previously said he does not plan a third.
- This is a public appeal about his candidacy, not about Israel policy.
- Reuters reports that 'a senior U.S. official' (unnamed) said Adelson had a behind-the-scenes role in urging Trump to keep pressing for the return of the hostages held by Hamas.
- A Spanish-language JPost tag headline says she pressed him on the hostage deal; the underlying article was not found.
- No filing, FARA record or on-record statement from Trump's office documents any specific policy ask from Adelson, Singer, Lauder or other donors named here, apart from Trump's own general remark that the Adelsons called and visited.
- Amounts: $250 million
- Dates: 16 Dec 2025
- Sources: [8](https://www.thedailybeast.com/megadonor-miriam-adelson-offers-donald-trump-250m-to-run-for-third-term) [25](https://mediaite.com/politics/trump-says-miriam-adelson-offered-him-another-250-million-to-run-for-a-third-term) [7](https://theprint.in/world/trump-salutes-mega-donor-miriam-adelson-for-help-shaping-us-decisions-on-israel/2763153)

### 6. Adelson made her 2024 giving conditional on Trump backing West Bank annexation.

**Unverified** · Evidence: Single-source allegation, denied

- In 2024, Haaretz (drawing on a New York magazine profile) reported that Adelson wanted Israeli annexation of the West Bank and US recognition of sovereignty, and other outlets (972 Magazine, The New Arab, Revolving Door Project) wrote that her support was reportedly tied to it.
- Her spokesman, Andy Abboud, denied the report that she was urging Trump to publicly support annexation in exchange for her backing (a New York Times report, seen only as a reposted copy).
- Trump never made such a commitment publicly, and in a Time interview of 15 Oct 2025 (published 23 Oct) he said annexation 'won't happen' because he 'gave my word to the Arab countries' and that Israel would lose all US support if it did it; Vance called the Knesset preliminary vote a 'stupid' stunt.
- This outcome is evidence against a delivered quid pro quo but does not prove the 2024 report false.
- Adelson gave very large sums regardless, and Reuters reports Trump credited the couple at the Knesset with earlier decisions (2017-2019); no source ties those decisions to a particular payment.
- Dates: 15 Oct 2025; 23 Oct
- Sources: [18](https://newarab.com/news/pro-israel-mogul-wants-west-bank-annexed-after-trump-donation) [19](https://forward.com/fast-forward/618034/miriam-adelson-funding-trump-israel?amp=1) <https://iowa.forums.rivals.com/threads/the-pro-israel-donor-with-a-100-million-plan-to-elect-trump.445819> [20](https://www.dawn.com/news/1950799) <https://timesofisrael.com/israel-would-lose-all-support-from-the-us-if-it-annexes-west-bank/>

### 7. AIPAC's super PAC, United Democracy Project, spent heavily in 2024 and 2026, on whom.

**Confirmed**

- Evidence grade: documented (FEC totals pulled directly; FactCheck.org read in full; Intercept and Sludge as excerpts).
- UDP is an American super PAC created in Jan 2022 by AIPAC; AIPAC PAC (separate) gives candidates up to $5,000 per election.
- 2024 cycle: FEC totals for UDP are receipts $87.2M and disbursements $61.4M (re-pulled in the audit); FactCheck puts $34.8M of its independent expenditures on Democratic House candidates.
- Headline races: Rep. Jamaal Bowman (NY-16), more than $14.5M in the primary (Sludge, search excerpt; about $9.9M against Bowman and $4.8M for George Latimer per FactCheck 2024), and Rep. Cori Bush (MO-1), about $5.2M against plus $3.3M for Wesley Bell.
- Intercept counts UDP at $41.4M direct in 2024 races.
- 2026 cycle (FEC, to 14 Oct 2026, re-pulled in the audit): receipts $112.8M (contributions $108.6M), disbursements $85.1M, independent expenditures $53.6M, plus $13.1M to other federal committees.
- Intercept: AIPAC (direct contributions) $53.3M and UDP $66.7M, with spending in 333 of 473 seats; more than $30M on the Michigan Senate Democratic primary (against Abdul El-Sayed or for Haley Stevens; El-Sayed won).
- FactCheck (Aug 2026): about $5M for Melissa Conyears-Ervin (IL-7, lost 17 Mar), over $2M against Tom Malinowski (NJ-11 special primary, lost 5 Feb), more than $1M for Adrian Boafo (MD-5, won 23 Jun), over $3M against Massie (KY-4) plus about $500,000 for Gallrein.
- Politico reported that over 40% of UDP's primary money was routed through pop-up and pass-through PACs.
- UDP's largest 2026 donors include Paul Singer, Blair Frank, Marc Rowan and Haim Saban.
- It is bipartisan and mostly congressional; no UDP spending for or against Trump himself was found in the sources read (its presidential filings were not searched).
- Amounts: up to $5,000; $87.2M; $61.4M; $34.8M; more than $14.5M
- Dates: Jan 2022; 14 Oct 2026; Aug 2026; 17 Mar; 5 Feb
- Sources: <https://api.open.fec.gov/v1/committee/C00799031/totals/ (cycle 2026)> [4](https://www.factcheck.org/2026/08/united-democracy-project-3/) [15](https://theintercept.com/2026/10/29/aipac-israel-midterms-spending-elections-congress/) [6](https://www.factcheck.org/2024/09/united-democracy-project-2/) [16](https://readsludge.com/2024/06/20/no-group-has-ever-spent-as-much-as-aipac-has-in-the-bowman-latimer-primary/)

### 9. Trump has commented on the pro-Israel lobby's influence over Congress.

**Confirmed**

- Evidence grade: documented in outlet reports (full transcripts not fetched; wording varies).
- In a late August 2025 Daily Caller interview Trump said Israel once had the strongest lobby in Congress of any body he had seen and 'Today, it doesn't have that strong a lobby', adding that he was 'a little surprised'.
- At a Hanukkah event in December 2025 he repeated that it was no longer the strongest in Congress, and in an August 2026 interview (Real America's Voice) he said pro-Israel groups have lost influence compared with 20 to 25 years earlier.
- He has not been recorded here describing AIPAC's spending as help to him.
- AIPAC criticised the US-Iran deal in June 2026 (per search excerpt), so its interests and the administration's do not always align.
- Dates: late August 2025; December 2025; August 2026; June 2026

### 10. Any agreement tying donor money to US policy toward Israel is documented.

**Unverified**

- Not documented. No filing, court record, FARA registration, official statement or on-record account shows an agreement, condition or payment-for-policy between Adelson, Singer, Lauder, Perlmutter, AIPAC or UDP and Trump or his team.
- What exists is (a) FEC proof of large American donations, (b) Trump's public remarks crediting Adelson with influence and access, (c) a 2024 report of an annexation condition that her spokesman denied and that Trump's later public position contradicts, (d) overlap in time between donations and decisions, which does not establish cause.
- Contributions were by American citizens to US committees; none of the donors is an agent of the Israeli government on the record we found, and FARA records were not searched for Adelson-linked entities.
- The responses of Adelson, Singer, Lauder and the White House to the ballroom and 2026 giving were not found beyond Abboud's 2024 denial.

## Denials & Context

- Adelson's spokesman Andy Abboud denied the 2024 report that she was urging Trump to support West Bank annexation in exchange for her backing (reposted NYT text; original not read).
  - In August 2024 CNBC reported Abboud telling Bedminster attendees that whatever Trump needed from Adelson he would get.
- Preserve America is an independent-expenditure super PAC; coordination with the campaign is illegal and none is alleged in the sources read.
- AIPAC and UDP describe themselves as nonpartisan and focus on candidates who support the US-Israel relationship; most of their spending is in House and Senate primaries, and UDP's 2024 independent expenditures went mainly to Democratic House races.
  - UDP's spokesman Patrick Dorton called Massie 'the most anti-Israel Republican in the House'.
  - AIPAC called Massie 'one of the most consistently hostile voices in Congress toward the U.S.-Israel relationship' (Intercept).
- Trump's own comments in 2025-26 say the lobby's influence has declined, and he told Time in Oct 2025 that West Bank annexation 'won't happen'.
- These are American citizens and US political committees giving under US law.
  - This check does not concern Israeli government money.

### Summary

- **Miriam Adelson** (American physician, Israeli-born, majority owner of the Dallas Mavericks) gave **$106M** to the pro-Trump super PAC Preserve America in six gifts in 2024 (FEC, queried directly). Counting all her gifts of $1M or more to Republican committees in 2024 the total is **$142M**. In the 2025-26 cycle the FEC shows $25M to MAGA Inc. (5 May 2026), $30M to the Senate Leadership Fund and $10M to the Congressional Leadership Fund (both 11 Mar 2026), about $66.8M in all.
- The **Adelson Family Foundation** (a US foundation based in Needham, Massachusetts, whose website says its purpose is to strengthen the State of Israel and the Jewish people) is reported on a Washington Post list of donor records (via the Boston Globe, 16 Oct 2026) to have **committed $25M** to the White House ballroom, the top tier. Single-source: the foundation could not be reached for comment and the White House's own October 2025 list gave no amounts. This is the foundation, not Adelson personally, and a commitment is not shown to be a payment.
- **Trump on the record:** at the Knesset on 13 Oct 2025 Reuters reports he credited the couple with his Jerusalem, embassy and Golan decisions, and he said "She's got $60 billion in the bank" (Forbes, quoted by the Boston Globe, puts the Adelson family's net worth at $31.2B, so the figure is his own); on 16 Dec 2025 he said she gave his campaign "indirectly and directly $250 million". His $250M figure is his own and does not match one filing total, and 'another $250 million' for a third term is his account of a remark made in his ear, not confirmed by Adelson.
- **AIPAC's super PAC, United Democracy Project (UDP):** 2024 cycle: receipts $87.2M, disbursements about $61.4M (FEC), including $34.8M on independent expenditures in Democratic House races (FactCheck). 2026 cycle through 14 Oct: receipts $112.8M (of which contributions $108.6M), disbursements $85.1M, independent expenditures $53.6M (FEC). It is bipartisan, mostly congressional and mostly primaries. Its largest Republican target in 2026 was Rep. Thomas Massie, whom Trump also opposed.
- **Not documented:** any agreement, condition or payment tying donations to policy. A 2024 report of an annexation condition was denied by Adelson's spokesman, and Trump said in Oct 2025 that annexation "won't happen".

### AIPAC and UDP spending

AIPAC is a US lobbying group. AIPAC PAC gives candidates up to $5,000 per election; UDP, its super PAC (created Jan 2022), makes independent expenditures.

- **2024:** UDP's FEC totals are receipts $87.2M and disbursements $61.4M (re-pulled in the audit); FactCheck (read in full) puts $34.8M of its independent expenditures on House Democratic candidates. Biggest races: Bowman (NY-16), more than $14.5M including support for Latimer (Sludge excerpt); Bush (MO-1), about $5.2M against plus $3.3M for Bell. Intercept: UDP $41.4M direct in 2024 races.
- **2026 (FEC to 14 Oct):** receipts $112.8M (contributions $108.6M), disbursements $85.1M, independent expenditures $53.6M, plus $13.1M to other federal committees (re-pulled in the audit). Intercept: AIPAC $53.3M and UDP $66.7M direct, spending in 333 of 473 seats; over $30M in the Michigan Democratic Senate primary (against El-Sayed or for Stevens; El-Sayed won). Others: about $5M for Conyears-Ervin (IL-7, lost), over $2M against Malinowski (NJ-11 special primary, lost), over $1M for Boafo (MD-5, won), over $3M against Massie plus about $0.5M for Gallrein. Politico reported over 40% of UDP's primary money went through pop-up and pass-through PACs.
- **On whom:** House and Senate candidates, mostly in primaries, both parties, and mostly Democratic races. No UDP spending for or against Trump personally was found (presidential filings not searched).
- **Massie race:** pro-Israel-linked groups spent more than $15.5M (Al Jazeera, FEC): UDP over $4.1M by 18 May, RJC Victory Fund about $3.9M, MAGA KY $7.5M. MAGA KY received $1M from Singer and $750,000 from Preserve America PAC on 27 Jun 2025 (FEC) and $250,000 from John Paulson (C86). Massie lost 54% to 45% on 19 May 2026. Both Trump and UDP opposed him; no source shows coordination or that anyone asked Trump to endorse.

Responses: UDP's spokesman Patrick Dorton called Massie "the most anti-Israel Republican in the House". AIPAC called him "one of the most consistently hostile voices in Congress toward the U.S.-Israel relationship". Massie said Trump and AIPAC were "panicked".
