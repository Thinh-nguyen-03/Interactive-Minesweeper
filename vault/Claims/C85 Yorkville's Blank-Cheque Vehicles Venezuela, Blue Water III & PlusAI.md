# C85: Yorkville's Blank-Cheque Vehicles: Venezuela, Blue Water III & PlusAI

**Verdict:** Partly accurate · medium confidence  
**Type:** Connection check · Trump Media & Markets

## The Claim

> What do Yorkville's blank-cheque companies target, what are the sponsors' founder stakes worth, and how do they relate to US policy on Venezuela?

## Bottom Line

The governance and money links are documented. Yorkville's sponsors hold founder blocks with a nominal value (our arithmetic, at $10 a share) of about $153M (YICC, bought for $25,000), $75M (TVA III, $7.4M) and $63M (BLUW, a reported $7.2M).

## What Holds Up

- Yorkville International Capital (YICC), a Venezuela/Latin America SPAC run by TMTG interim CEO McGurn, closed a $230M IPO and its Yorkville sponsor holds about 15.33M founder shares bought for $25,000.
- Texas Ventures Acquisition III, whose board included Trump Organization EVPs Glick and Garten, was the announced merger vehicle for a spin-off of Truth Social (Feb-Jun 2026).
- After the spin-off fell away, TVA III signed PlusAI. Redemptions of about 79% leave the Yorkville sponsor's founder shares larger than the public float (mostly accurate)

## What Needs Correcting

- PlusAI stands to benefit from the administration's federal autonomous-vehicle rules (partly accurate)
- Blue Water III's $10B Citgo bid carried over into the Yorkville/TMTG-staffed vehicle (false)
- The Citgo auction outcome: sold to Elliott's Amber Energy (misleading)
- YICC's IPO closed at $230M (23M units, over-allotment exercised), not the $200M priced figure used in C78 and by Wyden.
- BLUW suspended its Citgo bid on 14 Nov 2025, before the 25 Nov 2025 Yorkville takeover. The Venezuela thread went to founder Hernandez's separate Blue Water IV (Maha/PetroUrdaneta LOI, Apr-May 2026), not to the Yorkville/TMTG-staffed BLUW.
- There is no Citgo 'auction outcome' yet. The $5.9B Amber Energy sale is court-approved but unlicensed by OFAC and under Third Circuit appeal.
- No PlusAI-specific federal AV action was found. DOT's strategy, NHTSA's interim rule and FMCSA's beacon exemption are industry-wide.

## What Is Unproven

- YICC's Venezuela plan depends on the post-Maduro OFAC licence regime, and no Yorkville licence is on record.

## In More Detail

- YICC closed at $230M and still has no Venezuelan target, licence or reply to Wyden.
- TVA III, with two Trump Organization EVPs on its board, was the announced merger vehicle for a Truth Social spin-off from Feb to Jun 2026 before signing PlusAI, and about 79% of its trust has since been redeemed.
- The policy side of the claim is not substantiated: BLUW abandoned Citgo before Yorkville took over, the Citgo sale to Elliott's Amber Energy remains unlicensed and frozen by Treasury, and federal AV actions are industry-wide with no PlusAI-specific decision.

### 1. Yorkville International Capital (YICC), a Venezuela/Latin America SPAC run by TMTG interim CEO McGurn, closed a $230M IPO and its Yorkville sponsor holds about 15.33M founder shares bought for $25,000.

**Confirmed**

- Evidence grade: documented (8-K, 10-Q and S-1/A via search summaries; sec.gov not read directly).
- The IPO closed on 17 Jun 2026: 23,000,000 units including the full 3M over-allotment, $230M in trust at $10.00 (C78 and Wyden used the $200M priced figure).
- Private warrants: 6.3M at $1, 4.0M to the sponsor and 2.3M to Cohen & Company Capital Markets, the sole book-runner.
- Yorkville International Capital Sponsor, LLC (Florida) bought 15,333,333 Class B shares for $25,000; the subscription letter reads 15,033,333, unresolved.
- The prospectus itself sizes the founder shares to convert to 40% of total shares outstanding (43.4% if the over-allotment had not been exercised), about double the usual 20%; audit re-check of S-1/424B4 search extracts, so this is documented rather than inferred.
- MarketChameleon's ~$378.7M value at $9.88 (30 Oct 2026) is consistent.
- The S-1 dates the $25,000 purchase to 31 Mar 2026, the 424B4 to 2 Apr 2026.
- Per the S-1, Yorkville founder Mark Angelo (YICC chairman) is sole managing member of YA II PN, Ltd., the sponsor's managing member, and holds voting and investment discretion over its shares.
- A June 2026 Schedule 13G reports 15,183,333 shares for the sponsor (vs 15,033,333 in the S-1 table); the balance is with officers and directors, and the gaps are unexplained.
- The block is ~$153M nominal at $10 (our arithmetic) and worthless on liquidation.
- No target and no reply to Wyden were found as of Nov 2026.
- The FT first reported the plan on 14-15 May 2026.
- Amounts: $230M; $10.00; $200M; $1; $25,000
- Dates: 17 Jun 2026; 30 Oct 2026; 31 Mar 2026; 2 Apr 2026; June 2026

### 2. YICC's Venezuela plan depends on the post-Maduro OFAC licence regime, and no Yorkville licence is on record.

**Unverified**

- Evidence grade: the regime is documented (law-firm alerts); its applicability to YICC is inferred.
- After Maduro's capture on 3 Jan 2026, OFAC issued: GL 46 (29 Jan 2026), covering oil production and sales by 'established U.S. entities' (read as organised on or before 29 Jan 2025) and excluding new investment; GL 46A and 46B (10 Feb and 13 Mar); GL 47 (diluents); GL 49 (negotiations and contingent contracts for new investment); GL 50 (PDVSA entities); and a March PDVSA direct-sales licence.
- Payments go to Treasury-controlled accounts.
- YICC was formed in the Caymans in March 2026, so on our reading it falls outside GL 46 and would need GL 49 or a specific licence for an actual investment.
- Wyden asked exactly this on 25 Jun 2026, plus the PDVSA 50 Percent Rule exposure.
- No answer and no licence were found.
- Dates: 3 Jan 2026; 29 Jan 2026; 29 Jan 2025; 10 Feb; 13 Mar
- Sources: [9](https://www.thompsonhinesmartrade.com/2026/02/ofac-issues-general-license-authorizing-certain-oil-activities-in-venezuela/) [10](https://www.kelleydrye.com/viewpoints/blogs/trade-and-manufacturing-monitor/new-ofac-venezuela-licenses-permit-goods-technology-software-services-and-other-activities-for-oil-and-gas-exploration-and-production) [11](https://www.jdsupra.com/legalnews/ofac-issues-venezuela-general-licenses-1590174/) [8](https://www.finance.senate.gov/ranking-members-news/wyden-launches-inquiry-into-trump-connected-blank-check-company-planning-venezuela-investments)

### 3. Texas Ventures Acquisition III, whose board included Trump Organization EVPs Glick and Garten, was the announced merger vehicle for a spin-off of Truth Social (Feb-Jun 2026).

**Confirmed**

- Evidence grade: documented (company release; Axios, CBS, MediaPost; Stocktwits/TNW on the abandonment).
- On 27 Feb 2026 TMTG, TAE Technologies and TVA III announced talks for TMTG to spin off Truth Social and other media assets into a new company.
- Its shares would go to TMTG holders before the TAE merger closed, and it would then merge with TVA III.
- On 26 Feb 2026, one day earlier, TMTG CEO Nunes and general counsel Glabe resigned from TVA III's board, citing no disagreement (8-K).
- Linking the resignations to the announcement is inferred from timing.
- On 10 Jun 2026 the parties said they would not pursue the spin-off 'at this time'; the media assets go to a post-TAE 'strategic alternatives' review.
- That President Trump's trust, as TMTG's largest holder, would have received spin-off shares is inferred from the announced pro-rata structure.
- The TAE merger's S-4 is effective, but the merger had not closed by mid-Nov 2026.
- Dates: 27 Feb 2026; 26 Feb 2026; 10 Jun 2026; mid-Nov 2026
- Sources: [12](https://www.axios.com/2026/02/27/truth-social-spin-off) [13](https://www.cbsnews.com/amp/news/trump-media-technology-group-tmtg-truth-social-spinoff) [16](https://www.stocktitan.net/sec-filings/TVA/8-k-texas-ventures-acquisition-iii-corp-reports-material-event-2566adb1a983.html) [14](https://stocktwits.com/news-articles/markets/equity/djt-stock-gains-truth-social-spin-off-plans-scrapped/cZ06mh1R7TM) [15](https://thenextweb.com/news/trump-media-drops-truth-social-spinoff-refocuses-on-6-billion-fusion-merger-with-tae-technologies) [38](https://finviz.com/news/417799/trump-media-technology-group-and-tae-technologies-announce-effectiveness-of-form-s-4-registration-statement-for-proposed-merger)

### 4. After the spin-off fell away, TVA III signed PlusAI. Redemptions of about 79% leave the Yorkville sponsor's founder shares larger than the public float.

**Mostly accurate**

- Evidence grade: documented via aggregator summaries of the 8-K and 8-K/A (not read in the filing itself).
- The deal: an $800M pre-money valuation, up to 63M earnout shares, and a $40M minimum-cash condition.
- At the extension vote holders redeemed 17,699,488 Class A shares at about $10.63 (~$188M), leaving 4,800,512 (~$51M).
- The deadline moves from 24 Oct 2026 toward 24 Jul 2027 by monthly deposits.
- Yorkville Acquisition Sponsor II issued a $145,000 note on 20 Oct 2026, and its deposit extended the deadline to 24 Nov 2026.
- A going-concern doubt was flagged in Aug 2026.
- The Yorkville sponsor paid $7.4M for 7.5M founder shares plus 4.7M warrants (C78), now ~$75M nominal at $10 and more than the 4.8M public shares.
- Forfeitures under the merger agreement, the post-merger board, and whether Glick and Garten remain are unverified (S-4 not readable).
- FreightWaves reports PlusAI targeting a year-end 2026 close; Yorkville-managed funds provide a significant share of the more than $60M in committed notes and PIPE.
- Amounts: $800M; $40M; about $10.63; ~$188M; ~$51M
- Dates: 24 Oct 2026; 24 Jul 2027; 20 Oct 2026; 24 Nov 2026; Aug 2026
- Sources: [17](https://equibles.com/stocks/tva/documents) [18](https://siliconangle.com/2026/09/03/self-driving-truck-firm-plusai-takes-third-run-at-listing-this-time-at-800m/) <https://www.freightwaves.com/news/plusai-texas-ventures-spac-close>

### 5. PlusAI stands to benefit from the administration's federal autonomous-vehicle rules.

**Partly accurate** · Evidence: The policies are documented; any PlusAI-specific benefit is unverified

- DOT released its national AV strategy ('AV 5.0', non-binding) on 3 Sep 2026, the same day the PlusAI-TVA III deal was announced; the timing match is our observation (inferred).
- NHTSA issued an interim final rule on 31 Jul 2026 allowing production before an exemption is granted.
- FMCSA granted a five-year exemption (7 Oct 2026 to 7 Oct 2031) for cab-mounted beacons on Level-4 trucks; Aurora applied and any Level-4 carrier can opt in.
- FMCSA's proposed rule on ADS-equipped trucks was still unpublished.
- Trade press lists PlusAI among the firms scaling in 2027.
- The rules are industry-wide.
- No source reports a PlusAI opt-in, lobbying or a company-specific decision.
- Dates: 3 Sep 2026; 31 Jul 2026; 7 Oct 2026; 7 Oct 2031
- Sources: [19](https://www.transportation.gov/briefing-room/trumps-transportation-secretary-sean-p-duffy-unveils-new-national-av-strategy) [20](https://www.hklaw.com/en/insights/publications/2026/09/the-road-ahead-for-driverless-vehicles-dot-unveils-av-5) [21](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/fmcsa-aurora-warning-beacon-exemption-five-year) [22](https://ttnews.com/articles/fmcsa-beacons-autonomous-trucks)

### 7. The Citgo auction outcome: sold to Elliott's Amber Energy.

**Misleading** · Evidence: Documented (OGJ, Bloomberg, Fortune, IISD, Law360)

- The Delaware court approved Amber Energy's $5.9B bid on 25 Nov 2025, but the sale has not closed.
- OFAC has not licensed it, and Fortune reports that Treasury has extended Citgo's protection from the sale six times since January 2026.
- On 14 Sep 2026 OFAC amended the licence to bar unauthorised governance changes at Citgo, Citgo Holding and PDV Holding, while Delcy Rodriguez's interim government sought to replace those boards.
- Venezuela's Third Circuit appeal (Greenberg Traurig as counsel since Jun 2026) had no ruling found as of mid-Nov 2026.
- Bloomberg (Apr 2026) cites administration concerns over price and political risk.
- Energy Secretary Chris Wright had applauded the sale to Elliott, whose founder Paul Singer is a GOP megadonor (Fortune, NHPR).
- No link to Yorkville or BLUW's post-Nov 2025 management was found.
- Amounts: $5.9B
- Dates: 25 Nov 2025; January 2026; 14 Sep 2026; Jun 2026; mid-Nov 2026

## Denials & Context

- A Trump Organization spokesperson said the Trump sons have no involvement with Yorkville (via Siegelman, C78).
- Yorkville has not publicly responded to Wyden's 25 Jun 2026 letter, which is a minority oversight request, not an enforcement action.
- TMTG, TAE and TVA III gave no reason for dropping the Truth Social spin-off.
  - Nunes's and Glabe's 8-K said their resignations involved no disagreement.
- Cheap founder shares ($25,000 promotes; about $1 a share on transferred blocks) are standard SPAC economics and are worthless without a completed deal.
  - They are not by themselves a Trump-specific benefit.
- Sponsor control: the YICC S-1 says Mark Angelo holds voting and investment discretion via YA II PN, Ltd. Whether McGurn, Rillo or any Trump Org or TMTG director holds an economic interest in any sponsor LLC is not disclosed in anything we could read. sec.gov, fortune.com, iisd.org and barchart were not read directly, so filing figures rely on search summaries and aggregators.
