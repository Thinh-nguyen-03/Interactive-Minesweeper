# C105: Steve Witkoff: Holdings, Divestiture & Overlapping Dealings

**Verdict:** Mostly accurate · medium confidence  
**Type:** Check · Foreign Deals

## The Claim

> What has Steve Witkoff disclosed and divested, and where do his diplomatic assignments overlap with his family's business counterparts?

## Bottom Line

Filings document most of the record. Witkoff served as State Department envoy from January to June 2025 and has been a full White House appointee since 30 June 2025.

## What Holds Up

- Witkoff's employment status: special envoy from January 2025, a full appointee from mid-2025, and for a time a special government employee (mostly accurate)
- Witkoff sold a $120 million interest in the Witkoff Group as part of divestiture; the buyer is undisclosed.
- No certificate of divestiture, ethics agreement, waiver or written recusal for Witkoff has been published.
- His other disclosed holdings include ongoing partnerships with counterparts linked to the countries he deals with (mostly accurate)
- UAE: Witkoff's envoy work with Abu Dhabi overlapped in time with WLF and family dealings with Sheikh Tahnoon's entities.
- Russia and Ukraine: Witkoff's negotiations overlapped with his firm's partnership with Access Industries and with a sale bid involving a Tahnoon-chaired group (mostly accurate)
- Pakistan (other counterpart): WLF and the Witkoff sons' stablecoin firm signed deals with Pakistan while Witkoff met its army chief.
- Members of Congress sought investigations; no authority has made a finding.

## What Needs Correcting

- Witkoff filed his public financial disclosure late, months after he began working (partly accurate)
- Qatar: Witkoff's Gaza mediation with Qatar overlapped with his family's Qatar-linked business (partly accurate)
- C14 and C42 describe the $107M as income 'from his WLF-linked holding company'. The record shows: the payer is Witkoff Holdings LLC, a broad holding company that also owns Banyan Cay, Ocean Terrace and golf and hotel operating companies besides WC Digital Fi (WLF) and WC Digital SC (SC Financial). The filing splits the $106,938,819 into $68,875,000 cash and $38,063,819 cryptocurrency but does not say how much came from WLF.
- C14 and C42 say the income rose from '$34M in 2024'. The record shows: the $34,363,535 is from the new-entrant report, whose income period runs from January 2024 to the August 2025 filing date, so it is not a 2024-only figure.
- C53 says his disclosures 'went uncertified'. The record shows: the White House DAEO, Scott F. Gast, certified the new-entrant report on 12 Dec 2025 and the revised version adds his State Department post (Jan-Jun 2025). The 2025 annual report (signed 29 Jul 2026) shows no reviewer signature as published.
- C53 dates the Warren-Murphy letter 18 Nov 2025. The letter is dated 17 Nov 2025; the press releases are dated 18 Nov.
- C53 says Meeks, Amo and Garcia wrote to the State and Commerce IGs 'and OGE'. The release read names the acting inspectors general of Commerce and State only; OGE is not mentioned in it.
- C53 says no IG outcome was found. Update: on 12 Dec 2025 State's OIG told Warren and Slotkin it had referred their request to its Office of Investigations; no result has been reported.
- C76 says Witkoff 'sold a $120M stake in his firm in Sep 2025'. The record shows: the sale was disclosed in September 2025 (report signed 13 Aug, published about 13 Sep); the sale itself took place between January and 13 August 2025, date undisclosed.
- C14 says no divestiture certificate or recipient of the WLF stake was found. Still true as of 17 Nov 2026; in addition, the annual report signed on 29 Jul 2026, which covers calendar 2025, still lists the WLF and SC Financial interests and no transaction disposing of them during 2025. It does not show his position in 2026, when the White House said he had divested.
- The 22 Oct 2025 senators' letter describes a '$2 billion investment from one of Sheikh Tahnoon's investment firms' in WLF. The record shows: MGX's $2B went into Binance and was settled in USD1; the Tahnoon-backed equity investment in WLF was Aryam's $500M for 49% (C07, C14).
- 2026-11-17: Witkoff holds two Board of Peace seats, on the founding Executive Board and on the Gaza Executive Board (White House statement, 16 Jan 2026, read in full for C112). Earlier text listed only his envoy posts.

## What Is Unproven

- Witkoff divested his World Liberty Financial interests, transferring them to his sons.
- Saudi Arabia and Israel: family or WLF dealings with counterparts there overlapped with his diplomacy.
- Witkoff used his diplomatic role for personal or family financial benefit (quid pro quo).

## In More Detail

- His SGE status in the first period is reported but not documented.
- His new-entrant 278e (signed 13 Aug 2025, certified only on 12 Dec 2025 after senators' letters, and corrected to add the State post) and his 2025 annual report (signed 29 Jul 2026, uncertified as published) show a $120M sale of a Witkoff Group interest (buyer, date and share undisclosed) while he keeps a stake worth over $50M, and both list his WLF and SC Financial interests inside Witkoff Holdings LLC, a broad holding company that paid him $106.9M in 2025 ($68.9M cash, $38.1M crypto; the form does not attribute any of it to WLF).
- The annual report covers calendar 2025: it shows he still held the WLF interest after the White House said in September 2025 that he was 'still in the process of divesting', and no disposal during 2025, but it cannot show his position when the White House said in February and September 2026 that he had divested.
- The White House says he has fully divested from WLF and does not take part in matters affecting his interests, but no certificate of divestiture, ethics agreement, waiver, recusal or recipient has been published.
- His UAE, Qatar, Russia and Pakistan work overlaps in time with family and WLF dealings with counterparts there (Aryam, MGX, Access Industries, Pakistan's government); no Saudi or Israeli dealing was found.
- State's inspector general referred one request to investigators in December 2025; no authority has made any finding.

## Sub-Claims

### 1. Witkoff's employment status: special envoy from January 2025, a full appointee from mid-2025, and for a time a special government employee.

**Mostly accurate** · Evidence: Appointments documented; SGE status unverified

- Trump named him Special Envoy to the Middle East on 12 November 2024.
- His new-entrant OGE Form 278e, as certified on 12 December 2025 (read in full), lists 'Special Envoy to the Middle East, Department of State (1/2025 - 6/2025)' and his current post, 'Assistant to the President and Special Envoy for Peace Missions' at the White House, with an appointment date of 30 June 2025 and appointment type 'Non-Career'.
- That makes him a regular (full) political appointee from 30 June 2025, subject to 18 U.S.C.
- 208 and the standards of conduct.
- He was sworn in at the Oval Office on 6 May 2025 (White House post cited in the Warren-Murphy letter).
- The form does not say whether the State Department role was as a special government employee (SGE), and the White House has said he takes no salary.
- That he was an SGE (a status capped at 130 days of service in 365) rests on watchdog research (Accountable.us, 22 Sep 2025, not readable here) and secondary summaries; CREW wrote in July 2025 that it was unclear in what capacity State employed him.
- No official document stating SGE status was found.
- A span of more than 130 calendar days would not by itself show the cap was exceeded, since only days worked count.
- Board of Peace: the White House statement of 16 Jan 2026 lists Witkoff on both the founding Executive Board (with Rubio, Kushner, Blair, Rowan, Banga and Gabriel) and the Gaza Executive Board; the 2025 278e covers calendar 2025 and so does not show these 2026 appointments.
- No pay or compensation for these seats was found.
- Dates: 12 November 2024; 12 December 2025; 30 June 2025; 6 May 2025; 22 Sep 2025

### 2. Witkoff filed his public financial disclosure late, months after he began working.

**Partly accurate**

- Grade: filing dates documented; lateness depends on an undetermined legal question. (1) Filings (read in full): the new-entrant report was e-signed on 13 August 2025 with the note 'Filer received a 30 day filing extension'; it was published by the White House around 13 September 2025 (Bisnow: 'released Saturday').
- As first published it listed no other federal position in the preceding 12 months and carried no ethics official's signature.

(3)/(4) Whether a public report was legally due for the State period depends on his status and pay then (an unpaid SGE may file only a confidential report); no public or confidential State-period report was found, and no authority has found a filing violation.

- No late-filing fee is recorded on either form.

### 3. Witkoff sold a $120 million interest in the Witkoff Group as part of divestiture; the buyer is undisclosed.

### 4. Witkoff divested his World Liberty Financial interests, transferring them to his sons.

**Unverified** · Evidence: Holdings documented; divestiture and transfer unverified

- Both 278e reports (read in full) list, under 'Witkoff Holdings LLC' (value over $50M), 'WC Digital Fi LLC' holding 'World Liberty Financial (cryptocurrency)' and 'WC Digital SC LLC' holding 'SC Financial Technologies LLC (stablecoin)'.
- Witkoff Holdings reported distributions of $34,363,535 in the new-entrant period (calendar 2024 to August 2025) and $106,938,819 in the 2025 annual, which an endnote splits into '$68,875,000 in cash distributions and $38,063,819 in cryptocurrency'.
- Witkoff Holdings also holds real-estate and operating interests (Banyan Cay, Ocean Terrace, golf and hotel management companies), so the filing does not show how much of either sum came from WLF.
- Statements: WLF (Zak Folkman, 23 May 2025) said he was 'in the process of fully divesting'; the White House said in Sept 2025 he was 'still in the process of divesting'; White House counsel David Warrington told CNN on 1-2 Feb 2026 that he 'has also divested from World Liberty Financial, notwithstanding his ability and willingness to recuse'; spokeswoman Anna Kelly told ABC on 9 Sep 2026 that he had 'fully divested'.
- What the annual report can and cannot show: it is the 2026 annual report ('Report Year: 2026'), whose reporting period is calendar 2025; it was signed on 29 July 2026 but describes 2025, not the position on the signing date.
- Part 6 line 35, 'Witkoff Holdings LLC' (value 'Over $50,000,000', 'Distributions $106,938,819'), lists underlying holdings including '35.8 WC Digital Fi LLC', '35.8.1 World Liberty Financial (cryptocurrency)', '35.9 WC Digital SC LLC' and '35.9.1 SC Financial Technologies LLC (stablecoin)'.
- These underlying lines carry no value or income of their own.
- The form's instructions say Part 6 lists each asset that 'ended the reporting period with a value greater than $1,000' or from which more than $200 in income 'was received during the reporting period', so the listing shows he held the interest through Witkoff Holdings during 2025 and is consistent with, but does not by itself prove, holding it on 31 December 2025.
- Part 7 (transactions) lists only the Reddit sale; whether a disposal of an interest held inside a private holding company would have to appear there is not settled by the form.
- Part 1 still lists him as managing member of Witkoff Holdings LLC from 5/2022 to 'Present'.
- Conclusion: the filings show he held the WLF interest after the May 2025 'in the process' statement and after the September 2025 'still in the process' statement, and record no disposal in 2025.
- They neither confirm nor contradict the White House statements of February and September 2026, which postdate the reporting period; a 2026 disposal would be expected on the next annual report (covering 2026) and, if it was a reportable securities transaction, on a 278-T, and none has been published.
- The statement that he has divested therefore rests on the White House's word, without a document.
- No certificate of divestiture, 278-T, buyer, recipient, price or date for the WLF interest was found.
- The claim that he was transferring the holdings to his sons comes from the Meeks-Amo-Garcia release (Jan 2026) and an expert quoted by Bloomberg (via Bisnow); no filing shows a transfer.
- Amounts: over $50M; $34,363,535; $106,938,819; $68,875,000; $38,063,819
- Dates: August 2025; 23 May 2025; Sept 2025; 2 Feb 2026; 9 Sep 2026

### 5. No certificate of divestiture, ethics agreement, waiver or written recusal for Witkoff has been published.

**Confirmed** · Evidence: Documented absence in the places checked

- The White House disclosures page lists only his new-entrant and 2026 annual reports, with no ethics agreement, waiver or periodic transaction report under his name (read 17 Nov 2026); the senators' letters of 22 Oct and 17 Nov 2025 asked for any waiver under 18 U.S.C.
- 208(b)(1) or (b)(3), any 2635.502(d) authorisation, written recusals and screening arrangements, and no reply was found.
- By contrast David Sacks's 208(b)(1) waiver was published (cited in the Warren-Slotkin IG letter). Rules: 18 U.S.C.
- 208 bars personal and substantial participation in a particular matter affecting his own financial interest (and his spouse's or minor children's, or an organisation in which he is a general partner or officer), unless waived; adult sons' interests are not imputed under 208, but 5 C.F.R.
- 2635.502 asks an employee to consider impartiality where a relative with whom he has a close personal relationship is a party or represents a party to a specific-party matter.
- The White House position is that he 'has not and does not participate in any official matters that could impact his financial interests' (Warrington, Feb 2026), which describes recusal without a published document.
- The White House ethics official's certification of 12 Dec 2025 found the new-entrant report in compliance 'on the basis of information contained in this report'.
- Note: his new-entrant report listed Reddit Class A shares ($1,000,001-$5,000,000), and his annual report lists their sale ('Reddit Inc.-CL A [RDDT] [sold 10/1/2025]'; Part 7: 'Sale 10/01/2025 $1,000,001 - $5,000,000'), made while he was a public filer.
- A 278-T is due within 30 days of notification and no later than 45 days after a transaction (here about 15 Nov 2025).
- The White House page lists 278-Ts for more than 20 other staff but none for him (checked 17 Nov 2026).
- Reports can also be obtained on request, so whether one was filed but not posted is not known, and no authority has found a filing lapse.
- Amounts: $1,000,001-$5,000,000; $1,000,001 - $5,000,000

### 8. Qatar: Witkoff's Gaza mediation with Qatar overlapped with his family's Qatar-linked business.

## Denials & Context

- White House counsel David Warrington (to CNN, Feb 2026): Witkoff 'takes seriously his compliance with the government ethics rules... has not and does not participate in any official matters that could impact his financial interests.
  - He has also divested from World Liberty Financial, notwithstanding his ability and willingness to recuse.'
- White House spokeswoman Anna Kelly (to ABC, 9 Sep 2026): he has 'fully divested from World Liberty Financial, and his prior commercial activities are completely unrelated to his efforts to end global conflicts'.
  - In Sept 2025 the White House said he was 'still in the process of divesting' and working with ethics officials, that he 'did not participate' in the UAE chip negotiations, and that the crypto deal was 'totally unrelated to any government business'.
- A Witkoff spokesman says he 'does not discuss WLF or any other personal business while conducting his official duties'; on Lukoil his spokesperson said he 'has no conflict of interest and no financial stake in this matter'.
- WLF (Zak Folkman, May 2025): Witkoff has 'no operational role, no financial interest in WLFI deals, and no influence on day-to-day decisions'.
  - WLF spokesman David Wachsman (Feb 2026): neither Trump nor Witkoff 'had any involvement whatsoever' in the Aryam transaction, and any link to chip decisions is '100% false'.
- The Witkoff sons, through lawyer Timothy Parlatore, threatened the NYT with a defamation suit over its September 2025 report on their Gulf fundraising; Qatar's International Media Office called that report false; Access Industries says Blavatnik and Witkoff never discussed the Russia-Ukraine negotiations.
- Context: holding an asset, or a family member doing business with a foreign counterpart, is not by itself a violation. 18 U.S.C.
  - 208 covers his own, spouse's and minor children's interests, not adult sons'.
  - The White House ethics official certified his new-entrant report as compliant on the basis of its contents.
  - No inspector general, OGE, DOJ or court finding exists.

**Evidence rule applied:** owning an asset, or a relative doing business with a foreign counterpart, is not by itself a violation. ### Rules that apply

- **18 U.S.C. 208** bars an executive-branch employee from taking part personally and substantially in a particular matter that affects his own financial interest, or that of his spouse, his minor children, or an organisation where he is an officer, director or general partner. The bar can be lifted by a written waiver under 208(b)(1) or (b)(3). The interests of adult sons are **not** imputed to him under 208.
- **5 C.F.R. 2635.502 (impartiality)** asks an employee to weigh whether to take part in a specific-party matter where a relative with whom he has a close personal relationship is a party or represents a party. An agency may authorise participation under 2635.502(d).
- **Special government employees** are limited to 130 days of service in any 365. Only an SGE paid above a set threshold and serving more than 60 days must file a public report; others may file a confidential OGE 450.
- **Public filers** must file a new-entrant report within 30 days of appointment (extensions are possible) and annual reports by 15 May. Periodic transaction reports (278-T) are due no later than 45 days after a securities transaction. An agency reviewer has 60 days to review and certify a report.
