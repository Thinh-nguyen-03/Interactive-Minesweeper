# C16: Qatar: The Gifted Jet, Arms Sales & a Trump Golf Project

**Verdict:** Mostly accurate · medium confidence  
**Type:** Check · Gulf Money

## The Claim

> Did Qatar give a Boeing 747 worth about $400 million for use as Air Force One while it negotiated arms purchases from the US and the Trump family struck a golf-course deal in Qatar?

## Bottom Line

Documented: the ~$400M Qatari Boeing 747-8 was accepted by the Pentagon on 21 May 2025 for Air Force One use, with reported plans to transfer it to Trump's library foundation by 2029.

## What Holds Up

- Qatar gifted a ~$400M luxury Boeing 747 for Trump to use as Air Force One.
- The plane would go to Trump's presidential library after his presidency.
- At the same time, Qatar was negotiating a $1.96B arms deal with the US (mostly accurate)
- The Trump family struck a luxury golf course deal in Qatar as part of a ~$5.5B project (mostly accurate)
- In 2026 the State Department approved two more possible sales to Qatar worth ~$5B (APKWS and Patriot replenishment).

## What Needs Correcting

- **Claimed:** The arms deal was 'at the same time' as the jet  
  **The record shows:** State approved the MQ-9B sale on 26 Mar 2025, about six weeks before the 11 May 2025 jet reports. It was signed on 14 May 2025.
- **Claimed:** The golf deal was part of a '$5.5 billion project'  
  **The record shows:** That is the total for Qatari Diar's Simaisma masterplan. The Trump Organization's role is brand licensing via Dar Global.
- **Claimed:** '$5 billion' for the 2026 sales  
  **The record shows:** They are $4.01B (Patriot) plus $992.4M (APKWS), approved under an emergency waiver tied to the Iran war.
- The 'favorable terms' claim could not be corroborated.

## What Is Unproven

- Qatar got 'pretty favorable terms' on the arms deal, per independent reporting.

## In More Detail

- Also documented: the $1.96B MQ-9B sale (approved 26 Mar 2025, signed 14 May 2025), a Trump-branded golf club and villas inside Qatari Diar's $5.5B Simaisma project (30 Apr 2025), and May 2026 approvals of Patriot ($4.01B) and APKWS ($992.4M) sales to Qatar.
- Not supported: I found no reporting of 'favorable terms'; the arms approval came about six weeks before the jet story; the $5.5B is the whole masterplan, not the Trump component; and the 2026 sales were emergency Iran-war replenishments.
- The link between gifts and arms is insinuation based on timing.

## Sub-Claims

### 1. Qatar gifted a ~$400M luxury Boeing 747 for Trump to use as Air Force One.

**Confirmed**

- ABC first reported the plan on 11 May 2025: a 747-8 from the Qatari royal family.
- Hegseth formally accepted it on 21 May 2025.
- The $400M is a widely repeated estimate that excludes the retrofit cost.
- The jet was unveiled in June 2026 and flew as Air Force One by July 2026.
- Qatar first called it a possible 'temporary use' transfer and disputed that it was a gift.
- Amounts: $400M
- Dates: 11 May 2025; 21 May 2025; June 2026; July 2026
- Sources: [3](https://abc7news.com/post/donald-trump-administration-poised-accept-boeing-747-8-jumbo-jet-gift-new-air-force-qatar-royal-family-sources/16382889/) [8](https://www.airandspaceforces.com/pentagon-accepts-qatari-747-use-by-trump-air-force-one-replacement/) [19](https://lasvegassun.com/news/2026/jul/01/retrofitted-qatari-jet-takes-flight-as-air-force-o/)

### 2. The plane would go to Trump's presidential library after his presidency.

**Confirmed**

- ABC's sources said ownership would pass to the Trump Presidential Library Foundation no later than 1 Jan 2029, with the Air Force paying the transfer costs, and Trump said it would go to his library.
- As of mid-2026 the destination was reported as uncertain.
- The suggestion that he might use it as a private citizen is speculation.
- Dates: 1 Jan 2029; mid-2026
- Sources: [3](https://abc7news.com/post/donald-trump-administration-poised-accept-boeing-747-8-jumbo-jet-gift-new-air-force-qatar-royal-family-sources/16382889/) [20](https://sundayguardianlive.com/world/air-force-one-us-president-donald-trump-to-house-historic-aircraft-at-library-while-qatari-jet-status-remains-unclear-254659/)

### 3. At the same time, Qatar was negotiating a $1.96B arms deal with the US.

**Mostly accurate**

- DSCA notified Congress of a possible $1.96B sale of 8 MQ-9B drones and munitions on 26 Mar 2025.
- Qatar signed the LOA ('nearly $2 billion') during Trump's 14 May 2025 Doha visit, three days after the jet story broke.
- The approval came about six weeks before the jet news.
- Amounts: $1.96B; nearly $2 billion
- Dates: 26 Mar 2025; 14 May 2025
- Sources: [1](https://www.dsca.mil/Press-Media/Major-Arms-Sales/Article-Display/Article/4136302/qatar-mq-9b-remotely-piloted-aircraft) [7](https://www.whitehouse.gov/fact-sheets/2025/05/fact-sheet-president-donald-j-trump-secures-historic-1-2-trillion-economic-commitment-in-qatar/)

### 4. Qatar got 'pretty favorable terms' on the arms deal, per independent reporting.

**Unverified**

- I found no reporting that the MQ-9B terms were unusually favourable.
- The documented context is that the Biden administration had not authorised the sale, Trump's State Department approved it quickly, and it would be the first MQ-9B sale to the Middle East.
- No evidence ties the sale to the jet.
- Sources: [2](https://abcnews.go.com/Technology/wireStory/us-initial-approval-qatar-purchase-mq-9-armed-120207144)

### 5. The Trump family struck a luxury golf course deal in Qatar as part of a ~$5.5B project.

**Mostly accurate**

- On 30 Apr 2025, Qatari Diar (a subsidiary of the Qatar Investment Authority) signed with Dar Global to develop the Trump International Golf Club and Trump Villas in Simaisma.
- The $5.5B is the whole Simaisma masterplan, not the Trump component.
- The Trump Organization licenses its brand through Saudi-linked Dar Global.
- Amounts: $5.5B

### 6. In 2026 the State Department approved two more possible sales to Qatar worth ~$5B (APKWS and Patriot replenishment).

**Confirmed**

- Around 1-2 May 2026, State approved Patriot replenishment for about $4.01B (200 PAC-2 GEM-T and 300 PAC-3 MSE) and 10,000 APKWS II rounds for about $992.4M, roughly $5.0B in total.

## Denials & Context

- The White House and Trump say the jet went to the US Air Force, not to Trump personally.
  - Leavitt said it complied fully with the law, and a Bondi legal analysis reportedly found no Emoluments Clause problem.
- Qatar first called the transfer 'under consideration' between defence ministries and disputed that it was a gift.
- Eric Trump said the Trump Organization has 'zero relationship' with Qatari Diar.
  - Mother Jones noted his own press release praised a 'collaboration with Qatari Diar and Dar Global.'
- Critics including Sen. Schumer, Sen. Jack Reed and Norman Eisen called the jet an ethics and emoluments problem.
- The 2026 sales followed Iranian strikes on Gulf states.
  - No source links them to Trump business interests, and Isaac Saul of Tangle News, the journalist who drew the connection, himself says 'maybe it's all coincidence.'

## Full Report

**Verdict: mostly accurate** (confidence: medium-high)

The core facts hold up. Qatar's royal family offered a Boeing 747-8, widely valued at about $400M. The Pentagon accepted it for use as Air Force One, and the plan was for it to go to Trump's presidential library foundation later. State approved a $1.96B MQ-9B drone sale to Qatar, and Qatar signed it during Trump's May 2025 visit. The Trump Organization announced a Trump-branded golf club and villas inside Qatar's $5.5B Simaisma project. In May 2026, State approved two more possible sales to Qatar, Patriot replenishment and APKWS, worth about $5.0B together.

Two claims go further than the record:
- I could not find the "pretty favorable terms" reporting for the drone deal.
- The timing is described as "at the same time". The drone approval actually came about six weeks before the jet story broke.

### The claim (as put by journalist Isaac Saul of Tangle News)

Qatar offered Trump a $400M plane to use as Air Force One, which would then go to his presidential library. At the same time, Qatar was negotiating a $1.96B arms deal "with pretty favorable terms." Trump's family struck a luxury golf deal in Qatar as part of a project worth about $5.5B. "This year" (2026), State approved two more possible sales to Qatar worth $5B, for "advanced precision kill systems" and Patriot missile replenishment. Saul adds: "Maybe it's all coincidence."

### Sub-claims

1. **A $400M luxury 747 from Qatar for use as Air Force One.** *Confirmed, with caveats.*
   - ABC News first reported the plan on 11 May 2025: a Boeing 747-8 from the Qatari royal family.
   - Trump called it a "GIFT, FREE OF CHARGE" to the Defense Department, and said he would be a "stupid person" to turn down a free plane.
   - Defense Secretary Hegseth formally accepted the jet on 21 May 2025.
   - The $400M figure is an estimate that outlets repeat. It does not include the retrofit, which one senator said could exceed $1B.
   - The retrofitted jet was unveiled in June 2026 and was flying as Air Force One by July 2026.
   - Qatar's media attaché first described it as a "possible transfer ... for temporary use" between the two defence ministries, not a gift.

2. **The jet would go to Trump's presidential library afterwards.** *Confirmed as the reported plan, but the outcome is unsettled.*
   - ABC's sources said ownership would pass to the Trump Presidential Library Foundation no later than 1 Jan 2029, with the Air Force paying the transfer costs.
   - Trump publicly said it would go to his library.
   - Reports from July and August 2026 call the library destination uncertain, and a House Democrat tried to block it with an amendment.
   - The suggestion that "maybe he'd get to use it ... as a private citizen" is speculation. The reported plan is that it goes to the library foundation.

3. **At the same time, Qatar was negotiating a $1.96B arms deal.** *Confirmed figure; the timing is roughly right but compressed.*
   - DSCA notified Congress on 26 March 2025 of a possible $1.96B sale: 8 MQ-9B drones, plus Hellfires, JDAM kits and bombs.
   - Qatar signed the letter of offer and acceptance, described as "nearly $2 billion", during Trump's Doha visit on 14 May 2025. That was three days after the jet story broke.
   - So the deal's approval came about six weeks before the jet news, and its signing came just after.

4. **"Pretty favorable terms," per independent reporting.** *Unverified.*
   - I found no reporting that the MQ-9B terms were unusually favourable to Qatar.
   - The documented context: the Biden administration had not approved the sale, and Trump's State Department approved it soon after taking office. It would be the first MQ-9B sale to the Middle East.
   - The link drawn between the jet and the arms deal is insinuation. It points to timing, not to any documented quid pro quo.

5. **A Trump family golf deal in Qatar, part of a ~$5.5B project.** *Mostly accurate.*
   - On 30 April 2025, Qatari Diar announced a deal with Dar Global to develop a Trump International Golf Club and Trump Villas in Simaisma, about 40 km north of Doha. Qatari Diar is a subsidiary of the Qatar Investment Authority.
   - The $5.5B is the value of the whole Simaisma masterplan, not the Trump part. Some trade press said no cost was disclosed for the Trump component.
   - The Trump Organization is a brand licensor through Dar Global, a Saudi-linked developer. Eric Trump said they have "zero relationship" with Qatari Diar. Mother Jones pointed to his own press release, which praised the "collaboration with Qatari Diar and Dar Global."

6. **In 2026, State approved two more possible sales worth ~$5B: APKWS and Patriot replenishment.** *Confirmed.*
   - Announced around 1-2 May 2026:
     - Patriot replenishment, about $4.01B: 200 PAC-2 GEM-T and 300 PAC-3 MSE interceptors.
     - APKWS II (the "Advanced Precision Kill Weapon System"), about $992.4M: 10,000 rounds.
   - The total is about $5.0B.
   - Secretary Rubio invoked emergency authority to waive congressional review, citing the conflict with Iran that began on 28 Feb 2026. The claim leaves this out: the sales were replenishment after Iranian strikes on Gulf states.

### Corrections
- "At the same time": the $1.96B MQ-9B sale was approved on 26 Mar 2025, and the jet story broke on 11 May 2025. The sale was formally signed on 14 May 2025, during the visit.
- "$5.5 billion project": that is the total value of the Simaisma masterplan led by Qatari Diar. The Trump Organization licenses its brand through Dar Global and has no disclosed equity stake.
- "$5 billion" in 2026: the two sales are $4.01B and $992.4M, about $5.0B combined, approved under an emergency waiver tied to the Iran war.
- "Favorable terms": I could not confirm this from any source.

### Denials and context
- Trump and the White House say the jet was given to the US Air Force, not to Trump personally. A legal analysis by AG Bondi's office reportedly found no violation of the Emoluments Clause. Leavitt said the deal was in "full compliance with the law."
- Qatar first called the transfer "under consideration" and disputed that it was a gift.
- The Trump Organization says it does not deal directly with foreign governments. Its revised ethics pledge bars direct deals with foreign governments but not deals abroad.
- Critics include Sen. Schumer ("premium foreign influence with extra legroom"), Sen. Jack Reed ("national embarrassment") and ethics lawyer Norman Eisen.
- The 2026 sales have their own security justification, Iran's strikes on Gulf states. No source links them to Trump family business.
