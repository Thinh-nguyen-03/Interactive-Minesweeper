# C39: Dominari Holdings & the Trump Sons' Small-Cap Network

**Verdict:** Mostly accurate · medium confidence  
**Type:** Check · Family Ventures & Wealth

## The Claim

> Did Donald Trump Jr. and Eric Trump become advisers to and shareholders of Trump Tower-based broker Dominari Holdings, and how is Dominari tied to their other listed ventures?

## Bottom Line

The core facts hold: Don Jr. and Eric Trump joined Trump Tower-based Dominari Holdings as advisers on Feb. 10-11, 2025.

## What Holds Up

- Don Jr. and Eric Trump became advisers to and major shareholders of Dominari Holdings.
- Dominari's stock soared after the Trump sons arrived (mostly accurate)
- Dominari is based in Trump Tower.
- Dominari co-founded American Data Centers/American Bitcoin with the Trump sons.
- Dominari co-underwrote the Trump sons' SPAC, and its president Kyle Wool sits on the SPAC board.
- The sons made large gains via Dominari-linked deals (mostly accurate)
- Dominari's business boomed after the sons joined.
- Unusual Machines: a Trump son's arrival preceded a share-price spike.

## What Needs Correcting

- Thumzup: a Trump son's arrival preceded a share-price spike (false)
- GrabAGun/Colombier II: a Trump son's arrival preceded a share-price spike (false)
- The claim implies the stock soared after the sons arrived. Most of DOMH's surge (about 530-580%) happened in the six weeks before the Feb. 11, 2025 announcement.
- The claim says Thumzup and GrabAGun spiked after a Trump son arrived. Thumzup fell more than 14% on the stake disclosure, and GrabAGun fell about 30% in its first two days.
- GrabAGun/Colombier II is an Omeed Malik deal; no Dominari role was found.
- No SEC or FINRA scrutiny of Dominari or the sons is documented. The scrutiny is a congressional letter (March 2026) and a FINRA fine on Revere, Wool's former firm.
- The 'half a billion dollars' gain is mostly paper value at American Bitcoin's peak, and that stock later fell more than 90%.
- 16 Nov 2026: Wording confirmed: no sale of Dominari shares by Donald Trump Jr. or Eric Trump is documented. Schedule 13G filings in 2026 show Donald Trump Jr.'s holding rising through warrant exercises (1,182,276 shares in May 2026; 1,398,414 in a Sept 2026 filing), and the shares fell to $0.78 by 13 Nov 2026.

## What Is Unproven

- Dominari or the Trump sons face SEC or FINRA scrutiny.

## In More Detail

- Each took about a 6.7% stake (roughly 13% combined) through share grants and a $3.47 private placement, and DOMH soared.
- Dominari co-formed American Data Centers/American Bitcoin with them and co-led their SPAC's IPO, with president Kyle Wool on the SPAC board.
- The claim overreaches in two places.
- Most of DOMH's spike came before the announcement.
- Thumzup and GrabAGun fell rather than spiked on the Trump news.
- No SEC or FINRA action against Dominari or the sons is documented; the scrutiny on record is a March 2026 House China committee letter on Dominari's Chinese IPOs and a 2026 FINRA fine on Wool's former firm, Revere.

## Sub-Claims

### 1. Don Jr. and Eric Trump became advisers to and major shareholders of Dominari Holdings.

**Confirmed**

- Advisory agreements date to Feb. 10, 2025, with an initial grant of 250,000 shares each.
- Each brother also bought about $1M in the $13.5M placement priced at $3.47 per unit.
- 13G filings show 966,138 shares each (6.71%) as of Feb. 21, 2025, plus 432,276 warrant shares each, putting each near 10% fully diluted.
- Their exact cost basis is not disclosed.
- Barron's noted the 13Gs excluded the 432,276 warrant shares as 'not currently exercisable due to certain beneficial ownership limitations', which kept each brother below the 10% insider threshold.
- Amounts: about $1M; $13.5M; $3.47; 6.71%; 10%
- Dates: Feb. 10, 2025; Feb. 21, 2025
- Sources: [1](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/12239/000121390026036897/ea0283159-10k_domin.htm) [3](https://itiger.com/news/2516537088) [2](https://www.itiger.com/news/2560137003) [4](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2025-05-19/how-donald-trump-jr-eric-trump-could-make-millions-from-dominari-shares)

### 2. Dominari's stock soared after the Trump sons arrived.

**Mostly accurate**

- DOMH was under $1 in December 2024.
- It rose about 529-580% in the six weeks before the Feb. 11 announcement, jumped as much as 84% on the day, and gained about 300% in the week.
- Forbes found daily volume went from about 11,500 to about 1.2M shares from Dec. 30; TipRanks gave different figures (under 2,000 to over 200,000 shares in the days before), so volume numbers vary by outlet and window.
- Most of the spike preceded the public news.
- The stock ended 2025 just under $5.
- Amounts: under $1; about 529-580%; 84%; about 300%; under $5
- Dates: December 2024; Feb. 11; Dec. 30
- Sources: [3](https://itiger.com/news/2516537088) [6](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2025-02-11/trump-s-sons-back-friend-s-penny-stock-spurring-84-price-jump) [7](https://www.axios.com/2025/02/14/trump-dominari-stock) [8](https://www.joemygod.com/2025/03/forbes-trump-sons-suspected-of-insider-trading/) [5](https://www.tipranks.com/news/the-fly/trading-in-obscure-stocks-soared-before-trump-appointments-disclosed-ft-says) [9](https://www.biospace.com/press-releases/dominari-2025-revenue-surges-487-in-2025-balance-sheet-strengthens-significantly) [40](https://www.tipranks.com/news/trump-family-appointments-spark-suspicious-surge-in-2-stocks-before-news-breaks)

### 6. The sons made large gains via Dominari-linked deals.

**Mostly accurate**

- Bloomberg said Wool helped them make 'more than half a billion dollars,' mostly the peak paper value of American Bitcoin, which has since fallen more than 90%.
- In May 2025 they could sell a combined $9.6M of Dominari stock registered for resale.
- No sale of Dominari shares by either brother is documented, and MarketWatch corrected an erroneous sale report.
- Later filings point the other way: Schedule 13G filings for Donald Trump Jr. show 1,182,276 shares (5.23%) as of 22 May 2026 and 1,398,414 shares (5.77%) in a September 2026 filing that reports his exercise of 216,138 Series A warrants on 18 Aug 2026 (filings seen as search excerpts, not read in full).
- The stock closed at $0.78 on 13 Nov 2026, against $3.47 in the Feb 2025 placement and just under $5 at end-2025, so the registered stock's $9.6M resale value of May 2025 has largely gone.
- By our arithmetic, 1,398,414 shares were worth about $1.1M at that close.
- Amounts: more than 90%; $9.6M; 5.23%; 5.77%; $0.78
- Dates: May 2025; 22 May 2026; September 2026; 18 Aug 2026; 13 Nov 2026

## Denials & Context

- Dominari: it had 'no reason to believe that any of these people breached any duty, whether legal or fiduciary' and did not know why investors bought before the announcement.
- Dominari spokesman Brett Maas (May 2025): neither advisers nor investors had sold shares.
- The sons call themselves passive investors in American Ventures, with no operating role and no dealings with federal agencies for its portfolio companies.
- Eric Trump denied any role in Dominari's Tron/SRM deal.
- Trump Organization: suggestions of the brothers' involvement in the Kazakhstan tungsten award are 'categorically false, misleading and libelous'; their Skyline stake predated the deal.
- Dominari, on the House CCP letter: IPOs were under 10% of its banking revenue, and it ended Chinese IPO work in 2024 and Hong Kong IPO work in mid-2025.
- Former White House ethics officials (WSJ) warn that the network lets investors with business before the government bet on the family's interests.
- Most primary pages (Bloomberg, WSJ, FT, Barron's, House committee PDF) were not read directly; findings rely on search excerpts and syndicated copies.

## Full Report

**Verdict:** mostly accurate (medium-high confidence)

**Summary.** The core of the claim holds up. Donald Trump Jr. and Eric Trump joined the advisory board of Dominari Holdings (Nasdaq: DOMH), a Trump Tower-based investment bank and broker, on Feb. 10-11, 2025. Each took about a 6.7% stake (roughly 13% combined), and the stock soared. Dominari co-formed American Data Centers with them; that company became American Bitcoin. Dominari Securities co-led the IPO of New America Acquisition I, whose advisory board includes its president Kyle Wool. The same bank arranged deals for Unusual Machines, Thumzup and SRM/Tron Inc.

Two parts of the claim overreach:
- **The "Trump son arrives, stock spikes" pattern is not general.** It fits Dominari and Unusual Machines, and in both cases most of the run-up came *before* the public announcement. It does not fit Thumzup, which fell about 14% when Don Jr.'s stake was disclosed, or GrabAGun, which lost about 30% in its first two trading days.
- **No regulator has acted against Dominari or the sons.** I found no SEC or FINRA action. The documented scrutiny is a March 2026 House China Select Committee letter about Dominari's Chinese small-cap IPOs, plus a 2026 FINRA fine against Revere Securities, the firm Wool ran before Dominari.

**Access note.** Bloomberg, WSJ, FT, Barron's, the House committee site and other primary pages were not read directly. The findings below rest on search-result excerpts, syndicated copies and secondary reports, cross-checked across several outlets.

### The claim

This check builds on C18 (American Bitcoin) and C27 (the New America SPAC). It claims that:
- Don Jr. and Eric became advisers to and major shareholders of Dominari, whose stock soared.
- Dominari co-founded American Data Centers/American Bitcoin with them.
- Dominari co-underwrote their SPAC, and Wool sits on its board.
- In related small-cap deals (Thumzup, GrabAGun/Colombier II, Unusual Machines), a Trump son's arrival preceded a share-price spike.

This check asks for stakes, gains, timing, any SEC or FINRA scrutiny, and denials.

C18 and C27 already established the American Bitcoin structure and the SPAC terms. They are only summarized here.

### Evidence by sub-claim

1. **The sons became Dominari advisers and major shareholders: confirmed.**
   - Dominari's FY2025 10-K dates the advisory agreements to Feb. 10, 2025. It says the company initially issued 250,000 shares each to Don Jr. and Eric when they were appointed [1].
   - Each brother also invested in the Feb. 11 private placement. The units were priced at $3.47 per share plus two warrants. WSJ says each bought $1M of stock in a $13.5M round [2][3].
   - Schedule 13G filings show 966,138 shares each (6.71%) as of Feb. 21, 2025, excluding 432,276 warrant shares each. Including those would put each brother near 10% [3].
   - FactSet, cited by Bloomberg, ranks them as the largest holders after insiders Hayes, Wool and Yu, with stakes worth more than $6M each in May 2025 [4]. FT reporting puts them at about 12% combined later on [16].
   - Barron's notes the 13G forms do not say what the sons paid. The exact cost basis remains unverified.

2. **Dominari's stock soared: confirmed, but mostly before the news.**
   - The stock traded below $1 in December 2024. Barron's reports it rose 529% from Dec. 31 to Feb. 10, the day before the announcement. The FT put the six-week gain at about 580% [3][5].
   - On the announcement it jumped as much as 84% intraday (Bloomberg), and Axios reported it up about 300% in a week [6][7].
   - Forbes found that average daily volume went from about 11,500 shares to about 1.2M after Dec. 30. Securities lawyers called this "suspicious" [8].
   - The company says the price was just over $1 at the start of 2025 and just under $5 at year end [9].

3. **Dominari is a Trump Tower-based broker: confirmed.**
   - It is a former biotech (Spherix, then AIkido) that bought a broker-dealer and rebranded as Dominari [2][4].
   - It holds two Trump Tower leases totaling more than $62,000 a month, on floors 22-23, two floors from the Trump Organization's headquarters [2][10].

4. **Dominari co-founded American Data Centers/American Bitcoin with them: confirmed (see C18).**
   - Dominari held about 32% of American Data Centers [3].
   - At American Bitcoin's Nasdaq debut in September 2025, Dominari held about 23.2M ABTC shares, worth about $170M [9].
   - Dominari reported selling its American Bitcoin investment in January 2026 for $32.4M in cash, against a $39.4M book value [9].
   - A later quarterly release still mentions a valuation change on the ABTC investment, so whether Dominari sold all of its stake is unverified.

5. **Dominari co-underwrote the SPAC, and Wool sits on its board: confirmed (see C27).**
   - Dominari Securities and D. Boral Capital led New America Acquisition I's $345M IPO, which closed in December 2025.
   - Kyle Wool, president of Dominari Holdings and head of Dominari Securities, sits on the SPAC's board and advisory board.

6. **The sons made large gains through Dominari deals: mostly accurate, with the gains largely on paper.**
   - Bloomberg (Oct. 13, 2025) wrote that Wool "has helped Donald Jr. and Eric make more than half a billion dollars." Most of that is the paper value of the American Bitcoin stake at its peak, which C18 documents falling more than 90% [10].

Later filings point the other way: Schedule 13G filings for Donald Trump Jr. show 1,182,276 shares (5.23%) as of 22 May 2026 and 1,398,414 shares (5.77%) in a September 2026 filing that reports his exercise of 216,138 Series A warrants on 18 Aug 2026 (seen as search excerpts, not read in full).
   - DOMH closed at $0.78 on 13 Nov 2026, against $3.47 in the Feb 2025 placement and just under $5 at end-2025. By our arithmetic, 1,398,414 shares were worth about $1.1M at that close; the May 2025 resale value of $9.6M has largely gone.

7. **Dominari prospered after the sons joined: confirmed (context).**
   - Revenue rose 452% in Q2 2025, to $34M from $6.17M [13].

    - I could not establish whether the conduct covered Wool's tenure. I also could not check Wool's own BrokerCheck record.
    - Dominari predecessor Spherix had Barry Honig-linked investors in 2013-14. Honig settled the SEC's 2018 pump-and-dump case without admitting or denying it. This link comes mainly from one newsletter (Wendy Siegelman), which itself says it shows no wrongdoing by Dominari [30].

### Corrections
- The claim implies the stock soared after the sons arrived. In fact most of Dominari's surge (about 530-580%) came in the six weeks *before* the Feb. 11, 2025 announcement, and only part of it after.
- The claim says Thumzup and GrabAGun spiked after a Trump son arrived. Both fell on the Trump news. Thumzup dropped more than 14% on the stake disclosure, and GrabAGun lost about 30% in its first two days.
- GrabAGun/Colombier II is an Omeed Malik deal with no documented Dominari role.
- "SEC/FINRA scrutiny": none of Dominari or the sons is documented. The scrutiny on record is a congressional letter (House China committee, March 2026) and a FINRA fine against Revere, Wool's previous firm.
- The "half a billion dollars" gain is mostly paper value from American Bitcoin's peak, and that value has since largely evaporated.

### Denials and other side
- Dominari said it had "no reason to believe that any of these people breached any duty, whether legal or fiduciary," and did not know why investors bought before the announcement [3].
- Dominari spokesman Brett Maas said neither advisers nor investors had sold shares (May 2025) [4].
- The sons describe themselves as "passive" investors in Dominari's American Ventures, with no operating role and no dealings with federal agencies for portfolio companies [16].
- In the Kazakhstan tungsten (Skyline Builders/Cove Kaz) matter, the Trump Organization called suggestions of the brothers' involvement "categorically false, misleading and libelous" and said their investment predated the deal [31].
- Eric Trump denied any role in Dominari's Tron/SRM deal [32].
- Don Jr.'s spokesperson denied contacting the administration for Unusual Machines (see C32).
- On the other side, former White House ethics officials quoted by WSJ say the network gives investors with business before the government a way to bet on the family [2].

### What remains allegation or insinuation
- **Insider trading before the announcements.** The pre-announcement trading in DOMH and UMAC is described as "suspicious" by commentators. No regulator has charged anyone.
- **Government influence.** That the sons trade on access to the administration is an inference. No filing shows them directing deals or contacting agencies.
- **Honig links.** These are historical and come from a single source.
