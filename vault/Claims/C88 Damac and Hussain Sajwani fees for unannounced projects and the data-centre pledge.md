# C88: Damac and Hussain Sajwani: fees for unannounced projects and the data-centre pledge

**Verdict:** Mostly accurate · medium confidence  
**Origin:** Connection hunt

## Bottom line

The money and the relationship are well documented. Damac paid about $10M in 2025 to two new Trump licensing entities that CREW says appear tied to unannounced Abu Dhabi developments, with no launched project by Nov 2026.

## What holds up

- Damac paid about $10M in 2025 for two Abu Dhabi Trump licences, and as of Nov 2026 no Trump-branded Abu Dhabi project has been launched.
- The $20B data-centre pledge has turned into land in Ohio and New Jersey and a planned site in Kansas; nothing is reported as operating (mostly accurate)
- The Trump-Sajwani relationship goes back more than a decade and kept going through the transition and into the presidency.
- Damac and Sajwani's public statements about the Trump ties and the 2025-26 payments.

## What needs correcting

- CREW links Damac's Ohio project to rising Ohio utility prices (misleading)
- **Claim:** Brief implies federal permitting or energy decisions benefited Damac's sites  
  **Record:** None was found. The documented approvals are local (Edgerton council override, 16 Jul 2026; Canton annexation and water).
- **Claim:** Brief lists 'New Year's Eve at Mar-a-Lago' as one event  
  **Record:** The documented on-stage praise is NYE 2016. NYE 2024 attendance rests on Sajwani's own posts as relayed by Raw Story.
- Some crypto outlets call the crypto-accepting Trump Tower Dubai a Damac project; it is Dar Global's.
- Damac's US footprint (New Jersey; Edgerton, Kansas) includes states that were not among the eight announced on 7 Jan 2025.
- One syndicated AP summary renders a disclosure figure as Sajwani 'contributing' $1-5M to Trump's 2016 campaign. That looks garbled (foreign nationals cannot donate), and it is not used here.

## What is unproven

- Federal permitting or energy decisions have benefited Damac's sites.
- DT Marks Al Raha Beach LLC was formed the same day as the data-centre permitting order.
- Sajwani or Damac has dealings with Trump family crypto ventures (WLF, USD1, $TRUMP).

## In more detail

- Sajwani's ties to Trump run from the 2013 golf deal through Mar-a-Lago, an April 2025 Oval Office visit and the May 2025 Abu Dhabi forum.
- The $20B pledge has become about $12B of land and power: a $36.5M site in Canton, Ohio, a New Jersey site and an $860M Kansas conversion, none of them operating.
- Every approval found is local.
- No federal permit, FAST-41 designation or energy decision for a Damac site was found, and no Sajwani link to WLF, USD1 or $TRUMP turned up.

### 1. Damac paid about $10M in 2025 for two Abu Dhabi Trump licences, and as of Nov 2026 no Trump-branded Abu Dhabi project has been launched.

**Confirmed**

- Evidence grade: documented (Trump's 2026 OGE disclosure as read by CREW, CNBC and The National).
- This repeats C70/C82 only to bring it up to date.

- New since C82: searches through Nov 2026 found no Damac or Trump Organization launch in Abu Dhabi.
- One property-portal analysis expects a Damac Abu Dhabi villa community in 2026 but names no site and no Trump brand.

- CREW (Mar 2026) puts cumulative Damac payments across disclosures at 'at least $13 million, and possibly as much as $19 million'.
- Disclosure forms partly use value ranges, so all of these totals are estimates.
- Amounts: $5M; $11.3M; about $12.5M; about $6M; at least $13 million

### 2. The $20B data-centre pledge has turned into land in Ohio and New Jersey and a planned site in Kansas; nothing is reported as operating.

**Mostly accurate**

- Evidence grade: documented for the pledge, the Davos update and the Canton and Edgerton sites.
- The New Jersey site is single-source on details.
- On 7 Jan 2025 Sajwani pledged at least $20B through Edgnex (now branded DAMAC Digital) for about 2GW across eight states (TX, AZ, OK, LA, OH, IL, MI, IN).
- He said 60-70% would be bank or bond debt.
- At Davos in Jan 2026 he said Damac had secured land and power worth about $12B, roughly 1GW, with land bought in Ohio and New Jersey and Texas under review.
- New Jersey is not on the original eight-state list.
- Ohio: county property records cited by CREW and trackers show DAMAC Digital Solution Ohio paid $36.5M in Dec 2025 for about 94 acres in south-east Canton (Stark County), bought from an Active Infrastructure-linked LLC (AIDC Canton 2).
- The 'Canton Orchard View' / Midwest Digital Gateway campus is on AEP Ohio's network and is planned for 2028, with phases to 2033.
- Canton annexed the land in late 2025.
- No megawatt figure has been published.
- Kansas: DAMAC Digital Solutions plans an $860M conversion of a nearly 400,000 sq ft warehouse at Logistics Park Kansas City in Edgerton.
- Edgerton is not on the original list either.
- Nothing operating in the US was found as of Nov 2026, and the 2-4 year build-out Sajwani gave runs to 2028-2030.
- Amounts: at least $20B; 60-70%; about $12B; $36.5M; $860M
- Dates: 7 Jan 2025; Jan 2026; Dec 2025; late 2025; Nov 2026

### 3. Federal permitting or energy decisions have benefited Damac's sites.

**Unverified** · Evidence: Documented for the general policies

- Any Damac-specific federal benefit is not found.
- On 10 Dec 2024 Trump posted that anyone investing $1B or more would get 'fully expedited approvals and permits, including, but not limited to, all Environmental approvals'.
- On 7 Jan 2025 he suggested expedited reviews for Sajwani's projects.
- EO 14318 (23 Jul 2025) defines qualifying data-centre projects by a commitment of $500M or more in capex, or more than 100MW of new load, among other tests.
- Such projects can get FAST-41 coverage and Commerce financial support.
- Edgerton ($860M) and probably the Canton campus would meet the dollar test.
- That is our inference: no source says either site has applied for or received FAST-41 coverage, federal financing, a DOE or FERC decision, or any other federal action.
- The approvals actually documented are local.
- Edgerton's planning commission denied the site plan 3-2 in June 2026.
- On 16 Jul 2026 the city council unanimously overrode that denial at a special meeting with no public comment.
- In Aug 2026 the city sued residents to void a citizen petition to ban hyperscale data centres.
- Canton handled the annexation and water service; its water superintendent estimated $3.7M to extend service to the site.
- Edgerton's project page says Damac sought no tax abatements.
- CNBC found no evidence that Damac's payments influenced federal policy.
- Amounts: $1B; $500M; $860M; $3.7M
- Dates: 10 Dec 2024; 7 Jan 2025; 23 Jul 2025; June 2026; 16 Jul 2026
- Sources: [26](https://fortune.com/2024/12/10/trump-permits-billion-dollar-investment-us-work-expedite/) [27](https://www.news4jax.com/business/2024/12/10/making-a-1b-investment-in-the-us-trump-pledges-expedited-permits-but-there-are-hurdles/) [7](https://fortune.com/2025/01/07/trump-announces-hussain-sajwani-20-billion-us-data-centers-investment/) [25](https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2025/07/accelerating-federal-permitting-of-data-center-infrastructure/) <https://www.presidency.ucsb.edu/documents/executive-order-14318-accelerating-federal-permitting-data-center-infrastructure> [20](https://www.kshb.com/news/local-news/data-centers/edgerton-city-council-overrides-data-center-development-denial-pushing-project-forward) [21](https://www.kcur.org/housing-development-section/2026-07-25/edgerton-data-center-city-council-planning-commission) [22](https://www.kctv5.com/2026/08/14/edgerton-calls-citizen-petition-void-invalid-files-suit-block-data-center-ban/) [19](https://johnsoncountypost.com/2026/06/11/edgerton-denies-data-center-288433/) [23](https://edgertonks.org/damac-data-center/) [3](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/10/trump-foreign-real-estate-licensing-income-gulf-deals.html) [4](https://timesbrasil.com.br/mundo/negocio-de-licenciamento-imobiliario-de-trump-no-exterior-dispara-para-us-595-milhoes-com-pagamentos-de-incorporadoras-do-golfo/)

### 8. CREW links Damac's Ohio project to rising Ohio utility prices.

**Misleading** · Evidence: Documented that CREW makes the claim (12 Mar 2026)

- CREW sets Damac's $36.5M Canton purchase beside a Feb 2026 Canton city utility-rate rise driven partly by electricity costs.
- Damac's Canton campus is not built and is planned for 2028, so it cannot have caused 2025-26 price rises.
- FactCheck.org (Oct 2026) says data centres contributed to Ohio's capacity-market increases (one estimate: about $16 a month, 63% of it attributed to data centres), but the cause is broader.
- The headline juxtaposition is CREW's framing, not evidence that Damac affected prices.
- Amounts: $36.5M; about $16; 63%
- Dates: 12 Mar 2026; Feb 2026; 2025-26; Oct 2026
- Sources: [2](https://www.citizensforethics.org/reports-investigations/crew-investigations/data-centers-are-spiking-ohio-utility-prices-one-developer-is-trumps-business-partner/) [28](https://www.factcheck.org/2026/10/data-centers-and-electricity-prices-its-not-as-simple-as-voters-may-think/) [15](https://baxtel.com/data-center/damac-canton-orchard-view)

## Denials and context

- Damac did not respond to CNBC (Aug 2026) and has not publicly addressed CREW's reports.
- CNBC found no evidence that Damac's payments influenced the July 2025 permitting order; the order is general policy covering any large data-centre sponsor.
- Hope Hicks (Jan 2017) said Trump and Sajwani did not discuss business at the 2016 New Year's party; Trump said he declined Damac's 2017 offer to avoid conflicts.
- The White House says there are no conflicts of interest and that Trump's businesses are run by his children.
- Sajwani conditioned the $20B on market conditions and bank financing.
  - Analysts (E&E News, Jan 2025; Bloomberg via Fortune) questioned grid capacity and his ability to fund it.
- Background: an Egyptian court convicted Sajwani in absentia in 2011 over a land deal; he called it politically motivated (BuzzFeed, 2017).
  - This is not tied to any Trump decision.

### Connections (sub-claims)

1. **About $10M for two Abu Dhabi licences, with still no project.** *Documented* fees; CREW's own wording is that the two entities "appear to be related to unannounced developments in Abu Dhabi". The disclosure facts are in C82. New here: nothing was launched through Nov 2026. CNBC says the Trump Organization has no active Abu Dhabi development. Estimates of cumulative Damac payments vary: about $6M a year (Forbes, Oct 2025); "at least $13M, possibly $19M" across disclosures (CREW, Mar 2026); $11.3M or $12.5M for 2025 alone (CNBC, or CREW and The National).
2. **The pledge on the ground.** *Documented* (New Jersey details single-source). The $20B has become about $12B of land and power: a $36.5M site in Canton, Ohio (AEP Ohio territory, target 2028, phases to 2033, no MW published), a New Jersey site with no reported details, and an $860M warehouse conversion in Edgerton, Kansas. New Jersey and Kansas were not among the eight states announced. Nothing is operating. Sajwani said 60-70% would be debt-financed and that the $20B depends on "the opportunity, the market".
3. **Federal permitting or energy benefits.** *Unverified.* The general policies are documented: the 10 Dec 2024 $1B pledge, the 7 Jan 2025 remark, and EO 14318 (FAST-41 eligibility and Commerce financing for projects with $500M+ capex or more than 100MW of load). No source shows a FAST-41 designation, federal loan, or DOE or FERC action for any Damac site. That the sites would meet the order's thresholds is our inference. The approvals actually documented are local: the Edgerton override and the Canton annexation and water service. CNBC found no evidence that the fees influenced policy.
4. **Same-day formation of DT Marks Al Raha Beach and the EO.** *Single-source (CREW).* No second source was found (see C82).
5. **The relationship.** *Documented.* It is unusually continuous: licensing since about 2013; family occasions (the 2017 club opening and the 2018 wedding); New Year's Eve at Mar-a-Lago (2016 documented, 2024 single-source); and during the presidency, an Oval Office visit (Apr 2025) and the Abu Dhabi forum (May 2025). This documents access. It does not show any request or favour.
6. **Crypto, WLF and $TRUMP.** *Unverified / not found.* Damac's only documented crypto deal is the MANTRA tokenisation, which has no Trump link. Outlets that call Trump Tower Dubai a Damac project have it wrong: it is Dar Global's.
7. **Damac's statements.** *Documented.* Damac has made no comment on the 2025-26 payments (it did not respond to CNBC). Its public posture is promotional (Nov 2024, Apr 2025 statements). In 2017 it confirmed the declined offer.
8. **CREW's Ohio utility-price framing.** *Misleading as a causal implication.* The Canton campus is unbuilt (target 2028), so it cannot explain the 2025-26 rate rises. FactCheck.org says data centres are one driver of Ohio's increases among several.

### Corrections

- No federal permitting or energy decision for a Damac site was found. Every approval documented is local.
- The documented on-stage NYE praise is from 2016. NYE 2024 rests on Sajwani's posts as relayed by Raw Story.
- Trump Tower Dubai (which accepts crypto) is Dar Global's, not Damac's, despite some crypto-press wording.
- Damac's US sites include New Jersey and Edgerton, Kansas, which were not among the eight states announced in Jan 2025.
- An AP-syndicated summary saying Sajwani "contributed" $1-5M to Trump's 2016 campaign appears garbled (foreign nationals cannot donate) and is not used.

### Denials and other side

- Damac did not respond to CNBC. No Damac comment on CREW's reports was found.
- CNBC found no evidence that the payments influenced the permitting order, which is general policy.
- In 2017 Trump said he declined Damac's offer to avoid conflicts. Hope Hicks said the two did not discuss business at the 2016 party.
- The White House says there are no conflicts and that the business is run by Trump's children (C70).

- Background only: Sajwani's 2011 in-absentia conviction in Egypt, which he called politically motivated (BuzzFeed, 2017). It is unrelated to any Trump decision.
