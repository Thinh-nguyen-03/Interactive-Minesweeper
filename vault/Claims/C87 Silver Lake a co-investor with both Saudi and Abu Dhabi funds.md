# C87: Silver Lake: a co-investor with both Saudi and Abu Dhabi funds

**Verdict:** Mostly accurate · medium confidence  
**Type:** Connection check · Tech and media companies

## The claim

> Is Silver Lake a partner in the EA buyout with PIF and Kushner's Affinity and in the TikTok US joint venture with MGX, and which of its deals needed US government approval?

## Bottom line

Silver Lake's dual position is well documented. Mubadala has owned a passive minority stake in the firm since 2020, and Silver Lake has held G42 shares (with Durban on the board) since 2021.

## What holds up

- Mubadala (Abu Dhabi) owns a minority equity stake in Silver Lake itself and anchors a $2B, 25-year Silver Lake strategy.
- Silver Lake is a G42 shareholder and its co-CEO Egon Durban sits on G42's board.
- Silver Lake and Tahnoon's MGX co-invested in Intel's Altera chip unit in 2025, the same month the TikTok framework was approved.
- Silver Lake is a 15% managing investor in the TikTok US joint venture, and Durban sits on its board.
- Silver Lake controls Endeavor (majority owner of TKO/UFC/WWE), taken private in 2025 with Mubadala as co-investor; TKO has Saudi state partnerships, an Ellison media deal and staged UFC on the White House lawn.

## What needs correcting

- The EA buyout began as a Durban-Kushner conversation, and Silver Lake brought in PIF (partly accurate)
- Cross-deal pattern: Silver Lake is the one private firm co-owning US-approval-dependent assets with both the Saudi/Kushner side and the Tahnoon/Abu Dhabi side (partly accurate)
- The claim implies a Mubadala 'stake in Silver Lake' that could confer influence. The 2020 interest was bought from Dyal's passive, non-voting under-10% holding, and its size was undisclosed.
- The claim lists G42 and Mubadala ties but not Altera. MGX also co-invested with Silver Lake in the 51% Altera purchase (Sept 2025), so Silver Lake and MGX co-own two US assets, not one.
- Mubadala is chaired by Mansour bin Zayed, not Tahnoon. It links to the Tahnoon network through MGX, which it co-founded.
- Some coverage of the Unity talks calls Silver Lake Kushner's firm. That is wrong: Kushner's firm is Affinity, and no report puts Affinity in the Unity consortium.

## What is unproven

- Silver Lake and PIF are working together again on a ~$25B Unity Software take-private (Nov 2026).
- Durban/Silver Lake principals have documented political donations or administration ties.

## In more detail

- It co-owns the TikTok US JV and Intel's Altera with Tahnoon's MGX, and co-bought EA with PIF and Kushner's Affinity, a deal Reuters says began with Durban and Kushner.
- It is reportedly in Unity takeover talks with PIF again.
- We found no documented Trump-related donations, lobbying or administration roles for Durban or Silver Lake.
- The idea that Silver Lake is a hub between the two Trump-connected Gulf networks is our own cross-reference, and nothing we found shows coordination or a quid pro quo.

## Sub-claims

### 1. Mubadala (Abu Dhabi) owns a minority equity stake in Silver Lake itself and anchors a $2B, 25-year Silver Lake strategy.

**Confirmed**

- Evidence grade: documented (joint Silver Lake/Mubadala release, Business Wire, 2020-09-30; TechCrunch, The National).
- Mubadala bought a minority interest in Silver Lake's management company in a secondary purchase from Dyal Capital (Neuberger Berman).
- Dyal had held a passive, non-voting interest of under 10% since 2016 and kept about half of it.
- Mubadala also committed $2B to a new long-term Silver Lake strategy with a 25-year horizon that co-invests alongside the flagship funds.
- The size of Mubadala's interest and its price were not disclosed by the parties; contemporaneous wire coverage (Investing.com) put it at under 5%, consistent with buying about half of Dyal's under-10% holding.
- It is described as passive and non-voting, so it shows an economic link, not control.
- Mubadala is chaired by Sheikh Mansour bin Zayed, not Tahnoon, but it is a founding partner of Tahnoon's MGX (C57).
- Amounts: under 10%; $2B; under 5%; 10%
- Dates: 2020-09-30
- Sources: [1](https://www.silverlake.com/silver-lake-and-mubadala-partner-to-establish-unique-long-term-investment-strategy) [2](https://www.mubadala.com/en/news/silver-lake-and-mubadala-partner-establish-unique-long-term-investment-strategy) [3](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20200930005365/en/Silver-Lake-and-Mubadala-Partner-to-Establish-Unique-Long-term-Investment-Strategy) [4](https://techcrunch.com/2020/09/30/silverlake-adds-a-2-billion-long-only-hedge-fund-backed-by-abu-dhabi-to-its-tech-finance-toolkit) <https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/mubadala-to-invest-2-billion-in-us-private-equity-firm-silver-lake-2311292>

### 2. Silver Lake is a G42 shareholder and its co-CEO Egon Durban sits on G42's board.

**Confirmed**

- Evidence grade: documented for the stake and the board seat (Gulf News, The National, April 2021; Durban's own Silver Lake bio lists G42 among his boards).
- The roughly $800M price is single-source: the WSJ cited unnamed people, and the terms were never disclosed.
- Silver Lake was G42's first big US investor, three years before Microsoft's $1.5B stake (2024).
- Bloomberg (about 2026-09, relayed by AI Weekly) lists Silver Lake, Mubadala and Microsoft as existing G42 holders while G42 holds exploratory talks on selling a majority stake to US buyers to keep its licence-free chip access past about April 2027 (C80).
- No report names Silver Lake as a prospective buyer.
- So Silver Lake already has a director inside a company whose ownership is being redesigned around a US export-control decision.
- Amounts: roughly $800M; $1.5B
- Dates: April 2021; 2026-09; April 2027
- Sources: [6](https://gulfnews.com/business/us-based-investment-firm-picks-up-stake-in-abu-dhabi-tech-firm-group42-1.1618407661489) [5](https://www.thenationalnews.com/business/technology/us-investor-silver-lake-buys-stake-in-abu-dhabi-s-g42-1.1203826) [7](https://www.silverlake.com/people/egon-durban/) [8](https://aiweekly.co/alerts/bloomberg-g42-weighs-selling-majority-stake-to-us-firms-to-safeguard-nvidia)

### 3. Silver Lake and Tahnoon's MGX co-invested in Intel's Altera chip unit in 2025, the same month the TikTok framework was approved.

**Confirmed**

- Evidence grade: documented (WAM state news agency, Reuters via The Star, Semafor, Altera release).
- Intel agreed in April 2025 to sell 51% of Altera at an $8.75B valuation, about $3.3B of equity value for the stake (reports citing a filing; Intel's headline $4.46B is simply 51% of the $8.75B valuation, before debt financing).
- The deal closed on 2025-09-12.
- On about 2025-09-16, MGX said it had joined the Silver Lake-led buyer group, with its contribution undisclosed, and Intel kept 49%.
- Before this, Silver Lake and MGX had been treated as separate co-investors only in the TikTok JV (C57/C68).
- Altera makes the second Silver Lake-MGX co-ownership, and it is in US semiconductors.
- Separately documented: the US government took a 9.9% Intel stake on 2025-08-22 (433.3M shares at $20.47, funded from CHIPS grants).
- Inferred (our cross-reference, asserted by no source): this makes the US government an indirect holder, via Intel, of the 49% of Altera not owned by the Silver Lake/MGX group, three weeks before closing.
- Unverified: we found no report of a CFIUS review of MGX's entry into Altera, even though Altera serves defence and aerospace customers.
- Amounts: 51%; $8.75B; about $3.3B; $4.46B; 49%
- Dates: April 2025; 2025-09-12; 2025-09-16; 2025-08-22
- Sources: [9](https://www.wam.ae/en/article/blqki86-mgx-joins-silver-lake-altera-acquisition) [10](https://www.thestar.com.my/tech/tech-news/2025/09/16/mgx-co-invests-with-silver-lake-in-altera-acquisition) [11](https://www.semafor.com/article/09/17/2025/abi-dhabi-ai-firm-mgx-lands-stake-in-us-chipmaker-altera) [12](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20250915168341/en/Altera-Closes-Silver-Lake-Investment-to-Become-Worlds-Largest-Pure-play-FPGA-Solutions-Provider) [13](https://siliconangle.com/2025/08/22/trump-says-us-government-will-take-10-stake-intel/) [14](https://www.techrepublic.com/article/news-trump-intel-deal-chip-production/) <https://www.arabianbusiness.com/gcc/uae/abu-dhabis-mgx-and-silver-lake-acquire-majority-stake-in-altera>

### 4. Silver Lake is a 15% managing investor in the TikTok US joint venture, and Durban sits on its board.

- Oracle, Silver Lake and MGX each own 15% of TikTok USDS Joint Venture LLC. Existing ByteDance investors hold 30.1% and ByteDance keeps 19.9%.
- The deal closed on 2026-01-22 under Trump's EO of 2025-09-25 and the Bessent-He Lifeng framework.
- Durban holds one of the seven board seats (SCMP; his own Silver Lake bio lists TikTok USDS JV), alongside MGX's David Scott.
- Single-source (syndicated coverage seen only in search snippets): some reports described Silver Lake as having 'backing from Abu Dhabi's Mubadala'.
- That is the only press framing we found that ties Silver Lake's ownership to the Abu Dhabi side of the deal.
- Durban's role in the negotiations is unverified; no report describes it.
- Amounts: 15%; 30.1%; 19.9%
- Dates: 2026-01-22; 2025-09-25

### 5. The EA buyout began as a Durban-Kushner conversation, and Silver Lake brought in PIF.

**Partly accurate**

- Evidence grade: single-source (Reuters, 2025-09-30, citing people familiar, widely syndicated; DealStreetAsia).
- Reuters reported that the talks started between Durban and Kushner in a spring 2025 brainstorming session.
- It said Kushner helped persuade PIF to back a take-private, and Silver Lake saw PIF, which already held about 9.9% of EA, as a natural partner.
- Reuters also said Durban had looked at EA as far back as 2011.
- EA and the consortium did not confirm Kushner's role.
- Documented (from C56/C80 and here): the deal was announced 2025-09-29 at $210 a share; the HSR waiting period expired on 2026-02-09; EA's 2026-07-30 filing said all approvals, CFIUS included, had been obtained; it closed on 2026-08-04 (PIF about 93.4%, Silver Lake about 5.5%, Affinity about 1.1%, press estimates).
- This is the only documented Silver Lake-Affinity co-investment we found.
- We found no Silver Lake commitment as an Affinity LP, and no shared board or staff.
- Amounts: about 9.9%; $210; about 93.4%; about 5.5%; about 1.1%
- Dates: 2025-09-30; 2025-09-29; 2026-02-09; 2026-07-30; 2026-08-04
- Sources: [19](https://www.thestar.com.my/tech/tech-news/2025/10/01/from-riyadh-to-silicon-valley-how-ea-became-the-jewel-of-saudi-arabia039s-gaming-vision) [20](https://media.dealstreetasia.com/stories/electronic-arts-saudi-arabia-gaming-458146) [21](https://www.axios.com/2025/09/29/electronic-arts-saudi-arabia-silver-lake) [22](https://gamesbeat.com/ea-is-poised-to-close-55b-sale-to-saudi-led-group/) [23](https://www.pif.gov.sa/en/news-and-insights/newswire/2026/electronic-arts-announces-completion-of-acquisition-by-pif-silver-lake-and-affinity-partners/) [24](https://www.hsgac.senate.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025-10-14-Letter-from-Blumenthal-and-Warren-to-Secretary-Bessent-re-Electronic-Arts.pdf)

### 9. Cross-deal pattern: Silver Lake is the one private firm co-owning US-approval-dependent assets with both the Saudi/Kushner side and the Tahnoon/Abu Dhabi side.

**Partly accurate**

- Nodes documented; the pattern is inferred (our cross-reference; no outlet we found frames Silver Lake as a hub).
- In 2025-26, Silver Lake co-owned with the Tahnoon network in G42 (board seat), the TikTok JV (MGX, 15% each, presidential EO) and Altera (MGX co-investor).
- It co-owned with PIF and Affinity in EA (CFIUS-cleared) and was reported in talks with PIF on Unity.
- In 2025 Mubadala co-invested with it in Endeavor, and Mubadala owns part of Silver Lake itself.
- Each of the TikTok, EA and (by inference) Altera deals needed or plausibly needed federal sign-off in the window when Tahnoon's Aryam held 49% of WLF (C06/C57) and Kushner's Affinity held PIF's $2B (C15).
- This shows that Silver Lake is a recurring, trusted partner for Gulf sovereign capital in the US.
- It does not show that Silver Lake brokered, benefited from or influenced any Trump-family deal.
- Amounts: 15%; 49%; $2B
- Dates: 2025-26
- Sources: [15](https://fortune.com/2025/12/19/tiktok-agrees-u-s-joint-venture-deal-with-oracle-silver-lake-and-mgx/) [9](https://www.wam.ae/en/article/blqki86-mgx-joins-silver-lake-altera-acquisition) [19](https://www.thestar.com.my/tech/tech-news/2025/10/01/from-riyadh-to-silicon-valley-how-ea-became-the-jewel-of-saudi-arabia039s-gaming-vision) [1](https://www.silverlake.com/silver-lake-and-mubadala-partner-to-establish-unique-long-term-investment-strategy) [28](https://enterpriseam.com/uae/2025/03/27/mubadala-silver-lake-take-nyse-listed-endeavor-private/) [25](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/business/silver-lake-and-the-saudi-arabia-pif-hold-talks-with-unity-to-explore-acquisition)

## Denials and context

- EA, Silver Lake, PIF and Affinity did not confirm Reuters' account of Kushner's role.
  - Unity, Silver Lake and PIF did not comment on the takeover talks.
- The White House rejected conflict-of-interest claims over the UFC White House event, and TKO says it lost money on it.
- Silver Lake is one of the largest technology PE firms and has co-invested with Gulf sovereigns (Mubadala, PIF) and Singapore's GIC for years before 2025.
  - Repeat partnerships are normal in mega-deals and are not in themselves evidence of political influence.
- No source reports a Silver Lake lobbying campaign, a Durban White House meeting, or Trump-directed donations by Silver Lake principals.
  - Older LittleSis-aggregated giving by Silver Lake people leans Democratic and was not verified against the FEC.
- Search results only: Semafor and several other outlets could not be read directly.

### Summary

Silver Lake's dual position is well documented:

- **Abu Dhabi side.** Mubadala has owned a passive minority stake in Silver Lake since 2020. Silver Lake has held shares in Tahnoon's G42 since 2021, and co-CEO Egon Durban sits on G42's board. Silver Lake co-owns two US assets with Tahnoon's MGX: the TikTok US joint venture and, a link not covered before, 51% of Intel's chip unit Altera.
- **Saudi-Kushner side.** Silver Lake co-bought Electronic Arts (EA) with Saudi Arabia's Public Investment Fund (PIF) and Kushner's Affinity. Reuters reported that those talks began between Durban and Kushner. As of November 2026, Silver Lake is reportedly in takeover talks with PIF again, this time for Unity.

This check also asked about donations and administration ties. We found no documented Trump-related donations, no lobbying of the administration and no administration roles for Durban or Silver Lake. The idea that Silver Lake is a hub between the two Trump-connected Gulf networks comes from our own cross-referencing; no outlet frames it that way. Nothing we found shows coordination or an exchange of favours.

Many outlets, including Semafor, could not be read directly. Some findings therefore rest on search-result text and syndicated copies.

### Connections found

#### 1. Mubadala owns part of Silver Lake (documented)

- **What happened.** On 30 Sep 2020, Mubadala bought a minority interest in Silver Lake's management company. It bought from Dyal Capital, which had held a passive, non-voting stake of under 10% since 2016 and kept about half of it.
- **The $2B strategy.** Mubadala also committed $2B to a Silver Lake strategy with a 25-year horizon. The strategy invests alongside Silver Lake's main funds.
- **What is not known.** The parties did not disclose the size of Mubadala's stake or the price. Contemporaneous wire coverage put it at under 5% (audit addition).
- **Context.** Mubadala is chaired by Mansour bin Zayed, not Tahnoon. Mubadala co-founded Tahnoon's MGX (see C57).

Sources [1–4].

#### 2. Silver Lake in G42, with Durban on the board (documented; price single-source)

- **The stake.** Silver Lake bought a minority stake in G42 in April 2021. The roughly $800M price is the WSJ's figure from unnamed sources; the terms were never disclosed.
- **The board seat.** Durban's own Silver Lake biography lists G42 among his boards.
- **Current position.** Bloomberg (around September 2026) lists Silver Lake, Mubadala and Microsoft as existing G42 shareholders. G42 is holding exploratory talks on moving to majority-US ownership so it can keep licence-free chip access under the 2026 export rule from the Commerce Department's Bureau of Industry and Security (C80). No report names Silver Lake as a buyer.

Sources [5–8].

#### 3. Silver Lake and MGX co-own Altera (documented; US-government link inferred)

- **The deal.** Intel's sale of 51% of Altera to a Silver Lake-led group closed on 12 Sep 2025. It valued Altera at $8.75B, or about $3.3B of equity for the stake.
- **MGX's part.** On about 16 Sep 2025, MGX announced it had joined as a co-investor. It did not disclose how much it put in. Intel kept 49%.
- **Why it matters.** This makes Altera the second US asset that Silver Lake and MGX co-own, after TikTok.
- **US government and Intel (documented).** The US government took 9.9% of Intel on 22 Aug 2025.
- **Inferred.** Through Intel, the US government indirectly holds part of the 49% of Altera that Intel kept. No source draws this link.
- **Unverified.** We found no report of a review by CFIUS (the Committee on Foreign Investment in the United States, which screens foreign investment in US companies) of MGX's entry into Altera.

Sources [9–14].
