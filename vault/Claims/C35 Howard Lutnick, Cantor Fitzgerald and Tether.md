# C35: Howard Lutnick, Cantor Fitzgerald and Tether

**Verdict:** Mostly accurate · medium confidence  
**Type:** Check · Regulators and enforcement

## The claim

> Does Cantor Fitzgerald, the former firm of Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick now run by his sons, custody Tether's reserves and hold a stake in Tether while the administration set stablecoin policy, and how complete was his divestment?

## Bottom line

The financial ties are well documented. Cantor custodies Tether's Treasury reserves and holds a $600M convertible bond convertible into ~5% of Tether, negotiated in 2024 under Lutnick.

## What holds up

- Cantor Fitzgerald custodies Tether's reserves.
- Cantor held a stake in Tether (mostly accurate)
- Lutnick divested and Cantor is now run by his sons.
- Cantor launched crypto/bitcoin SPACs, including Twenty One Capital with Tether and SoftBank.

## What needs correcting

- Tether financed the children's trust during the divestiture (partly accurate)
- The administration passed stablecoin legislation that benefited Tether/Cantor (partly accurate)
- 'Held a stake in Tether': it is a $600M convertible bond convertible into ~5%, not direct equity (Lutnick testimony, Bloomberg).
- Congress passed the GENIUS Act and Trump signed it. Commerce has no stablecoin regulatory role (Treasury/OCC).
- The ~$10B current value of the stake is Sen. Blumenthal's estimate, not a disclosed figure.
- Divestiture timing: May 2025 was the announcement and the effective date for BGC/Newmark pieces; the sale of the Cantor interest to the children's trusts closed later (Bloomberg: October 2025), the same month as the 7 Oct 2025 Tether credit filing.

## What is unproven

- Commerce controls AI-chip export licences, creating a Tether-linked conflict.

## In more detail

- Lutnick's divestiture was announced and partly took effect in May 2025, but the sale of his Cantor interest to trusts for his children closed later (Bloomberg: October 2025); Bloomberg reported that Tether lent to one of those trusts under a credit document filed on 7 Oct 2025, around the same time.
- Cantor's SPAC took the Tether/Bitfinex/SoftBank-backed Twenty One Capital public, and Cantor is custodian for Tether's GENIUS-compliant USAT.
- No policy action by Lutnick or Commerce benefiting Tether is documented, and no link between Tether and chip-export licences was found.

## Sub-claims

### 1. Cantor Fitzgerald custodies Tether's reserves.

**Confirmed**

- WSJ (Nov 2024) reported Cantor holds most of Tether's ~$133B in assets for tens of millions of dollars a year in fees.
- Lutnick acknowledged the custody at his 29 Jan 2025 hearing.
- Cantor is also USAT's reserve custodian and primary dealer (Jan 2026).
- Amounts: ~$133B
- Dates: Nov 2024; 29 Jan 2025; Jan 2026
- Sources: [1](https://www.theblock.co/post/327961/lutnick-cantor-fitzgerald-negotiated-a-5-ownership-stake-in-tether-wsj) [4](https://decrypt.co/303457/bitcoin-bull-howard-lutnick-defends-tether-senate) [19](https://decrypt.co/356045/tether-launches-us-regulated-stablecoin-issued-anchorage-digital)

### 2. Cantor held a stake in Tether.

**Mostly accurate**

- The holding is a $600M convertible bond that converts into ~5% equity.
- Lutnick helped negotiate it in April 2024 and testified it is not direct equity.
- Sen. Blumenthal estimates its value at ~$10B (unverified estimate).
- Context for that estimate: Tether sought to raise $15-20B at a ~$500B valuation from Sept 2025, cut the target raise to ~$5B after investor pushback (FT, Feb 2026) and set a two-week commitment deadline in April 2026; no closed round at $500B was found.
- A 5% stake would be ~$25B at $500B, so the ~$10B estimate implies a much lower valuation.
- Amounts: $600M; ~5%; ~$10B; $15-20B; ~$500B
- Dates: April 2024; Sept 2025; Feb 2026; April 2026
- Sources: [1](https://www.theblock.co/post/327961/lutnick-cantor-fitzgerald-negotiated-a-5-ownership-stake-in-tether-wsj) [4](https://decrypt.co/303457/bitcoin-bull-howard-lutnick-defends-tether-senate) [9](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/features/2026-03-18/tether-made-loan-to-lutnick-s-children-as-they-bought-his-assets) [14](https://decrypt.co/380619/senate-democrat-cantor-fitzgerald-tether-lutnick)

### 3. Lutnick divested and Cantor is now run by his sons.

**Confirmed**

- Lutnick was confirmed 18 Feb 2025 (51-45).
- The divestiture package was announced 19 May 2025 and parts took effect 16 May 2025 (BGC bought back shares for $151.5M, Newmark for $127M), but the transfer of his Cantor interest to trusts for his children was to close after regulatory approvals (expected Q3 2025); Bloomberg later reported the sale to the trusts happened in October 2025.
- Brandon is chairman/CEO and controlling trustee, Kyle executive vice chair, and minority stakes went to 26North and Glenn August.
- Amounts: $151.5M; $127M
- Dates: 18 Feb 2025; 19 May 2025; 16 May 2025; October 2025
- Sources: [6](https://www.theblock.co/news/regulation/2025-02-18-us-senate-confirms-pro-crypto-howard-lutnick-as-secretary-of-commerce-341680) [7](https://www.investmentnews.com/ria-news/commerce-chief-howard-lutnick-divesting-ownership-in-cantor-fitzgerald/260569) [8](https://www.financemagnates.com/institutional-forex/bgc-group-buys-back-151m-in-shares-from-us-commerce-secretary-howard-lutnick-after-cabinet-appointment) [23](https://news.bloomberglaw.com/banking-law/lutnick-divests-cantor-fitzgerald-to-children-investor-26north)

### 4. Tether financed the children's trust during the divestiture.

**Partly accurate**

- Bloomberg (18 Mar 2026) reported Tether lent an undisclosed sum to 'Dynasty Trust A', under a New York credit document filed on 7 Oct 2025, secured by 'all assets' of the trust; a Cantor executive said it was backed by the Tether convertible bond.
- Bloomberg reported the sale of Lutnick's Cantor stake to the trusts took place in October 2025, i.e. the loan is contemporaneous with the closing.
- Whether the loan funded the purchase is still unverified; Cantor declined to give its size or use.
- Warren/Wyden (29 Apr 2026, answers requested by 13 May) and Blumenthal (8 Oct 2026) sought records, and no public answers were found.
- Dates: 18 Mar 2026; 7 Oct 2025; October 2025; 29 Apr 2026; 13 May
- Sources: [9](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/features/2026-03-18/tether-made-loan-to-lutnick-s-children-as-they-bought-his-assets) [10](https://news.bloomberglaw.com/mergers-and-acquisitions/as-lutnick-sold-cantor-to-his-children-tether-gave-them-a-loan) [11](https://www.banking.senate.gov/newsroom/minority/warren-wyden-probe-national-security-risks-surrounding-reported-lutnick-tether-loan) [12](https://www.coindesk.com/policy/2026/04/30/senator-warren-questions-commerce-secretary-lutnick-on-tether-loan-to-family) [13](https://www.hsgac.senate.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026-10-08-Letter-from-Sen-Blumenthal-to-Chairman-Lutnick.pdf)

### 5. The administration passed stablecoin legislation that benefited Tether/Cantor.

**Partly accurate**

- The GENIUS Act became law in July 2025.
- Tether launched the GENIUS-compliant USAT on 27 Jan 2026 (issuer Anchorage Digital, custodian Cantor, CEO Bo Hines, former White House crypto council director).
- Critics cite foreign-issuer loopholes.
- No evidence was found that Lutnick shaped the Act, and Commerce has no stablecoin regulatory role.
- Dates: July 2025; 27 Jan 2026
- Sources: [19](https://decrypt.co/356045/tether-launches-us-regulated-stablecoin-issued-anchorage-digital) [20](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-01-27/tether-anchorage-digital-launch-us-focused-stablecoin-usat) [21](https://www.reed.senate.gov/news/releases/reed-seeks-to-close-alarming-loophole-in-genius-act)

### 6. Commerce controls AI-chip export licences, creating a Tether-linked conflict.

**Unverified**

- Commerce (BIS) does license chip exports, including under the UAE deal.
- No reporting ties Tether or Cantor to any chip-licence decision.
- Audit addition: Cantor's 13F showed ~$1.19B of Nvidia exposure in Q3 2024 (including large put and call option positions), and a much smaller common position (~1.78M shares, ~$310M, per aggregator data) in Q1 2026; Accountable.US argued in 2025 that this creates a conflict with Commerce's chip oversight (advocacy framing).
- Axios (11 Nov 2026) reported Lutnick resisted curbs on Megaspeed, a Singapore Nvidia customer accused of diverting chips to China.
- No reporting links Cantor's holdings or Tether to that decision, so the conflict remains inference.
- Amounts: ~$1.19B; ~$310M
- Dates: 11 Nov 2026
- Sources: [22](https://axios.com/2026/11/11/trump-lutnick-nvidia-china-chip-export-controls) [27](https://benzinga.com/markets/24/11/42158373/trumps-commerce-secretary-pick-howard-lutnicks-fund-owns-1-19b-in-nvidia-804m-in-tesla-here-is-a-look-at-his-portfolio) [22](https://axios.com/2026/11/11/trump-lutnick-nvidia-china-chip-export-controls)

### 7. Cantor launched crypto/bitcoin SPACs, including Twenty One Capital with Tether and SoftBank.

**Confirmed**

- Announced 23 Apr 2025 as a ~$3.6B deal: Tether ~$1.6B in BTC, Bitfinex ~$600M, SoftBank ~$900M. Merged with Cantor Equity Partners (chair/CEO Brandon Lutnick).
- Listed on the NYSE as XXI on 9 Dec 2025 with >43,500 BTC and $585M of financing.
- Amounts: ~$3.6B; ~$1.6B; ~$600M; ~$900M; $585M
- Dates: 23 Apr 2025; 9 Dec 2025
- Sources: [15](https://decrypt.co/315941/tether-bitfinex-cantor-softbank-public-bitcoin-company) [16](https://www.pymnts.com/cryptocurrency/2025/cantor-fitzgerald-tether-softbank-launch-3-6-billion-bitcoin-investment-firm) [17](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20251208019809/en/) [18](https://cointelegraph.com/news/jack-mallers-twenty-one-capital-begins-trading-nyse)

## Denials and context

- Tether (2024): the relationship is purely professional, about managing reserves, with no regulatory influence from Lutnick's role.
- Commerce: Lutnick fully complied with his ethics agreement, including divestiture and recusal requirements.
- Cantor: the trusts' purchase was 'funded through multiple sources... at market rates and market prices'.
  - It declined to give the loan's size or use.
- Lutnick backed stablecoin audits and 100% Treasury backing at his hearing and denied owning Tether.
- The congressional letters come from Democrats in the minority without subpoena power.
  - None alleges a specific crime.
- Research limits: the original pages at theblock.co, decrypt.co, bloomberglaw, banking.senate.gov and hsgac.senate.gov were not read directly, so findings rest on search summaries that agree across outlets.
  - Tether's valuation and Cantor's Nvidia holdings were checked in the audit pass; Cantor's other SPACs were not.

## Full report

**Verdict: mostly-accurate** (confidence: medium)

This check builds on C11 (GENIUS/CLARITY Acts), C08 (UAE chips) and C19 (bitcoin reserve). The claim is that Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick's former firm Cantor Fitzgerald, now run by his sons, custodies Tether's reserves and holds a stake in Tether, while the administration passed stablecoin legislation and Commerce controls AI-chip export licences. It also says Cantor launched bitcoin SPAC vehicles such as Twenty One Capital.

### Summary

The core financial ties are well documented:

- Cantor is custodian of Tether's Treasury reserves.
- Cantor holds a ~$600M convertible bond that converts into about 5% of Tether. This was negotiated in 2024, while Lutnick ran Cantor.
- Lutnick announced the handover of Cantor to trusts for his children in May 2025; Bloomberg reports the sale to the trusts closed in October 2025. Brandon Lutnick is now chairman and CEO.
- Bloomberg reported in March 2026 that Tether lent money to one of those trusts while the children were buying his assets.
- Cantor's SPAC, Cantor Equity Partners, took Twenty One Capital public. Twenty One is majority-owned by Tether and Bitfinex.
- The GENIUS Act became law in July 2025. Tether then launched a GENIUS-compliant US coin, USAT, with Cantor as reserve custodian.

What is **not** documented is any policy action by Lutnick or Commerce that benefited Tether or Cantor. Nothing ties the chip-export licences Commerce controls to Tether. That part of the claim rests on juxtaposition, not evidence.

### Research limits

The original pages at theblock.co, decrypt.co, news.bloomberglaw.com, banking.senate.gov and hsgac.senate.gov were not read directly. The findings below therefore rest on search-result summaries that agree across several outlets: The Block, Fortune, Cointelegraph, Decrypt, Bloomberg/Bloomberg Law, CoinDesk, InvestmentNews, Business Wire and Axios. The research ended before I could check three things:

- Tether's latest funding valuation
- Cantor's other bitcoin SPACs (for example the Bitcoin Standard Treasury deal)
- The full text of the Bloomberg loan story

### Sub-claims

#### 1. Cantor custodies Tether's reserves — confirmed

- The WSJ reported on 24 Nov 2024 that Cantor holds most of Tether's (then ~$133B) assets and earns tens of millions of dollars a year in fees.
- At his 29 Jan 2025 confirmation hearing, Lutnick acknowledged that Cantor custodies Tether's US Treasury bills.
- At the USAT launch (27 Jan 2026), Cantor was named USAT's reserve custodian and preferred primary dealer. Coverage said Cantor already handles the reserves of the ~$186B USDT.

#### 2. Cantor "held a stake in Tether" — mostly accurate (needs a precise description)

- **What the 2024 reporting said:** the WSJ (Nov 2024) reported that Cantor negotiated a ~5% stake worth up to ~$600M.
- **What the instrument is:** Lutnick testified that Cantor holds a convertible bond, not direct equity, and that the bond could convert into 5%. Bloomberg (Mar 2026) describes a $600M convertible bond entitling Cantor to a 5% stake, and says Lutnick personally took part in the negotiations in April 2024.
- **Current value:** Sen. Blumenthal's 8 Oct 2026 letter says the stake has grown from $600M to an estimated ~$10B. That figure is the senator's estimate, not an audited one, so it is **unverified**.

#### 3. Divestment; Cantor "now run by his sons" — confirmed

- **Confirmation:** the Senate confirmed Lutnick on 18 Feb 2025, 51–45, and he resigned his Cantor posts.
- **Transfer to trusts:** the divestiture was announced on 19 May 2025 and parts took effect on 16 May 2025. The transfer of Lutnick's Cantor interest to trusts for his adult children was to close after regulatory approvals (expected Q3 2025); Bloomberg later reported the sale to the trusts took place in October 2025 (audit correction).
- **Who runs Cantor now:** Brandon Lutnick is chairman and CEO. Kyle Lutnick is executive vice chair.
- **Minority stakes:** these went to 26North and to Glenn August.
- **Buybacks:** BGC bought back ~16.4M shares from Lutnick for $151.5M. Newmark bought 11M shares for $127M. Cantor keeps control of both companies.

#### 4. Tether financed the children's trust during the divestiture — partly documented

- **The loan:** Bloomberg (18 Mar 2026) reported that "Dynasty Trust A", which benefits all four children, borrowed an undisclosed sum from Tether.
- **Timing:** the credit document was filed in New York on 7 Oct 2025, the same month Bloomberg says the trusts bought Lutnick's stake.
- **The collateral:** according to filings Bloomberg cites, the loan is secured by all of the trust's assets, including the $600M Tether convertible bond.
- **What is unproven:** whether the loan paid for the purchase of Lutnick's stake is **unverified**. Cantor declined to give the loan's size or say what it was used for.
- **Ethics experts' view:** experts told Bloomberg that if the loan helped fund the sale, it would undermine the spirit of the divestiture rules.
- **Congressional follow-up:** Sens. Warren and Wyden wrote to Lutnick and to Tether CEO Paolo Ardoino on 29 Apr 2026 and asked for answers by 13 May. I found no public response.
- **Blumenthal's letter:** on 8 Oct 2026, Sen. Blumenthal, ranking member of the Permanent Subcommittee on Investigations (PSI), wrote to Brandon Lutnick with 13 requests and a 23 Oct deadline. He cites more than $250M in income to Howard Lutnick since January 2025, including a $192M Cantor distribution. I found no reported response.

#### 5. Stablecoin legislation while these ties existed; documented policy benefit — legislation confirmed, benefit unverified

- **The law:** Congress passed the GENIUS Act and Trump signed it (see C11).
- **Tether's US coin:** Tether used the Act's framework to launch USAT on 27 Jan 2026. Anchorage Digital Bank issues it, Cantor is custodian, and Bo Hines, the former executive director of the White House crypto council, is CEO. That makes Cantor a direct commercial beneficiary of Tether's US expansion.
- **Critics' view:** Sen. Warren and others say the Act's foreign-issuer provisions leave "loopholes" that let Tether into the US market, and Sen. Reed has introduced a bill to close them.
- **Who regulates:** the Commerce Department has no formal role in regulating stablecoins. Treasury and the OCC run the GENIUS framework.
- **What I found no evidence of:** any record that Lutnick lobbied on the bill or shaped its provisions for Tether. Lutnick publicly backed audits and 100% Treasury backing.

#### 6. Commerce controls AI-chip export licences — confirmed as fact; any link to Tether or Cantor is unverified

- Commerce's Bureau of Industry and Security (BIS) licenses AI-chip exports, including those under the UAE deal (C08).
- I found no reporting that ties Tether or Cantor to a chip-licence decision.
- Two related items are not part of this claim:
  - **Advocacy claim on Nvidia:** Accountable.US, an advocacy group, claims Cantor holds large Nvidia interests. This is unverified.
  - **Axios report:** Axios reported on 11 Nov 2026 that Lutnick resisted curbs on companies suspected of diverting Nvidia chips to China. This is listed below as a lead.

#### 7. Cantor's crypto/bitcoin SPAC vehicles, including Twenty One Capital with Tether and SoftBank — confirmed

- **Announcement:** the deal was announced on 23 Apr 2025 as a ~$3.6B bitcoin company.
- **Ownership and contributions:** Tether and Bitfinex are majority owners and SoftBank is a minority owner. Tether contributed ~$1.6B in BTC, Bitfinex ~$600M and SoftBank ~$900M.
- **SPAC partner:** Twenty One merged with Cantor Equity Partners, whose chairman and CEO is Brandon Lutnick.
- **Listing:** Twenty One began trading on the NYSE as "XXI" on 9 Dec 2025, holding more than 43,500 BTC and with $585M of financing arranged at closing.
- **Bitcoin lending:** in Nov 2024, Fortune and DL News also reported a planned ~$2B Cantor bitcoin-lending program linked to Tether.

### Corrections

- "Held a stake in Tether": the instrument is a $600M convertible bond that converts into ~5%. Lutnick testified that it is not direct equity.
- "The administration passed stablecoin legislation": Congress passed the GENIUS Act and Trump signed it. Commerce has no regulatory role over stablecoins.
- The ~$10B value of the stake is Sen. Blumenthal's estimate, not a disclosed figure.

### Denials and other side

- **Tether (2024):** the relationship is purely professional, about managing reserves, and Lutnick's transition role gave it no regulatory influence.
- **Commerce Department:** Lutnick has fully complied with his ethics agreement, including all divestiture and recusal requirements.
- **Cantor:** the acquisition by the children's trusts "was funded through multiple sources, multiple companies and multiple trusts at market rates and market prices".
- **Lutnick at his hearing:** he denied owning Tether, defended its use against comparisons with the dollar and euro in crime, and backed audits.
- **The senators' letters:** they are investigative requests from Democrats in the minority, who lack subpoena power. No letter alleges a specific crime.
