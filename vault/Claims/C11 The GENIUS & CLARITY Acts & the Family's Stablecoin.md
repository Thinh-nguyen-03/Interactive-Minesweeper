# C11: The GENIUS & CLARITY Acts & the Family's Stablecoin

**Verdict:** Mostly accurate · medium confidence  
**Type:** Check · Regulators & Enforcement

## The Claim

> Did Trump personally push stablecoin and crypto market-structure legislation, including late-night calls to lawmakers, while his family's company issues the USD1 stablecoin?

## Bottom Line

Trump publicly pushed the GENIUS Act and lobbied House holdouts personally, including late-night calls that he described himself, while his family's World Liberty Financial issues the USD1 stablecoin.

## What Holds Up

- Trump pushed for and made statements supporting the GENIUS Act.
- There were reports of nighttime calls from Trump to solicit votes.
- The Trump family benefits and profits from its own stablecoin, USD1.

## What Needs Correcting

- Trump also pushed the CLARITY Act alongside GENIUS (partly accurate)
- The regulation was written around the time USD1 was created, giving USD1 a leg up (partly accurate)
- The GENIUS and CLARITY Acts are often paired as legislation Trump is getting passed. Only GENIUS became law (signed 2025-07-18). CLARITY passed the House 294-134 but failed Senate cloture 49-50 on 2026-09-15.
- **Claimed:** A Trump son boasted USD1 is 'the second largest GENIUS compliant stablecoin'  
  **The record shows:** No such post was found. Eric Trump's documented January 2026 X post said USD1 had passed PayPal's PYUSD, and by total market cap USD1 was roughly 4th-6th, not 2nd.
- The 'nighttime calls' are sourced mainly to Trump's own remarks at the signing (calls at 2-4 a.m.). Press reports document an evening Oval Office meeting with holdouts on 2025-07-15.
- The write-up previously gave '$57.3M from WLF' as the 2025 disclosure figure; that was the June 2025 filing covering 2024. The filing for calendar 2025 (released 2026-06-30) reports about $526M from WLF token sales.
- 2026-11-16 (disclosure audit, C96/C97): Claimed: Trump 'reported about $57.3M from WLF' for 2024 and the family 'holds ~60% of WLF'. The record shows: the $57,355,532 is World Liberty Financial, Inc.'s own token-sale revenue (line 391 of the 2024 report), not a distribution to Trump; DT Marks DEFI (70% Trump, 30% family) held 75% of WLF Holdco at the end of 2024 and 38.25% at the end of 2025. The 2025 receipts ($526.8M token proceeds, $65.6M and $196.9M from stake sales) sit on entity lines that are 30% family-owned.

## What Is Unproven

- A Trump son boasted on X that USD1 is now the second-largest GENIUS-compliant stablecoin.

## In More Detail

- The CLARITY Act passed only the House and failed a Senate cloture vote in September 2026.
- No X post with the exact boast 'second largest GENIUS compliant stablecoin' could be found.
- The closest documented posts are Eric Trump's January 2026 post that USD1 passed PYUSD and his September 2025 description of USD1 as 'GENIUS Act compliant'.
- The 'leg up' framing is interpretation, not a documented causal fact.

## Sub-Claims

### 1. Trump pushed for and made statements supporting the GENIUS Act.

**Confirmed**

- On June 18, 2025, after the Senate passed the bill 68-30 on June 17, Trump posted on Truth Social urging the House to pass a 'clean' bill 'LIGHTNING FAST... NO DELAYS, NO ADD ONS'.
- He met House holdouts and signed the bill on July 18, 2025, after House passage 308-122 on July 17.
- 'Shaping the legislation' is supported only in the sense that he pressed for a clean bill.
- Amendments to bar presidential-family profit were not adopted.
- No evidence was found that he drafted the text.
- Dates: June 18, 2025; June 17; July 18, 2025; July 17
- Sources: [3](https://www.theblock.co/news/ecosystems/2025-06-18-trump-urges-house-to-pass-genius-stablecoin-bill-358821) [4](https://cointelegraph.com/news/donald-trump-urges-quick-passing-of-genuis-stablecoin-bill) [2](https://www.axios.com/2025/07/17/house-passes-genius-act-stablecoins-trump) [1](https://abc7ny.com/post/what-is-genius-act-trump-sign-1st-major-federal-cryptocurrency-bill-law/17181655/) [11](https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/congress/senate-passes-landmark-crypto-regulation-bill-bipartisan-vote-sending-rcna213437)

### 2. Trump also pushed the CLARITY Act alongside GENIUS.

**Partly accurate**

- CLARITY was part of the July 2025 'Crypto Week' rule package that Trump lobbied for, and it passed the House 294-134 on July 17, 2025.
- It is not law: it cleared Senate Banking 15-9 in May 2026, failed Senate cloture 49-50 on September 15, 2026, and is stalled.
- Dates: July 2025; July 17, 2025; May 2026; September 15, 2026
- Sources: [12](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/15/senate-cloture-vote-on-clarity-act-fails-dealing-regulatory-setback-to-crypto-industry.html) [13](https://www.dlapiper.com/en/insights/publications/2026/09/senate-fails-to-advance-the-clarity-act) [14](https://www.orrick.com/en/Insights/2026/10/The-CLARITY-Act-Stalls-in-the-Senate-Whats-Next-for-Digital-Asset-Regulation)

### 3. There were reports of nighttime calls from Trump to solicit votes.

**Confirmed**

- On the evening of July 15, 2025, after the rule failed 196-223, Trump posted shortly before 9 p.m. ET that 11 of the 12 holdouts were in the Oval Office and had agreed to vote yes.
- Reports describe multiple conversations with Trump.
- At the July 18 signing, Trump said he had been making calls 'at 2:00, 3:00, 4:00 in the morning' and was 'about four short at 3:30 in the morning'.
- The source for the hour is Trump's own account.
- Dates: July 15, 2025; July 18
- Sources: [5](https://www.washingtonexaminer.com/?p=3472562) [6](https://www.nbcnewyork.com/news/business/money-report/trump-backed-crypto-regulation-bills-fail-to-clear-key-hurdle-in-congress/6336676/) [7](https://www.semafor.com/article/07/17/2025/they-took-it-to-the-next-level-emmer-breaks-down-the-republican-crypto-rebellion) [8](https://www.democrats.senate.gov/newsroom/trump-transcripts/transcript-president-trump-signs-crypto-stablecoin-legislation-into-law-71825) [9](https://www.presidency.ucsb.edu/documents/remarks-signing-the-guiding-and-establishing-national-innovation-for-us-stablecoins-genius)

### 4. The Trump family benefits and profits from its own stablecoin, USD1.

**Confirmed**

- USD1 is issued by World Liberty Financial.
- DT Marks DEFI, an entity 70% owned through Trump's companies (DTTM Operations 69% plus a 1% member corporation) and 30% by unnamed 'Trump Family Members', is entitled to 75% of WLF's net token-sale revenue; its stake in WLF Holdco was 75% at the end of 2024 and 38.25% at the end of 2025 according to Trump's OGE filings (WLF's own statements gave about 60% and 40% in between).
- Trump's June 2025 disclosure (covering calendar 2024) lists $57,355,532 of token sales on the line of World Liberty Financial, Inc. itself, which is company revenue rather than a distribution to him.
- His disclosure for calendar 2025, released 2026-06-30, lists $526.8M of token-sale proceeds distributed by WLF to DT Marks DEFI, $65.6M from a WLF Holdco equity sale, and $196.9M to DT Marks SC from Stablecoin Holdco, the USD1 business; the form does not say whether the amounts on these lines are the entities' totals or Trump's share.
- The White House denies any conflict.
- Amounts: 70%; 69%; 1%; 30%; 75%
- Dates: June 2025; 2026-06-30
- Sources: [10](https://cointelegraph.com/news/senators-amendment-genius-act-world-liberty-financial-stablecoin) [11](https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/congress/senate-passes-landmark-crypto-regulation-bill-bipartisan-vote-sending-rcna213437) [22](https://www.foxbusiness.com/politics/trump-financial-disclosure-reveals-more-than-1b-crypto-income-during-first-year-back-office)

### 5. A Trump son boasted on X that USD1 is now the second-largest GENIUS-compliant stablecoin.

**Unverified**

- No post with this wording was found.

- In September 2025 he called USD1 'GENIUS Act compliant'.
- Among US-regulated stablecoins, being second only to USDC is a plausible framing, but by total market cap USD1 ranked about 4th-6th.
- 'GENIUS compliant' is self-description: the Act's rules are not yet effective, and BPI (June 2026) argued USD1's reserves would not meet the requirements.
- Amounts: ~$4.4-4.5B
- Dates: January 23, 2026; September 2025; June 2026
- Sources: [15](https://finviz.com/news/285959/eric-trump-says-trump-family-linked-world-liberty-financials-stablecoin-usd1-has-eclipsed-paypals-digital-dollar-the-shift-is-happening) [16](https://en.bloomingbit.io/feed/news/104628) [17](https://www.coinage.media/s4/inside-trumps-plan-to-launch-a-digital-dollar-at-world-liberty-financial) [18](https://decrypt.co/356279/world-liberty-financials-usd1-tops-5b-market-cap-as-trump-meme-coin-stumbles) [19](https://bpi.com/not-all-stablecoins-will-follow-genius-reserve-asset-requirements/)

### 6. The regulation was written around the time USD1 was created, giving USD1 a leg up.

**Partly accurate**

- The timing is accurate: the GENIUS Act was introduced in the Senate in February 2025, and WLF announced USD1 with BitGo custody on March 25, 2025, while the bill was being negotiated.
- That this gave USD1 a 'leg up' is an interpretation.
- It is plausible but not documented as causal.
- Dates: February 2025; March 25, 2025
- Sources: [20](https://www.theblock.co/amp/post/347995/world-liberty-financial-debuts-plan-for-usd1-stablecoin-as-president-trump-continues-push-for-crypto-legislation) [21](https://blockworks.co/news/bitgo-to-custody-wlfi-usd1) [11](https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/congress/senate-passes-landmark-crypto-regulation-bill-bipartisan-vote-sending-rcna213437)

## Denials & Context

- White House: Trump's assets are in a trust managed by his children; 'there are no conflicts of interest.'
- The GENIUS Act was bipartisan: 18 Senate Democrats and 102 House Democrats voted yes, so passage was not Trump's doing alone.
- Democratic amendments to bar the president and his family from profiting from stablecoins were not included.
  - Reporting says the ethics provision covers members of Congress but not the president's family.
- 'GENIUS compliant' is a WLF/Trump-family marketing claim.
  - Implementing rules are not yet effective, and BPI disputes that USD1's reserves would qualify.

## Full Report

**Verdict: mostly-accurate (confidence: medium)**

### The claim
- Trump is "deeply involved" in passing the GENIUS Act and the CLARITY Act, and the legislation directly affects Trump-family businesses.
- "His son boasts on Twitter that USD1 is now the second largest GENIUS compliant stable coin."
- Trump helped shape the GENIUS Act and made public statements supporting it.
- There were "reports of nighttime calls from Donald Trump to essentially solicit votes."
- Meanwhile his family profits from its own stablecoin, USD1. USD1 got "a leg up" because the rules were written around the same time USD1 was created.

### Evidence by sub-claim

1. **Trump pushed the GENIUS Act: confirmed.** After the Senate passed the bill 68-30 on June 17, 2025, Trump posted on Truth Social that the House should move "LIGHTNING FAST" and pass a "clean" GENIUS Act: "Get it to my desk, ASAP — NO DELAYS, NO ADD ONS" [3][4]. He also stepped in personally when 12-13 House Republicans (mostly Freedom Caucus members) voted down the procedural rule 196-223 on July 15, 2025. That evening he announced from the Oval Office that 11 of the 12 holdouts had agreed to back it [5][6][7]. The House passed the bill 308-122 on July 17, and Trump signed it on July 18, 2025 [1][2]. "Shaping the legislation" is supported in a looser sense: the White House pushed for a clean bill, and Democratic amendments that would have barred the president and his family from profiting were not adopted [9][10]. I found no evidence that Trump personally drafted the text.

2. **Trump pushed the CLARITY Act: confirmed, with context.** CLARITY was part of the same "Crypto Week" package. The holdout deal covered the rule for all the crypto bills, and the House passed CLARITY 294-134 on July 17, 2025 [2][11]. The claim implies CLARITY was being passed alongside GENIUS. In fact it never became law. It cleared Senate Banking 15-9 in May 2026, then failed a Senate cloture vote 49-50 on September 15, 2026, and it is stalled as of November 2026 [11][12][13].

3. **"Nighttime calls" to get votes: confirmed, mainly from Trump's own words.** News reports document an evening Oval Office meeting (Trump posted shortly before 9 p.m. ET on July 15, 2025) and "multiple conversations with Trump" before the holdouts switched [5][6][7]. At the signing ceremony, Trump himself said he had been making phone calls "at 2:00, 3:00, 4:00 in the morning" and recalled being "about four short at 3:30 in the morning" [8]. So the late-night calls are documented, but the detail about the hour comes from Trump's own account.

4. **The family profits from USD1: confirmed in substance.** World Liberty Financial (WLF) issues USD1. DT Marks DEFI, an entity about 70% owned through Trump's companies and 30% by unnamed family members, is entitled to 75% of WLF's net token-sale revenue. According to Trump's OGE filings, its stake in WLF Holdco was 75% at the end of 2024 and 38.25% at the end of 2025; WLF's own statements gave about 60% and 40% in between [9][21]. Trump's June 2025 financial disclosure (covering 2024) lists $57,355,532 of token sales on the line of World Liberty Financial, Inc. itself. That is company revenue, not a distribution to him. His disclosure for calendar 2025, released June 30, 2026, lists $526.8M of token-sale proceeds distributed by WLF to DT Marks DEFI, $65.6M from a WLF Holdco equity sale and $196.9M to DT Marks SC from Stablecoin Holdco, the USD1 business. The form does not say whether these are the entities' totals or Trump's share [21]. USD1 reserve income flows to WLF. The figures for that income and for Binance are covered in C10.

### Corrections
- The CLARITY Act passed only the House (294-134, July 17, 2025). It failed Senate cloture 49-50 on September 15, 2026 and is not law. Only the GENIUS Act was enacted (signed July 18, 2025).
- I found no evidence of an X post calling USD1 "the second largest GENIUS compliant stablecoin." The documented posts are Eric Trump's January 2026 post that USD1 passed PYUSD and his September 2025 statement that USD1 is "GENIUS Act compliant." By total market cap, USD1 was not second.
- The "nighttime calls" come from Trump's own remarks at the signing. The press documented an evening Oval Office meeting and phone negotiations.
