# C62: The financiers, custodians and underwriters behind the vehicles

**Verdict:** Mostly accurate (confidence: medium)

## Lead investigated

> Connection hunt: financial intermediaries that recur across Trump-linked vehicles and also have matters before Trump-appointed regulators — Yorkville Advisors/Yorkville America (TMTG ETFs, CRO SPAC, TMTG financing), Dominari, Cantor Fitzgerald, Anchorage Digital, BitGo (USD1 custody; Craft stake; OCC charter), Jane Street, DRW/Cumberland, Jump, Galaxy, Kraken, Crypto.com, DWF Labs, Web3Port, Hut 8, Tuttle Capital, Clear Street, plus who received OCC charters, SEC dismissals or CFTC relief in the same period. Map who financed what and which regulator decisions each obtained.

## Summary

A small set of intermediaries recurs across Trump-family vehicles, and several obtained favorable federal decisions in the same period. Documented: Cantor Fitzgerald (Lutnick family) was financial adviser on TMTG's $2.44B bitcoin raise; Yorkville is TMTG's equity-line buyer, placement agent, ETF adviser and SPAC partner; and Kevin McGurn simultaneously runs the Yorkville SPAC and the Dominari-underwritten Trump-sons SPAC while serving as TMTG adviser and then interim CEO. The custodians of TMTG and USD1 (Anchorage, Crypto.com, BitGo) each won OCC relief under Comptroller Gould, and DRW and Crypto.com got SEC closures on the same day before their TMTG roles. Every individual link is sourced. The pattern across them is our own cross-referencing; no source shows any regulatory decision was traded for financing, and the OCC and SEC actions were also extended to non-Trump-linked firms.

## Corrections

- The brief lists Galaxy, Kraken, DWF Labs and Web3Port as intermediaries behind Trump vehicles. I found no Galaxy role at all, and Kraken's link is donations, lobbying and regulatory relief rather than financing. DWF and Web3Port are WLFI token buyers, not financiers of TMTG or the sons' vehicles.
- The '$375M Jane Street' figure is FT-via-CoinGape. The 13G documents 16.57M shares (6.0%), and Jane Street had largely exited by 2026.
- A search-indexed report that Cantor 'declined to advise' TMTG conflicts with TMTG's own closing release naming Cantor as financial adviser. The release is treated as authoritative.

## Denials and context

- The White House says Trump's assets are in a trust managed by his children and that there are no conflicts of interest.
- DRW/Cumberland and Crypto.com deny any link between their SEC outcomes and TMTG dealings. The Cumberland dismissal was agreed in principle on Feb 20, 2025.
- The OCC says career staff reviewed charter applications. The Dec 2025 batch also included Circle, Ripple, Paxos and Fidelity, and Anchorage's order was lifted on stated remediation.
- Lutnick divested his Cantor interests to his children's trusts effective May 16, 2025, and says he follows Commerce ethics guidance.
- The Kraken master account came from the Kansas City Fed, a regional reserve bank, not a presidential appointee.
- No SEC or FINRA action against Dominari exists. Absence of enforcement is not evidence of forbearance.
