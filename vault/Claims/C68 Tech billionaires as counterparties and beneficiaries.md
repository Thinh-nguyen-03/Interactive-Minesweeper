# C68: Tech billionaires as counterparties and beneficiaries

**Verdict:** Mostly accurate · medium confidence  
**Type:** Connection check · Tech and media companies

## The claim

> Did tech principals such as the Ellisons, Elon Musk, Sam Altman, Mark Zuckerberg and Jeff Bezos pay Trump, his family or his projects while receiving federal decisions in their favour?

## Bottom line

Nearly every principal named in the claim is documented as both paying Trump, his family or his projects and receiving a federal decision.

## What holds up

- Ellison family / Paramount: a $16M settlement with Trump was followed by FCC approval of Skydance-Paramount, then by a Trump-commented contest for Warner Bros. Discovery that the Ellisons won.
- Jared Kushner's Affinity Partners was a named backer of the Ellisons' hostile WBD bid alongside Saudi PIF, QIA and Abu Dhabi's L'imad, then withdrew.
- Oracle (Larry Ellison) is a counterparty in three administration-blessed deals: Stargate, the TikTok US joint venture (with MGX), and a ~$300B OpenAI compute contract.
- Silver Lake sits in two Trump-adjacent consortia: the TikTok JV (with Oracle and MGX) and the $55B Electronic Arts buyout (with PIF and Kushner's Affinity).
- Elon Musk: the largest 2024 Trump donor, then DOGE head; his companies won federal AI business and went public in a record IPO, and Don Jr.'s 1789 Capital holds stakes in xAI and SpaceX (mostly accurate)
- Larry Ellison financed Musk's Twitter takeover, and X later paid about $10M to settle Trump's suit.
- OpenAI's leaders gave heavily to Trump vehicles and to an AI super PAC, and Trump then signed an order attacking state AI laws.
- Meta/Zuckerberg: inaugural donation, a $25M settlement, a ballroom gift, and the FTC breakup case lost by the government (mostly accurate)
- Amazon/Bezos paid the First Lady via the 'Melania' documentary, gave to the inauguration and ballroom, and has major matters before the government.
- Nvidia/Jensen Huang gave $25M to the ballroom anonymously while winning export decisions from the President, whose portfolio bought Nvidia stock.
- Palantir/Thiel: Vance's patron's company won large federal contracts, gave to the ballroom, and was touted by Trump by ticker after his portfolio bought it.
- Microsoft gave $10M to the ballroom, and Trump publicly demanded it fire a former DOJ official, citing its government contracts.
- The President personally traded the stocks of the tech companies whose deals his administration approved (Oracle, Nvidia, Microsoft, Meta, Amazon, Palantir) (mostly accurate)

## What needs correcting

- The claim implies the Ellisons bought Warner outright on administration approval. In fact WBD first chose Netflix; Paramount won through a hostile bid. Netflix declined to match on 2026-02-26. DOJ clearance came in summer 2026 and the deal closed in about late September 2026.
- Oracle, not Larry Ellison personally, owns 15% of the TikTok JV. Early reports of a 45-50% Oracle/Silver Lake/MGX block were superseded at closing (15% each).
- xAI's Pentagon award is a contract ceiling of up to $200M shared in form with Google, OpenAI and Anthropic, not a $200M payment. Palantir's $10B Army deal is also a ceiling.
- The Meta FTC outcome was a court ruling (Boasberg, 2025-11-18), not an administration decision. Meta's attempt to settle with the administration reportedly failed.
- 'Ballroom?' for Nvidia: confirmed. $25M, anonymous, per the Washington Post's leaked spreadsheet (2026-10-15).
- Brockman's $25M MAGA Inc gift is filed as $12.5M each from Greg and Anna Brockman.
- Paramount-WBD closing date: the deal closed on Tuesday 2026-10-06 (AP), not about 2026-09-29 as first inferred.

## In more detail

- The Ellisons settled for $16M, then won FCC approval and the WBD contest in which Kushner's Affinity briefly co-invested.
- Oracle sits beside Tahnoon's MGX in Stargate and the TikTok JV.
- Don Jr.'s 1789 holds xAI/SpaceX stakes ahead of SpaceX's record IPO.
- OpenAI's Brockman gave $25M to MAGA Inc. before the state-AI preemption order.
- Nvidia ($25M), Palantir and Microsoft ($10M each), and Meta and Amazon ($5M each) funded the ballroom while their matters were pending.
- The money and the decisions are documented; causation is not, and several links (Silver Lake as hub, Ellison-X-settlement, TikTok-Meta ruling) are our own inference.

## Full report

**Verdict: mostly accurate (confidence: medium).**

**What holds up.** Every principal named in the claim is documented on both sides of a ledger:
- they made payments to Trump, his family, or his projects (settlements, inaugural gifts, ballroom pledges, super-PAC money, deal stakes);
- they received federal decisions (merger clearances, export licences, contracts, an executive order).

**What does not hold up.**
- No source establishes that any payment caused any decision.
- Several decisions were made by courts, or apply to a whole industry.
- A few of the links below are our own cross-referencing, and are labelled that way.

**Method note.**
- I drew on C08, C20, C32, C33, C34, C44, C50, C56, C57, C66 and C67 first, then searched outward.
- Most news sites were not read directly. Findings rest on search-result summaries that agree across several outlets.
- I did not read the primary OGE 278-T PDFs or the SEC PDFs directly.

Evidence grades:
- **documented**: filings, official records or several outlets.
- **single-source**: one outlet or anonymous sourcing.
- **inferred**: our own observation; no source asserts it.

### Connections

#### 1. The Ellisons: settlement, FCC approval, WBD and CNN (documented; causation not shown)

**The Paramount sequence.** Paramount paid $16M to end the "60 Minutes" suit. FCC approval of the Skydance merger followed three weeks later (C33).

**The WBD contest.**
- After WBD signed with Netflix, Trump said the Netflix deal "could be a problem" on market share. He said he might be "involved" in the decision, and that CNN should be sold.
- The WSJ, as relayed by NBC, reported that David Ellison had visited Trump and promised "significant changes" at CNN.
- On 2025-12-22, Larry Ellison gave an "irrevocable personal guarantee" of $40.4B of the bid's equity.
- Netflix declined to match on 2026-02-26.
- Shareholders approved $31 a share in April 2026. That is about $81B of equity, or about $110-111B including debt.
- The DOJ cleared the deal and it closed in late September 2026.

**Result.** The Ellisons now control both CBS (with Bari Weiss as editor-in-chief) and CNN.

**Other side.** Trump said none of the bidders were "particularly great friends." Paramount denies any link between the settlement and the approvals.

#### 2. Kushner's Affinity in the Ellisons' WBD bid (documented)

- Paramount's tender filing named three backers alongside Affinity Partners: Saudi PIF, QIA and Abu Dhabi's L'imad, with $24B among them.
- All of them took non-voting equity with no board seats.
- Affinity withdrew on 2025-12-16. The Gulf funds stayed in, and re-confirmed their commitments in April 2026.

For about a week, the President's son-in-law was a co-investor in a deal the President was publicly weighing in on.

#### 3. Oracle across Stargate, OpenAI and TikTok, next to Tahnoon's MGX (documented)

- **Stargate.** Oracle is an initial partner (2025-01-21).
- **OpenAI.** The WSJ reported OpenAI would spend about $300B over five years on Oracle compute, starting in 2027 (reported around 2025-09-10).
- **TikTok.** Trump's executive order of 2025-09-25 approved the US TikTok structure.
  - At the 2026-01-22 closing, Oracle, MGX and Silver Lake took 15% each, and ByteDance kept 19.9%.
  - Oracle is the "trusted security partner."
- **MGX.** MGX is chaired by Tahnoon bin Zayed, whose Aryam bought 49% of WLF (C06, C57). Oracle's TikTok co-owner is therefore also the Trump family's largest crypto counterparty.

#### 4. Silver Lake as a shared co-investor (each deal documented; the overlap is inferred)

- Silver Lake holds 15% of the TikTok JV, alongside Oracle and MGX.
- It also holds about 5.5% of EA, alongside PIF (about 93.4%) and Affinity (about 1.1%). The EA deal closed on 2026-08-04 after US approvals; JPMorgan provided $20B of debt.

No source frames Silver Lake as a link between the Tahnoon network and Kushner/PIF. This is a shared deal partner, not evidence of coordination.

#### 5. Musk, DOGE, xAI/SpaceX and Don Jr.'s 1789 Capital (documented, with one inferred step)

**Documented.**
- Musk spent about $277-290M on the 2024 election, then led DOGE.
- In July 2025, xAI got a Pentagon CDAO contract with a ceiling of up to $200M. Google, OpenAI and Anthropic got the same form of award. xAI also launched "Grok for Government" on the GSA schedule.
- SpaceX acquired xAI in February 2026, at about $1.25T.
- SpaceX's IPO priced on 2026-06-11 at $135 a share, raising about $75B at a valuation of about $1.77T.
- 1789 Capital, where Don Jr. has been a partner since November 2024, invested in SpaceX, xAI and Neuralink. Reuters, relayed by SMH and Marine Link, reports more than $50M across xAI and SpaceX. According to Reuters sources, the deals followed Omeed Malik meeting Musk during the campaign.
- After his 2025 feud with Trump, Musk is back as a Republican funder. America PAC had raised $50.3M for the 2026 cycle by 2026-06-30, almost all of it from Musk (FactCheck.org).

**Inferred.** 1789's xAI stake converted into SpaceX stock and gained value at the IPO. No source gives 1789's gain.

**Other side.** 1789 says Don Jr. recuses himself from dealing with the federal government.

#### 6. Ellison financed Musk's X, and X paid Trump (both ends documented; the chain is inferred)

- A 2022 SEC filing shows Ellison committed $1B to the Twitter buyout. Texts released in court show his reply: "A billion...or whatever you recommend."
- X paid about $10M to settle Trump's suit in February 2025.

No source links Ellison to the settlement. What this shows is that the Ellison and Musk camps are financially intertwined.

#### 7. OpenAI money and the state-AI preemption order (documented; the link is timing only)

**The money.**
- Altman gave $1M to the inauguration.
- Greg and Anna Brockman gave $25M to MAGA Inc. It arrived on 2025-09-12 and was nearly a quarter of what the PAC raised in the second half of 2025 (Bloomberg).
- The Brockmans gave another $25M to Leading the Future, a pro-AI super PAC with more than $100M. Its other backers include a16z and Joe Lonsdale. It uses Fairshake's strategist.

**The decision.** On 2025-12-11 Trump signed "Ensuring a National Policy Framework for AI." It:
- creates a DOJ task force to challenge state AI laws;
- ties broadband funds to state AI policy.

a16z and OpenAI had earlier lobbied for a moratorium on state AI laws.

**Family layer.** Josh Kushner's Thrive Capital is a major OpenAI investor, and OpenAI took a stake in Thrive Holdings on 2025-12-01 (C66).

**Other side.** OpenAI calls Brockman's giving personal. No source asserts that the donations caused the order.

#### 8. Meta: $1M, $25M, $5M, and the FTC case (documented; the outcome was a court's)

- Meta gave $1M to the inauguration, paid the $25M settlement on 2025-01-29, and pledged $5M to the ballroom.
- Bloomberg reports Meta tried to settle the FTC case with the administration, and failed.
- Judge Boasberg ruled for Meta on 2025-11-18, finding that TikTok and YouTube compete with it.

#### 9. Amazon/Bezos and the First Lady (documented; see C44)

#### 10. Nvidia: $25M to the ballroom, anonymously, alongside export decisions (documented)

**The gift.** The Washington Post's leaked spreadsheet (2026-10-15) shows Nvidia pledged $25M, tied with Apple for the largest gift. Nvidia asked for anonymity and was left off the White House's October 2025 list of 37 donors. Nvidia says the money went to a nonpartisan trust.

**The decisions.**

- Axios (2026-11-11) reports Lutnick stalled curbs on chips being diverted to China (C50).

**Trump's portfolio.** It bought Nvidia, including $1M-$5M on 2026-02-10.

### Corrections

- **WBD.** The Ellisons won WBD through a hostile bid after WBD first picked Netflix. It was not a straight approval.
- **TikTok.** Oracle owns the TikTok stake, not Larry Ellison personally. At closing it was 15% each for Oracle, MGX and Silver Lake, not a 45-50% bloc.
- **Contract ceilings.** xAI's "$200M" and Palantir's "$10B" are contract ceilings.
- **Meta.** The Meta antitrust win came from a court, not the administration.
- **Nvidia and the ballroom.** Confirmed: $25M, anonymous.
- **Brockman.** His $25M is filed as $12.5M from Greg and $12.5M from Anna Brockman.

### Denials and other side

- **Paramount:** the settlement was unrelated to the approvals. **Trump:** the bidders are not "particularly great friends."
- **1789 Capital:** Don Jr. recuses himself from dealing with the government, and the firm is a passive investor.
- **OpenAI:** Brockman's giving is personal.
- **Nvidia:** its gift went to a nonpartisan trust.
- **Bezos:** he denies any role in the documentary deal.
- **White House:** the ballroom is privately funded, and the trades comply with the STOCK Act.
- **Context:** many of these decisions benefit whole industries, including non-donors, and follow stated policy. A documented payment plus a documented decision is not proof of a quid pro quo.
