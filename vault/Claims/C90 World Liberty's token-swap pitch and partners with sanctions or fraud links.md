# C90: World Liberty's token-swap pitch and partners with sanctions or fraud links

**Verdict:** Mostly accurate · medium confidence  
**Origin:** Connection hunt

## Bottom line

Blockworks' report that WLF pitched crypto teams a swap ($10M+ WLFI at a $1.5B FDV plus a 10% fee, with WLF buying their token) is documented, and WLF did buy the tokens of most of its partners (TRX, MOVE, ENA, ONDO, SEI, AVAX, MNT, SUI reserve).

## What holds up

- Connection 1: WLF pitched crypto teams a token swap. A team buys at least $10M of WLFI plus a 10% fee, and WLF buys the same amount of the team's token.
- Connection 6: WLF's USD1 partner AB (AB DAO / AB Chain) had a planned Timor-Leste resort run by people sanctioned in the US-UK Prince Group action (mostly accurate)
- Connection 7: Senators Warren and Reed asked Treasury and DOJ (May 2026) to investigate WLF's AB tie as part of a pattern of failed vetting.
- Connection 9: Reuters found Iran's Nobitex moved at least $2.3B over Tron and BNB Chain, rails built by WLF backers Justin Sun and CZ/Binance. OFAC then sanctioned Nobitex.
- Connection 10: USD1 was issued natively on Tron through a deal with Justin Sun. Sun later alleged WLF tried to extort him into buying USD1 and promoting it on Tron.

## What needs correcting

- Connection 2: WLF bought TRX while Justin Sun's TRON DAO bought WLFI (TRX to WLFI) (partly accurate)
- Connection 3: WLF bought MOVE a day after MOVE's market maker Web3Port bought $10M of WLFI (MOVE to WLFI) (partly accurate)
- Connection 4: WLF bought ENA and ONDO around the time it announced partnerships with Ethena (Dec 2024) and Ondo (Feb 2025) (partly accurate)
- Connection 8: WLFI was sold to a wallet that also transacted in A7A5, the ruble stablecoin whose issuer is sanctioned (partly accurate)
- Connection 11: WLF says all buyers passed KYC. The record shows several counterparties it did not catch or flag (partly accurate)
- **Claim:** The brief calls A7A5 'the sanctioned ruble stablecoin'  
  **Record:** OFAC's 2025-08-14 action designated its issuer Old Vector, the A7 entities and Grinex. The token itself is not an SDN entity, though it is widely called sanctioned.
- C71's lead dates the Reuters Nobitex report to 2026-05-01. Syndicated copies are dated 2026-05-18, and one aggregator put it in April. We record it as May 2026.
- Secondary reports disagree on which account used A7A5: the Senate letter names '0x9009', while one write-up names 'Shryder.eth'. We follow the letter.
- Some outlets date the Prince Group sanctions to October 2024. They were imposed on 2025-10-14, about four weeks before the AB-WLF USD1 deal of 2025-11-12/13.
- The swap pitch is sometimes described as executed deals. Blockworks reported an offer, and no team is documented as having accepted it.

## What is unproven

- Connection 5: WLF bought SEI, AVAX and MNT and added SUI to its reserve. Did those projects buy WLFI?

## In more detail

- A matching WLFI purchase within days is documented only for Tron (TRON DAO's $45M) and MOVE (via its market maker Web3Port's $10M), and both parties deny a swap.
- The AB partnership, struck four weeks after the US-UK Prince Group sanctions hit its resort principals, the Accountable.US A7A5 finding, Reuters' Nobitex-on-Tron/BNB report and the USD1-on-Tron deal are each documented, but none shows illicit money reaching WLF.
- WLF's claim that all 85,000+ buyers passed KYC is its own; the record shows repeated vetting gaps but no regulatory finding.

## Sub-claims

### 1. Connection 1: WLF pitched crypto teams a token swap. A team buys at least $10M of WLFI plus a 10% fee, and WLF buys the same amount of the team's token.

**Confirmed** · Evidence: Documented as reporting

- The underlying messages are single-source (Blockworks, about 2025-02-03).
- Blockworks saw a WLF representative's message offering unlaunched WLFI at a $1.5B fully diluted valuation, first come, first served.
- Deals were to close on a video call with co-founder Zak Folkman, who with Herro did not reply to Blockworks.
- CoinDesk, The Block and crypto.news relayed the report.
- WLF said it was 'not selling tokens' and was 'reallocating assets for ordinary business purposes'.
- TRON and Movement denied any swap agreement.
- Our own arithmetic, which no source makes: a $1.5B FDV on 100B WLFI is $0.015 a token.
- That equals the first public tranche, while the second public tranche then on sale was $0.05.
- If the message is accurate, swap partners were offered WLFI at about 30% of the public price, or about 33% after the 10% fee.
- No source names any team that accepted.
- Amounts: $1.5B; $0.015; $0.05; about 30%; about 33%
- Dates: 2025-02-03
- Sources: [1](https://blockworks.co/news/trump-backed-project-token-swaps) [2](https://dev.coindesk.com/markets/2025/02/04/tron-move-deny-token-swap-deal-for-world-liberty-fi-inclusion) [3](https://www.theblock.co/post/338563/trump-world-liberty-financial-disputes-token-swap-allegations) [4](https://crypto.news/trump-backed-world-liberty-financial-offers-token-swaps-to-crypto-firms-report/)

### 11. Connection 11: WLF says all buyers passed KYC. The record shows several counterparties it did not catch or flag.

**Partly accurate**

- The claim is documented; doubts about its effectiveness are documented and partly inferred.
- WLF's 2025-03-17 release says more than 85,000 participants underwent KYC to determine eligibility.
- Blockworks (October 2024) described the process: country of residence, a linked wallet, ID proof and a liveness check.
- WLF says it applies 'rigorous AML/KYC' and later burned and reallocated about 166.7M WLFI from frozen wallets to holders who passed new KYC (The Block, November 2025; C42).
- Against that, the record shows (a) a USD1 partner whose resort principals had been sanctioned four weeks earlier (sub-claim 6), (b) buyers with A7A5, Nobitex or Tornado Cash exposure (sub-claim 8, watchdog-sourced), and (c) Aqua 1/Zhou and the OKX-funded $100M (C71).
- Note the timing: most WLFI was sold before A7A5's issuer (August 2025) and Nobitex (June 2026) were designated.
- KYC of the buyer's identity is also not the same as screening a wallet's history.
- That distinction is our own observation.
- No regulator has made a finding on WLF's KYC.

### Summary

The swap pitch Blockworks reported is real. It asked a crypto team to buy at least $10M of WLFI at a $1.5B fully diluted valuation (FDV), plus a 10% fee. In return, WLF would buy the same amount of that team's token.

WLF did buy the tokens of nearly every project it partnered with in late 2024 and early 2025. A matching WLFI purchase within days is documented only twice, and in both cases the parties deny that any swap took place:

- **Tron:** TRON DAO bought $45M of WLFI on 2025-01-19. WLF bought about $7M of TRX between 2025-01-20 and 2025-01-23.
- **MOVE:** MOVE's market maker, Web3Port, bought $10M of WLFI on 2025-01-27. WLF bought MOVE on 2025-01-28.

The sanctions and illicit-finance threads are each documented:

- WLF's USD1 deal with AB came four weeks after its resort principals were sanctioned in the Prince Group action.
- A WLFI buyer used A7A5, the ruble stablecoin. This is watchdog-sourced.
- Reuters found Nobitex moved $2.3B or more over Tron and BNB Chain. OFAC then designated Nobitex.
- USD1 runs natively on Tron.

None of these threads shows illicit money reaching WLF or the Trump family. WLF's statement that all buyers passed KYC is its own claim, and no regulator has tested it.

### What the brief asks, and what the evidence shows

#### 1. The swap pitch (DOCUMENTED reporting; underlying message single-source)

- **What Blockworks reported (about 2025-02-03):** It saw a WLF representative's message offering unlaunched WLFI on these terms:
  - a $10M minimum, plus a 10% fee;
  - a $1.5B FDV;
  - first come, first served;
  - closing on a video call with Zak Folkman.
- **Relayed by:** CoinDesk, The Block and crypto.news.
- **Responses:**
  - WLF said it was "not selling tokens" and was "reallocating assets for ordinary business purposes".
  - TRON said "there is no token swap agreement".
  - Movement said it bought no WLFI.
- **Our arithmetic (no source makes this point):**
  - $1.5B across 100B WLFI is $0.015 per token. That was the first-tranche price, while the public price at the time was $0.05.
  - A swap partner would therefore have bought at about 30% of the public price, or about 33% including the fee.
  - Under the Gold Paper, 75% of WLF's net revenue goes to the Trump-family entity DT Marks DEFI (C42). Any fee and token proceeds from swap partners would fall under that split.
