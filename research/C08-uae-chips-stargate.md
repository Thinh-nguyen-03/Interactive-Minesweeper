# C08: UAE AI chips deal, Stargate, currency swap

**Verdict: mostly-accurate** (confidence: medium)

The video's main points have documentation behind them. The UAE put Emirati money into Trump-family crypto projects, and soon after the US approved large AI-chip exports to the UAE. MGX was a founding equity funder of Stargate. Trump did say a currency swap with the UAE was "under consideration" and called it "a good ally." World Liberty Financial (WLF) denies any link between the deals. Three things are overstated or framed loosely:

- The Biden administration delayed and restricted chip exports to the UAE. It did not refuse them outright.
- Trump did not say he *would* help the UAE financially, only that a swap was being considered. No swap line has been reported as signed.
- MGX's Stargate role was announced before the USD1 deal, so it does not fit a "benefits after the deals" sequence.

No reporting has found evidence of an explicit quid pro quo.

Note: this session's network blocked most page fetches. The findings below come from search-result summaries of the cited articles, not from full-text reads. That is the reason for medium confidence.

## What the video says (transcript lines 381-405)
- The UAE won "enormous AI chips deals" that it had long wanted and the US had refused, because of fears the chips could fall into the wrong hands or be used for military purposes.
- The chips deal was approved "shortly after" deals began between Trump crypto projects and the UAE.
- WLF denies any political quid pro quo and says it has no influence over policy.
- MGX was announced as an investor in Project Stargate, alongside SoftBank, OpenAI and Oracle.
- "The president said he would financially assist the UAE during the Iran crisis because they've been so good to us." The clip that follows has a reporter asking about a currency swap, and Trump answering "It is [under consideration] ... it's been a good ally of ours ... these are unusual times."

## Sub-claims

1. **The US had long refused or restricted sensitive AI chips to the UAE over security fears. Partly accurate.**
   - Under Biden, export approvals tied to the Microsoft–G42 partnership were delayed over concerns that the technology could reach China. US intelligence also flagged G42's links to Huawei and other Chinese firms (NYT, April 2024).
   - Some licences were in fact cleared in December 2024 under the Microsoft deal (Axios, 2024-12-07).
   - The January 2025 AI Diffusion Rule put the UAE in a restricted "tier 2" with licensing caps.
   - So "restricted and slowed" is accurate. "Refused" overstates it.
   - The security concerns (China diversion, military use) are well documented.

2. **The chips deal was approved shortly after the UAE–Trump crypto deals. Confirmed on timing; causation unproven.**
   - 2025-01-16: per the WSJ, an entity linked to Sheikh Tahnoon (Aryam Investment 1) secretly agreed to buy 49% of WLF. Eric Trump signed four days before the inauguration.
   - 2025-05-01: Zach Witkoff announced at TOKEN2049 Dubai that MGX's $2B investment in Binance would close in WLF's USD1 stablecoin.
   - About 2025-05-15: during Trump's Gulf trip, the US–UAE AI Acceleration Partnership and the 5GW G42 campus were announced. Press reports said the UAE could import up to 500,000 advanced Nvidia chips a year; that figure was not in official statements.
   - The Biden-era diffusion rule was rescinded around the same time.
   - Actual licences followed later: Microsoft's internal transfer in September 2025, and 35,000 units direct to G42 in November 2025 (AGBI). In July 2026 the UAE government and approved firms got licence-free access.
   - The NYT (2025-09-15) reported the two deals moved in parallel. Steve Witkoff, WLF's co-founder, backed the UAE's chip access while WLF received the Emirati money. The NYT found no evidence of an explicit exchange.

3. **WLF denies any quid pro quo. Confirmed.**
   - A WLF spokesperson called the $2B investment "completely independent of government matters."
   - The White House said David Sacks acted appropriately and that Witkoff was working with ethics officials.
   - Treasury Secretary Bessent has denied that Trump-family UAE ties are linked to the swap-line discussions.

4. **MGX was announced as an investor in Stargate with SoftBank, OpenAI and Oracle. Confirmed.**
   - The 2025-01-21 Stargate announcement at the White House named SoftBank, OpenAI, Oracle and MGX as initial equity funders. One Fortune report noted that OpenAI's own statement listed only SoftBank, OpenAI and MGX.
   - The announced scale was $500B over four years, with $100B to be deployed immediately.
   - This came about three months before the USD1/MGX announcement and five days after the reported Aryam–WLF signing. It is not a later "benefit."

5. **Trump said a currency swap or financial help for the UAE during the Iran crisis was under consideration because it was "a good ally." Mostly accurate, but the paraphrase overstates it.**
   - In a CNBC interview on 2026-04-21, set against the US-Israeli war with Iran and the Hormuz disruption, Trump said a swap with the UAE was "under consideration." He called the UAE "a good ally" and said it was "really rich."
   - The video's own paraphrase, "said he would financially assist ... because they've been so good to us," overstates a statement that the idea was under consideration.
   - US officials said the UAE had made no formal request. UAE officials said they have no liquidity problem.
   - Bessent told senators that both countries would benefit.
   - As of the latest reports found (May to August 2026), no swap line had been signed or announced. Whether it was ever finalized remains unverified.

## Corrections
- Transcript says the US "had refused" these chip deals. The record shows Biden-era delays, a tier-2 cap under the AI Diffusion Rule, and partial approvals (December 2024 Microsoft licences), not a blanket refusal.
- Transcript says Trump "said he would financially assist the UAE." He said a currency swap was "under consideration." No swap had been reported as finalized as of mid-2026.
- Transcript says "shortly after ... this chips deal was approved." The framework came about two weeks after the 2025-05-01 USD1/MGX announcement. Actual export licences came in September and November 2025.
- MGX's Stargate role was announced 2025-01-21, before the USD1 deal, so it does not fit an after-the-fact benefit sequence.

## Denials and context
- WLF says the investment was "completely independent of government matters" and that it has no policy influence. The White House says Witkoff and Sacks complied with ethics rules.
- The NYT found no explicit quid pro quo. The concern raised is overlapping timing plus Witkoff's dual role.
- The UAE has been a US security partner for a long time. The Gulf chip deals were also part of a broader Trump AI-export strategy that covered Saudi Arabia too.
- Critics, including Senators Warren and Slotkin, called for investigations. The Senate Banking minority staff report and analysts warned of a China-diversion risk.
