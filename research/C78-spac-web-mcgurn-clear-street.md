# C78: The SPAC web: McGurn, Yorkville, Texas Ventures, Clear Street

**Verdict: mostly-accurate (medium confidence).** The web is real, and larger than the brief describes. Yorkville affiliates control four blank-cheque companies:

- Yorkville Acquisition Corp (MCGA)
- Texas Ventures Acquisition III (TVA)
- Blue Water Acquisition Corp III (BLUW)
- Yorkville International Capital (YICC)

Kevin McGurn is CEO of at least three of them while serving as Trump Media's interim CEO. Two Trump Organization executive vice presidents, Lawrence Glick and Alan Garten, sit on TVA III's board, and TVA III is merging with PlusAI at an $800M pre-money valuation.

Two points in the brief need correcting:

- **McGurn no longer runs the Trump sons' SPAC.** He resigned from New America Acquisition I on Aug 5, 2026, and Dominari's Kyle Wool now runs it.
- **The two clusters are separate.** The Dominari/D. Boral underwriting cluster and the Yorkville sponsor cluster are joined only through McGurn.

Clear Street's Trump tie is the TMTG bitcoin raise alone, and it postponed its own IPO in February 2026. I found no SEC approval for any Truth Social crypto ETF.

Each governance overlap is documented. No source shows a transaction that benefits the Trump family beyond the sons' NWAX founder shares, and nothing here shows a regulatory favour.

Evidence grades used below:

- **documented:** filings, official records or several outlets
- **single-source:** one outlet or anonymous sourcing
- **inferred:** our own cross-referencing, which no source asserts

**Research limits.** sec.gov, newstracs.com and wendysiegelman.substack.com could not be fetched in this session. Filing details below come from search-result snippets of SEC documents and from secondary summaries. Figures marked "aggregator" were not checked against the filing itself.

## Connections (sub-claims)

1. **McGurn holds the most posts in the web.** *Documented.*
   - CEO of MCGA, BLUW and YICC.
   - CEO, chairman and CFO of NWAX at various points until Aug 5, 2026, and sole member of its sponsor.
   - Director of D. Boral Acquisition I.
   - TMTG adviser since December 2024 and interim CEO since Apr 21, 2026.

   Renaissance Capital's YICC note describes him as TMTG's interim CEO. His reported TVA III CEO role is contradicted by the PlusAI release, which names Troy Rillo, so that role is *unverified*. Multiple SPAC directorships are common, and conflicts are disclosed in the prospectuses.

2. **Texas Ventures III: a Yorkville takeover with Trump Organization directors.** *Documented (8-K, 10-K).*
   - TV Partners III sold control to Yorkville Acquisition Sponsor II for $7.4M. That covered 7.5M founder shares and 4.7M warrants, about $0.99 per founder share, against $10 per public unit.
   - The old board resigned.
   - The 10-K lists Glick (Trump Org EVP of Development) and Garten (Trump Org EVP and Chief Legal Officer) as directors.
   - Then-TMTG CEO Nunes is listed as a director only by NewsTRACS and Siegelman (*single-source*).
   - I found no director pay or founder-share grants for the Trump Org executives.

3. **PlusAI merger.** *Documented.* The deal was signed Sep 2-3, 2026:
   - about $800M pre-money valuation;
   - up to about $300M of capital, of which more than $60M is committed (including Yorkville Advisors Global funds) and about $236M sits in trust;
   - an S-4 filed Oct 9, 2026.

   PlusAI's $1.2B deal with Churchill Capital IX ended by mutual consent on Apr 20-21, 2026. An aggregator reports that 17.7M of 22.5M shares were redeemed at an extension vote (*unverified*). I found no federal action involving PlusAI, so any regulatory angle on autonomous trucking is speculation and is not asserted here.

4. **Blue Water III: from Citgo bidder to TMTG-staffed shell.** The sponsor change is *documented* (8-K). McGurn's directorship is *documented* (filing profile). Nunes, Glabe and Angelo as directors, and the $7.2M price, are *single-source* (Siegelman/NewsTRACS).
   - Under founder Joseph Hernandez, BLUW bid about $10B for PDV Holding in September 2025 and dropped the bid by mid-November.
   - Elliott's Amber Energy won the auction at about $5.9B.
   - Fortune (Sep 2026) reports that OFAC has since blocked further leadership changes at Citgo's parent companies.
   - I found no sign that the Yorkville-run BLUW pursued Citgo.

5. **Yorkville International: a Venezuela SPAC and a Senate inquiry.** *Documented.*
   - $200M IPO in June 2026, with an emphasis on Latin America and Venezuela.
   - Officers: McGurn (CEO), Rillo (CFO) and Angelo (chairman). Cohen & Company was sole book-runner.
   - On Jun 25, 2026 Sen. Wyden wrote to Angelo. He cited the parent firm's billions in Trump-business ties and the eased Venezuela sanctions, and asked whether Yorkville had sought an OFAC licence.

   I found no Yorkville reply and no licence on record.

6. **MCGA after the CRO unwind.** *Documented.* The trust was $181.17M at Sep 30, 2026. The Trump Media Group CRO Strategy deal was mutually terminated on Aug 7, 2026 (see C41 and C63). I found no new target or liquidation.

7. **NWAX passed from McGurn to Dominari's Wool, and Passantino joined.** *Documented.*
   - Feb 6, 2026: Wool became chairman and Stefan Passantino joined as an independent director.
   - Aug 5, 2026: McGurn resigned as CEO and director, "not the result of any disagreement", and Wool became CEO.
   - Aug 26, 2026: CFO George O'Leary left; Tim Ledwick (CFO) and Christopher Devall (COO) were appointed.

   A Trump Organization spokesperson told Siegelman that the sons "have no involvement with Yorkville" and that McGurn had already stepped down. Measured against the filings, the timing of that statement is unclear. Passantino also joined the board of JFB Construction, the XTEND drone vehicle Eric Trump invested in (C51 lead). That two-board pattern is our *inferred* cross-reference.

8. **Founder-share economics.** *Documented* (S-1 via AP and Reuters; TVA 8-K).
   - Eric Trump has a 3M and Don Jr. a 2M founder-share interest through New America Sponsor I LLC, worth about $50M on paper at $10. That value exists only if a merger closes.
   - McGurn has a 1M interest.
   - What the sons paid is *not found*. The typical $25,000 sponsor price was not confirmed.
   - Yorkville's purchases of founder blocks at TVA III ($7.4M) and BLUW ($7.2M reported) show the standard economics: founders pay about a tenth of the public price.

9. **Dominari and D. Boral are one cluster; Yorkville is another.** *Documented.*
   - Dominari and D. Boral co-led NWAX's $345M IPO.
   - D. Boral was sole book-runner for its affiliate D. Boral Acquisition I ($287.5M), where McGurn sits on the board and was added to the audit and compensation committees on Nov 6, 2026.
   - Both firms received the House China committee's March 2026 letter (C47).
   - I found no Dominari or D. Boral role in the Yorkville SPACs. The two clusters connect through McGurn.

10. **Clear Street.** *Documented.*
    - It was co-lead placement agent, with Yorkville Securities, on TMTG's roughly $2.44B bitcoin raise in May 2025.
    - Reuters calls it an underwriter on several Strategy offerings and for TMTG.
    - Its Nasdaq IPO (CLRS) launched in February 2026 at up to $1.05B and an $11.8B valuation. On Feb 12 it was cut to about $351-364M and postponed the same day, citing market conditions. I found no relaunch.
    - I found no Clear Street role in the SPACs and no Trump-era regulatory decision involving it.

11. **Cohen & Company and Troy Rillo.** Each fact is *documented*; the bridge is *inferred*.
    - Cohen & Company was co-agent on the TMTG raise and sole book-runner for YICC.
    - Rillo is co-CEO of Yorkville Securities, which co-led the TMTG raise, and CFO of YICC and TVA III. A single-source item also shows him as an MCGA insider buyer.

    So the team that placed TMTG's stock also runs the SPACs on whose boards TMTG and Trump Org executives sit.

12. **Truth Social crypto ETFs: SEC status.** *Documented through Feb 2026; later status unverified.*
    - The Bitcoin ETF 19b-4 was filed Jun 3, 2025 and delayed in August 2025. The final deadline was about Jan 29, 2026.
    - The Bitcoin & Ethereum ETF and the Crypto Blue Chip ETF (S-1, July 2025) were also delayed.
    - On Feb 13, 2026 Yorkville America Equities filed a Bitcoin and Ether ETF and a Cronos Yield Maximizer ETF.
    - A February 2026 report said no crypto fund had launched, and I found no approval since.
    - The named custodian was Foris DAX (Crypto.com). Crypto.com's servicing of Yorkville America ETFs ended Aug 7, 2026 (C41), so the custody arrangement is now unclear.
    - The equity Truth Social ETFs did launch, on Dec 30, 2025 (C24).

13. **Financier-client governance overlap.** The seats are *documented* for Glick, Garten and McGurn and *single-source* for Nunes and Glabe; the characterization is *inferred*. Yorkville is TMTG's largest outside financier: about $450M in equity-line purchases, co-lead on the 2025 raise, ETF adviser and partner in the CRO SPAC (C62). It has placed TMTG's CEO, general counsel and interim CEO, and the Trump Org's chief legal officer and development chief, on boards it controls. This is a conflict-of-interest question, not evidence of any payment.

## Corrections to the brief and to earlier files

- McGurn resigned from NWAX on Aug 5, 2026. The brief's "while interim CEO" holds for only about 3.5 months of overlap, from Apr 21 to Aug 5, 2026.
- McGurn as CEO of TVA III is unverified. The Sep 2026 release names Rillo.
- "D. Boral, Dominari and Yorkville as recurring sponsors/underwriters": these are two distinct clusters. Yorkville's SPACs used Cohen & Company, as far as found.
- C62 grades TVA III's Trump Org directors as single-source. The 10-K makes them documented.
- Clear Street's IPO did not price. It was postponed on Feb 12, 2026.

## Denials and context

- The Trump Organization says the sons have no involvement with Yorkville.
- The NWAX 8-K says McGurn's departure involved no disagreement.
- Cheap founder shares, serial directorships and sponsor swaps are routine for SPACs.
- The SEC's handling of the Truth Social crypto ETFs shows delay, not favour.
- Wyden's letter is a minority inquiry, not enforcement.

## Not connected in the coverage we found

These are our own cross-references:

- **The same Yorkville team placed TMTG stock and staffs the SPAC boards with TMTG and Trump Org insiders** (Rillo and Cohen & Company spanning both). *Inferred.*
- **Passantino on two Trump-son-linked boards** (NWAX and JFB/XTEND) in 2026. *Inferred* from two documented appointments.
- **The NWAX handover tracks McGurn's move into TMTG.** McGurn left NWAX about 3.5 months after becoming TMTG interim CEO, and two days before the TMTG/Crypto.com/Yorkville CRO unwind (Aug 7, 2026). *Inferred timing; no source links them.*
- **BLUW passed from a Citgo bidder to TMTG insiders, while a sister Yorkville SPAC (YICC) targets Venezuela.** *Inferred.* No source says BLUW's new team has Venezuela plans.
- **The Pentagon's Kollitides and McGurn on D. Boral Acquisition I** is a shared board seat only (C74). *Documented co-occurrence; no transaction.*
