# C73: Qatar's access network inside and around the administration

**Verdict: mostly-accurate (confidence: medium).** Most of the people-to-Qatar ties in the claim are documented in FARA filings, financial disclosures or reporting from several outlets. The list includes Bondi, Patel, Kerik, Zeldin, Kushner/Affinity, the Witkoff Park Lane sale, Barrack's Colony-era deals and his UCC partner, the Trump Organization's Qatari Diar licence, and Newsmax. The US decisions that benefited Qatar are documented and dated too. Two parts of the claim are weaker than stated:

- **The Witkoff $100M.** The "Apollo-run trust partly owned by Qatar" claim was corrected. The lender was reported to be Apollo Global Management itself, not the Qatar-invested trust.
- **Susie Wiles and Mercury.** Her Mercury link to Qatar is thin. Mercury's documented Qatar contracts predate her time at the firm.

No source shows any payment or investment buying a decision. The overlaps in timing are our own cross-referencing.

Builds on: C16 (jet, arms, golf), C53 (Witkoff Gulf money), C61 (shared lawyers), C64 (lobbyists), C65 (officials' prior ties). This report extends those files and does not repeat them. Many outlets (jpost.com, freebeacon.com) could not be read directly. Where that happened, findings rest on search-result text from several outlets, and we say so.

## What was checked

Map every documented Qatari financial or client tie to people now in or around the administration. Set those ties against the US decisions that benefited Qatar (the jet, the security-guarantee executive order, arms sales, Al Udeid, the Idaho facility), with dates and evidence grades.

## Connections found (each graded)

### 1. Pam Bondi: Ballard's Qatar FARA work, then the jet memo
**Grade: documented. Already established in C61, C64 and C65.**

The new material is about the memo itself. The DOJ memo clearing the jet has still not been released. The Freedom of the Press Foundation sued for it (American Oversight is counsel; D.D.C. 25-2432, filed July 2025). DOJ estimated it would take until **25 January 2027** to process the request, citing a 620-day average. We found no court-ordered release through November 2026. DOJ says career ethics officials found no conflict.

### 2. Kash Patel: Qatar embassy consulting, a waiver, then FBI-Qatar security deals
**Grade: documented.**

- Patel's Trishul LLC listed Qatar's embassy as a client. He never registered under FARA.
- After confirmation he received a **4 March 2025 ethics waiver** from the FBI's ethics official for "a particular matter" related to Qatar. The matter is not named in the public filing.
- In **December 2025** he signed bilateral security agreements in Doha (Jewish Insider). The FBI did not announce the trip or the agreements.
- Jewish Insider reports that his **November 2024 one-year recusal pledge had expired** the month before.
- Public Citizen has asked OGE to rescind the waiver, and a separate FARA complaint is open. We found no outcome for either.

**Correction to C64:** C64 says he "would recuse from related work for one year". The pledge was made in November 2024, and the waiver already allowed him to take part.

### 3. Lee Zeldin: consulting for a Qatari royal's fund that ties into Newsmax and the Menendez case
**Grade: each link documented; the chain is our inference.**

**(a) Zeldin's consulting.** Zeldin's 2025 nominee disclosure (OGE 278e) lists **Heritage Advisors** (London) as a consulting client of Zeldin Strategies. Jewish Insider reports compensation above $5,000 for April-December 2023 (The Intercept, 2025-02-11). Jewish Insider described the payer as a Qatari-royal investor.

**(b) Heritage Advisors in the Menendez case.** Heritage Advisors was founded by **Sheikh Sultan bin Jassim Al Thani**. NYT, ICIJ and The Real Deal identify it as the "Qatari Investment Co." in the superseding Menendez indictment (January 2024). Prosecutors alleged that Menendez steered that fund to invest with developer **Fred Daibes**, partly by taking pro-Qatar actions. Neither the Sheikh nor Qatar was accused.

**(c) Heritage Advisors and Newsmax.** The same Sheikh Sultan invested about **$50M in Newsmax in 2019-2020** (Washington Post/ICIJ, March 2024).

**(d) Daibes and the pardon market.** Daibes later paid Keith Schiller's Javelin Advisors **$1M in Q1 2025** in a failed bid for a pardon (C64). Javelin also worked for Pakistan.

**What this shows and does not show.** One Qatari royal's fund links an EPA administrator, a leading pro-Trump cable network, and a convicted senator's co-defendant, who then bought access to the Trump pardon market. No source connects these threads to each other. We found no EPA decision touching Qatar.

### 4. Bernard Kerik: hired by Qatar's embassy weeks before the jet and the Doha visit
**Grade: documented (O'Dwyer's, Shomrim), with contract terms varying by source.**

Qatar's embassy hired the Kerik Group under a contract effective **1 April 2025**. Reported terms:
- $240K for a one-year term (O'Dwyer's).
- Alternatively, $240K for the first 2.5 months, then $80K a month, with a bar on working for other Middle Eastern governments (Shomrim).

Kerik was pardoned by Trump in 2020 and had worked alongside Giuliani on Trump's 2020 election challenges. FBI Director Patel announced his death on **29 May 2025**, eight days after the Pentagon accepted the jet. Kerik held no administration post. We found no record of what he did for Qatar. Correction: the hire was real, but it lasted under two months before his death.

### 5. Susie Wiles and Mercury
**Grade: partly accurate. Employment documented; Qatar link single-source (from C64).**

- Mercury's documented Qatar embassy contracts are from 2015 and 2019, plus a $100K-a-month April-June contract in an earlier period. All predate Wiles's time at Mercury (2022-24).
- No 2025-26 Mercury contract with Qatar was found.
- Ballard, her earlier firm, did represent Qatar (2018-2020).

### 6. Ballard Partners back on Qatar's payroll?
**Grade: unverified.**

- The Free Beacon reports an embassy contract at **$175K a month**, but its date could not be established. *Audit note:* $175K x 12 matches the $2.1M one-year pact Qatar signed with Ballard in spring 2018 (Florida Politics), so the figure is most likely historical. That is our inference.
- An industry tracker (everything-pr) lists a **$2.1M one-year** Ballard Qatar pact.
- Sludge's February 2026 list of Ballard foreign clients, used in C64, does not name Qatar.

We could not confirm a 2025-26 Ballard-Qatar FARA registration.

### 7. Steve Witkoff: Park Lane and the corrected $100M
**Grade: Park Lane documented (C53); Qatar-trust link weakened by a correction.**

- **Park Lane.** QIA bought the Park Lane for about $623M in August 2023 from a Witkoff-Mubadala joint venture.
- **The $100M.** The NYT reported (September 2025) that the Witkoff Group received $100M "as recently as May" from an Apollo-run trust partly owned by Qatar. Commercial Observer described it as a $100M Apollo refinancing of the Witkoff/Access West Palm Beach golf resort (Dutchman's Pipe/Belgrove).
- **The correction.** A correction relayed by the Jerusalem Post and others says the lender was **Apollo Global Management, not the subsidiary in which Qatar had invested**. Apollo says Qatar-linked investors have no say in investment choices. The Jerusalem Post page was not read directly and rely on search-result text.
- **Official responses.** The White House counsel said there was "no evidence" that Qatar's relationship with the Witkoffs affected his diplomacy. The Witkoffs threatened to sue the NYT.

### 8. Jared Kushner: QIA in Affinity, a new raise while envoy, and the WBD bid
**Grade: documented.**

- **QIA in Affinity.** QIA and Abu Dhabi's Lunate put about **$1.5B** into Affinity in December 2024 (C15).
- **The new raise.** On **13 March 2026** the NYT (five sources) reported that Kushner was seeking **$5B or more** from Saudi, Qatari and Emirati sovereign funds while serving as a US negotiator during the Iran war. Sen. Wyden opened an inquiry. Whether the round closed is unclear (Dealroom says raised; others say talks).
- **The WBD bid.** In December 2025, **QIA (via QIA TMT Holding) and Affinity were co-backers** of Paramount Skydance's hostile Warner Bros. Discovery bid, as non-voting equity designed to avoid CFIUS review. Affinity exited on 16 December 2025, and QIA stayed (C68).
- **666 Fifth.** The 666 Fifth episode (2017-18) is historical. In 2017 a reported $500M commitment from former Qatari PM Hamad bin Jassim collapsed. Brookfield, in which QIA held about 9% of the real-estate unit, then took the tower, and it denies any Qatari role (C66).

### 9. Tom Barrack: two decades of deals with Qatari state funds, and his UCC partner in Syria and Iraq
**Grade: documented.**

- **Miramax.** Colony Capital (Barrack's firm) and a QIA unit bought **Miramax** from Disney in 2010 for about $660M. They sold it to Qatar's **beIN Media** in March 2016 (Bloomberg, Fortune).
- **PSG.** Colony sold **Paris Saint-Germain** to Qatar Sports Investments: 70% in May 2011 and the remaining 30% in March 2012. Sources name Sébastien Bazin, not Barrack, as Colony's lead on that deal.
- **Syria power MoU.** As US envoy, Barrack attended the **29 May 2025** Damascus signing of a ~$7B power MoU with a consortium led by Qatar's **UCC Holding** (Moutaz Al Khayyat). Final concessions for eight plants (5 GW) followed on 6 November 2025.
- **Kirkuk-Baniyas pipeline.** UCC is also in the **Kirkuk-Baniyas pipeline** consortium with TI Capital, whose links to Barrack Reuters reported (C65; MoUs signed July 2026).

What this shows: Barrack has long been a business partner of Qatari state money, and as envoy he presided over deals for a Qatari conglomerate. No source says he profits from UCC.

### 10. Trump Organization and Qatari Diar
**Grade: documented (C16).**

On 30 April 2025, QIA's real-estate arm Qatari Diar signed with Dar Global for the Trump golf club and villas at Simaisma. Eric Trump attended. AP/Dawn put the Trump development at about $3B within the larger masterplan. The Trump Organization licenses its name; it does not own or develop the project. We found no reports on construction progress in 2026.

### 11. Newsmax and Qatar's push into conservative media
**Grade: Newsmax investment documented; coverage-softening allegation single-source and disputed.**

- **The investment.** Sheikh Sultan bin Jassim invested about $50M in 2019-20 for a significant minority stake (Washington Post/ICIJ).
- **Coverage allegation.** Two employees said staff were urged to "soften" coverage of Qatar. Newsmax denies it.
- **Earlier talks.** QIA passed on a stake around 2017.
- **Truth Social link.** Newsmax is carried on Truth Social's Truth+ (C21).
- **Lumen8.** Lumen8 Advisors (Rebecca Diaz-Bonilla) holds a $180K-a-month contract with Qatar's International Media Office. According to FARA filings it arranged Tucker Carlson's **March 2025** interview with Qatar's prime minister. The filings do not show Carlson was paid, and he denies taking Qatari money.
- **Overall spend.** FDD and Quincy tally **$225M-$250M** in Qatari FARA spending since 2016-17. Quincy counts 28 firms retained at once.

## Not connected in the coverage we found

All of the following are our own cross-referencing; no outlet asserts them.

- **Zeldin, Newsmax and Daibes through Heritage Advisors.** Heritage Advisors / Sheikh Sultan bin Jassim connects Zeldin's consulting income, the $50M Newsmax stake, and the Menendez-Daibes investment. Daibes then paid Trump-tied Javelin for a pardon bid. *Inferred.*
- **Kerik's contract and the Doha events.** The Kerik contract started six weeks before the Doha visit and the jet acceptance. *Inferred from timing.*
- **Mountain Home and the Gaza deal.** The Idaho facility (10 October 2025) came the day after the Gaza ceasefire phase-one deal brokered with Qatar by Witkoff and Kushner. Hegseth himself linked the two. *His framing is documented; any quid pro quo is inferred.*
- **Kushner's fundraising and his negotiating role.** Kushner raised money from QIA (December 2024, and the March 2026 talks) while negotiating with Qatar as mediator (Gaza, Iran). *The co-occurrence is documented; influence is unverified.*
- **Barrack's Qatari partners over time.** His Colony-era partnerships with QIA (Miramax, PSG) came before his envoy role, in which he attended UCC's Syria signing. *Inferred continuity.*
- **The P4-HBJ registration.** The jet's registration initials may tie to Hamad bin Jassim, the would-be 666 Fifth financier. *Unverified.*

## Denials and context

- Qatar says its investment decisions are separate from diplomacy. QIA disputes that it had any 666 Fifth role.
- Brookfield denies any Qatari involvement in 666 Fifth.
- Apollo says Qatar-linked investors do not direct its trust's investments.
- The White House called the NYT Witkoff story a "bogus smear".
- DOJ says career officials cleared Bondi's participation in the memo.
- Patel says he did not lobby and had a waiver.
- Newsmax denies softening its coverage.
- Hegseth says Qatar gets no US base.
- The arms sales follow long-standing Qatar requests (the MQ-9B was stalled under Biden) and the 2026 Iran war.
- Qatar's lobbying is legal and disclosed under FARA.
