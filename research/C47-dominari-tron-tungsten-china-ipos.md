# C47: Dominari's other deals: Tron/SRM with Justin Sun, Kazakhstan tungsten, Chinese IPOs

**Verdict: mostly-accurate** (confidence: medium)

**Builds on:** C39 (Dominari and the Trump sons), with C05 (Justin Sun) and C27 (New America SPAC).

## Summary

All five threads in the claim are documented in the record, but several need qualifiers.

- **Tron/SRM:** Dominari Securities was exclusive placement agent on SRM Entertainment's June 2025 Tron treasury deal. Eric Trump publicly denied any role.
- **American Ventures:** this is a roughly $1B Dominari-controlled vehicle (Dominari holds 90%), and both sons are passive investors in it.
- **Kazakhstan tungsten:** the sons reportedly entered Skyline Builders through an SPV linked to Dominari/American Ventures. Skyline then took a 20% stake in Cove's Kaz Resources, and the mine holds up to $1.6B in US backing. That $1.6B is **non-binding letters of interest**, not disbursed money.
- **China IPOs:** the House China committee sent Dominari an information-request letter on March 8, 2026. No public finding has followed.
- **Revere:** FINRA fined Revere $800K in October 2026. The release does not name Kyle Wool, and how far the conduct overlaps his tenure is unverified.

No source shows that the sons influenced any government decision; that part is insinuation.

## Sub-claims

### 1. Dominari arranged the SRM Entertainment to Tron Inc. reverse-merger treasury deal with Justin Sun: **confirmed**
- **June 16, 2025:** Nasdaq-listed SRM Entertainment, a theme-park merchandise maker, announced it would rename itself Tron Inc. and build a TRX treasury of up to $210M. The deal had two parts:
  - $100M from a private investor for Series B convertible preferred, convertible at $0.50.
  - 220M warrants, also at $0.50.
- Dominari Securities was exclusive placement agent, and Justin Sun became an adviser.
- SRM stock rose roughly 460-580% on the news; outlets give different figures.
- The investor was Bravemorning Ltd. Its $110M warrant exercise in September 2025 brought its total to $210M and gave it about 86.6% of Tron Inc.
- One repost-style outlet says Sun's father, Weike Sun, got board control. That rests on a single source and is unverified here.
- C39 already covers the roughly $120M paper gain on the Dominari-arranged position, so it is not repeated here.

### 2. Eric Trump is reportedly linked to the deal and denies a role: **confirmed**
- The only documented link is that Eric Trump and Don Jr. sit on Dominari's advisory board and own part of it. Early reports said he was expected to take a role at Tron Inc.
- On X in June 2025, he said: "I'm the biggest fan of Tron… I am not involved in Tron's public listing plans" (Decrypt, Cointelegraph, Benzinga).
- No filing names him at Tron Inc.

### 3. Dominari launched American Ventures, a roughly $1B microcap fund with the Trump sons as investors: **mostly-accurate**
- American Ventures is managed by American Ventures Management LLC, and Dominari holds 90% of it (SEC filing, May 2026). The sons own roughly 12-13% of Dominari (Reuters reported 6.71% each from February 2025 filings).
- The FT reported in early May 2026 that the brothers had put money into most of its vehicles; the amounts were not disclosed.
- On June 25, 2026, Bloomberg reported hundreds of millions of dollars in **unrealized** gains on at least 10 thinly traded microcaps. Many gains came from warrants. Bloomberg Línea reported that the fund sold some stakes while media attention was lifting the shares.
- The "$1B" is the fund's stated size, not money proven to have been raised. The fund's focus is drones, AI and crypto, not microcaps generally; microcaps are what it actually holds.
- Spokespeople for both sons say they are passive investors with no operational role. Dominari says the strategy is to back US companies leading in new technologies.

### 4. Dominari was tied to the Skyline Builders / Cove Kaz Kazakhstan tungsten deal with up to $1.6B in US-government-linked financing: **mostly-accurate**
Timeline (FT spring 2026, via Mining.com, Northern Miner and Intellinews; NYT June 28, 2026):

| Date | Event |
|---|---|
| **Aug 2025** | The sons reportedly buy into Nasdaq-listed Skyline Builders (SKBL) through an SPV run by a Dominari subsidiary / American Ventures. Sourcing is anonymous, and SEC filings do not name the brothers. |
| **Sept 22, 2025** | Kazakhstan's president Tokayev reportedly tells Trump he plans to award the project to Cove Kaz Capital. |
| **Oct 28, 2025** | A private placement of almost $24M in Skyline, in which the sons reportedly added to their stake. |
| **Oct 31, 2025** | Skyline agrees to pay $20M for 20% of Kaz Resources, a Cove Capital unit. |
| **Nov 6, 2025** | The US-Kazakh tungsten deal is signed. |
| **Nov 2025** | EXIM issues a letter of interest for up to $900M and DFC issues letters of interest for $700M. Together these make up the $1.6B. |
| **May 12, 2026** | The FT reports Cove is seeking about $400M more from the Pentagon's Office of Strategic Capital. |
| **Aug 3, 2026** | Skyline and Cove Kaz sign a merger agreement to form Kaz Resources (KAZR). |

Caveats:
- **The $1.6B is non-binding** and needs further approvals.
- The project's own development cost is about $1.1B.
- Kazakhstan's state miner Tau-Ken Samruk holds the other 30% of the project.
- Cove CEO Pini Althaus credited "direct support from President Trump, Secretary of State Marco Rubio, and Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick." He said he had never spoken to either Trump brother.
- The NYT reported that the Trump and Lutnick families have ties to at least 14 critical-minerals companies.
- The FT found **no evidence** that the sons knew of the award in advance or influenced it.

### 5. The House China Select Committee is probing Chinese small-cap "ramp-and-dump" IPOs that Dominari underwrote: **confirmed** (as an inquiry)
- On March 8, 2026, Chairman Moolenaar and Ranking Member Khanna sent letters to Dominari Securities, D. Boral Capital and Revere Securities.
- The letters ask for records back to 2020 on due diligence, KYC and off-channel communications.
- The committee says 11 of the 29 Chinese IPOs Dominari underwrote since 2020 fit a manipulative profile. It cites Bloomberg's estimate of about $16B lost to these schemes.
- These are letters requesting information. One aggregator's claim that subpoenas were issued is unconfirmed.
- No hearing, referral or finding has been located as of November 2026.
- Dominari's March 16, 2026 shareholder letter said it will cooperate. It said IPOs make up under 10% of its banking revenue, and that it stopped Chinese IPO work in 2024 and Hong Kong IPO work in mid-2025.

### 6. FINRA fined Kyle Wool's former firm, Revere: **mostly-accurate**
- In October 2026, FINRA fined Revere Securities $800,000 for AML and supervisory failures. The cases involved foreign small-cap IPOs with ramp-and-dump hallmarks, about 60 IPOs since 2022, more than 40 of them from China or Hong Kong.
- One example: $1.5M of shares went to eight foreign-referred accounts, and that IPO rose 318% on its first day, then fell more than 50% by the close.
- Revere settled without admitting or denying the findings.
- Wool's Revere roles:
  - President of wealth management, about Feb. 2021 to Oct. 2022.
  - President of Revere Securities, about Apr. to Oct. 2022.
  - Non-executive chairman until about April 2023 (board seat possibly until June 2023).
  - He became CEO of Dominari Securities in March 2023.
- The 2022 conduct period at least partly overlaps his tenure. **FINRA does not name Wool**, and no source attributes the conduct to him.

## Corrections
- The $1.6B in "US-government-linked financing" is non-binding letters of interest from EXIM ($900M) and DFC ($700M). Nothing has been disbursed, and the project's development cost is about $1.1B.
- Dominari's link to the tungsten deal is indirect. The sons' Skyline stake reportedly went through a Dominari-subsidiary / American Ventures SPV, and Skyline bought 20% of Kaz Resources. Dominari was not a party to the Kazakh award.
- The congressional action is information-request letters, not subpoenas or charges.
- The FINRA action names Revere, not Wool.
- Eric Trump's only documented link to Tron is Dominari's advisory board, and he denies involvement.

## Denials and other side
- Eric Trump said: "I am not involved in Tron's public listing plans."
- The Trump Organization sent the NYT a retraction demand calling any suggestion of the brothers' involvement "categorically false, misleading and libelous." It said they had "absolutely no involvement in the award." Eric called himself "a passive investor with absolutely no management role." The NYT replied that the letter "does not deny the main point of our story."
- A Don Jr. spokesman said he "does not interface with the federal government on behalf of any company."
- White House: "The only special interest guiding the Trump administration's decision-making is the best interest of the American people."
- Dominari said it will cooperate with the House committee and described Chinese IPOs as a small part of its business that has ended.
- Revere settled without admitting or denying.
- Oversight on the Democratic side includes:
  - Sen. Warren's letters to Lutnick, Hegseth and EXIM President Jovanovic (July 7-8, 2026).
  - A Blumenthal-Schiff-Warren letter.
  - CREW FOIA requests (July-August 2026).
- No outcomes from that oversight have been located.
