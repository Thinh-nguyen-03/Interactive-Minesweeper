# C76: Russia-linked money around the family and envoys

**Verdict: partly-accurate (confidence: medium).** Several Russia-adjacent links are documented. Others in the brief are single-source, unverified, or not about Russia at all. None of the sources we found shows Witkoff or Kushner holding a personal financial stake in any Russia deal. A documented relationship is not evidence of wrongdoing.

Origin: connection hunt that builds on C53 (Witkoff family money; it logged the Blavatnik lead), C66 (extended family; it logged the Kremlev and Guilfoyle leads), C37 (alleged GRU hack of Kushner's phone) and C15 (Kushner/Affinity).

**Method note.** Direct fetches of bylinetimes.com and propublica.org failed because the hosts are not allowed in this session. WSJ and NYT findings were read through syndications (To Vima) and relaying outlets (Meduza, Ukrainska Pravda, Kyiv Independent, Moscow Times). Each point is graded on that basis. We ran about 27 web searches.

Evidence grades: **documented** (filings, official records or several outlets), **single-source** (one outlet or anonymous sourcing), **inferred** (our own observation; no source asserts it).

## Connections found

### 1. Kremlev → Don Jr.'s wedding celebrations (documented)
- ProPublica reported on 14 Sep 2026 that Umar Kremlev spent "hundreds of thousands of dollars" on the May 2026 Bahamas wedding weekend. Kremlev is the Russian president of the International Boxing Association (IBA) and has been photographed with Putin. The spending included renting one of the two private islands and a fireworks show. The money came through **IB Challenger**, a Dubai entity affiliated with the IBA.
- The family confirmed the payments. A Trump Jr. spokesperson called Kremlev "a personal friend of Don" but "not someone he has a business relationship with." Bettina Anderson says he paid for two after-parties, not the wedding itself. The family called it a gift and promised to repay it, and on 6 Oct 2026 Kremlev said he had been paid back (AP). The amount, and whether the money went to Kremlev or to IB Challenger, were not disclosed, so the repayment rests on Kremlev's word. The IBA says it paid nothing itself.
- Response: Rep. Robert Garcia sent records requests to the White House and Trump Jr. on 15 Sep 2026, asking among other things about a security clearance and whether Kremlev sought favourable treatment. Because Democrats are in the minority, the requests are voluntary. Sen. John Curtis (R-UT) asked Senate Judiciary to investigate. AG Todd Blanche: "What is there to investigate." Trump: "no idea who Umar is."
- Layer: the IOC has criticised the IBA for depending on Gazprom sponsorship. Kyiv Post could not identify IB Challenger's own funding sources. We found no report that Kremlev sought or received a US benefit.

### 2. Witkoff Group ↔ Blavatnik's Access Industries (partnership documented; "conflict" framing single-source)
- The two firms bought the XI project out of a ~$900M Dec 2021 foreclosure and renamed it One High Line. They refinanced it with a $1.15B JPMorgan/Tyko loan (Aug 2024) and a $525M loan (Jan 2026). They also co-own Banyan Cay (West Palm Beach) and announced a North Beach, Miami project with Sandor Scher in Feb 2025 (The Real Deal, Bloomberg).
- Counteroffensive (Apr 2025), Popular Information and Byline Times (1 Dec 2025) present this as an undisclosed conflict during the Russia talks. They note that Ukraine sanctioned Blavatnik for ten years in Dec 2023 on unpublished grounds.
- Byline's further claim that Blavatnik was a "former adviser" to Dmitriev is **unverified**. In the coverage we could see, the named pre-2022 RDIF foreign advisers are Leon Black and Stephen Schwarzman.
- Witkoff sold a $120M stake in his firm in Sep 2025. His son Alex still runs it, so the family's exposure to the partnership continues. That point is **inferred**.
- Denials: Access Industries says Blavatnik and Witkoff "have never spoken about the Russia-Ukraine negotiations." Blavatnik says he exited Russia in 2022.

### 3. Dmitriev ↔ Witkoff and Kushner: "make money not war" (documented)
- Dmitriev (RDIF CEO, US-sanctioned 2022) visited Washington on 2-3 Apr 2025 at Witkoff's invitation. CNN and Reuters, citing anonymous sources, reported a temporary sanctions waiver so he could get a visa.
- He held three days of talks with Witkoff and Kushner in Miami (24-26 Oct 2025, Axios), which produced the Nov 2025 28-point plan. Axios published the text: $100B of frozen Russian assets would go to US-led reconstruction, with the US taking 50% of the profit. Remaining frozen funds would go into a joint US-Russian investment vehicle.
- The WSJ's 29 Nov 2025 "Make Money Not War" reports Dmitriev pitching joint deals in rare earths, Arctic energy and shipping. It quotes Witkoff hoping Russia, Ukraine and the US become "business partners."
- Further meetings: Jan/Feb 2026 in Miami, and Mar 2026 on energy (Moscow Times, Interfax).
- The White House says it took input from both sides. The deals described are state or corporate proposals, not personal holdings of the envoys.

### 4. The 2016-17 precedent: Tahnoon → Dmitriev → Gerson → Kushner (documented, Mueller report)
- Per the Mueller report, the UAE national security adviser introduced Dmitriev to Kushner's friend Rick Gerson in Nov 2016. The two drafted a "reconciliation plan," which Dmitriev implied Putin had cleared. Gerson passed it to Kushner in Jan 2017, and Kushner shared it with Bannon and Tillerson. Kushner told investigators no one followed up.
- This is the same UAE-Dmitriev-Kushner triangle that runs through C06/C15/C57, nine years earlier. It shows a channel existed, not that money moved.

### 5. Lukoil's ~$20B foreign assets in the talks (single-source core; consortium reported by several relays)
- NYT (early Oct 2026, three unnamed people): Putin raised the Lukoil asset sale with Witkoff and Kushner in the Kremlin on 5 Sep 2026.
- The lead bidder is a consortium headed by Todd Boehly, a Trump donor ($1M-$2M; outlets disagree). Reported members are Tahnoon-chaired IHC, Al Hail Holding, the Qatar-based Al-Khayyat brothers and the US DFC (reported mid-teens stake).
- The Al-Khayyats are reported as former partners of Kushner and Ivanka Trump on the southern Albania resort project (C45). That is single-source in what we saw.
- Carlyle signed a deal in Jan 2026 and later withdrew. Any sale needs OFAC and Kremlin approval. *Update:* OFAC's GL 131K authorises negotiations only to 21 Nov 2026. Transfer needs a separate licence, and the proceeds must be blocked. No signed sale had been reported by mid-Nov 2026.
- Witkoff's spokesperson: "no conflict of interest and no financial stake in this matter." The NYT found no indication of personal profit for Witkoff or Kushner.

### 6. Gentry Beach ↔ Novatek (documented; FT layer single-source)
- Beach is a Dallas investor and friend of Trump Jr. Per the NYT (Feb 2026), he signed an LNG technology agreement with sanctioned Novatek after the Aug 2025 Alaska summit, for the North Slope "Polar Train" project, using a mobile plant built at Novatek's Murmansk site.
- In Oct 2026 the FT (relayed) reported allegations that Novatek was financing and controlling the ~$25B project, and that most of its management left. The allegation comes mainly from former CEO Joel Riddle. Polar LNG denies it (Alaska Journal of Commerce, 9 Nov 2026).
- Beach denies doing business with the Trump family.

### 7. WLF tokens to Russia-linked wallets (unverified)
- Accountable.US (Sep 2025) says more than 10,000 WLFI went to a wallet that had transacted in A7A5, the sanctioned ruble stablecoin.
- Senators Warren and Reed asked DOJ and Treasury to investigate (18 Nov 2025), and Senate Banking Democrats raised it again on 13 May 2026. The letters are documented. The underlying on-chain claim is single-source.
- WLF cites its AML/KYC checks, and some analysts call the hits false positives. We found no government finding.

### 8. Russian buyers of Trump property (documented, historical only)
- Reuters (2017) counted at least 63 Russia-linked buyers paying $98.4M in seven Trump-branded Florida towers. A 2018 analysis found 86 all-cash purchases totalling about $109M by buyers tied to Russia or other former Soviet states.
- Most of these units were in licensed buildings developed by others. The Trump Organization says it does not track buyers' nationality. We found nothing for 2025-26.

### 9. Kushner's Cadre ← Milner (documented, historical)
- The Paradise Papers (2017) show that VTB funded a $191M Twitter stake through Milner's DST vehicles in 2011. Milner's family trust put $850k into Cadre in 2015.
- Milner denies any VTB role in the Cadre investment.

### 10. Guilfoyle pay-to-play (documented, but not Russia-linked)
- WSJ (Oct 2026): messages show Guilfoyle pressing donor Eric Deters in July 2025 to wire $100,000 to her Amex account, and offering to lobby DOJ and the IRS for him. Deters calls it "pay to play."
- Her lawyer disputes the messages' authenticity. Oversight Democrats requested records on 8 Oct 2026. The White House asked her to resign, and she resigned on 13 Oct 2026. Deters has reportedly filed a defamation suit (single-source).
- We found no Russia connection. Including it in this cluster is therefore misleading.

## Not connected in the coverage we found
- **Blavatnik as an adviser to Dmitriev or RDIF.** Only Byline Times says this; no independent confirmation. *Single-source / unverified.*
- **RDIF as "one of Kushner's biggest creditors."** Byline claim; we could not verify it and did not adopt it. *Unverified.*
- **Blavatnik partnership → Witkoff's Russia positions.** No source shows any influence, and Access denies any discussion. Pairing the deal timeline with the talks is *inferred*.
- **Lukoil consortium → Witkoff/Kushner personal benefit.** No source asserts it, and the NYT says it found no indication of one. *Inferred* web of prior relationships only: Tahnoon's IHC and Lunate in Affinity (C15/C57), MGX and WLF (C06/C07), and the Al-Khayyats in Albania.
- **Kremlev payments → any US policy benefit** (Olympic boxing recognition, sanctions, visas). None reported. *Not connected.*
- **Gentry Beach's Novatek deal → Trump Jr.'s business.** Beach denies it; there is no evidence. Their friendship is *documented*; any business link is *not connected*.
- **Guilfoyle → Russia.** *Not connected.*
- **Alleged GRU access to Kushner's phone (C37) → his current Russia role.** This is Raskin's framing and stays a single-source book claim. *Inferred* if read as leverage.
