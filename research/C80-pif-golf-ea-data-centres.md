# C80: Sovereign-fund megadeals needing US sign-off: PGA-PIF, EA, Aligned, G42

**Verdict:** mostly-accurate (confidence: medium)

**Type:** connection check, second layer from C56 and C57 (builds on C56, C57, C15; also C68)

## Summary

The deals themselves are well documented. The approvals behind them often are not.

- **Golf:** Trump hosted the PGA Tour and PIF governor Yasir Al-Rumayyan at the White House in February 2025. No deal followed. PIF is quitting LIV after 2026, and LIV's new lead investor had not been named as of August 2026.
- **Trump Doral:** In the same period, the PGA Tour moved a $20M signature event to Trump Doral. So both sides of the dispute Trump was mediating held events at his resort.
- **EA:** The $55B EA buyout closed on 4 Aug 2026. Press reports say CFIUS cleared it, but no decision or mitigation terms are public.
- **Aligned:** MGX's $40B Aligned purchase closed in July 2026. No CFIUS outcome was reported.
- **UAE chips:** On 10 Jul 2026 the UAE was upgraded to Country Group A:5. G42 and Core42 got licence-free chip access for about 270 days, and MGX got a promise of "favorable review".
- **G42 in the US:** The report that G42 will become a US company is relayed, contested and unconfirmed.
- **Lunate and G42's China fund:** Lunate's takeover of the fund is a single-source 2024 Bloomberg report. Lunate is also an LP in Kushner's Affinity, which links G42's China fund to the Kushner fund through the same holder.

Research note: the original pages at most outlets, including Game Developer, Enterprise AM and Il Foglio, were not read directly. Findings rely on search-result coverage across several outlets. The WSJ and Bloomberg originals on G42 were not accessed.

## Connections, with evidence grades

### 1. Trump as PGA Tour-PIF broker (DOCUMENTED; no deal resulted)
- **2025-02-04:** Monahan and Adam Scott met Trump at the White House, at the Tour's request.
- **2025-02-20:** Monahan, Tiger Woods and Scott held a session of about four hours with Trump and Al-Rumayyan. The joint statement called it "constructive".
- **On the table:** about $1.5B of PIF money into PGA Tour Enterprises.
- **Aftermath:** Golf Digest (March 2025) reported that the talks stalled and that Al-Rumayyan found the Tour's proposals inadequate. AP said not even Trump could smooth the way.
- **New CEO:** Brian Rolapp later said there was "no merger, no conversations".
- **DOJ:** The Justice Department's antitrust review dates from 2023. We found no 2025-26 DOJ decision.

### 2. Both sides of the golf dispute became Doral customers (each event DOCUMENTED; the pairing is INFERRED)
- **LIV:** LIV played Doral in April 2025, Trump National DC in May 2026 and Bedminster in August 2026 (C56).
- **PGA Tour:** The Cadillac Championship ($20M purse, announced around 2025-12-15) was played at Trump Doral on 30 Apr to 3 May 2026. It was the Tour's first event there since 2016.
- **Unknowns:** Host fees are undisclosed. No source links the venue choice to the talks.

### 3. LIV's new lead investor (UNVERIFIED identity)
- **PIF exit:** On around 2026-04-30, PIF said it would stop funding LIV after 2026. It has put in more than $5B.
- **New investor:** On 2026-08-05, O'Neil announced a board-approved term sheet with an unnamed lead investor. Players are to become majority owners, with a close targeted for September.
- **Status:** We found no name and no confirmed close.

### 4. EA buyout and CFIUS (closing DOCUMENTED; CFIUS clearance from press accounts only)
- **Timeline:** Announced 2025-09-29. Shareholders approved it on 2025-12-22. The EU cleared it in late July 2026. Reports say all approvals were in by 2026-07-30. It closed on 2026-08-04 at $210 a share.
- **Stakes:** PIF about 93.4%, Silver Lake about 5.5%, Affinity about 1.1%. These are press estimates.
- **Critics:** Blumenthal and Warren wrote to CFIUS chair Bessent on 2025-10-14 that the buyers were "betting $1 billion that Mr. Kushner can deliver" approvals. Lawfare predicted "the CFIUS review that will never be".
- **Gap:** No mitigation agreement is public.

### 5. Aligned Data Centers (deal DOCUMENTED; approvals UNVERIFIED)
- **Buyers:** MGX, AIP and BlackRock's GIP bought 100% of Aligned from Macquarie-managed funds for about $40B EV. The deal closed in July 2026, with about $5B of additional capital.
- **AIP:** Its co-founders are BlackRock, GIP, MGX, Microsoft and Nvidia. KIA and Temasek are anchor investors.
- **CFIUS:** A review was "expected" at announcement. No outcome was found.

### 6. UAE export-control upgrade (DOCUMENTED; the lobbying account is SINGLE-SOURCE)
- **The rule:** BIS moved the UAE from D:3/D:4 to A:5, announced around 10 Jul 2026 and published in the Federal Register around 14 Jul.
- **G42 and Core42:** Both are listed in Supp. 8 to Part 740 for licence-free receipt of advanced-computing items, but not under STA. The approval sunsets after about 270 days, reported as around 2027-04-06.
- **MGX:** Commerce said it would "favorably review" MGX licence applications.
- **Stated basis:** UAE safeguards and the UAE's support in the 2026 Iran war.
- **Lobbying (single-source):** The WSJ, as relayed, says Tahnoon lobbied the White House directly.
- **Criticism:** Warren (2026-07-10) tied the rule to the Tahnoon-linked 49% stake in WLF. The White House and WLF deny any link.

### 7. G42 "reincorporating" in the US (UNVERIFIED)
- **WSJ (around July 2026, via aggregators):** G42 plans to reincorporate in the US, majority US-owned.
- **Enterprise AM (17 Jul 2026):** take it "with a kilo of salt".
- **Bloomberg (around 4 Sep 2026):** only "exploratory talks" on a US-majority stake or a new US vehicle. No buyers were named, and G42 would not comment.
- **Status:** Still an open question in November 2026.

### 8. Lunate and G42's China fund (SINGLE-SOURCE, Bloomberg 2024)
- **The transfer:** In July 2024, management of 42XFund passed from a G42 subsidiary to Lunate. 42XFund holds about $10B and has stakes in ByteDance and JD.com. This was a change of manager, not a confirmed sale.
- **Timing:** It followed G42's February 2024 statement that it had exited China.
- **Ownership:** Bloomberg calls Lunate an IHC unit. C57 records Chimera as its majority owner. Both are Tahnoon-linked.
- **Gap:** We found no 2025-26 follow-up.

## Not connected in the coverage we found

These links are our own cross-referencing. No source asserts them.

- **Lunate in two roles (INFERRED):** Lunate both runs G42's former China fund and is a top-three LP in Kushner's Affinity. Both facts are documented separately; no outlet joins them.
- **Doral (INFERRED):** The PGA Tour and PIF-funded LIV both held events at Trump Doral within about 13 months while Trump mediated between them.
- **Silver Lake (INFERRED):** It co-owns EA with Affinity, the TikTok JV with MGX (C68), and G42. It is a shared partner across three US-approval-dependent deals on both the Saudi/Kushner and Tahnoon sides.
- **Microsoft and Nvidia (INFERRED):** Both co-own AIP, which bought Aligned with MGX. Microsoft owns part of G42, and Nvidia supplies chips under the UAE licences. Both were also ballroom donors (C68).
- **MGX in the July rule (INFERRED as a pattern):** MGX got favourable-review language in the same rule that freed G42. In the same network, Aryam holds 49% of World Liberty (C57).

## Corrections
- "Brokering" overstates the result. Trump was invited in, and no PGA-PIF deal exists as of 2026.
- G42's US reincorporation has not happened. There is only a relayed report and exploratory talks.
- Lunate took over management of the China fund. Ownership was not confirmed to have changed.
- Some golf pages show 2026 crawl-date banners on February 2025 stories. There was no "October 2026" Trump meeting.

## Denials and other side
- **PGA Tour:** It asked for Trump's help, and Monahan said Trump "bolstered" the talks.
- **Commerce:** It justified the UAE upgrade on security commitments and wartime support.
- **White House and WLF:** They deny any link between chips and the family's crypto business.
- **G42 and Lunate:** G42 does not comment on confidential talks, and Lunate declined to comment.
- **CFIUS secrecy:** CFIUS decisions are confidential by law, so the lack of public terms is normal. It does not show that a review was skipped.

## Audit (second review, Nov 2026)

Result: **upheld**. Re-checked: the EA buyout close and CFIUS; the UAE A:5 rule; Tahnoon's reported lobbying; G42's restructuring.

- **EA buyout.** GamingBolt, Mobilegamer.biz and Tech-Insider report that EA's 30 Jul 2026 filing said all approvals were in hand. CFIUS was the last hurdle and pushed the close past 30 Jun. One relay of ABC News dates the close to 5 Aug rather than 4 Aug. No Treasury statement or mitigation terms were found.
- **UAE rule.** Law-firm alerts (Latham, Skadden, Morgan Lewis, Akin, Steptoe) give the effective date as 10 Jul 2026. G42/Core42's approved status expires on 6 Apr 2027 unless they become US companies or get approval through BIS's advisory-opinion process. That expiry is the stated driver of the G42 restructuring talks.
- **Still single-source.** The Tahnoon lobbying account (WSJ, via relays) and the G42 reincorporation report. The latest item found is Bloomberg's September 2026 report of exploratory talks. I found no deal through Nov 2026.
