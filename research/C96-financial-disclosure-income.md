# C96: What Trump's own financial disclosures report

**Verdict: confirmed** (confidence: high)

Both second-term annual reports on OGE Form 278e were downloaded from the Office of Government Ethics and read in full:

- **The report for calendar 2025:** 927 PDF pages, received by OGE on 29 June 2026 and released on 30 June 2026.
- **The report for calendar 2024:** 234 PDF pages, signed and certified on 13 June 2025.

The figures below come from the forms themselves, line by line. Totals are our own sums unless they are attributed to an outlet.

**Source limits.** The August 2024 candidate report is not on OGE's index and was not read; it is described from CREW's and Axios's readings. Forbes, PolitiFact, CNBC and CREW's 2026 analysis were blocked for direct fetch, so they are cited from search excerpts. The Reuters, AP and NYT reports were read in full through syndicated copies.

## What was checked

What income, assets and liabilities did Donald Trump himself declare on his OGE Form 278e reports filed in 2025 and 2026? What did they show by source: crypto, licensing, golf and resorts, Trump Media, royalties, merchandise and other income? How do they compare with the last pre-presidency filing?

The 2026 form is paginated "of 847" and is followed by a 23-page Schedule 1 and a trademark exhibit. That is why some outlets report 847 pages and others 927. OGE's index also lists 22 periodic transaction reports (278-T) filed between August 2025 and October 2026.

## Sub-claims

### 1. Crypto was the largest declared source in 2025: confirmed

Figures from the 2026 report, Schedule 1:

- **CIC Digital LLC** (100% owned by Trump's revocable trust):
  - $635,068,835 of royalties under a "License agreement with Celebration Coins" (line 21.6)
  - $493,846 from a sublicense with JBCZ Group
  - $510,808 of Ethereum staking rewards and $45,932 of USDC interest
  - a bitcoin wallet valued at "Over $50,000,000"
- **DT Marks DEFI LLC:**
  - $65,625,000 of net proceeds from selling equity in WLF Holdco (line 124.2)
  - token-sale proceeds "distributed by World Liberty Financial" (lines 124.3 to 124.12) of $526,810,321 in total: $236,250,000 in cash plus nine crypto-wallet lines (ETH $150.6M, USDC $56.0M, USD1 $42.25M, BTC $33.5M, and LINK, AAVE, ENA, MOVE and ONDO)
  - $1,821,628 of staking rewards
- **DT Marks SC LLC:** $196,875,000 of "net proceeds received from capital contribution from new member(s) of Stablecoin Holdco LLC and sale of Class C Units" (line 114). Stablecoin Holdco, the USD1 business, reports $8,326,828 of net operating income.
- **Who owns the two crypto entities.** DT Marks DEFI and DT Marks SC are each owned 69% by DTTM Operations, 1% by a DTTM-owned member corporation and 30% by "Trump Family Members". At the end of 2025 each held 38.25% of its holding company. In the 2024 report, DT Marks DEFI held 75% of WLF Holdco.
- **Token holdings.** DTTM Operations lists 15.75 billion WLFI governance tokens, valued "Over $50,000,000".

These lines add up to about $1.425 billion. The outlets' figures map onto the filing as follows:

- **Reuters:** "more than $1.4B" in total, with "almost $800M" from World Liberty. The filing gives $789.3M (526.8 + 65.6 + 196.9).
- **PolitiFact:** about $791M from World Liberty, which also includes the staking rewards.
- **Bloomberg:** about $594M from World Liberty (526.8 + 65.6 + 1.8 of staking), with the Stablecoin Holdco line counted separately.
- **AP:** about $1.2B.

### 2. 2024 crypto income was far smaller: confirmed

It is the company's gross token revenue, not a payment to Trump; DT Marks DEFI's own line shows no 2024 income.

### 9. Comparison with the candidate filing: mostly accurate

- **CREW's reading of the August 2024 report** (about January 2023 to August 2024): at least $732.1M and possibly over $782.5M of business income, and liabilities of at least $346M.
- **Line items:** Doral $160M, Mar-a-Lago $56M (Axios: $56.9M), Bedminster $37M, about $6M from Truth Social, and over $15M of licensing including about $7M from NFTs.
- **Comparability:** the report spans about 19 months, so it does not compare cleanly with calendar years.
- **Year-on-year totals:** the NYT compares a 2024 minimum of $622M with at least $2.2B for 2025. Forbes compares about $760M with about $2.4B, an estimate that also uses other records.

### 10. What the form does not capture

1. **Ranges.** Many amounts are brackets with no upper bound.
2. **Revenue, not profit.** Business lines show gross revenue.
3. **Family shares.** Income is reported at the entity level even where family members own 20-30%. Their separate ventures do not appear, and family members are not named.
4. **Unrealised value.** Gains or losses on shares, tokens and wallets are not income.
5. **Salary.** The government salary is excluded.
6. **Timing.** Calendar 2025 includes the three days of TRUMP meme-coin sales before the inauguration.

Revenue reported during the term is not the same as revenue earned because of the office.

## Corrections

- Claimed (C42, C11): Trump "disclosed about $57M from WLF" for 2024. The record shows: $57,355,532 is World Liberty Financial, Inc.'s own token-sale revenue. It is booked on that company's line, and DT Marks DEFI shows no 2024 income.
- Claimed (C29, C42): The Block itemizes "$236.25M of token proceeds". The record shows: that is only the cash line. With nine crypto-wallet lines the total is $526,810,321.
- Claimed (C42): DT Marks DEFI's stake in WLF Holdco was "about 60%, then 40%, then 38.25%". The record shows: the forms give 75% at the end of 2024 and 38.25% at the end of 2025.
- Claimed (C42): "The 22.5B-token stake was valued at about $1.3B." The record shows: the forms list 15.75B WLFI under DTTM Operations, valued only as "Over $50,000,000". 15.75B is 70% of 22.5B, consistent with the 30% family share (our arithmetic). The $1.3B is a press valuation.
- Claimed (C29): Bloomberg's "about $594M" for the WLF component. The record shows: 526.81 + 65.63 = $592.44M, or about $594.3M with staking rewards. The Stablecoin Holdco figure is exactly $196,875,000, on the DT Marks SC line.
- Claimed (C11, C29, C42): the receipts are described as Trump's income. The record shows: they are entity totals of which Trump's indirect share is about 70%.
- Claimed (AP and others): Trump "owes E. Jean Carroll $50 million". The record shows: the form gives "Over $50,000,000" and "$1,000,001 - $5,000,000"; exact amounts are not shown.
- Claimed (InvestmentNews): an 847-page report. The record shows: the PDF has 927 pages; 847 is the pagination of the form proper.

## Other side

- **White House spokesperson Anna Kelly (30 June 2026):** "Neither the President nor his family has ever engaged — or will ever engage — in conflicts of interest. President Trump proudly made the United States the crypto capital of the world through executive actions." She added that the administration's actions are "taken in the best interest of the American people".
- **Trump Organization spokesperson:** "At nearly 1,000 pages, it represents one of the most comprehensive financial disclosure reports ever submitted and demonstrates a level of financial transparency unmatched in presidential history."
- **Trump (2 July 2026):** "nothing illegal, there's nothing wrong with it"; "I was there [in crypto] before I was in office". He said institutions manage his money.
- **Karoline Leavitt (June 2025, on the 2024 report):** the President and staff "completed required ethics briefings and financial reporting obligations… The Trump Administration is committed to transparency."
- **Former acting OGE director Don Fox (to Reuters):** presidents are exempt from the conflict-of-interest statute, but post-Watergate presidents acted as if they were not; "With Trump, those norms are just totally out the window."
- **The White House's position on control:** it says the children run the businesses. Reuters notes that Trump remains the beneficiary of the trust that receives the income.
