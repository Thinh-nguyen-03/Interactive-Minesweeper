# C10: Binance driving USD1 adoption

**Verdict:** mostly-accurate (confidence: medium)

## What the video says (transcript lines 453-467)
- Binance was "one of the first" exchanges to list USD1 for trading.
- Binance offered promotions that effectively paid customers about 20% annual yield to hold USD1 ("unheard of").
- The Wall Street Journal charted USD1's adoption, and each major jump in usage traced back to Binance.
- World Liberty denies anything suspicious and calls it standard industry practice for marketing a stablecoin.
- The revenue flows to World Liberty Financial, which pays the president and his family. WLF boasts that it makes about $159M a year from USD1 and calls this "compounding, accelerating".

## Evidence by sub-claim

1. **Early Binance listing: confirmed, with a nuance.** Binance opened USD1/USDT spot trading on 2025-05-22 and waived the listing fee. HTX and MEXC had listed USD1 earlier, and KuCoin listed it on 2025-05-21. So Binance was "one of the first" large venues, as the video says, but it was not the first. Before that, in May 2025, Binance had also been named as the recipient of MGX's $2B investment, which was settled in USD1. Later Binance added USD1 trading pairs, zero-fee conversions and a BUSD-to-USD1 collateral switch. [1][2][3]
2. **Up to 20% yield: confirmed, but it was a capped, time-limited promotion.** Binance ran a "booster" offering up to 20% APR on flexible USD1 Earn products from about 2025-12-24 to 2026-01-23. The rate was Binance's real-time APR plus tiered bonus rewards, and reports said the bonus covered only the first ~50,000 USD1 per user (one source says the cap was not confirmed). USD1's market cap rose from about $2.74B to $2.89B after the announcement. A further $40M rewards pool for USD1 holders was announced on 2026-01-22. A person familiar with the matter told Bloomberg that WLF gives Binance a marketing budget used for these promotions. The video's phrase "paying customers to hold" fits that description. Calling 20% "unheard of" is commentary: short exchange boosts at that level are uncommon but do happen. [4][5][6][7]
3. **WSJ chart linking each jump to Binance: unverified as worded, but the substance is supported.** I could not get to the WSJ article (wsj.com is blocked for this tool), so I could not confirm a chart that ties each step in usage to Binance. Independent reporting backs the underlying point:
   - Supply rose from about $130M to about $2.1B in early May 2025, which matched the MGX-Binance deal.
   - Growth followed the Binance listing and promotions, and USD1 passed $5B by January 2026.
   - Arkham, Nansen and Kaiko data put about 85-87% of USD1 in Binance accounts. Forbes reported about $4.7B of $5.4B on 2026-02-09.
   - Bloomberg's own analysis (2026-06-05) credited WLF's stablecoin profits partly to "a promotional arrangement with Binance."

   The video may be describing a WSJ graphic accurately, but I could not confirm that graphic. [8][9][10][11][7]
4. **WLF says this is standard practice: confirmed.** A WLF spokesman reportedly called the incentives "standard practice" for stablecoin issuers and said WLF funds them. WLF told Forbes that "any implication that Binance can exert control or influence over World Liberty Financial is patently false" and said it has run promotions on other exchanges too. Binance says it "has not provided preferential treatment," that it hosts more than 15 stablecoins and runs promotions for other issuers, and that WLF does not share revenue with it. Binance CEO Richard Teng denied that Binance boosted WLF ahead of the CZ pardon. [7][10][12]
5. **About $159M a year: confirmed.** In April 2026 WLF posted on social media a USD1 annualized revenue "run rate" of $159.5M. This figure is a run rate, meaning reserve interest at that moment scaled up to a full year. It is not booked annual revenue. Bloomberg separately estimated nearly $150M for 2026, assuming about 3.5% yield on about $4.7B of reserves minus BitGo custody fees. I could not confirm the exact "compounding, accelerating" wording. [7][13][14]
6. **Revenue flows to WLF and the Trump family: broadly supported.** Bloomberg values WLF's stablecoin business at about $1.7B and credits the Trump family with a stake of about $630M. Trump's financial disclosure shows large income from crypto businesses. The exact split of USD1 interest income is not public. [7][15]

## Corrections
- Binance was not the first exchange to list USD1. HTX, MEXC and KuCoin listed it before Binance did on 2025-05-22.
- The "20% annual yield" was a promotional APR that ran for about one month and applied only to a capped amount per user. It was not a standing yield on all USD1 holdings.
- The $159M is WLF's own run-rate figure ($159.5M, April 2026), not audited annual revenue.
- I could not independently confirm the WSJ chart. Bloomberg and Forbes published comparable analyses.

## Denials and context
- WLF says the incentives are standard practice, that it funds them, and that Binance has no control over WLF.
- Binance denies giving WLF preferential treatment and says it promotes other stablecoins too.
- Critics, including an NYU professor quoted in secondary coverage and Senators Warren and Merkley, see the Binance relationship as a conflict of interest, given the MGX deal and the CZ pardon. That conflict is an inference. No documented quid pro quo has been shown.
- It is unclear how much of the USD1 sitting at Binance belongs to the exchange and how much to its customers.

## Second-review audit (2026-11-14)
- Re-checked: WLF's $159.5M run rate (April 2026, confirmed as WLF's own social-media figure) against Bloomberg's ~$147-150M 2026 estimate; the up-to-20% APR booster (2025-12-24 to 2026-01-23); and Binance's 85-89% share. **Result: upheld.** The ~50,000 USD1 bonus cap is widely reported but not confirmed from Binance's own terms.
- Later update: Cointelegraph (2026-10-19, CoinGecko data) reported USD1 supply fell about $401M (9.1%) from 2026-10-06 on redemptions, with reserves of about $4.01B. A shrinking base cuts against WLF's "compounding, accelerating" framing. Arkham later put Binance's custody share at about 89%.
