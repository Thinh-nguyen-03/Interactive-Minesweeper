# C97: Crypto Income During the Presidency

**Verdict: mostly accurate** (confidence: medium)

The question: how much have Donald Trump and his family received from crypto ventures since January 2025, and how much of it is cash rather than paper value?

**Short answer.** Trump's own disclosure for calendar 2025, the first year of the second term, documents about **$1.44B of gross crypto-related income**. The disclosure is OGE Form 278e, released June 30, 2026, and it was read in full for this check. Most of that income is cash or crypto that was actually paid out. Press estimates for the whole family run from about $1.2B to $2.4B. They are estimates and count different things, some of them paper value. The paper value of what the family still holds has fallen steeply: WLFI is down about 86% from its peak, $TRUMP about 99%, American Bitcoin about 94% and AI Financial (formerly ALT5) about 98%. Income for 2026 has not been disclosed; the next annual filing is due by May 15, 2027.

**Source limits.** sec.gov, Reuters, The Block, NOTUS and the WSJ could not be read directly from this environment. Figures from those outlets and from SEC 13D filings come from search excerpts or from outlets that relayed them, and the source list marks which is which.

## Sub-claims

### 1. World Liberty token sales: confirmed
- Trump reported **$526.8M** of World Liberty token-sale proceeds for 2025. About $290M of it arrived as crypto rather than dollars. [1]
- The money comes from a service agreement. World Liberty's site disclaimer (as quoted by CAP, accessed September 2025) says DT Marks DEFI is "entitled to receive fees ... equal to 75% of $WLFI token sale proceeds after deduction of agreed reserves, expenses and other amounts". The Gold Paper holds $15M of initial net revenue in reserve. [3]
- The current World Liberty homepage, fetched for this check, no longer carries the ownership disclaimer in its served HTML. [26]
- For calendar 2024, before the term began, the earlier filing reported $57.36M. [3]
- **Open question:** the form does not say whether the DT Marks DEFI amounts are the entity's total or only Trump's roughly 70% share. If they are Trump's share, other family members received up to about $340M more in 2025. That figure is our arithmetic, and no source states it. CAP reads the $262.5M stake-sale figure as Trump's 70% share. [4]

### 2. Stake sales (the Aryam/UAE deal): mostly accurate
- The form shows **$65.6M** from the sale of WLF Holdco equity and **$196.9M** from new members of Stablecoin Holdco and the sale of Class C units. That is **$262.5M** in total, and no buyer is named. [1]
- The WSJ reported on Feb. 1, 2026 that Aryam Investment 1, backed by Sheikh Tahnoon bin Zayed, agreed to pay $500M for 49% of World Liberty, and that about $187M of the first $250M went to Trump-family entities. The WSJ text was not read directly; this comes from relays (see C06). [3]
- CAP observes that $262.5M is exactly 75% × 70% × $500M, which would fit the full $500M having been paid and distributed. This is an inference, and no document shows the second $250M was paid. [4]

### 3. The "$594M versus $800M" dispute: resolved by the filing
- Bloomberg and NOTUS report "about $594M": $526.8M + $65.6M = $592.4M, counting DT Marks DEFI only.
- Reuters and the NYT report "almost $800M": the same plus the $196.9M Stablecoin Holdco line, which is $789.3M. Reuters splits it into "more than $520M" from tokens and "more than $250M" ($262.5M) from "the sale of interests in the World Liberty business". [6]
- The Block's $236.25M is line 124.3 alone.
- The two figures differ only in classification. C29's audit had proposed this reconciliation as its own inference; the primary document confirms it.

All of these are well above the $526.8M on the 2025 form. Possible explanations, none confirmed:
- 2026 sales that have not yet been disclosed;
- money kept inside family LLCs or paid to other family members (CAP's explanation);
- the outlets' price assumptions.

### 5. ALT5 Sigma / AI Financial: partly accurate
- ALT5's August 2025 announcement, read in full in the SEC filing mirror, confirms the structure. The $1.5B offering's private placement was "contributed in the form of $WLFI tokens from World Liberty Financial", and the net proceeds were for buying WLFI. A September 2025 release reported 7.28B WLFI held. [15][16]
- Reuters and CAP put ALT5's cash purchases of WLFI at **$717M**. More than $500M routed to the Trump family is an estimate based on the 75% fee, and no filing itemizes it. [4][8]
- **Overlap:** this money is part of World Liberty token sales, so it sits inside the $526.8M and the press totals above.
- **Paper value:** the AI Financial shares World Liberty took for $750M of WLFI are now capped by the whole company's market value of about **$22.5M** (Nov. 13, 2026, about $0.17 a share). [20]

### 6. USD1 reserve income and the MGX-Binance settlement: partly accurate
- **Documented:** $8.33M of stablecoin net operating income for 2025. [1]
- **CAP estimates:** about $26.3M of gross 2025 reserve income for the family, and about $140,000 a day by July 2026. [4]
- **Bloomberg (June 5, 2026):** World Liberty "on track to generate nearly $150 million this year" from USD1. That is company revenue; the family's 38.25% would be about $57M (our arithmetic). [10]
- **MGX-Binance:** MGX paid no fee to World Liberty. Its value to World Liberty is the reserve yield while the $2B in USD1 stays outstanding, and that yield is already inside these estimates. No source found shows whether MGX's USD1 has been redeemed. Binance holds most of the supply, and World Liberty says it does not share USD1 revenue with Binance. [10]
- **Supply:** USD1 was about $3.08B on Nov. 16, 2026, down from about $4.7B in mid-2026. [22]

### 7. $TRUMP: confirmed for the reported royalties; on-chain estimates vary
- **Reported:** $635.07M of royalties to CIC Digital, plus small staking and interest income. This is licensing income. Reuters' phrase "from the sale of his Trump meme coins" is looser than the form. [1][6]
- **On-chain estimates, each measuring something different:**

| Source | Figure | Coverage |
|---|---|---|
| FT (March 2025) | ≥$314M from token sales + $36M in fees | Its own on-chain analysis, first three weeks [12] |
| Chainalysis (via DL News, May 2025) | >$320M in trading fees | Fees only [13] |
| CAP | about $313M of net USDC withdrawals from Meteora pools by insider wallets | Token sales [3] |
| Reuters (June 2026) | about $616M for the family | Through April 2026 [8] |

  Most of these cover both issuers, CIC Digital and Fight Fight Fight, and they overlap with the royalty figure.
- **2026:** Crypto Briefing (single source) reports that team wallets still held about 718M tokens and had moved about $249M of $TRUMP to exchanges over eight months. Deposits on an exchange do not prove sales. [14]
- **Paper value:** at $0.944, 718M tokens are worth about $678M, with the split between issuers unknown. Forbes values Trump's own $TRUMP at about $183M. [22][23]

### 8. $MELANIA: partly accurate
- **Reported:** $6.01M of NFT and collectibles licensing income to MKT World. The form does not name the coin. [1]
- **FT (May 2025):** the launch entities withdrew $64.7M from fees and primary sales; Melania Trump's share is unknown. [4]

### 9. WLFI holdings and unlocks: confirmed as paper value only
- **Holdings:** 22.5B WLFI are held by DT Marks DEFI and family members. [3]
- **Value:** at $0.0468 (Nov. 16, 2026) they would be worth about $1.05B before any discount for the lock-up. Forbes carries Trump's WLFI at about $78M. [22][23]
- **Lock-up:** after the May 2026 vesting vote, founders who opt in face a two-year cliff followed by three years of linear vesting, so no sales before about mid-2028. [24]

### 10. American Bitcoin: confirmed as paper value
- **Eric Trump:** his 13D/A (December 2025) shows 68,432,664 shares, 7.4%, held through his revocable trust. [18]
- **Current value:** after the 1-for-15 reverse split, the stake is about 4.56M shares, worth about **$23M** at $5.07 on Nov. 13, 2026 (our arithmetic). [19]
- **Earlier values:** about $548M after the September 2025 listing, and more than $70M at the end of April 2026 per Reuters, which found no evidence that he contributed capital. [8][21]
- **Donald Trump Jr.:** his stake is below 5% and undisclosed.
- No sales or dividends were found.

### 11. Combined totals: mostly accurate, but they overlap and must not be summed
- **Reuters, at least $2.3B:** World Liberty more than $1.6B, $TRUMP about $616M, American Bitcoin about $70M. The American Bitcoin part is paper value, and ALT5 is counted inside World Liberty. [8]
- **CAP, $2.405B:** $262.5M + $1.447B + $54.0M + $635.6M + $6.0M. [4]
- **House Oversight Democratic staff:** "nearly $2.25B" of profits, and up to $9.72B including paper wealth. [5]
- **Forbes (September 2026):** about $494M of crypto within a $7B net worth. [23]

### 12. Other treasury companies: unverified
Trump Media's crypto treasury is covered in C98, and the Thumzup and Metaplanet links in C18. No cash payment to a family member from these was found.

## Corrections
- **C29 and C42**, "contested $594M-$800M": the filing resolves it. $592.4M (DT Marks DEFI only) and $789.3M (adding DT Marks SC) are the same money classified two ways.
- **C42**, "$515-529M" from token sales: the exact sum is $526.8M.
- **C42**, the 22.5B tokens "valued at about $1.3B": that is a press valuation from July 2026. The filing says only "Over $50,000,000" for 15.75B. The value is about $1.05B at today's price before any lock-up discount, and Forbes carries about $78M.
- **C01 and the Reuters wording:** the $635M is royalties under a license, not proceeds from Trump selling coins.
- **C06**, the second $250M: still undocumented. CAP's 75% × 70% × $500M arithmetic would fit full payment, but this is an inference.
- **C55**, the Reuters $2.3B: it includes about $70M of paper value (American Bitcoin), so it is not purely cash.
- **House Oversight Democratic staff:** the $1.45B ALT5 figure is not all token sales for cash; about $750M was WLFI exchanged for shares.

## Responses
- **White House** (Anna Kelly, to Reuters, June 30, 2026): "Neither the President nor his family has ever engaged — or will ever engage — in conflicts of interest. President Trump proudly made the United States the crypto capital of the world through executive actions." The White House says his children run the businesses; Trump remains the beneficiary of the trust. [6]
- **Trump Organization:** the filing "demonstrates a level of financial transparency unmatched in presidential history." [6]
- **World Liberty Financial:** declined to comment on the disclosure. It confirmed its private token sales to Bloomberg without naming buyers or prices. Company representatives disputed parts of Reuters' accounting. [6][8][9]
- **$TRUMP:** the coin's website says it is "not distributed or sold by Donald J. Trump, The Trump Organization or any of their respective affiliates or principals." [3]
- Income received during the presidency is not shown to be income received because of it. No regulator or court has found wrongdoing.
