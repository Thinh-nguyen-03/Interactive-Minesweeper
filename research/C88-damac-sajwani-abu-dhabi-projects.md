# C88: Damac and Hussain Sajwani: fees for unannounced projects and the data-centre pledge

**Verdict: mostly-accurate (confidence: medium)**

This is a third-layer connection check. It builds on C70 (Damac's fees, the $20B pledge, the permitting order), C82 (the DT Marks Abu Dhabi entities and the payer subsidiaries) and C31 (Gulf licensing). It does not repeat those reports. It adds what the pledge has turned into on the ground, the full timeline of the relationship, and a check for crypto links.

Several parts of the claim hold up well: the money, the long relationship, and continued access to the president. The parts about federal permitting or energy decisions benefiting Damac's sites, and about Sajwani-family crypto dealings with Trump ventures, found no support. Both are rated **unverified**, not disproven.

Method note: citizensforethics.org, cnbc.com and tahawultech.com could not be read directly. Their findings come from search-result excerpts and syndicated copies (Times Brasil for CNBC). The Delaware register was not reachable.

## Connections (sub-claims)

1. **About $10M for two Abu Dhabi licences, with still no project.** *Documented* fees; CREW's own wording is that the two entities "appear to be related to unannounced developments in Abu Dhabi". The disclosure facts are in C82. New here: nothing was launched through Nov 2026. CNBC says the Trump Organization has no active Abu Dhabi development. The 2025 figure is exact, not an estimate. The OGE filing lists DT Marks Abu Dhabi LLC $5,000,000 (licensee DAMAC Sky View Real Estate LLC), DT Marks Al Raha Beach LLC $5,000,000 (licensee DAMAC Properties Investment LLC), DT Marks Dubai LLC $1,335,140 and DT Dubai Golf Manager LLC $1,254,744 (both Damac Crescent Properties LLC): $12,589,884 in all. CNBC's $11.3M is the three licence fees without the management fee. Cumulative figures remain estimates: about $6M a year (Forbes, Oct 2025) and "at least $13M, possibly $19M" across disclosures (CREW, Mar 2026).
2. **The pledge on the ground.** *Documented* (New Jersey details single-source). The $20B has become about $12B of land and power: a $36.5M site in Canton, Ohio (AEP Ohio territory, target 2028, phases to 2033, no MW published), a New Jersey site with no reported details, and an $860M warehouse conversion in Edgerton, Kansas. New Jersey and Kansas were not among the eight states announced. Nothing is operating. Sajwani said 60-70% would be debt-financed and that the $20B depends on "the opportunity, the market".
3. **Federal permitting or energy benefits.** *Unverified.* The general policies are documented: the 10 Dec 2024 $1B pledge, the 7 Jan 2025 remark, and EO 14318 (FAST-41 eligibility and Commerce financing for projects with $500M+ capex or more than 100MW of load). No source shows a FAST-41 designation, federal loan, or DOE or FERC action for any Damac site. That the sites would meet the order's thresholds is our inference. The approvals actually documented are local: the Edgerton override and the Canton annexation and water service. CNBC found no evidence that the fees influenced policy.
4. **Same-day formation of DT Marks Al Raha Beach and the EO.** *Single-source (CREW).* No second source was found (see C82).
5. **The relationship.** *Documented.* It is unusually continuous: licensing since about 2013; family occasions (the 2017 club opening and the 2018 wedding); New Year's Eve at Mar-a-Lago (2016 documented, 2024 single-source); and during the presidency, an Oval Office visit (Apr 2025) and the Abu Dhabi forum (May 2025). This documents access. It does not show any request or favour.
6. **Crypto, WLF and $TRUMP.** *Unverified / not found.* Damac's only documented crypto deal is the MANTRA tokenisation, which has no Trump link. Outlets that call Trump Tower Dubai a Damac project have it wrong: it is Dar Global's.
7. **Damac's statements.** *Documented.* Damac has made no comment on the 2025-26 payments (it did not respond to CNBC). Its public posture is promotional (Nov 2024, Apr 2025 statements). In 2017 it confirmed the declined offer.
8. **CREW's Ohio utility-price framing.** *Misleading as a causal implication.* The Canton campus is unbuilt (target 2028), so it cannot explain the 2025-26 rate rises. FactCheck.org says data centres are one driver of Ohio's increases among several.

## Corrections

- No federal permitting or energy decision for a Damac site was found. Every approval documented is local.
- The documented on-stage NYE praise is from 2016. NYE 2024 rests on Sajwani's posts as relayed by Raw Story.
- Trump Tower Dubai (which accepts crypto) is Dar Global's, not Damac's, despite some crypto-press wording.
- Damac's US sites include New Jersey and Edgerton, Kansas, which were not among the eight states announced in Jan 2025.
- An AP-syndicated summary saying Sajwani "contributed" $1-5M to Trump's 2016 campaign appears garbled (foreign nationals cannot donate) and is not used.

- Claimed: Damac's 2025 payments are an estimate of $11.3-12.5M. The record shows: four exact fee lines totalling $12,589,884 (correction of 16 Nov 2026).

## Denials and other side

- Damac did not respond to CNBC. No Damac comment on CREW's reports was found.
- CNBC found no evidence that the payments influenced the permitting order, which is general policy.
- In 2017 Trump said he declined Damac's offer to avoid conflicts. Hope Hicks said the two did not discuss business at the 2016 party.
- The White House says there are no conflicts and that the business is run by Trump's children (C70).
- Feasibility doubts were raised early (E&E News on grid limits; Bloomberg via Fortune, "Trump's $13 billion man in Dubai draws skeptics").
- Background only: Sajwani's 2011 in-absentia conviction in Egypt, which he called politically motivated (BuzzFeed, 2017). It is unrelated to any Trump decision.

## Not connected in the coverage we found

These links come from our own cross-referencing. No outlet asserts them.

- Damac's Edgerton ($860M) and Canton projects would meet EO 14318's $500M threshold for FAST-41 and Commerce support. *Inferred.* No application or designation was found.
- The MANTRA $1B deal was signed two days after the Mar-a-Lago announcement. *Inferred, timing only.* No Trump link to MANTRA was found.
- The Oval Office visit (22 Apr 2025) was followed the next day by Damac's release praising Trump's coming visit, and Sajwani then attended the Abu Dhabi forum in May. The 2025 Abu Dhabi licences followed in the same year. *Inferred sequence.* No source ties the licences to these meetings.
- The Canton site's street address listed by datacenterHawk is "1616 Trump Avenue SE". *Coincidence.* This is an existing Canton street name, and nothing suggests a connection.
- Trump publicly declined Sajwani's new deals in 2017, and the family took two new Damac licences in 2025. *Our observation.* Both facts are documented; the contrast is ours.

## What this does and does not show

It shows a Trump licensee with a decade-long personal relationship with the family. That licensee paid about $10M in 2025 for licences with no visible project, had repeated access to the president, and is now building US data centres that fit the profile of the administration's fast-track policy. It does not show that any federal agency has acted on a Damac site, that the fees bought anything, or that Sajwani has any stake in the family's crypto ventures.

Result: **upheld** (verdict and confidence unchanged).

- **Abu Dhabi fees.** Upheld: $5M to each entity (CREW, from the OGE disclosure). The summary now repeats CREW's "appear to be related" wording. No Damac or Trump Organization Abu Dhabi launch was found through Nov 2026. The Trump-branded Gulf launches we found are Dar Global's.
- **US sites.** Upheld: ~94 acres in SE Canton (Dec 2025) and the $860M Edgerton conversion (3-2 commission denial, later go-ahead). Neither is operating.
- **Federal approvals.** None found. The same-day formation of DT Marks Al Raha Beach remains single-source (CREW), and its relationship label now says so.
