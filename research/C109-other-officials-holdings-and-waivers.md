# C109: Other senior officials: waivers, late divestitures and outside interests

**Verdict:** mostly-accurate (confidence: medium)

**Question:** Which other senior officials received ethics waivers or divested late, and what happened to the offices that oversee them?

## Summary

Filings and official letters document:

- **A few published conflict-of-interest waivers.** The White House posts six waivers under 18 U.S.C. 208(b)(1). They cover Kennedy (an interim waiver for family-office funds), Wright (for a conference appearance while divesting), Sacks (two), Lutnick (one) and one task-force official.
- **Retained interests approved in ethics agreements.** Patel keeps his Elite Depot (Shein parent) stock units. Isaacman keeps his Shift4 stake with less voting control.
- **One missed certification deadline flagged in a complaint.** The Campaign Legal Center told OGE that McMahon had not certified her divestitures more than a month after her 1 June 2025 deadline.
- **A gap in Kennedy's case.** His amended ethics agreement said he would assign his Gardasil fee interest to his adult son. HHS's own letter, as quoted by senators, says he assigned it to the law firm Wisner Baum instead.
- **Musk** served as a special government employee. His disclosure was confidential, and no waiver for him has been published.

Nothing late was found for Bisignano, whose Fiserv sale met its deadline, or for Oz, Hegseth or Boulos.

The oversight offices changed sharply:

- **OGE:** the director was removed in Feb 2025. Since then the office has been run by acting heads who held other jobs, or has had no head at all.
- **Inspectors general:** a court found the Jan 2025 removals unlawful but did not reinstate anyone.
- **Ethics pledge:** the Biden appointee pledge was revoked and not replaced.

**No authority has found that any official named here violated 18 U.S.C. 208 or an ethics agreement.**

**Evidence rule applied:** holding an asset is not a violation. A late transaction report is a reporting lapse with a $200 fee, not a late divestiture. An allegation without a finding is marked unverified. This check builds on C65 (Patel, Boulos), C68 (Musk) and C103 (Hegseth, OGE). The waivers for Lutnick (C104) and Sacks (C106) are only listed here.

## Sub-claims

### 1. Musk: confirmed (status); no finding
As a special government employee (SGE), limited to 130 days in any 365, Musk was subject to 18 U.S.C. 208. That law bars an official from working on particular matters that affect his own financial interests unless he has a 208(b) waiver or an exemption.

- **Disclosure:** a White House official said in Feb 2025 that Musk would file a confidential report (AP, excerpt).
- **Waiver:** none is on the White House waiver list (read in full).
- **Letters:** Sen. Schiff wrote to OGE Director Huitema on 10 Feb 2025, the day Huitema was removed, asking whether a waiver existed (read in full). House Oversight Democrats asked White House Counsel Warrington on 1 May 2025 for the disclosure and any 208(b) determination (read in full).
- **Exposure:** the PSI minority staff memo counted 65 actions by 11 agencies against his companies. It estimated at least $2.37B of potential liability across 40 of them (excerpt; a staff estimate, not a finding).
- **Departure:** Musk announced the end of his SGE term on 28 May 2025.
- **Requests and findings:** CLC asked OGE to review DOGE staff's disclosures (read in full). No OGE, inspector-general or court finding was found.

### 2. Patel: confirmed; no finding
His ethics agreement (read in full) sets out the following:

- **Elite Depot:** he keeps his Elite Depot stock units, which vest quarterly to 1 Nov 2025. Instead of divesting, he recuses: the Justice Department judged that matters involving the company were "remote". That is neither a waiver nor a late divestiture.
- **Qatar:** after consulting for its embassy until Nov 2024, he has a one-year covered relationship with Qatar. The agreement says a written authorization to take part in Qatar matters "will" be granted; whether it was granted is not public.
- **Trump Media:** he agreed to resign from the board.
- **Stock sales:** 19 stocks are to be sold within 90 days.

Schiff and colleagues objected to the retained Shein-linked stake on 12 Feb 2025. No finding was found.

### 3. Kennedy: mostly-accurate (waiver and discrepancy documented; no finding)
- **Waiver:** a limited, interim waiver dated 18 Feb 2025 (read in full) let him keep four Park Holdings Group funds, worth under $380K in total, until they could be divested in April 2025.
- **Fee interest:** his 21 Jan 2025 agreement kept a 10% contingency-fee interest in cases he had referred to Wisner Baum. The 1 Feb amendment said he would assign it "irrevocably" to his adult son. According to the senators' 24 Sept 2026 letter (excerpt), HHS wrote on 25 Aug 2026 that he assigned it to Wisner Baum on 11 Feb 2025. His 12 May 2025 compliance certification did not mention this.
- **Responses:** HHS says it confirmed his compliance in May 2025. Kennedy says he gave up the interest before taking office.
- **Finding:** no OGE determination was found.

### 4. Oz: unverified
His divestiture pledges are documented (NPR and Fierce, excerpts). No compliance record, letter or finding on late divestiture was located.

### 5. Hegseth: unverified
The broker approach is reported by one outlet and denied by the Pentagon. It is covered in C103. No waiver or finding was found.

### 6. Wright: confirmed
The waiver of 7 Mar 2025 (pages 1-2 read) covers a CERAWeek appearance while he was still divesting. It records:

- an OGE certificate of divestiture issued on 18 Feb 2025;
- 2,582,967 Liberty Energy shares, to be sold on a daily schedule ending 21 Mar 2025;
- CAZ funds to be exited by 1 Apr 2025.

All of this falls within 90 days of his confirmation on 3 Feb 2025.

### 7. Burgum: partly-accurate
- **Certification:** reportedly filed (excerpt).
- **Late reports:** his transaction reports (278-T) self-certify 106 of 153 transactions as late. This is late reporting, not late divestiture.
- **Department matter:** Interior appointee Karen Budd-Falen's redacted ethics records drew calls for an inspector-general inquiry (High Country News, excerpt).

### 8. McMahon: mostly-accurate (complaint documented; no finding)
CLC's complaint of 9 July 2025 (read in part) notes the following:

- her agreement listed 81 holdings to sell by 1 June 2025, 78 of them bonds;
- no compliance certification or transaction report was public more than a month after that deadline.

Her later 278-T filings run to 24 June 2025, and 153 of 156 transactions are self-certified as late. Whether the post-deadline sales were assets from her ethics agreement is not shown. No response from OGE or McMahon was found.

### 9. Loeffler: partly-accurate
- **Newsmax:** Fortune reported that she held Newsmax parent stock that was not on her divestiture list.
- **Groq:** NOTUS (read in full) reported a $3M investment in Groq in Oct 2025, weeks before Groq's $20B licensing deal with Nvidia.
- **ICE:** a filing records a $5M-$25M sale of ICE stock on 19 Nov 2025 (as reported).
- **Response:** the SBA says her portfolio is managed independently.
- **Waivers and findings:** none found.

### 10. Bisignano: false (as "late")
He sold Fiserv between 16 May and 1 July 2025, inside his 120-day window. The senators' question is about timing: the stock fell later in 2025. The agency says he complied with his agreement.

### 11. Isaacman: confirmed (retained stake)
- **Agreement:** dated 17 Nov 2025 (relevant sections read). He keeps his Shift4 stake, his voting control is reduced to about 25%, and he recuses from matters affecting Shift4.
- **Purchases:** Sludge reported purchases in May 2026.
- **Late reports:** 128 of 147 transactions are self-certified as late.
- **Finding:** none.

### 12. Boulos: unverified
No public financial disclosure, ethics agreement, waiver or finding was located.

### 13. Ethics pledge and waiver publication: mostly-accurate
- **Pledge:** EO 13989 was revoked on 20 Jan 2025, and no replacement pledge appeared in the reporting we found or in later searches.
- **Waivers:** the White House disclosures page lists six 208(b)(1) waivers.
- **Agency waivers:** these are filed with OGE; that collection was not searchable here.

### 14. OGE leadership: confirmed
- **Removal:** Huitema was removed on 10 Feb 2025 (OGE notice, read in full).
- **Acting heads:** Collins (Feb 2025), then Greer (about Apr to Sept 2025), then Ueland (from about Aug or Sept 2025). Senators say Ueland's service ended in early Dec 2025.
- **Vacancy:** the 300-day acting limit expired, and on 18 May 2026 senators said OGE had no head even in an acting capacity.
- **Since June 2026:** Sonderling was named acting director and Chamberlain was nominated, both on 23 June 2026 (read in full). No confirmation vote was found.

### 15. Inspectors general: confirmed
- **Removals:** about 17 inspectors general were removed on 24 Jan 2025 without the notice to Congress and reasons that the IG Act requires.
- **Ruling:** in Storch v. Hegseth (24 Sept 2025), Judge Reyes found the President had almost certainly violated the law but denied reinstatement. The case was then stayed pending Trump v. Slaughter, which the Supreme Court decided on 29 June 2026. No later ruling in Storch was found.
- **Since then:** Cato (Apr 2026) counts at least 21 inspectors general removed or replaced and 28 of 72 posts vacant. OMB withheld FY2026 funding from the inspectors general's council (CIGIE) in Oct 2025, then partly reversed that decision.

### 16. GAO and court findings on ethics: unverified
- **GAO:** its findings concern the Impoundment Control Act, not officials' holdings. On 16 Nov 2026 the President nominated Ueland to lead GAO (NOTUS, read in full).
- **Courts:** the only court finding in scope is the inspector-general ruling above.

### 17. Any violation finding: unverified
None was found for any official named here.

## Corrections

- **C103** says CREW's 18 May 2026 letter went to "OGE Acting Director Eric Ueland". CREW did address it that way, but senators wrote on the same day that his acting service had ended in early Dec 2025. Sonderling became acting director on 23 June 2026.
- **C65 (Patel)** can add from the ethics agreement (read in full): the retained Elite Depot stock units, the Qatar covered relationship and the Trump Media resignation. The verdict does not change.
- **C68 (Musk)** can add that no waiver for Musk has been published and that his disclosure was confidential.
- **C86:** no overlap; no correction.
- **Bisignano:** the claim that he divested late is wrong. He sold inside the 120-day window.
- **Isaacman:** the claim that he divested Shift4 is wrong. He was allowed to keep the stake.

## Denials and context

- **Responses:** HHS (Kennedy), the SBA (Loeffler), SSA (Bisignano), the Pentagon (Hegseth) and the White House (Musk) say each official complied or deny the reports; details are in the table.
- **Late-report counts:** these are officials' own certifications, tallied by a third-party tracker (Open Cabinet).
- **Advocacy figures:** the PSI estimate and the inspector-general vacancy counts come from partisan staff or advocacy groups.
- **No finding is not exoneration:** OGE was led by acting heads or had no head for most of the term.
