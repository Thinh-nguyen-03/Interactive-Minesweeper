# C59: Foreign Trump Organization deals timed with tariff negotiations

**Verdict: partly-accurate (confidence: medium)**

This is a follow-up lead from C31 (Gulf deals) and C45 (Kushner's Albania and Serbia projects).

## Bottom line

The Trump Organization really does have a large set of Trump-branded projects outside the Gulf: in Vietnam, Indonesia, India, Serbia, Georgia, the Maldives and Romania. CREW counts about two dozen foreign projects in the second term; its tracker has read 21, 24 and 25 at different times.

**Vietnam** is the strong case for "deals timed with tariff talks". After Trump threatened Vietnam with a 46% tariff in April 2025, Hanoi fast-tracked the $1.5B Hung Yen project. The prime minister broke ground alongside Eric Trump, and the NYT reported that legal steps were skipped. Hanoi then got a 20% rate.

Elsewhere the framing overreaches:

- **Indonesia** halted the MNC Lido project in February 2025 rather than fast-tracking it.
- **India's** tariffs rose to 50% while Trump-branded towers kept launching there.
- **Georgia, Romania and the Maldives** show favour-seeking or ordinary licensing, not tariff outcomes.

No direct quid pro quo is documented anywhere. Every counterparty is a private developer, so the January 2025 ethics policy permits these deals on paper. That policy does not reach state approvals, state land or special laws that sit behind the private partner.

## Findings by sub-claim

1. **CREW count of ~24 foreign projects: confirmed.**
   - CREW's tracker title has moved from 21 to 24 to 25 projects. It says 13 were announced after Trump became a candidate in 2022.
   - CREW says foreign revenue exceeded $117M in 2025, against at least $149M over the whole first term.
   - The FT, as reported in search results, puts foreign licensing and management revenue at $63M in 2025, up 40%.
2. **Vietnam golf project fast-tracked during tariff talks: confirmed.**
   - PM Pham Minh Chinh said Eric Trump's visit "motivated us to expedite this project".
   - The NYT (25 May 2025, read via syndication) reported that legal experts said at least half a dozen required steps were skipped. It also published an official letter saying the project had "special attention from the Trump administration and President Donald Trump personally".
   - Reuters described Vietnam as having "fast-tracked approvals as it negotiated a crucial trade deal".
   - Farmers were offered about $12-30/m2. Eviction notices had gone out by June 2026, and the first phase is due around APEC 2027.
3. **Vietnam tariff outcome: partly-accurate.** The rate fell from a threatened 46% to 20%. The link to the golf project is temporal, and nothing shows the project was part of the negotiation. The deal was not finalised as of June 2026, and the US later opened trade investigations into Vietnam.
4. **HCMC tower: partly-accurate.** It is still only a proposal. CREW reports five new Trump trademark applications in Vietnam filed after the tariff deal.
5. **Indonesia: misleading as briefed.**
   - These are old deals, and the project was halted rather than advanced.
   - A lift of the suspension is reported only by Golf Industry Asia (Aug 2026), uncorroborated.
   - The real story is the hot-mic exchange. Prabowo asked Trump for a meeting with Eric and mentioned "Hary", and Trump said he would have Eric call. Eric says they have not met.
6. **India: mostly-accurate.** There are many licensed towers, all with private partners. The tariff path (up to 50%) contradicts a favour narrative.
7. **Serbia: confirmed (see C45).** It collapsed in December 2025 amid a forgery indictment.
8. **Georgia: mostly-accurate.**
   - The tower's land came from a fund linked to the Ivanishvili family. This rests on the Guardian and the WSJ, both read second-hand.
   - Georgia's ruling party presents the tower as a diplomatic reset.
   - No US concession to Georgia was found.
9. **Maldives: confirmed.** This is a Dar Global deal with a tokenisation and SEC angle.
10. **Romania: partly-accurate.** The projects were announced but are stalled or delayed.
11. **Fees: unverified** per project. Only aggregate figures are available.
12. **The ethics pledge permits these deals: confirmed.** The 10 Jan 2025 policy bars only deals directly with foreign governments. It does not address government approvals, state land or special laws behind a private partner, nor family meetings with heads of government.

## Corrections

- The Hung Yen deal was signed in October 2024, before the election and before the tariffs. What happened during the tariff talks was its approval and groundbreaking.
- The HCMC tower has not been approved or started.
- The Indonesian resorts predate the second term and were halted in February 2025.
- India got higher tariffs (50%) before the February 2026 cut to 18%, which was tied to oil rather than property.

## Denials and context

- The White House says "all of the president's trade discussions are totally unrelated to the Trump Organization". It also says neither the president nor his family engages in conflicts of interest.
- The Trump Organization says it is "fully compliant with all applicable ethics and conflicts of interest laws". It says it only licenses its brand, and that it is not involved in investment or compensation in Vietnam.
- Ivanishvili's lawyer says most of the racecourse land was donated to the state for a park.
- Vietnam's 20% rate is comparable to Indonesia's 19%, and Indonesia's deal came after its Trump project was halted. That cuts against reading tariff outcomes as rewards, though I did not run a full counterfactual.
- **Research limits.** The pages of CREW, Asia Sentinel, the WSJ, the NYT and AP could not be fetched (hosts blocked), so several findings rest on syndications and search summaries. The shared web-search budget ran out before I could check the India fee figures and the AP piece of 13 Apr 2026.
