# C72: Pakistan: World Liberty deal, Trump-tied lobbyists, minerals and tariffs

**Verdict: mostly-accurate (confidence: medium).** Every dated step in the claim is documented. The second layer turns up three further links:
- a Seiden Law mandate that was explicitly about minerals;
- Rep. Ronny Jackson, Trump's former White House physician, named in Javelin and Conscience Point filings (single-source);
- a 14 May 2025 minerals pitch by Squire Patton Boggs.

**What is not substantiated:** causation. No source says the lobbyists arranged the WLF deal, Munir's lunch, the 19% tariff or the Powerus order.

**Correction:** Seiden Law was hired *before* the Pahalgam attack, not after it.

**Method note:** This report builds on C64 (item 9: lobbyists and the WLF letter of intent), C51 (Powerus) and C67. It does not repeat them. The original pages at Al Jazeera, Nukta, Global Witness, Daily Times and the FARA eFile were not read directly, so the findings rest on search-result excerpts from Dawn, Arab News/Reuters, Business Recorder, ThePrint, Business Today, Cointelegraph, Bloomberg (via ThePrint), Mother Jones, Fortune, SCMP and others. About 25 searches were used.

## Connections (sub-claims)

### 1. Lobbying hires and the WLF letter of intent (confirmed; documented)
- **Before the attack:** Seiden signed on 8 Apr 2025, two weeks before Pahalgam.
- **After the attack:** Javelin registered on 24-25 Apr, and WLF signed its letter of intent on 26 Apr.
- **Munir's presence:** Arab News and Dawn say the WLF delegation met Munir, Sharif and Dar. Business Today, citing Dawn, says Munir sat in on the closed-door session.

### 2. Seiden's mandate was minerals, and it was routed to Trump's ex-bodyguard (confirmed; documented)
- **The contract:** Global Witness, working from the FARA filings, says the contract was to "promote the exploration and sale of" rare earths and critical minerals, and was outsourced to Javelin.
- **Named figures:** Robert Seiden and George Sorial are named as Trump-circle figures.
- **Termination:** An unofficial compilation says Javelin's engagement ended on 8 Oct 2025, after $200K in total payments. This is single-source.

### 3. Ronny Jackson (mostly-accurate; single-source)
The Economic Times reported on filings by Javelin and Conscience Point Consulting; Business Today and Bizz Buzz relayed it. The reported contacts are:
- a 1 May 2025 in-person meeting with Pakistani officials;
- a 28 May call followed by an email with a draft MoU;
- Javelin contacting Jackson's office during the conflict (ThePrint).

We could not read the filings ourselves and found no comment from Jackson. A claimed $4,000 contribution to Jackson on 28 Oct 2025 comes from an unofficial site and is not verified.

### 4. Squire Patton Boggs' wartime minerals pitch (confirmed; documented as reported)
- **During the conflict:** the firm sent 50+ emails between 7 and 10 May.
- **After the ceasefire:** on 14 May it emailed a US diplomat proposing a trade partnership with minerals at its centre.
- **Other embassy lobbying:** the embassy also hired Ervin Graves Strategy Group. The first contract (Oct 2025, $25K/month) was partly over the FATF grey list. A two-year, ~$1.2M contract followed (effective 2026-05-01), seeking a resumed defence dialogue, with a section on minerals cooperation.

### 5. Ceasefire, lunch and Nobel nomination (confirmed; documented)
- **Credit for the ceasefire:** India rejects any US mediation.
- **Arranging the lunch:** Dawn's insiders say it was secured by "unconventional means" but name no one.

### 6. Checkmate via Greystone (partly-accurate; corrected in audit)
- **Contract:** FARA No. 7534. Greystone paid $100K on 2025-05-14 for a 30-day Phase I, with a $200K Phase II option. The filings describe general government-relations work (O'Dwyer's).
- **Correction:** the two-year, ~$1.2M defence-and-minerals contract that Daily Times/AFP describe is the embassy's contract with **Ervin Graves Strategy Group** (effective 2026-05-01, $50K/month). It is not Checkmate's.
- **Who pays:** Greystone's owners are not named in any coverage we found.

### 7. Trade deal, oil and tariff (confirmed; documented)
No Trump-family or WLF party appears in these steps.

### 8. Minerals: USSM, the Oval Office box, Pasni and Reko Diq (confirmed; documented)
- **USSM:** the MoU is non-binding, and the first shipment went out in October 2025.
- **Pasni:** officials denied the port pitch.
- **Reko Diq:** US EXIM is financing $1.25B of Barrick's project.
- **Family stake:** we found no Trump-family stake in USSM, Reko Diq or Pasni.

### 9. Bin Saqib's regulator lets in Binance and HTX (confirmed; documented)
This puts three WLF-linked figures on one Pakistani regulatory track:
- Bin Saqib, the former WLF adviser who now runs the regulator;
- CZ, a Pakistan Crypto Council adviser who was pardoned in Oct 2025;
- Justin Sun, an adviser to HTX.

Bin Saqib says the NOCs are "not blanket approval".

### 10. The January 2026 USD1 MoU (confirmed; documented)
- **Signatories:** Zach Witkoff signed as CEO of SC Financial, which co-owns the USD1 brand. Sharif and Munir witnessed.
- **Status:** the MoU is non-binding and covers study only.

### 11. Iran broker role tied to the WLF courtship (partly-accurate)
- **Documented:** the talks themselves.
- **Framing only:** Bloomberg's account of the courtship is framing, not proof. Mother Jones says it is "not clear" that the WLF relationship is the reason for Pakistan's role.

### 12. The Powerus order (confirmed; documented)
- **Timing:** the order came the day after Velicovich met Munir.
- **What it is:** the value is undisclosed, and ISPR confirmed only the meeting.
- **India:** less than three months earlier, Powerus had granted an Indian maker an exclusive licence.
- **Response:** a spokesperson says the president has no involvement.

### 13. Al Jazeera's "$500m for Trump" (false as a Pakistan payment)
The figure is WLF's 2025 token-sale revenue. It is not Pakistani money, it is not the USSM MoU, and it is not Aryam's $500M stake (C06).

## Corrections
- **Seiden's timing:** Seiden predates Pahalgam. Only Javelin's registration and the WLF letter of intent fall days after the attack.
- **Javelin and Checkmate:** Javelin was a subcontractor. Checkmate's counterparty is Greystone, not the Pakistani government.
- **Powerus:** the order was a limited procurement order of undisclosed value. The merger had not yet closed when it was placed.
- **Ronny Jackson:** he is a Representative, not a Senator.
- **"$500m":** there are three separate $500M figures, and they must not be conflated.

## Denials and context
- **India:** denies that the US mediated the ceasefire.
- **Pasni:** US and Pakistani officials deny the port proposal.
- **Pakistani officials:** stress that the MoUs and NOCs are non-binding or preliminary.
- **Powerus and the Trump sons:** the sons say they are passive investors, and Powerus says the president is not involved.
- **WLF:** says Bin Saqib was unpaid.
- **Strategic drivers:** Pakistan also has non-transactional reasons for its pivot (Iran access, hedging against China, minerals).
- **Legal status:** FARA disclosures are not findings of wrongdoing, and we found no DOJ action.

## Not connected in the coverage we found
- **Javelin's registration (25 Apr) and the WLF letter of intent (26 Apr):** *inferred* from timing. No source says the lobbyists brokered the letter.
- **Lobbyists (Seiden, Javelin, Checkmate, Jackson) and the 18 Jun Munir lunch:** *inferred*. Dawn cites "unconventional means" without names.
- **Jackson's draft MoU (May 2025) and the USSM-FWO MoU (Sep 2025):** *single-source* for the reported link. Business Today's headline calls Jackson a "key link" in the $500M deal. That the two documents are the same text is *inferred*.
- **WLF/USD1 courtship and the 19% tariff rate or the EXIM Reko Diq loan:** *inferred* (co-occurrence only).
- **The Trump sons' Powerus stake and the Trump-Munir relationship, or the MoD order:** *inferred*. No source says the sons or officials were involved.
- **Checkmate's work for both Binance and Pakistan, and CZ's Pakistan adviser role:** *inferred* (C64).
- **Who owns Greystone:** unknown, not inferred.
- **The $4,000 contribution to Jackson:** unverified, and discarded.
