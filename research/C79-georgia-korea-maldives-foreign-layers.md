# C79: Lesser-known foreign layers: Tbilisi, South Korea, Maldives tokens, Vietnam trademarks

**Verdict: mostly-accurate (confidence: medium)**

All five leads from C59 turned out to be real and are documented. The South Korean "aggregator lead" is real: the New York Times reported it in July 2026, and senators' letters and the company's own announcement confirm it. Two new layers came out of this check. First, the Maldives tokenisation is now run by **World Liberty Financial**, the Trump family's crypto company, so the family is on both sides of the project: brand licensor and token platform. Second, Eric Trump visited the Korean golf site in February 2026, **five months before** the payment became public. What the record does *not* show is any US decision that favours these counterparties. The Korean firm's Commerce case moved against it (a July 2026 preliminary ruling could roughly quadruple its duty). US sanctions on Ivanishvili are still in force. Vietnam got a new 12.5% Section 301 duty in July 2026. The SEC never "approved" the Maldives tokens: the offering was restructured into an unregistered Reg D 506(c)/Reg S private placement for accredited investors only.

Method note: the Guardian, WSJ, NYT, Korea JoongAng Daily, Asia Economy, Senate Finance and govinfo pages were not read directly. Findings below rest on search-result text from several outlets, and each one is attributed.

## What C59/C70 already established (not repeated)

- C59 confirmed these projects exist: Trump Tower Tbilisi, the land bought from the Ivanishvili-linked Cartu fund, the Maldives resort with Dar Global and the initial plan to sell tokens to US retail buyers, CREW's five Vietnam trademark filings, and Hung Yen evictions by June 2026.
- C70 covered the Kinh Bac $5M licence fee and the 46%→20% tariff cut, and Dar Global's roughly $21.9M a year in fees.

## Connections found (each with evidence grade)

### 1. Base Group (South Korea) paid Trump's company $2M while its subsidiary fought a Commerce trade case. Evidence: **documented**. Verdict: confirmed
- **Payment.** Trump's 2026 financial disclosure lists a $2M "nonrefundable development fee" tied to a "letter of intent" from Base Group. Base Group is the parent and lead investor of aluminium producer **Korea Aluminium**. The NYT first reported it on 14 July 2026. Election Law Blog, Political Wire, Boston Globe, Korea JoongAng Daily and dnyuz carried it.
- **Trade case.** Korea Aluminium was named in Commerce's 2022-23 inquiry into whether Korean aluminium foil made from Chinese inputs evades the China AD/CVD orders. Commerce found circumvention on a countrywide basis in its final determination of 27 Nov 2023. Korean press (via search) reports that a **13 July 2026 preliminary Commerce ruling** could raise the rate on Korea Aluminium's foil to **105.8%**, roughly four times the prior rate. That number matches the China-wide dumping margin, which suggests Commerce applied the China rate. This is our reading, not something a source states. I found no final ruling as of Nov 2026.
- **Explanation.** Base Group and the Trump Organization say the money relates to an **August 2025** letter of intent to manage a golf course. On **13 Aug 2026** they announced **Trump International Golf Club Korea** in Hakam-dong, Hanam, Gyeonggi, billed as the first Trump golf project in Northeast Asia, with Eric Trump quoted. Base Group denies breaking US trade rules. The NYT found **no evidence** that Trump or his family intervened with officials.
- **Oversight.** Senators Warren, Blumenthal and Andy Kim wrote to Base Group chairman **Kim Sung-jip** on 6 Aug 2026. They asked for answers by 21 Aug and cited the federal bribery statute. Ranking Member Wyden wrote to Commerce Secretary Lutnick asking whether Commerce officials knew of the payment.
- **What it shows:** a foreign party paid a Trump business during an open US trade proceeding against its subsidiary. **What it does not show:** any favourable decision. The only decision found so far went against the company.

### 2. Eric Trump visited Hanam with Base Group and the Hanam mayor on 10-11 Feb 2026. Evidence: **documented** (several Korean outlets, upgraded in audit: Kyeongin Ilbo, Hankyung, Korea Times). No source confirms that the plot he toured is the announced golf site. Verdict: confirmed
This puts a family member on site months before the disclosure made the payment public. The timeline runs: LOI Aug 2025, payment in 2025, site visit Feb 2026, preliminary ruling 13 Jul 2026, NYT 14 Jul 2026, public announcement 13 Aug 2026.

### 3. Trump Tower Tbilisi: land tied to the sanctioned Ivanishvili family and partners close to his party. Evidence: **documented**. Verdict: confirmed
- **Land.** The site is the former Soviet hippodrome. Official registry records cited by the Guardian (May 2026) and Georgian outlet BM.ge show it still registered to the **International Charity Fund Cartu**. Cartu is about 35% owned by **Uta Ivanishvili**, Bidzina's son, through Cartu Group JSC. A 2023 agreement sells it to **Central Park Avenue LLC**, a company linked to Wissol/INVIA and Biograpi Living, and the transfer is not complete. The WSJ (13 Sep 2026, via JAMnews and Charter97) says developers bought it from the family fund in 2023. Ivanishvili's lawyer says much of the family's 511,880 m² was donated to the state for a park.
- **Partners.** The Daily Beast (May 2026) reports that **Archi Group**'s founder and chairman **Ilia Tsulaia** is a former Georgian Dream MP. Archi co-founder **Meni Benish** is Georgia's honorary consul in Israel (Ynet). The **Sapir Organization** (Alex Sapir) is co-developer; per the Daily Beast, the FBI investigated its late founder Tamir Sapir in 1998 but never charged him. Biograpi Living's co-owner says it is "neither a shareholder nor a participant". Partner lists differ across releases.
- **Dates.** Announced 19 Apr 2026 (Imedi, Business Wire). The WSJ feature ran on 13 Sep 2026. The official launch on 15 Sep 2026 was attended by **PM Irakli Kobakhidze**, who called it a $2B, 600,000 m² development of 62 storeys and 282 m. Eric Trump cancelled his trip and appeared by video.
- **Sanctions.** Treasury sanctioned Bidzina Ivanishvili on 27 Dec 2024. I found no delisting as of Nov 2026.

### 4. US-Georgia policy in the same window: a "reset" in talks, a stalled sanctions bill, no concession. Evidence: **documented** for each event; any link to the tower is **inferred**. Verdict: partly-accurate
- The State Department suspended the strategic partnership in Nov-Dec 2024. In Feb-May 2026 the US and Georgia began talks on a "reset": a technical team, a meeting with DAS Sonata Coulter, and a **State Department delegation in Tbilisi on 25-29 May 2026** that said the Trump administration was ready for a "constructive, forward-looking relationship". The Guardian's land story ran in the same week. That timing is our observation and no source links the two.
- The **MEGOBARI Act** passed the House 349-42 on 6 May 2025. It was reintroduced in the Senate by Risch and Shaheen and has stalled there. One opinion piece (OC Media) says Sen. Markwayne Mullin is withholding unanimous consent. That is **single-source** and an opinion.
- Reuters (Gram Slattery) reported that **Steve Nicandros**, a Republican donor and co-founder of Frontera (now TXN Energy) with oil interests in Georgia, lobbied Congress against MEGOBARI. Georgia's ambassador says the government has no US lobbying affiliations. I found no tie between Nicandros and the tower project.
- **Not found:** any lifting of sanctions, a restored partnership charter, or other concrete US concession. As of June 2026, bne IntelliNews wrote that the "promised US reset keeps failing to arrive". Commentators (Veridica, Jamestown) describe the tower as Georgian Dream courting Trump through business. That is analysis, not evidence of a deal.

### 5. Maldives: the family's own crypto firm became the token platform, through a private placement, not SEC approval. Evidence: **documented** (company press releases). Verdict: confirmed, with a correction
- Nov 2025 (Reuters via The Block and The Real Deal): Dar Global planned to tokenise up to 70% of the ~$300M project for **US retail investors** and was "in discussions" with the SEC.
- **18 Feb 2026:** **World Liberty Financial** announced it would tokenise **loan revenue interests** in the Trump International Hotel & Resort Maldives with **Securitize** (a registered broker-dealer, transfer agent and ATS operator) and DarGlobal (Business Wire, Dar Global press page, Crowdfund Insider). The structure is a **Reg D 506(c)** sale to verified accredited investors plus **Reg S** offshore. It is unregistered and needs no SEC approval. Holders get a fixed yield and loan revenue, not equity. The Trump Organization says it does not issue or promote the tokens and that the name is licensed. Eric Trump called it the "first of many" tokenisation projects. Completion has moved to about 2030.
- **The layer:** the Trump family earns from the Trump Organization's licence with Dar Global (C31) **and** owns a large stake in WLF (C04/C42), which now runs the financing tokens. The family therefore sits on both sides of one foreign project. I found no amount raised and no closing date.
- **US decision:** none specific. The general SEC stance on tokenisation under Chairman Atkins is covered in C23/C11. An aggregator mentions a CFIUS probe tied to the Abu Dhabi stake in WLF. That is thin and was not verified, so it is not included as a finding.

### 6. Trump trademarks in Vietnam. Evidence: **documented** (CREW, from international trademark databases). Verdict: confirmed
**DTTM Operations LLC** filed five applications on **27 Nov 2025**, and they were published about two months later. They cover marks such as Trump International Hung Yen, Trump Residences Vietnam and Trump International Golf Club Vietnam, in classes ranging from real estate to clothing. The filings came about a month after the 26 Oct 2025 framework joint statement and about five months after the 2 Jul 2025 tariff announcement. Granting the marks is a decision for Vietnam's IP Office, so it is a foreign-government benefit to watch under the Emoluments Clause. In the first term, CREW counted 72 trademarks from 11 foreign governments. I found no record of whether the 2025 marks were granted.

### 7. Hung Yen farmers and the Vietnam trade picture since. Evidence: **documented**. Verdict: confirmed
- Reuters (Aug 2025) reported offers of about $12/m² plus rice. One farmer was offered $3,200 for 200 m². The state pays compensation but developers fund it. SCMP reported that hundreds of farmers petitioned for at least double. Bloomberg (via The Star, 26 Jun 2026) reported enforcement against holdouts from **26 Jun 2026**. Golf Industry Asia and others report graves being exhumed (single-source trade press). The Trump Organization says it is not responsible for compensation.
- **US decisions since:** Vietnam has still not signed the trade agreement. Hanoi says it sent the final text in Oct 2026. A new **Section 301 duty of an additional 12.5%** on Vietnamese goods took effect on **24 Jul 2026**, according to one Vietnamese outlet (VnEconomy), so the 12.5% rate is single-source. The IEEPA tariffs were replaced by a Section 122 surcharge. None of this looks like special favour to Vietnam, which weakens a straightforward quid-pro-quo reading.

## Corrections
- The South Korean payment is not just an aggregator claim. It is NYT reporting backed by the financial disclosure, senators' letters and the company's own confirmation.
- The Maldives tokens were not cleared by the SEC for US retail buyers. The offering became an unregistered 506(c)/Reg S placement for accredited investors only, run by WLF and Securitize.
- The Tbilisi tower is 62 storeys and 282 m in the launch materials, not "70 storeys" as early reports said. The land transfer from Cartu was **not complete** as of mid-2026.
- The "105.8%" Korea Aluminium rate is reported by Korean press citing the NYT. It matches the China-wide margin and is a preliminary rate, not final.

## Denials and other side
- The Trump Organization says the Base Group fee is standard business for a golf LOI. Base Group denies trade violations. The NYT found no intervention.
- The White House says neither the president nor his family engages in conflicts of interest. The Trump Organization says it is only a licensor in Tbilisi and the Maldives.
- Ivanishvili's lawyer says most of the land was donated for a park. Biograpi Living denies participation.
- Georgia's ambassador denies US lobbying affiliations.
