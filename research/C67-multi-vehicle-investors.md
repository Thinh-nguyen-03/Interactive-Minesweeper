# C67: Investors who show up in several Trump-family vehicles

**Verdict: mostly-accurate (confidence: medium).** The connections we found are reasonably well substantiated, but fewer parties are true repeat investors than the brief suggests.

Only a few parties are documented putting money into more than one Trump-family vehicle:

- **Justin Sun** is the clearest case.
- **The Tahnoon bin Zayed network** (Aryam, MGX, StringZ, Lunate) is a second.
- **Web3Port and Aqua 1** look like two buyers but, per the New York Times, trace to one person: Guren "Bobby" Zhou, who is under a UK money-laundering investigation.
- **Crypto.com** is the most frequent *commercial* counterparty, and it is also a top super-PAC donor.

Most other names in the brief appear in a single vehicle: DWF Labs, Oddiyana, Hut 8, DRW, Kraken, GD Culture and the other $TRUMP-treasury small caps. Nothing found shows that any investment caused a government decision.

Method note: we first mined C01-C60 for recurring names, then searched outward. The web-search budget ran out mid-pass. Many outlets could not be fetched directly: NYT, WSJ, Caixin, The Block, Cointelegraph, CryptoSlate and Bitcoin.com. Several findings therefore rest on search-result summaries drawn from multiple outlets.

## Connections in detail

### 1. Justin Sun: four vehicles and a dropped SEC case (documented)

Sun is the only individual documented across WLF, $TRUMP, a Trump-sons-brokered listing and a WLFI treasury company.

- **WLF:** $75M in total ($30M plus $45M via TRON DAO). He was named WLF adviser the day after the first purchase.
- **$TRUMP:** He was the top holder at the dinner. On 2025-07-09 he posted "We are committed to buying $100 million of TRUMP," just before a roughly $474M unlock.
- **Tron Inc.:** Dominari Securities was the exclusive placement agent on the SRM/Tron Inc. deal. Both Trump sons are Dominari advisers and own about 6.7% each. Eric Trump denies any role.
- **ALT5 and WLFI pledge:** A day after WLF froze his tokens, Sun pledged $10M each in ALT5 shares and WLFI.

What followed at the SEC: the case was stayed in February 2025 and dismissed in March 2026, with Rainberry paying $10M.

What this does and does not show:
- The SEC eased other crypto cases with no Trump ties, so the outcome fits a broader policy shift.
- The relationship has since collapsed into lawsuits in both directions.
- Completion of the $100M and $10M pledges is unverified.

### 2. The Tahnoon network: WLF equity, USD1, the trust bank and TikTok (documented, one link single-source)

Four separate Tahnoon-linked entities touch Trump-family or Trump-ally ventures:

- Aryam: 49% of WLF.
- MGX: used USD1 for its $2B Binance deal.
- StringZ: a passive investor in WLTC Holdings, the parent of WLF's trust bank. The OCC record confirms StringZ as an investor but not its owner. The Tahnoon link rests on anonymous WSJ sourcing.
- Lunate: an Affinity investor.

Over the same period the administration made several decisions affecting the network:
- AI-chip access for the UAE and G42.
- Approval of the TikTok divestiture framework, in which MGX took 15% of the new US joint venture at closing on 2026-01-22.
- The OCC's conditional charter for WLF's trust company.

Treating these entities as one investor is our own aggregation by common patron (inferred). No quid pro quo is documented, and WLF, MGX and the White House deny any link (see C06, C07, C57).

### 3. Web3Port and Aqua 1: two "different" buyers, one man (documented purchases; single-source identification)

How the story built up:

1. **January 2025:** Web3Port, a Hong Kong-linked market maker that had faced manipulation accusations, announced a WLFI purchase. It was reported as $10M; Arkham, as cited by the NYT, traced about $20M.
2. **June 2025:** Aqua 1 Foundation, presented as a UAE fund with almost no history, bought $100M of WLFI.
3. **July 2025:** Jacob Silverman and BeInCrypto alleged shared infrastructure between the two. Aqua 1 and co-founder "Dave Lee" denied any connection.
4. **August 2026:** The NYT reported the following (we read it only through The Block, Cointelegraph, Unchained, AML Intelligence and IBTimes):
   - A BVI Web3Port entity renamed itself **Aqua 1 GP Limited** two weeks before the $100M announcement.
   - The figure behind the purchase is **Guren "Bobby" Zhou**. He was arrested in Britain in 2021 on suspicion of money laundering, has not been charged, and the investigation remains active.
   - Up to **$75M** went to a company controlled by Trump and his sons, and the Witkoff family also benefited.
5. **August 2026:** Caixin calls Zhou a "blacklisted Chinese businessman." We could not read the article, so the basis for that description is unverified.

The source of the funds is unknown. We found no US government review of the purchase.

What this does and does not show:
- **What it does show:** this is the most significant under-reported repeat buyer. Money that reads as Emirati in the announcements appears to be Chinese-controlled.
- **What it does not show:** that WLF knew who was behind the purchase.

### 4. Crypto.com: the most frequent counterparty (documented)

Crypto.com has been TMTG's partner on:
- the Truth.Fi ETFs;
- custody of TMTG's bitcoin treasury;
- the $6.4B CRO treasury venture, including an agreed $50M purchase of TMTG stock (terminated in August 2026);
- Truth Predict;
- the planned shareholder token.

It also gave about $35M to MAGA Inc. Regulatory outcomes that followed:
- The SEC closed its probe on 2025-03-27, three days after the ETF tie-up.
- The CFTC backed Crypto.com's legal position in an amicus brief.
- The OCC granted conditional approval for its trust bank.

Most of these are commercial or services ties rather than equity in a family entity. Crypto.com denies any link (see C22, C41, C43).

### 5. Jump Trading: on both the WLF side and the TMTG side (inferred / single-source)

- **WLF side:** on-chain trackers reported large WLFI transfers from WLF multisigs to Jump addresses. Analysts infer a market-making role, which neither party has confirmed.
- **TMTG side:** Reuters (anonymous sources, Oct 2026) named Jump as a paying Truth API subscriber. Jump declined to comment.
- **Regulatory history:** Jump's SEC matter, Tai Mo Shan's $123M settlement, predates the term. A 2024 CFTC probe was reported, but we found nothing on how it was resolved.

### 6. Single-vehicle parties (documented)

These parties appear in only one Trump-family vehicle each:

- **DWF Labs:** bought $25M of WLFI and acts as a fee-free USD1 liquidity provider. Watchdogs cite market-manipulation allegations against it.
- **Kraken:** named Asset Manager for ALT5's WLFI treasury. Its SEC case had been dropped earlier, and it received a Fed master account in March 2026.
- **DRW:** bought about $100M of TMTG stock. The SEC case against Cumberland DRW was dismissed.
- **Hut 8:** owns 80% of American Bitcoin.
- **Oddiyana Ventures:** made a WLF investment of undisclosed size (February 2025).

We found no second vehicle for any of them.

### 7. $TRUMP holders and the dinner (documented proxies)

Exchange-use analyses suggest most top holders were outside the US:
- Bloomberg: 19 of the top 25 leaderboard wallets, and at least 56% of the top 220, used exchanges that bar US customers.
- CBS: at least 52 of the top 100 did the same.

Exchange use indicates likely residence, not nationality. Identified dinner guests include Sun, whose SEC case was pending, and Singapore's MemeCore, whose wallet held its full balance while most top-10 wallets sold.

China-linked Nasdaq small caps also announced TRUMP purchases:
- GD Culture: up to $300M to buy bitcoin and TRUMP.
- Addentax: talks to buy up to $800M of TRUMP and bitcoin.
- Fr8Tech: a $20M TRUMP treasury, with about $2M reportedly bought.

The WSJ described the China-linked purchases as attempts to avoid delisting. Actual holdings are unconfirmed.

### 8. Bill Zanker: operator, not investor (documented)

Zanker's Fight Fight Fight LLC, owned by Celebration Cards LLC, co-holds the $TRUMP supply with Trump's CIC Digital. In October 2025 it sought $200M to $1B for a TRUMP treasury company, which remained a proposal. A Trump-branded wallet plan was shelved after a dispute with WLF. We found no sign of Zanker investing his own money in other family vehicles.

### 9. Gulf state entities (documented)

Qatari Diar, QIA, PIF and Dar Global recur across three sets of deals:
- the Trump Organization's projects;
- Kushner's Affinity;
- Witkoff's business (QIA bought the Park Lane Hotel from a Witkoff-Mubadala joint venture).

Qatar also supplied the 747-8 jet. Major US arms and security decisions followed. Details are in C16, C31, C53, C56 and C57. No link between any deal and any decision is documented.

### 10. 1789 Capital limited partners (unverified, discarded)

1789's LPs are not public. Rep. Raskin's August 2026 letter asks about insider access, and 1789 rejected the inquiry. We found no named LP who also invested elsewhere in the Trump family's ventures.

## Not connected in the coverage we found

These are our own cross-references. No source asserts them.

- **DWF Labs and Aqua 1 both present as UAE-based WLFI buyers in 2025:** inferred co-occurrence. No source links the two.
- **Same month, January 2025:** Aryam signed for 49% of WLF (about 2025-01-16), Sun added $45M (about 2025-01-20), and Web3Port bought WLFI. This is inferred timing clustering only.
- **Sun's ALT5 pledge and Kraken's ALT5 role:** both touch the same WLFI treasury vehicle. Inferred, no relationship between Sun and Kraken found.
- **"Celebration Cards LLC" and "Celebration Coins":** the owner of Fight Fight Fight and the name of the royalty line on Trump's OGE disclosure share a name. Inferred from naming only.
- **Jump as a common thread** between WLF liquidity and TMTG's data feed: this rests on an inferred market-making role plus a single-source subscriber report.
- **Zhou to Trump sons:** "up to $75M to a Trump-and-sons company" comes from the NYT (single-source as relayed). That Zhou sought any government favour is not alleged by anyone we found.

## Denials and context

- WLF, the White House and Trump deny any link between investments and policy. Trump says he knew nothing about the Aryam deal.
- Aqua 1 and Dave Lee denied the Web3Port link. Zhou's representative disputed the NYT portrayal without naming errors. Zhou is uncharged.
- Eric Trump denies any role in Tron Inc. Crypto.com denies any link between its deals, its donations and regulatory outcomes. 1789 rejects the Raskin inquiry.
- The SEC's 2025 crypto retreat also covered firms with no Trump ties.
