# C84: Heritage Advisors and Sheikh Sultan bin Jassim: one Qatari fund across three stories

**Verdict: mostly-accurate (confidence: medium).**

The fund's three roles in this claim are documented:
- It paid Lee Zeldin's consultancy in 2023.
- It holds a large Newsmax stake.
- It was the Qatari investor in Fred Daibes's Edgewater property in the Menendez case.

This check adds four layers to C73:
- **The Newsmax stake's size.** An SEC ownership chain shows about 19.7M shares, roughly 15% of the company (our arithmetic). At the March 2025 listing that was worth about $1.6B (our arithmetic), against about $50M invested.
- **The Daibes deal's terms.** Heritage paid $45M for a quarter stake.
- **The IS EG Halal overlap.** The Daily Beast reports that the joint Heritage-Daibes entity controls the base of co-defendant Wael Hana's IS EG Halal. This is single-source.
- **The jet.** A Free Beacon claim, from one anonymous source, ties Sheikh Sultan to Hamad bin Jassim, the reported prior owner of the Qatari Air Force One jet.

No source shows the threads were coordinated or that any of them produced a US government benefit. We found no EPA matter involving Qatar. Newsmax is suing to overturn an approval by Trump's FCC rather than benefiting from it. Daibes's $1M pardon bid through Javelin failed.

Builds on: C73 (Qatar access network) and C64 (lobbyists and fixers). This report extends them and does not repeat them. icij.org, sec.gov and oge.gov could not be read directly. Findings rest on search-result text across several outlets and filing aggregators.

## What was checked

Map Heritage Advisors' other US investments and relationships. Then cover:
- Newsmax's dealings with the administration: the FCC, its defamation settlements and its IPO.
- Zeldin's disclosures, and any EPA matters involving Qatari interests.
- Whether any source connects these threads.

## Connections found (each graded)

### 1. Sheikh Sultan, a Cayman/US holding chain, and about 15% of Newsmax
**Grade: documented (SEC Form 3, relayed by filing aggregators).**

- **The holding.** A Form 3 filed around 1 May 2025 shows **Naples Investment Holdco LLC** holding **19,737,553 Newsmax Class B shares**. Newsmax had listed on the NYSE on 31 March 2025.
- **The ownership chain.** Holdco's sole member is **Heritage Investments LP**, which sits under Heritage GP and a trust. Sheikh Sultan is the trust's sole beneficiary. ICIJ and the Washington Post had reported in 2024 that the stake was moved into a Cayman Islands structure.
- **Its size (our arithmetic).** Ruddy holds 39.24M shares, about 30.4%, so the Heritage block is roughly **15%**. That matches a Forbes-based figure relayed by a low-authority ownership site.
- **Its value (our arithmetic).** At the first-day close of $83.51 it was worth about **$1.65B**, against about $50M paid in 2019-20.
- **What is not established.** The lockup expired on 29 September 2025. We did not establish later sales or the current value, and the stock fell sharply after its debut.

### 2. The Menendez deal: $45M for a quarter of Daibes's Edgewater holdings, including IS EG Halal's base
**Grade: deal documented; IS EG overlap single-source (Daily Beast).**

- **The indictment.** The January 2024 superseding indictment says Menendez introduced Daibes to a royal principal of a "Qatari Investment Company". It says the fund signed a letter of intent, then formed a 2023 joint venture to invest tens of millions in a New Jersey project.
- **The fund's identity.** Trial documents and press identify the fund as Heritage.
- **The terms.** FDD, citing property records, says Heritage closed a **$45M** deal with Daibes in **December 2022**.
- **The IS EG overlap.** The Daily Beast reports that the money bought a one-quarter stake through **Hudson Edgewater Associates**. It also reports that this entity controls the site serving as the base of **IS EG Halal**, the company of Daibes's co-defendant Wael Hana in the Egypt strand of the same case. Heritage's lawyers say Sheikh Sultan had no link to IS EG and did not know about the overlap.
- **No charges.** Neither Sheikh Sultan nor Qatar was charged.

### 3. A Biden aide arranged the 2021 Menendez-Sultan meeting
**Grade: single-source (ICIJ, July 2024, based on an email).**

- **The meeting.** A former Biden aide, identified as Doyle, set up Menendez's 2021 meeting with Sheikh Sultan and Qatari official **Ali Al Thawadi**.
- **Doyle's account.** He says it was a favour to a friend, that he was unpaid, and that he told law enforcement about it.
- **The donation.** He gave Menendez $2,900 about six weeks later.

This layer shows the fund sought access across both parties; it is not a Trump link.

### 4. Sheikh Sultan, Hamad bin Jassim, and the Air Force One jet
**Grade: single-source, anonymous. Unverified.**

C73 saw only the Free Beacon's headline. The article itself makes two claims:
- **The jet.** From flight records, it ties tail numbers **P4-HBJ** and A7-HJJ to former PM Hamad bin Jassim (HBJ). It says P4-HBJ is the 747-8 that went to Trump. The NYT, as relayed, found P4-HBJ registered with Global Jet Isle of Man, which would not name the current owner.
- **The partnership.** A "source familiar" calls Sheikh Sultan an **HBJ confidant and business partner**.

This is the only source joining the Heritage cluster to the jet and, through HBJ, to the 666 Fifth episode (C66). No filing we saw documents a Sultan-HBJ partnership.

### 5. Zeldin: Heritage was a 2023 client; he divested the firm
**Grade: documented (OGE 278e; Jewish Insider; The Intercept).**

- **The payment.** Heritage Advisors (London) paid Zeldin Strategies more than $5,000 for April-December 2023.
- **Zeldin's account.** He says the work required no FARA registration and involved no lobbying or media work for the fund.
- **His overall income.** E&E News reports $775K in total salary from Zeldin Strategies.
- **The ethics agreement.** It requires him to divest the firm and to recuse from EPA matters involving clients of his wife Diana, who works there. This comes from search excerpts; the OGE PDF could not be read directly.
- **Timing.** Quincy notes the engagement began shortly after his 2023 Newsweek op-ed criticising Qatari influence.
- **Not established.** We could not read the entries in his 2026 annual 278e (signed 13 May 2026), so whether Heritage stayed a client of the firm is unknown.

### 6. EPA matters involving Qatar
**Grade: none found.**

- No Zeldin trip to Doha, meeting with Qatar's environment ministry, LNG action naming Qatar, or role in the May 2025 Doha visit appears in US coverage or Qatari state media (QNA, The Peninsula).
- No source alleges any EPA action benefiting Heritage.

### 7. Newsmax and the administration: adversarial more than favoured
**Grade: documented.**

- **FCC.** In March 2026 Newsmax joined DirecTV, cable groups and public-interest groups to challenge the FCC Media Bureau's **19 March 2026** approval of Nexstar-Tegna. The challengers cited Trump's February Truth Social endorsement and Chair Carr's backing. The D.C. Circuit dismissed the challenge in **July 2026** for lack of jurisdiction over a bureau-level order, not on the merits; a full-Commission ruling was expected by end of 2026.
- **Pentagon.** Newsmax got workspace in Hegseth's February 2025 rotation. It refused his press rules in October 2025, turned in its badges, and was denied briefing access in December 2025.
- **Settlements.** Newsmax paid Smartmatic $40M (Axios, March 2025) and Dominion $67M (AP, August 2025). These were private suits; no government role was found.
- **IPO.** The March 2025 Reg A+ offering raised $75M at $10 a share, and the stock closed its first day up 735%. Ruddy holds about 30% of shares and about 81% of votes.
- **Trump business tie.** Trump Media's Truth+ carries Newsmax (C21).

We found no FCC or other federal benefit to Newsmax.

### 8. Heritage's other investments
- **Fort Partners (Miami real estate).** **Documented.** Sidley Austin announced on 29 June 2023 that it advised Heritage, "led by HE Sheikh Sultan bin Jassim Al Thani", on an add-on investment. No amount was given.
- **The News Movement.** **Single-source (ICIJ).** Heritage invested in this Gen Z news startup co-founded by **Will Lewis**, who became Washington Post publisher in January 2024. Newsmax's spokesman pointed to this tie when the Post reported the Newsmax stake.
- **Indy Eleven.** **Single-source plus a database entry.** ICIJ cites a "professional soccer team in Indiana", and PitchBook lists Heritage Advisors (UK) as a minority investor in Indy Eleven. Club releases name a different Heritage (Jeff Laborsky's Heritage Group), so this needs checking.
- **Trump businesses.** We found no Heritage investment in any Trump-family business.

### 9. Daibes, Javelin and the pardon market; Menendez's own bid
**Grade: engagement documented (LDA filings via NBC); $1M amount single-source (NBC, three unnamed sources); NYT via New Jersey Globe. Verdict: mostly-accurate (audit).**

- **Daibes.** He reportedly paid Keith Schiller's Javelin **$1M in Q1 2025** (NBC, unnamed sources) for "executive relief" and ended the engagement in June 2025.
- **Menendez.** He pushed publicly for a Trump pardon. The NYT reported in January 2026 that Trump was not considering it.
- **Outcome.** We found no clemency for Menendez, Daibes or Hana through November 2026, and no source ties Heritage to Daibes's pardon effort.

### 10. Does any source join the threads?
**Partly.**

- FDD/Courage.Media and Accountable.US identify Zeldin's client as the Menendez-case fund. That shared identity is documented.
- Only the Free Beacon adds the HBJ and jet layer.
- No outlet alleges that the Zeldin payment, the Newsmax stake and the Daibes deal were coordinated, or that any of them produced a US action.

## Not connected in the coverage we found

- **The three-story chain.** Heritage paid Zeldin (2023), holds about 15% of Newsmax, and partnered with Daibes, who later bought Trump-world pardon lobbying. *Inferred* (our chain). Each link is documented.
- **Heritage's Daibes venture and the IS EG Halal site.** This places one Qatari fund physically in both the Qatar and Egypt strands of the Menendez case. *Single-source* (Daily Beast); no prosecutor's assertion was found.
- **Sheikh Sultan and HBJ, the jet's reported prior owner.** If true, the Newsmax/Zeldin fund sits one step from the Air Force One gift. *Single-source, anonymous* (Free Beacon).
- **Heritage's investments on both sides of the media divide.** It backs Newsmax and The News Movement, co-founded by the Washington Post publisher. *Documented holdings* (Newsmax filings; ICIJ). Any editorial effect is *unverified*, and Heritage denies control.
- **Newsmax's challenge to the Trump-endorsed Nexstar-Tegna approval while a Qatari royal holds about 15%.** *Inferred co-occurrence.* No source links the stake to the litigation.
- **Zeldin's anti-Qatar op-ed followed by Heritage consulting.** *Documented sequence* (Quincy, Jewish Insider). Any causal link is *inferred*.

## Corrections

- **The Newsmax stake.** C73 gives only the $50M invested. SEC filings show about 19.7M shares, roughly 15%, worth about $1.65B at listing (our arithmetic).
- **"Dealings with the administration".** The claim's framing implies favour. The record shows Newsmax opposing the FCC and leaving the Pentagon, and its settlements were private.
- **The Daibes deal.** It closed in December 2022, $45M for 25% (FDD, Daily Beast).
- **The Free Beacon article.** It does not say Heritage owns the jet. It says Sheikh Sultan is an HBJ partner, citing one anonymous source.

## Denials and context

- Heritage says it is independent of any government, invests only for value, and has no editorial or management control over its media holdings.
- Heritage says Sheikh Sultan knew nothing of IS EG.
- Neither Sheikh Sultan nor Qatar was charged.
- Doyle says he was unpaid and cooperated with law enforcement.
- Newsmax denies softening its coverage of Qatar.
- Zeldin declined to comment; Senate Republicans say his disclosures were complete.
- Daibes's lawyer says Javelin was dropped in June 2025. The White House says the President alone decides pardons.
