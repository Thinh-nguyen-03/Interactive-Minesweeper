# C85: Yorkville's blank-cheque vehicles (Venezuela, Blue Water III and PlusAI)

**Verdict: partly-accurate.** The governance and money links are well documented. The policy links are not.
**Confidence:** medium.

This is a third-layer hunt from C78; the other parent is C62. C78 already maps the people across the four Yorkville-sponsored SPACs: Kevin McGurn, Mark Angelo, Troy Rillo, Lawrence Glick, Alan Garten, Devin Nunes and Scott Glabe. It is not repeated here. This report adds four things:

- what each vehicle has done since C78;
- the Venezuela sanctions and oil-licence record each vehicle is exposed to;
- the federal autonomous-vehicle (AV) record PlusAI is exposed to;
- the founder-share economics.

**Access note.** sec.gov, barchart, fortune.com and iisd.org were all blocked for direct fetch in this session. Filing figures below come from search-result summaries of the 8-K, 10-Q and S-1/A filings and from SEC-filing aggregators (Equibles, Medici List, Stock Titan). They are graded on that basis.

## Summary

The brief holds up partly.

**What holds up**
- **Yorkville International Capital (YICC)**
  - It closed a **$230M** IPO on 17 June 2026, larger than the $200M C78 reported.
  - Its sponsor holds about **15.33M founder shares bought for $25,000**, about 40% of the shares outstanding.
  - It is still hunting for a Latin America/Venezuela target. No target was found as of November 2026, and no reply to Wyden.
- **Texas Ventures Acquisition III (TVA III)**
  - It was the planned merger partner for a **spin-off of Truth Social itself**. Trump Media (TMTG), TAE and TVA III announced this on 27 February 2026 and dropped it on 10 June 2026. C78 and the parent files did not cover this.
  - TVA III's board then included two Trump Organization executives.
  - After the spin-off was dropped, TVA III signed with PlusAI.
  - Shareholders redeemed **about 79%** of the trust at the October 2026 extension vote. That leaves the Yorkville sponsor's 7.5M founder shares outnumbering the 4.8M public shares.
- **Blue Water Acquisition Corp III (BLUW)** has named no target since Yorkville took it over. Its Venezuela ambitions left with its old sponsor, Joseph Hernandez. His new SPAC, **Blue Water IV**, signed and then dropped a letter of intent (LOI) for Venezuelan oil assets in April-May 2026.
- **The Citgo auction is unresolved.** Treasury has repeatedly held up the court-approved $5.9B sale to Elliott's Amber Energy and froze Citgo's governance on 14 September 2026.

**What does not hold up**
- No source ties Yorkville or Trump-family interests to any OFAC licence, to the Citgo outcome or to a federal AV decision.

## Connections (sub-claims)

### 1. YICC: a Venezuela-focused SPAC with an outsized promote, still without a target (documented)

**The IPO**
- Units began trading on 16 June 2026.
- The IPO closed on 17 June 2026 at **23,000,000 units**, including the full 3M over-allotment, for **$230M**. The $230M sits in trust at $10.00 a unit.
- **Private placement warrants:** 6.3M at $1.00, of which the sponsor bought 4.0M and Cohen & Company Capital Markets (CCM) bought 2.3M. CCM was sole book-runner and was also a co-placement agent on TMTG's 2025 raise (C78).

**The founder shares**
- The S-1/A summary says Yorkville International Capital Sponsor, LLC (Florida) bought **15,333,333 Class B founder shares for $25,000**, about $0.0016 a share.
- The subscription letter reads 15,033,333, a discrepancy we could not resolve.
- MarketChameleon's market value of about $378.7M at $9.88 (30 Oct 2026) implies about 38.3M shares, which matches 23M public plus 15.33M founder shares. On that count, founders hold about **40%** of the company, roughly double the usual 20% promote. This is our arithmetic.
- At $10 the block is worth about **$153M on paper**, and nothing if the SPAC liquidates.

**Target and scrutiny**
- The Financial Times first reported the Venezuela plan on 14-15 May 2026 (via La Patilla).
- MarketChameleon showed no news or press releases as of 30 Oct 2026, and no target or definitive agreement was found.
- Wyden's 25 June 2026 letter asked whether Yorkville sought or received OFAC guidance or a specific licence. It also asked about exposure to PDVSA under the 50 Percent Rule and requested beneficial-ownership charts. No reply was found.

### 2. YICC and the post-Maduro licence regime (documented regime; applicability inferred)

**The licences OFAC issued after the 3 January 2026 capture of Maduro**
- **GL 46** (29 Jan 2026): oil production and sales, limited to "established U.S. entities", which one law firm reads as those organised on or before 29 January 2025. It excludes new investment.
- **GL 46A and 46B:** amendments on 10 Feb and 13 Mar 2026.
- **GL 47:** sales of US-origin diluents.
- **GL 49:** negotiations and contingent contracts for new investment.
- **GL 50:** PDVSA entities.
- **March 2026:** a licence letting PDVSA sell directly to pre-2025 US businesses.
- Under all of them, payments must go to Treasury-controlled accounts.

**What this means for YICC (our reading, not a source's)**
- YICC was formed in March 2026 in the Cayman Islands, so the GL 46 "established entity" carve-out would not seem to cover it.
- An actual Venezuelan investment would apparently need GL 49 (contingent contracts) or a specific licence.
- This is exactly Wyden's question, and it is unanswered.
- No OFAC licence to any Yorkville entity is on record.

### 3. TVA III was set to absorb Truth Social (documented)

**The plan**
- On 27 Feb 2026, TMTG, TAE Technologies and TVA III announced talks for TMTG to spin off Truth Social and other media assets into a new company.
- Its shares would go to TMTG holders of record before the TAE merger closed, and the new company would then merge with TVA III (Axios, CBS, MediaPost, company release).

**The board at the time**
- TVA III's board included Trump Organization EVPs Glick and Garten (C78).
- TMTG's CEO Nunes and general counsel Glabe had resigned from it on 26 Feb 2026, **one day before** the announcement (8-K). The timing is documented. That they resigned to manage a conflict is our inference; the filing says only that they had no disagreement.

**What it would have meant**
- Spin-off shares would have gone pro rata to TMTG shareholders, the largest of which is Donald Trump's trust. The President would therefore have received shares in an entity that a Yorkville/Trump Org-staffed SPAC was set to merge with. This is our inference from the announced structure.

**How it ended**
- On 10 June 2026 the parties said they would not pursue the spin-off "at this time".
- After the TAE merger, the combined company will instead weigh "strategic alternatives" for the media assets.
- The TAE merger (S-4 effective, Q4 2026 target) had not closed as of mid-November 2026.

### 4. TVA III and PlusAI: redemptions hollow out the trust and leave the sponsor dominant (documented, via aggregator summaries of 8-K and 8-K/A)

**The deal and the extension vote**
- **Merger terms:** an $800M pre-money valuation plus up to 63M earnout shares. Closing needs at least $40M of trust cash plus note and PIPE proceeds.
- **Redemptions:** at the extension vote, holders redeemed **17,699,488 Class A shares at about $10.63**, roughly $188M. That leaves **4,800,512** public shares, about $51M at $10.63.
- **Deadline:** extended from 24 Oct 2026 toward 24 Jul 2027, through monthly sponsor deposits. The Yorkville Acquisition Sponsor II note was $145,000 on 20 Oct 2026, and its deposit moved the deadline to 24 Nov 2026.
- A going-concern doubt was flagged in August 2026.

**The sponsor's position**
- Yorkville's sponsor paid $7.4M for 7.5M founder shares plus 4.7M warrants (C78).
- After the redemptions, **the founder shares outnumber the public float** (7.5M vs 4.8M).
- At a nominal $10 they are worth about **$75M**, about 10x cost. Whether any were forfeited or transferred under the merger agreement is unverified because the S-4 could not be read.

**Who sits on the board now**
- No source says whether Glick or Garten are still directors, or who sits on the post-merger PlusAI board.

### 5. PlusAI and the federal AV agenda (documented policy; no PlusAI-specific action)

**What the administration did**
- **DOT national AV strategy ("AV 5.0"):** released 3 September 2026, which is **the same day the PlusAI-TVA III deal was announced**. The strategy is non-binding and directs FMCSA to revamp its rules for trucks with automated driving systems (ADS).
- **NHTSA:** an interim final rule on 31 July 2026 lets vehicles enter production before an exemption is granted.
- **FMCSA:** on 7 October 2026 it granted a five-year exemption (to 7 Oct 2031) letting Level-4 trucks use cab-mounted beacons instead of warning triangles. Aurora was the applicant, and any Level-4 carrier can opt in.
- **Still pending:** FMCSA's proposed rule on ADS-equipped trucks had not been published.
- **Congress:** the BUILD America 250 Act passed committee 62-2 on 22 May 2026.

**Where PlusAI fits**
- Trade press names PlusAI among the firms planning commercial scale in 2027, alongside Aurora, Volvo, Waabi and Torc. These are general, industry-wide rules that benefit PlusAI along with its competitors.
- No source reports PlusAI opting into the beacon exemption, lobbying for these rules, or getting any company-specific decision.
- The same-day timing of the strategy and the deal announcement is our observation only.

### 6. BLUW: the Citgo bidder lost its Venezuela thesis when Yorkville bought it (documented)

**How the bid ended**
- BLUW suspended its Citgo participation on 14 Nov 2025, citing market and regulatory conditions.
- It referred to an unnamed investment partner that had committed capital; the funding of the $10B bid was never disclosed.

**The Yorkville takeover**
- On 25 Nov 2025, Yorkville BW Acquisition Sponsor bought **6,325,000 Class B shares and 430,000 private units**. The prior sponsor was Blue Water Acquisition III LLC.
- The price of **$7.2M** comes from a 10-K summary (Medici List) and was not confirmed in the filing text, so it is single-source.
- The quoted market value (about $332M at about $10.32) is consistent with about 32.1M shares outstanding.
- At $10 the founder block is about $63M nominal. It would be worthless on liquidation.

**After the takeover**
- No target has been announced.
- An aggregator still lists the stated focus as biotech, healthcare and technology.

**Where the Venezuela ambition went**
- Hernandez's new vehicle, **Blue Water Acquisition Corp IV** (NYSE: BWIV), signed a non-binding LOI on 28 April 2026 to buy Maha Capital AB's subsidiaries. They hold an indirect 24% stake in the PetroUrdaneta joint venture on Lake Maracaibo; Law360 valued the deal at $490M.
- The LOI was terminated on 15 May 2026.
- No Yorkville or TMTG person is reported at BWIV. That makes this a separate, non-Trump strand.

### 7. The Citgo auction outcome: unresolved and held by Treasury (documented)

**The sale so far**
- Judge Stark approved Amber Energy's (Elliott) $5.9B bid for PDV Holding on 25 Nov 2025.
- Venezuela/PDVSA appealed to the Third Circuit in January 2026 and hired Greenberg Traurig in June 2026.
- **OFAC actions:**
  - It has not licensed the sale.
  - Fortune says Treasury has extended Citgo's protection from the sale six times since January.
  - On 14 Sep 2026 OFAC amended the licence to block unauthorised governance changes at Citgo, Citgo Holding and PDV Holding. Delcy Rodríguez's interim government had been preparing to replace those boards.

**The administration's position**
- Bloomberg (April 2026) reported concerns over price and political risk.
- Fortune frames Citgo as leverage over Caracas.
- Energy Secretary Chris Wright had applauded the sale to Elliott, whose founder Paul Singer is a GOP megadonor (Fortune, Jan 2026; NHPR).

**Third Circuit and the Yorkville link**
- No Third Circuit ruling was found through mid-November 2026.
- Nothing links Yorkville or BLUW's new management to the auction after November 2025.

## Corrections to the brief and to C78

- **YICC size:** the IPO closed at **$230M** (23M units), not $200M (C78 and Wyden used the priced amount).
- **BLUW's role:** the brief says BLUW "previously bid ~$10B for Citgo's parent; now staffed with TMTG people". The bid was dropped before Yorkville took over, and the Venezuela thread passed to Hernandez's Blue Water IV, not to the Yorkville-run BLUW. Nunes and Glabe on BLUW's board remains single-source (C78).

- **"Federal AV rules":** no PlusAI-specific federal action was found. The rules and exemptions are industry-wide.

## Denials and context

- **Trump Organization:** a spokesperson told Siegelman the Trump sons "have no involvement with Yorkville" (C78).
- **Yorkville:** it has not publicly answered Wyden. The Wyden letter is a minority oversight letter, not an enforcement action.
- **TMTG, TAE and TVA III:** they gave no reason for dropping the spin-off.
- **Sponsor economics:** founder-share economics ($25,000 promotes, about $1 a share for transferred blocks) are standard for SPACs and are not by themselves a Trump-specific benefit. They are worth nothing without a completed deal.
