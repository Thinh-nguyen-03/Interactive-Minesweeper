# C57: The UAE network: Tahnoon, G42, MGX and their US deals

**Verdict:** mostly-accurate (confidence: medium)

**Type:** follow-up lead (parents: C06, C07, C08, C12, C15; also builds on C34)

## Summary

Almost every link in the claim is documented.

- **World Liberty:** Tahnoon-linked vehicles hold 49% of World Liberty Financial (Aryam, signed January 2025). The WSJ reported in August 2026 that they also hold 49% of the holding company behind WLF's OCC trust bank (StringZ).
- **MGX:**
  - It settled its $2B Binance investment in USD1.
  - It is a Stargate equity funder.
  - It holds 15% of the TikTok US joint venture that Trump approved by executive order.
  - It invested in xAI, Anthropic and OpenAI in 2026.
- **G42:** It has Microsoft's $1.5B, Commerce chip authorizations and the Stargate UAE campus. Its head of crypto reportedly advised WLF.

Three points are weaker:

- **"ADQ/Lunate":** Lunate (with QIA) is the documented Affinity investor. ADQ is not.
- **StringZ:** That Tahnoon is behind StringZ rests on anonymous WSJ sourcing. The OCC decision names StringZ but not its owner or stake.
- **G42 in 2026:** Several of G42's 2026 developments are single-sourced.

No quid pro quo has been documented. WLF, the White House and Trump deny any link between the UAE money and US policy.

Research note: the original pages at most news sites were not read directly, and the research ended before the TikTok closing details and the G42 reincorporation report could be re-checked. Findings rely on search-result coverage across several outlets, plus the parent claims' sourcing.

## Map of entities (who controls what)

- **Sheikh Tahnoon bin Zayed** is UAE national security adviser and brother of President Mohamed bin Zayed. He chairs or oversees:
  - **G42**, the AI group.
    - **Peng Xiao** is its CEO. He gave up US citizenship for UAE nationality.
    - **Martin Edelman**, a US lawyer, is a G42 official. One unverified dossier calls him general counsel.
    - **Fiacc Larkin** is its head of crypto.
  - **MGX**, the AI investment firm. G42 and Mubadala founded it in 2024.
  - **Aryam Investment 1**, which holds 49% of WLF.
  - **StringZ Holding**, reportedly. It is said to hold 49% of WLTC Holdings, the parent of WLF's trust bank.
  - **Chimera Investment**, which majority-owns **Lunate**, an LP in Kushner's Affinity Partners.
  - **ADQ**, which he chaired until it was folded into L'IMAD in January 2026.

## Sub-claims

### 1. Aryam's 49% of WLF; board seats for Peng Xiao and Martin Edelman: confirmed
- **The deal:** The WSJ reported on 2026-02-01, as relayed by The Block, Cointelegraph and others, that Aryam agreed on about 2025-01-16 to pay $500M for 49% of WLF. Eric Trump signed the deal.
  - $250M was paid upfront.
  - About $187M of that went to Trump-family entities.
  - At least $31M went to Witkoff-linked entities.
- **Board seats:** Two Aryam executives who also hold top G42 posts, Peng Xiao and Martin Edelman, joined WLF's five-member board.
- **US approvals:** None were sought or disclosed.
  - In February 2026, Sens. Warren and Kim asked Treasury Secretary Bessent, who chairs CFIUS, whether CFIUS should review the stake. They set a 2026-03-05 deadline.
  - A House inquiry opened on 2026-02-05.
  - No public CFIUS outcome was found.

### 2. MGX's $2B Binance investment settled in USD1: confirmed (see C07)
- MGX announced the $2B Binance stake on 2025-03-12. WLF said on 2025-05-01 that USD1 would settle it.
- MGX is chaired by Tahnoon.
- No US approval applied, because a foreign investor was buying into an offshore exchange.
- MGX says it chose USD1 itself and has not invested in WLF.

### 3. MGX and Stargate: confirmed
- MGX was named an initial Stargate equity funder at the White House on 2025-01-21, alongside SoftBank, OpenAI and Oracle (see C08).
- MGX also co-founded the AI Infrastructure Partnership with BlackRock, GIP, Microsoft and Nvidia.
- With GIP, that partnership agreed in October 2025 to buy Aligned Data Centers for about $40B. The deal closed around July 2026.
- A CFIUS review was expected, but no outcome was reported.

### 4. MGX's stake in the TikTok US joint venture: confirmed
- **The order:** Trump signed an executive order on 2025-09-25 declaring the framework a "qualified divestiture" under the 2024 divest-or-ban law. Vance valued the US business at about $14B.
- **Terms:** Agreements were signed around 2025-12-18/19.
  - Oracle, Silver Lake and MGX each own 15% of TikTok USDS Joint Venture LLC.
  - Affiliates of existing ByteDance investors own 30.1%.
  - ByteDance retains 19.9%.
- **Closing:** closed on 2026-01-22 (audit: Axios, Hollywood Reporter). The board named Adam Presser CEO; MGX's David Scott holds a board seat.
- **Approvals:** The required US approval was the president's own determination under the statute. MGX's price was not disclosed.

### 5. MGX's investments in OpenAI, xAI and Anthropic: confirmed
- **xAI:** MGX joined the $20B Series E announced in early January 2026, alongside QIA, Nvidia, Cisco and others.
- **Anthropic:** MGX was among the co-leads of the $30B Series G in February 2026, at a $380B post-money valuation. GIC and Coatue led that round. MGX is also named in the $65B Series H of 2026-05-28, at a $965B post-money valuation.
- **OpenAI:** MGX was a lead investor in the $122B round at an $852B valuation, which closed on 2026-03-31. Most outlets call it a co-lead alongside SoftBank, a16z, D. E. Shaw and TPG.
- **Amounts and approvals:** Per-investor amounts were not disclosed. No US government approval of these minority stakes was reported.
- **MGX fund:** MGX closed a fund of about $49-50B in mid-2026.

### 6. G42: Microsoft, chip licences, Stargate UAE: mostly-accurate
- **Microsoft:** It paid $1.5B for a minority stake on 2024-04-16.
  - The deal runs under an Intergovernmental Assurance Agreement shaped by the US and UAE governments.
  - Brad Smith joined G42's board.
  - G42 reportedly agreed to drop Huawei equipment and exit China.
- **Stargate UAE:** It was announced in May 2025.
  - It is a 1GW cluster built by G42 and operated by OpenAI and Oracle, with Nvidia, Cisco and SoftBank.
  - It sits inside a planned 5GW US-UAE AI campus.
  - The first 200MW was due in 2026. No confirmation that it is online was found.
- **Chip licences:**
  - Commerce authorized Microsoft's chip transfers to the UAE in September 2025.
  - On about 2025-11-20, it authorized up to 35,000 GB300-equivalent chips for G42, and the same for Saudi Arabia's Humain, under security and reporting conditions.
- **Unverified 2026 items:**
  - A reported 2026-07-10 move of the UAE into Country Group A:5, which would mean license-free access.
  - A WSJ-relayed plan for G42 to reincorporate as a mostly US-owned company.
  - A Bloomberg-cited report that initial permits excluded G42, which conflicts with the November approval.
- **Sacks:** His role in pushing for Emirati chip access is covered in C34.

### 7. A G42 crypto executive advising WLF: mostly-accurate
- The NYT reported in September 2025 that Fiacc Larkin, G42's head of crypto, served as WLF's "chief strategic adviser" from January 2025 while remaining at G42.
- Fortune relays the report and cites his LinkedIn. The Senate Banking minority staff report repeats it.
- The NYT original was not accessed. No WLF or G42 comment on his role was found.

### 8. StringZ Holding in WLF's trust bank: mostly-accurate
- **The OCC filing:** OCC Corporate Decision #1385 (preliminary conditional approval, 2026-08-14) lists StringZ Holding RSC (DE) LLC among investors giving passivity commitments. It does not name StringZ's owner or the size of its stake.
- **The WSJ report:** The WSJ reported on 2026-08-27, citing people familiar with the matter, that:
  - Tahnoon's group is behind StringZ.
  - StringZ owns 49% of WLTC Holdings.
  - A Trump-family entity owns about 38%.
- **Registration:** StringZ was registered in Abu Dhabi in April 2025 and in Delaware a month later.
- **WLF response:** One outlet says a WLF spokesperson confirmed the arrangement without detailing it.
- **Approvals:** The approval needed is the OCC charter, which is still preliminary. It carries passivity agreements from the major holders. See C12.

### 9. ADQ/Lunate in Kushner's Affinity: partly-accurate
- **The investment:** Lunate and QIA added $1.5B to Affinity on 2024-12-20, bringing its assets under management to $4.6B. Kushner names PIF, QIA and Lunate as the three largest LPs.
- **ADQ:** No source shows ADQ itself investing.
- **The Tahnoon link:**
  - Lunate is majority-owned by Chimera Investment, which Tahnoon oversees.
  - Lunate was seeded with ADQ's alternatives portfolio while Tahnoon chaired ADQ.
  - ADQ was rolled into L'IMAD, chaired by Crown Prince Khaled bin Mohamed, in January 2026.
- **Approvals:** None were required.

## Corrections
- The claim says "ADQ/Lunate". The documented Affinity investor is Lunate (with QIA), not ADQ.
- That Tahnoon owns StringZ and that its stake is 49% are anonymous WSJ sourcing, not OCC-disclosed facts.
- MGX holds 15% of the TikTok US joint venture. Early reports of a 45% combined Oracle/Silver Lake/MGX share were superseded.
- MGX's exact role in the OpenAI round, and its dollar amounts in the OpenAI, xAI and Anthropic rounds, are undisclosed or inconsistently described.

## Denials and context
- **Trump:** He said he did not know of the Aryam deal ("my sons are handling that"). The White House says his assets are in a trust run by his children.
- **WLF:**
  - A spokesperson said any claim the deal had anything to do with the administration's chip actions is "100% false".
  - Spokesman David Wachsman said Trump and Witkoff had no involvement.
- **Anonymous source:** A person familiar with the deal told the WSJ that the Aryam money was not G42's and was never discussed with Trump.
- **MGX:** It says it chose USD1 on its own and has not invested in WLF.
- **OCC:** It says career staff reviewed the WLTC application, and it rejected the conflict-of-interest comments.
- **Context:** Much of MGX's portfolio is large-scale sovereign co-investment alongside US firms, with no evidence it required favours. The policy-relevant US approvals are:
  - the chip licences (Commerce/BIS);
  - the TikTok divestiture determination (the president);
  - the trust-bank charter (OCC);
  - any CFIUS review, of which none is public.
- **Disclosure:** This write-up was produced with an Anthropic model, and Anthropic is one of the MGX portfolio companies listed here. Facts about that investment come from Anthropic's announcements and press coverage.

## Leads
- MGX and the $40B Aligned Data Centers takeover, and whether CFIUS reviewed it.
- G42's reported reincorporation as a US company, and the UAE's 2026 export-control upgrade. Both are unverified.
- The outcome of the Warren/Kim CFIUS request and the House inquiry. The same questions now apply to StringZ.
- Lunate's 2024 takeover of G42's China fund.
