# C65: Officials who decided matters involving their former clients or partners

**Verdict: mostly-accurate (confidence: medium).** Most of the officials named in the claim have documented prior ties (clients, holdings, partners or friends) to parties their offices later affected. In five cases a specific decision lines up with the tie:

- Bondi and the Qatar jet memo
- Blanche and the NCET memo
- Selig and the prediction-market fight
- Navarro and the Vulcan loan
- Feinberg's Cerberus alumni running Pentagon investment offices

Barrack's pipeline push (Reuters) and Bessent's Argentina swap (Mother Jones and Popular Information) are reported by one outlet or a pair of advocacy-leaning ones. For Quintenz, Gould, Kratsios, Patel, Boulos and Charles Kushner we found ties but no benefiting decision. No ethics office, inspector general or court has found a violation in any of these cases. (Audit, Nov 2026: Bondi was fired as AG on Apr 2, 2026; Blanche was acting AG until the Senate confirmed him 50-49 on Aug 8, 2026, and he was sworn in on Aug 10, 2026.)

Method: we drew on C12, C14, C16, C25, C26, C34-C36, C38, C43, C49, C52-C54 and C60, then searched outward. Many outlets were not read directly, so most findings rely on search-result text from several outlets. The research ended before every thread could be finished; the gaps are listed at the end.

## What was checked
This check asks for administration officials whose decisions benefited entities they previously worked for, invested in or were paid by, together with their holdings, recusals, waivers and specific decisions.

## Connections (each graded)

### 1. Bondi, Qatar and the jet memo (documented)
- **The Qatar contract.** Ballard Partners was paid $115K a month by Qatar's embassy, roughly July 2019 to June 2020. Bondi was registered as key personnel on the contract.
  - She left in November 2019 to join Trump's impeachment defence and rejoined in March 2020.
  - Her personal share of the fee is not on record. A fact-check rates "she personally made $115K/month" as undetermined.
- **The jet memo.** In May 2025 an OLC-drafted memo signed by Bondi (with White House counsel Warrington) found it "legally permissible" to accept the ~$400M 747 and later transfer it to the library foundation.
  - A DOJ official told CNN that career ethics officials found no conflict requiring recusal.
  - CREW and Sen. Durbin say she should have recused.
  - The memo is unreleased. Freedom of the Press Foundation and American Oversight sued after DOJ quoted a FOIA wait of more than 600 days.
- **What this shows:** a paid foreign-principal tie followed by a decision that favoured that principal. It does not show that the lobbying shaped the memo.
- Ballard also employed Susie Wiles, and Kraken and Blockchain.com hired it in December 2024 (C52).

### 2. Bondi's brother's clients (dismissals documented; AG role unverified)
- **Dismissals.** DOJ dropped the case against ex-legislator Carolina Amesty (August 2025) and a wire-fraud indictment of two St. Louis developers shortly after they hired Brad Bondi of Paul Hastings (July 2025).
- **Milton.** Brad Bondi was lead trial counsel for Trevor Milton, who was pardoned in March 2025 (C36, C49).
- **Oversight requests.** Rep. Min, Sen. Schiff and others asked the DOJ IG for a review (letter of March 11, 2026). No outcome has been found.
- **DOJ response:** "the Attorney General had no role in them."

### 3. Bondi's Trump Media stock (documented)
- Bondi's periodic transaction report, disclosed May 14, 2025, shows a sale of $1M-$5M of DJT stock plus $250K-$500K of warrants on April 2, 2025 ("Liberation Day").
- Her ethics agreement required her to sell the stake within 90 days of confirmation, so the sale itself was mandated; the issue is its timing.
- Rep. Raskin referred the sale to the DOJ IG.
- This is a holding in the president's company, not a decision.

### 4. Blanche, crypto holdings and the NCET memo (documented)
- **Holdings and timing.** Blanche held about $159K-$485K of BTC, ETH and SOL when he signed the April 7, 2025 "Ending Regulation by Prosecution" memo, which disbanded NCET.
- **Divestment.** He divested May 31-June 3, 2025. As reported by the Daily Beast, his June 2025 filing says the holdings "were gifted in their entirety to my grandchild and adult children" rather than sold.
- He became acting AG on April 2, 2026, when Trump fired Bondi. The Senate confirmed him as AG 50-49 on August 8, 2026, and he was sworn in on August 10, 2026 (Axios; AP via WTOP, PBS).
- **Ethics questions.** Former ethics officials told ProPublica this likely breached 18 U.S.C. 208 and his ethics agreement. Six senators asked on January 28, 2026 whether he had a 208(b)(1) waiver. None has been published.
- The benefit went to the crypto sector as a whole, not to a former client.

### 5. Atkins, Patomak and his former holdings (documented)
- **Patomak.** His consultancy's clients included banks, exchanges and DeFi firms, and it is an FTX creditor under a January 2022 board-advisory agreement.
- **Holdings.** His disclosure showed up to ~$6M of crypto-related assets: Anchorage Digital equity, Securitize options plus a board seat (which he resigned in February 2025), and a stake in Off the Chain Capital. Reported ranges vary between outlets.
- **Ethics terms.** He agreed to divest, with a one-year bar on specific-party matters. He certified compliance on July 18, 2025.
  - The bar does not reach industry-wide policy, which is how most crypto relief came (C23, C43).
  - Warren's question about who bought Patomak has not been answered publicly. An audit re-check (Nov 2026) found only Patomak's April 22, 2025 announcement of a new supervisory board chaired by Richard Breeden and a seven-partner executive committee. The buyer is still unverified.

### 6. Securitize, Cantor and the SEC (documented pieces; conflict link inferred)
- Securitize agreed in October 2025 to merge with Cantor Equity Partners II, a SPAC sponsored by an affiliate of the Lutnick-family firm Cantor.
- The SEC declared the S-4 effective on June 5, 2026. The merger closed on July 1, 2026, and Securitize began trading on the NYSE as SECZ on July 2.
- Atkins's one-year Securitize bar would have lapsed around February 2026, and S-4 effectiveness is normally a staff action.
- **No source alleges impropriety.** The link is our own cross-reference.

### 7. Selig and the prediction-market fight (links documented; benefit inferred)
- **Willkie clients.** At Willkie, Selig's clients included Paradigm, eToro and Alluvial. Alluvial met staff of the SEC Crypto Task Force while Selig was its chief counsel.
- **The Kalshi letter.** He co-signed Paradigm's July 2024 letter urging the CFTC to allow Kalshi's political event contracts. Paradigm invested in Kalshi in June 2025.
- **Polymarket.** Zeteo reports that Willkie represents Polymarket.
- **As CFTC chair** he filed the February 17, 2026 amicus asserting exclusive federal jurisdiction over event contracts, and sued states from April 2, 2026 (C25, C38).
  - These actions favour Kalshi and Polymarket. Don Jr. advises both, and 1789 has invested in Polymarket.
- **Recusal.** His recusal covers only specific-party matters for one year. No source alleges a breach.

### 8. Quintenz (documented; no decisions)
- Quintenz sat on Kalshi's board and was a16z's crypto policy head.
- His CFTC nomination was withdrawn on September 30, 2025 after a clash with the Winklevoss twins over Gemini's case.
- His replacement, Selig, later joined Gemini's motion to vacate its CFTC judgment, and the twins then gave about $10M to MAGA Inc. (C52).

### 9. Gould (background documented; no prior-client benefit found)
- Gould was OCC chief counsel, then Bitfury's chief legal officer until 2022, then at Jones Day.
- As Comptroller he oversaw a wave of crypto trust charters, including World Liberty Trust's preliminary approval on August 14, 2026 (C12). He has rejected the "Trump fixer" charge.
- We found no Bitfury matter before the OCC and no retained-interest detail.

### 10. Navarro and Vulcan (documented)
- ProPublica (May 28, 2026) reported that Navarro initiated the $620M OSC loan, the only OSC deal initiated by a top aide to the president.
- Navarro is a friend of Don Jr.: he dedicated his latest book in part to him, and Don Jr. visited him in prison.
- 1789 Capital took a stake in Vulcan in its August 2025 Series A.
- No financial interest of Navarro's in Vulcan is reported. 1789 and the Pentagon deny any influence, and Navarro calls the reporting "fake news".

### 11. Lutnick's Commerce and Vulcan (documented)
- Commerce put a $50M CHIPS letter of intent (reported partly as equity) into the same November 3, 2025 Vulcan package.
- For Cantor's links to other minerals beneficiaries, see C35, C50 and C60.

### 12. Hegseth's Pentagon, Feinberg and Cerberus (documented; steering unverified)
- **OSC.** Director David Lorch (since November 2025) is a former Cerberus managing director and is also a senior adviser to Deputy Secretary Feinberg, Cerberus's co-founder.
- **EDU/BOND.** George Kollitides heads the Economic Defense Unit, which runs BOND; he does not run OSC (C74). He is a former Cerberus MD and a former partner at Alvarez & Marsal Capital.
  - He joined the board of D. Boral Acquisition I six days after his February 4, 2026 appointment. He resigned "for personal reasons" effective September 23, 2026 (8-K, per C74).
  - On that board he sat with Kevin McGurn, then CEO of the Trump sons' New America Acquisition I (he resigned effective August 5, 2026) and TMTG interim CEO from April 21, 2026 (Bloomberg Government, per C74). This is a shared board seat, not evidence of a transaction.
  - Alvarez & Marsal Federal won no-bid DoD work worth up to ~$350M, of which about $31M has been paid. ProPublica did not establish that Kollitides chose the firm.
- **Feinberg.** He placed his Cerberus interests in trusts for his adult children and kept a Cerberus services contract. Warren counts at least four Cerberus-related firms among Golden Dome awardees.

### 13. Bessent (farmland documented; Citrone link single-source/advocacy)
- **Farmland.** OGE said Bessent missed his April 28, 2025 divestment deadline. About $25M of North Dakota soybean farmland remained while he led China trade talks; he says he sold it in October 2025.
- **Argentina.** Mother Jones and Popular Information report that his friend and former Soros colleague Rob Citrone (Discovery Capital) held large Argentine positions and added to them days before the $20B swap was announced. The NYT says Citrone's role is unclear. Bessent denies it was a bailout.

### 14. Patel (documented tie; no decision)
- TMTG granted Patel 25,946 shares (~$779K-840K) on January 28, 2025.
- Patel says he declined them. Mother Jones reports he had a $120K-a-year TMTG consulting role.
- We found no FBI action benefiting TMTG.

### 15. Kratsios (earlier holding documented; current status unverified; no decision)
- ProPublica's disclosure tracker lists $1M-$5M of Scale AI Class B shares from an earlier filing.
- His 2026 annual OGE report was certified compliant. A search excerpt of it shows a $5M-$25M bank cash account. The excerpt is partial, so we could not tell whether Scale shares remain. A cash-out when Meta bought 49% of Scale (June 2025) is possible, but that is our own inference.
- We found no OSTP decision benefiting Scale.

### 16. Witkoff (documented; see C14, C53)
- QIA bought the Park Lane from a Witkoff-Mubadala joint venture for about $623M in 2023.
- Aryam (Tahnoon) bought 49% of WLF. No specific envoy decision benefiting these counterparties is shown.

### 17. Barrack (single-source: Reuters, October 9, 2026, seen via syndication)
- Barrack has had business ties to Ziad Ghandour and his TI Capital since 2020, starting with a $250M venture, and disclosed a paid consulting role in 2024.
- He reportedly pressed Iraqi and Syrian officials to include TI Capital in the $15B+ Kirkuk-Baniyas pipeline. TI Capital, Chevron and Qatar's UCC Holding signed MoUs in July 2026.
- OGE has no waiver on file. Reuters found no evidence that he profits from the pipeline, and State denies any role or interest.

### 18. Boulos (role documented; no personal benefit found)
- Boulos ran SCOA Nigeria; how large it is remains disputed. He mediated the June 27, 2025 DRC minerals-for-security deal, and KoBold Metals credits him for its Manono lithium deal.
- Africa Confidential (single source) says Gentry Beach, a longtime Don Jr. friend, was a prime mover in the talks.
- The WSJ (single source, relayed; see C75) reports that Don Jr.'s lawyer sent Beach a cease-and-desist in August 2025 telling him to stop presenting himself as Don Jr.'s business partner. Beach denies receiving it.
- C75 found no Boulos or Trump-family stake in any DRC asset awarded under the framework.

### 19. Charles Kushner (nothing found)
- Coverage concerns diplomatic rows with France. We found no decision tied to his business interests.
