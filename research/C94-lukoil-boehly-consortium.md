# C94 — The Lukoil foreign-assets sale: Boehly's consortium and who is in it

**Verdict: mostly-accurate (confidence: medium).**

This is a third-layer connection check from C76 (Russia-linked ties) and C57 (the Tahnoon/UAE network). 

Most of the claim holds up:
- The bidder sequence is documented: Gunvor withdrew, Xtellus/Boehly was rejected, Carlyle's deal lapsed, and Boehly's consortium now leads.
- The consortium's membership is reported consistently by the FT and Reuters through many relays: IHC, Al Hail, Allied Investment Partners, the Al-Khayyat brothers and the DFC.
- The OFAC licence chain is documented.
- The Al-Khayyat brothers' partnership with Kushner and Ivanka Trump in Albania is now **documented**. C76 had it as single-source. The brothers confirmed it to the NYT, and CBS and OCCRP cite corporate filings.

The politically weightiest points remain **single-source** (the NYT, anonymous sourcing):
- Putin raised the sale with Witkoff and Kushner on 5 Sep 2026.
- Per a senior administration official, the two envoys "helped negotiate the terms" of the federal government's DFC stake.

No source says the Trump family, Witkoff or Kushner has a financial stake in the deal, and Witkoff's spokeswoman denies one. **As of 14 Nov 2026, no sale had been signed or licensed.** The OFAC negotiation licence ran to 21 Nov 2026. Reuters reported on 3 Nov that a share-swap deal was "expected within weeks".

Search caveat: most outlets (NYT, FT, Reuters, Bloomberg, Politico) could not be read directly. Their reporting is cited through relays such as Meduza, Kyiv Post, Kurs, Novaya Gazeta, Mediaite, IBTimes, oilandgas360 and Moscow Times. Grades below reflect the *original* reporting, not the number of relays.

## Background

- **Sanctions.** The US sanctioned Lukoil and Rosneft in Oct 2025, effective 21 Nov 2025. Lukoil put Lukoil International GmbH (LIG) up for sale. LIG is its Austrian holding company for its foreign assets, which include:
  - West Qurna-2 in Iraq (75%);
  - the Burgas refinery in Bulgaria and the Petrotel refinery in Romania;
  - filling-station networks.
- **Valuation.** Initial estimates were about $20-22B. Reuters reports the value has fallen as some assets stopped operating.
- **Iraq.** Iraq's cabinet moved to nationalise operations at West Qurna-2.

## Connections, each graded

### 1. Gunvor's bid and its withdrawal (documented, Nov 2025)
- Gunvor, the Geneva trader co-founded by Gennady Timchenko, agreed to buy LIG for about $22B in late Oct 2025.
- On 6 Nov 2025 the US Treasury posted on X that "the Kremlin's puppet, Gunvor, will never get a licence to operate and profit". Gunvor withdrew the bid, calling the statement "fundamentally misinformed and false".
- Timchenko sold his stake to Torbjörn Törnqvist in 2014. Törnqvist's stake is reported as 85% or 88%, depending on the outlet.
- **No Trump-family tie was found.** This is the one bidder the administration publicly killed.

### 2. Xtellus Partners (ex-VTB Capital US) fronted the first Boehly/Allied bid; Treasury rejected it (documented as Reuters reporting, Dec 2025)
- Xtellus is a New York investment bank formed by a 2018 management buyout of VTB Capital's US arm. VTB is a sanctioned Russian state bank.
- In early Dec 2025 Xtellus pitched a **cashless swap** to Treasury: frozen Lukoil shares held by US investors would be exchanged for LIG's assets. Per Reuters, Xtellus was advising **Todd Boehly and the UAE's Allied Investment Partners**.
- Reuters (10 Dec) reported that Lukoil favoured this bid. Reuters (15-16 Dec, four sources) then reported that Treasury rejected it, insisting Lukoil must not profit in cash or shares.
- The Nov 2026 Boehly structure (see 5) is the same swap idea, with frozen shares held in **escrow** instead.
- So Boehly's interest dates to **Dec 2025, before** the NYT's "peace-talk" framing. That cuts against the idea that the bid was born in the Witkoff/Kushner talks.

### 3. Lukoil hired former Trump campaign adviser Bryan Lanza (single-source: Politico, Dec 2025)
- Politico, relayed by Ukrainska Pravda, Euromaidan Press and Moscow Times, reported that Lanza and Mercury Public Affairs began advising Lukoil International in late 2025. Lanza was a senior adviser to Trump's 2024 campaign.
- Politico said the firm's contacts with Treasury helped win the extension from 13 Dec to 17 Jan.
- Politico also reported that the work was treated as commercial, so no FARA or LDA registration was required. We found no filing.
- Lanza, Mercury and Lukoil did not comment.

### 4. Carlyle signed, then the deal lapsed (documented)
- Lukoil announced a non-exclusive conditional deal with Carlyle on 29 Jan 2026. Kazakhstan was excluded, and no price was disclosed.
- Peskov said Russia's priority was protecting the Russian company's interests.
- Carlyle explored UAE partners in Feb 2026.
- The agreement lapsed without OFAC approval, according to LIG's annual report. Bloomberg reported on 8 Oct 2026 that Carlyle had confirmed it had expired. Relays describe Carlyle as having "withdrawn".
- **No Trump-family or administration tie to Carlyle surfaced** in our searches.

### 5. The Boehly consortium and its members (documented as FT and Reuters reporting; terms unconfirmed)
**Members:**
- **Todd Boehly** (Eldridge Industries).
- **IHC** (International Holding Company), chaired by Sheikh Tahnoon bin Zayed.
  - IHC publicly declared its interest in Lukoil's foreign assets and notified Treasury in Nov 2025 (Bloomberg, Gulf News). That makes its interest documented independently of the consortium reports.
- **Allied Investment Partners** (UAE), Boehly's partner since the Xtellus bid.
- **Al Hail Holding** (Abu Dhabi). Its CEO is Hamad Jassim Al Darwish. **We could not establish its owners**, and no source we found ties it to Tahnoon.
- **The Al-Khayyat brothers** (Syrian-born Qatari citizens; UCC Holding / Assets Group).
- **The US DFC.**

**Reported terms (FT, Sep 2026):**
- The DFC takes a "mid-teens" stake. Other relays say 10-20%.
- Boehly and the DFC would control a majority of the board.
- IHC and Allied "lead" on the Gulf side.

**Reuters (3 Nov 2026):**
- The deal is a securities swap using frozen Lukoil shares held by Western funds, including Capital Group.
- An unnamed foreign bank had already bought Lukoil paper.
- The shares go into escrow rather than to Lukoil.
- A deal was expected "within weeks". Lukoil and Treasury did not comment.

**Grade:** membership is documented as press reporting, consistent across FT, Reuters and NYT relays. The stakes, the escrow terms and the DFC's commitment are not confirmed by any filing or official announcement.

### 6. Boehly → Trump (documented, amount disputed)
- NBC's FEC analysis lists Boehly among new $1M+ donors to MAGA Inc.
- Eldridge gave $1M to the inaugural (C86).
- Novaya Gazeta describes $1M to MAGA Inc. plus $1M to the inaugural, and the NYT relays say $2M in total.
- **No source links Boehly personally to Witkoff.** Searches turned up nothing.

### 7. IHC / Tahnoon → Trump family and Kushner (documented in project; asserted by the NYT)
- Tahnoon-linked Aryam bought 49% of World Liberty Financial (C57/C06). WLF was co-founded by the Witkoffs and the Trump sons.
- Tahnoon-overseen Lunate invested in Kushner's Affinity Partners (C57).
- The NYT, via relays, describes the consortium as including "two Middle Eastern groups that have done business with Kushner or Witkoff's family". It also says Trump, his three sons, Kushner, Ivanka Trump and the Witkoff family "all have financial ties to partners in the deal", but that none has a direct stake in it.
- C76's audit notes the FT reported Tahnoon's personal role in the consortium.

### 8. Al-Khayyat brothers → Kushner and Ivanka Trump (Albania) (documented; upgraded from C76's single-source)
- Ramez Al-Khayyat confirmed the Sazan partnership to the NYT (via Balkanweb).
- A Sazan Real Estate spokesperson confirmed the brothers' involvement to CBS. CBS saw filings naming the Qatar-based Assets Group as partner.
- OCCRP cites a March 2026 Albanian registry declaration naming Moutaz and Ramez Al-Khayyat as ultimate beneficial owners.
- SPAK froze accounts of Albania Land Development, owned by the brothers, in May-June 2026 (C45 context).
- IBTimes describes a joint venture with Kushner and Ivanka Trump. Kushner calls himself a minority partner (C45).

### 9. Al-Khayyat brothers → US envoy Tom Barrack (documented, C73)
- As US envoy, Barrack attended the 29 May 2025 signing of a roughly $7B Syria power MoU led by UCC Holding (Moutaz Al-Khayyat).
- UCC is also in the Kirkuk-Baniyas pipeline consortium (C65/C73).
- See "Not connected" for the Lukoil link.

### 10. Putin raised the sale with Witkoff and Kushner, who helped shape the DFC terms (single-source: NYT, 3 Oct 2026)
- Per the NYT (three unnamed people), Putin raised the sale at a 5 Sep 2026 Kremlin meeting with Witkoff and Kushner.
- A **senior administration official** told the NYT that Witkoff and Kushner "helped negotiate the terms of the federal government's investment", aiming for "a substantial upfront payment and profits interest for the United States".
- A DFC representative said the deal would serve US economic security, foreign policy and lower energy prices.
- Per relays, the Kremlin confirmed on 21 Oct 2026 that Russia and the US were discussing the future of Lukoil's assets.
- **Denials and context:**
  - Witkoff's spokeswoman: he "has no conflict of interest and no financial stake in this matter".
  - The NYT found no indication that either envoy would profit personally.
- Earlier contacts: Kirill Dmitriev (RDIF, sanctioned) met Witkoff and Kushner on Russian energy in March 2026 (Moscow Times).
- After the NYT story, Dmitriev defended Kushner on X as a "peacemaker" (IBTimes, Daily Beast). Per IBTimes, he was replying to a Kushner post rebutting a separate allegation about an Israel deal. **We found no Kushner statement specifically on Lukoil.**

### 11. OFAC licences and deadlines (documented)
- **The GL 131 series** authorises negotiations and *conditional* agreements for LIG only. Any closing needs a separate OFAC licence, and proceeds must go to a blocked account (C76 audit).
- **Deadline history, as reported:**
  - 13 Dec 2025, extended to 17 Jan 2026;
  - then 28 Feb, 1 Apr and 30 May;
  - then 27 Jun and 25 Jul;
  - then 22 Oct;
  - on 21 Oct 2026 (Interfax; GL 131K per C76's audit), extended to **21 Nov 2026**.
- OFAC can revoke early if the parties do not work in good faith.
- Separate licences for LIG's filling stations and Bulgarian assets run to **24 Apr 2027**.
- Some relays cite a "13 December" date. That is the 2025 deadline, not a 2026 one.
- **Structural conflict, noted by commentators:** a US agency (the DFC) would invest in the buyer while another arm of the same administration (Treasury/OFAC) must licence the sale.

### 12. Outcome through 14 Nov 2026 (documented as absence)
- No signed sale, no OFAC transfer licence and no announced DFC commitment.
- Reuters (3 Nov) said a deal was expected within weeks.
- The 21 Nov negotiation licence expiry was pending at the time of writing.

## Not connected in the coverage we found

These are our own cross-references. No source we found asserts them.

- **Barrack ↔ Lukoil deal** (*inferred*). Barrack, as envoy, presided over UCC/Al-Khayyat deals in Syria. The same family sits in the Boehly consortium. No source puts Barrack in the Lukoil talks.
- **Xtellus's continuing role** (*inferred*). The Nov 2026 swap mirrors the rejected Dec 2025 Xtellus plan, plus escrow. Whether Xtellus, a former VTB unit, still advises Boehly or is the "unnamed foreign bank" buying Lukoil paper is not reported.
- **Lanza ↔ Boehly** (*inferred, co-occurrence only*). Lanza lobbied for the seller while Boehly bid. No source links them.
- **Tahnoon's double position** (*inferred*). The same principal is (a) the 49% buyer of WLF (C57) and (b) a reported lead investor in a deal whose federal terms Witkoff and Kushner reportedly shaped. Each half is sourced. The NYT notes the WLF tie but asserts no exchange.
- **Al Hail Holding ownership** (*unestablished*). We found no source tying it to Tahnoon or another royal. Do not assume it.
- **Boehly ↔ Witkoff** (*nothing found*).

## What this does and does not show

- **What it shows.** One deal combines:
  - a Trump megadonor as lead;
  - two Gulf investor groups that are already business partners of the Trump family (WLF), Kushner (Affinity, Albania) and a sitting US envoy (Barrack/UCC);
  - a US government equity stake whose terms the president's peace envoys reportedly negotiated;
  - a sale the same administration must licence;
  - an asset raised by Putin in peace talks.
- **What it does not show.** No source documents a personal financial interest for Trump, his family, Witkoff or Kushner in the transaction. No signed deal exists. The terms are unconfirmed. Boehly's interest predates the peace-talk framing by about nine months.
