# C92: The ballroom donor list in full: who gave, what they had pending

**Verdict: partly accurate (confidence: medium).**
- **The donor roster is documented.** The White House released it on 23 Oct 2025.
- **The amounts rest on one leaked spreadsheet.** The Washington Post reported it on 15 Oct 2026, and Reuters did not verify it.
- **Most of the federal matters are documented.** Many donors had one within six months of the list date.
- **Every link from a gift to a decision is our own inference.** No source asserts a quid pro quo, and the Post says its reporting does not show that any donation led to a government action.

This is a third-layer connection hunt that builds on C33, C68 and C69. It does not repeat:
- the settlement findings in C33;
- the Nvidia, Microsoft, Palantir, Meta and Amazon analysis in C68;
- the 45-day pairs in C69 (Microsoft, Lockheed, Palantir, Ripple, Nvidia, Amazon, Ergen).

**Method note.** Every direct fetch failed: the Post, WYPR/NPR, IBTimes, Daily Beast, Public Citizen and DNyuz would not resolve from this session. The findings come from search-result summaries across several outlets, so treat amounts as "WaPo per secondary outlets". About 27 searches were used.

**Timing caveat.** Gift dates are mostly unknown. For nearly every donor, only two dates are public: the 15 Oct 2025 dinner and 23 Oct 2025 list, and the 15 Oct 2026 publication of the leak. Six-month windows below are measured from the list date unless a gift date is known. A gift could fall on either side of the official act it is paired with.

## The two lists

- **October 2025 list (documented).** It names about 36-37 donors and gives no amounts. The White House said it was incomplete. Reported counts are 21 corporations plus 15-16 individuals or foundations.
  - Corporations: Altria, Amazon, Apple, Booz Allen Hamilton, Caterpillar, Coinbase, Comcast, Google, HP, Hard Rock International, Lockheed Martin, Meta, Micron, Microsoft, NextEra Energy, Palantir, Reynolds American, Ripple, T-Mobile, Tether America and Union Pacific.
  - Individuals and foundations: the Adelson Family Foundation, Charles and Marissa Cascarilla, J. Pepe and Emilia Fanjul, Harold Hamm, Benjamin Leon Jr., the Lutnick Family, the Laura & Isaac Perlmutter Foundation, Stephen Schwarzman, Konstantin Sokolov, Paolo Tiramani, and Cameron and Tyler Winklevoss (listed separately).
  - Also reported on the AP list but not re-verified in this run: Stefan Brodie, the Betty Wold Johnson Foundation, and Edward and Shari Glazer.
- **Washington Post spreadsheet (single source).** As of late September 2026 it showed:
  - 67 donations above $10,000, totalling about $333M (some outlets say $328.5M);
  - at least $77M from donors who asked for anonymity;
  - no in-kind gifts, and nothing from Trump himself.
  - Each entry is marked "won", except AT&T's, which is marked "pledged".

## Connections (sub-claims)

1. **Rocket: an FHFA approval, then a $10M pledge three weeks later.**
   - The FHFA, under Bill Pulte, approved the $9.4B Mr. Cooper takeover on 26 Aug 2025, with 20% caps at Fannie and Freddie.
   - Per the Post, a Rocket subsidiary pledged $10M about three weeks later.
   - Six months earlier, on 27 Feb 2025, the CFPB had dismissed its Rocket Homes kickback suit with prejudice.
   - Rocket was among the donors kept off the public list.
   - Grades: the approval is documented; the gift is single-source; the link is inferred. The Post sets out the timing itself and does not claim causation.
2. **Herrera: a $2.5M pledge in Sep 2025, then a pardon on 15 Jan 2026.**
   - Isabela Herrera also gave MAGA Inc. about $3.5M.
   - Her company hired Alina Habba on a reported deal worth more than $50M.
   - The White House denies any role for the donations, and Habba denies involvement in the pardon.
   - Grades: the pardon is documented; the gift is single-source; the link is inferred.
3. **Ergen/EchoStar: the gift sits between two FCC actions.**
   - Carr's letter of 8 Sep 2025 closed the buildout probe. It came hours after the $17B SpaceX spectrum deal.
   - Ergen gave $12.5M in Dec 2025, anonymously, and $12.5M in Feb 2026.
   - The FCC approved the spectrum sales on 12 May 2026.
   - **Timing cuts both ways.** The probe closed before any money moved, and the May order carried an escrow condition that EchoStar protested.
   - Grade: inferred.
4. **Winklevoss twins and the CFTC chair.**
   - The White House withdrew Brian Quintenz's nomination late on 30 Sep 2025, about three weeks before the twins appeared on the list.
   - This followed their lobbying, and Quintenz's release of Tyler Winklevoss's texts about Gemini's CFTC case.
   - Grades: the withdrawal and the lobbying are documented by several outlets. Linking them to the ballroom is inferred. Reports also cite Senate holds and Quintenz's Kalshi ties.
5. **Loeffler-Sprecher, ICE, Polymarket and Don Jr.**
   - A sitting cabinet official's household gave $5M.
   - Her husband's exchange, ICE, put up to $2B into Polymarket on 7 Oct 2025.
   - Polymarket's other strategic investor, 1789 Capital, and adviser Don Jr. had come in on 26 Aug 2025 (C25).
   - Every link in this chain is documented except the ballroom gift (single-source). The chain as a whole is inferred.
   - The federal relief for Polymarket, the closure of the DOJ and CFTC probes on 15 Jul 2025, came before all of this.
6. **Paxos (Cascarilla).**
   - The OCC approved Paxos's charter conversion on 12 Dec 2025, 50 days after its CEO and his wife were listed as donors.
   - It was one of five same-day charters, along with Ripple, Circle, BitGo and Fidelity Digital Assets.
   - Grade: inferred, with a batch explanation.
7. **Apple.**
   - Trump exempted Apple from the planned roughly 100% chip tariff on 6 Aug 2025, at the Oval Office event where Apple raised its pledge to $600B.
   - Apple's $25M gift date is unknown.
   - The DOJ antitrust case was in settlement talks as of Jul 2026 (Bloomberg; no deal announced).
   - Grade: inferred.
8. **T-Mobile, Micron and Altria.**
   - T-Mobile: the FCC approved its deals in Jul 2025, after it told Carr it was ending DEI programmes.
   - Micron: Commerce renegotiated its CHIPS award and added $275M in Jun 2025.
   - Altria: the FDA authorized its on! PLUS pouches in Dec 2025.
   - All three are documented acts within six months of the list. The links are inferred.
9. **Tether America.**
   - It hired former White House crypto official Bo Hines in Sep 2025.
   - USAT launched on 27 Jan 2026 through federally chartered Anchorage, with Cantor (the Lutnicks' former firm) as reserve custodian.
   - This is a documented structural and revolving-door link (C35, C50, C64), not a dated favour.
10. **Counter-cases (documented).**
    - Treasury cancelled all of Booz Allen's contracts on 26 Jan 2026. AP framed this as happening despite its ballroom gift.
    - The FCC opened two probes of Comcast in 2025.
    - The STB rejected Union Pacific's merger filing on 16 Jan 2026.
    - Meta, Google and Amazon each faced adverse federal action (C33, C69).
    - AT&T denies its "pledged" $5M.
11. **Appointees.**
    - Ambassadors Popolo ($1M) and Leon were named in Jan 2025, before the ballroom existed, so the timing cuts against an appointment-for-gift reading.
    - Loeffler is a sitting official.
    - Sokolov's Tripp+ appointment (Jul 2026, C70) falls about nine months after the list.

## Corrections
- Ergen's $25M came in two $12.5M tranches.
- Google's $22M is a settlement payment.
- AT&T is "pledged" and denies giving.
- The leak is a late-September 2026 snapshot and excludes in-kind gifts.
- 15 Oct 2026 is a disclosure date, not a gift date.
- No leaked amounts were found for the crypto donors. A "$5M Ripple / $5M Coinbase / $5.1M Winklevoss" figure in crypto media looks like a tally of political spending, and is unverified as ballroom giving.
- One early summary mentions an unidentified "third $25M donor", which conflicts with the four $25M names reported elsewhere.

## Denials and other side
- **The White House** calls the conflict claims "fake" and says it will pursue the leaker. It also denies that the Herrera donations played any role in the pardon.
- **The Post** does not claim causation, and does not suggest that Popolo's post resulted from his gift.
- **Companies and individuals:**
  - Nvidia says its gift was independent of business interests.
  - AT&T denies giving.
  - Habba denies any role in the pardon.
  - Gemini disputes Quintenz's account.
- Several official acts were general or batch decisions: the tariff exemption for US investors, the OCC charter batch, and the renegotiation of all CHIPS awards.
- Public Citizen's tallies of contracts and enforcement are advocacy figures:
  - Nov 2025: 14 of 24 corporate donors faced federal enforcement or had it suspended.
  - Jun 2026: about $50B in new contracts went to 14 of 27 donors.
