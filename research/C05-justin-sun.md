# C05: Justin Sun, World Liberty Financial and the SEC

**Verdict: mostly accurate** (confidence: high)

Every factual claim in this segment checks out, with three gaps. The $75M was two separate purchases: $30M in November 2024 and $45M in January 2025. "Charged with fraud" refers to a civil SEC complaint, not a criminal charge. The settlement was proposed in March 2026, more than a year after Trump took office. Saying the SEC deal followed Sun's investment is accurate on the timing. That the investment caused the deal is an insinuation, and there is no direct evidence for it.

## What the video says (transcript lines 281-303)
- Justin Sun invested $75 million in World Liberty Financial (WLF).
- At the time, the SEC had charged him with fraud.
- He became an adviser to WLF.
- After Trump took office, the SEC dismissed its case against Sun and settled with his company.
- Everyone involved denies corruption.
- Sun later broke with WLF and accused it of treating the crypto community as a "personal ATM" and of an "absurd governance scam." WLF denies this.
- Lawsuits have gone both ways.
- Sun was WLF's first champion and its largest public investor.

## Sub-claims

1. **$75M investment: confirmed, with nuance.**
   - On Nov 25, 2024, Sun announced a $30M WLFI token purchase and called himself the largest investor. On-chain data tied the purchase to HTX, an exchange he is closely associated with. [1][2]
   - On about Jan 19-20, 2025, the eve of Trump's inauguration, Sun announced that TRON DAO would add $45M, bringing the total to $75M. [3][4]
   - Sun himself cites $75M (Sept 2025). [5]
   - So the $75M was two tranches, and the second came after the election.

2. **Charged with fraud by the SEC at the time: confirmed.**
   - On Mar 22, 2023, the SEC sued Sun, Tron Foundation, BitTorrent Foundation and Rainberry Inc. (formerly BitTorrent, wholly owned by Sun). [6][7]
   - The SEC alleged:
     - unregistered offers and sales of TRX and BTT;
     - fraud and market manipulation, including more than 600,000 TRX wash trades;
     - paid celebrity promotion that was not disclosed.
   - This is a civil enforcement case, not a criminal charge. Sun said the complaint lacked merit.

3. **Became an adviser to WLF: confirmed.** WLF named Sun an adviser on Nov 26, 2024, the day after his $30M purchase. [2][8]

4. **After Trump took office, the SEC dismissed the case against Sun and settled with his company: confirmed.**
   - On Feb 26, 2025, the SEC and Sun jointly asked the court to stay the case to "explore a potential resolution." This was about five weeks after the inauguration. [9]
   - On Mar 5, 2026, the SEC filed a proposed final judgment (Lit. Rel. No. 26496) under which:
     - Rainberry pays a $10M civil penalty on a wash-trading claim under Securities Act §17(a)(3), without admitting or denying it;
     - all claims against Sun, Tron Foundation and BitTorrent Foundation are dismissed. [10][11][12]
   - Some outlets reported that a final judgment was entered; others said court approval by Judge Ramos in S.D.N.Y. was still pending. I could not confirm entry from the docket, so this point is unverified.
   - Former SEC chief of staff Amanda Fischer criticized the deal and urged the judge to reject it. [12]

5. **Sun broke with WLF and accused it of treating the crypto community as a "personal ATM" and of an "absurd governance scam": confirmed.**
   - In September 2025, WLF blacklisted Sun's wallet and froze his WLFI tokens. [5]
   - In mid-April 2026, Sun called a WLF token-lockup governance proposal "one of the most absurd governance scams I have ever seen." [13]
   - In a separate post, Sun accused the WLFI team of extracting fees, implanting backdoor controls, freezing investor funds and treating "the crypto community as a personal ATM." [14][15]

6. **WLF denies the accusations: confirmed.**
   - WLF said Sun's "favorite move is playing the victim while making baseless allegations to cover up his own misconduct."
   - CEO Zach Witkoff called Sun's legal claims "entirely meritless." [15][16]

7. **Lawsuits in both directions: confirmed.**
   - About Apr 22, 2026, Sun sued WLF in federal court in N.D. Cal. (*Sun et al. v. World Liberty Financial LLC*, No. 3:26-cv-03360). He alleges an unlawful token freeze, threats to "burn" his tokens, and exclusion from governance. [16][17]
   - On May 4, 2026, WLF sued Sun for defamation in Miami-Dade circuit court. WLF alleges a smear campaign, straw purchases and a short-selling campaign against WLFI. Sun called it a "meritless PR stunt." [18][19]
   - I found no rulings in either case.

8. **Sun was the initial champion and largest public investor: largely confirmed.**
   - Sun described himself as the largest investor in November 2024.
   - Bloomberg reported that WLF's token sale was "a bust" until Sun stepped in. [1][20]
   - The video's next point, that a larger hidden investor existed (Tahnoon/Aryam), belongs to C06.

## Corrections and nuance
- The $75M was not one investment. It was $30M in November 2024 plus $45M in January 2025, and the second tranche came through TRON DAO.
- "Charged with fraud" means a civil SEC complaint that included antifraud and market-manipulation counts. It was not a criminal charge.
- The SEC first moved to pause the case in February 2025. The settlement and dismissal came in March 2026, not right after the inauguration.
- The settling entity was Rainberry. It paid $10M without admitting wrongdoing, and the claims against Sun personally were dropped.
- Whether the court has formally entered the judgment is unclear from the sources I could reach.

## Denials and context
- Sun, WLF and the administration deny any quid pro quo. There is no direct evidence linking Sun's WLF purchases to the SEC's decision.
- Under the Trump administration, the SEC dropped or paused many crypto cases, including Coinbase, Binance and Kraken. The Sun resolution fits that broader shift, though Sun's ties to WLF make it unusually conspicuous.
- Sun's falling-out with WLF began after his tokens were frozen. Each side accuses the other of misconduct, and those accusations are allegations, not findings.
