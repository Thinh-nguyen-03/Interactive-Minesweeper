# C111 Jared Kushner, Affinity Partners & Israel

**Verdict: mostly-accurate. Confidence: medium.** (Checked 17 Nov 2026.)

## The claim
What has Affinity Partners invested in Israeli companies, with what money and when; what other Israel ties do Kushner and his family business have; and how do they overlap in time with his role in Gaza, hostage and regional diplomacy since January 2025? This report states overlaps in time only.

## Summary
The Israeli investments are documented in company-side reporting (Globes, Calcalist) and CNN's 1 Oct 2026 investigation. Affinity holds a 15% stake in Shlomo Group's vehicle and credit arm (signed Sept 2023, closed 1 Feb 2024 at about $110M) and was Phoenix Financial's largest shareholder (9.9% from 15 Jan 2025) until a 2.5% sale on 28 Jul 2026 for about NIS 1.05B ($343M), leaving 7.4%. His role in the Oct 2025 ceasefire, the Board of Peace and other talks is documented, as are his unpaid, undisclosed status and the CREW suit. The investments and the role overlap in time. No source shows that one affected the other; CNN says it found no indication of that, and Kushner, his lawyer and the White House deny any link. No authority has made a finding.

**Phoenix's holdings.** CNN used Israeli disclosure rules to show Phoenix has invested more than $456M in nine companies that supply Israel's military, about $265M of it in Elbit Systems (figures from the Garcia letter; CNN's own text gives "hundreds of millions" in Elbit and "at least eight other companies"). The records cover only a fraction of Phoenix's portfolio. Some stakes pre-date Affinity, others came after. Affinity holds none of these companies directly. Phoenix declined to comment on the findings. Owning defence shares through an insurer's portfolio is ordinary for an Israeli institution; the data show what Phoenix holds, not why.

**Other Israeli investments.** Nothing else was found; Affinity is a private adviser and does not publish holdings (it told CNN's reporters it had made 28 investments in all). Globes reported an earlier attempt to buy 25% of Phoenix's insurance agencies; no completed deal was found.

## Where the money came from
- Affinity's capital: Saudi PIF $2B (2021), QIA and Lunate about $1.5B (Dec 2024); about $6.16B regulatory assets and about 99% non-US clients per Form ADV (see C15, C99). Foreign management fees are in C99 (Senate Finance, Sept 2024). The Garcia-Wyden letter of 19 Mar 2026 estimates more than $60M of foreign fees in 2025, including $39M from Saudi Arabia; that is the Democrats' estimate.
- Gulf approval: Globes (Sept 2023) says Saudi Arabia approved the Shlomo investment. No document or on-record statement from PIF was found. For Phoenix, Globes reported only that Israeli approval was uncertain because Affinity is a "Saudi" fund; no report says a Gulf investor approved or financed it. No source traces any Israeli purchase to a particular Gulf investor's money; the fund's capital is pooled.
- Kushner's stated rationale: Phoenix was a bet on Israel's economy ("long-term bullish on Israel", July 2024); he later called it Affinity's "best investment" and told a podcast in Sept 2025 that his Middle East investors "are now looking at Israel" (CNN, Forbes via CNN).

## Other Kushner family ties to Israel
All pre-date the second term. Menora Mivtachim put about $30M into Kushner Companies' Maryland apartments in 2017 before his Israel trip (NYT via the Forward, Jan 2018; Menora said his role "didn't make us do the deal"). Kushner Companies has borrowed from Bank Hapoalim and Bank Leumi and had equity from Psagot and Harel (Globes), and in Dec 2020 filed for a $100M TASE bond sale. No 2025-26 Israeli loan or investment in Kushner Companies was found; the company is private, so this is a "nothing found", not a denial.

## His diplomatic role and status
- Started as an unofficial adviser in May 2025 (CNN). Sept 2025: Oval Office call with Netanyahu after the Doha strike; the 20-point plan published 29 Sept. Oct 2025: Sharm el-Sheikh talks with Witkoff, including a direct meeting with Hamas negotiators (Axios); ceasefire in effect 10-11 Oct, summit 13 Oct 2025.
- 22 Jan 2026: "New Gaza" masterplan at Davos. Board of Peace named mid-Jan 2026 with Kushner on the executive board; first meeting 19 Feb 2026, where he said volunteers are not "personally profiting".
- Also Iran, Russia-Ukraine and Lebanon talks (C15, Raskin letters).
- Status: the White House says he is an unpaid volunteer with no government salary who pays his own expenses; he has filed no disclosure. CREW sued on 14 Oct 2026 (D.D.C. 26-cv-3580) arguing the Appointments Clause and ethics-disclosure law apply; his lawyer calls the suit meritless and says ethics lawyers found his arrangements compliant. No ruling found.

## Complaints and responses
- 19 Mar 2026: Wyden and Garcia letters to Affinity and the White House (fundraising, foreign fees). No reply found.
- 17 Apr 2026: Raskin (House Judiciary) opens a probe; 25 Aug 2026: says Kushner never replied and Affinity ignored staff follow-up, expands to Albania. Affinity's chief legal officer said Kushner "has complied with all applicable laws and requirements" (reported via search excerpt).
- 5 Oct 2026: Garcia to Kushner on Phoenix, with six requests due 19 Oct (foreign investments since 20 Jan 2025, Affinity-Phoenix transactions, Phoenix's military investments, Israel and Gaza visits, full disclosure). No public reply found.
- 14 Oct 2026: CREW suit.
- Responses: Kushner (2 Oct, X): "deeply misleading", Phoenix is "not a defense company", "I have no role in deciding what Phoenix buys or sells", the investment pre-dates Trump's re-election and his return to public service (true of the July 2024 tranche; the second tranche was approved on 15 Jan 2025, after the election), and CNN's own text says it "found no indication that Kushner's actions as a diplomat directly affected the investments reviewed" (CNN also says the records "include only a fraction of Phoenix's holdings" and that Affinity "doesn't directly hold a stake in the nine companies"). The wording of his lawyer's statements comes from secondary coverage (CNN is paywalled): he said Kushner "regularly spoke with Phoenix management about the company, broader market trends and potential strategic opportunities", less often now, and that "any suggestion that Jared's diplomatic work influenced Phoenix's investments ... or vice versa, is incorrect". His lawyer: never directed Phoenix's decisions, never on its board, but "regularly spoke with Phoenix management", less since his volunteer work. White House (Anna Kelly): a "tired narrative"; his business "has nothing to do with his diplomatic engagements". CNN: "We stand by our reporting."
- All congressional letters are from minority Democrats without subpoena power. None is a finding.

## Allegations that go beyond the record
- That his diplomacy influenced Phoenix's investments, or that he profited from the Gaza war: unverified; CNN found no indication; no one has shown a link.
- That Gulf money specifically funded the Israeli deals, or that Gulf funds directed them: not shown (Saudi approval of Shlomo is a single Globes statement).
- "Criminal", "on the payroll of foreign governments" and "financial pawn" (Garcia-Wyden, Raskin letters): characterisations; no charge or finding exists.
- Affinity's "look-through" share of Phoenix's $456M is a fraction (7-10%); some readings treat the whole sum as Affinity's.
