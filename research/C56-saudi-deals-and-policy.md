# C56: Saudi Arabia, family deals alongside US policy

**Verdict: mostly-accurate** (confidence: medium)

The claim describes two parallel tracks: Trump-family business with Saudi-linked parties, and major US-Saudi government decisions. Both are well documented and overlap closely in time. The claim overreaches if it implies a demonstrated link. Several deals predate the second term, LIV host fees are undisclosed, and no evidence ties any specific policy action to the family's business. The connection rests on timing, shared counterparties (PIF, chaired by MBS), and Trump's misleading claim that the family did "very little" Saudi business.

This builds on C31 (the Dar Global deals and licence fees), C15 (Kushner, Affinity and PIF's $2B) and C08 (the UAE chip-deal pattern). Facts established there are not repeated.

## Sub-claims

1. **Dar Global and Diriyah deals run in parallel with policy: confirmed.** Jeddah (2024) and the first Riyadh announcement (Dec 2024) predate the term. Trump Plaza Jeddah (Sep 2025) and the Diriyah launch (Jan 2026) fall inside it. The family's income is licence fees, about $38M across Gulf projects in 2025 per The National (C31). The site's master developer is Diriyah Company, which PIF lists in its own portfolio (audit; see C31's audit for the master-plan link).
2. **PIF-funded LIV events at Trump courses: confirmed.** Doral (Apr 2025), Trump National DC (May 2026, title sponsor Ma'aden) and Bedminster (Aug 2026). Host fees go to the clubs and are folded into club revenue in Trump's disclosures, so the amounts are unknown. PIF is exiting LIV after 2026.
3. **Kushner's PIF money and the EA buyout: confirmed.** EA was announced on 29 Sep 2025 and closed on 4 Aug 2026. Affinity's stake is small (~1.1%, press estimate). Senators Blumenthal and Warren asked CFIUS (chaired by Bessent) for close scrutiny, alleging the structure "bets $1 billion that Mr. Kushner can deliver federal approval." That is an allegation. I found no public record of the CFIUS outcome; one outlet (Gameluster) says final approvals came by 30 Jul 2026.
4. **The 18 Nov 2025 visit produced F-35 approval, MNNA status, a nuclear framework and a $1T pledge: confirmed.** These were announcements. Audit: MNNA status was formalized by Presidential Determination No. 2026-03 (13 Jan 2026; Federal Register 23 Jan 2026, 91 FR 3017).
5. **F-35 sale formalized: confirmed, as a notification.** On 17 Sep 2026 Congress was notified of 48 jets worth ~$24.3B. This is not a signed contract. Some members, including Rep. Krishnamoorthi, objected. Audit: Sen. Rand Paul reportedly filed a disapproval resolution (S.J.Res. 217, 24 Sep 2026); no floor vote was found, and one specialist outlet reports the review lapsed on 17 Oct 2026 without a block, with the binding LOA not yet issued.
6. **Nuclear talks ran alongside business expansion: confirmed.** The 123 agreement was signed on 22 Jul 2026 and sent to Congress on 25 Aug. Reporting conflicts on enrichment. Reuters reported a path to enrichment. Wright says that is "just not true", and Brookings says no enrichment is permitted from the outset, but there is no UAE-style renunciation. Forbes (23 Jul 2026) tied a Trump licensing move into a crown-prince-backed project to the same period; a Trump Organization representative said the deal was not finalized while the president was negotiating. The Forbes original could not be read directly; I read it through Forbes Italia and Spain excerpts.
7. **AI-chip sales to Humain: confirmed.** The Nvidia framework came in May 2025 and the Commerce licences on 19 Nov 2025, the day after the visit, with the same allowance to the UAE's G42.
8. **Khashoggi remarks: confirmed.** "Things happen, but he knew nothing about it, and we can leave it at that." That contradicts the US intelligence assessment that MBS approved the operation. MBS called the killing a "huge mistake."
9. **A link between the deals and the policy decisions: unverified.** No document, testimony or official finding shows a trade. The overlap in timing and counterparties is real. The Saudi asks (F-35s, a 123 deal, chips) long predate the business deals.

## Corrections
- The Dar Global Jeddah tower and the first Riyadh announcement predate the second term; only later projects "proceeded alongside" the Nov 2025 decisions.
- The F-35 "approval" of 18 Nov 2025 was formalized only as a congressional notification on 17 Sep 2026.
- Trump's claim that the family had done "very little" business with Saudi Arabia was contradicted by Forbes's Dan Alexander on NPR. Forbes estimated ~$50M from Saudi-linked deals in 2024 (figure via secondary reporting).
- Affinity's EA share is ~1.1%; PIF owns the bulk.

## Denials and context
- **Karoline Leavitt (May 2025):** the idea that Trump acts for his own benefit is "ridiculous"; he "is abiding by all conflict of interest laws." The White House also cites the trust run by his children and the 2025 ethics agreement, which allows deals with private foreign firms.
- **Trump (18 Nov 2025):** "I have nothing to do with the family business." In May 2025 he said he did not discuss LIV in Saudi Arabia.
- **Trump Organization, via Forbes:** the Diriyah-related deal was not finalized while the nuclear deal was being negotiated.
- **Critics:** AEI's Timothy Carney says Trump still profits when the company does well. Blumenthal and Warren raise the EA concerns above.
- **Context cutting the other way:** LIV's use of Trump courses began in 2022, when Trump was out of office; G42 received the same chip approval the same day as Humain; Saudi pursuit of a 123 agreement spans several administrations.
