# C40: Insiders behind the $TRUMP and $MELANIA launches (Hayden Davis, Meteora, Kelsier)

**Verdict: mostly-accurate** (confidence: medium)

The claim is accurate as a description of what was **alleged** and what on-chain analysts **reported**. A Burwick Law class action did accuse Meteora co-founder Benjamin Chow and Hayden Davis (Kelsier) of running MELANIA, LIBRA and other tokens as a coordinated pump-and-dump. It called Melania Trump and Javier Milei "props" and attributed about $10M in insider gains to MELANIA. Davis himself admitted he was the "launch strategist" for LIBRA and MELANIA and that his team sniped both.

The legal track has gone the other way. Judge Jennifer Rochon (SDNY) dismissed the LIBRA/M3M3 suit **with prejudice** on Sept 29, 2026, and refused as futile to add MELANIA. An Argentine federal judge closed the LIBRA criminal case against Milei, his sister and Davis in October 2026. No court has found fraud. No US regulator (SEC, DOJ or CFTC) has brought any action; the only moves are letters from Democratic senators.

On $TRUMP, the operators (Fight Fight Fight LLC / Bill Zanker and CIC Digital), Meteora's role as launch venue, the fee and sale revenue estimates and the early-buyer windfalls are all documented. No source puts Davis or Kelsier in charge of $TRUMP.

## The claim

This check builds on C01/C02. It claims that:
- a class action and on-chain reporting allege that $MELANIA and $LIBRA were run by Davis/Kelsier with Chow as a pump-and-dump, with insiders taking about $10M+ and public figures used as "props";
- this check also asks who operated $TRUMP, what early-wallet sniping and trading-fee revenue amounted to, and the status of litigation and regulatory action.

## Sub-claims

### 1. A class action alleged that Chow/Meteora and Davis/Kelsier ran MELANIA and LIBRA as a pump-and-dump, using Melania Trump and Milei as "props". Confirmed (as allegation)
- The case is *Hurlock et al. v. Kelsier Ventures et al.*, SDNY No. 1:25-cv-03891-JLR. It was filed by Burwick Law in New York state court in March 2025 over LIBRA and removed to federal court.
- On Oct 21, 2025, plaintiffs filed a proposed second amended complaint (Dkt 205-2). It named Benjamin Chow, Meteora, Kelsier Ventures, Hayden Davis, Gideon Davis and Charles Davis.
- The amended complaint alleged a scheme across at least 15 tokens and focused on five: LIBRA, MELANIA, ENRON, TRUST and M3M3. It said Chow assembled trusted collaborators, including the Davis family acting through Kelsier.
- It described the celebrity figures as "props" used to legitimize "a coordinated liquidity trap" (Decrypt, Fortune, The Block).
- Melania Trump was **not** a defendant. The filing does not allege that she did anything wrong.

### 2. Insiders took about $10M+ from MELANIA. Mostly accurate (figures are allegations and analyst estimates)
- Coverage of the complaint says plaintiffs allege that insider wallets tied to Chow and Davis took about **$10M** from the MELANIA collapse alone. One outlet put total alleged fraud across tokens at about $107M.
- Separate on-chain reporting gives larger numbers:
  - **Bubblemaps (Apr 2025):** about 50M MELANIA (about $30M) was moved out of "community" wallets and sold with no explanation. Bubblemaps also tied an earlier about $2M single-sided-liquidity extraction to Davis, using the same technique as in LIBRA.
  - **EmberCN, via BeInCrypto:** the team sold about 23M tokens (about $14.75M) in one month.
- Davis said in a recorded interview in February 2025: "The MELANIA team made no money. We withdrew no liquidity." He later reframed this when shown the on-chain data.
- No court has tested any of these figures. "$10M+" fairly summarizes the complaint, and the on-chain estimates run higher.

### 3. Hayden Davis/Kelsier ran the MELANIA and LIBRA launches. Confirmed (by his own admission); criminal wrongdoing unproven
- In February 2025 Davis said in a recorded interview, as reported by CoinDesk, that he was the launch strategist for LIBRA and MELANIA and that his team sniped both tokens when the contracts went live.
- Bubblemaps' wallet clustering linked the LIBRA creators to MELANIA and to other short-lived tokens.
- Chow resigned as Meteora CEO on Feb 18, 2025. Meteora co-founder Meow (Jupiter) said Fenwick & West would investigate. No published report from that investigation was found.
- Chow denies receiving tokens or having inside information on LIBRA.
- MELANIA's issuer of record is MKT World LLC (see C02). Davis's admitted role was launch strategist, not owner.

### 4. Who actually operated $TRUMP. Confirmed
- $TRUMP's issuers are **Fight Fight Fight LLC** and **CIC Digital LLC**, which together hold 80% of supply.
  - Fight Fight Fight is run by **Bill Zanker** and owned by **Celebration Cards LLC**, per the Warren/Schiff/Blumenthal letter of Apr 8, 2026.
  - CIC Digital is a Trump Organization affiliate.
- The token launched on **Meteora**, and its creators receive a share of Meteora trading fees (Bloomberg via BNN). Chow said he did not personally deal with the team, which reached out to co-founder Meow.
- Arkham reported that three TRUMP-team wallets (the developer address and two first-day liquidity providers) received about $4.2M in Meteora's MET airdrop in October 2025. Two MELANIA-launch wallets received about $1.2M.
- **No source shows Davis or Kelsier operating $TRUMP.** The only link is a second-hand report of Davis describing a pre-launch "Trump-associated" token sale at a DC dinner. That claim is unverified.

### 5. Early-wallet sniping profits. Mostly accurate (estimates vary widely)
- **MELANIA:** the FT reported that 24 wallets spent about $2.6M roughly 2.5 minutes before the announcement and took nearly $100M. Lookonchain said three wallets bought more than 420M tokens minutes before DEX listing. CryptoSlate cited more than $150M including pre-sale trades. Davis's admission covers his own team's sniping.
  - A TipRanks item says Arkham linked one early wallet to an address "once used by Barron Trump." This is **unverified** and not corroborated by a major outlet.
- **TRUMP:** Chainalysis data (via CNBC, May 2025; see C01) showed 58 wallets made more than $10M each. DL News profiled one buyer who turned $1M into about $304M unrealized. One sniper says he made about $100M (Decrypt).
  - No credible source shows that $TRUMP's operators sniped their own launch. Totals such as "$1.2B to 45 insiders" or "$4B" come from low-quality aggregators and are unverified.

### 6. Trading-fee and sale revenue. Confirmed (as estimates)
- **First two weeks of fees** (Jan 17–30, 2025): about $86M (Merkle Science), about $94M (Chainalysis), about $100M (a third firm).
- **FT, March 2025:** at least **$350M** for the operating entities, made up of $314M in token sales plus $36M in fees.
- **CoinDesk/Chainalysis, May 2025:** about $320M in fees, with about 5% going to Meteora.
- **Later liquidity withdrawals:** Lookonchain and Arkham, via Cointelegraph, reported TRUMP-team wallets pulling about $94M USDC from Meteora pools in December 2025 (about $33M on Dec 31) and sending it to Coinbase.
- **Trump's own filing:** his 2025 OGE disclosure reports $635M in royalties via CIC Digital (C01). That is a different measure and should not be added to the figures above.

### 7. Status of the litigation. Confirmed (it collapsed)
- **Aug 19, 2025:** Rochon lifted the May 2025 TRO that had frozen about $57.6M USDC. She found the defendants were not "evasive actors" and voiced doubt that the suit would succeed.
- **Sept 29, 2026:** Rochon dismissed the LIBRA/M3M3 class action **with prejudice**:
  - RICO failed because six months did not show a "substantial period" for a pattern of racketeering.
  - State claims against the Kelsier defendants failed for lack of personal jurisdiction.
  - Claims against Chow failed for pleading defects.
  - Meteora was not shown to be a suable unincorporated association.
  - Leave to add MELANIA, ENRON and TRUST was denied as futile.
- The ruling made **no finding** on whether fraud or manipulation occurred. As of early October 2026, no notice of appeal to the Second Circuit had been reported.

### 8. Regulatory action. Partly accurate: none by regulators
- **SEC:** staff said in February 2025 that meme coins are generally not securities, while noting that fraud can still be pursued by other agencies.
- **SEC, DOJ, CFTC:** no enforcement action against Davis, Kelsier, Chow, Meteora, Fight Fight Fight or MKT World was found. Argentine reports of a US DOJ probe into LIBRA are unconfirmed.
- **Congress:**
  - Blumenthal wrote to Zanker on May 6, 2025, citing $350M in fees.
  - Warren, Schiff and Blumenthal wrote to Zanker on Apr 8, 2026 about the Mar-a-Lago "Crypto & Business Conference."
  - Warren and Blumenthal asked SEC Chair Paul Atkins to investigate $TRUMP in August 2026 (The Block, CNN).
  - These are letters from minority-party senators, not subpoenas. One aggregator's "subpoena" wording is not supported.
- **Argentina:**
  - A congressional commission report in November 2025 said Milei may have breached the Public Ethics Law.
  - An Argentine judge froze funds linked to Davis in late 2025.
  - In October 2026, Judge Marcelo Martínez de Giorgi dismissed the criminal case against Milei, Karina Milei, Davis and others for lack of evidence of a specific deception. Opposition lawmakers are contesting the closure.

## Corrections
- The claim says the class action alleged a MELANIA pump-and-dump. That is true, but MELANIA was only in a **proposed** amended complaint. The court never allowed it into the case and later rejected it as futile.
- The "$10M+" figure comes from the complaint. On-chain estimates of team sales run higher (about $30M per Bubblemaps). FT-reported sniper profits (about $100M) are a separate category from team extraction.
- The litigation did not produce any finding. The SDNY case is dismissed with prejudice, and the Argentine criminal case was dismissed in October 2026.

## Denials and other side
- **Davis:** denies wrongdoing. He calls LIBRA "a plan gone miserably wrong" rather than a rug pull, said the MELANIA team "made no money," and said sniping "is not illegal" in the meme market.
- **Chow:** denies leaking information or receiving tokens. **Meow** said there was "no financial impropriety."
- **Meteora:** co-lead Soju defended the airdrop.
- **Fight Fight Fight:** has not publicly answered the senators' letters. Zanker told CoinDesk that Trump Mobile is not affiliated with his memecoin company.
- **Melania Trump and the White House:** no comment found on the lawsuit (Fortune got no response).
- **Context:** meme coins are legal products. Sniping and single-sided liquidity sales are aggressive tactics but have not been ruled illegal in any of these cases.
