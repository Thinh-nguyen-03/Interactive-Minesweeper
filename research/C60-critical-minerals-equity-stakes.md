# C60: Government equity stakes and critical-minerals deals: who was positioned to profit

**Verdict: mostly-accurate** (confidence: medium)
Origin: follow-up lead (parents: C26, C47, C50, C51)

## Summary

The core of the lead is documented. On 2026-06-28 the New York Times published an investigation by Eric Lipton and Paul Sonne (syndicated by the Boston Globe). It counted **at least 14 critical-minerals companies** with financial ties to the Trump or Lutnick families that had received, or were being considered for, **more than $8.9B** in federal support. Senators Blumenthal, Schiff and Warren built their 2026-07-30/31 letters on those findings.

The best-documented instances:
- **1789 Capital and Vulcan Elements**
- **Dominari / American Ventures and the Kazakh tungsten project**, through Skyline Builders and Kaz Resources. Cantor also reportedly raised $210M for a partner in the project.
- **Cantor as lead placement agent on USA Rare Earth's $1.5B private raise**, announced the same day as a $1.6B Commerce letter of intent.

Cantor's links to The Metals Company and Critical Metals are also real.

The brief overreaches in two places:
- **MP Materials, Lithium Americas and Intel are not on the reported list of 14.** No Trump- or Lutnick-family position in them was found.
- **Family gains are largely unquantified, and no evidence shows influence over any decision.** The White House, Commerce, Cantor and the Trump family deny wrongdoing.

## What the brief says

The brief says the administration took equity stakes in, or gave loans or price floors to, MP Materials, Lithium Americas, Trilogy Metals, Vulcan Elements, Intel, USA Rare Earth and others. It says reporting (NYT, FT, Bloomberg) links Trump-family or Lutnick-family firms, as investors or advisers, to a dozen or more of those companies. The firms named are 1789 Capital, Cantor Fitzgerald, and Dominari/American Ventures (through Skyline and Cove Kaz).

## Findings by sub-claim

1. **The NYT found family-linked firms in a dozen-plus federally backed minerals companies: confirmed (as reporting).** The NYT count is at least 14 companies and more than $8.9B. Secondary coverage lists them as:
   - Kaz Resources
   - USA Rare Earth
   - Perpetua Resources
   - ASP Isotopes
   - Critical Metals
   - Vulcan Elements
   - The Metals Company
   - Trilogy Metals
   - American Ocean Minerals
   - Guardian Metal Resources
   - Titan Mining
   - Westwater Resources
   - ReElement / American Resources Corp.
   - Energy Fuels

   According to the Times, Cantor (run by Brandon and Kyle Lutnick) frequently served as adviser, underwriter or placement agent to these companies. Most of the $8.9B is commitments, letters of interest and conditional loans, not money disbursed.
2. **1789 Capital and Vulcan Elements: confirmed.** See C26. 1789 invested in Vulcan's $65M Series A in August 2025. The $1.4B package followed on 2025-11-03, including a $620M conditional loan from the Pentagon's Office of Strategic Capital (OSC) and $50M from Commerce. ProPublica reported that Navarro initiated the government deal. 1789 and Don Jr. deny any role.
3. **Dominari / American Ventures and Kaz Resources: mostly-accurate.** See C47. The sons reportedly bought into Skyline in August 2025. Then:
   - 2025-09-22: Tokayev reportedly tells Trump that Cove Kaz will get the project.
   - 2025-10-31: Skyline agrees to take 20% of Kaz Resources.
   - 2025-11-06: the deal is signed.
   - Non-binding letters of interest follow: up to $900M from the Export-Import Bank (EXIM) and about $700M from the US International Development Finance Corporation (DFC).
   - May 2026: Cove Kaz seeks about $400M more from OSC.

   The NYT adds that Cantor helped raise $210M for one of the project's investment partners. The partner and Cantor's fee were not identifiable in the sources reachable here. SEC filings do not name the brothers. A Trump family lawyer called the NYT report "libelous".
4. **Cantor and USA Rare Earth: confirmed.** On 2026-01-26 USA Rare Earth announced a non-binding letter of intent under the Commerce Department's CHIPS program:
   - $277M in funding plus a $1.3B senior secured loan.
   - In return, 16.1M shares plus about 17.6M warrants, roughly 10% of the company (8-16% depending on warrants).
   - No price floor.

   The same day it announced a $1.5B private placement of common stock at $21.50 a share, anchored by Inflection Point. Cantor was lead placement agent and Moelis was co-agent. USA Rare Earth says Cantor had no role in the government deal. Warren, Wyden and Van Hollen wrote on 2026-02-26 and also allege that billionaire Trump allies got access to the placement shares. That allegation is unconfirmed.
5. **Cantor and The Metals Company (TMC): confirmed.** Cantor is TMC's banker. TMC sought US deep-sea mining licences after Trump's April 2025 executive order and refiled in January 2026. NOAA, which sits under Lutnick's Commerce Department, certified an exploration application, and Earthjustice has sued over it. This is a permit, not funding. No record of a Lutnick recusal was found.
6. **Cantor and Critical Metals: mostly-accurate.** The Deep Dive reports that Cantor is among the largest investors in Critical Metals, but the size of the stake was not found. Cantor initiated coverage at Speculative Buy with an $18 target on 2026-07-14. Reuters reported in October 2025 that officials had discussed a US stake. No completed stake was found.
7. **Perpetua, ASP Isotopes and Westwater: partly-accurate.**
   - **Perpetua:** EXIM advanced a loan of about $2.7-2.9B in May 2026 (reported figures vary). Cantor's documented role here is analyst coverage.
   - **ASP Isotopes:** Cantor was a joint book-runner on its $50M offering in June 2025.
   - **Westwater:** received EXIM graphite financing in August 2026.
   - For Energy Fuels, Titan, Guardian and American Ocean Minerals, neither the family tie nor the federal action could be verified here.
8. **MP Materials, Lithium Americas, Trilogy and Intel as conflicted deals: unverified.** The government actions are real:
   - MP Materials: Pentagon preferred equity, a price floor and an offtake agreement (July 2025).
   - Lithium Americas: about a 5% Department of Energy stake (October 2025).
   - Intel: about a 10% stake (August 2025).
   - Trilogy: about a 10% stake for $35.6M (2025-10-06), with warrants that could take it toward 17.5%. The stock roughly tripled.

   Only Trilogy is on the NYT list, and its specific family tie could not be confirmed. The MP, Lithium Americas and Intel deal terms come from widely reported 2025 announcements and were not re-sourced in this pass.
9. **Documented gains: unverified.** Cantor earned placement fees, but the amounts are not public. The 1789, Skyline and Kaz stakes are unrealized. No realized family profit tied to a specific government action is documented.
10. **Influence on decisions: unverified.** No document shows a Trump or Lutnick son lobbying for any of these awards. Requests for records so far:
    - House Natural Resources Democrats (February 2026)
    - Senate letters (February and July 2026)
    - FOIA requests from Citizens for Responsibility and Ethics in Washington (CREW)

    Post-midterm coverage (November 2026) says Democrats plan formal investigations.

## Corrections

- MP Materials, Lithium Americas and Intel are not among the 14 reported companies, and no family tie to them was found.
- "Dozen-plus" means "at least 14". The $8.9B is support provided or under consideration, not disbursed.
- USA Rare Earth's federal package is a non-binding letter of intent with no price floor. Cantor's role was the parallel private placement.
- Kaz Resources' $1.6B consists of non-binding EXIM and DFC letters of interest. The sons' stake in it is indirect.
- The Metals Company's link is a NOAA permit, not federal money.

## Denials and context

- **White House** (Kush Desai): "the only special interest guiding the Trump administration's decision-making is the best interest of the American people."
- **Commerce:** neither Lutnick nor anyone at the department "has interacted with or had any discussions whatsoever with Cantor Fitzgerald" about the rare-earth companies.
- **Cantor:** it acts as a capital-raising partner to the industry.
- **1789 and Don Jr.:** deny any role in the Vulcan loan.
- **Trump family lawyer:** demanded that the NYT retract its Kaz story.
- **Structural overlap:** the administration has done roughly 160 minerals deals worth about $40B (one tally), and Cantor is a dominant banker in the sector, so some overlap is to be expected.
- **Source limits:** NYT, Boston Globe, CNBC and Senate sites, among others, could not be fetched directly in this session. The web-search budget ran out before MP Materials, Lithium Americas, Intel and the four less-covered companies could be checked one by one. Those items rely on search-result summaries.
