# C87: Silver Lake, the co-investor on both the Saudi-Kushner and Abu Dhabi sides

**Verdict:** mostly-accurate (confidence: medium)
**Origin:** connection check, third layer from C68, C80 and C57.

## Summary

Silver Lake's dual position is well documented:

- **Abu Dhabi side.** Mubadala has owned a passive minority stake in Silver Lake since 2020. Silver Lake has held shares in Tahnoon's G42 since 2021, and co-CEO Egon Durban sits on G42's board. Silver Lake co-owns two US assets with Tahnoon's MGX: the TikTok US joint venture and, a link not covered before, 51% of Intel's chip unit Altera.
- **Saudi-Kushner side.** Silver Lake co-bought Electronic Arts (EA) with Saudi Arabia's Public Investment Fund (PIF) and Kushner's Affinity. Reuters reported that those talks began between Durban and Kushner. As of November 2026, Silver Lake is reportedly in takeover talks with PIF again, this time for Unity.

This check also asked about donations and administration ties. We found no documented Trump-related donations, no lobbying of the administration and no administration roles for Durban or Silver Lake. The idea that Silver Lake is a hub between the two Trump-connected Gulf networks comes from our own cross-referencing; no outlet frames it that way. Nothing we found shows coordination or an exchange of favours.

Many outlets, including Semafor, could not be read directly. Some findings therefore rest on search-result text and syndicated copies.

## Connections found

### 1. Mubadala owns part of Silver Lake (documented)

- **What happened.** On 30 Sep 2020, Mubadala bought a minority interest in Silver Lake's management company. It bought from Dyal Capital, which had held a passive, non-voting stake of under 10% since 2016 and kept about half of it.
- **The $2B strategy.** Mubadala also committed $2B to a Silver Lake strategy with a 25-year horizon. The strategy invests alongside Silver Lake's main funds.
- **What is not known.** The parties did not disclose the size of Mubadala's stake or the price. Contemporaneous wire coverage put it at under 5% (audit addition).
- **Context.** Mubadala is chaired by Mansour bin Zayed, not Tahnoon. Mubadala co-founded Tahnoon's MGX (see C57).

Sources [1–4].

### 2. Silver Lake in G42, with Durban on the board (documented; price single-source)

- **The stake.** Silver Lake bought a minority stake in G42 in April 2021. The roughly $800M price is the WSJ's figure from unnamed sources; the terms were never disclosed.
- **The board seat.** Durban's own Silver Lake biography lists G42 among his boards.
- **Current position.** Bloomberg (around September 2026) lists Silver Lake, Mubadala and Microsoft as existing G42 shareholders. G42 is holding exploratory talks on moving to majority-US ownership so it can keep licence-free chip access under the 2026 export rule from the Commerce Department's Bureau of Industry and Security (C80). No report names Silver Lake as a buyer.

Sources [5–8].

### 3. Silver Lake and MGX co-own Altera (documented; US-government link inferred)

- **The deal.** Intel's sale of 51% of Altera to a Silver Lake-led group closed on 12 Sep 2025. It valued Altera at $8.75B, or about $3.3B of equity for the stake.
- **MGX's part.** On about 16 Sep 2025, MGX announced it had joined as a co-investor. It did not disclose how much it put in. Intel kept 49%.
- **Why it matters.** This makes Altera the second US asset that Silver Lake and MGX co-own, after TikTok.
- **US government and Intel (documented).** The US government took 9.9% of Intel on 22 Aug 2025.
- **Inferred.** Through Intel, the US government indirectly holds part of the 49% of Altera that Intel kept. No source draws this link.
- **Unverified.** We found no report of a review by CFIUS (the Committee on Foreign Investment in the United States, which screens foreign investment in US companies) of MGX's entry into Altera.

Sources [9–14].

### 4. The TikTok US joint venture (documented, extends C57 and C68)

- **Ownership.** Oracle, Silver Lake and MGX each own 15%. ByteDance keeps 19.9%.
- **Approval.** Trump approved the framework by executive order on 25 Sep 2025. The deal closed on 22 Jan 2026.
- **Durban's role.** Durban holds one of the seven board seats, alongside MGX's David Scott.
- **Mubadala framing (single-source).** Some syndicated coverage described Silver Lake as having "backing from Abu Dhabi's Mubadala". We saw this only in search snippets. It is the only press framing we found that ties the two sides together.
- **Unverified.** No report describes Durban's role in the negotiations.

Sources [15–18, 7].

### 5. The EA buyout began with Durban and Kushner (single-source; the deal itself is documented)

- **How it started.** Reuters (30 Sep 2025, citing people familiar with the talks) reported that discussions began in a spring 2025 brainstorming session between Durban and Kushner. Kushner reportedly helped bring in PIF, which already held about 9.9% of EA. The parties did not confirm this account.
- **Approvals and closing.** The waiting period under US merger law (the Hart-Scott-Rodino Act) expired on 9 Feb 2026. EA's filing of 30 Jul 2026 said all approvals had been obtained, including CFIUS. The deal closed on 4 Aug 2026.
- **Ownership split.** Press estimates put the stakes at PIF about 93.4%, Silver Lake about 5.5% and Affinity about 1.1%.
- **Silver Lake and Affinity.** EA is the only documented co-investment between them. We found no Silver Lake commitment as an investor in Affinity's fund, and no shared directors or staff.

Sources [19–24].

### 6. Unity takeover talks with PIF (single-source)

- **The report.** Reuters reported on about 5 Nov 2026, citing anonymous sources, that Silver Lake and PIF held talks to take Unity private at about $25B. No deal had been signed and no party commented.
- **Silver Lake's existing position.** Silver Lake already owns about 8% of Unity, and Durban sits on its board.
- **Affinity.** No report places Affinity in this group.
- **Inferred.** A PIF-majority deal would again face a CFIUS review chaired by Treasury Secretary Bessent.

Sources [25–26].

### 7. Endeavor and TKO: Saudi, Ellison and White House ties (each link documented; the chain is inferred)

- **The take-private.** Silver Lake completed its roughly $25B take-private of Endeavor on 24 Mar 2025. Mubadala was among the co-investors, and Ari Emanuel rolled his equity into the new company.
- **TKO.** Endeavor still majority-owns TKO, the listed parent of UFC and WWE.
- **Saudi tie.** On 5 Mar 2025, TKO launched a boxing promotion with Saudi official Turki Alalshikh and Sela, a PIF subsidiary.
- **Ellison tie.** In August 2025, TKO sold seven years of UFC media rights for $7.7B to Paramount Skydance, which David Ellison runs.
- **White House tie.** On 14 Jun 2026, TKO staged UFC Freedom 250 on the White House South Lawn. TKO says it lost about $30M on the event. The White House rejected conflict-of-interest claims.
- **Inferred.** No source connects these links into one chain running back to Silver Lake.

Sources [27–33].

### 8. Donations and administration ties (not found)

- **Donations.** We found no 2024–26 donations by Durban or co-CEO Greg Mondre to Trump, MAGA Inc., the inaugural committee or the White House ballroom fund.
- **Lobbying and access.** We found no Silver Lake lobbying registration aimed at the administration and no reported Durban White House meeting.
- **Older giving.** LittleSis collects older donations by people linked to Silver Lake, including co-founder Glenn Hutchins and Durban. Most went to Democrats. We did not check these against FEC records.
- **Indirect ties that are documented.**
  - Durban sat on Twitter's board in 2022, where Fortune described him as an Elon Musk ally.
  - Durban has been a Dell director since 2013. Michael and Susan Dell pledged $6.25B to "Trump Accounts" on 2 Dec 2025, with Susan Dell appearing at the White House. That pledge is the Dells' personal giving; no source ties it to Silver Lake.

Sources [34–37].

## Corrections

- The Mubadala stake is passive and its size is undisclosed. It does not by itself give Mubadala control or influence.
- The claim misses Altera, a second co-investment by Silver Lake and MGX.
- Mubadala's chairman is Mansour bin Zayed. Its tie to Tahnoon runs through MGX.
- Some coverage of the Unity talks wrongly calls Silver Lake Kushner's firm.

## Denials and other side

- **EA.** The parties did not confirm Kushner's reported role.
- **Unity.** Unity, Silver Lake and PIF did not comment.
- **UFC event.** The White House denies any conflict of interest.
- **Silver Lake's history.** Silver Lake has co-invested with Gulf sovereign funds and with Singapore's GIC for years, so repeat partners are normal in very large deals. Nothing found shows Silver Lake brokered, benefited from or influenced any Trump-family transaction.

## Audit (second-opinion pass, Nov 2026)

Result: **upheld** (verdict and confidence unchanged).

- **Altera.** Upheld. MGX joined the Silver Lake-led group after the 2025-09-12 close; its contribution is undisclosed. The ~$3.3B is the filing-based equity figure. Intel's $4.46B headline is 51% of the $8.75B valuation.
- **Mubadala in Silver Lake.** Upheld. It was a secondary purchase from Dyal's passive under-10% stake. Wire coverage put it at under 5%.
- **Unity.** Upheld as single-source (Reuters, 5 Nov 2026, people familiar). Silver Lake owns ~8%, and about $25B was discussed. No deal by mid-Nov 2026.
- **Labels.** The "hub" pattern and the Altera-US government link remain labelled inferred.
