# C102: Trades by officials, family and associates

**Verdict:** mostly-accurate (confidence: medium)

**Question:** What trades by officials, family members and associates around government actions are documented, and which allegations go beyond the record?

## Summary

Disclosure filings and on-record reporting document many well-timed trades around tariff and policy news:

- **Attorney General Pam Bondi** sold $1M-$5M of Trump Media shares plus $250K-$500K of warrants on 2 April 2025, the day of the "Liberation Day" tariffs. Her ethics agreement required the sale; the date is what drew attention.
- **Dan Scavino** (deputy chief of staff) sold $1M-$5M of Trump Media stock on 1 April 2025, and **Sergio Gor** sold smaller holdings on 27 March (USA Today; single-source).
- **Transportation Secretary Sean Duffy** sold about 34 stocks ($90K-$650K) on 11 and 13 February 2025, around the reciprocal-tariff plan, **not in April**.
- **ProPublica** found more than a dozen officials and aides with well-timed sales, including State Department official Gautam Rana ($830K-$1.7M on 19 March 2025).
- **Trump's own accounts** made 327 purchases (up to about $12.8M) on 8 April 2025, the day before the tariff pause. They were disclosed only in July 2026, more than 14 months late. His accounts also bought at least $82M of corporate and municipal bonds between late August and early October 2025.
- **Members of Congress** traded heavily around the 9 April pause: Greene ($21K-$315K of purchases), Moskowitz ($20K-$300K), Bresnahan (171 trades on 8 April), Khanna (438 transactions), Hern (up to $5M in one trade). The Campaign Legal Center counted 2,200+ trades by 53 members.
- **Small caps:** pre-announcement run-ups are documented for Dominari and Unusual Machines and reported for ALT5 Sigma. No buyer has been identified. SEC and FINRA reportedly contacted some crypto-treasury companies, with no action known.
- **Prediction markets:** the only US charge tied to a government action is a soldier's Maduro bets. Don Jr. advises Kalshi and Polymarket, and 1789 Capital invested in Polymarket, but no family bet is documented.

What is **not** established: none of these trades has produced a charge, inspector-general finding, SEC/CFTC action or ethics-committee finding against the official, member or family member concerned. Several sales were mandated divestitures, and most filers say outside managers made the trades.

This extends C58 (market-moving posts, unidentified options and oil traders), C20 and C68 (Trump's 2026 stock trades), C65 (Bondi), C25 and C38 (prediction markets and Don Jr.), and C39 and C91 (Dominari). The investigations themselves are covered in C103.

**Evidence rule applied:** a documented, disclosed trade is not by itself evidence that anyone used non-public information. Each point separates (1) the filing, (2) what outlets infer, (3) who alleges wrongdoing, and (4) whether any trader has been identified or any authority has made a finding.

## Sub-claims

### 1. Bondi's Trump Media sale on 2 April 2025: confirmed (trade); misuse unverified
- (1) Filing: her OGE periodic transaction report, public in mid-May 2025, shows a sale of $1M-$5M of TMTG shares and $250K-$500K of warrants on 2 April 2025 [1][2]. Trump announced the tariffs after the close. The form does not show the time of day. DJT fell about 13% within days (CBS).
- (2) Outlets called the timing fortunate. (3) Rep. Raskin asked DOJ Inspector General Horowitz to investigate on 20 May 2025 [3][4]. (4) No finding was found as of Nov 2026.
- Context: her ethics agreement required divesting the stake within 90 days of taking office.
- Response: DOJ did not answer early questions. At a June 2025 House hearing Bondi said she complied with ethics rules and lost money on the stock (secondary summary [5]).

### 2. Scavino and Gor sold Trump Media stock before 2 April: mostly-accurate (single-source)
- USA Today reported that Scavino sold $1M-$5M on 1 April 2025 and Gor sold smaller holdings on 27 March [6][7]. USA Today found no evidence of advance knowledge.
- A later report says Scavino's 2025 annual disclosure shows $1M-$5M of capital gains from the holding (single-source [8]).
- Response: White House assistant press secretary Taylor Rogers said senior staff fully comply with ethics rules. This quote was seen only through a repost.

### 3. Duffy's sales: mostly-accurate (dates corrected)
- ProPublica (from OGE records) reported that Duffy sold shares in about three dozen companies on 11 and 13 February 2025, worth $90K-$650K, around the 13 February reciprocal-tariff plan [9][10].
- ProPublica said it was unclear whether he had any advance information.
- Response: the Transportation Department said an outside manager made the trades in a retirement account, Duffy "had no input on the timing", and his ethics agreement required seven of the sales.

### 4. Other officials' well-timed sales: mostly-accurate
- ProPublica reviewed hundreds of disclosures and found more than a dozen officials and aides with well-timed sales [11][12]. Examples:
  - Gautam Rana (State Department, later ambassador to Slovakia) sold $830K-$1.7M on 19 March 2025.
  - An unnamed White House lawyer sold shares in nine companies just before a tariff announcement.
- ProPublica states there is no evidence these trades used non-public information. Officials said they had no inside information or did not respond. Stephanie Syptak-Ramnath said she knew nothing beyond public information.
- The full list of names was not read.

### 5. Trump's accounts bought the day before the pause: confirmed (trades); who directed them is not shown
- (1) Trump's annual OGE disclosure, released around 1 July 2026, lists 327 purchases dated 8 April 2025 [13][14][15]:
  - The combined maximum is about $12.8M, in Apple, Microsoft, Nvidia, Amazon, Alphabet and others.
  - None appeared on the 45-day 278-T reports. A note says late fees were paid; the statutory fee is $200.
- (2) The filing does not show who placed the trades, the time of day, or any gain.
- (3) Critics and Democrats cited the trades. (4) No authority has alleged a violation or made a finding.
- Response: White House spokesperson Anna Kelly said: "All of the President's assets are held in fully discretionary accounts managed by independent third-party financial institutions. There are no conflicts of interest." A spokesperson also pointed to trading by Nancy Pelosi.

### 6. Trump's bond purchases: confirmed
- 278-T filings released in November 2025 show at least $82M of corporate and municipal bonds bought from late August to early October 2025. The upper bound exceeds $337M [16][17][18][20].
- Corporate issuers include Broadcom, Qualcomm, Meta, Home Depot, CVS, Goldman Sachs, Morgan Stanley and JPMorgan. Reuters-based reports say the accounts bought Intel bonds shortly after the government took its Intel stake.
- One trade publication puts bond purchases for the term at over $104M (single-source [19]).
- Watchdogs call these potential conflicts. A White House official said a third-party institution oversees the portfolio.
- The 2026 stock trades (Palantir, Nvidia, Dell) are covered in C20 and C68 [21][22][23].

### 7. Members of Congress around the 9 April pause: confirmed (trades); no finding
- **Greene:** purchases in about 17-20 companies, dated mainly 8-9 April 2025 (dates vary by outlet), worth $21,021-$315,000, plus a $50,001-$100,000 Treasury sale on 8 April [24][25]. She denied insider trading, said her manager "bought the dip", and called calls for an investigation "utterly absurd" [26][27].
- **Moskowitz:** $20K-$300K across about 20 companies on 7 April [28][29]. His office says an outside adviser manages his trades.
- **Bresnahan:** 171 trades on 8 April. **Khanna:** 438 transactions from 31 March to 9 April. **Hern:** one transaction up to $5M [30][31]. Each cites outside management or managed accounts where they responded.
- **Campaign Legal Center:** 53 members made 2,200+ trades worth $34.9M-$140M from 13 February to 9 April 2025, split roughly evenly by party [32][33]. CLC used this to argue for a trading ban, not to allege insider trading.
- No House Ethics or other official finding on these trades was found.

### 8. Small-cap trading before family involvement: partly-accurate
- **Dominari:** 530-580% run-up before 11 February 2025; no buyer identified (C39, C91).
- **Unusual Machines:** up more than 220% in the four weeks before Don Jr. joined on 27 November 2024 (before the term began) [34][35]. The company said only three senior officers, its board and counsel knew of his planned role.
- **PSQ Holdings:** nearly tripled on the day of the board news (3 December 2024); no run-up was reported [36].
- **GrabAGun:** fell on its debut [37]. **Gryphon/American Bitcoin:** the spike came on the announcement (C91).
- **ALT5 Sigma:** The Block reported "some evidence of unusual trading activity" before the 11 August 2025 announcement, and said ALT5 is not accused of wrongdoing [38]. Reported price figures conflict [39].
- **Regulators:** the WSJ reported in September 2025 that the SEC and FINRA had contacted some of the 200+ crypto-treasury companies about pre-announcement spikes [40][41]. It is not established that ALT5 or Trump Media was among them. No enforcement action or identified trader was found.

### 9. Identified prediction-market traders: partly-accurate
- **Van Dyke (Army):** indicted in SDNY (unsealed 23 April 2026), with a parallel CFTC case, over more than $400K of Polymarket bets on the Maduro operation. These are allegations; the case is pending [42][43].
- **Spagnuolo (Google):** charged on 27 May 2026 over about $1.2M of bets on search rankings. This was not a government decision [44].
- **Israel:** a reservist and a civilian were indicted in February 2026 over bets on Israeli military operations [45].
- **Kalshi:** fined candidates who bet on their own races (platform action) [46].
- **Unidentified:** the Iran-strike wallets (about $1M) [47].
- None of these cases involves a US political appointee or Trump family member. The White House warned staff in March 2026 [48].

### 10. Platform ties to the family: confirmed
- Don Jr. has been a Kalshi strategic adviser since January 2025 and has sat on Polymarket's advisory board since 26 August 2025 [49][50].
- 1789 Capital invested in Polymarket in 2025 and planned about $300M more in 2026 [51].
- Trump Media's Truth Predict was announced in October 2025 and shelved in August 2026 [52].
- No bet by any family member is documented.
- Responses: Don Jr.'s spokesperson says he does not engage with the government on behalf of companies he advises. Kalshi says he advises on marketing [53].

### 11. Misuse of non-public information shown: unverified
- No charge or official finding exists against any official, member of Congress or family member for any trade above.
- Requests for investigation and their outcomes are covered in C103.

## Corrections
- Claimed: the Transportation Secretary sold just before the April 2025 tariffs. The record shows: Duffy's sales were on 11 and 13 February 2025.
- C58's statement that no public evidence ties Trump to trades around the pause holds for the anonymous options traders. Trump's own accounts, however, made 327 purchases on 8 April 2025, disclosed in July 2026.
- C65's Bondi figures add to $1.25M-$5.5M (Bloomberg Law: $1M-$5.5M). No change of substance.
- Greene's trade dates vary by outlet; most give 8-9 April 2025.
- C13 does not record the reported unusual trading in ALT5 before 11 August 2025.
- Unusual Machines' run-up predates the second term (November 2024).

## Denials and context
- Disclosure forms give value ranges and dates only. They do not show the time of day, profit, or who made the decision.
- Each person's response is recorded under the relevant sub-claim above. Most cite outside managers or mandatory divestiture.
- **Research limit:** original pages (ProPublica, OGE and White House PDFs, CBS, USA Today, House Judiciary) could not be fetched from this environment. All findings rest on search excerpts and syndicated copies, and every source is marked as an excerpt.
