# C102: Trades by officials, family and associates

**Verdict:** mostly-accurate (confidence: medium)

**Question:** What trades by officials, family members and associates around government actions are documented, and which allegations go beyond the record?

## Summary

Disclosure filings and on-record reporting document many well-timed trades around tariff and policy news:

- **Attorney General Pam Bondi** sold $1M-$5M of Trump Media shares plus $250K-$500K of warrants on 2 April 2025, the day of the "Liberation Day" tariffs. Her ethics agreement required the sale; the date is what drew attention.
- **Dan Scavino** (deputy chief of staff) sold $1M-$5M of Trump Media stock on 1 April 2025 (USA Today; single-source). **Sergio Gor** sold $15,001-$50,000 of Trump Media on 27 March 2025 (his own 278-T, read in full).
- **Transportation Secretary Sean Duffy** sold 34 stocks ($90K-$650K) on 11 and 13 February 2025, around the reciprocal-tariff plan, **not in April**.
- **ProPublica** found more than a dozen officials and aides with well-timed sales, including State Department official Gautam Rana ($830K-$1.7M on 19 March 2025).
- **Trump's own accounts:** his OGE annual report (read in full) lists 327 purchases in one account on 8 April 2025, the day before the tariff pause, worth $3.6M-$12.8M by range. The report was made public on 30 June 2026, about 13 months after the 45-day deadline, and the reviewer notes that late fees were paid. The same account also traded heavily on other days (462 trades on 4 April, 273 purchases on 10 April). His accounts also bought at least $82M of corporate and municipal bonds between late August and early October 2025.
- **Members of Congress** traded heavily around the 9 April pause: Greene ($21K-$315K of purchases), Moskowitz ($20K-$300K), Bresnahan (171 trades on 8 April), Khanna (438 transactions), Hern (up to $5M in one trade). The Campaign Legal Center counted 2,200+ trades by 53 members.
- **Small caps:** pre-announcement run-ups are documented for Dominari and Unusual Machines and reported for ALT5 Sigma. No buyer has been identified. SEC and FINRA reportedly contacted some crypto-treasury companies, with no action known.
- **Prediction markets:** the only US charge tied to a government action is a soldier's Maduro bets. Don Jr. advises Kalshi and Polymarket, and 1789 Capital invested in Polymarket, but no family bet is documented.

What is **not** established: none of these trades has produced a charge, inspector-general finding, SEC/CFTC action or ethics-committee finding against the official, member or family member concerned. Several sales were mandated divestitures, and most filers say outside managers made the trades.

This extends C58 (market-moving posts, unidentified options and oil traders), C20 and C68 (Trump's 2026 stock trades), C65 (Bondi), C25 and C38 (prediction markets and Don Jr.), and C39 and C91 (Dominari). The investigations themselves are covered in C103.

**Evidence rule applied:** a documented, disclosed trade is not by itself evidence that anyone used non-public information. Each point separates (1) the filing, (2) what outlets infer, (3) who alleges wrongdoing, and (4) whether any trader has been identified or any authority has made a finding.

## Sub-claims

### 1. Bondi's Trump Media sale on 2 April 2025: confirmed (trade); misuse unverified
- (1) Filing: her OGE periodic transaction report, public in mid-May 2025, shows a sale of $1M-$5M of TMTG shares and $250K-$500K of warrants on 2 April 2025 [1][2][54] (together $1.25M-$5.5M). Trump announced the tariffs after the close. The form does not show the time of day. DJT closed at $18.76 that day and opened at $17.92 the next morning (ProPublica, read in full), and fell about 13% within days (CBS).
- (2) Outlets called the timing fortunate. (3) Rep. Raskin asked DOJ Inspector General Horowitz to investigate on 20 May 2025 [3][4]. (4) No finding was found as of Nov 2026.
- Context: her ethics agreement required divesting the stake "not later than 90 days after my confirmation". She was confirmed on 4 February 2025, so the deadline was about 5 May 2025. The 278-T itself (read in full in C108) was signed 5 May 2025 and OGE-certified 12 May 2025.
- Response: DOJ did not answer early questions. At a June 2025 House hearing Bondi said she complied with ethics rules and lost money on the stock (secondary summary [5]).

### 2. Scavino and Gor sold Trump Media stock before 2 April: mostly-accurate (Gor documented; Scavino single-source)
- **Gor (documented):** his own 278-T, signed 20 May 2025 and read in full, lists a sale of Trump Media & Technology Group worth $15,001-$50,000 on 27 March 2025 [55].
- **Scavino (single-source):** USA Today reported, from his disclosure, a sale of $1M-$5M on 1 April 2025 [6][7][56]. His form was not read. USA Today found no evidence that either man had advance knowledge.
- A later report says Scavino's 2025 annual disclosure shows $1M-$5M of capital gains from the holding. This is **unverified** (one report relayed by a blog [8]; the form was not read).
- Response: a White House spokesperson (named in reposts as assistant press secretary Taylor Rogers) said senior staff fully comply with ethics rules, and the White House said Gor's sale "had nothing to do with the tariff announcement". Both quotes were seen only in syndicated excerpts.

### 3. Duffy's sales: mostly-accurate (dates corrected)
- ProPublica (from OGE records; read in full) reported that Duffy sold 34 stocks on 11 and 13 February 2025, worth $90K-$650K, around the 13 February reciprocal-tariff plan [9][10]. Seven sales were required by his ethics agreement; the other 27 were worth $27K-$405K.
- ProPublica said it was unclear whether he had any advance information.
- Response: the Transportation Department said an outside manager made the trades in a retirement account and Duffy "had no input on the timing of the sales".

### 4. Other officials' well-timed sales: mostly-accurate
- ProPublica (22 May 2025, read in full) reviewed hundreds of disclosures and found more than a dozen officials and aides with well-timed sales [11][12]. Examples:
  - Gautam Rana (longtime State Department official, ambassador to Slovakia when the report appeared) sold $830K-$1.7M, largely broad index funds, on 19 March 2025. He declined to comment.
  - A career lawyer serving as acting general counsel of the White House Office of Administration (named by ProPublica) sold an index fund and nine companies ($12K-$180K) the day before Trump confirmed the Mexico and Canada tariffs. He said his wife made the sale from her own account to pay tuition and that he had no nonpublic information.
  - A trade-office (USTR) official sold $2K-$30K of Target and Freeport-McMoRan on 25 and 27 March. He did not respond.
- ProPublica states there is no evidence these trades used non-public information. Stephanie Syptak-Ramnath said she knew nothing beyond public information and traded because of family obligations and a changing economy.

### 5. Trump's accounts bought the day before the pause: confirmed (trades); who directed them is not shown
- (1) **The filing (read in full).** Trump's OGE Form 278e for 2025 was received by OGE on 29 June 2026 and made public on 30 June [57]. Our line-by-line count of Part 7 finds:
  - 327 transactions dated 8 April 2025, all purchases, all in the account labelled "Investment Account #7".
  - By range: 139 at $1,001-$15,000, 176 at $15,001-$50,000, 7 at $50,001-$100,000 and 5 at $100,001-$250,000. That sums to **$3.63M-$12.84M**. Names include Apple, Microsoft, Nvidia, Amazon and Alphabet.
  - No transactions on 7 or 9 April. The same account made 462 trades (224 purchases, 238 sales) on 4 April and 273 purchases ($3.0M-$10.5M) on 10 April. The report lists about 21,000 transactions across eight accounts in 2025, so 8 April was one of many heavy-trading days. ABC News noted the account traded similarly through the year and more in volatile periods, consistent with rebalancing [58].
- **Late disclosure.** The reviewing official's comments (30 June 2026) read: "The filer was granted a 45-day extension to file this annual report" and "The filer paid late filing fees related to transactions not previously reported on 278-Ts." The note does not say which trades or how much was paid. Sludge reports that no 278-T covered the April trades or virtually any 2025 stock trade, and that the statutory late fee is $200 [13]. A 278-T is due no later than 45 days after a trade, so these surfaced about 13 months after the deadline and nearly 15 months after the trades.
- (2) The filing does not show who placed or directed the trades, the time of day, or any gain.
- (3) Critics and Democrats (including Sen. Schiff and Rep. Levin) cited the trades and asked for full disclosure [15]. (4) No authority has alleged a violation or made a finding.
- **Responses.**
  - White House: all of the President's assets are held in "fully discretionary accounts managed by independent third-party financial institutions", with "no conflicts of interest". The wording is attributed to spokesperson Anna Kelly and was repeated in July 2026 coverage [59]; the original occasion was not confirmed.
  - ABC News: the White House said the accounts are run by professional advisers who "do not regularly communicate with Trump". Trump told reporters: "You know why I'm profiting? Because the stock market's going up, everybody's profiting." [58]
  - A spokesperson also pointed to trading by Nancy Pelosi [15].
  - The Trump Organization (May 2026, on the 2026 trades): neither Trump, his family nor the company "plays any role in selecting, directing, or approving specific investments", and they "receive no advance notice of trading activity" [60].
  - The accounts are not a blind trust.

### 6. Trump's bond purchases: confirmed
- 278-T filings released in November 2025 show at least $82M of corporate and municipal bonds bought from late August to early October 2025. The upper bound exceeds $337M [16][17][18][20].
- Corporate issuers include Broadcom, Qualcomm, Meta, Home Depot, CVS, Goldman Sachs, Morgan Stanley and JPMorgan. Reuters-based reports say the accounts bought Intel bonds shortly after the government took its Intel stake.
- One trade publication puts bond purchases for the term at over $104M (single-source [19]).
- Watchdogs call these potential conflicts. A White House official said a third-party institution oversees the portfolio.
- The 2026 stock trades (Palantir, Nvidia, Dell) are covered in C20 and C68 [21][22][23].

### 7. Members of Congress around the 9 April pause: confirmed (trades); no finding
- **Greene:** purchases in about 17-20 companies, dated mainly 8-9 April 2025 (dates vary by outlet), worth $21,021-$315,000, plus a $50,001-$100,000 Treasury sale on 8 April [24][25]. She denied insider trading, said her manager "bought the dip", and called calls for an investigation "utterly absurd" [26][27].
- **Moskowitz:** $20K-$300K across about 20 companies on 7 April [28][29]. His office says an outside adviser manages his trades.
- **Bresnahan:** 171 trades on 8 April (single-source: one published data chart). **Khanna:** 438 transactions from 31 March to 9 April (CNN; CLC counts 446 over a different window). **Hern:** one transaction of up to $5M on 4 April [30][31][61].
- Responses: Greene told ProPublica, "Since my portfolio manager makes my trades for me, I usually find out about them when the media asks." Moskowitz's spokesperson said his trades are "managed by an outside third-party financial advisor" (Business Insider, read in full). Khanna's office said the transactions were in a trust managed by an independent third party. Bresnahan said he never trades "my own stocks" and later added guardrails [62]. No response from Hern was found.
- **Campaign Legal Center:** 53 members made 2,200+ trades worth $34.9M-$140M from 13 February to 9 April 2025, split roughly evenly by party [32][33]. CLC used this to argue for a trading ban, not to allege insider trading.
- No House Ethics or other official finding on these trades was found.

### 8. Small-cap trading before family involvement: partly-accurate
- **Dominari:** 530-580% run-up before 11 February 2025; no buyer identified (C39, C91).
- **Unusual Machines:** up more than 220% in the four weeks before Don Jr. joined on 27 November 2024 (before the term began) [34][35]. The company said only three senior officers, its board and counsel knew of his planned role.
- **PSQ Holdings:** nearly tripled on the day of the board news (3 December 2024); no run-up was reported [36].
- **GrabAGun:** fell on its debut [37]. **Gryphon/American Bitcoin:** the spike came on the announcement (C91).
- **ALT5 Sigma:** The Block reported "some evidence of unusual trading activity" before the 11 August 2025 announcement, and said ALT5 is not accused of wrongdoing [38]. Reported price figures conflict [39].
- **Regulators:** the WSJ reported in September 2025 that the SEC and FINRA had contacted some of the 200+ crypto-treasury companies about pre-announcement spikes [40][41]. It is not established that ALT5 or Trump Media was among them. No enforcement action or identified trader was found.

### 9. Identified prediction-market traders: partly-accurate
- **Van Dyke (Army):** indicted in SDNY (unsealed 23 April 2026) on five counts, with a parallel CFTC case, over more than $400K of Polymarket winnings on the Maduro operation. He pleaded not guilty; trial is set for 7 December 2026. These are allegations [42][43].
- **Spagnuolo (Google):** charged on 27 May 2026 over about $1.2M of bets on search rankings. This was not a government decision [44].
- **Israel:** a reservist and a civilian were indicted in February 2026 over bets on Israeli military operations [45].
- **Kalshi:** fined candidates who bet on their own races (platform action) [46].
- **Unidentified:** the Iran-strike wallets (about $1M) [47].
- None of these cases involves a US political appointee or Trump family member. The White House warned staff in March 2026 [48].

### 10. Platform ties to the family: confirmed
- Don Jr. has been a Kalshi strategic adviser since January 2025 and has sat on Polymarket's advisory board since 26 August 2025 [49][50].
- 1789 Capital invested in Polymarket in 2025 and planned about $300M more in 2026 [51].
- Trump Media's Truth Predict was announced in October 2025 and shelved in August 2026 [52].
- No bet by any family member is documented.
- Responses: Don Jr.'s spokesperson says he does not engage with the government on behalf of companies he advises. Kalshi says he advises on marketing [53].

### 11. Misuse of non-public information shown: unverified
- No charge or official finding exists against any official, member of Congress or family member for any trade above.
- Requests for investigation and their outcomes are covered in C103.

## Corrections
- Claimed: the Transportation Secretary sold just before the April 2025 tariffs. The record shows: Duffy's sales were on 11 and 13 February 2025.
- C58's statement that no public evidence ties Trump to trades around the pause holds for the anonymous options traders. One of Trump's own accounts, however, made 327 purchases on 8 April 2025, disclosed on 30 June 2026 (verified in the OGE report). Applied to C58 on 16 Nov 2026.
- Audit, 16 Nov 2026: earlier text said these purchases surfaced "more than 14 months late" in "July 2026". The report was made public on 30 June 2026, nearly 15 months after the trades and about 13 months after the 278-T deadline. The 327 count and the $12.8M maximum were confirmed; the minimum is about $3.6M.
- Audit, 16 Nov 2026: earlier text described an "unnamed White House lawyer" and "no response from Rana". ProPublica names the lawyer and records his response, and reports that Rana declined to comment.
- Audit, 16 Nov 2026: Gor's sale is now documented from his own 278-T. Scavino's sale remains single-source, and his reported capital gains are unverified.
- C65's Bondi figures add to $1.25M-$5.5M (Bloomberg Law: $1M-$5.5M). No change of substance.
- Greene's trade dates vary by outlet; most give 8-9 April 2025.
- C13 does not record the reported unusual trading in ALT5 before 11 August 2025.
- Unusual Machines' run-up predates the second term (November 2024).

## Denials and context
- Disclosure forms give value ranges and dates only. They do not show the time of day, profit, or who made the decision.
- Each person's response is recorded under the relevant sub-claim above. Most cite outside managers or mandatory divestiture.
- **Research limit:** the first pass rested on search excerpts. In the audit, Trump's OGE annual report, Gor's 278-T, the three ProPublica articles, Sludge, ABC News and Business Insider's Moskowitz report were read in full. USA Today (Scavino), CNN, Fortune and Bloomberg Law remain excerpts.
