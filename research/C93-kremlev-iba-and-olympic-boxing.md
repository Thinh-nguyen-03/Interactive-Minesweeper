# C93: Umar Kremlev, the IBA and US decisions on boxing and Russia

**Verdict: partly-accurate (confidence: medium).** The gift is documented. So is the IBA's interest in the US government: in January 2025 it publicly asked Trump to "look into" boxing's exclusion from LA28. Kremlev's later contacts with Don Jr. are also on the record, mostly well sourced and partly single-source.

What the record does **not** show is any US action, request or benefit tied to the gift. The IOC and CAS settled Olympic boxing governance before the wedding. We found no US sanctions or visa decision on Kremlev, and the coverage contains no link between Kremlev and TKO, Dana White or the Ali Act revision. A documented gift between people the family calls friends is not evidence of a quid pro quo.

Origin: a third-layer hunt from C76, which established the payments, the IB Challenger route, the repayment claim and the first responses. This report extends that work and does not repeat it.

**Method note.** Every direct fetch failed because the hosts are not allowed in this session: axios.com, occrp.org, iba.sport, tovima.com (FT syndication), pravda.com.ua, protothema.gr, liga.net and thesportsexaminer.com. Everything below therefore rests on search-result relays from several outlets, and each point is graded on that basis. We ran 26 web searches.

Evidence grades: **documented** (filings, official records or several outlets), **single-source** (one outlet or anonymous sourcing), **inferred** (our own observation; no source asserts it).

## Connections and interests

### 1. The gift (documented; extends C76)
- ProPublica (14 Sep 2026), as relayed by IBTimes and Verstka, itemises the spending. About $100,000 went on the private-island rental; relays disagree on whether that was per night or per day. About $70,000 went on fireworks. Relays round the total to "at least $170,000" within ProPublica's "hundreds of thousands."
- The money came through IB Challenger, a Dubai entity. Kremlev's side says the IBA itself paid nothing.
- The couple calls it a gift from a "dear friend" who hosted "two incredible nights" after the wedding. Repayment was pledged on 18 Sep and, per Kremlev, made by 6 Oct. The amount and the recipient were not disclosed.

### 2. The IBA asked Trump for help on LA28 (documented)
- In an open letter dated **22 Jan 2025**, Kremlev and Secretary General Chris Roberts congratulated Trump and endorsed his stance against transgender athletes in women's events.
- They asked him to "look into" boxing's omission from LA28, offered to collaborate on US boxing, and proposed an IBA World Championship in the US.
- ProPublica (relayed) says Kremlev saw the administration as a potential ally against the IOC.
- The IBA's site also carries a Kremlev statement urging sports bodies to "protect women's sport" after Trump's remarks on Imane Khelif. We could not confirm its date.
- **No White House reply or US action has been reported.**

### 3. Olympic boxing governance and the US role (documented)
- The IOC suspended the IBA in 2019 and withdrew recognition in June 2023, citing governance, finance and refereeing.
- CAS dismissed the IBA's appeal on 2 Apr 2024 (CAS 2023/A/9757).
- World Boxing received provisional IOC recognition on 26 Feb 2025. The IOC Executive Board proposed boxing for LA28 on 17 Mar 2025, and the Session approved it that month.
- The IOC says boxers from IBA-aligned national federations cannot compete at LA28.
- Trump's Aug 2025 executive order created a White House Task Force on the 2028 Games. It sits at DHS, with Vance as vice chair, and its remit is security, transport and entry/exit. The IOC welcomed it.
- **We found no US lever over which federation runs Olympic boxing.** The question was decided before the wedding.

### 4. Gazprom (documented)
- In 2022 the IOC called the IBA too dependent on Gazprom.
- At CAS, the reported concerns were that Gazprom was a single, short-horizon, state-owned revenue source.
- The deal reportedly ended in Dec 2022.
- IB Challenger's funding is unknown (Kyiv Post). No source links Gazprom money to the wedding payments.

### 5. Sanctions and travel (documented for Ukraine; Latvia reported; no US measure found)
- **Ukraine:** Kremlev is listed in Ukraine's GUR war-and-sanctions register and is sanctioned by Ukraine.
- **Latvia:** Latvia reportedly banned his entry around 17-18 Sep 2026 (Charter 97, Euronews Romania). We did not see the official notice.
- **United States:** we found no US sanction, visa ban, visa application or US visit. We could not query the OFAC list directly, so this is an absence in coverage, not a verified clean record.
- Any future US exposure would sit with Treasury and State. None is shown to be in play.

### 6. Istanbul forum, 29 Sep 2025 (documented)
- Trump Jr. shared the IBA Sport + Business Forum stage with Kremlev, alongside Manny Pacquiao and Rasheda Ali Walsh.
- His spokesperson says he was unpaid and already in Turkey for an unrelated event.
- They were introduced by an unnamed mutual hunting friend. Kremlev's office says they met "a couple of years ago."

### 7. Mongolia hunt, late Aug 2026 (single-source: FT, 27 Oct 2026, via Meduza, Ukrainska Pravda, United24 and Mediazona)
- On 27 Aug Kremlev flew by charter from an IBA press conference in Kazakhstan to Ulaanbaatar. He then went by helicopter to Trump Jr.'s camp and stayed about two days.
- One participant said the two avoided being photographed together.
- According to the FT, he travelled with **Arthur Martirosov**, a partner in "the US-sanctioned Voskhod fund."
  - OFAC lists an *LLC Voskhod Management Company* under EO 14024.
  - We have **not** established that this is Martirosov's firm, and we found no designation of Martirosov himself.
- Trump Jr.'s side: a personal friendship.

### 8. Kazakhstan business talk (single-source, second-hand; denied)
- IStories with OCCRP (30 Oct to 4 Nov 2026), echoed by the FT: two sources say Kremlev told them he met Trump Jr. in Kazakhstan in 2026 to discuss business deals. No details were given.
- Trump Jr.'s spokesperson: they "have never entered into any business deals with each other and have no plans to do so."
- IStories notes both sides have Kazakh interests. For the Trump sons, that is the reported Skyline Builders stake, Skyline's 20% of Kaz Resources and the tungsten project (C47/C60). IStories did not establish that these were discussed.

### 9. Motive (single-source analysis: The Telegraph, via relays)
- The Telegraph says Kremlev is "likely" seeking to regain control of Olympic boxing by cultivating Trump's son. It also judges him "a long way" from succeeding.
- Kremlev's office says he never discusses politics with American friends, and the couple rejects any political motive. This is an attribution, not a finding.

### 10. TKO, Dana White and the Muhammad Ali Act (bill documented; Kremlev link not connected)
- H.R. 4624 (Rep. Brian Jack) passed the House by voice vote on 24 Mar 2026. The Cruz-Rosen Senate version is in Commerce, and we found no Senate vote.
- Critics call it a TKO/Zuffa initiative; that is advocacy. TKO's ties to Trump are covered in C87.
- **No report links Kremlev or the IBA to TKO, White or the bill.**

### 11. Responses (documented)
- **Garcia:** voluntary minority records requests on 15 Sep 2026 to Susie Wiles and Trump Jr. One relay says investigators are also asking which Bahamian bank received the funds (single-source).
- **Curtis:** asked Senate Judiciary to investigate. His request was bipartisan in scope, also covering Hunter Biden, and did not allege lawbreaking. Newsweek lists other Republican critics.
- **Blanche:** "What is there to investigate."
- **Trump:** "no idea who Umar is"; said his son would repay; said it is not illegal to pay for a friend's party.
- **Melania Trump's spokesman:** she has "never had" a relationship with Kremlev.
- **Eric Trump's spokesperson:** Eric has no knowledge of Kremlev.
- **Kremlev's office:** he was not in Putin's official China delegation, although Chinese state media listed him.
- No committee action or government finding had been reported by mid-Nov 2026.

### 12. Background (documented)
- Kremlev was born Lutfulloev and changed his name in 2010.
- Proekt reports convictions for extortion (2004) and assault (2007); he has not denied them.
- Putin awarded him the Order of Friendship in Apr 2026.
- Proto Thema says his key patron is FSO Maj. Gen. Alexei Rubezhny (single-source).

## Not connected in the coverage we found
- **Gift → any US benefit** (Olympic recognition, sanctions relief, visa, contract). None is reported, and the IOC and CAS decided recognition before the wedding. *Not connected.*
- **IBA's Jan 2025 letter → White House engagement.** No reply is reported. Garcia's records request could test this. *Not connected.*
- **Kremlev/IBA ↔ TKO, Dana White or the Ali Revival Act.** No source. The IBA runs its own pro events (IBA Pro), which makes it a potential competitor; that is our *inferred* observation only.
- **Kazakhstan talks → the Trump sons' Kaz Resources/tungsten stake.** Placing the two side by side is our observation; IStories only notes that both sides have interests there. *Inferred.*
- **Gazprom money → the wedding payments.** IB Challenger's funding is unknown. *Not connected.*
- **Martirosov's Voskhod → the OFAC-listed Voskhod Management Company → Trump Jr.** The FT's "US-sanctioned" label is *single-source*; the entity match is unverified.
- **Melania or Eric Trump ↔ Kremlev.** Both deny any relationship. *Not connected.*
