# C44: Melania Trump's Amazon documentary deal

**Verdict: mostly-accurate** (confidence: medium)

**The claim (builds on C02/C29):** Amazon reportedly paid about $40M to license a Melania Trump documentary, far above rival bids, and she reportedly netted about $10.7M+ in 2025. Meanwhile Amazon and Jeff Bezos had major business before the administration: the antitrust case, federal cloud and Blue Origin/Kuiper contracts, and tariffs. The claim also covers Bezos's inauguration donation and the Washington Post changes.

**Bottom line:** The money is well documented. About $40M for the rights (Puck/WSJ, never officially confirmed by Amazon), about $35M more on marketing (about $75M total), and $16.6M at the global box office. Trump's 2025 OGE disclosure lists $10.71M of net proceeds from the film as spouse income, that is, Melania Trump's.

The "far above rival bids" part rests on one Wall Street Journal report: Disney about $14M, Paramount about $4M, Netflix and Apple not bidding. The Warren/Johnson letter repeats it, but the bids were never confirmed on the record.

Amazon's business before the government is real and large: the FTC case, AWS federal deals, Blue Origin launch awards, the Amazon Leo FCC waiver, and the tariff episode. The bribery or pay-to-play theory remains an allegation from Democratic lawmakers. No documents, investigation or official finding link the deal to any government decision. Amazon, Bezos and Melania's office all deny it.

Source note: warren.senate.gov and hankjohnson.house.gov could not be read directly. The letter, Amazon's reply and the press releases are described from search results that quote them (Variety, Washington Times, Bloomberg Law, The Hill/AOL) and from search snippets of the official pages. The WSJ, Puck and the OGE PDF were not read directly.

## The claim
This check arose from C02 (Melania's $6M NFT income) and C29 (family wealth disclosures). C02 already notes that most of Melania's roughly $17.2M in 2025 income came from the documentary, not crypto. This file does not repeat the NFT and coin material.

## Sub-claims

1. **Amazon paid about $40M to license the documentary: confirmed as reported.**
   - Puck first reported the price in January 2025. The WSJ and many others followed.
   - Amazon confirmed the licensing deal (theatrical plus Prime Video, plus a follow-up docuseries) but has not publicly confirmed the price.
   - Amazon MGM reportedly spent another about $35M on marketing, about $75M in all, and the most ever paid for a documentary.
   - The film opened January 30, 2026 (Kennedy Center premiere January 29). It took about $7M on opening weekend and about $16.6M globally, and streamed on Prime from March 9, 2026.
   - A two-part follow-up, *Melania, The Series*, is set for Prime Video on November 20, 2026. That is a reduction from the three episodes first planned.

2. **Amazon's bid was far above rival bids: partly-accurate, unverified on the record.**
   - The WSJ reported that Disney bid about $14M, Paramount (a "low") about $4M, and that Netflix and Apple declined to bid. That puts Amazon's offer at nearly three times the next bid.
   - Warren and Johnson cite the gap as $26M above Disney.
   - Puck and the Daily Beast said the losing bid figures were not public.
   - Amazon told lawmakers it ran a "thorough and competitive bidding process" but did not disclose the rival bids. Warren's office says Amazon dodged that question.
   - No primary document confirms the rival figures.

3. **Melania netted about $10.7M+ in 2025: confirmed.**
   - Trump's 2025 OGE 278e (released June 30, 2026) lists the fee in Part 5, "Spouse's Employment Assets & Income", under MKT World LLC: "License agreement with Designers Manager - Film 'Melania'", net proceeds $10,710,000. The same section lists $6,011,259 from NFTs and collectibles and $521,161 from the memoir.
   - So it is the First Lady's income reported on his form, as Newsweek and Notus describe it. Variety and TheWrap's headlines call it Trump's.
   - Her other 2025 income was about $6.01M from NFTs (see C02) and about $0.52M from the Skyhorse memoir license, about $17.2M in total.
   - The WSJ had reported her share at more than 70% of the $40M (about $28M). The gap with $10.71M is unexplained. It may reflect timing (payments in 2026), costs, or gross versus net, but no source states which.

4. **Amazon and Bezos had major business before the administration: confirmed.**
   - **FTC.** The monopolization suit was filed in 2023 under Lina Khan. Judge John Chun of the Western District of Washington has set trial for March 29, 2027, and on October 9, 2026 refused Amazon's request to push it to June 2027.
   - **FTC Prime case.** Amazon settled the separate Prime "dark patterns" case on September 25, 2025 for $2.5B ($1B civil penalty plus $1.5B in refunds). It neither admitted nor denied wrongdoing. Chair Andrew Ferguson called it "record-breaking"; Lina Khan called it "a drop in the bucket".
   - **AWS.** GSA signed a OneGov deal in August 2025 (up to $1B in savings through 2028). AWS announced up to $50B of government AI and supercomputing infrastructure in November 2025. The lawmakers' letter also cites new cloud contracts with immigration and other agencies.
   - **Blue Origin.** The Space Force awarded it NSSL Phase 3 Lane 2 work in April 2025: 7 missions, about $2.3B, out of about $13.5B total.
   - **Amazon Leo (formerly Kuiper).** The FCC granted a deployment-milestone waiver in June 2026, over SpaceX's opposition.
   - **Tariffs.** On April 29, 2025, Karoline Leavitt called a reported Amazon plan to show tariff costs a "hostile and political act". Trump phoned Bezos and said he "solved the problem very quickly". Amazon said the idea was only for Amazon Haul and was never approved.

5. **Timing of the deal versus government decisions: partly-accurate.** The chronology is real, but no causal link is documented.
   - Bezos dined with Trump at Mar-a-Lago in mid-December 2024 and Amazon pledged $1M to the inauguration fund, plus streaming the inauguration on Prime (valued at about $1M in kind). Filming began in December 2024 and the deal was reported on January 5-7, 2025, before the inauguration.
   - The later decisions listed above (the FTC settlement, AWS deals, NSSL award, FCC waiver) followed. The NSSL award is a multi-vendor competition, and the FTC trial is still proceeding.
   - Nothing on the record ties any specific decision to the film.

6. **Bezos's inauguration donation and the Washington Post changes: confirmed as context.**
   - Amazon gave $1M to the inauguration fund.
   - On February 26, 2025, Bezos said the Post's opinion section would defend "personal liberties and free markets". Opinion editor David Shipley left.
   - Columnist Ruth Marcus resigned in March 2025 after a column criticizing the change was killed.
   - These are documented but separate from the film deal. Linking them is interpretation.

7. **The deal was a bribe or pay-to-play: unverified (allegation).**
   - On March 15-16, 2026, Sen. Elizabeth Warren and Rep. Hank Johnson, joined by Sen. Ben Ray Luján and Reps. Pramila Jayapal and Dan Goldman, wrote to CEO Andy Jassy. They warned of possible federal bribery-law violations and asked whether Amazon discussed the bid with Melania or administration officials.
   - Amazon's reply came from VP of Public Policy Brian Huseman (reported as dated March 30). It rejected any impropriety and cited "the access, the story, and its cultural and historical relevance".
   - On April 13, 2026, Warren called the deal "bribery in plain sight".
   - No DOJ, inspector general or congressional-majority probe was found.

## Corrections
- The $10.71M appears on Donald Trump's 2025 OGE report, in Part 5 (spouse income) under MKT World LLC: it is Melania Trump's income reported on his form, not a separate filing by her. Some headlines call it Trump's (correction of 16 Nov 2026).
- The "far above rival bids" figures (Disney $14M, Paramount $4M) come from one anonymously sourced WSJ report. Amazon never confirmed them.
- "Kuiper" is now Amazon Leo. Its 2026 FCC action was a deadline waiver, not a contract. Blue Origin's large federal award was the April 2025 NSSL launch contract.
- The Melania payout: the WSJ reported more than 70% (about $28M), while the 2025 disclosure shows $10.71M. The two figures are not reconciled.

## Denials and other side
- **Amazon (Huseman).** The bidding process was "thorough and competitive". Amazon releases documentaries on figures across the political spectrum, and nothing improper happened.
- **Jeff Bezos (CNBC Squawk Box, May 20, 2026).** He had "nothing to do with" the deal. He called the curry-favor claim "a falsehood that will not die" and the film "a very wise business decision".
- **Marc Beckman, Melania's adviser.** The film is "not political at all", and Melania pitched the project herself after the 2024 election.
- **Context cutting against the bribery theory.** The FTC still extracted a record $2.5B Prime settlement and is pressing the antitrust case to trial. The NSSL awards went to three vendors.
- **Context cutting toward it.** The film lost money on its theatrical run (break-even was about $40-45M domestic against $16.6M global). Amazon nonetheless ordered a follow-up series, and Amazon is also a White House ballroom donor (see C33).
