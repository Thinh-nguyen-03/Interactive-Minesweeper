# C45 — Kushner's Albania and Serbia developments

**Verdict: mostly-accurate** (confidence: medium)

## The claim

This check builds on C15, C37 and C31. The claim: Jared Kushner's Affinity Partners is pursuing a luxury resort on Albania's Sazan island and the Zvernec coast, plus a Belgrade development on the former army HQ site. Both need foreign-government approvals while Kushner acts as a US envoy. Also covered: Rep. Raskin's probe, right-wing criticism such as Candace Owens's, any Trump branding, deal terms, concessions, status (Belgrade reportedly collapsed amid a forgery probe) and denials.

Method note: the original pages at npr.org, intellinews.com, democrats-judiciary.house.gov and jaredkushner.com were not read directly. Findings rest on search-result summaries from several outlets: Reuters (via syndication), AP, RFE/RL, Al Jazeera, France 24, Forbes, Balkan Insight, Exit News, European Western Balkans and others.

## Summary

The core of the claim is documented. In Dec 2024 Albania granted a Kushner-linked company strategic-investor status for a roughly EUR 1.4B Sazan island resort. It had already passed a Feb 2024 law opening protected areas to "strategic" tourism. In spring 2026 it gave preliminary approval to a larger Zvernec/Vjosa-Narta project, which set off a mass protest movement. Both projects still await approvals while Kushner serves as Trump's envoy.

The Belgrade General Staff project is confirmed. It came with a 99-year rent-free lease and a Nov 2025 special law, and it was to carry the Trump brand under licence. It collapsed on 15 Dec 2025, hours after prosecutors indicted the culture minister and other officials for forging the documents that lifted the site's heritage protection.

Raskin's probe and Candace Owens's attacks are both confirmed. The claim overreaches in two places. Affinity denies being the investor or developer of Zvernec, and Kushner calls himself a personal minority partner. And no Trump branding is documented in Albania. Any suggestion that the envoy role bought concessions is unproven.

## Sub-claims

1. **Affinity is pursuing the Sazan/Zvernec resorts: partly-accurate.**
   - On 30 Dec 2024 Rama's Strategic Investment Committee gave strategic-investor status for Sazan (about EUR 1.4B) to Atlantic Incubation Partners LLC, which is reported as an Affinity affiliate.
   - The Zvernec land was bought in April 2026 by Albania Land Development from Artur Shehu. Albania Land Development is owned by Sazan Real Estate Development and other investors, and one report says it is Qatari-owned.
   - In June 2026 Affinity said it is neither investor nor developer and that the partners invest personally.
   - Kushner's 8 Oct 2026 reply to The New Yorker:
     - he is a minority partner focused on design, and Asher Abehsera is CEO;
     - the partners paid EUR 230M for the land in a private deal;
     - the project could reach about EUR 4B.
2. **Albanian government concessions: mostly-accurate.**
   - Law 21/2024 (22 Feb 2024) amended the protected-areas law. The European Parliament has called for its repeal.
   - Strategic-investor status brings fast-track approvals. Forbes reports a 10-year tax exemption during construction (single source).
   - A planned joint venture would put the state's stake with the Albanian Investment Corporation and the seaports company.
   - A June 2026 draft decision would move Sazan to the Economy Ministry.
   - Zvernec got preliminary approval in spring 2026.
   - Rama's office says there is "no definitive agreement" on Sazan.
   - Reported status: construction suspended pending an EU-prompted environmental impact assessment, and the Zvernec land transaction suspended until ownership is clarified.
3. **Belgrade General Staff project and concessions: confirmed.**
   - Affinity announced the project in 2024. Semafor reported Mohamed Alabbar as a partner in Jan 2025.
   - Cost was about $500M.
   - The 2024 contract promised a 99-year rent-free lease, an option to take ownership, and removal of heritage status.
   - A special law passed 130-40 on 7 Nov 2025.
4. **Belgrade collapsed amid a forgery probe: confirmed.**
   - The indictment proposal against Culture Minister Nikola Selakovic and others was filed on 15 Dec 2025, and Affinity withdrew the same day.
   - The trial opened on 4 Feb 2026 with not-guilty pleas. On 15 Apr Selakovic called it political. I found no verdict.
   - Vucic denied any forgery in May 2025, offered pardons, and later said Serbia had lost at least EUR 750M of investment.
5. **Albanian forgery/anti-corruption probe: confirmed.**
   - SPAK suspects the seller, Shehu, of forged deeds and illicit funds.
   - About EUR 110M of the proceeds is frozen. One report (Lawyer Monthly) gives a provisional seizure value above EUR 128M and a separate, reportedly short-lived freeze on Albania Land Development's accounts; SPAK has not confirmed either detail publicly.
   - Per Reuters, no wrongdoing is alleged against Kushner or the investors. Shehu's lawyer denies the allegations.
   - Villagers have disputed Shehu's title since 2013.
6. **Approvals pending while Kushner is envoy: confirmed for Albania.**
   - Kushner was named envoy on 19 Feb 2026 (C15), and he acknowledges the Albanian approvals are incomplete.
   - Belgrade predates the formal title and collapsed in Dec 2025.
7. **Raskin probe: confirmed.**
   - The first letter came in April 2026.
   - The 24 Aug 2026 expansion targets the "suspicious $120 million" land purchase, raises the FCPA, and set a 7 Sep deadline.
   - A 29 Oct letter covers bank accounts and recusal.
   - Raskin says Kushner did not respond. These are minority letters with no subpoena power.
8. **Candace Owens criticism: confirmed.** In June 2026 podcast segments she attacked Ivanka and Kushner over the resort. The quotes are known from secondary reporting.
9. **Trump branding: partly-accurate.**
   - Belgrade was to be a Trump-licensed hotel, and Kushner said he discussed it with Eric Trump.
   - I found no Trump brand in Albania.
10. **Implied quid pro quo via the envoy role or family ties: unverified.** The timing is suggestive, but no document shows US intervention or a trade. The FCPA question has only been raised by Raskin, and no US agency is known to be investigating.

## Corrections

- Zvernec is not an Affinity project by Affinity's account. Kushner says he is a personal minority partner.
- Albania has its own forgery probe, aimed at the seller, not at Kushner.
- "$120M" is Raskin's figure. SPAK cites about EUR 110M for the Shehu sale, and Kushner cites EUR 230M for the land in total.
- Belgrade predates the formal envoy title.

## Denials and other side

- **Kushner:** the land was bought lawfully in a private deal, he is a minority partner, and Albania decides whether the project goes ahead.
- **Affinity:** it has no role in Zvernec. On Belgrade it said it was stepping aside because projects "should unite rather than divide".
- **Rama:** the projects comply with Albanian and EU law, and the protests are orchestrated.
- **Selakovic and co-defendants:** they plead not guilty and call the case political.
- **Vucic:** there was no forgery.
- **Context:** guards beat protesters at Zvernec on 30 May 2026. Two security firms lost their licences and police officers were suspended.
