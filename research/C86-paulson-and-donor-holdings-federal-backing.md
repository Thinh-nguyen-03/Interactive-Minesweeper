# C86: Trump mega-donors holding stakes in companies that received federal backing

**Verdict: partly-accurate** (confidence: medium). This is a third-layer connection check from C77 (Greenland, seabed and rare earths) and C60 (critical-minerals equity stakes). It does not repeat their findings on Cantor, 1789 Capital or the family firms.

## Summary

**The strongest case is John Paulson.** Both links are documented:
- He is a Trump fundraiser (he hosted the April 2024 event that raised $50.5M). He is also the largest shareholder of Perpetua Resources, which won a $2.9B EXIM loan approval in May 2026. The loan had not closed as of our search.
- He held about 8.7% of Trilogy Metals when the U.S. took a stake in October 2025. Outlets estimate his paper gain at $60-70M. He sold the whole position in Q4 2025.

Fairness requires one caveat: much of Perpetua's federal support (the record of decision, the first EXIM letter, most of the DoD money) began under Biden.

**Two more donors are well documented, but the federal action is a deal approval or a pending bid, not a stake, loan or price floor:**
- **Jeff Yass** gave $16M to MAGA Inc. His Susquehanna holds ByteDance and sits in the TikTok USDS joint venture, which Trump approved by executive order.
- **Todd Boehly** gave $1M+. He leads a bid for Lukoil's foreign assets in which the DFC would reportedly take equity. The bid is pending and unsigned.

**The other names are weaker:**
- **Schwarzman, Griffin and Cohen.** That they bought into the USA Rare Earth PIPE rests on one Senate letter. Griffin is a public critic of Trump. We found no Trump donation by Cohen. Any PIPE buyer is under water.
- **Kelcy Warren and Harold Hamm.** Both are major donors who received regulatory decisions, not financial backing.

## Connections (with evidence grades)

### 1. Paulson → Perpetua → EXIM (documented)
- **Donor link:** Paulson hosted Trump's Palm Beach fundraiser in April 2024 ($50.5M raised; see C77). He was considered for Treasury Secretary. He gave $250,000 to MAGA KY, a Trump-allied anti-Massie super PAC (2025). We found no MAGA Inc. contribution from him.
- **Holding:** Paulson & Co. bought $100M of Perpetua's ~$425M raise in June 2025, at $13.20 a share. It is the largest holder, at about 25-26% (32.3M shares, Q1 2026, per 13F trackers); Bloomberg Government puts his total investment at about $185M.
- **Federal actions:**
  - FAST-41 priority listing (April 2025).
  - Army Corps final permit (May 2025).
  - EXIM notified Congress of a $2.7B loan (March 2026), and its board approved $2.9B (May 2026). The loan is still subject to definitive documents, and no close was found.
- **Biden-era groundwork:**
  - Forest Service record of decision, 2025-01-03.
  - EXIM letter of interest for up to $1.8B, April 2024.
  - Most of the ~$80M in DoD awards.
- **Paper gain (our arithmetic, inferred; not stated by any source):** about 7.58M placement shares at the 2026-05-21 close of US$26.72 come to about $203M, a gain of roughly $100M on that tranche alone. No Paulson statement on a conflict was found.

### 2. Paulson → Trilogy Metals → U.S. equity stake (documented)
- Paulson held about 8.7% of Trilogy, making him its fourth-largest holder.
- On 2025-10-06 the Department of War signed a binding LOI to pay $35.6M for about 10%. Only half went to Trilogy: $17.8M for 8,215,570 new units at $2.17. The other $17.8M bought existing shares from South32, plus a call option. All of it is to be reinvested in Ambler Metals. Warrants and the option could take the stake toward 17.5%. The administration also approved the Ambler Road, and the stock jumped more than 200%.
- Estimated paper gain: $60-70M (Popular Information; Dermot Cole).
- His 13F shows he sold all 14.3M shares between Oct 1 and Dec 31, 2025. Exact dates and prices are not disclosed, so the realised gain is unknown.
- The largest holder was Thomas Kaplan's Electrum (about 18.5%, worth more than $136M in March 2026). We did not check Kaplan's political giving.

### 3. Paulson → Donlin Gold (holding documented; federal backing not found)
- Paulson paid $800M for 40% of Donlin (closed 2025-06-03).
- On 2026-07-21 NovaGold agreed to absorb that stake in an all-stock deal valued at about $4.2B. Paulson keeps about 40% of the economic interest. The deal is expected to close in Q4 2026.
- The only federal action found is a court-ordered Army Corps supplemental environmental review. We found no stake, loan or price floor. **Unverified** as a federal-backing case.

### 4. Schwarzman / Griffin / Cohen → USA Rare Earth PIPE (single-source for the purchases)
- **The allegation:** the 2026-02-26 Warren/Wyden/Van Hollen letter says SEC filings show these three bought discounted USAR shares in the Cantor-placed $1.5B PIPE (at $21.50). The PIPE closed on 2026-01-29; Commerce took a stake of about 10% and committed a $1.3B loan. USAR's release names only Inflection Point as a buyer.
- **Schwarzman:** $5M to MAGA Inc. in H2 2025 (FEC, via Bloomberg on 2026-01-02). He is also a ballroom donor. *Documented.*
- **Griffin:**
  - He gave $1M to the inauguration but did not endorse Trump in 2024.
  - In January 2026 he said the administration had been "very enriching to the families" of officials.
  - "Trump ally" is therefore contestable.
  - Citadel held about 5.6M USAR shares in Q3 2026 (C77), but a 13F does not show PIPE participation.
- **Cohen:**
  - We found no Trump or MAGA Inc. donation.
  - His documented Trump-family link runs instead through Point72's 4% stake in Alt5 Sigma, the WLF treasury vehicle (Bloomberg, 2025-08-19; C13).
- **Gains:** the stock was about $9.30 in late October 2026, so PIPE buyers had paper losses. Cantor says it had no role in the government deal.

### 5. Boehly → Lukoil International → DFC equity (donations documented; consortium single-source)
- **Donations:** Eldridge gave $1M to the inaugural. NBC's FEC analysis lists Boehly among new $1M+ MAGA Inc. donors; the amount and date are unconfirmed because the article could not be read directly.
- **The bid:** the FT (relayed by Meduza and WION) reported in September 2026 that Boehly's consortium for Lukoil International includes:
  - Tahnoon-chaired IHC;
  - the Al-Khayyat family;
  - the DFC, with a reported mid-teens equity stake.
- **Status:** OFAC's license (extended 2026-10-21) runs to 2026-11-21 and covers negotiations only. Nothing was signed, and no DFC commitment was announced. See C76 for the Witkoff/Kushner angle and their denials.

### 6. Kelcy Warren → Energy Transfer → DOE (documented, regulatory only)
- **Donations:**
  - At least $5M toward the 2024 campaign.
  - $25M to MAGA Inc. in February 2025, together with Energy Transfer (Forbes: $12.5M each).
- **Regulatory history:**
  - Under Biden, DOE refused an extension for Lake Charles LNG and denied rehearing.
  - Trump ended the LNG export pause on day one.
  - In August 2025 Secretary Wright granted Lake Charles more time to start exports.
- **Outcome:** Energy Transfer suspended the project on 2025-12-18. There was no stake, loan or price floor, and no realised benefit.

### 7. Harold Hamm → Continental (donations documented; financial backing not found)
- **Donations:**
  - $3,300 to Trump's campaign in 2023.
  - $1.6M-$4.3M to pro-Trump committees, per advocacy sources that disagree with each other.
- **Role:** he led energy policy on the 2024 transition and was a ballroom donor (October 2025).
- **Federal action:** critics cite BLM permits, including 71 wells in one county (High Country News, Revolving Door Project). We found no stake, loan or price floor. **Unverified.**

### 8. Yass → Susquehanna/ByteDance → TikTok USDS (documented)
- **Donations:** $16M to MAGA Inc.: $1M in January 2025 and $15M in March 2025. In 2024 his spokesperson had said he never gave to Trump.
- **Holdings:** Susquehanna reportedly owns about 15% of ByteDance, and Yass about 7% personally. Valuations vary widely.
- **Federal action:**
  - Trump turned against a TikTok ban after meeting Yass in March 2024. No source shows the meeting caused the change.
  - He repeatedly delayed enforcement of the ban.
  - He approved the framework by executive order on 2025-09-25.
  - The joint venture closed on 2026-01-22.
- **Susquehanna's place in the joint venture:** its affiliate Vastmere is an investor (one outlet attributes Vastmere to Dell), and SIG's Mark Dooley has a board seat. ByteDance keeps 19.9%.
- **Gains:** undisclosed.

### 9. Congress asked the donor question directly (documented)
- On 2026-02-02, House Natural Resources Democrats asked DoD, Commerce, Energy and Interior whether major Trump donors or Trump Organization affiliates hold equity in companies receiving federal stakes. No answer was found.
- Fortune (2026-07-26) found no public ledger of the roughly $27B in stakes, and some private-company stakes have no SEC filings.

Each link is sourced on its own; no outlet has tied them together.

## What this does and does not show
- **What it shows:** several of Trump's biggest donors hold positions that federal decisions in 2025-26 directly affected.
- **What it does not show:** no source shows that any donation caused a decision.
- **Realised gains:** none is documented except Paulson's undisclosed Trilogy sale.
- **Pending deals:** the Perpetua loan and the Boehly bid were still pending in November 2026.

## Corrections

- **Ballroom donor list (2026-11-17):** The Adelson Family Foundation (a US family foundation, not Israeli money) is reported to have committed $25M to the White House ballroom, per a Washington Post list of donor records relayed by the Boston Globe (16 Oct 2026). Single-source; the foundation did not comment. No sentence here ties a donation to a decision. See C110.
