# C58: Market-moving posts and insider-trading allegations

**Verdict:** mostly-accurate (confidence: medium)

## Summary

Most of the pattern described in the claim is on the record:

- **9 April 2025.** Trump posted "THIS IS A GREAT TIME TO BUY!!!" less than four hours before the 90-day tariff pause. Reuters documented a burst of bullish options buying minutes before the pause post. Senate and House Democrats asked the SEC to investigate. Trump said on camera that Charles Schwab and Roger Penske had made billions that day.
- **Iran oil trades.** In March-April 2026 the CFTC, with DOJ, probed oil-futures trades placed shortly before Trump's Iran posts.
- **Prediction markets.** Suspicious Polymarket bets came before the Maduro raid and before the Iran strikes. A soldier was charged over the Maduro bets.
- **White House email.** Staff were warned not to trade on nonpublic information.
- **Lawsuit.** The Intercept and the Freedom of the Press Foundation (FPF) sued over the paid Truth API. The case is pending.
- **Rogan podcast.** On the Joe Rogan Experience, guest Shane Gillis called the Truth API "literally insider trading."

What is **not** established: no public evidence, investigation finding or charge ties Trump, his family or any named official to any of these trades. The Intel and Fed "well-timed trades" threads are thin. The best-documented "pardon bet" was on Biden's pardons, not Trump's.

This builds on C20 (stock posts), C21 and C41 (Truth API, the Warren-Schiff letter to the SEC, the Intercept lead), C25 (Don Jr.'s Kalshi and Polymarket roles) and C30 (Rogan).

## Sub-claims

### 1. "Great time to buy" post before the tariff pause: confirmed
- On the morning of 9 April 2025, Trump posted "THIS IS A GREAT TIME TO BUY!!! DJT" on Truth Social.
- Less than four hours later he announced a 90-day pause on most "reciprocal" tariffs, excluding China. The S&P 500 rose about 9.5% that day [1][2].
- The White House said he was reassuring markets.

### 2. Options activity: confirmed (identity of traders unknown)
- Reuters reported that unidentified traders placed bullish SPY and QQQ call bets worth millions of dollars in the minutes before the pause post [3].
- One block of SPY $509 same-day calls went from about $624K to about $10M in value.
- Reuters could not tell whether one trader or many placed the bets, or whether any gains were realised.
- Claims of a "$2.5M to $70M+" trade come from secondary sites only and are unverified.
- AOC demanded that members of Congress disclose their trades [4].

### 3. Democrats asked the SEC to investigate: confirmed; no SEC action found
- On 11 April 2025, Warren and Schumer, joined by Schiff, Wyden, Gallego, Kelly and others, wrote to SEC Chair Atkins [5][6].
- They asked whether Trump, Cabinet members, donors or insiders had traded on the pause, and whether the White House had contacted the SEC.
- Maxine Waters sent a House letter on 10 April 2025 [7].
- An SEC spokesperson declined to comment. I found no SEC response, probe or enforcement action. Audit: House FSC Democrats wrote on 2026-02-24 that they had "received no substantive response" in nearly a year, and that GAO says it cannot compel an investigation.

### 4. Trump boasting about friends' gains: confirmed
- In an Oval Office clip from 9 April 2025, Trump said of Schwab and Penske: "He made two-and-a-half billion today, and he made $900 million. That's not bad." [8][9]
- These are paper gains that Trump claimed. There is no suggestion either man traded on advance knowledge.

### 5. Well-timed trades before other announcements: partly-accurate
- **Iran (documented).** Reuters reported in mid-April 2026 that the CFTC, working with DOJ, was probing oil-futures trades on CME and ICE [10][11][12].
  - The trades came before Trump's 23 March 2026 post (pausing strikes and announcing talks with Iran) and his 7 April 2026 post.
  - The CFTC sought "Tag 50" trader identities from the exchanges.
  - Estimates of the size conflict: about $2.6B (ABC) versus about $7B (Reuters).
  - CFTC Chair Selig vowed to pursue insider trading but did not comment on the specific case.
  - Audit update: Bloomberg reported on 2026-10-13 (anonymous sources) that Selig adviser Mel Gunewardena had a supervisor retract a staff referral to enforcement over the 23 March bets; a source later said enforcement is still examining the trades. No charges as of mid-Nov 2026.
- **Intel (thin).** A trading-platform blog pointed to unusual Intel options activity before the 22 August 2025 government-stake news [32]. Rep. Tim Moore bought Intel shares on 29 July 2025. Neither shows insider trading, and I found no probe.
- **Fed (thin).** Kevin Warsh's odds on Polymarket and Kalshi rose before his nomination [31]. The moves tracked press reports, and I found no investigation.

### 6. Prediction-market bets before military news: confirmed
- **Maduro raid.**
  - A new Polymarket account turned about $34K into about $410K hours before Maduro's capture on 3 January 2026 [13].
  - SDNY unsealed an indictment of Army Master Sgt. Gannon Ken Van Dyke on 23 April 2026. The charges are three Commodity Exchange Act counts, wire fraud and an unlawful monetary transaction. Prosecutors say he helped plan the raid [14][15].
  - The CFTC brought a parallel civil case, its first insider-trading case on event contracts.
  - Polymarket says it flagged the trades to DOJ.
- **Iran strikes.**
  - Bubblemaps found six freshly funded wallets that made about $1M on a "US strikes Iran by 28 Feb 2026" contract [16].
  - The NYT found 150+ accounts betting the day before the strikes [17][18].
  - No one has been identified or charged.
- None of these bets is linked to Trump or his family. Don Jr. advises both Kalshi and Polymarket (C25).

### 7. Bets before pardon announcements: misleading
- The documented case is Biden's pardons. Bubblemaps told NPR (16 April 2026) that a Polymarket trader made about $316K on Biden's pre-emptive pardons and the Hunter Biden pardon [19].
- I found no documented suspicious wagers before Trump pardons such as CZ's.
- Prosecutors have announced no probe of the pardon bets.

### 8. Official responses: confirmed
- On 24 March 2026, the day after Trump's Iran pause post, the White House Management Office emailed staff [20][21][22].
  - It said that trading prediction or futures contracts on nonpublic information breaks ethics rules and can bring criminal penalties.
  - Spokesman David Ingle confirmed the email. He called any suggestion that officials were trading this way "baseless and irresponsible reporting."
- In April 2025 the White House denied manipulation, and Sen. Cornyn called the allegations ridiculous.

### 9. The Intercept and FPF suit: confirmed (pending)
- **The case.** *The Intercept Media, Inc. v. Trump*, 1:26-cv-06867, was filed in SDNY on 12 August 2026 and assigned to Judge J. Paul Oetken [23][24][25].
- **Defendants.** Trump, Natalie Harp and Dan Scavino (in their official capacities), plus the EOP and the White House Office.
- **The claim.** Routing official announcements through a platform that sells faster access ($60K-$100K a month) violates the First and Fifth Amendments.
- **The relief sought.** Represented by a Yale Law clinic, the plaintiffs asked for a preliminary injunction against official posting there while Truth API runs [26].
- **DOJ's position.** Courts cannot direct how the president carries out official duties, and TMTG, a private company, runs Truth API.
- **Status.** Oetken set a 30 October 2026 answer deadline, plus two weeks [27]. A press report indicates he reserved decision after a PI hearing in early October [28]. I found no ruling.

### 10. Rogan's "insider trading" remark: mostly-accurate
- On a mid-August 2026 episode, guest Shane Gillis called the Truth API "literally insider trading" [29][30].
- Rogan asked how it could be legal and called the $100K price "literally crazy."
- So the phrase is mainly Gillis's.
- TMTG says critics "invented a new theory of 'insider trading' based on publicly available information."

## Corrections
- The documented pardon bet concerned Biden's pardons, not Trump's.
- Gillis, not Rogan, said "literally insider trading."
- The $2.5B and $900M figures are Trump's own claims about paper gains.
- Estimates of the oil-futures bets conflict ($2.6B vs $7B).
- The "$70M options trade" is not confirmed by Reuters.

## Denials and context
- The White House, Ingle and Cornyn deny any manipulation or insider trading by officials.
- TMTG and DOJ defend the Truth API as faster delivery of public data.
- No one tied to the administration has been publicly identified as a trader. The only person charged is a soldier.
- Fast markets, press leaks and informed speculation can explain some price moves.
- **Limits of this check.** The research ended before I could check post-mid-2026 updates on the CFTC oil probe or the SEC's handling of the April 2025 letters. The original pages at CourtListener, Inner City Press and Mediaite were not read directly, so the Intercept docket status rests on search snippets.
