# C70: Foreign intermediaries: what each partner got from Washington

**Verdict: mostly-accurate (confidence: medium)**

The claim is a connection check: for each foreign business partner of the Trump family, find the deal, its date and value, and the US government decision that benefited that partner's company or country in the same period. For most of the named partners, both halves are documented:

- Damac
- Dar Global
- Qatari Diar
- Kinh Bac
- the UAE (Tahnoon) network
- Binance
- Kazakhstan
- Pakistan

Pakistan is the clearest two-way case. WLF affiliates signed agreements with the Pakistani state, and Pakistan's Ministry of Defence placed an order with Powerus, a drone maker the Trump sons back.

Indonesia, India and Serbia have the deal side documented, but the US decisions there cut against a "favour" story. For El Salvador, Azerbaijan, Japan/SoftBank and "Bilt" I found no family deal. For Albania I found no US concession.

No case documents causation, and every party denies a link. Most counterparties are private companies, so the January 2025 Trump Organization ethics policy permits these deals. The exceptions are Qatari Diar, which is owned by Qatar's sovereign fund QIA, and the Pakistani state counterparties.

This report builds on C31, C45, C56, C57, C59, C64 and C67 and does not repeat their detail. Much of the web was reachable only through search results. citizensforethics.org was not read directly, so the CREW findings below come from search snippets.

## Key connections, with evidence grades

1. **Pakistan: state money into family-linked firms (documented).** Pakistan's government signed a USD1 payments MoU with SC Financial Technologies. SC Financial is the Delaware co-owner of the USD1 brand, and Steve Witkoff's August 2025 disclosure lists it as an asset. Zach Witkoff signed the MoU, and his delegation also met PM Sharif and Field Marshal Munir. Eight months later Pakistan's Ministry of Defence placed an initial drone order with Powerus, which the Trump sons back through American Ventures. Munir received the Powerus delegation. Both events followed the 19% tariff deal and Munir's White House lunch. WLF says it is not paid under the MoU, Don Jr.'s spokesperson denies any role in the Powerus deal, and the White House says there are no conflicts. Powerus had licensed its interceptor in India three months earlier.
2. **Damac: licence fees, a pledge and a permitting order (documented; date match single-source).** Damac-linked entities paid $11.3-12.5M in 2025, including $10M for Abu Dhabi projects that have not been announced. That came a year after Sajwani stood beside Trump to pledge $20B in US data centres. CREW reports that one of the new Trump licensing LLCs was formed the same day as the July 2025 permitting order. CNBC found no evidence that the payments influenced the order. The order is general policy, not a Damac-specific approval.
3. **Reliance: a $10M fee for a project that may not exist, then a sanctions licence (payment documented; pairing inferred).** A Reliance unit paid the Trump Organization a $10M development fee for a Mumbai licence that the WSJ could not tie to any project. In February 2026 the US issued Reliance a licence to buy Venezuelan oil directly. CREW draws the connection. No source shows causation, and Reliance declined to comment.
4. **Kinh Bac: the partner gains from tariff policy itself (documented; Forbes framing single-source).** Beyond the $5M licence fee and the fast-tracked approval (C59), Forbes reports that Dang Thanh Tam's industrial-park business grew on tariff-driven supply-chain shifts. The partner's interest in US-Vietnam tariff outcomes is therefore commercial and direct, not only through the golf licence.
5. **Sokolov / Tripp+ (documented).** The State Department named Konstantin Sokolov, a White House ballroom donor with Armenian telecom and mining holdings and a past running a Kremlin-adjacent investment firm (Reuters), to chair the $201M fund attached to the Trump-brokered South Caucasus corridor. This is the only Azerbaijan-region connection found, and it is a donor-to-appointment link, not a family business deal.

## Not connected in the coverage we found (our own cross-referencing)

- **Serbia NIS waivers and the Kushner Belgrade hotel.** Inferred; no source links them. The timing argues *against* a favour: the waiver was refused in October 2025 while the project was still alive.
- **Reliance's Venezuela licence and its $10M Trump fee.** Inferred by us; CREW frames it as "wins" without asserting causation.
- **Metaplanet (Eric Trump) and SoftBank or the US-Japan $550B deal.** Inferred and weak; no source links them.
- **Damac's July 2025 Trump LLC formation and the permitting order.** The date match is CREW's (single-source). That the order benefited Damac specifically is not shown.
- **Indonesia's tariff cut and MNC.** Inferred from the hot mic. The identity of "Hary" is unconfirmed, and Jakarta acted *against* the Trump project.
- **Pakistan's tariff, the Munir lunch, the WLF deals and Powerus.** Each element is documented. That they are linked is inference.

## Corrections

- "Bilt" has no documented Trump-family connection.
- Damac's 2025 fee total is reported as $12.5M (The National, CREW) or $11.3M (CNBC).
- No second-term family deal was found in El Salvador or Azerbaijan. Both have only government-level US actions.
- Indonesia halted the Trump-linked Lido project rather than favouring it.
- The NIS sanctions waiver was refused while the Belgrade project was alive.

## Denials and other side

- **White House:** the president "only acts in the best interests of the American public", there are "no conflicts of interest", and trade talks are "totally unrelated to the Trump Organization".
- **Trump Organization:** says it is fully compliant with ethics laws, and that the Diriyah deal was not finalized while the president was negotiating the nuclear agreement.
- **Powerus side:** Don Jr.'s spokesperson says he had no role. Powerus calls the brothers passive investors.
- **WLF:** says it is not compensated under the Pakistan MoU. White House counsel says Steve Witkoff is divesting from WLF.
- **CZ:** says he "didn't do much" to get the pardon. Trump said "I don't know who he is".
- **Albania:** Rama calls reports of a sale "lies".
- **Reliance:** declined to comment.
- **Ethics policy:** the 2025 policy permits deals with private foreign firms.
