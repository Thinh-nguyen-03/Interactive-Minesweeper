# C113: US decisions on Israel since January 2025

**Verdict:** mostly-accurate (confidence: medium)

**Question:** What has the administration decided on Israel since January 2025, with dates and amounts?

## Summary

The record since 20 Jan 2025 documents many US decisions that Israel's government sought: release of held bomb shipments (Jan-Feb 2025), DSCA notifications worth several billion dollars (about $6.75B and $660M on 7 Feb 2025, $510M on 30 Jun 2025, about $6.67B on 30 Jan 2026), emergency determinations skipping congressional review (1 Mar 2025, Mar 2026), the ICC sanctions order (EO 14203, 6 Feb 2025) with later designations through 9 Oct 2026, lifting of settler sanctions (20 Jan 2025), US vetoes of Gaza ceasefire drafts, joint strikes on Iran, and Trump's repeated public calls for a Netanyahu pardon (Knesset 13 Oct 2025, letter 12 Nov 2025). It also records decisions that cut against Israel's government: the Qatar security guarantee and Netanyahu's apology (29 Sep 2025), annexation warnings, a 15% tariff above the 10% Israel sought, and exclusion from US-Iran talks. No pardon has been reported. The DSCA notices themselves were blocked, so values rest on secondary excerpts, and nothing here shows that any donation or investment caused any decision.

Every row is an act of the US government unless stated; none is an Israeli company or an American donor. Today is 17 Nov 2026.

## Sub-claims

### Since 20 Jan 2025 the administration released shipments of heavy bombs that the previous administration had held, and the first deliveries followed.

**Verdict:** confirmed

Act of the US government (Pentagon/White House); Israel's government requested it. Grade: documented by multiple outlets (excerpts). On 24-25 Jan 2025 Trump said the 2,000-pound bombs would go ahead ('Because they bought them'), ending the Biden-era hold imposed in May 2024; Israel's defence ministry announced receipt of a shipment of MK-84 bombs around 16 Feb 2025. Counts differ by outlet (about 1,800 MK-84 and a further batch of about 1,700 500-pound bombs). No dollar value is attached; these were previously purchased items. No legal instrument beyond a Pentagon decision was found.

Sources: https://www.newarab.com/news/trump-ends-biden-hold-sending-2000-pound-bombs-israel, https://www.newarab.com/news/israel-receives-shipment-heavy-bombs-cleared-trump

### Formal Congress notifications of Israel arms sales (DSCA) since 20 Jan 2025, with values.

**Verdict:** confirmed

Act of the US government at Israel's request. Grade: documented in DSCA notifications (dsca.mil returns 403 to plain curl, so every notice below was read as a search excerpt or secondary summary, not the notice itself). 7 Feb 2025: munitions guidance kits, fuzes and support, about $6.75B; and 3,000 AGM-114 Hellfire missiles, $660M (transmittal 24-104). 28 Feb 2025: two notifications, about $2.04B munitions and $295M Caterpillar D9 bulldozers (see emergency item). 14 Apr 2025: Eitan armoured-vehicle powerpack engines, $180M. 30 Jun 2025: 3,845 KMU-558B/B and 3,280 KMU-572 F/B JDAM guidance kits plus support, $510M, principal contractor Boeing. 30 Jan 2026: four packages totalling about $6.67B, including AH-64E Apache helicopters $3.8B and Namer power packs $740M (JLTV and AW119Kx values not retrieved). Sales are paid for by Israel (or with US FMF funds); a notification is not a delivery. Possible further notices (for example Nov 2025 to May 2026 Federal Register entries) were not individually read, so this list may be incomplete.

### The administration used emergency determinations under the Arms Export Control Act to skip congressional review of sales to Israel.

**Verdict:** confirmed

Act of the US government (Secretary of State Rubio) at Israel's request. Grade: documented by several outlets (excerpts; the determinations themselves were not read). (a) Declared 1 Mar 2025: emergency waiver covering the 28 Feb 2025 notifications (about $2.04B munitions and $295M D9 bulldozers; the same declaration was described as fast-tracking about $4B), bypassing the informal committee pre-clearance practice. (b) About 6-7 Mar 2026, during the war with Iran: 12,000 BLU-110A/B 1,000-lb bomb bodies, $151.8M, principal contractor Repkon USA; Rep. Gregory Meeks called it 'an emergency of the Trump administration's own creation'. One NYT-based report put two packages at nearly $660M; the $151.8M figure is for the single notice cited. (c) May 2026: 10,000 APKWS rounds, $992.4M, reported in a search summary only (not verified against the notice). Context: the Biden administration also used emergency waivers for Israel (December 2023).

### The International Criminal Court was sanctioned by executive order, and further designations followed.

**Verdict:** confirmed

Act of the US government; the order cites the ICC arrest warrants for Netanyahu and Gallant, and Israel's government sought it and welcomed it. Grade: documented. EO 14203 (6 Feb 2025, read in full) invokes IEEPA, the National Emergencies Act, INA 212(f) and 3 USC 301, and says the ICC engaged in 'illegitimate and baseless actions targeting America and our close ally Israel'. Designations: Prosecutor Karim Khan (Feb 2025); four judges on 5 Jun 2025 (two on the Afghanistan inquiry, two on the Netanyahu/Gallant warrants); two judges and two deputy prosecutors on 20 Aug 2025; two Appeals Chamber judges on 18 Dec 2025 (State Department release headed 'Targeting of Israel'); two ICC officials (Tomoko Akane, Abdoulaye Seye) in Aug 2026; and the ICC as an institution on 9 Oct 2026 (Global Sanctions and a Council on Foreign Relations-hosted summary; a Treasury notice of 15 May 2026 on the program was found but its content was not shown). Three ICC judges have sued over the sanctions; the ICC called them an attack on judicial independence.

Sources: https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2025/02/imposing-sanctions-on-the-international-criminal-court/, https://www.hrw.org/news/2025/06/05/us-sanctions-international-criminal-court-judges, https://www.hrw.org/news/2025/08/20/us-sanctions-two-additional-icc-judges-and-the-courts-deputy-prosecutors, https://www.globalsecurity.org/military/library/news/2025/12/mil-251218-state02.htm, https://globalsanctions.com/2026/10/us-designates-international-criminal-court/, https://cof.org/node/20406, https://ofac.treasury.gov/recent-actions/20260515

### Sanctions on West Bank settlers were lifted on the first day.

**Verdict:** confirmed

Act of the US government that Israel's government sought. Grade: documented. The Initial Rescissions order of 20 Jan 2025 (read in full) lists Executive Order 14115 of 1 Feb 2024 ('Imposing Certain Sanctions on Persons Undermining Peace, Security, and Stability in the West Bank'). OFAC published the termination of the emergency and designation removals on 24 Jan 2025. Outlets reported more than 30 settler individuals and entities were affected (Al Jazeera figure not independently checked).

### The US voted against Gaza ceasefire drafts in the UN Security Council and later sponsored the resolution endorsing its own Gaza plan.

**Verdict:** mostly-accurate

Act of the US government; Israel's government opposed the ceasefire drafts as framed. Grade: documented by several outlets (UN meeting records were blocked to curl; excerpts only). 4 Jun 2025: draft by the elected ten members, 14 in favour, US the sole vote against (veto); the US objected that it did not link a ceasefire to hostage release. 18 Sep 2025: a further draft for an immediate, unconditional, permanent ceasefire, reported 14-1 with the US veto (the 14-1 count was not stated by a dated source in the excerpts). 17 Nov 2025: Resolution 2803, drafted by the US, endorsing the Trump Gaza plan, a Board of Peace and a temporary stabilisation force, adopted 13-0 with China and Russia abstaining.

### The US struck Iranian nuclear sites in June 2025 and took part with Israel in a war on Iran from 28 Feb 2026.

**Verdict:** confirmed

Acts of the US government in conjunction with Israel's military; a US-Israel coordination was publicly stated. Grade: documented, secondary excerpts. 13 Jun 2025 Israel began strikes; 22 Jun 2025 US strikes on Fordow, Natanz, Isfahan (Operation Midnight Hammer); ceasefire 24 Jun 2025. Dates of the 2026 sequence differ between outlets; war-powers or congressional votes were not researched.

**Verdict:** partly-accurate

Act of the US government; Israel's government sought lower rates. Grade: documented in trade and Israeli press, no primary Federal Register text read. A tariff-tracker claim of a 12.5% Section 301 duty from 24 Jul 2026 could not be verified and is not included in the record.

### Annual military aid under the 2016 memorandum continued, with talks on a successor and on winding down aid.

**Verdict:** mostly-accurate

Act of the US and Israeli governments. Grade: documented for the MOU terms, single-source and preliminary for 2026 negotiations. The 2016 MOU (FY2019-2028) provides about $3.8B a year: $3.3B in foreign military financing plus $500M for cooperative missile defence, appropriated annually by Congress. Netanyahu has said he wants to taper aid over a decade; the two governments announced on 5 Jun 2026 formal talks on a successor; Rubio confirmed discussion of an Israeli proposal to wind down aid. Reports on the outcome conflict (aid halved; a five-year package of about $16.5B; Trump quoted as saying aid would end by end of 2026). No Israel-specific emergency supplemental for the 2026 Iran war was found in searches; this is a 'nothing found' result, not proof none exists.

### The administration also pressed Israel against its government's wishes: Qatar guarantee, apology, annexation.

**Verdict:** confirmed

Acts and statements of the US government that Israel's government opposed or accepted under pressure. Grade: documented. 9 Sep 2025 Israel struck Hamas officials in Doha (six killed, including a Qatari security officer per the White House). 29 Sep 2025 Trump signed an order (published 1 Oct, read in full) stating the US policy 'to guarantee the security and territorial integrity of the State of Qatar against external attack' and committing to 'diplomatic, economic, and, if necessary, military' measures. The same day Netanyahu telephoned an apology to Qatar's prime minister from the White House (reported by Axios and Ynet; the White House gave few details). Annexation: 15 Oct 2025 (Time) Trump said it 'won't happen' and that Israel would lose all US support; after a 25-24 preliminary Knesset vote on 22 Oct 2025, Vance called it 'a very stupid political stunt' and Trump said Israel 'is not going to do anything with the West Bank'; Rubio said it could threaten the peace deal. Netanyahu's office distanced itself from the bills. In Apr 2026 Israel was left out of the US-Iran ceasefire talks. The Oct 2025 Gaza ceasefire and hostage release was a US-brokered arrangement the Israeli government accepted.

## Corrections

- Some summaries describe the March 2025 emergency as a single $4B package: the notifications behind it were about $2.04B (munitions) and $295M (bulldozers), with the roughly $4B figure referring to the wider fast-tracked aid as described by one outlet.
- The 2026 emergency sale is $151.8M for the notice in question; a figure of 'nearly $660 million' from one NYT-based report refers to two packages and does not match it.
- The West Bank settler order is often dated 24 Jan 2025 (OFAC action); the revocation of EO 14115 is in the 20 Jan 2025 rescissions order, with OFAC's follow-up on 24 Jan.
- Not located: a dated source stating the 14-1 count for the 18 Sep 2025 veto; the exact text of Trump's Truth Social posts on the pardon (only reports of them); any 2026 supplemental for Israel.

## Denials and context

- The administration describes sales as in the US national security interest and says the sales do not change the regional military balance; Israel pays for sales (or uses FMF).
- Democratic members (for example Rep. Meeks) and arms-control groups objected to the use of emergency waivers; Republicans and pro-Israel groups welcomed them. Biden-era emergency waivers for Israel and the earlier hold on bombs show the instruments are not new.
- Not every decision favoured Israel's government: the Qatar security guarantee, Netanyahu's apology to Qatar, the annexation warnings, the 15% tariff (above the 10% Israel sought) and Israel's exclusion from the Apr 2026 US-Iran talks.
- Herzog's office disputes Trump's account that a pardon was promised. Netanyahu denies the charges and has called the trial a political persecution; critics in Israel and the US say Trump's pressure is interference in Israeli justice.
- This is a dated record only. Nothing here asserts that donations, investments or business ties of any person caused any decision; related money flows are in C110 and sibling checks.
