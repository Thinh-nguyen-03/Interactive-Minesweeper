# C91: Dominari option grants, pre-announcement trading and the Trump sons' arrival

**Verdict:** mostly accurate (medium confidence)

**Summary.** The figures in the claim hold up against the filings.

- **The grant.** On Feb. 10, 2025, the day before Donald Trump Jr. and Eric Trump were announced as advisers, Dominari's compensation committee gave CEO Anthony Hayes and president Kyle Wool fully vested options on 5M shares each (10M in total).
- **The price and conditions.** The options were struck at fair market value, $6.16, which was the close after a roughly 530-580% six-week run-up. They could not be exercised until stockholders approved a 10M-share plan increase on April 1, 2025.
- **The placement.** The same day, Hayes, Wool and other insiders bought into the $3.47 private placement alongside the sons.
- **The pay.** Bloomberg puts the pair's 2025 pay at about $66M. That figure includes option value as well as cash.

Three parts of the claim are weaker:
- **Who traded is unknown.** No source identifies who bought during the run-up.
- **No inquiry or lawsuit.** No SEC, FINRA or Nasdaq inquiry and no filed shareholder suit were found.
- **The pattern does not generalise.** Of the comparators, only Unusual Machines (documented) and SRM/Tron (single source) show a run-up before the announcement. Thumzup and Gryphon do not.

The hunt also found a new layer: three further Trump Organization executives are Dominari shareholders, and two of them also sit on a Yorkville SPAC board.

**Access note.** sec.gov, panabee.com, quantisnow.com and equibles.com could not be read directly. Filing details below come from search-result excerpts of the filings and from secondary coverage, cross-checked where possible.

## The claim

This is a third-layer hunt from C39. An analysis cited in C39's leads alleges three things:
- 10M options were granted on Feb. 10, 2025, the day before the announcement.
- The stock rose about 530-580% in the six weeks before.
- Executives were paid about $66M for 2025.

This check looks at the proxy statement and Form 4s, establish who traded, look for any inquiry or lawsuit, and compare Unusual Machines, Thumzup, Gryphon and SRM/Tron.

The parents already establish:
- the sons' stakes (about 6.7% each), the Trump Tower base, and the 84% jump on the announcement day (C39);
- the SRM/Tron deal, the House China committee letter and the FINRA fine on Revere (C47);
- Wool's takeover of the New America SPAC (C78).

None of that is repeated here.

## Connections and findings, with evidence grades

1. **The 10M option grant on Feb. 10, 2025: confirmed (documented).**
   - The 8-K reports a nonqualified option on 5,000,000 shares each to Wool and Hayes, at fair market value on the grant date, with a 10-year term.
   - The 10-Q says the options were "fully vested" and conditioned on stockholder approval of a reserve increase [1][2].
   - The FY2025 annual report lists each grant at a **$6.16** exercise price, expiring Feb. 10, 2035. The proxy shows $30.8M of disclosed value per grant [3][4]. *Audit:* Hayes's Form 4 gives the expiry as Feb. 9, 2035 and says the option becomes exercisable on filing of a Form S-8. The $30.8M equals 5M x $6.16; the audit could not confirm whether the proxy states it or it was derived.
   - Aggregator Form 4 listings show the awards reported on Apr. 16, 2025, after the approval.
   - One aggregator figure ("10M to Wool at $3.85") conflicts with the filings and is discarded.

2. **The strike was set after the run-up; the gain came from the announcement: confirmed (prices documented; the arithmetic is ours).**
   - Barron's: DOMH rose 529%, from $0.98 on Dec. 31 to $6.16 at the Feb. 10 close [13]. The FT figure is about 580% [17].
   - Bloomberg: up as much as 84% on Feb. 11 [14]. Axios: about $13 at the Feb. 13 close [15].
   - *Our own arithmetic, which no source states:* at $13 the 10M options carried about $68M of intrinsic value. The stock ended 2025 just under $5, so the options were then under water.

3. **Insiders bought at $3.47 alongside the sons on grant day: confirmed (documented).**
   - Wool's Form 4 and 13D show 288,184 shares plus Series A ($3.72) and Series B warrants, at a combined $3.47, for about $1M of personal funds. That brought his beneficial holding to 2,200,769 shares, including 1,241,989 held by his spouse Soo Yu [9].
   - Hayes also bought in the placement: Investing.com gives 288,184 shares, the same as Wool [10]. His direct holding of 975,007 shares afterwards comes from one aggregator [11].
   - Forbes: the CEO, COO, president and four directors bought 664,000 shares at $3.47, and six insiders received 402,000 bonus shares [16].
   - *Audit:* Investing.com also reports COO Christopher Devall buying 351,851 shares and director Kyle Haug 14,409. With Hayes and Wool that is 942,628 shares (our arithmetic), more than Forbes's 664,000. The mismatch is unresolved and may reflect how units or warrants were counted.
   - Dominari called the $13.5M raise "priced at the market under Nasdaq rules" [12]. That price was about 44% below (our arithmetic) the $6.16 close used for the options the same day.
   - *Inferred, no source states it:* the most likely reason is that Nasdaq's minimum-price test can use a five-day average, which lags a fast-rising stock.
   - No open-market buying by Hayes or Wool during January 2025 appears in coverage. This is unverified, because the Form 4s could not be read directly.

4. **The stockholder vote that unlocked the options: confirmed (documented); the voting-power point is inferred.**
   - The special meeting was held Apr. 1, 2025, with a **Feb. 24 record date**. It raised the plan reserve from 1,404,404 to 11,404,404 shares.
   - Quorum was 6,027,100 shares (about 42%). The amendment passed 5,684,835 for, 321,884 against and 20,381 abstaining [5][6][7].
   - *Inferred, no source makes this point:* the record date fell after the Feb. 10-12 issuances. The sons (966,138 shares each) and Hayes and Wool (about 3.2M with Wool's spouse) together held about 5.1M shares, close to the 5.68M votes in favour.
   - How anyone actually voted is not disclosed, and some of those shares may not have been entitled to vote.

5. **About $66M in 2025 pay: mostly accurate (totals documented; breakdown single-source).**
   - Bloomberg: Hayes received about $34M and Wool almost $32M [20][21].
   - An aggregator's breakdown of Wool's pay: $0.5M salary, $11M cash bonus, $0.86M stock, $13.1M options and $6.2M non-equity incentive [8]. So the headline figure mixes cash with the grant-date accounting value of options.
   - June 2025: both employment agreements were amended. The executives gave up further stock grants in exchange for a cash bonus tied to net revenue [22].
   - March 4, 2026: stockholders approved another 10M-share increase. The proxy contemplated 6M of it for special awards to Hayes and Wool, plus an evergreen of up to 20% of outstanding shares each year from 2027 to 2032 [23][24].

6. **Who traded before the announcement: unverified.**
   - Forbes: daily volume averaged about 1.2M shares from Dec. 30, against about 11,500 before [16]. TipRanks gives other figures [18].
   - The FT quoted Adam Pritchard (University of Michigan), who called the trading "clearly unusual" and said an adviser may not face the restrictions a director does [17][19].
   - No buyer has been named. The sons each stayed below 10% and so filed no Form 4s (C39).

7. **Regulatory inquiry or lawsuits: none found.**
   - No SEC, FINRA or Nasdaq inquiry into the trading was found through November 2026.
   - Purcell & Lefkowitz announced a "shareholder investigation" on Oct. 27, 2025 into whether directors breached their duties over unspecified "recent corporate actions" [25][26]. That is a solicitation by a plaintiffs' firm, not a filed complaint.
   - The April 2025 letter from Democratic senators asking the SEC to investigate insider trading concerned the tariff pause, not Dominari [27].
   - Official scrutiny of Dominari remains limited to the House China committee letter of March 2026 [28] and the FINRA fine on Revere [35] (C47).

8. **Other Trump Organization executives hold Dominari stock: confirmed (documented).**
   - The May 2025 S-3 registered 13.94M shares for resale [31]. Its selling stockholders include Don Jr., Eric, **Alan Garten** (chief legal officer), **Lawrence Glick** (EVP Development) and **Ronald Lieberman** (EVP, and a Dominari director since December 2024).
   - Together they hold about 5.03M shares, about 20%. Garten, Glick and Lieberman account for about 8% [29][30].
   - Bloomberg (via Raw Story) separately reports that three other Trump Organization executives hold shares [32].
   - When and at what price Garten and Glick bought is not established.

9. **Revere related-party revenue: mostly accurate (single-source filing excerpt).**
   - Wool held 30% of Revere until May 2025.
   - Dominari earned $5.8M in 2025 and $930K in 2024 from underwritings in which Revere also took part [8].
   - FINRA fined Revere $800K in October 2026 [35]. FINRA does not name Wool.

10. **Comparator run-ups:**
    - **Unusual Machines: confirmed (documented).** The stock rose more than 220% in the four weeks before Don Jr. was appointed on Nov. 27, 2024. Average daily volume was 290K shares, against 93K before. A lawyer at Osborne Clarke said the pattern had "the potential to involve insider dealing" [19][36].
    - **SRM/Tron: mostly accurate (single source).** Protos reports a 67% rise in the two days before the June 16, 2025 deal placed by Dominari, with no visible leak [37][38].
    - **Thumzup: false as a pre-announcement anomaly.** The stock had risen strongly year to date, but it fell 14-17% when Don Jr.'s stake was disclosed on July 9, 2025 [39][40].
    - **Gryphon: false as a pre-announcement anomaly.** The roughly 300% jump came on the May 12, 2025 announcement day, and no earlier run-up was reported [41][42]. Daily price data was not checked.

## Not connected in the coverage we found (our own cross-referencing)

- **The option grant, the advisory agreements and the insider placement purchases were all dated Feb. 10, 2025.** Grade: documented separately; no source analyses them together as a package. Inferred.
- **The Feb. 24 record date let shares issued to insiders and the sons that week vote on the plan increase that unlocked the options.** Grade: inferred. The vote breakdown is not disclosed.
- **The options were struck at $6.16 while insiders bought at $3.47 the same day.** Grade: both prices documented; the comparison is ours.
- **Garten and Glick hold Dominari stock and also sit on the Yorkville-controlled Texas Ventures III board (C78).** Each fact is documented, but no source connects the two Trump Organization-adjacent financial networks through them. Inferred.
- **The options moved from about $68M in the money (Feb. 13) to under water (year-end 2025).** Grade: inferred from published prices.

## Corrections

- The options did not capture the pre-announcement run-up. They were struck at the Feb. 10 close and became exercisable only after the April 1 vote.
- The "$66M paid" figure includes grant-date option value, not just cash.
- Thumzup and Gryphon show no reported run-up before their announcements.
- No regulatory inquiry and no filed lawsuit exists in the record. The only shareholder item is a plaintiffs' firm's investigation notice.

## Denials and other side

- **Dominari** had "no reason to believe that any of these people breached any duty, whether legal or fiduciary," and did not know why investors bought (C39).
- **Dominari spokesman Brett Maas** said no advisers or investors had sold (May 2025).
- **Don Jr.** bought Thumzup on his adviser's recommendation and has no other role there (Bloomberg via The Block).
- **What the record does not show.** A run-up before an announcement can reflect momentum or speculation as well as leaks. No source shows that anyone traded on nonpublic information.
- **The grants were disclosed.** They were priced at market and approved by stockholders.

Result: **upheld**.

Checked:
- 5M-share options each to Hayes and Wool, granted 10 Feb 2025 at a $6.16 strike
- Hayes, Wool and other insiders bought at $3.47 in the same placement as the Trump sons (Forbes: 664,000 shares by seven insiders)
- Bloomberg: about $66M of 2025 pay ($34M Hayes, almost $32M Wool)

Changes:
- Options: $6.16 strike confirmed by Hayes's Form 4; recorded that the Form 4 gives a 9 Feb 2035 expiry and S-8 exercisability, and flagged the $30.8M figure as equal to 5M x $6.16 (source vs. derived not confirmed).
- Placement: labelled the '44% below' figure as our arithmetic; added Investing.com's per-insider counts (Hayes 288,184; Devall 351,851; Haug 14,409) and the unresolved mismatch with Forbes's 664,000 total; updated Hayes's relationship label.
- Pay: confirmed Bloomberg's figures; recorded a reported, unconfirmed Dominari rebuttal in denials.
- No SEC/FINRA/Nasdaq inquiry or filed suit found through 14 Nov 2026.
