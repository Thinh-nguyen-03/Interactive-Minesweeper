# C89: Unannounced Trump Organization deals: Hungary, Serbia and others in the filings

**Verdict: partly-accurate (confidence: medium)**

This is a third-layer connection check that extends C82 (Abu Dhabi entities), C45 (Kushner's Albania and Serbia projects), C59 (foreign deals and tariffs) and C70 (foreign intermediaries). It does not repeat them.

## Bottom line

The claim's starting point holds up. Forbes (24 Jul 2025) read corporate filings as pointing to at least three unannounced deals, in **Abu Dhabi, Serbia and Hungary**. It also reported new entities named for Pune, Noida, Gurgaon, Hungary's capital and the Philippines, all registered after the November 2024 election.

The Hungary entities now have names: **DT Marks Budapest LLC** and **DT Marks Budapest Member Corp.** They appear in CREW's August 2026 freedom-of-information requests to Hungarian ministries.

The **Serbian entity's name, formation date, partner and fee were not found** in anything I could reach. Nor was any fee for Budapest. The 2025 disclosure, as summarised by PolitiFact and CNBC, lists licensing income from Oman, Qatar, the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Romania, the Philippines, Vietnam and India. It lists none from Hungary or Serbia.

This check asked for a complete list of every DT Marks/DTTM entity formed since November 2024, with country, partner and fee. **That cannot be built from public reporting.** The Delaware register, congress.gov and govinfo were not consulted, and the entity-level data CREW cites was not accessible. The table below gives what is documented and marks the rest.

The most useful new link is not in the filings. It is a **partner overlap**. Mohamed Alabbar's Abu Dhabi-based **Eagle Hills** was Kushner's partner on the Trump-branded Belgrade project (announced 17 Jan 2025). Three days later, on 20 Jan 2025 (inauguration day), Eagle Hills became the Orbán government's developer for the EUR 12.3B "Grand Budapest" district. A VSquare source said Kushner might join it, and a Trump-style tower was floated. Budapest's municipality exercised a pre-emption right, and the government dropped the project in early 2025. The Trump-linked Budapest entities exist, but no Budapest project or fee has surfaced.

US policy toward Hungary in 2025 was unusually favourable:

- sanctions on Orbán's cabinet chief Antal Rogán lifted (15 Apr 2025);
- Paks II nuclear sanctions relief, announced by Hungary (Jun 2025);
- a one-year exemption from Russian oil and gas sanctions (7 Nov 2025);
- a "financial shield" that Orbán claimed and Trump later denied;
- Vance campaigning for Orbán before his April 2026 defeat.

These line up in time with Don Jr.'s April 2025 Budapest trip and the dormant DT Marks Budapest entities. **No source links them**, and the policy has obvious ideological and geopolitical explanations.

For Serbia, timing still argues against a favour. Treasury refused the NIS waiver in October 2025 while the Belgrade project was alive (C70). The 2026 relief came after the project collapsed: repeated OFAC licences for a sale to Hungary's MOL, the 35% tariff suspended, and a strategic dialogue launched on 17 Jul 2026.

## Connections (sub-claims)

1. **Forbes: filings point to unannounced Serbia and Hungary deals.** *Single-source (Forbes), echoed by its licensed editions.* The 24 Jul 2025 piece: "Corporate filings point to at least three other unannounced deals—in Abu Dhabi, Serbia and Hungary." It also says the Trumps "registered entities named after three Indian locales—Pune, Noida and Gurgaon—as well as Hungary's capital and the Philippines." I could read it only through search snippets; forbes.com and its regional sites could not be read directly.
2. **DT Marks Budapest LLC and DT Marks Budapest Member Corp. exist.** *Documented* by CREW's FOI requests of Aug 2026 to Hungary's Trade Licensing Office and Ministry of Construction and Transport. The requests name both companies and ask for records on a Trump property in Budapest and on Kushner's role in "Grand Budapest". The formation date is not public in what I could reach; Forbes places it after the Nov 2024 election and before Jul 2025.
3. **Eagle Hills / Alabbar: the shared partner across Belgrade and Budapest.** *Documented for each leg; the overlap is reported by VSquare.*
   - Eagle Hills and Affinity announced the Trump-branded Belgrade project around 17 Jan 2025 (C45, Semafor).
   - On 18 Jan 2025 Orbán met Vučić in Belgrade. VSquare's source says Orbán briefed him on Grand Budapest; the official readouts do not mention it (*single-source*).
   - On 20 Jan 2025 Hungary announced the EUR 12.3B Grand Budapest contract with Eagle Hills.
   - A VSquare source close to the government said Kushner "may become involved", possibly with a Trump-Tower-style skyscraper. Another source said Affinity was "not currently involved". Neither company commented. Kushner's role is **unverified**.
   - Orbán was photographed with Alabbar during an Abu Dhabi state visit.
4. **Grand Budapest was dropped before any Trump deal surfaced.** *Documented.* Budapest's municipal company exercised a pre-emption right over the Rákosrendező site, and the government abandoned the project in early 2025 (bne IntelliNews, Property Forum). The DT Marks Budapest entities were formed in the same window, but no Budapest project, partner or fee has been disclosed. Whether the entities were meant for Grand Budapest is **inferred**: no source says so, though CREW's FOI request asks about both together.
5. **Don Jr.'s Budapest and Belgrade visits.** *Documented.*
   - Don Jr. met Vučić in Belgrade on 11 Mar 2025, while Kushner's Trump-branded project was pending. He had dinner with Vučić again on his late-April regional trip (Bloomberg).
   - On 25 Apr 2025 he had a brief, informal encounter with Foreign Minister Szijjártó in Budapest (Telex: officially nothing was discussed, and it was closed to the press) and spoke at the invitation-only "Trump Business Vision 2025" forum, organised by Zoltán Aczél and Portfolio. Reports said he was expected to meet 4iG chairman Gellért Jászai.
   - Bloomberg framed the trip as aimed at expanding the family's business ties. No deal was announced. His spokesperson rejected conflict-of-interest suggestions in Serbia.
6. **Hungary: US decisions in the same period.** *Each decision documented; the link to the family's business is inferred.*
   - **15 Apr 2025:** Treasury removed Antal Rogán, Orbán's cabinet chief, from its sanctions list. The Biden administration had imposed the sanctions in Jan 2025 over corruption in public contracting. State said continued designation was inconsistent with US interests (Bloomberg called it "Trump's gift to Orbán"). This was ten days before Don Jr.'s Budapest visit. Senators Shaheen and Warren said the Global Magnitsky Act requires 15 days' notice to Congress before a termination. No source links the delisting to the trip.
   - **~29 Jun 2025:** Hungary said the US had lifted sanctions blocking the Rosatom-built Paks II expansion. This rests mainly on the Hungarian government's account; I found no detailed US confirmation.
   - **7 Nov 2025:** After a White House meeting, Hungary got a one-year exemption from US sanctions on Russian oil and gas. The White House said one year; Orbán claimed it was open-ended. One account ties it to more than $1.4B of Hungarian purchase commitments for US nuclear, defence and energy.
   - **9 Nov 2025:** Orbán claimed a US "financial shield", described as a currency swap. In Dec 2025 Trump denied promising it, and Szijjártó said talks continued. I found no swap line finalised.
   - **Apr 2026:** Vance campaigned in person for Orbán. Tisza's Péter Magyar won the 12 Apr 2026 election in a landslide (RFE/RL, CNN).
   - I found no report on what happened to the US exemption under the Magyar government. Hungary's EU pipeline-oil exemption runs to 2027.
7. **Serbia: Belgrade licence and US decisions.** *Project documented; licensing entity and fee unverified; the policy pairing is inferred and cuts against a favour.*
   - In Jan 2025 Kushner said he and Eric Trump had "agreed on licensing". Eric Trump said the contract would be with the developer, not the state that owns the land.
   - Vreme refers to a contract signed at the outset, and asks whether Kushner could claim about EUR 50M from Serbia. No claim has been confirmed.
   - Treasury refused the NIS sanctions deferral in Oct 2025 while the project was alive (C70). The project collapsed on 15 Dec 2025 (C45).
   - Gazprom Neft and MOL signed binding heads of agreement for the 56.15% NIS stake on 19 Jan 2026.
   - OFAC kept extending NIS's operating licences and MOL's negotiation licences through 2026. On ~5 Nov 2026 it gave MOL a licence to 28 Feb 2027 for preparatory work, which does not authorise closing.
   - The US suspended its 35% tariff on Serbia in Jul 2026; Serbian officials partly credit a US Supreme Court ruling. A strategic dialogue was launched on 17 Jul 2026.
8. **The NIS resolution routes through Orbán's MOL and possibly ADNOC.** *Documented that MOL is the buyer and that ADNOC may take a minority stake. The cross-link to this story is inferred.* The US-approved way out of Serbia's sanctions problem ran through MOL, Hungary's national oil champion, which is close to the Orbán government. An Abu Dhabi state company may take part, and Abu Dhabi capital (Eagle Hills) was also behind the Belgrade and Budapest projects. No source connects the NIS licensing to any Trump-family interest.
9. **No Hungary or Serbia fee in the 2025 disclosure.** *Documented as an absence, from secondary summaries.* PolitiFact's country list for about $65M of overseas licensing deals, and CNBC's $59.5M analysis, name Oman, Qatar, the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Romania, the Philippines, Vietnam and India. I could not read the 927-page OGE form itself, so a small Hungary or Serbia line cannot be excluded.

## Corrections

- The claim calls Hungary and Serbia "deals". The filings show **entities**, not signed deals with known partners or fees. Budapest's anchor project was dropped in early 2025. Belgrade collapsed in Dec 2025.
- The "currency-swap/financial shield" was claimed by Orbán and **denied by Trump** (Dec 2025). It is not an established US decision.
- The Russian-energy exemption is a one-year exemption per the White House, not the open-ended exemption Orbán claimed.
- The NIS relief came **after** the Belgrade project died, and is tied to a sale to MOL. In Oct 2025, while the project was alive, the waiver was refused.

## Denials and context

- The White House says the president's dealings are unrelated to the Trump Organization, and that neither he nor his family has conflicts of interest. The Trump Organization says it only licenses its brand to private developers. Its 2025 ethics policy bars only deals directly with foreign governments.
- Eric Trump: the Belgrade hotel contract would be with the developer, not the Serbian state.
- Don Jr.'s spokesperson rejected conflict-of-interest claims over his Serbia visit.
- Affinity Partners and Eagle Hills did not answer VSquare about Budapest. A source said Affinity was not involved.
- There are other explanations for the Hungary decisions. Orbán was the administration's closest ideological ally in the EU. Energy exemptions for landlocked pipeline-dependent states also have EU parallels, since Hungary and Slovakia hold EU carve-outs. And the Rogán delisting followed a Biden-era designation made days before he left office.
- **Research limits:** forbes.com and its regional sites, citizensforethics.org, congress.gov, govinfo.gov and the Delaware register could not be read directly. Findings rest on search-result snippets from several outlets.

## Not connected in the coverage we found

These links come from our own cross-referencing. No outlet asserts them.

- **DT Marks Budapest formed for Grand Budapest / Eagle Hills.** *Inferred.* Both sit in the same Nov 2024 to Jul 2025 window, and CREW asks about them together, but no source says the entity was for that project.
- **The Rogán delisting (15 Apr 2025) and Don Jr.'s Budapest business trip (25 Apr 2025).** *Inferred, timing only.*
- **The Nov 2025 Russian-energy exemption and the dormant Budapest licensing entities.** *Inferred, timing only.* No fee or project has been disclosed.
- **Eagle Hills as the common Gulf partner in the Belgrade and Budapest Trump-adjacent projects.** *Documented for each project.* The pattern is our observation, except for VSquare's single-source report of the Orbán-Vučić briefing.
- **The MOL/ADNOC route for NIS as a Hungary-Serbia-UAE loop.** *Inferred.* The deal facts are documented; no source ties them to the Trump family.
- **The Serbian DT Marks entity as the Belgrade licensee.** *Inferred.* Forbes does not name the entity, and Kushner only said licensing was agreed.

## What this does and does not show

It shows that Trump's licensing arm opened Hungary and Serbia vehicles in the first half of 2025. Those vehicles came alongside a Trump-branded Belgrade project with Gulf partner Eagle Hills, a Budapest megaproject by the same partner, and visits by Don Jr. to both leaders. It also shows that Hungary won a run of US sanctions decisions in 2025. It does not show that any fee was paid for Hungary or Serbia, that any US decision was traded for business, or that Kushner joined Grand Budapest.
