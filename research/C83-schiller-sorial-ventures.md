# C83: Keith Schiller and George Sorial, the ex-bodyguard and ex-executive across deals

**Verdict: mostly-accurate. Confidence: medium.**

This is a third-layer connection hunt. It builds on C49 (the pardon market), C64 (lobbyists and fixers), C72 (Pakistan, WLF and minerals) and C77 (Greenland and GreenMet). Facts already set out there are only summarised here.

## Summary

- **Ownership.** Javelin Advisors is owned in equal thirds by Keith Schiller, George Sorial and Robert Seiden (Javelin's FARA filings). Pakistan's $200K/month minerals contract with Seiden Law was therefore partly passed on to a firm that Seiden co-owns.
- **Clients beyond Pakistan and Daibes:**
  - Greg Lindberg, seeking a pardon (still unresolved);
  - Taiwan's de facto embassy, TECRO (FARA No. 7705, March 2026);
  - I-Mei Foods of Taiwan;
  - Logical Strategies LLC, a New York company whose owners are not disclosed.
- **GreenMet.** Schiller and Sorial's other shared vehicle sells Washington access to foreign miners: Kamituga Mining (DRC) pays it $1M. GreenMet is also Tanbreez's development partner.
- **What is not documented:**
  - any White House meeting or access for either man since November 2024;
  - any US decision credited to them. Daibes got no clemency, and every policy overlap below is our own inference.

## What the brief asks, and what was found

### 1. Javelin is a three-way partnership that includes Pakistan's prime contractor. Grade: documented. Verdict: confirmed
- **The filing:** Javelin's FARA registration No. 7705 was received on 2026-03-23. It says the firm is "co-owned and controlled equally by George A Sorial, Keith Schiller, and Robert W. Seiden". All three are managing directors.
- **Who signs and where:** Sorial is the responsible officer, and Schiller signed the Exhibit AB. The address is 1701 Pennsylvania Ave NW, Suite 200, a few blocks from the White House.
- **Corroboration:** Business Today (2026-01-07) also reports that Seiden holds a stake.
- **The money chain:**
  1. Pakistan paid Seiden Law $200K a month (FARA 7570, signed 2025-04-08).
  2. Seiden subcontracted about $50K a month to Javelin (FARA 7579, 2025-04-25), a firm he co-owns.
- **What this does and does not show:** The arrangement is disclosed and lawful. No source calls it improper.
- **Access caveat:** efile.fara.gov could not be reached. The filing contents come from search-engine extracts of the PDFs.

### 2. Founding and registration. Grade: documented. Verdict: confirmed
- **Launch:** Javelin launched in December 2024, after the election (PR Newswire). Its LDA registration is effective 2024-12-10.
- **Schiller's listed government role:** The LDA form lists him as Deputy Assistant to the President and Director of Oval Office Operations, from about 2017-01-20 to 2017-09-20.
- **Address:** Boca Raton, FL in 2025, later Washington, DC.
- **First foreign client:** Pakistan, according to Global Witness.

### 3. Fred Daibes: about $1M for a clemency push. Grade: engagement documented; amount single-source. Verdict: mostly-accurate
- **The engagement:** LDA forms list "executive relief" for Daibes, who is serving a seven-year sentence for bribing Sen. Menendez.
- **The amount:** NBC's three unnamed sources say he paid $1M and hired Schiller for a "full court press on the White House".
- **His side:** His lawyer, Robert Travers, said Daibes had not worked with Schiller or Javelin since June 2025.
- **Outcome:** No clemency.
- **Qatar thread:** The Heritage Advisors / Qatar link to Daibes is in C73.

### 4. Greg Lindberg: a new pardon client. Grade: documented. Verdict: confirmed
- **The engagement:** Lindberg pleaded guilty in a roughly $2B fraud and money-laundering scheme. He hired Javelin in 2025 to lobby for an "executive pardon".
  - NOTUS says he hired them in the summer; another report says late October.
  - Insurance Business says he appeared on Javelin's filings for three quarters.
- **Opposition:**
  - On 2025-12-23, NC Insurance Commissioner Mike Causey, whom Lindberg was convicted of trying to bribe, urged Trump not to pardon him.
  - Sen. Thom Tillis was quoted saying he told Pardon Attorney Ed Martin "it was a bad idea". January 2026 North Carolina coverage (via search extracts) says Sen. Ted Budd also weighed in against a pardon.
- **Status:** We found no grant or denial. The latest coverage we saw (about March 2026) describes the request as pending.
- **Precedent:** Lindberg's earlier co-defendant, ex-Rep. Robin Hayes, was pardoned at the end of Trump's first term.

### 5. Taiwan: TECRO becomes a Javelin foreign principal. Grade: documented. Verdict: confirmed
- **The registration:** FARA No. 7705 (2026-03-23) names the Taipei Economic and Cultural Representative Office in the United States as foreign principal. Schiller signed the Exhibit AB.
- **Unknowns:** Fees and scope could not be read. A later registry date of 2026-09-25 is unexplained (possibly an amendment or termination).
- **Parallel hire:** Bloomberg Government (May 2026) reports that TECRO also retained Checkmate Government Relations ahead of the US-China summit.
- **The pattern:** Javelin and Checkmate also worked in parallel on Pakistan in 2025 (C64).
- **Name confusion to avoid:** The DSCA notice of 2025-12-17 on a Javelin missile sale to TECRO concerns the weapon, not this firm.

### 6. I-Mei Foods (Taiwan). Grade: single-source. Verdict: mostly-accurate
- **The contract:** Taiwan News reports that I-Mei signed Javelin for "US government relations services connected to potential contractual opportunities".
- **The signing:** Schiller reportedly visited I-Mei's Longtan plant for it.
- **Unknowns:** The article's date is unknown. An unofficial claim that I-Mei appears on Javelin's Q4 2025 LDA filing under a defense issue code is unverified.

### 7. Logical Strategies LLC. Grade: client record documented; amount and issue unverified. Verdict: unverified
- **What is documented:** A Senate LDA client record for Logical Strategies LLC (New York) lists Schiller as contact.
- **What is not:** The reported fee (about $1.2M in Q3 2025) and issue (the EPA asbestos rule) come only from an unofficial dossier site. No press coverage was found.
- **Why it matters if true:** The EPA under Lee Zeldin moved in 2025 to reconsider the 2024 asbestos ban. Any link between this client and that decision is *inferred*.

### 8. Pakistan follow-ups. Grade: mandate documented; congressional contacts single-source. Verdict: partly-accurate
- **Already established (C72):** Global Witness found a rare-earths mandate in the FARA filings, and Ronny Jackson was a reported contact.
- **New here:**
  - Bizz Buzz says the outreach also targeted Reps. Dan Crenshaw and Morgan Luttrell. We could not tell whether Javelin's or Conscience Point's filing records this.
  - There is an amended No. 7579 Exhibit AB dated 2025-05-09.

### 9. GreenMet shareholdings and Sorial's board listing. Grade: documented, with one single-source conflict. Verdict: mostly-accurate
- **Shareholdings:** CNBC and OCCRP/NPR (2026-01-22) report that Schiller and Sorial helped set up GreenMet in 2021 and remain shareholders. OCCRP named Schiller as a director.
- **Their position:** Sorial told CNBC: "We are passive minority shareholders in GreenMet and have no management role in the company."
- **The conflict:** An undated Sorial biography on acp-usa.org lists him as a member of GreenMet's board of directors. It may be out of date.

### 10. GreenMet's access business since November 2024. Grade: documented (Kamituga), otherwise single-source. Verdict: mostly-accurate
- **Kamituga Mining (DRC):** A one-year, $1M contract effective Sept 30 (2026), paid in 12 monthly payments of $83,333.37, to reach the "highest levels" of Washington (O'Dwyer's, 2026-10-22).
- **Locksley Resources (Australia):** Horn represents the antimony and rare-earth miner (E&E News, 2025-08-26).
- **Nigeria and Cameroon:** Horn is reportedly hunting deals there (billionaires.africa, 2026-06).
- **GreenMet's own lobbying:** It has lobbied since 2021 (about 27 LDA disclosures, on S.789 and other bills). Sabrina Katz registered as its in-house lobbyist in September 2025.
- **West Virginia hub:** A $150M, privately financed rare-earth hub in Rupert, WV was announced in July 2026.
- **What this shows for Schiller and Sorial:** As shareholders they would benefit from these contracts (NPR). No source says they worked on any of them.

### 11. US decisions touching their clients. Grade: decisions documented; link inferred. Verdict: partly-accurate
- **Tanbreez:** EXIM sent a $120M letter of interest for the project (June 2025). GreenMet is Critical Metals' development partner.
- **Pakistan:** Its 2025-26 gains are set out in C72.
- **DRC:** The US-DRC minerals partnership (December 2025) predates the Kamituga contract.
- **Pardon clients:** No clemency for Daibes, and none found for Lindberg.
- **Attribution:** No source credits Schiller, Sorial or their firms with any of these outcomes.

### 12. White House access since November 2024. Grade: not found. Verdict: unverified
- **What we looked for and did not find:** No visitor log, reported meeting or White House statement places either man with Trump or senior officials.
- **What the coverage does say:**
  - NBC's "full court press on the White House" describes what Daibes hired Javelin to do, not access that was proven.
  - Vanity Fair says "a lobbyist" asked senior officials to review Lindberg's application. We could not confirm that it was Schiller.
- **Donations (both unverified against FEC records):**
  - An unofficial dossier claims a joint $5,000 donation to Sen. Katie Britt's joint fundraising committee (September 2025).
  - An unofficial claim of a $4,000 donation to Ronny Jackson (C72).

## Corrections to the brief
- **Ownership:** Javelin is not only Schiller and Sorial's firm. Robert Seiden is an equal third owner.
- **The Daibes $1M:** The amount is NBC's figure, from unnamed sources. The LDA forms establish the engagement and its purpose, not the sum.
- **Tanbreez:** GreenMet is Tanbreez's development partner, not an equity holder (C77).

## Denials and other side
- **Sorial and Horn:** They say Sorial and Schiller are passive GreenMet investors with no management role.
- **Daibes:** His lawyer says he cut ties with Javelin by June 2025. Schiller and Javelin did not comment, and the White House does not comment on pardons.
- **Lindberg:** His lawyer says pardons should be given when the facts warrant it.
- **What registration means:** FARA and LDA registration is disclosure. No source alleges illegality or any outcome bought.
- **Access limits:** efile.fara.gov, lda.senate.gov, Taiwan News, NPR station sites and the dossier site were all blocked for direct fetch here. Contents were taken from search-result extracts.

## Not connected in the coverage we found
- **Seiden Law to Javelin as self-dealing.** *Documented* ownership and payment. That it is self-dealing, or that it matters, is our own framing. No source characterises it.
- **Javelin and Checkmate shared clients (Pakistan 2025, TECRO 2026).** *Inferred* from co-occurrence. No source says the two coordinated.
- **Logical Strategies and the EPA asbestos reconsideration.** *Inferred*, and the client's issue itself is unverified.
- **Javelin's Pakistan minerals mandate and GreenMet's minerals business as one Schiller/Sorial "minerals access" play.** *Inferred*, as already flagged in C77.
- **TECRO's hire and any US Taiwan decision (arms sales, tariffs, the US-China summit).** *Inferred* from timing only. The December 2025 arms notices predate the registration.
- **Daibes, Heritage Advisors / Qatar, and Javelin's other work.** *Inferred* (C73).
