# C98: Trump Media, licensing, golf and merchandise income during the presidency

**Verdict: mostly accurate** (confidence: high for 2025 figures; 2026 is not yet disclosed)

The question is what Donald Trump and the Trump Organization have earned outside crypto since January 2025, with cash kept separate from paper value. Most of it can be answered from primary documents.

- **Cash and reported income (calendar 2025).** Trump's own OGE Form 278e, filed 30 June 2026, reports:
  - about $413.6M of revenue from US golf clubs and resorts, including Mar-a-Lago at $77.5M and Doral at $121.9M;
  - GBP 30.4M from his two Scottish courses and EUR 18.9M from Doonbeg, about $59-63M in total;
  - about $63.1M of foreign licensing, management and development fees by our line-by-line sum ($38.4M from the Gulf);
  - about $5.0M of merchandise royalties and about $3.0M of book royalties.

  All of these are revenue, not profit.
- **Paper value.** Trump Media pays him nothing. The trust's 114,750,000 shares have not been sold, earn no dividend and were not reported as pledged. Their market value fell from about $4.1-4.6B at the start of the term to about $0.53-0.64B in October-November 2026.
- **No figure available.** We found no number for income in 2026, LIV Golf fees, the share of Mar-a-Lago revenue that comes from its reported $1M initiation fee, or any Trump Mobile payment to Trump.

**Method and access.** The 927-page 2026 OGE filing, the 2025 filing, and the SEC filings (Schedule 13D/A, 10-K, 10-Q, the TAE merger prospectus and the 2025 resale prospectus) were downloaded from OGE and EDGAR and searched in full; the business schedules were read line by line. Sums labelled "ours" are our own arithmetic from those lines. DJT prices come from market data and match the two prices stated in the SEC prospectus. CNN, NBC (via Yahoo), CREW, IndexBox and Front Office Sports were read in full. CNBC and Forbes blocked direct access, so their figures rest on search excerpts.

## The claim
What have Donald Trump and the Trump Organization earned since January 2025 from Trump Media (stake value, any sales or dividends), foreign and domestic licensing and development deals, golf clubs and resorts including Mar-a-Lago, and branded merchandise? Cash and paper value are kept separate.

## Sub-claims

### 1. The Trump Media stake is held in trust, unsold and paid nothing: confirmed
- **Who holds it.** Schedule 13D/A (22 Dec 2025): the Donald J. Trump Revocable Trust holds 114,750,000 DJT shares. Trump is settlor and sole beneficiary. Donald Trump Jr. is sole trustee with sole voting and investment power. The stake was 41.5% of 276,497,911 shares. The November 2026 TAE merger prospectus gives "approximately 41%". [4][5]
- **How the stake was diluted.** The April 2025 resale prospectus registered all 114.75M shares for possible resale, when they were about 52% of 220.6M shares. The May 2025 share issuance (55.9M PIPE shares) cut the percentage without any sale. [8][6]
- **No sales.** The only Forms 4 under Trump's own SEC number are from December 2024. The 13D/A reports no transactions in the prior 60 days and no pledge. [9][4]
- **No income.** The 2026 OGE filing lists the stake as "Over $50,000,000" with income "None (or less than $201)". [1]
- **No dividend or token.** The FY2025 10-K says there are no current plans to pay cash dividends. The Q3 2026 10-Q says the planned digital-token distribution to shareholders (record date 2 Feb 2026) was discontinued. [7][6]
- **No licence fee.** TMTG's name-and-likeness licence is royalty-free: $100 paid once, "and as of the date of this Annual Report… has not paid any other amounts" to Trump. [7]
- **Merger lock-up.** Under a 18 Dec 2025 support agreement, the trust must vote for the TAE merger (special meeting 14 Dec 2026) and is subject to transfer restrictions. [4][5]

### 4. Mar-a-Lago's $1M initiation fee: unverified (single source)
- CNN reports the fee rose from $100,000 before 2016 to $1M in 2024, "according to a person familiar with the matter". [11]
- The filing does not separate initiation fees from other club revenue, so no dollar figure for fee income can be given.
- CNN also cites $1M-a-head MAGA Inc. dinners at Mar-a-Lago, more than $1M of RNC spending at Mar-a-Lago and Doral in 2025-26, and FEC records of political committees paying both venues. [11]

### 5. LIV Golf, PGA and G20 bookings: unverified amounts
- **The events.** LIV played Doral in 2025. In 2026 it played Trump National Washington DC and was scheduled at Bedminster, and the PGA Tour's Cadillac Championship moved to Doral. [15]
- **No fees disclosed.** No event fee has been disclosed; Trump has called LIV's payments a "rental fee" and "peanuts". Whatever the fees are, they sit inside each club's revenue line.
- **G20 at Doral.** The White House says the 14-15 Dec 2026 G20 at Doral will be billed "at-cost" with "no profit". CREW says no method has been explained, and no accounting has been published. [20][21]

### 9. Income for 2026: not yet disclosed
The latest annual filing covers 2025. Club, licensing and merchandise income for January-November 2026 will appear only in the filing due in 2027. The 278-T reports filed in 2026 cover securities trades, not business income. [3]

### 10. The ethics arrangement: partly accurate as described
- **What it says.** Under the January 2025 white paper the businesses sit in a trust managed by Trump's children. New deals directly with foreign governments are barred, but private foreign deals are allowed. Outside counsel William Burck reviews major deals, and profits from foreign-government spending are to be donated to the Treasury. [19]
- **Where the money goes.** The filings show the income still accrues to Trump through the revocable trust.
- **Treasury donations.** No 2025 donation figure was found. The last reported donation was $151,470, for 2017. [24]
- **State-linked counterparties.** The Qatar licensee is Dar Global, but the project sits within Qatari Diar's state-owned development (see C16 and C31).

### 11. New borrowing: confirmed, collateral unstated
- The 2026 filing lists a Charles Schwab Bank "Pledged Asset Line" of over $50M at 3.90%, incurred in 2025. The collateral is not named. [1]
- In the same year the Seven Springs, 1125 South Ocean and 40 Wall Street mortgages were paid off. [1]
- No pledge of DJT shares appears in the 13D/A. [4]

## Corrections
- **Dar Al Arkan fees (C31, The National/IndexBox).** Claimed: about $21.9M from Dar Al Arkan in 2025, "nearly identical" to 2024. The record shows: the three Dar Al Arkan licensee lines sum to $20.55M, and with the $5.25M Doha fee from Dar Global Holdings the total is $25.80M, the figure CNBC reports. The Gulf total of about $38.4M is correct.
- **Damac fees (C31/C88).** Claimed: Damac's roughly $12.5M is an estimate drawn from value ranges. The record shows: the four Damac fee lines are exact and sum to $12,589,884.
- **Trust's share of TMTG (C20 and some Forbes pieces).** Claimed: the trust holds about 52-53% of TMTG. The record shows: that was true through March 2025. After the May 2025 PIPE the same 114.75M shares were 41.5% (December 2025) and about 41% (November 2026).
- **Foreign licensing totals.** CNN ("nearly $60M"), CNBC ($59.5M), CREW ($59M) and our line-by-line sum ($63.1M) differ only in which lines they count.
- **Bedminster entity.** Front Office Sports attributes Bedminster's $37.6M to Lamington Family Holdings LLC. The filing shows the revenue under Lamington Farm Club, LLC.
- **C17's 2024 royalty figures** check out against the primary filing.

## Denials and context
- **Trump Organization:** the filing "demonstrates that The Trump Organization continues to maintain a strong financial position, supported by world class, valuable assets, substantial liquidity and a conservative balance sheet", and its length "underscores our commitment to transparency". [12]
- **White House:** "Neither the President nor his family has ever engaged — or will ever engage — in conflicts of interest." [12]
- **Trump:** "You know why I'm profiting? Because the stock market's going up. Everybody's profiting." He also said: "I never speak to any of the people that run the money." [11]
- **The ethics plan:** the Trump Organization says outside institutions manage investments with automated trading. Eric Trump said the company would "vastly exceed" its legal and ethical obligations. [12][19]
- **Context:** most of this revenue comes from businesses Trump owned before the term. The licence fees come from private developers, which the 2025 policy allows. Nothing here shows that income was earned because of the presidency. The Trump Media decline is a paper loss, as the earlier gain was a paper gain.
