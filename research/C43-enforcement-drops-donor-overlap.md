# C43: Systematic overlap between dropped enforcement and Trump donors

**Verdict: mostly-accurate** (confidence: medium)

The overlap is documented and large. The SEC eased about 60% of the crypto cases it inherited, and many of the firms that benefited donated to Trump or did business with his family. Causation is not shown. Firms with no known Trump ties got the same treatment. The NYT found no evidence of direct pressure. The SEC and its chair deny any political influence.

## The claim
This check builds on C23 (parents C22 and C09). It covers five items:
- the NYT "about 60%" analysis;
- Robinhood's closed SEC crypto probe;
- Public Citizen's tracker;
- Crypto.com's Trump-connected lobbyist;
- Gemini's MAGA Inc. donation.

It also asks for the counter-arguments and official responses. C23 already covered the Coinbase, Kraken, Binance, Gemini and Ripple dismissals and the inaugural, parade and ballroom donations; they are not repeated here.

**Method note:** these sites could not be read directly: yellow.com, cointelegraph.com, theblock.co, citizen.org, pbs.org and thedailybeast.com. The NYT original was also not reachable. Findings rest on search-result excerpts from several outlets, and anything known from only one secondhand account is flagged.

## Sub-claims

### 1. The NYT found the SEC eased about 60% of inherited crypto cases, several tied to Trump. Verdict: mostly-accurate
- **Publication:** About Dec 14, 2025; reported by Cointelegraph, Yellow, Traders Union and The Daily Beast.
- **Main finding:** Of the crypto cases active on Jan 20, 2025, the SEC dismissed, paused or cut penalties in more than 60%, reportedly 14 of 23.
- **Comparison:** About 4% of inherited non-crypto cases got the same treatment.
- **New filings:** No new crypto cases were filed after the inauguration.
- **Trump-tied cases:** Binance, Ripple, Justin Sun/Tron and Gemini are among those with Trump ties.
- **Breakdown is unverified:** Secondhand accounts disagree on how many of the 14 had Trump ties. One says "eight", another implies all, and a third gives five dismissals plus three of seven other softened cases.
- **No pressure found:** The NYT found no evidence that Trump directly pressured the SEC.

### 2. Robinhood, an inaugural donor, had its SEC crypto probe closed. Verdict: confirmed
- **The closure:** The SEC told Robinhood on Feb 21, 2025 that it was closing the Robinhood Crypto investigation with no action. The probe had followed a May 2024 Wells notice. Robinhood announced the closure on Feb 24.
- **The donation:** Robinhood gave $2M to the Trump-Vance Inaugural Committee, per Senate filings reported by Bloomberg and InvestmentNews.
- **No documented link:** Nothing ties the closure to the donation beyond timing. It came the same week as the Coinbase, Uniswap and OpenSea closures.

### 3. Public Citizen tracks halted enforcement against firms with Trump ties. Verdict: confirmed
- **Counts over time:**
  - early 2025: 109 thwarted actions;
  - end of year one: 159 canceled or halted actions against 166 corporations;
  - September 2026 update: canceled inherited actions against 205 corporations.
- **Crypto figures:** Its "Deleting Tech Enforcement" report found that 23 of the dropped or halted tech actions involved crypto firms (20 withdrawn, 3 halted).
- **Political spending:** It ties these to about $1.2B in tech political influence spending, including Coinbase at $76.1M and Ripple at $55M.
- **Caveats:** Public Citizen is an advocacy group, and its counts mix investigations with cases. The donor link is its framing, not a statistical test.

### 4. Crypto.com hired a Trump-connected lobbyist. Verdict: mostly-accurate
- **AP's report (Dec 16, 2025):**
  - From late 2024 Crypto.com increased its payments to Jeff Miller of Miller Strategies, a GOP fundraiser and finance chair of Trump's inaugural festivities.
  - Miller lobbied the White House and the SEC.
  - Biden-era commissioners had authorized charges, and Crypto.com negotiated to delay any filing until after the inauguration.
- **Responses:** Crypto.com says Miller "had no involvement" in the SEC probe. Miller did not comment.
- **Unverified:** The fee amounts paid to Miller were not found. Crypto.com's parent mainly lobbies in-house and reported $500K in Q3 2025.

### 5. Gemini donated to MAGA Inc. Verdict: confirmed
- **USDC gift:** An FEC filing reported in early January 2026 shows Gemini Trust Company gave 1.5M USDC, liquidated on receipt. Foris DAX (Crypto.com) gave $20M in the same filing.
- **Bitcoin gifts:** On June 19, 2026, Cameron and Tyler Winklevoss each gave more than $5M in BTC.
- **CFTC timing:** The BTC gifts came 23 days after the CFTC joined Gemini's May 27-28 motion to vacate its $5M 2025 consent judgment.
- **Ruling:** Judge Hellerstein (SDNY) reportedly denied the motion in Oct 2026; only one source was found for this.
- **No documented link:** Nothing ties the gifts to the CFTC's move.

### 6. The policy shift also covered firms with no Trump ties. Verdict: confirmed
- **Closed probes:** The SEC closed its probes into Uniswap Labs (Feb 25, 2025), OpenSea (Feb 21), Yuga Labs (Mar 3) and Immutable (Mar 25). None is a known Trump donor.
- **SEC's stated reason:** It cited a reassessment by its Crypto Task Force under Commissioner Peirce.
- **What this means:** It supports a sector-wide policy explanation. The gap between crypto and non-crypto cases (about 60% vs 4%) is why critics still see favoritism.

### 7. Official responses. Verdict: confirmed
- **SEC:** It told the NYT that politics played no role.
- **Atkins at the Feb 11, 2026 House Financial Services hearing:**
  - He said "no" when Rep. Sylvia Garcia asked whether Trump, his family, cabinet or staff had asked him to take or decline an enforcement action.
  - He said "we are bringing cases".
  - On the Trump family's business: "I can't speak to that."
- **House Democrats:** Reps. Waters, Casten and Sherman wrote on Jan 15, 2026 that the pattern creates "the unmistakable inference of a pay-to-play scheme".
- **Peirce:** "There's not a pass on securities violations."
- **White House:** Leavitt says the President and his family never engage in conflicts of interest.

### 8. The overlap proves pay-to-play. Verdict: unverified
No document, testimony or official finding shows an exchange of donations for outcomes. The case rests on correlation and timing.

## Corrections
- "About 60%" means cases that were eased (dismissed, paused or given lower penalties), not only dropped.
- How many of those cases had Trump ties varies between secondhand accounts.
- Gemini's corporate MAGA Inc. gift was 1.5M USDC. The larger BTC gifts came from the twins personally.
- Crypto.com's fees to Miller are unconfirmed.

## Denials and context
- The SEC and Atkins deny political influence.
- Crypto.com says its probe was closed on the merits.
- Robinhood says its probe "never should have been opened."
- Some settlements still carried payments: Rainberry paid $10M in the Justin Sun case (Mar 2026), and Ripple's $125M judgment stands.
- Public Citizen and House Democrats are advocacy or partisan sources.
