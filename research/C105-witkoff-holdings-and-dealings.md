# C105: Steve Witkoff: holdings, divestiture and overlapping dealings

**Verdict:** mostly-accurate (confidence: medium)

**Question:** What has Steve Witkoff disclosed and divested, and where do his diplomatic assignments overlap with his family's business counterparts?

## Summary

Most of the record is documented in his own disclosure filings, which were read in full:

- **Status.** He was Special Envoy to the Middle East at the State Department from January to June 2025. Since 30 June 2025 he has been a full ("Non-Career") White House appointee: Assistant to the President and Special Envoy for Peace Missions. That he was a special government employee (SGE) in the first period is reported by watchdogs but is not stated in any document we found.
- **Filings.**
  - His new-entrant OGE Form 278e was signed on 13 August 2025 and published around 13 September.
  - It was certified by the White House ethics official only on 12 December 2025, after senators complained, and was corrected at that time to add the State post.
  - His annual report (labelled 2026, covering 2025) was signed on 29 July 2026. As published, it carries no reviewer signature.
- **Witkoff Group.** He sold an interest in the Witkoff Group for $120M "as part of divestiture planning". The forms name no buyer, date or share, and he still holds a stake worth over $50M.
- **World Liberty Financial.** Both reports still list his World Liberty Financial and SC Financial interests inside Witkoff Holdings LLC. Witkoff Holdings paid him $106.9M in 2025: $68.9M in cash and $38.1M in cryptocurrency.
- **Divestiture claims.** The White House says he has "fully divested" from WLF and does not take part in matters affecting his interests. No certificate of divestiture, ethics agreement, waiver, written recusal, buyer or recipient has been published.
- **Overlaps.** His work on the UAE, Qatar, Russia and Pakistan overlaps in time with family and WLF dealings with counterparts in those places (Aryam, MGX, Access Industries, Pakistan's government). We found no Saudi or Israeli dealing.
- **Investigations.** State's inspector general referred one request to its investigators in December 2025. No authority has made any finding.

**Evidence rule applied:** owning an asset, or a relative doing business with a foreign counterpart, is not by itself a violation. Each point separates (1) what the filings and official letters show, (2) what outlets report or infer, (3) who alleges wrongdoing, and (4) whether any authority has made a finding.

This extends C14 (WLF divestiture), C42 (the $107M), C53 (family Gulf money and letters), C65 (Qatar counterparties), C70 (Pakistan) and C76 (Access Industries, Dmitriev, Lukoil).

## Rules that apply

- **18 U.S.C. 208** bars an executive-branch employee from taking part personally and substantially in a particular matter that affects his own financial interest, or that of his spouse, his minor children, or an organisation where he is an officer, director or general partner. The bar can be lifted by a written waiver under 208(b)(1) or (b)(3). The interests of adult sons are **not** imputed to him under 208.
- **5 C.F.R. 2635.502 (impartiality)** asks an employee to weigh whether to take part in a specific-party matter where a relative with whom he has a close personal relationship is a party or represents a party. An agency may authorise participation under 2635.502(d).
- **Special government employees** are limited to 130 days of service in any 365. Only an SGE paid above a set threshold and serving more than 60 days must file a public report; others may file a confidential OGE 450.
- **Public filers** must file a new-entrant report within 30 days of appointment (extensions are possible) and annual reports by 15 May. Periodic transaction reports (278-T) are due no later than 45 days after a securities transaction. An agency reviewer has 60 days to review and certify a report.

## Sub-claims

### 1. Employment status: mostly-accurate (appointments documented; SGE status unverified)
- **Certified 278e** [2], read in full: it lists "Special Envoy to the Middle East, Department of State (1/2025 - 6/2025)". It gives the current post as "Assistant to the President and Special Envoy for Peace Missions" at the White House, with an appointment date of 30 June 2025 and appointment type "Non-Career".
- **Earlier dates:** Trump named him envoy on 12 November 2024. He was sworn in at the Oval Office on 6 May 2025 (a White House post cited in [6]).
- **SGE status:** this rests on watchdog research (Accountable.us [21], not reachable here) and secondary summaries. CREW wrote in July 2025 that it was unclear in what capacity State employed him and whether he was paid [17]. The White House says he takes no salary. No official document states that he was an SGE.

### 2. "Filed late": partly-accurate
- **(1) Filings, read in full:**
  - The new-entrant report was e-signed on 13 August 2025 with the note "Filer received a 30 day filing extension" [1]. It was published around 13 September [15].
  - As first published, it listed no other federal position in the prior 12 months and had no ethics official's signature.
  - On 12 December 2025, White House Designated Agency Ethics Official (DAEO) Scott F. Gast signed it as reviewer and certifying official. The revised version adds the State post [2].
  - The annual report was signed on 29 July 2026 with a 75-day extension. As read on 17 November 2026, it has no reviewer signature [3].
  - Counted from the 30 June 2025 appointment, the new-entrant report was on time, given the extension.
- **(2)/(3) The senators' case.** Warren and Murphy (17 November 2025 [6]) wrote:
  - that he "appears to have worked for months before disclosing his financial interests to the public";
  - that the 60-day review deadline passed in October 2025;
  - that the form wrongly listed no prior federal post.
- **(4) No finding.** Whether a public report was legally due for the State period depends on his status and pay at the time, which is not established. No State-period report, public or confidential, was found, and no authority has found a filing violation. The certified version fixed the omission. Neither form records a late-filing fee.

### 3. The $120M Witkoff Group sale: confirmed
- **What the forms show:**
  - Both reports, Part 2: "The Witkoff Group LLC (real estate management company)", value "Over $50,000,000", income $120,000,000, "Proceeds from the sale of an interest in the company as part of divestiture planning" [1][3].
  - Because the line appears in both reporting periods, the sale took place between 1 January and 13 August 2025.
  - No buyer, date or share is given, and he still holds a stake worth over $50M.
- **Managing roles.** Endnote 1 (December 2025) says he "is beginning the process of resigning" as managing member of 77 entities and will receive "only passive investment income". In July 2026 he was still "in the process ... in collaboration with relevant partners and lenders", and all 77 positions still read "Present".
- **Who runs the firm.** He told Bloomberg in March 2025 that he was divesting. His son Alex runs the firm [14].
- **The buyer.** Popular Information speculates the buyer could be a family member [16]. That is speculation; no source identifies the buyer.

### 4. World Liberty Financial divested and transferred to his sons: unverified
- **Filings, read in full.** Under "Witkoff Holdings LLC" (value over $50M), both reports list:
  - "WC Digital Fi LLC", holding "World Liberty Financial (cryptocurrency)";
  - "WC Digital SC LLC", holding "SC Financial Technologies LLC (stablecoin)".
- **Distributions.**
  - New-entrant period (January 2024 to August 2025): $34,363,535.
  - 2025: $106,938,819. The endnote splits this into "$68,875,000 in cash distributions and $38,063,819 in cryptocurrency".
  - Witkoff Holdings also owns real estate (Banyan Cay, Ocean Terrace) and golf and hotel operating companies, so the filing does not show how much came from WLF.
- **Statements over time:**
  - WLF (Zak Folkman, 23 May 2025): he was "in the process of fully divesting".
  - White House (September 2025): "still in the process of divesting" [19].
  - White House counsel Warrington to CNN (1-2 February 2026): he "has also divested from World Liberty Financial, notwithstanding his ability and willingness to recuse" [13].
  - Spokeswoman Anna Kelly to ABC (September 2026): "fully divested" [12].
- **What the July 2026 report shows.** It still lists the WLF and SC Financial interests and no transaction disposing of them. It reports 2025 holdings, so a disposal in 2026 would not necessarily appear.
- **What is missing.** No certificate of divestiture, 278-T, buyer, recipient, price or date was found.
- **"Transferred to his sons."** This comes from the Meeks-Amo-Garcia release [11] and an expert quoted via Bisnow [14]. No filing shows such a transfer.

### 5. No certificate of divestiture, ethics agreement, waiver or written recusal published: confirmed
- **What was checked.** The White House disclosures page lists only his two reports, with no ethics agreement, waiver or 278-T [4].
- **Unanswered questions.** The 22 October and 17 November 2025 letters asked for:
  - any 208(b)(1) or (b)(3) waiver;
  - any 2635.502(d) authorisation;
  - written recusals and any screening arrangement.

  No reply was found [5][6].
- **Comparison.** David Sacks's 208(b)(1) waiver was published [8].
- **White House position.** He "has not and does not participate in any official matters that could impact his financial interests" [13]. That describes a recusal, but no recusal document has been published.
- **Ethics review.** The ethics official certified the new-entrant report as compliant "on the basis of information contained in this report" [2].
- **Reddit sale.** The annual report lists a Reddit sale of $1,000,001-$5,000,000 on 1 October 2025. No 278-T for it appears on the White House page [3][4]. Whether one was filed but not posted is unknown.

### 6. Other holdings tied to counterparts: mostly-accurate
Holdings are documented in the filing; the identity of the partners comes from trade press, carried over from C53, C73 and C76.
- **One High Line.** "WG 76 11th LLC" (over $50M) holds "76 Eleventh Avenue Property Owner LLC", the owner of One High Line. Witkoff developed the project with Access Industries from December 2021 [28][16].
- **Other entities in the filing:**
  - Banyan Cay entities (co-owned with Access);
  - Ocean Terrace entities (with Alex Blavatnik, per Popular Information's correction);
  - a Bally's Corporation consultancy (12/2022-9/2023 in the certified version);
  - two Cayman Islands boat companies;
  - a 2025 JPMorgan aircraft loan of $25M-$50M.
- **Park Lane (our inference).** "WG 36 CPS Internal LLC" reports $3,350,000 of "sale proceeds and distribution" in the new-entrant period. 36 Central Park South is the address of the Park Lane Hotel, which the Qatar Investment Authority bought in August 2023 from a Witkoff-Mubadala joint venture [30]. The filing does not name the hotel; the link is our inference.

### 7. UAE overlap: confirmed (in time only)
- **Family and WLF side:**
  - Aryam Investment 1, backed by Tahnoon, agreed about four days before the inauguration to buy 49% of WLF for $500M. At least $31M of the upfront payment went to Witkoff-family entities (WSJ, via CNN and The Block, 1 February 2026 [13][20]).
  - On 1 May 2025, Zach Witkoff announced in Dubai that USD1 would settle MGX's $2B investment in Binance [25].
- **Official side:**
  - On 9-10 December 2024 (before the term), Witkoff spoke at Bitcoin MENA in Abu Dhabi as WLF co-founder and incoming envoy [22].
  - Gulf trip, with the UAE chip framework announced on 15 May 2025 [26].
  - Russia-Ukraine talks hosted in Abu Dhabi on 23-24 January and from 4 February 2026 [23], and again in November 2026 (date as captioned by CBS [24]).
- **The chip deal.** The NYT (15 September 2025, read via relay [19]) reported that he discussed the chip deal in White House meetings and pushed colleagues to finish it. The White House said he "did not participate" in the negotiations.
- **Not shown.** Whether the $31M is part of his $68.9M cash distributions is not shown. That is our own cross-reference.

### 8. Qatar overlap: partly-accurate
- **Before the term:** the Park Lane sale (August 2023) and Alex Witkoff's 2024 pitch to QIA, which QIA declined (C53).
- **Corrected report:** the reported $100M of Qatari-linked money in 2025 was corrected. The lender was Apollo itself [29][31].
- **In office:** he worked with Qatar as mediator on the January and October 2025 Gaza ceasefires. Accounts conflict over his warning to Qatar about Israel's strike on Doha on 9 September 2025 [33][34].
- **No Qatar-linked family transaction** dated within the term is documented. Qatar calls the NYT account false [32].

### 9. Saudi Arabia and Israel: unverified (no dealing found)
- He represented the US in Riyadh on 18 February 2025 and in Saudi meetings in January 2025 [6], and he works with Israel on Gaza.
- CREW says that after his appointment was announced, and before the term, he met sovereign-fund leaders in Saudi Arabia, the UAE and Qatar, without naming any deal [17].
- No dealing during the term was found between Witkoff companies or WLF and Saudi or Israeli counterparts.

### 10. Russia and Ukraine overlap: mostly-accurate
- **Negotiations:** meetings with Putin (including 6 August 2025 and 22 January 2026); Dmitriev in Washington (2-3 April 2025) and Miami (24-26 October 2025); the 28-point plan [37][38] (C76).
- **Business:** the One High Line partnership with Access landed a $525M loan in January 2026 [27]. Access says Blavatnik and Witkoff never discussed the negotiations.
- **Lukoil (single-source, NYT via relays [39]).** Putin raised the Lukoil foreign-asset sale with Witkoff and Kushner on 5 September 2026. The bidders reportedly include Tahnoon-chaired IHC. His spokesperson says he has "no conflict of interest and no financial stake". No family stake is documented.

### 11. Pakistan (other counterpart): confirmed (in time only)
- **26 April 2025:** WLF signed a letter of intent with the Pakistan Crypto Council [36].
- **18 June 2025:** Witkoff attended the Munir lunch.
- **14 January 2026:** SC Financial, which appears in his filings, signed a USD1 MoU with Pakistan's government, signed by Zach Witkoff [35].
- WLF says it receives no compensation from the MoU.

### 12. Complaints and outcomes: confirmed (no finding)
- **23 September 2025:** Warren and Slotkin asked the State and Commerce IGs and OGE to investigate Witkoff and Sacks [8].
- **22 October 2025:** eight senators (Schiff, Wyden, Kim, Durbin, Cortez Masto, Peters, Slotkin, Booker) put nine questions to Witkoff [5].
- **17 November 2025:** Warren and Murphy wrote to the White House counsel, OGE and the White House ethics official [6][7].
- **12 December 2025:** State's OIG "referred your request to our Office of Investigations for review", adding that it does not comment on criminal allegations [9][10].
- **8 January 2026:** Meeks, Amo and Garcia asked the acting IGs of Commerce and State to investigate [11].
- **Outcome:** no reply from Witkoff, the White House or OGE was found. As of 17 November 2026 there is no IG report, OGE action or finding.

### 13. Quid pro quo: unverified
- Lawmakers allege he "potentially" acted "in exchange for his personal financial benefit" [6].
- The record shows overlapping timing and continuing holdings, not a decision taken because of them.
- No authority has made a finding. Witkoff, the White House and WLF deny any link.

## Corrections for older checks
- **C14 and C42:** the $107M was paid by Witkoff Holdings LLC. It is a broad holding company: besides WC Digital Fi (WLF) and WC Digital SC, it owns real-estate and operating companies. The filing splits the sum into $68,875,000 cash and $38,063,819 crypto, but it does not attribute any of it to WLF.
- **C14 and C42:** the "$34M in 2024" figure comes from the new-entrant report, whose income period runs from January 2024 to August 2025. It is not a 2024-only figure.
- **C53 ("disclosures went uncertified"):** the new-entrant report was certified on 12 December 2025 and corrected to add the State post. The 2025 annual report is uncertified as published.
- **C53:** the Warren-Murphy letter is dated 17 November 2025; the press releases are dated 18 November.
- **C53:** the Meeks-Amo-Garcia release names the acting IGs of Commerce and State, not OGE.
- **C53 ("no IG outcome"):** State's OIG referred the Warren-Slotkin request to its Office of Investigations on 12 December 2025. No result has been reported.
- **C76:** the $120M sale was disclosed in September 2025, but it took place between January and 13 August 2025.
- **C14:** still no certificate of divestiture or recipient. The July 2026 annual report still lists the WLF interest.
- **The 22 October 2025 senators' letter** describes a "$2 billion investment" by a Tahnoon firm in WLF. In fact, MGX's $2B went into Binance and was settled in USD1. The equity investment in WLF was Aryam's $500M.

## Denials and responses
- **White House counsel Warrington (February 2026):** Witkoff "has not and does not participate in any official matters that could impact his financial interests. He has also divested from World Liberty Financial, notwithstanding his ability and willingness to recuse."
- **Anna Kelly (September 2026):** "fully divested from World Liberty Financial, and his prior commercial activities are completely unrelated to his efforts to end global conflicts".
- **White House (September 2025):** he "did not participate" in the chip negotiations, and the crypto deal is "totally unrelated to any government business".
- **Witkoff's spokespeople:** he "does not discuss WLF or any other personal business while conducting his official duties". On Lukoil, he has "no conflict of interest and no financial stake".
- **WLF:** Folkman said he has "no operational role, no financial interest in WLFI deals". Wachsman said neither Trump nor Witkoff "had any involvement whatsoever" in the Aryam deal, and that any link to chip decisions is "100% false".
- **His sons:** through lawyer Timothy Parlatore, they threatened the NYT with a defamation suit over its September 2025 report.
- **Qatar** calls that report false. **Access Industries** says Blavatnik and Witkoff never discussed the Russia-Ukraine negotiations.
