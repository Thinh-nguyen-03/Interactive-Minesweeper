# C61: Shared lawyers and law firms across the deals

**Verdict: mostly-accurate (confidence: medium).** Most of the individual lawyer-to-deal ties are documented in filings, congressional letters or several outlets. Several lawyers and firms do recur across Trump-family ventures, their counterparties and the government. Three things are not shown:

- **Coordination.** The firm-level overlaps (Paul Hastings, Dhillon Law Group) are our own cross-referencing.
- **Some links have only one source.** Four rest on a single outlet: the Edelman–Witkoff friendship, Edelman on the Board of Peace, Roth at Dhillon Law Group, and Goody Guillén acting for Justin Sun.
- **Wrongdoing.** Every principal denies it.

**Method note:** we mined C06, C14, C15, C27, C33, C34, C36, C37, C46, C48, C49, C52 and C57, then searched the web. Direct fetches failed here, including ProPublica's disclosure database, so some details come from search-result excerpts. These are flagged.

## The brief

This was a connection hunt for lawyers and firms that recur across the deals. The named starting points were:

- Goody Guillén/BakerHostetler, Paul Hastings, Brito, Roth, Kiley, Mizelle and Edelman
- Clare Locke and Sullivan & Cromwell
- Blanche's and Bondi's former clients
- Warrington, the White House counsel who signs ethics waivers

## Connections found

### 1. Goody Guillén: one lawyer for WLF and for Binance/CZ. Grade: documented
- **WLF letter (May 29, 2025).** Teresa Goody Guillén and Jonathan R. Barr of BakerHostetler wrote "on behalf of World Liberty Financial, Inc." to Sens. Warren and Merkley. They were answering the senators' May 14 questions about MGX's $2B Binance investment being settled in USD1 [1].
- **Binance/CZ lobbying.** Binance and Zhao had already registered her as a lobbyist, in late March 2025 per Bloomberg Law [3]. They paid her about $290K in 2025 (C36, C49).
- **CZ letter (Oct 28, 2025).** She wrote to Warren on Zhao's behalf demanding a retraction of Warren's post-pardon remarks (The Block). This came after the Oct 23 pardon. The Nov 2, 2025 document [2] is reported to be the reply from Warren's counsel, not her letter (audit correction).
- **Earlier ties.** The Trump transition considered her for SEC chair (CoinDesk, Nov 2024). She was an SEC Office of General Counsel attorney from 2009 to 2011 [7].
- **Justin Sun.** Molly White reported in Sept 2025 that Sun hired her for his suit against Bloomberg [6]. This is single-source.
- **What it shows:** the same lawyer spoke to Congress for both sides of the MGX/Binance/USD1 arrangement, and then pursued CZ's pardon.
- **What it does not show:** a quid pro quo. She calls those claims false [4][5].

### 2. Paul Hastings: three tracks at one firm. Grade: each tie documented; the pattern is inferred
- **SPAC counsel.** Paul Hastings was issuer's counsel on the S-1 for New America Acquisition I, the Trump sons' SPAC (Aug 4, 2025). It was again issuer's counsel on the $345M IPO (Dec 5, 2025) [8][9]. The firm called the "public policy tailwinds" line a scrivener's error (C27).
- **Martin Edelman** is Of Counsel there [10]. He is also G42's general counsel and an adviser to Abu Dhabi's rulers. He took one of Aryam's two WLF board seats after the $500M deal for 49% in Jan 2025 [11][13] (C06, C57).
- **Brad Bondi**, the then-AG's brother, co-chairs the firm's white-collar practice [16]. He joined Paul Hastings from Cahill Gordon in Nov 2024 (Lawdragon), so his Milton defence (2021-22) was a Cahill engagement.
- No source links these engagements. The firm has well over a thousand lawyers.

### 3. Brad Bondi's clients and his sister's DOJ. Grade: facts documented; influence alleged and denied
- **Milton's case.** Bondi was lead trial counsel for Trevor Milton. Milton was a Trump donor at about $1.8M [18], was pardoned in Mar 2025, and saw the SEC drop his civil case with prejudice in Sept 2025 [17].
- **Schiff/Min letter (Mar 11, 2026).** Sen. Schiff, Rep. Min and 10 colleagues asked the DOJ OIG whether Pam Bondi recused herself in four matters involving her brother's clients: Milton, Amesty, Chakraverty and Mehr [14][15].
- **DOJ's answer:** the decisions went "through the proper channels, and the Attorney General had no role in them."
- No OIG finding was found.

### 4. Edelman, Witkoff and the Board of Peace. Grade: single-source
- **Friendship.** Bloomberg Law's profile says Edelman and Witkoff have been friends since 1980s–90s New York real estate [11].
- **Money.** Witkoff-linked entities received at least $31M of Aryam's $250M upfront payment (C14). The two facts sit side by side, but no source says the friendship shaped the deal.
- **UAE honour.** The UAE gave Edelman its Order of the Union in June 2026 [12].
- **Board of Peace.** A leaked draft resolution reported by Drop Site News names Edelman (with Susie Wiles) to the Gaza Board of Peace executive board [19]. The White House list of Jan 16, 2026 did not include him [20]. This is unverified.

### 5. Dhillon Law Group: waivers, the inaugural committee and the library fund. Grade: documented for Warrington, single-source for Roth
- **Warrington's background.** David Warrington was a Dhillon partner from 6/2021 to 1/2025 [22] and the Trump campaign's general counsel. He was named White House counsel on Dec 4, 2024 [21].
- **Firm clients and pay.** Per ProPublica's summary of his disclosure, the firm's clients included Trump, Save America, the 2024 campaign and the Trump Vance transition. His salary was about $1.2M [23]. Yahoo reports the firm took in more than $13M from Trump-related work [24].
- **Inaugural committee roles.** ProPublica's summary also shows Dhillon partners as officers of the inaugural committee. We could not open the page to confirm who held which role.
- **Ethics decisions as counsel.** Warrington signed the Sacks waiver dated Mar 5, 2025 [25] (C34). He also told CNN that Witkoff had divested from WLF (C14).
- **Jacob Roth.** Roth was "with the Dhillon Law Group" as of 2025, per OpenSecrets as relayed by IBTimes [26].
  - He incorporated the Trump-Vance inaugural committee in Nov 2024.
  - He incorporated the Trump Presidential Library Fund on Dec 20, 2024, days after the ABC settlement [26][27].
  - His comment: "my job is simply to be the registrant."
- **Harmeet Dhillon.** The firm's founder is now Assistant AG for Civil Rights [28].

### 6. Brito: holding the escrow and paid by the PAC. Grade: documented
- **The escrow.** ABC wired the settlement on Dec 19, 2024 to the "Brito Law, PLLC Trust Account" [29]. In 2026 ABC said the $15M was still in escrow (C33).
- **PAC payments.** Save America paid Brito's firm nearly $300K in 2025 [30].
- **Other cases.** Brito handles Trump's suits against the NYT, WSJ, BBC and JPMorgan.
- **Other law firms the PAC paid:** Dhillon Law Group and Sullivan & Cromwell [31][32].

### 7. Kiley: a former Trump lawyer as library trustee. Grade: documented
- James Kiley represented Trump from 2020 to 2022 [34]. He is a trustee of the library foundation along with Eric Trump and Michael Boulos [33] (C48).
- That foundation holds the Miami parcel and is slated to receive the Qatari 747 by 2029 (C16).

### 8. Bondi: Qatar lobbyist, then the Qatar jet memo. Grade: documented
- **Lobbying.** Bondi was FARA-registered for Qatar's embassy at Ballard Partners in 2019–2020. Qatar paid Ballard, not Bondi personally, $115K a month [35][36].
- **The memo.** As AG she signed the DOJ memo finding it legally permissible to accept the jet [37]. The memo is unreleased; American Oversight has sued for it [38], and CREW has requested it under FOIA [39].
- **The two sides.** DOJ says career ethics officials found no conflict. Sen. Durbin says she had "a clear conflict of interest."
- **Ballard's crypto clients.** Ballard, Wiles's former firm too, later signed Kraken and Blockchain.com [40] (C52).

### 9. Blanche: from Trump's defence lawyer to dismantling crypto enforcement. Grade: documented
- **The memo.** As Deputy AG, Blanche disbanded NCET, the unit behind the Binance case, on Apr 7, 2025.
- **His crypto holdings.** At the time he held about $158K–$470K in BTC and ETH plus Coinbase stock [41]. He later shifted part of it to his adult children and a grandchild [41].
- **Complaints and recusal.** The Campaign Legal Center filed an OIG complaint [42], and senators pressed him [43]. CNN reported he had been advised to recuse from Trump matters [44].
- **DOJ's answer:** his actions were "cleared in advance."
- **Who replaced him for Trump.** Sullivan & Cromwell's Robert Giuffra took over from Blanche and Bove on Trump's hush-money appeal [45].

### 10. Mizelle: Affinity, then DOJ, then Kushner's lawyer. Grade: documented
- **Affinity.** Affinity hired Mizelle in 2022 [46]; he was its chief legal officer [47].
- **DOJ.** He was Bondi's DOJ chief of staff from Jan to about Sept 24, 2025 [48].
- **Kushner's lawyer.** By Oct 2026 he was representing Kushner against CREW's suit [49] and against Condé Nast (C15, C37, C46).
- No source alleges he touched Kushner matters at DOJ.

### 11. Clare Locke and Sullivan & Cromwell. Grade: no cross-deal role found
- **Clare Locke** appears only as Sacks's defamation counsel (C34).
- **Sullivan & Cromwell** appears only as Trump's appellate counsel and a recipient of PAC money [45][31].

## Not connected in the coverage we found
These are our own cross-references. No source asserts them.
- **Paul Hastings links its SPAC work, Edelman and Brad Bondi.** *Inferred.* The three are separate engagements at a large firm.
- **Edelman's friendship with Witkoff explains the Witkoff family's $31M from Aryam.** *Inferred.* The friendship itself is single-source.
- **Warrington and Roth worked together on the library fund or the inaugural committee.** *Inferred* from shared firm affiliation. Roth's affiliation is itself single-source.
- **Goody Guillén's WLF role helped secure the CZ pardon.** This is alleged by critics and denied. It is *inferred* from her dual role; no record shows it.
- **Bondi's past Qatar or Ballard work influenced the Kraken dismissal or other crypto outcomes.** *Inferred*; nothing documents it (C52).
- **Mizelle's DOJ tenure overlapped with Kushner or Affinity business before DOJ.** *Inferred*; no reported matter.
- **The ABC money in Brito's escrow reached the Roth-registered fund or the successor foundation.** *Unverified.* ABC says the money is still in escrow.

## Corrections
- The SPAC counsel role and Edelman are unrelated engagements at Paul Hastings.
- The CBS/Paramount $16M case was handled by Edward Paltzik, not Brito.
- Bondi is no longer AG. Trump announced her departure on Apr 2, 2026; Todd Blanche was acting AG from then and was confirmed by the Senate 50-49 on Aug 8, 2026.
- Qatar paid Ballard, not Bondi personally, $115K a month.
- Mizelle's Kushner roles came before and after his DOJ stint, not during it.

## Denials and context
- **Goody Guillén** denies pay-to-play.
- **DOJ** denies any Bondi role in her brother's cases and any conflict over the jet memo. It says Blanche's actions were cleared in advance.
- **Roth** says he is only the registrant.
- **Mizelle** calls the CREW suit meritless.
- **WLF and the White House** deny that Trump or Witkoff were involved in the Aryam deal.
- Reusing trusted counsel is normal. These ties map a network; they are not evidence of wrongdoing.
