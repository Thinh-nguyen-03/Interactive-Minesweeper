# C41 — Trump Media follow-ups: API customers, SEC complaint, Nunes, CRO treasury deal

**Verdict: mostly-accurate** (confidence: medium)

This is a follow-up lead from C21, C22 and C24, not a claim from the video. Most of the brief is documented. Two items need correcting:

- Nunes was named PIAB chair in December 2024, not when he left TMTG in April 2026.
- "TAE's CEO lobbying DOE" is a documented industry meeting, not registered lobbying tied to the merger.

The customer names rest on one anonymously sourced Reuters report.

Research note: direct fetches of thestandard.com.hk and abc7.com failed (the hosts are blocked here), and the Senate LDA API returned 403. Findings below rely on search results across several syndicated outlets.

## Sub-claims

### 1. Bloomberg, Susquehanna and Jump Trading subscribe to the Truth API: mostly-accurate
- Reuters (Anirban Sen and Edmund Lee, **Oct 28, 2026**) reported, citing people with direct knowledge, that these three signed up [1][2][3].
  - Bloomberg LP is a data provider; Susquehanna International Group and Jump Trading are HFT firms.
  - The price is up to $100K a month.
  - TMTG staff estimated the speed advantage at about **50 milliseconds**.
- All three firms declined to comment. TMTG has not named clients beyond saying in August that it had more than 10 agreements (see C21).
- Some syndicated copies are headed "Exclusive-Bloomberg…", but Bloomberg is a reported customer, not the reporting outlet.

### 2. Warren and Schiff asked the SEC to investigate, and the SEC responded: confirmed
- The letter to SEC Chair Paul Atkins is dated **July 28, 2026** and was released on July 29 [4][5]. It asks whether Truth API complies with federal securities laws.
- An SEC spokesperson **confirmed receipt and declined further comment** [6]. That is the whole of the "response."
- I found no report through mid-November 2026 that the SEC opened an inquiry. All three sitting commissioners are Republicans.
- Related: The Intercept and the Freedom of the Press Foundation sued in SDNY on **Aug 12, 2026** [7].
  - The suit seeks to stop the feed and to bar Truth Social-exclusive White House announcements.
  - I found no ruling yet.

### 3. Nunes left TMTG in April 2026 and was named PIAB chair: partly-accurate
- **The exit is confirmed.** TMTG announced on **Apr 21, 2026** (some outlets say Apr 22) that Nunes was stepping down, with Kevin McGurn as interim CEO [8][9].
  - Nunes said he would "focus more intently on my role as Chairman of the President's Intelligence Advisory Board and on other ventures."
- **The sequence is wrong.** Trump announced Nunes as PIAB chair on **Dec 14, 2024**, saying he would remain Truth Social CEO [10][11]. The appointment was formalized in February 2025.
- So Nunes held both roles for about 15 months. He was not newly named in April 2026.

### 4. Lighthizer and Swider resigned: confirmed
- Robert Lighthizer, the former USTR, resigned from the board and its committees **effective Mar 6, 2026** [12][13].
- Eric Swider notified TMTG on **Apr 6, 2026** that he was resigning effective immediately [14][15]. Some outlets dated the filing Apr 9-10.
- Both 8-Ks say there was no disagreement with management or the board.

### 5. The ~$6.4B CRO treasury venture with Crypto.com and Yorkville was terminated: confirmed
- **The deal.** Announced Aug 26, 2025 (see C22), it was a roughly $6.42B package [19]:
  - $1B in CRO
  - $200M in cash
  - $220M in warrants
  - a $5B equity line from a Yorkville affiliate
  - The vehicle was to be listed via Yorkville Acquisition Corp (MCGA).
- **The termination.** On **Aug 7, 2026**, TMTG, Crypto.com and Yorkville mutually ended it [16][17][18][19]. They cited "prevailing market conditions" and "shifting business and stakeholder priorities."
- **Also dropped:**
  - Crypto.com's servicing of the Yorkville America ETFs.
  - Direct prediction-market integration into Truth Social, replaced by a marketing deal for Crypto.com's prediction products.
- **What remains:** the Truth Social-branded ETFs and TMTG's CRO holdings.
- **TMTG's explanation:** McGurn told Axios the digital-asset treasury market had become crowded.
- **Market reaction:** CRO fell about 4%.

### 6. A planned Truth Social utility token: mostly-accurate
The plan was real but changed shape several times:
- **Apr 29, 2025:** Nunes's shareholder letter floated a utility token and wallet, first for paying for Truth+ [20].
- **July 2025:** TMTG teased the token again [21].
- **August 2025:** TMTG dropped its own token in favor of letting users convert "gems" into Crypto.com's CRO [22].
- **Dec 31, 2025:** TMTG announced a non-transferable **shareholder rewards token** [23][24].
  - One token per DJT share.
  - Crypto.com mints and custodies it, possibly on Cronos.
  - The record date was **Feb 2, 2026**.

I found no confirmation that the shareholder token has been distributed, and TMTG reserved the right to cancel it. No proprietary Truth token has visibly launched.

### 7. TAE's CEO lobbied DOE before the merger: partly-accurate
- **What is documented:** Michl Binderbauer and other fusion executives met DOE officials in December 2025, in the week before the Dec 18 merger announcement [25][26][28].
  - They urged DOE to steer billions toward fusion.
  - The meeting came weeks after DOE created its fusion office.
- TAE has held DOE grants since Trump's first term [27].
- **What I did not find:**
  - A Lobbying Disclosure Act filing naming TAE. The Fusion Industry Association's registered lobbyist does lobby DOE [29], but I could not query the LDA database directly.
  - Any report tying the meeting to the merger or to an award.

C24 already covered this briefly; this section adds the dates and the lobbying-registration gap.

## Corrections
- Nunes was named PIAB chair on Dec 14, 2024, not in April 2026.
- The customer list comes from anonymous Reuters sources. It has not been confirmed by TMTG or the three firms.
- The SEC's "response" was only an acknowledgment of the letter. No probe has been reported.
- "Lobbying" overstates the TAE item: the record shows an industry meeting with DOE about fusion funding.

## Denials and the other side
- **TMTG on Truth API:** it calls the feed an "industry-standard API product" with broad demand and says Democrats "continue to mischaracterize" it.
- **The three firms:** Bloomberg, Susquehanna and Jump declined to comment.
- **Board resignations:** both filings cite no disagreement.
- **CRO deal:** the termination was described as mutual and market-driven. Parts of the Crypto.com tie continue.
- **TAE:** its DOE grants predate the merger and span both parties' administrations. Rep. Don Beyer and ethics experts still flag conflict risk because Trump's trust would own about 20% of the merged company.
- **White House:** it denies the president's family engages in conflicts of interest.

## Leads
- **Intercept / FPF lawsuit against Truth API:** filed in SDNY on 2026-08-12, no ruling found [7].
- **Truth API's effect on TMTG revenue:** Q3 2026 reporting says feed sales tripled revenue (ADN/AP, 2026-11-10).
- **Shareholder token:** status of the token minted by Crypto.com is unknown after the record date [24].
- **TAE unpaid bills:** Bloomberg Government reported claims over unpaid bills against TAE.
